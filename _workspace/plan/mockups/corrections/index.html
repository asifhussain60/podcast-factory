A Sample Book of Study

On Patience in Study

Every book that reaches a reader has passed through many hands, and each pair of hands leaves something of itself behind. A patient reader is one who notices where that has happened, and who is willing to slow down where the page asks it.

When the teacher heard of the student's difficulty, the teacher said to him that patience in study is the first door, and that no one enters the second without passing through it. The student returned to his books that same evening and read until the lamp burned low.

Their word for this was الصَّبْر, the steadiness that holds a person to the work when the work refuses to yield. It is not the absence of struggle. It is what remains standing in the presence of it, and it is the quality every later virtue in the book is said to depend upon.

A second paragraph on the same theme follows, so that there is enough surrounding text to select a sentence, a phrase, or a whole passage and see how the bar and the panel behave against real reading width. Nothing here is drawn from any book in the library.

The chapter ends, as most of them do, with a short summary of what was said and a reminder of what remains to be read.
