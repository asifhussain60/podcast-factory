### Implication for transcription

Automatic transcription will work on all of it, but **accuracy will diverge sharply by tier**.
The 11 kHz / 16–24 kbps majority is exactly the condition where speech-to-text confuses
Arabic terminology, proper names and quoted verses. Any pipeline over this collection should
carry per-file confidence and treat the low tier as needing human verification, not as
finished text.
