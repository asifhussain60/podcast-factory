Quotation-card marks — the four in use

Chosen by Asif on 2026-08-11 from twenty candidates. Font Awesome Free 6.5.0, already vendored in this repo, drawn through a mask so each mark takes the ink of its own card. Each is shown large and again at the size it actually prints inside a header — the second is the one that decides.

Qur'an

AL-AHZAB · 6

Two shapes with real space between them, so it still reads as itself at header size. Replaces Font Awesome's book-quran, whose five interior lines silted up into a blob.

Prophetic tradition

Chosen from the Qur'an row rather than the speech-bubble family. It is the busiest of the four at 1.05em — the dome, minaret and arcade compress — which is a known trade and Asif's call.
