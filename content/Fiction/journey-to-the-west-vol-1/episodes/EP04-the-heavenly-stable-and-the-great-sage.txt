# The Heavenly Stable and the Great Sage Equal to Heaven

## Opening directive

Open: "Welcome. We're with Wu Cheng'en's *Journey to the West* and the chapter where Heaven tries to settle Sun Wukong with an office — first a stable, then a title with nothing inside it." Land the spine verbatim: *An empty title — an office without emolument — and there's an end to it.* Start mid-question. Forbidden openers: "today we'll discuss", "let's dive in", "deep dive", faux-profundity.

## Host dynamic

Host A (male) = scholar/teacher. Host B (female) = seeker/questioner. Roles do not rotate.

## Name discipline

- Sun Wukong → "Wukong" / "the Monkey King"; first mention "Sun Wukong".
- Heavenly King Virūḍhaka → "the gate-warden"; first mention "Heavenly King Virūḍhaka".
- Li Jing → "Heavenly King Li"; first mention "the Pagoda-Bearing Heavenly King Li Jing".
- Nezha → "Nezha"; first mention "the third prince Nezha".
- The Great White Planet of Venus → "the Planet"; first mention full.
- The Mighty Magic Spirit → same label every time.
- The Jade Emperor, the Two One-Horned Demon Kings, the Numinous Empyrean Treasure-Hall → same labels. No Chinese book titles spoken.

## Three-part focus

Walk source order across ten movements in three parts; land the spine verbatim once per part.

1. **Audience and stable.** Southern Gate barred by Virūḍhaka and the eight soldiers; Planet's hailing-cry; Numinous Empyrean rhapsody block-quoted; Wukong fails court rites — *That'd be old Monkey here*; Star Lord of Wuqu reports the vacancy; Wukong made BanMaWen, Keeper of the Horses; thousand-horse inventory; banquet rank-misreading — *Not great, not great. It is merely called "unranked."*; desk overturned; fight out through the Southern Gate.

2. **Four-word banner and two battles.** Homecoming — *One day in Heaven is a whole year in the world below.* Two One-Horned Demon Kings offer the russet-yellow robe: *Why not proclaim yourself "Great Sage Equal to Heaven"?* Banner up. Li Jing descends with the Mighty Magic Spirit, Fish-Belly General, Yaksha General; battle-ax broken; *Soft sack! Soft sack!* Nezha's six weapons in order: demon-slaying sword, demon-hacking saber, demon-binding rope, demon-subduing pestle, embroidered ball, fire-wheel. Wukong: three heads, six arms, cudgel multiplied; hair-double strikes Nezha's left arm; the seven sworn brothers each style themselves a Great Sage.

3. **Empty title.** Planet descends again; voice the doctrine precisely: *give him no affairs to manage and no salary to draw. Keep him quietly somewhere between Heaven and earth, so as to gather in his wayward heart and let no wild presumption arise.* Mansion of the Great Sage beside the Garden of Immortal Peaches; Bureau of Peace and Quiet; Bureau of Tranquil Spirit; two bottles of imperial wine; ten golden flowers. Close on the couplet: *His immortal name forever set in the scroll of long life; no more to fall through the wheel of rebirth — handed down for all the ages.* End on a reflective question: what does a listener today do when offered a title that flatters but commits to nothing — and what does the chapter suggest will follow?
