# Show notes — EP04

**Title:** The Heavenly Stable and the Great Sage Equal to Heaven

**Blurb:** The chapter in which Heaven's amnesty-and-recruitment policy is tested and answered with a second elegant pivot. Sun Wukong rides the cloud through the Southern Gate of Heaven, fails the rites of court before the Jade Emperor with "That'd be old Monkey here," and is installed in the only vacancy — the Imperial Stable — as a BanMaWen, a Keeper of the Horses. He tends a thousand heavenly horses sleek and fat for half a month before learning at the stable-officers' banquet that the post is "unranked": the lowest of the low. He overturns the desk, fights his way back through the Southern Gate, returns to Flower-Fruit Mountain, and on the advice of two One-Horned Demon Kings raises a four-word banner at his cave-mouth — GREAT SAGE EQUAL TO HEAVEN. The Pagoda-Bearing Heavenly King Li Jing and the third prince Nezha descend with their host; the vanguard Mighty Magic Spirit has his patterned battle-ax broken clean in two; Nezha transforms into three heads and six arms with the demon-slaying sword, demon-hacking saber, demon-binding rope, demon-subduing pestle, embroidered ball, and fire-wheel — and Wukong, matching the transformation, plucks a single hair, makes it spar in his likeness, and slips behind Nezha to strike his left arm. Heavenly King Li retreats. The Great White Planet of Venus comes forward a second time with an even more elegant proposal: let the title stand, but make it an empty title — an office without emolument, no affairs and no salary, only a name to settle the wayward heart. The Jade Emperor agrees, and Wukong is installed in a Mansion of the Great Sage Equal to Heaven on the right-hand side of the Garden of Immortal Peaches, with the Bureau of Peace and Quiet and the Bureau of Tranquil Spirit, two bottles of imperial wine, and ten golden flowers, on the charge of never again behaving wildly.
