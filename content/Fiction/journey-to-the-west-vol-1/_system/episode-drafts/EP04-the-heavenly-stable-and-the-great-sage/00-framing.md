# The Heavenly Stable and the Great Sage Equal to Heaven

## Opening directive

Open verbatim: "Welcome. We're with Wu Cheng'en's *Journey to the West* and the chapter where Sun Wukong rides up to Heaven, is appointed Keeper of the Horses, discovers the post has no rank at all, and forces Heaven into the elegant second pivot — title without office." Land the spine verbatim, three times across the episode: *Bestow on him only an empty title — an office without emolument — and there's an end to it.* Forbidden openers: "deep dive", "today's episode", "let's dive in", faux-profundity, "wow", "right?".

## Host dynamic

Host A (male) = scholar/teacher. Host B (female) = seeker/questioner. Roles do not rotate.

## Three-part focus

Walk in source order. Land the spine verbatim once per part.

1. **Through the gate and into office.** Virūḍhaka and his eight named soldiers bar the Southern Gate; the Planet's hailing-cry opens it; read the Numinous Empyrean rhapsody whole; Wukong's failure of court rites — *That'd be old Monkey here*; the immortals' alarm and the Jade Emperor's pardon; the Star Lord of Wuqu reports the one vacancy; *let him be made a BanMaWen — a Keeper of the Horses.*
2. **The unranked office and the four-word banner.** Read the horse-inventory whole; half a month of diligent grooming; the stable-officers' banquet; the rank-misreading — *Not great, not great. It is merely called "unranked." The very bottom*; the revolt-speech, overturned desk, fight out; *One day in Heaven is a whole year in the world below*; the Two One-Horned Demon Kings; the russet-yellow robe and the four-word banner — *Why not proclaim yourself "Great Sage Equal to Heaven"?*
3. **Two defeats and the empty-title pivot.** Land the spine. Li Jing and Nezha descend; the Mighty Magic Spirit's armor-verse, combat-verse, *Patterned-Blossom* ax broken clean, *Soft sack! Soft sack!*; Nezha's six weapons named in order; the hair-double strikes the left arm; the Planet returns — *give him no affairs to manage and no salary to draw*; Mansion of the Great Sage; Bureau of Peace and Quiet, Bureau of Tranquil Spirit; two bottles of imperial wine, ten golden flowers. Land the spine a third time. Close on the couplet: *His immortal name forever set in the scroll of long life.*
