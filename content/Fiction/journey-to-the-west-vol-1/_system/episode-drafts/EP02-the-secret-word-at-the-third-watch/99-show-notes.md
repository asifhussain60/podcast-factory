# Show notes — EP02

**Title:** The Secret Word at the Third Watch

**Blurb:** The chapter in which Sun Wukong actually receives the Way. Six or seven years of sweeping pass in the Cave of the Three Stars before the Patriarch Subodhi offers Wukong four "gates" of the Tao — Arts, Schools, Stillness, Action — and Wukong rejects every one because none of them grants eternal life. The Patriarch strikes him three times on the head, claps his hands behind his back, and walks into the inner quarters — a riddle the assembly misreads as anger and Wukong alone reads as a secret summons to the third watch by the back door. There, beneath the master's couch, the oral formula of the Golden Elixir is passed: essence, breath, and spirit held firm; the jade hare in the moon, the crow in the sun; the Five Phases gathered and reversed. Three years on, Wukong is warned of the Three Calamities — thunder, yin-fire from the Bubbling Spring point to the Niwan Palace, and a Mighty Wind through the nine apertures — that Heaven sends as the price of stealing its work of creation. He learns the seventy-two Earthly-Fiend transformations and the Somersault Cloud that covers a hundred and eight thousand miles in a single leap. A pine-tree transformation performed in jest gets him driven from the cave forever under threat of flaying. He returns home to find his troop bullied by the Monstrous King of Havoc, and there he tests his new arts for the first time — including the *Body-Outside-the-Body* method, in which one fistful of his eighty-four thousand hairs becomes two or three hundred little monkeys to swarm the fiend. The cave is burned, the captive monkeys are flown home on a wild gale, and the whole nest is given the master's surname Sun.
