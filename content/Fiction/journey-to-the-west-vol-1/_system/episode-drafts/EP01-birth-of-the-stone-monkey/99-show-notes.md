# Show notes — EP01

**Title:** Birth of the Stone Monkey

**Blurb:** The opening chapter of Wu Cheng'en's *Journey to the West* — the cosmological frame, the magic stone on Flower-Fruit Mountain, the stone monkey born from the wind-touched egg, the leap through the waterfall onto the iron bridge into the Water-Curtain Cave, and the coronation as the Handsome Monkey King. Three or four hundred years of innocent kingship pass, and then, at a banquet, the king weeps at the thought that King Yama will one day come for him. A Common-Backed Gibbon names the three classes of beings exempt from death — Buddhas, Immortals, Holy Sages — and the Monkey King rafts across the Eastern Sea to the Southern Continent of Jambu and walks eight or nine years through cities of men bent on fame and gain. He rafts a second time, across the Western Sea to the Continent of Cattle-Offering, and on a forested mountain hears a woodcutter sing a song an immortal has taught him. The woodcutter refuses to come along (he must look after his old mother) but points the way to the Cave of the Slanting Moon and Three Stars. There, after a sharp test, the Patriarch Subodhi takes a surname for the stone-born creature from the character-radicals of *sun* — child and small, *which fits exactly the root meaning of the newborn babe* — and gives him the Dharma-name Sun Wukong, *Monkey Awakened to Emptiness*. The novel has its protagonist; the work of the rest of the series can begin.
