# The Four Seas and a Thousand Mountains Bow in Submission

## Opening directive

Open: "Welcome. We're with Wu Cheng'en's *Journey to the West* — the sixteenth-century Chinese novel — and the chapter where Sun Wukong arms himself at every level of the cosmos and Heaven first puts him on notice." Land the spine sentence verbatim: *A guest cannot trouble two hosts.* Repeat that spine three times: open, extortion pivot, close. Forbidden openers: faux-profundity, "deep dive", "today's episode", "buckle up".

## Host dynamic

Host A (male) = scholar/teacher. Host B (female) = seeker/questioner. Roles do not rotate.

## Name discipline

- Sun Wukong → "Sun Wukong" / "the Handsome Monkey King" / "the stone monkey".
- Aoguang → "Aoguang of the Eastern Sea" first; then "Aoguang" or "the Dragon King".
- Brothers → Aoqin (South), Aorun (West), Aoshun (North).
- Kshitigarbha → "the Bodhisattva Kshitigarbha" first; then "Kshitigarbha".
- Ten Kings → "the Ten Kings"; "King Qinguang" at the memorial; "King Yama" when summoned.
- Jade Emperor → "the Jade Emperor".
- Great White Planet → "the Great White Planet of Venus" first; then "the Great White Planet".

## Three-part focus

1. **Arming the troop, raiding Aolai.** The king's worry — *Here we are at our games, but I fear that play may turn to earnest.* Somersault-Cloud; the wind verse (*Where the cannon-clouds rise, heaven and earth are shaken … the lofty Five-Phoenix Tower rocks to its very roots*); body-dividing magic empties the armory; forty-seven thousand monkeys mustered; seventy-two caves of monster-kings.
2. **Crystal Palace, cudgel, four-sea armor.** Iron-plank bridge down; saber, nine-pronged fork, Square-Heaven halberd rejected; the Dragon Mother points to the Heavenly-River-anchoring rod of the Great Yu; inscription — *The As-You-Will Gold-Banded Cudgel — weight, thirteen thousand five hundred catties.* Extortion: *A guest cannot trouble two hosts.* Cap, armor, shoes; exit on *Sorry to trouble you! Sorry to trouble you!*
3. **Darkness, Jade Emperor, amnesty.** Drunken sleep, soul-takers' noose, Hall of Judgment; Wukong's reply (*Old Sun has risen above the Three Realms and stands no longer within the Five Elements*); register struck out for every monkey. Aoguang's memorial and Kshitigarbha's memorial via King Qinguang on the same morning. The Great White Planet: *the host need not be stirred nor the army wearied; and an immortal is gathered in by the way of the Way.* Close on the spine.
