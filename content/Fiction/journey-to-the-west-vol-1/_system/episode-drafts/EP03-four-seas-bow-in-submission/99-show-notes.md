# Show notes — EP03

**Title:** The Four Seas and a Thousand Mountains Bow in Submission

**Blurb:** The chapter in which Sun Wukong arms his troop, his own person, and the wrong agencies of the cosmos all at once. He raids the kingdom of Aolai with a sand-flinging wind and empties its armory; musters forty-seven thousand monkeys and the seventy-two caves of monster-kings of the mountain; descends through the iron-plank bridge to the Crystal Palace of Aoguang, Dragon King of the Eastern Sea, and turns up the Heavenly-River-anchoring rod the Great Yu once used to measure the floods — a black-iron pillar bound in gold that shrinks and grows at his thought, the As-You-Will Gold-Banded Cudgel of thirteen thousand five hundred catties. He bullies the four Dragon Kings into a phoenix-winged purple-gold cap, chain-link golden armor, and cloud-walking shoes of lotus thread, and walks out crying *Sorry to trouble you! Sorry to trouble you!* Back on the bridge he shrinks the cudgel to an embroidery needle and tucks it in his ear, then grows ten thousand fathoms tall, the cudgel reaching the Thirty-Third Heaven above and the Eighteenth Hell below. He appoints Marshal Ma, Marshal Liu, General Beng, and General Ba, swears brotherhood with the Bull, Serpent, Roc, Lion-Camel, Macaque, and Marmoset Kings, and one night, drunk after a banquet, his soul is dragged down to the Region of Darkness. He bludgeons the soul-takers, climbs into the Hall of Judgment, and strikes the monkey-clan out of the Registers of Life and Death. Aoguang's memorial and Kshitigarbha's memorial both reach the Jade Emperor on the same morning, and the Great White Planet of Venus proposes the elegant solution: an edict of amnesty and recruitment — bring him up to Heaven, give him an office, bind his name to the rolls. The episode closes with Wukong and the Planet riding their clouds up beyond the Southern Heaven Gate.
