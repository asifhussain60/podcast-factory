# Show notes — EP05

**Title:** The Great Sage Plunders the Peaches and Steals the Elixir

**Blurb:** The chapter in which Heaven's "empty title" doctrine collapses on the very arrangement designed to prevent it. Idle in his mansion beside the Garden of Immortal Peaches, the Great Sage swears brotherhood with every star and constellation of the upper realm until the Perfected Man Xu Jingyang warns the Jade Emperor that an unoccupied monkey-spirit will breed mischief. Set as warden of the Peach Garden — three thousand six hundred trees ripening in three, six, and nine thousand years — Wukong eats his fill of the choicest fruit, and when the Queen Mother sends her seven robed maidens to gather peaches for the Festival of Immortal Peaches, they find him shrunk to a two-inch manikin asleep in the leaves. He fixes them where they stand with a body-binding spell, gulls the Barefoot Great Immortal away from the feast with a counterfeit summons to the Hall of Universal Brightness, takes the Immortal's likeness, and steals into the empty Jade Pool — sleep-insects on the wine-officers, the hundred-flavored delicacies and the jade-fluid and jasper-nectar consumed at leisure. Staggering homeward he blunders into the Tushita Palace, where he crunches down all five gourds of Lord Lao Tzu's refined golden elixir like roasted beans, then slips through the Western Heaven Gate by invisibility and goes down to Flower-Fruit Mountain. The maidens, the wine-officers, Lord Lao, the residence clerks, the Barefoot Immortal, and the Inspecting Numinous Officer all report; the Jade Emperor musters a hundred thousand heavenly soldiers under the Four Great Heavenly Kings, Pagoda-Bearing Heavenly King Li, and Prince Nezha, and spreads eighteen frames of heaven-and-earth net about the mountain. The Nine Luminaries are beaten back at the iron-plank bridge; the Four Heavenly Kings and the Twenty-Eight Constellations join battle from the hour of the Dragon until sundown and take the seventy-two cave-kings and the One-Horned Demon King captive, but Wukong, plucking a handful of hairs and crying "Change!", scatters a thousand and a hundred Great Sages across the field and routes the heavenly princes back to their evening camp. The cave holds; the night-watch rings the mountain; tomorrow's battle waits for the dawn.
