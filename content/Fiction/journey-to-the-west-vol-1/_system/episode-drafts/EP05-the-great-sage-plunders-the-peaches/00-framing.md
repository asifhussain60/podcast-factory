# The Great Sage Plunders the Peaches and Steals the Elixir

## Opening directive

Open with one warm welcome sentence naming Wu Cheng'en's *Journey to the West* and previewing Heaven's "empty title" collapsing on a warden set over three thousand six hundred trees of immortality. Land the spine verbatim: *With nothing to do, he will one day breed mischief in his idleness.* Forbidden openers: "deep dive", "today's episode", "let's dive in", faux-profundity, "wow", "right?".

## Host dynamic

Host A (male) = scholar/teacher. Host B (female) = seeker/questioner. Roles do not rotate.

## Name discipline

- Sun Wukong → "Wukong".
- Xu Jingyang → first "the Perfected Man Xu Jingyang", then "Xu Jingyang".
- Barefoot Great Immortal → first full, then "the Barefoot Immortal".
- Lord Lao Tzu → first "Lord Lao Tzu, Supreme Patriarch of the Way", then "Lord Lao".
- Pagoda-Bearing Heavenly King Li → "Heavenly King Li".
- Queen Mother, Dipankara, Nezha, Jade Emperor, Inspecting Numinous Officer → as written.
- Beng and Ba → name once, then "the generals".
- No Chinese book titles. Concept-words English ("body-fixing spell", "Nine-Cycle Golden Elixir", "heaven-and-earth net").

## Three-part focus

1. **Diagnosis and orchard.** Brotherhood with every star; Xu Jingyang's warning (spine, second placement). Warden of three thousand six hundred trees, three cohorts of twelve hundred ripening in three, six, nine thousand years. Secret peach-eating; two-inch manikin asleep at the top.

2. **Three transgressions.** Seven-robed maidens; body-fixing spell *Stay! Stay! Stay!*. Barefoot Immortal gulled by counterfeit summons to the Hall of Universal Brightness. Jade Pool plundered — sleep-insects, jade-fluid and jasper-nectar. Drunken misturn into Tushita Palace; five gourds of golden elixir *crunched down like roasted beans*. Wukong's self-justification verbatim — *inner and outer are one and the same*.

3. **Muster, melee, night-watch.** Invisibility through the Western Heaven Gate; *Sweet or not sweet, it's the water of home.* Cascading reports. Hundred-thousand-soldier muster: Four Heavenly Kings, Heavenly King Li, Nezha, Twenty-Eight Constellations, Nine Luminaries with Rahu and Ketu, eighteen frames of heaven-and-earth net. Nine Luminaries beaten back at iron-plank bridge; melee from hour of the Dragon to sundown; *Victory and defeat are the common lot of soldiers.* Land the spine a third time, then close on one grounded question: what arrangement in our own lives gives a wayward heart a perch to plunder what it was set to guard? End on the question.

## Tone constraints

Three governing analogies, at most once each: (1) the orchard-warden — the office becomes the means; (2) the blameless decoy — the Barefoot Immortal's turn to the wrong hall; (3) the diagnosis the policy ignored — Xu Jingyang's warning. Source-images (manikin, sleep-insects, roasted-beans elixir, hair-double) may be used in passing. No invented modern analogies. Preserve verbatim: *Stay! Stay! Stay!*; *Sweet or not sweet, it's the water of home*; *These several matters — they are true, all true. But what of it now?*
