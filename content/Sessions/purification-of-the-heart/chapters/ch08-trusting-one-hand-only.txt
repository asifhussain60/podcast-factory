That is why he says: fear of other than Allah and desire for other than Allah are incompatible with تَوَكُّل. Allah says: وَعَلَى ٱللَّهِ فَلْيَتَوَكَّلِ ٱلْمُتَوَكِّلُونَ — upon Allah let those who trust, place their trust. If you are preoccupied with other people, you do not have تَوَكُّل. And if you lack تَوَكُّل, the reason is an absence of يَقِين — certainty. The يَقِين you are missing is this: that all good comes from Allah, and that no harm can befall you except by His will. That is what تَوْحِيد is teaching us — to be utterly certain that only Allah can harm you or benefit you, and for that reason, fear of human beings dissolves.

This does not mean becoming reckless or impetuous — driving like a madman and saying, "It is all written by Allah." That is not تَوَكُّل. The Prophet, peace and blessings be upon him, told the Bedouin: "Tie your camel, then put your trust in Allah."

Then he says: the root of every disease is a lack of certainty. What is prohibited of these two — fear and desire directed at the creation — is specifically that which prevents an obligation from being fulfilled. If it leads instead to neglecting something recommended, it is considered reprehensible. In either case, flee to your Lord from both. Do not desire the creation and do not fear the creation. And the cure for both, he reminds us, is to know with certainty that no one harms or benefits except Allah.
