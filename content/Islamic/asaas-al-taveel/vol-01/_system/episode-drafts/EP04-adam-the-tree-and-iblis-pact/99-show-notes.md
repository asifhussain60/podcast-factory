# Show notes — EP04

**Title:** Adam, the Tree, and the Pact of Iblis

**Blurb:** Al-Nu'man opens his interpretation of the prophetic cycles at the origin of the lower hadd of Imamate itself — Adam, peace be upon him. The Qur'anic scene of the angels' objection, the formation of Adam from clay, the long pause before the spirit, the teaching of the names, Iblis's refusal to prostrate, the creation of Eve, the garden, the forbidden tree, the pact of respite, the fall, the repentance — every element is read on its inner side. The hadd that Iblis vacated is filled by Eve as Adam's twelve-rank hujja. The garden is the rank of supportive knowledge, al-ta'yid. The tree is not wheat, fig, or grape, but a likeness for the Master of the Resurrection — the disclosure entrusted to him alone, seized prematurely. Adam's stripping is the loss of the garment of piety, which is the word. His repentance restores him to the rank of caliph and Imam, and the Imamate begins from him as the architecture of the four limits requires.
