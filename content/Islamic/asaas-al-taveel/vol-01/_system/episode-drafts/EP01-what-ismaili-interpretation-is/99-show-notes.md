# Show notes — EP01

**Title:** What Ismaili Interpretation Is

**Blurb:** The editor's introduction to Asas al-Ta'wil — Arif Tamir's 1960 Beirut framing of why this 10th-century Fatimid treatise on inner interpretation was kept in private custody for a thousand years, what the Ismailis mean by ta'wil (as distinct from tafsir), why the Speaker bears revelation and the Imam- Foundation bears its inner meaning, and how al-Nu'man's two books — Da'a'im al-Islam and this one — together form the body and soul of the Fatimid intellectual project. Sets up the six Speaker-Prophet chapters that follow: Adam, Noah, Ibrahim, Moses, Jesus, Muhammad. The seventh chapter, on the Awaited Imam, is deliberately unwritten.
