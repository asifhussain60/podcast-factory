# Show notes — EP02

**Title:** The Call to Inner Meaning

**Blurb:** Al-Nu'man's own opening of Asas al-Ta'wil. He explains why he wrote a second book after his apparent-law volume Da'a'im al-Islam, why inner interpretation has to be taught by stages — milk before solid food, suckling for two years, symbols before subtleties — and why the testimony "There is no god but Allah," from the very first day the Prophet spoke it, already contained the whole sacred law, against the common opinion that says the easy creed came first and the heavy obligations later. Sets up the structural claim that the next chapter will unfold: that every created thing is paired, outward and inward, body and soul — and that the Creator is described by none of the pairs.
