# Show notes — EP05

**Title:** The Two Parties and the Line to Noah

**Blurb:** Al-Nu'man closes the first prophetic cycle. He develops the two- parties doctrine at length, threading together the Qur'an's own naming of Hizb Allah against the party of Satan. He refuses the popular literalism that froze the forbidden tree into wheat, fig, or grape and read human fingernails as its residue. He refuses, by name, the inherited Jewish-source tale of Adam and Eve hiding naked among the trees while God walked there asking "Where are you?" — calling out Ka'b al-Ahbar and Abdullah ibn Salam as the channels through which the material entered the tradition. He rehabilitates Adam to the rank of caliph and Imam. He gives the chain of five upper and five lower limits in the Prophet's own voice. He walks the lineage from Adam through Seth, Idris/Enoch, and the seven Imams of the first cycle down to Lamech, who completes the cycle and hands the matter to Noah. Then he opens the chapter on Noah, Hud, and Salih, and the listener hears Noah's people refuse him in the same shape Iblis had refused God — "you are nothing but a human being like us." The architecture's claim on history begins to walk.
