### Host context (from review)

**Enoch / Idris — 365 years as cosmological signature (Asif, 2026-06-09)**

The 365-year lifespan is a deliberate cosmological encoding, not a literal biographical claim. In Ismaili philosophy, numbers function as cosmological signatures — outer forms pointing to inner spiritual realities. The specific correspondence: 365 years of earthly life = 365 degrees (days) in the solar cycle (shams — the Sun). This is the macrocosm / microcosm (alam / insan) harmony that Ismaili philosophy encodes throughout its symbolic system: the physical world is a reflection of the spiritual world, and the celestial spheres and their movements are purposeful and interconnected with the human soul.

The clock analogy (already wired into the friction beat above, reproduced here for clarity): think of the universe as a grand, perfectly calibrated clock — just as the hands of a clock align to complete an hour, the life of a prophet aligns with the grand cycles of the cosmos. For Idris, each year of earthly life mirrors a single day in the Sun's celestial revolution.

The Saturn detail: tradition records that Idris ascended and revolved with the sphere of Saturn for thirty years. This deepens his cosmic rank — Saturn is the outermost classical sphere, associated with the highest levels of spiritual station. When introducing this in conversation, attribute it to "broader Islamic cosmological tradition" or "the tradition records that Idris is associated with the sphere of Saturn" — do NOT attribute it to al-Nu'man's text, which does not mention Saturn.

Closing logic: Allah elevates His chosen prophets to high stations on the basis of this cosmic and spiritual purity. The 365 is not incidental but declarative — his earthly duration acts as speculative framing showing his complete synchronization with the celestial cycle. The physical number is the outer representation of an inner, spiritual completeness.
