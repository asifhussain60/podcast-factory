HOST_B: [curious] What if the tree in the garden of Adam was never about a piece of fruit at all? What if the whole story has been hiding a different shape underneath?

HOST_A: That is precisely the question this chapter of Foundations of Inner Interpretation by the author al-Nu'man opens with. And he gives an answer that almost everyone, on a first hearing, finds a little startling. The tree is not a tree — it is a likeness for the Master of the Resurrection, the disclosure entrusted to him alone.

HOST_B: Say that again. He is telling us the tree stands in for a person, not a plant.

HOST_B: The seven Speaker-Prophets.

HOST_A: Yes. Adam, Noah, Abraham, Moses, Jesus, the Prophet of Islam, peace and blessings of Allah be upon him and his family, and the seventh — the Master of the Resurrection, who closes the cycles. The earth, he insists, is never devoid of a guardian of God with His proof, even for the twinkling of an eye.

HOST_B: And he cites the verse for that.

HOST_A: He cites the chapter of the prophets, verse twenty-two — if there were gods in them other than God, they would both be corrupted. One after one, he says. A proof of the unity of the Almighty.

HOST_A: Two names. The Speaker, because he pronounces the apparent law. And the silent one, his proof, because he is silent from the outward and carries the inner. HOST_B: [interrupting] Wait. He is saying the Imam standing behind the Prophet is the night to the Prophet's day. That is not a poetic flourish for him — that is the actual structure.

HOST_A: It is the structure. And it is why he must begin the prophetic history at Adam, because Adam, he says, is the lower limit of the Imamate itself. The Imamate is one limit below the limit of prophecy, even though their obedience is equally binding.

HOST_B: [thoughtful] All right. So I have a modern question already. If Adam is the model — the origin of the whole chain — and Adam is also the one who slips, who reaches for what he should not reach for, that seems backwards. You would think the origin should be the unbroken one.

HOST_B: [thoughtful] And the author translates that.

You raised me up in spiritual cultivation, the adversary is saying, and granted me brilliant supportive knowledge carrying no dense body with it.
