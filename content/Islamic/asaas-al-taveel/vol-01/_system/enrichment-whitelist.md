## Works (Tier 1 for this book)

- *Asas al-Taweel* (Foundations of Esoteric Interpretation) -- the book itself, used for cross-volume self-reference. Al-Numan explicates the inner meanings of the six prophetic cycles; the ta'wil of one prophet illuminates another.
- *Daaim al-Islam* (Pillars of Islam) -- al-Numan's foundational Fatimid legal code in two volumes, defining the zahir (exoteric law) whose batin (esoteric meaning) Asas al-Taweel unlocks. The two works are companion pieces.
- *Iftitah al-Dawah* (The Opening of the Mission) -- al-Numan's history of the early Ismaili da'wa and the Fatimid rise to power. Contextualizes the da'wa as itself a prophetic-cycle continuation and provides narrative background to the cyclical theology of Asas al-Taweel.
- *Kitab al-Himma fi Adab Atba al-Aaimma* (The Book of Resolve: Conduct of the Followers of the Imams) -- al-Numan's ethical guide for the da'wa community. Enumerates the obligations of the initiated believer to the Imam, directly connected to the prophetic covenants in Asas al-Taweel.
- *Al-Majalis wal-Musayarat* (Sessions and Conversations) -- al-Numan's record of private sessions with Imam al-Muizz. Contains the Imam's oral ta'wil rulings that supplement and authorize the written interpretations in Asas al-Taweel.
- *Sharh al-Akhbar* (Exposition of Traditions) -- al-Numan's three-volume hadith commentary from the Ismaili perspective. Provides the prophetic hadith substrate that Asas al-Taweel reads esoterically; hadith cited in Asas al-Taweel chapters are usually traceable here.
- *Tawil al-Daaim* (Esoteric Interpretation of the Pillars) -- al-Numan's esoteric companion to Daaim al-Islam. Where Daaim al-Islam states the exoteric rule, Tawil al-Daaim gives the ta'wil; Asas al-Taweel often operates on the same symbolic level.
- *Kitab al-Iqtisar* (The Book of Brevity) -- al-Numan's legal compendium. A condensed legal reference that establishes the zahir frame for ta'wil work elsewhere.

## Adjacent Ismaili Da'wa corpus (Tier 2 -- same tradition, not same author)

The Ismaili da'wa predecessors and contemporaries al-Numan draws on, argues with, or is authorized by. Citations from these works are **second-priority** enrichment; they should not displace al-Numan's own corpus when both are available.

- *Kitab al-Kashf* (Abu Yaqub al-Sijistani, d. ca. 971) -- early Ismaili ta'wil treatise on prophetic cycles and the seven Imams; Asas al-Taweel operates within the same symbolic universe.
- *Ithbat al-Nubuwwat* (Abu Hatim al-Razi, d. 322/934) -- defense of prophethood and the Imamate from the Ismaili perspective; one of the foundational da'wa texts al-Numan inherits.
- *Kitab al-Islah* (Abu Hatim al-Razi) -- correction of cosmological errors; part of the Ismaili disputational tradition Asas al-Taweel participates in.
- *Kitab al-Yanabii* (The Wellsprings, Abu Yaqub al-Sijistani) -- ta'wil of Quranic cosmology; shares vocabulary and symbolic levels with Asas al-Taweel.
- *Rahat al-Aql* (Hamid al-Din al-Kirmani, d. ca. 411/1020) -- written after al-Numan but the most systematic account of the Ismaili cosmological framework that Asas al-Taweel presupposes; useful for explanatory enrichment.
- *Imam al-Muizz's oral rulings* (transmitted in Al-Majalis wal-Musayarat) -- the Imam's own ta'wil pronouncements carry the highest authority in the tradition.
- Imam Jafar al-Sadiq's reported sayings (peace be upon him) -- the foundational hadith corpus of the Imami line; al-Numan grounds many ta'wil moves in Imam Jafar's reported interpretations.
