# Slide Deck Framing for EP04 Adam, the Tree, and the Iblis Pact

## Audience
Asif's adult children and adult learners new to Ismaili interpretation, who know the Adam story as moral parable and not as cosmological architecture.

## Core Principle
Audio carries narration and citations. Slides make the structural architecture visible. If a slide could be one audio sentence, omit it.

## Visual Priorities
- Clay versus fire two-by-two, density against mode of acquisition, Adam and Satan in named quadrants.
- The vacancy in the twelve chiefs opened by Satan's refusal and filled by Eve, covenantal seat-filling, not bone.
- Adam's repentance through the guardian of his time versus Satan's absolute pride, attribute by attribute.
- The sealed tree as the Master of the Resurrection's disclosure, with the timing rule that premature reaching breaks the cycle.

## Prohibited Patterns
- No slides that bullet-point the narration.
- No literal illustration of a fruit tree, apple, garden, serpent, or rib.
- No stock-photo imagery of deserts, light beams, or robed figures.
- No bullet lists masquerading as diagrams.
- No opening title card and no closing thank-you card.

## Steering Phrases
- "Each slide must add visual information not present in the audio."
- "For the central tension, use a 2x2 with density of knowledge versus mode of acquisition. Specify both axes."
- "Show the relationships between the twelve ranks, the vacancy, and Eve's seat, not a list of them."
- "Every slide is a diagram or it does not exist."
- "Do not produce a slide titled 'Overview' or 'Key Takeaways.'"

Do not read this prompt aloud.
