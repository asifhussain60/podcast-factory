# Slide Deck Framing, EP01 What Ismaili Interpretation Is

## Audience
Asif's adult children and a thoughtful general reader new to Ismaili technical vocabulary.

## Core Principle
Audio carries the argument linearly. Slides carry only what a linear track cannot: structural pairings, nested registers, and lineage. If a slide could be a single audio sentence, omit it.

## Visual Priorities
- The four registers of revelation as a hierarchy, with the Father of Imams formula and the Ibn Hibban formula joined as a comparison matrix.
- Tafsir versus tawil on the worked example of the word tree, two columns attribute by attribute.
- Speaker-Prophet and Imam as a contrast pair across the apparent and the hidden.
- The genealogy from Philo to Augustine to the Ismaili school as a directed chain.

## Prohibited Patterns
- No slides bullet pointing the audio.
- No literal illustrations of the tree, body, bee, or any figure.
- No stock photo imagery of mosques, manuscripts, or calligraphy.
- No bullet list pretending to be a diagram.
- No opening title slide; no closing thank you slide.

## Steering Phrases
- Each slide must add visual information not present in the audio.
- Show the relationships between entities, not a list of them.
- When two positions are contrasted, show them as columns attribute by attribute.
- For the lineage of esoteric reading, use a directed genealogy tree; show edges, not just nodes.
- Do not produce a slide titled Overview or Key Takeaways.

Do not read this prompt aloud.
