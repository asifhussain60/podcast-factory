# Slide Deck Framing for EP05 Two Parties and the Line to Noah

## Audience
Asif's children and thoughtful young adults who have followed this volume's audio. They know Adam's story but have not seen the covenantal line drawn as a chain.

## Core Principle
The audio carries the argument. Slides render what prose cannot show at a glance: the two-party pair, the ten-rank hierarchy, the Adam-to-Noah genealogy, and the literalist versus inner contrast.

## Visual Priorities
- The two parties as side-by-side columns, the Quranic verdict bolded under each.
- The Five Upper plus Five Lower Limits as one ranked hierarchy, with the rebel as a sixth position across both.
- Adam to Noah as a directed chain of eight bequeathals, Seth marked as Foundation, the seven Imams numbered.
- Literalist versus inner reading as a comparison matrix, one row per disputed claim.

## Prohibited Patterns
- No slides that bullet-point the audio.
- No literal illustrations of Adam, Noah, the tree, or the idols.
- No stock-photo imagery of paths, hearts, or scrolls.
- No bullet-lists masquerading as diagrams.
- No title slide; no thank-you slide.

## Steering Phrases
- "Each slide must add visual information not present in the audio."
- "For the central tension, use a contrast pair with both sides named."
- "When influence flows from A to B, show a directed arrow from A to B."
- "Every slide must have a named diagram type."
- "Do not produce 'Overview' or 'Key Takeaways' slides."

Do not read this prompt aloud.
