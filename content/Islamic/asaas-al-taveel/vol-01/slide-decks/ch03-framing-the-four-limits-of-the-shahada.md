# Slide Deck Framing, EP03-the-four-limits-of-the-shahada

## Audience
Asif's adult children and adult learners new to Ismaili interpretation, who have recited the Shahada all their lives but never seen it as cosmological architecture.

## Core Principle
Audio carries narration and citations. Slides make the four-limit architecture spatially visible. If a slide could be one audio sentence, omit it.

## Visual Priorities
- The four words of the testimony mapped to four ranked limits (Pen, Tablet, Speaker-Prophet, Silent One), as a vertical hierarchy with affirmation above negation.
- The contrast pair between Natiq ("Naa-tiq") and Asas ("A-saas"), side-by-side columns of role, image, bodily, heavenly, and earthly likeness.
- The directed genealogy of seven Speaker-Prophets and seven Silent partners across the cycles.
- The seven plus twelve equals nineteen matrix, across testimony, prophetic line, cosmos, and creation.

## Prohibited Patterns
- No slides that bullet-point what the narrators just said.
- No literal-text slides whose only content is a chapter heading.
- No stock-photo imagery of pens, tablets, mosques, or starscapes.
- No bullet lists masquerading as diagrams.
- No opening title card; begin with the first content slide.

## Steering Phrases
- "Each slide must add visual information not present in the audio."
- "Every slide must have a named diagram type."
- "For the four limits, use a vertical hierarchy with affirmation above negation."
- "When influence flows from A to B, show a directed arrow from A to B."
- "Every slide is a diagram or it does not exist."

Do not read this prompt aloud.
