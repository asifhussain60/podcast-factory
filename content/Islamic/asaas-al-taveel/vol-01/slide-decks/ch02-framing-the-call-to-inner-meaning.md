# Slide Deck Framing , EP02 The Call to Inner Meaning

## Audience
Asif's children and thoughtful young adults, new to Ismaili interpretation, who have heard the audio and want a visual scaffold.

## Core Principle
Each slide must add visual information the audio cannot carry: outward and inward as paired structure, the five messengers' foundations, the vertical stack of seventy faces.

## Visual Priorities
- Outer conformity with and without inner acceptance, as a two-column pair where the outward acts are identical.
- The five messengers, as a comparison matrix with one shared inward row.
- The seventy faces of revelation, as a vertical hierarchy where each face is a rung of comprehension.
- Concentric circles placing Allah outside every pairing of the created order.

## Prohibited Patterns
- No slides that restate the audio.
- No literal-text slides of Quranic verses.
- No stock-photo imagery.
- No bullet-list slides masquerading as diagrams.
- No Overview, Agenda, Key Takeaways, or Thank You slides.

## Steering Phrases
- "Each slide must add visual information not present in the audio."
- "For the five messengers, use a comparison matrix: rows for the messenger, columns for outward foundation versus shared inward law."
- "Show the relationships between entities, not a list of them."
- "Every slide must have a named diagram type."
- "Avoid stock-photo-style imagery."

Do not read this prompt aloud.
