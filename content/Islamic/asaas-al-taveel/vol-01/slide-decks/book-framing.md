# Slide Deck Framing, Foundations of Inner Interpretation, Volume One

## Audience
Asif's children, thoughtful young adults who have followed the five audio chapters. Familiar with the narrative, not yet trained in the Ismaili vocabulary.

## Core Principle
The deck visualizes the book's structures; the audio carries the argument. Each slide adds visual information the audio cannot carry.

## Visual Priorities
- The outward-vs-inward contrast that opens chapter one, as two attribute-by-attribute columns with a shared row.
- The four-words-to-four-limits architecture of the testimony in chapter three, as a vertical annotated structure with two upper and two lower limits.
- The forbidden tree of chapter four, as an annotated structure naming the tree as the rank reserved for the Riser, not wheat, fig, or grape.
- The genealogy from Adam through Seth down to Noah, as a directed chain with Speaker, Silent, and Imam labels marked.
- The two-parties contrast of chapter five, as side-by-side columns of orientation, behavior, and end state.

## Prohibited Patterns
- No slides whose only content is the chapter title.
- No slides that bullet-point what the audio already said.
- No literal illustrations of Quranic verses or of the tree, ark, staff, or cross as objects.
- No stock-photo imagery, landscapes, mosques, scrolls, calligraphy panels.
- No bulleted lists pretending to be diagrams.
- No closing slide that says Questions or Thank you.

## Steering Phrases
- "Each slide must add visual information not present in the audio."
- "Show the relationships between entities, not a list of them."
- "When two positions are contrasted, show them side by side as columns, attribute by attribute."
- "For lineages, use a directed chain with explicit arrows."
- "Every slide must have a named diagram type."

## Visual Style
Black-and-white pen-and-ink line-art only. Hierarchies as tree diagrams. Contrast pairs as two-panel layouts split by a vertical rule. Genealogies as arrow chains with named nodes. Process flows as numbered-step illustrations. Annotated structures as a drawn whole with callout lines. No colour, no photographs, no gradients. Each slide is a drawn diagram, not a bullet list.
