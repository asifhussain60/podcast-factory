The Almighty, having forgiven Adam, made clear that His party are those who cling to Him and who have taken His guardians as their own. The party of Satan are those whom the adversary has acquired and cut off for himself: he shared with them in their guardians among them, so they took as their guardians his enemies — who are also their enemies — and disowned His guardianship and theirs, and took as their guardians Satan and his lieutenants. But whoever was abased by Satan and regretted his abasement, who resigned from his stumbling and turned back to his Lord — that one is not of Satan, nor of his party. The Almighty says: "And whoever does evil or wrongs himself but then seeks forgiveness of Allah will find Allah Forgiving and Merciful." The sixth Imam, peace be upon him, said: "Do not consider as a guardian one whom Satan has abased entirely; rather, consider as a guardian a man who turns back, relying on his Lord."

The two parties are named in the Quran itself. Describing the party of Satan, the Almighty said: "Have you not considered those who make allies of a people upon whom Allah has placed His wrath" — meaning the nation of misguidance — "they are neither of you nor of them, and they swear to falsehood while they know. Allah has prepared for them a severe punishment; evil indeed is what they used to do. They took their oaths as a cover and averted people from the way of Allah, so for them is a humiliating punishment. Never will their wealth or their children avail them against Allah at all. Those are the companions of the Fire; they will abide therein eternally — on the Day when Allah will resurrect them all, and they will swear to Him as they swear to you, thinking that they are upon something. Unquestionably, it is they who are the liars. Satan has overcome them and made them forget the remembrance of Allah. Those are the party of Satan. Unquestionably, the party of Satan — they will be the losers." Then He described His own party: "You will not find a people who believe in Allah and the Last Day having affection for those who oppose Allah and His Messenger" — meaning the Imams of injustice and their followers — "even if they were their fathers or their sons or their brothers or their kindred. Those — He has decreed faith within their hearts and supported them with a spirit from Himself. And He will admit them to gardens beneath which rivers flow, wherein they will abide eternally. Allah is pleased with them, and they are pleased with Him. Those are the party of Allah. Unquestionably, the party of Allah — they are the successful."

The Prophet, peace and blessings be upon him, was reported to have said that there is in every body a piece of flesh which, if it is sound, the whole body is sound, and if it is corrupt, the whole body is corrupt — and that piece is the heart. The Party of Allah is the body whose heart has been turned toward the guardian; the party of Satan is the body whose heart has been turned away.

The cursed adversary abased Adam and Eve when he said to them what Allah Almighty has reported concerning him in His Book: "Your Lord did not forbid you this tree except that you might become kings, or that you might become among the immortals," and he swore to them: "Indeed I am to you among the sincere advisors." He made it clear to them that the limit of the permissible was what Allah had warned them about, and said to them that the limit of obligatory action is the limit of effort and exertion, and that if they were to leave it they would end up among the gentle spiritual angels and be stripped of the dense material world — that they would be two kings eternal in what they desired. As the Almighty said: "But if they had not been beguiled, they would not have tasted of the tree, and it appeared evil to them." That is, they transgressed the limits of speech that had been permitted to them, into the limit belonging to the Lord of the Resurrection — that of which Allah had warned them — and they extended their hands to what was not theirs. So they ate from it; they ate from the tree, and they fell into sin and disobedience, and that which supported them was cut off. That support is the garment of piety which Allah mentioned in His Book: "And that is from the signs of Allah."

The general interpretation took the fall on its face: that the tree was a wheat tree, while others said a fig tree, and others said a grape tree; and that its clothing was made of a nail-like material, so it was forbidden to them, and they repented, and what remained of it remained in their fingertips, and that remained in their children; and that this is why most animals have nails and the like — like the hooves of cows and sheep, and the claws of predators and birds. This is a claim which the minds do not accept and which is not correct on examination. The forbidden tree was not a botanical fact. It was a likeness for the Master of the Resurrection, as has already been laid out, and the lesson of the trespass was the lesson of reaching for a disclosure that belonged to a higher limit. To freeze the parable into a wheat plant and to look for the proof of the story in human fingernails is to fix the outer and to lose the inner.

The same applies to what they claimed: that Adam and Eve had their sins exposed, so they sewed for themselves a covering of fig leaves. This too is something that is not proven, because it did not happen; and Allah has remembered him, and he is too noble for that, being a prophet whom He has chosen and honored. Even granting that he is one of the followers, that He would look at him as a man would look at a slave who had angered him, then strip him of his clothes and leave him naked, beginning with the figure of his disgrace by that act — they attribute this to their idol, but Allah is exalted above that. The picture of God walking through Paradise's trees, calling out "Where are you?" to a hiding Adam, asking him whether he ate from the tree, hearing Adam blame Eve, hearing Eve blame the serpent, and hearing the serpent blame the adversary who came through the underground — this is inherited material that came into the tradition from those who became Muslim from among their Jewish ancestors. Among others who claim it was thus in their tradition. In the Torah and the Gospel, as in the Quran, there are proverbs, signs, and symbols, and an outward that needs interpretation, which is the stored knowledge. Allah Almighty says: "Say: O People of the Book, you stand upon nothing until you uphold the Torah and the Gospel and what has been revealed to you from your Lord." From this it is known that they do not establish this except by interpretation. The Torah is like the outward and the Gospel is like the inward; and Allah Almighty will establish them all with what Allah has sent down of the knowledge that Adam deposited and transmitted.

As for the saying mentioned above, "And their Lord called them" — His call is a call from Him to them — not a call like the call that is heard from those who call out. The same applies to His sayings, blessed be His name — this has been explained before. The servants engage with that by attributing to Allah what is contrary to Him and not appropriate for Him. What is customary among worshippers is that a voice comes only from a voice, from a hollow and a passageway, and that speech is the same only with lips and tongue. The whole picture of God walking among trees and shouting out a question is a projection of human voicing onto a Lord exalted above any such thing.

When Allah turned in repentance to Adam, He did not remove him from the rank of the caliphate and the Imamate which He had set up for him, nor did He deprive him of his substance, his word, and his wisdom in general. Rather, He brought him down — and Eve with him — from the limits of interpretation without a veil. He said: "Go down from it together, and you will have therein dwelling and enjoyment for a time." He said: "Therein you will live, and therein you will die, and from it you will be brought forth." He said: "O children of Adam, We have bestowed upon you clothing to conceal your private parts, and as adornment. But the clothing of righteousness — that is best. That is from the signs of Allah, that perhaps they will remember." So Allah brought Adam down from the limit of interpretation without a veil; yet He did not deprive him of substance. What was connected to him of material descended with him from the upper celestial limits, connected to the signs of Allah.

The Messenger of Allah said: "I take the revelation from Gabriel, and Gabriel takes it from Michael, and Michael takes it from the Trumpet-bearer, and the Trumpet-bearer takes it from the Tablet, and the Tablet takes it from the Pen." The support is connected to the word of the Five Upper Limits — the Pen, the Tablet, the Trumpet-bearer angel, Michael, Gabriel — and they are connected to those who respond by the Five Lower Limits: the Foundations; the Imams; the Pilgrims, who are the proofs of their own time; the Captains, who are the chief deputies of the call; and the Wings, who are the preachers of the call. The sixth of the two ranks of limits is the rebel — and Satan became an enemy to Adam and against him, from Allah Almighty and against Allah Almighty — whether the limit he stands against be a Speaker, a Silent One, an Imam, or a Proof. Whoever takes them as guardians and is loyal to them is from the party of Adam, which is the party of Allah; and whoever has dismissed their command or claimed their place is from the party of the adversary.

The same thing that happened to Adam and Eve happened in their descendants — and their descendants, even if they were numerous, did not differ in their kinds, from male and female, as it was with Adam and Eve. And so it happened in all of obedience and disobedience, as happened in Adam and Eve. The repentance of those who repent is like their writing. The destruction of those of them who perished in disobedience and came to the punishment of Allah by disbelief and by committing what He forbade — this is because of the adversary's possession of them and the fact that they were of his party, and their cessation from Adam, and their departure from his lineage. So they entered the entrance of those who became from him, and they became other than him — that is, other than from Adam, because they were cut off from him. The Almighty said: "And whoever takes them as allies among you, then indeed he is of them." Whoever takes the adversary as ally is from him, and they are his sons by calling, and his family by calling. For the Almighty said concerning the son of Noah when he disobeyed him: "He is not one of your family."

He named them the party of Satan and called them by his name. He said: "Indeed, Satan is a clear enemy." And He said: "Over My servants you shall have no authority. Except for those of the deviators who follow you, even if they know not their promised time. It has a number of gates; to each gate is an allotted portion." By "the gates," I mean the messengers, who are the gates of the pure outward truth — for each of them there is a known sacred law. Some of those who repaid that law gave themselves over to the Fire. Of this the Prophet said to the Father of Imams: "You are the Guardian of the Fire," for he will say to it on the Day of Resurrection: "This one is yours, take him; and this one is mine, leave him." The sinner is the son of Adam who persists in his sin — neither repenting of it nor casting it off. He is like Satan in his persistence: he has departed Adam's party and entered Satan's. The repentant who turns back from what he has committed, and the obedient born of Adam, are Adam's party, which is the Party of Allah. The Almighty says: "And those who, when they commit an immorality or wrong themselves, remember Allah and seek forgiveness for their sins — and who can forgive sins except Allah? — and they do not persist in what they have done while they know. Their reward is forgiveness from their Lord, and gardens beneath which rivers flow, wherein they shall abide forever."

The Father of Imams, in the canonical collection of his sermons known as *The Peak of Eloquence*, frames the choice in the very same terms when he says that the two ways are not far apart but that one of them is paved by remembrance and the other by forgetfulness, and that the worshipper does not walk between them but on one or the other — and that whoever stands still on the road of forgetfulness is, in his standing, already walking. Letter 31 of *The Peak of Eloquence*, in the rendering of Sayed Ali Reza. The two-party doctrine is, on the Father of Imams' framing, a doctrine about which road the worshipper is on at any given moment, not about which side he claims to belong to.

Allah Almighty benefited Adam through His will: He settled him on the earth and made him a successor over those whom He placed there. Adam's descendants multiplied with him, Allah's argument was established against them, and Adam was the bearer of the message to them. He entrusted his son Abel. His brother Cain envied him and killed him, as Allah has described. That occurred during Adam's lifetime, so that the lesson of the will would be made plain to him. Whoever entertained evil thoughts, persisted in disobedience, and did not repent of his sin was of Satan's party. Allah then granted Adam, from among his descendants, a son whom he named the Gift of God. To him Adam entrusted the inner knowledge; he took him into his confidence and commanded him to keep the secret, fearing that Cain would kill him as he had killed Abel. The custom was thus established of guarding the secrets of the covenant and preserving them. Allah prepared Adam for the reward He had set aside for him in the Abode of Decision, and after him the matter of the Imamate passed to the will of Seth — the Gift of God and Seth being the same person, the descriptive name and the personal name standing for one named successor.

Things came to pass with his son in the manner described: remembrance after remembrance, station after station, Imam after Imam — until the matter reached the one whom Allah mentioned in His Book concerning Enoch. This was within the Cycle of Adam; then it concluded with the era of Noah. The Resident Imam in Adam's era was the first of the proofs of his time, and his proofs were twelve, among them the adversary, who refused submission to Adam. Adam descended at Aden — perhaps Aden in Yemen — then migrated to Mecca, whose name was then Faran, where he died at 930 years of age and was buried at the foot of a nearby mountain. Before that he had bequeathed to his son Seth, who was called the Gift of God. Seth was born in the year 230 from Adam's descent, died in 1142, lived 912 years, and bequeathed to his son Enosh. Enosh died in 1385, lived 950 years, and bequeathed to his son Kenan. Kenan, born in 625, died in 1535, lived 910 years, and bequeathed to his son Mahalalel. Mahalalel, born in 795, died in 1690, lived 895 years, and bequeathed to his son Jared. Jared was born in 960, died in 1922, lived 962 years, and bequeathed to his son Enoch — the same prophet called Hermes the Thrice-Blessed and the first to write with the pen. Enoch was born in 1122 and was raised up in 1487, having lived 365 years, and bequeathed to his son Methuselah. Methuselah was born in 1287 and died in 2242, the year of the flood; his death came just before the flood. He lived 955 years and bequeathed to his son Lamech. Lamech, born in 1454, died in 2346 — 104 years after the flood — and lived 891 years. He completed the cycle of Adam, and was the seventh of its Imams. He handed the matter to his son Noah, after the divine will decreed the change of the old law and its replacement by a new one: the Law of Noah, the Second Speaker. Seven names, seven Imams, one cycle. The chain is preserved exactly as the four-limits chapter required: a Speaker at the top, a Silent One paired with him, seven Imams across his cycle, twelve proofs in his time.

Allah sent Noah as a messenger speaking a sacred law different from the law of Adam. The Almighty says: "For each of you We have appointed a law and a way." Adam's cycle ended when Noah's era came upon them. None of them rose to messengership until he had first held fast to the law of those who came before him; the predecessor's covenant was taken up, and he bound himself to it. The Almighty says: "And when We took from the prophets their covenant — and from you, and from Noah, Abraham, Moses, and Jesus, son of Mary — We took from them a solemn covenant." The cycle of a Speaker does not simply end. It hands over. The new Speaker rises only after he has held the law of the one before him, and the covenant between them is what binds the cycles into one chain.

In the name of Allah, the Most Gracious, the Most Merciful. Praise be to Allah, Lord of the Worlds. May Allah bless the Prophet, the Seal of the Prophets, and his executor, the Father of Imams, the best of the guardians; and the pure Imams from his successive progeny until the Day of Judgment. Allah sent Noah as a prophet bearing a new sacred law, after the law of Adam had altered and corruption had increased among his descendants. So Noah called the people to his law, to worship Allah Almighty and affirm His oneness outwardly, as Allah informed him in His Book: "We sent Noah to his people, saying: 'O my people, worship Allah; you have no god other than Him. Will you not be conscious of Him?'"

Those who responded from among the weak responded; the arrogant rejected him; and those who were content with the law of Adam answered him by saying: "You are nothing but a human being like us." That is, you have come to us in the same outward form known to us; we hold in our hands what we inherited from those who came before you, and you began with us with the very things they began with us. You are human beings, just as we are. In the Arabic language, the word for "human being" used here refers to the appearance of the skin, and to direct skin-to-skin contact. The point is that they were saying: what he has brought us is already with us, so he is like us and has no merit over us. They said: "None follows you but the lowliest of us, without consideration" — that is, the lowliest of us in knowledge.

The Speaker is a caller to the outward. "Noah said: 'My Lord, they have disobeyed me and followed one whose wealth and child have only increased him in loss.'" That is, their scholars of the outward, whose knowledge increased nothing and who called him to nothing but loss. Wealth, in the inner sense, is like knowledge; the child is like the responder, the one who answers his call and accepts what is preached to him, whether rightly or wrongly. "And they devised a great plot, and they said: 'Do not forsake your gods'" — meaning their scholars from whom they took, and the elders among their scholars whom they revered, who misled many of the creation. So Noah called them outwardly for a long time. Then Allah Almighty entrusted him with the verdict: "None of your people will believe except those who have already believed."

So understand what has been recited to you of the interpretation of what Allah has revealed to you. Know the truth of what it contains, so that you may worship Him by it, and grasp the inner revelation, of which the Almighty informed you in His Book — that none knows it but He and those firmly rooted in knowledge: His guardians, whose authority Allah has favored you with, and whose following has placed you in His party. He has promised you His reward, and through it has removed you from the party of Satan — whose punishment you dread. Carry out what Allah has prescribed; be sincere before His face. Perform what Allah has obligated upon you, outwardly and inwardly. Whoever of you has committed a sin: let him repent to Allah from it, and let him take refuge in the guardian of his own time, so that Allah may forgive him and absolve him of his sin. He will thereby become one of those whom Allah has promised — the repentant, who seek forgiveness for their sins — that he may belong to the party of your father Adam and to the party of the guardians of Allah from his progeny, which is the Party of Allah. Leave the party of Satan, who persisted in their sins as Satan persisted in his — they became satanic like him. Just as Satan despaired of his Lord, do not despair of your Lord or of His mercy upon you. The Almighty said: "Do not despair of the mercy of Allah; surely none despair of Allah's mercy except the disbelieving people." And as Allah cited from the words of His friend Abraham: "And who despairs of the mercy of his Lord but those who go astray?"

May Allah guide you to what He loves and is pleased with, protect you from violating His prohibitions and disobeying Him, and open for you the gates of obedience to His guardians. May Allah bless the Prophet and the pure Imams from his progeny, his household, and peace.
