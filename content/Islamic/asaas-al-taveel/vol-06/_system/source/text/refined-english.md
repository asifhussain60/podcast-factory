In the name of Allah, the Most Gracious, the Most Merciful.

When we intended to compose this book — which is the book *Asas al-Ta'wil* (The Basis of Interpretation) — and to expound in it what we had set forth in the book *Da'a'im al-Islam* (The Pillars of Islam) concerning the mention of monotheism, the knowledge of Islam, faith, prophethood, imamate, knowledge, scholars, halal and haram, and the issues and rulings with which the one who responds to the call of the guardian of the time should begin — so that he may be set right in his outward worship of Allah, manifesting his command, performing acts of obedience, and taking that from the direction of him — we mentioned that this is comparable to what should be done for the one born in the outward sense, who should be tended to first by setting right the outward form of his body: by being wrapped and adjusted, swaddled with bands, and by cutting his umbilical cord and severing him from what is not part of him — the placenta that was upon him and emerged with him — which is like the outward affiliations that the respondent assumes apart from his guardian.
