## Three-part focus
Source order; each movement lands once.
1. The crisis first — an agreement may fail before anyone lies. Three pillars, five conditions. Use decides what may be sold: the guard dog, the dog kept for nothing. Then the void sales — air, fish in the water, grass in a field, any garment you like: what cannot be separated from what is not sold stays indeterminate. Indeterminacy is not fraud; an honest sale is still void.
2. The pivot — lending divides by intention alone. The loan for God's sake is devotion: eighteen-fold for brotherhood, twenty-four-fold for kinship, no benefit from the principal. The worldly loan is lawful, every right on the lender's side a right to take less. Quote the command to give up what remains of riba and grant the debtor time; the treatise reads that as remission and concedes most jurists read it otherwise — the classical Ismaili reading, not a consensus. The pre-Islamic background and conquest are stakes, not a history lesson: two minutes at most, landing one claim only — guidance waited on capacity.
3. Trust replaces ownership — public bankruptcy; specification in hire; the tenant's word where the owner lacks proof; no rent for a house never handed over; the working partner shielded from a loss he did not cause. Close on the hundred dinars and two depositors: bring your companion, take what is yours. Say once that transmission here is damaged; repair nothing.
