# Show notes — EP02

**Title:** Sale, Debt, and the Trust

**Blurb:** 'The treatise turns to the transaction itself. Three teachings: that a sale stands on three pillars and five conditions, all of them defending against a single flaw — anything left indeterminate, from air to an uncaught fish to an unnamed garment; that all lending divides by intention alone, so that the loan made for God''s sake may yield its lender no benefit whatever while the worldly loan''s every rule points toward taking less; and that in hire, partnership, pre-emption, borrowing and safekeeping the governing category stops being ownership and becomes trust, with the ruling consistently favouring whoever holds the weaker position.
