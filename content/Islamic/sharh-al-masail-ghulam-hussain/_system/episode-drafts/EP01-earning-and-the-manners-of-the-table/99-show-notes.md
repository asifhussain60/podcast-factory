# Show notes — EP01

**Title:** Lawful Earning and the Table

**Blurb:** 'The treatise opens where a person''s day actually opens — at work, and then at the table. Three teachings: that earning a lawful living is ranked with striving in the way of God and measured by whether a family is spared from having to ask; that the manners of eating, feeding, hosting and being hosted form a single discipline aimed away from the self; and that the boundary of the lawful runs from what may be eaten, through how an animal must be killed and what may be hunted, to what may be worn and displayed.
