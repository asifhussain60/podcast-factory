# Show notes — EP05

**Title:** Maintenance, Dissolution, and Inheritance

**Blurb:** 'The treatise stops explaining how a marriage is made and starts explaining what it costs. Three teachings: what a household is owed, where the obligation survives a husband''s disappearance, runs at a median standard that is neither wasteful nor miserly, covers instruction as well as food, holds for a woman who does not pray, and follows a divorced woman through the nursing of her child; dissolution and the waiting period, where a judge investigates the complaint and may issue the divorce over an unwilling husband''s head, where the pronouncement must be spoken and not merely written, where a wife may buy her own release, and where the third divorce is closed against every staged workaround; and wealth that leaves a person''s hands — the gift that can be reclaimed, the endowment that cannot, the testament of the Father of Imams as the working model, the will that reaches only a third of an estate, and the fixed fractions that take the rest.
