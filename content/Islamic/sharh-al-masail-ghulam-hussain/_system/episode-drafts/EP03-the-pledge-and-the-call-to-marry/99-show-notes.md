# Show notes — EP03

**Title:** Pledge, Shared Wall, and Marriage

**Blurb:** 'The treatise finishes its law of property and turns to obligations between people. Three teachings: that the security in a pledge is the written record and not the goods, so that the pledge held in hand without a document is the form the law condemns while a deed goes on proving the loan after the object is destroyed; that what two neighbours owe each other is decided by harm rather than by title, from a judge compelling a man to build on his own land to a division of ground that must price the well and the pathway along with the field; and that abstaining from marriage is an injury a person does to himself — with the home relocated from the building to the couple, the license for more than one wife read as a permission its own second clause withdraws, and the choosing governed by character before beauty.
