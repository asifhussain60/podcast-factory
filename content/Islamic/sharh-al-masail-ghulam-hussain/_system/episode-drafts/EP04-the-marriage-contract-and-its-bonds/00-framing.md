# The Marriage Contract And Its Bonds

## Opening directive
A male scholar leads; a female seeker questions. Open with a warm welcome naming the treatise *Explaining the Issues* and its author, then preview in one sentence: how one swallow of milk can bar two people from ever marrying. Then say, word for word: "Marriage is not merely a contract in the legal sense; it is meant to keep the lineage alive through what is right and just on every side."

## Three-part focus
Source order; each lands once.
1. The crisis first — the choosing and the look. A bad match ruins a life, so the choice leaves the two it most affects and goes to the discerning of their age, matched near eighteen, suspended where disorder makes waiting dangerous. Marrying inside the community and schooling girls as first teachers is the author's own reckoning with his period in India: present it as his, do not adjudicate — two minutes at most, once. One look only; the knock at a sister's door is for her startle.
2. The pivot — the contract binds from outside. A marriage concluded through the wrong person is void, not defective. Say the spine sentence verbatim here. A stipulation cutting against the Book is a nullity. Four powers to the husband, each qualified by fairness; the discord verse is the boundary, its closing clause operative. The payment stays small and stays hers — a ring will serve, a judge sets it, no pillar of the contract. Name the asymmetry of the two rights-lists; leave it open.
3. The stakes — the forbidden degrees, which cannot be obeyed, only discovered. Two witnesses present and the marriage still impossible. Kinship by blood, by marriage, and by one swallow inside two years; intention declared irrelevant. Proof is three days or ten sucklings. Say once that transmission here is damaged; repair nothing.

## Tone constraints
Only these three images:
Analogy 1: the garment — each spouse a covering for the other.
Analogy 2: the garden of varied beauty.
Analogy 3: limbs of one body helping one another.
Short sentences, thinking out loud. The source gives no chapter-and-verse; supply none.

## Landing
Say the spine sentence verbatim a third time, then turn outward: these bonds hold whether or not anyone intended them. End on a question — which obligations you carry were never chosen? No tidy resolution, no recap.
