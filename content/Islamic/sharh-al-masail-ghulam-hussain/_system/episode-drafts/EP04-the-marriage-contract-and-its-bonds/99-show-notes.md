# Show notes — EP04

**Title:** The Marriage Contract and Its Bonds

**Blurb:** 'The treatise stops urging the reader to marry and starts explaining how a marriage is actually made. Three teachings: the choosing and the look, where the decision belongs to the discerning people of the household rather than to the couple, the ages are matched at around eighteen, the pool is narrowed to the community, and the glance a man is permitted is rationed to one; the contract and its terms, where a marriage concluded through the wrong person is void outright, a stipulation contrary to the Book is a nullity rather than a hard bargain, the husband''s four powers are each qualified by fairness in the same breath, and the marriage payment is kept small, kept the wife''s, and set by a judge rather than by the man paying it; and the forbidden degrees, which cannot be complied with but only discovered — kinship by blood, kinship manufactured by marriage, and kinship created by a single swallow of milk inside a child''s first two years.
