# The Dignity of Earning Your Bread

A reminder on the virtues of earning one's living opens with the word of God Most High:

«فانتشروا في الأرض وابتغوا من فضل الله»

*"Travel the earth and seek of God's bounty."*

And in the hadith:

«التاجر الصدوق يحشر يوم القيامة مع الصديقين والشهداء»

*"The truthful merchant will be raised on the Day of Resurrection with the truthful ones and the martyrs."*

The Messenger of God (ص) said:

«من طلب الرزق حلالاً وتعففاً عن المسئلة وسعياً على عياله وتعطفاً على جاره لقي الله ووجهه كالبدر»

*"Whoever seeks his sustenance lawfully, refraining from begging, striving for his household and showing kindness to his neighbour, will meet God with a face like the full moon."*

And he said:

«اتقوا الله وأجملوا في الطلب»

*"Be mindful of God, and be seemly in seeking."*

And he said:

«لأن يأخذ أحدكم حبله فيحتطب على ظهره خير من أن يأتي رجلاً أعطاه الله من فضله فيسأله أعطاه أو منعه»

*"It is better for one of you to take his rope and gather firewood on his back than to come to a man whom God has given of His bounty and beg from him, whether that man gives or withholds."*

It is related of him that he passed on an expedition by a strong young man driving his camels early in the morning, and those present said:

«لو كانت قوة هذا وجلده وسمنه في سبيل الله لكان أحسن»

*"O Messenger of God, if this man's strength and endurance and vigour were spent in the way of God, that would be better."*

The Messenger of God (ص) called the young man and asked him what he was labouring for. The young man said:

«لي زوجة وعيال فأنا أكتسب عليها وأنفق على عيالي وأغنيهم عن الناس وأقضي ديناً عليّ»

*"I have a wife and children, so I earn for her sake, and I spend upon my dependants, and I keep them from needing others, and I pay off a debt upon me."*

He was asked whether anything else moved him, and he said no. When he had turned away, the Messenger of God (ص) said:

«إن كان صادقاً فإن له أجراً مثل أجر الغازي وأجر الحاج وأجر المعتمر»

*"If he is truthful, he has a reward like the reward of the warrior, the reward of the pilgrim, and the reward of the one who performs the ʿumrah."*

And he (ص) said that when a man's circumstances grow straitened:

«فليخرج من بلده ويضرب في الأرض يبتغي من فضل الله ولا يغم نفسه وأهله»

*"Let him go out from his town and strike out in the earth seeking of God's bounty, and let him not bring grief upon himself and his family."*

And it is related of him (ص) that he said, concerning the day on which there is no shade but God's shade:

«يوم لا ظل إلا ظله رجل خرج ضارباً في الأرض يطلب من فضل الله ما يكف به نفسه ويعود به على عياله»

*"On the day when there is no shade but His shade: a man who went out striking the earth, seeking of God's bounty what would suffice him and what he would return with to his dependants."*

Likewise he (ص) said:

«إن الله يحب من العباد أن يكون سهل البيع وسهل الشراء وسهل القضاء وسهل الاقتضاء»

*"God loves of His servants one who is easy in selling, easy in buying, easy in settling what he owes, and easy in seeking what is owed to him."*

Al-Ḍaḥḥāk said as much in describing trade, commending it and counselling people to it, for in it lies much good and abundant blessing.

Once the Messenger of God (ص) passed by a people who in those days were called brokers, and he said to them:

«ألا إني لا أسميكم السماسرة ولكني أسميكم التجار»

*"I do not name you brokers; rather I name you merchants."*

And he said:

«التاجر الفاجر في النار»

*"The corrupt merchant is in the Fire."*

At this they shut their doors and held back from trading. The Messenger of God (ص) came out and asked what had befallen the people, and it was said to him that they had heard what he said the day before and had held back. He said:

«وأنا أقوله اليوم إلا من أخذ الحق وأعطاه»

*"And I say it today also, except for one who takes what is right and gives what is right."*

In this same sense a narration has come down that a man said to the Commander of the Believers (ع) that he wished to take up trade, and he said:

«أفقهت في دين الله»

*"Have you gained understanding in the religion of God?"*

The man answered that he had something of it, and he said:

«ويحك الفقه ثم التجارة»

*"Woe to you: understanding first, then trade."*

And he (ص) used to say:

«إني لأبغض الرجل يكون كسلان عن أمر دنياه لأنه إذا كان كسلاً في أمر دنياه فهو عن أمر آخرته أكسل»

*"I dislike the man who is idle in the affair of his world, for if he is idle in the affair of his world he is idler still in the affair of his hereafter."*

And among his sayings is this:

«ما من غدوة أحدكم في سبيل الله بأعظم من غدوته يطلب فيه لولده وعياله ما يصلحهم»

*"No morning setting out of one of you in the way of God is greater than his setting out to seek for his child and his dependants what will set them right."*

It is related that a man asked Imam Jaʿfar ibn Muḥammad (ع) to pray to God to grant him sustenance, and he answered:

«لا أدعو لك، اطلب كما أمرت»

*"I will not pray for you. Seek, as you have been commanded."*

Then he said:

«ينبغي للمسلم أن يلتمس الرزق حتى يصيبه حر الشمس»

*"It befits the Muslim to seek his sustenance until the heat of the sun reaches him."*

And he counselled one of his companions:

«لا تكن دواراً في الأسواق ولا تكن شراء دقائق الأشياء بنفسك فإنه لا ينبغي لك ولا للمرء المسلم ذي الدين والحسب أن يلي شراء دقائق الأشياء بنفسه»

*"Do not be one who circles about the markets, nor one who buys petty things himself; for it does not befit you, nor any Muslim of religion and standing, to see to the buying of petty things in person."*

And one day he looked at a man of the Companions carrying a herb in his hand, and he said:

«يكره للرجل السري أن يحمل الشيء الدني لئلا يجترئ عليه أحد»

*"It is disliked for a man of honour to carry a lowly thing, lest anyone grow bold against him."*
