# Slide Deck Framing, Sharh al-Masa'il, whole book

## Audience
Asif's children and the adults of the community: readers who know the audio episodes but have never seen how the treatise's rulings fit together as one structure.

## Core Principle
The slides carry the book's structures; the audio carries its argument. Every slide diagrams a relation, boundary, sequence or lineage that prose cannot hold in one glance.

## Visual Priorities
- The opening chapter's descending line: earning is devotion, eating is discipline, substance has a boundary. A three-level tree.
- The conditions on the object of a sale: air, the fish in the water, the fetus and the growing grass, inseparable from what surrounds them.
- The two forms of pledge: the object in the creditor's grip against the written deed that outlives it.
- The family that milk creates: one nursing woman, arrows running outward to foster-siblings, foster-aunts and foster-grandchildren.
- The order in which an estate is emptied: shroud, debt, the third, then the fractions by class of proximity.

## Prohibited Patterns
- No literal-text slides reproducing a verse or hadith as the whole content.
- No slides that restate the audio.
- No stock-photo imagery, and no scenes of markets, meals or weddings.
- No bullet lists presented as diagrams, and no slide whose only content is a section title.

## Steering Phrases
- "Each slide must add visual information not present in the audio."
- "Show the relationships between entities, not a list of them."
- "When two positions are contrasted, show them side by side as columns, attribute by attribute."
- "Do not produce 'general illustration' slides. Every slide must have a named diagram type."

## Visual Style
Black and white only on a white background: minimal yet elegant line art, conceptual diagrams in the manner of pen-and-ink scholarly illustration. Hierarchies as tree diagrams, contrast pairs as two-panel layouts, genealogy chains as flowing arrow diagrams, process flows as numbered-step illustrations. No colour fills, no photographs, no gradients, no dark backgrounds. Clean geometric shapes and lines only. Each slide must be a drawn diagram rather than a text-heavy bullet list.
