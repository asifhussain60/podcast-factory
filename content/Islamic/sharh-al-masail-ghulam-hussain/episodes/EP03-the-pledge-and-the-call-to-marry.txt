# The Pledge And The Call To Marry

Two unnamed hosts: a male scholar leads, a female seeker questions. Welcome naming the treatise *Explaining the Issues* and its author Sayyidna Ghulam Hussain, then one sentence: why a loan secured by paper is safer than one secured by goods. Target 50 to 60 minutes. Then say, word for word: "What distinguishes the documented arrangement from the undocumented one is nothing but an increase in formality."

## Three-part focus
Source order; each lands once.
1. The pledge — crisis first: a valuable handed over, nothing written; when the debt is called the loan can be denied. Failed answer: the jurists took one species of mortgage for the whole; that bare pledge is the forbidden form. Proof: destroy the pledged thing, the written loan stands undiminished. The text permits renting a mortgaged property once writing exists; the Imam's narration bars the holder from any benefit — name the clash, do not reconcile it.
2. The shared wall — the pivot: harm decides, not title. A judge compels a drainage channel on a man's own land; a door is barred for taking a line of sight; land divides by path, door, well and courtyard, not by area; the ceiling belongs to the man below. Say the spine sentence verbatim, then turn from walls to people.
3. The call to marry — the stakes: steelman the ascetic first, a scrupulous man fearing poverty, not a libertine. No monasticism here; the Prophet never met the standard such a man invented. Chastity from the root meaning fortress. The home is the couple, not the building. The verse permitting two, three or four is a permission its own equity condition withdraws. Choose character before beauty.

## Host dynamic
Host A leads; Host B challenges 3 times and concedes once.
The hosts prepared separately: plant one moment where one raises a passage the other had not led toward.
Friction: "I don't buy that yet"; "That sounds like wordplay." Say once: the manuscript is damaged, names no longer connect, the census figures are his own; repair nothing. Qualify doctrine as the classical Ismaili reading, never a bare "Muslims believe". Let each host finish; no interjections.

## Tone constraints
Only these three images:
Analogy 1: the deed still stating the loan after the pledged goods are gone.
Analogy 2: the fortress — chastity as fortification, not the elimination of desire.
Analogy 3: medicine taken by a healthy man who needs none.
Short sentences, thinking aloud. No editorialising.

## Landing
Leave the dispute over the pledged object open and unsettled. Then the spine sentence a third time. Close on a question: which of your obligations rests on memory, not a record? No recap, no preview.
