## Session 9: Power vs Strength
*He reveals the interpretations of the phrase there is no power nor strength except through Allah and ties it back to the king's dream in the story of Yousuf A.S.*

#  Recap
in the previous session the scholar taught us how everything operates in 3 dimensions. The outer, the inner and the innermost. Through the marriage of the outer and inner dimensions, the innermost dimension is born - just like the union of male and female, brings a child into the world.

He explained that people who operate at the level of outer dimension resemble beasts. Those who function at the level of inner dimension, operate at the level of true human beings. Those who get to the innermost dimensions, operate at the level of angels and become worthy of prophethood.

[BOOK-CONTENT]
He highlights the importance of effort through the example of a farming fertilizing the ground, sowing seeds and watering it to bring forth plants. But then he also clarifies, that despite all his efforts, it is Allah who allows the fruits of labor to come forth. He reveals to him that the Imam is the rope of Allah who can only be approached through his BAAB and HUJJA. To that the young man says

Of course that is weak and really doesn t resemble words of wisdom at all, and I do suppose that its essential meaning is something else, which must be more effective and of more substantial significance. [BOOK-CONTENT]
As for the word "⟪ar:حول⟫" (power), that refers to Allah s ⟪ar:ناطق⟫, or his Imam. His faithful and true representatives - the Master of the twelve chiefs (⟪ar:نقبا⟫)  just as ⟪ar:حول⟫ in its meaning as a year, is only completed by the twelve months. 

[BOOK-CONTENT]
- Now the imam is called ⟪ar:حول⟫ because he  transforms and conveys  (⟪ar:تحویل⟫) the Creator s speech with all the subtlety of its essential meaning, converting them into words of language for human beings, so that they can understand it. 
- Thus Allah s meanings of wisdom get veiled behind the words, such that the the words of wisdom become the body and form, while the Creator s speech becomes the true spirit of life and light of salvation. 
- Hence these words of wisdom are ennobled above all the other forms of speech, because of the specialty and nobility of the Creator s speech in its inner dimension.
And as for (the word)  ⟪ar:قوۃ⟫  (strength), that refers to the imam s ⟪ar:باب ⟫(gateway) because Allah has given Him strength to bear His mighty words. Therefore the ⟪ar:باب ⟫take that divine speech from the imam, in its condense, all-inclusive form (مجملا), and disseminates it to the ⟪ar:دعات⟫ (callers), in more detailed and particular form, according to their various levels. 

[BOOK-CONTENT]
So the ⟪ar:حجۃ ⟫is called  strength,  but he has no strength except through Allah. Thus the statement means  there is no imam and no ⟪ar:حجۃ ⟫except through Allah,  and their light and their support comes only from Allah.  And as for the band of men, who were possessors of great strength, they are the twelve ⟪ar:حجۃ ⟫of an Imam. 

[BOOK-CONTENT]
So there are two words. There is ⟪ar:حول⟫ (power) and ⟪ar:قوۃ⟫ (strength). ⟪ar:حول⟫ has to do with scheming, or strategy, which is a function of the spirit and the mind. ⟪ar:قوۃ⟫ has to do with physical capabilities. The mind comes up with a strategy, and the body uses physical strength to execute it. So ⟪ar:حول⟫ is spiritual in its nature, whereas ⟪ar:قوۃ⟫ is physical in its nature. Then he explains two different context. 

[BOOK-CONTENT]
In the first context, ⟪ar:ناطق ⟫is ⟪ar:حول ⟫and his gateway is ⟪ar:قوۃ⟫. Why? Because ⟪ar:ناطق ⟫is providing the strategy, he is converting the meanings of divine speech and transforming them into language. His ⟪ar:وصی ⟫is the one who has the strength to bear the weight of that speech without misinterpreting it; and he is the one whose job is to disseminate that message to us. That's why in this first context ⟪ar:حول ⟫represents ⟪ar:ناطق ⟫and his ⟪ar:وصی ⟫represents ⟪ar:قوۃ⟫.

[BOOK-CONTENT]
In a second context, the ⟪ar:امام ⟫is represented by ⟪ar:حول⟫, and his ⟪ar:حجۃ ⟫is represented by قوۃ for the same reasons. The ⟪ar:امام ⟫provides the strategy, whereas his ⟪ar:حجۃ ⟫carry them out and further simplify it for the ranks below them which are the ⟪ar:دعات ⟫- who then spread out in the world and deliver it to us.

But then he explains لاحول ولا قوۃ الا باللہ - That there is no strategy, nor is there strength except that Allah is the source of it all. So in the outer realm, when we recite this statement, we acknowledge and admit, that against Allah's decree, neither my mental strategy nor my physical strength will work, except that Allah himself enables it or allows it. 

In the first context of تاویل , where حول is the ناطق and قوۃ is the وصی - the phrase  لاحول ولا قوۃ الا باللہ , means, neither the ناطق nor the وصی has the capability to provide revelation, except that Allah enables or allows it.

In the second context of تاویل , where حول is the امام  and قوۃ is the حجۃ - the phrase  لاحول ولا قوۃ الا باللہ , means, neither the امام nor the حجۃ has the capability to disseminate this knowledge to the lower ranks, except that Allah enables or allows it.

## Session 10: Importance Of Acting With Knowledge
*the scholar explains how action subsists through knowledge and vice versa. He also explains why the ignorant assumed that the people of knowledge considered the world illicit for themselves.*

# Pairing of inner & outer dimensions

[BOOK-CONTENT]
 [184] The young man continued: But then how is it that Allah has prohibited this physical world, by saying:
⟪ar:"فَلَا تَغُرَّنَّكُمُ ٱلْحَيَوٰةُ ٱلدُّنْيَا⟫", "Then do not let the life of this physical world deceive you"?  

[185] Scholar said:

Allah did speak truly. He didn t say,  Don t let this "physical world" deceive you.  Instead he said:  Don't let the "**life**" of this physical world deceive you.  Because  life  has four meanings:

- Physical life in this inferior world
- and its ultimate outcome of passing away
- Spiritual life in the superior world
- and its ultimate outcome of lasting eternally

[BOOK-CONTENT]
Hence He said that you shouldn t act for this passing, transient life, but you should act for the everlasting eternal life. For that particular reason is the statement: ⟪ar:يَلَيْتَنِى قَدَّمْتُ لِحَيَاتِى⟫ On the day of judgment, man will say in regret, I wish I had prepared for my life - which is to say,  If only I had prepared for my eternal life, during this transient life.

## Session 11: Differences In Opinions

# Differences In Opinions

[201] The young man said "Praise be to Allah Who guided us to His religion, for we would not have been rightly guided without His generosity. So inform me about the origin of these creatures and their outer and inner make-up. Were they a single creation, all alike? Or were they different, judging from the many differences in their opinions and passions"

[202] The scholar began

- As for the creation of their bodies, they vary, as you can see from the difference in their languages and colors. That is a sign of Allah s power, in order for them to recognize each other. 
- Through these distinctions a child recognizes his parents, and parents recognizes their own children; a husband identifies his wife from other women, and the wife recognizes her husband from among other men  all because of Allah s justice toward His creatures.

- But as for the creation of their internal and external instruments and faculties, they are all created in an identical fashion. 
- The similarity of their outer aspects should indicate to you that they are similar in their inner faculties as well. 
- For did you ever see a scholar with three eyes, so that his extra knowledge was clearly visible? Or did you ever see an ignorant person with just one eye, so that his deficiency was outwardly visible? 
- If that were the case, people could say  the scholar sees more with his additional instrument of sight, but the ignorant sees less due to his deficiency 
- Did you ever really see an increase in any physical instruments of one person in comparison to another? 
- No! you will not see anything but equality in the creation of their physical instruments; and the essential reality of their genetic make-up will always be the same.

- Now since their external design is identical, so logically we can deduce that their inner instruments (of will, intellect and reasoning) are also identical based on the pattern of divine justice. 
- For Allah would not treat His creatures justly in their outer aspect, and unjustly in their inner aspects. 
- He has not placed two hearts within the scholar's chest, nor has he forbidden any means of understanding for the ignorant. 
- For the instrument of knowledge is one, and that internal design extends to everyone. 
- Now, it is by this instrument of knowledge (the intellect), Allah has established His argument against them, because he provided them with the necessary instruments which they failed to employ. 

[BOOK-CONTENT]
As Allah says in the Quran: They have hearts but they do not understand with it; they have eyes but they do not see with it; they have ears by they do not listen with it. Do you think that Allah, despite all His bounties and generosity, would impose a task upon them, which they are unable to perform or bear ? Of course not! Indeed he is more just than that!

 

[203] The young man said:  That was not my intention, and neither do I believe that! I was merely asking about the difference in people s passions and factions. Their laziness prevented them from worshiping him; and they were unable to return to the truth. They did not restrict themselves to what was licit (⟪ar:حلال⟫), and they did not avoid what was forbidden (⟪ar:حرام⟫).  
 

[205] The young man asked: But if their origin, and the faculty of their intelligence was the same, then where did the difference in their passions and factions come from? Because the essential reality of what they were seeking was the same, and every one of them, under the guidance of their intellect, was seeking what was good for him.

 

[206] 

 By my life!  responded the scholar.  The essential reality of what they were seeking was indeed one. But they all differed in actually reaching that unified goal. This is similar to people trying to shoot arrows at a particular target. Even though they were alike in their intention, and their instruments for shooting the arrows were the same, yet they all differed in actually hitting the target.

- This same pattern applies to the hearts of human beings which are in agreement with their minds; and their minds are in agreement about preserving and respecting their souls. 
- Yet when they were all called to the truth, they differed among themselves. 
- Some concluded, that responding to the call of divine messengers, and surrendering their will to them was part of respecting their souls and preserving it from corruption. While others among them believed, that denying the messengers and refusing to obey them was likewise part of respecting their souls and preserving it from the constraints of servitude. 

So the root in both cases was the same. They were both seeking ease for their bodies and proper respect for their souls. However one group accepted what the messengers had promised them of Allah s eternal recompense, even though it was insignificant and rare in this inferior world. While the other group chose the enjoyments of this lower world; because even though its pleasures were fleeting, it was readily available; it was easy to seek and was quick in its rewards. 

- Therefore the first group chose the blessings of the superior world with their hearts; while the latter sought the enjoyments of this lower world with their hearts. 
- The fact is, they were all concerned with the salvation of their souls; and they all chose the action that they considered to be the best for them. 
- They were all alike in their quest, yet differed in what they actually obtained. 
- All that is from Allah s justice, generosity and his bounty. Each one receives from him what he seeks. Those who desire the rewards of this world, they strive for it; and those who desire the rewards of the hereafter strive for it.
