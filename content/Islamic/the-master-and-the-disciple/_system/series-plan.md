## Audit-trail-only sections (no human review)

### Audience (orchestrator config default)
Thoughtful adult readers approaching the tenth-century Yamani dialogue *Kitab al-Alim wa al-Ghulam* — *The Book of the Master and the Disciple* by Sayyidina Ja'far ibn Mansur al-Yaman — for the first time, who know it as the timeless story of a seeker's quest for true knowledge and the process through which he gradually achieved it. This first episode walks the opening of that arc: a group of the truly faithful and a group of those who call people to the true religion approach one of their scholars with three thanks owed at once — for the call, for the knowledge, for the practice — and the Master answers with a doctrine that turns gratitude into deeds and names the three-stage spiritual path of trial, guidance, and piety. The episode then narrates the story of a Persian seeker whose thirst for knowledge led him through mirage after mirage until he met Allah at the point where the mirages dissolved into nothing, and how that meeting placed him under a debt of transmission that could be repaid only by setting out, in his turn, to find a disciple of his own. The episode lands at the far edge of al-jazira, with the scholar's journey still unfinished.

## NotebookLM input checklist (per-episode workflow)

1. **Open NotebookLM** → "+ New notebook" (or use existing per-book notebook)
2. **Sources panel** → "+ Add source" → "Upload from file" → select the file
   listed in the **Upload** column of the Episode list
3. **Customize panel** (top right) → "Customize" → paste the entire contents of
   the **Customize** file

   (deep_dive vs debate), focus areas, pronunciation block, tone constraints
5. **Generate** → ~10–15 min for NotebookLM to render audio
6. **Download** the MP3 → save at `audio/EP##-<slug>.mp3`
7. **Transcribe** via `python3 scripts/podcast/transcribe_episode.py`
   → drops at `transcripts/EP##-<slug>.transcript.txt`
8. **Audit** via `python3 scripts/podcast/audit_transcript.py <BOOK_DIR> EP##-<slug>`
   — catches Arabic pronunciation drift, missing phonetic cues, fabricated quotes
9. If audit flags issues: edit `pronunciation.md` overrides → re-paste customize
   prompt → re-generate
