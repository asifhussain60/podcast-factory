# Show notes — EP15

**Title:** Emptying the Cup — The Demolisher's Art

**Blurb:** Episode fifteen of *The Master and the Disciple* opens the central debate of Part Four. Abu Malik arrives at al-Bakhtari's door and is met by a threefold riddle that re-arranges every hierarchy he carries with him — the son is the father; the Lord is on earth with a servant; the mortal lives forever. Abu Malik mistakes it for news of death and recites the verse for the deceased. Inside the house, Salih receives him with perfect manners and prays for the ears of his heart to be opened. Then Salih asks which of three kinds of seeker he is — the one who knows, the one beginning to know, or the one who desires to know. Abu Malik admits he does not know the modes of seeking at all and places himself among the beginners. The source steps back and names the technique: kasrah, demolition; the rank in the spiritual hierarchy that performs it is the mukasir, the Demolisher. Then Salih draws the distinction that will govern everything that follows: ilm versus marifa, knowledge versus gnosis, khabar versus iyan, report versus vision. The alim knows heaven and hell exist; the arif burns to reach the one and avoid the other. Abu Malik confesses he found that intense neediness in his own soul. Salih cautions him against rushing the rebuilding; every seed requires its ground to be prepared, and every knowledge requires a heart first purified by proper argument.
