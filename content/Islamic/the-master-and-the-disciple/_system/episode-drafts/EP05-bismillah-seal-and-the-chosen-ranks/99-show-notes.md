# Show notes — EP05

**Title:** Bismillah Seal and the Chosen Ranks

**Blurb:** Episode five of *The Master and the Disciple* shows how the architecture of creation is sealed into the Bismillah — a cryptographic lock whose three names and letter-counts mirror the three primordial words and the sevens-and-twelves of cosmology, point for point. Then it names who fills the houses of prophethood: distinguished personalities and guiding figures by category and function — Natiq, Imam, Bab, Hujaj, Nuqaba, Du'at, ulama. And it mirrors the hierarchy in the visible sky: the Imam is the sun, his Bab the moon, the Du'at the stars. A finer distinction is preserved that listeners often miss: the twelve regional Hujaj are proofs, but the Bab is the one Gateway — the Hujja al-Imam, the single moon to the one sun.
