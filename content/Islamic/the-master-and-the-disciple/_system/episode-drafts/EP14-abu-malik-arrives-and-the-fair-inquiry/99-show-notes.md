# Show notes — EP14

**Title:** Abu Malik Arrives and the Fair Inquiry

**Blurb:** Episode fourteen of *The Master and the Disciple* brings the champion of the opposing community on stage. The former co-religionists of al-Bakhtari, who are afraid of losing his favors and afraid of being on the wrong side of his conversion, send for the Yemeni scholar Abu Malik — Kab al-Ahbar of his age, the glory of the yearned. He responds with striking fairness. He refuses to condemn the two converts without hearing them and refuses to assume their guidance without testing it; both, he says, would be injustice. He warns his own companions against the satisfaction of past communities who said no messenger would come after theirs and did not see the way of proceeding beyond what they had attained. He gives the conversion three possible readings — decorative addition; real increase of knowledge as the believer's lost camel; or true prophetic guidance — under each of which the right move is to go and meet. He names the inversion of signs by which falsehood wears conformity and truth wears trial, and defines the sound heart as the one that does not deny what enters it. He insists on humility before the truth, not after. And he refuses delay: today, not in three days, not in two. The greatest scholar of the opposing camp has just set the terms of his own debate by the standard of justice.
