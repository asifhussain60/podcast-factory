# Show notes — EP10

**Title:** The Union of Inner and Outer

**Blurb:** Episode ten of *The Master and the Disciple* answers the question every reader of esoteric doctrine must ask. If the inner is everything, what becomes of the outer — of prayer, of fasting, of the daily obligations of shari'a? The scholar answers in three movements. First the union of inner and outer as a single living religion: the seeker who keeps only the inner is fruit rotting before ripening; the one who keeps only the outer is a body without spirit, numbered among the dead; only their union is faith, and for the believer this world becomes licit. Second the pale faces of the true scholars: not a refusal of the world but the body's record of vigilant attention to the next, distracted from luxury by reflection and engrossed in the sweetness of Allah's remembrance. And third the doctrine of divine justice in acceptance: the instrument of knowledge is identical in every chest; the difference is in what each seeker does with it. The episode closes with mercy under necessity, told through the thirsting woman in the time of the second caliph and the Father of Imams' ruling that the verse of necessity left her free of sin.
