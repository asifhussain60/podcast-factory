# Show notes — EP17

**Title:** Who Is Allah? — Beyond Names and Adjectives

**Blurb:** Episode seventeen of *The Master and the Disciple* opens the climax of the debate. Salih refuses to let the conversation about Allah's justice and unity happen at the level of the school's standard creed. Names cannot cook food. The four letters of the divine name are signs, not containers. The analogical proof from manufactured things secretly forges a likeness. The standard formula 'nothing like His image' even smuggles an image back under the table. The eye that cannot bear the sun cannot weigh the Creator. By the end Abu Malik says it himself: his words have left him; intellects are bewildered; imaginations cannot reach Him. That confession is the doorway into the next episode's syllogism.
