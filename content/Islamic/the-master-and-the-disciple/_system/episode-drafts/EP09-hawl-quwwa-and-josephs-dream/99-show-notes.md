# Show notes — EP09

**Title:** Hawl and Quwwa, and the Dream of Joseph

**Blurb:** Episode nine of *The Master and the Disciple* takes apart the line every Muslim recites in difficulty: there is no power and no strength except through Allah. The disciple offers the common readings — hawl as "a year," quwwa as "mules" — and the scholar walks them into absurdity before opening the inner meaning. Hawl is the Imam who converts the Creator's speech into the words of wisdom that human beings can hear; quwwa is the Bab who bears the divine speech in its condensed, all-inclusive form and distributes it to the Du'at in the form each can carry. The formula is the confession of an entire chain that is itself dependent at every link. Then the same architecture is read out of two of the great dreams of Surat Yusuf — Joseph's eleven stars and the sun and the moon (chapter 12, verse 4) and the king's seven fat cows and seven green ears — and the cosmology of Part Two is heard inside Part Three's formulae and dreams.
