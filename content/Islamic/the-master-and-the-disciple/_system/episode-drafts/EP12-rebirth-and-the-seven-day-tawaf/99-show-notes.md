# Show notes — EP12

**Title:** Rebirth of the Disciple and the Seven-Day Tawaf

**Blurb:** Episode twelve of *The Master and the Disciple* opens Part Four. The disciple and the scholar reach the Sheikh. The Sheikh begins with a sustained praise of Allah — justice as the balance between command and prohibition, the honor of intellect as its use in the search, the duties of truth as obligatory, the signs to be shown in the cosmos and in the self. Then he turns to the disciple and refuses every name offered until the disciple has no answer left. A name, he says, can only be given after seven days, in honor of the new-born infant; the universe itself received its life from the seventh letter, and the embryo passes through seven stages before another creation is brought forth. On the seventh day, after ablution and a fresh garment, the disciple is drawn close, taught the realities pens cannot write, and brought into a tawaf the source reads as the planets orbiting their sun — the Imam as the centre, gravity as love, marifa as the orbit in which the planet's life is held.
