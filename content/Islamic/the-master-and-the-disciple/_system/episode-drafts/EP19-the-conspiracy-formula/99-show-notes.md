# Show notes — EP19

**Title:** The Conspiracy Formula

**Blurb:** Episode nineteen of *The Master and the Disciple* delivers the book's most distinctive piece of social analysis — the conspiracy formula. Three actors in every period — a rebellious devil, a willing oppressor, and a hypocritical religious scholar — collaborate to sever the outward traces of prophecy from its living continuation so that the traces can be exploited as a commodity. The slogan that holds the severance in place is 'no prophet after our prophet,' propagated until inquiry itself becomes redundant. Salih traces the pattern across the Zoroastrian, Torah, and Gospel communities, names this community as the latest case, and turns the consensus argument back: by that very logic, the earlier communities hold the greater claim. The Creator's freedom to abrogate and confirm voids every closure argument anyway.
