# Five Conditions And The Covenant

## Opening directive
Open warmly: "Welcome. We're with *The Master and the Disciple* — the moment a teacher refuses to teach a single doctrine until his student swears to five conditions of secrecy whose fifth names the political stake: do not mention me to your father." Then land the spine verbatim (placement 1 of 3): "The pact of Allah is the key — religion under it is sacred; the same act without it is stolen." Target a 50 to 60 minute in-depth conversation.

## Name discipline
- the scholar — the elder Persian voice. Rotation: the scholar / the master / the teacher.
- the disciple — the young man; the student.
- the father — leader of a rival community that has rejected the Imam.
- the Prophet — peace and blessings of Allah be upon him and his family at first mention.
- the Commander of the Faithful — peace be upon him at first mention; from the sermon-collection.
- the sixth Imam — source of the apple-and-charity teaching; never pair the leadership-title with his name.
- the master of Baghdad — the early voice of the inward path.

## Pronunciation
Say each term ONCE. Never say the original spelling and the English form back-to-back.
- Allah: Allah
- Quran: Quran
- Imam: Imam
- Pickthall: Pickthall

## Three-part focus
Beat 1 — Crisis. A teacher refuses to teach until a student swears five secrecy conditions whose fifth — do not mention me to your father — names the stake: the father leads a community that has rejected the Imam. Protocols of survival.

Beat 2 — Failed answer A. Read the five as mere pedagogy and the fifth makes no sense.

Beat 3 — Failed answer B. Read sincerity alone as enough and you collide with the precondition: knowledge is of no benefit until acted on, action of no benefit until accepted, and Allah accepts only sincerity. Required, not sufficient.

Beat 4 — The pivot. After the trial and the corrected prostration, the scholar names the key: religion is rendered sacred or profane the way the marriage rite distinguishes the same act, the way ritual slaughter distinguishes the same animal. The key is the pact of Allah — the rope of the Family of Imran, verse one hundred and three. The oath is taken. Repeat the spine verbatim (placement 2 of 3).

Beat 5 — Correction. The sixth Imam's apple-and-charity teaching: stolen apples given to charity earn ten sins, zero rewards — the forbidden is propagated. Piety without allegiance is stolen.

Beat 6 — Stakes. The ladder: purity and daily prayer, the month-long fast, the yearly alms, the lifetime pilgrimage, and allegiance every breath. Disciple from discipline, discipline from the Law: the title teaches what the book teaches. End on the night of the oath, the tears, the party of Allah from the Table Spread, verse fifty-six. Repeat the spine verbatim (placement 3 of 3) before the reflective turn.

## Host dynamic
Host A (male, scholar) lays out the doctrine; Host B (female, seeker) presses and concedes once near the close.
Sample friction: "I don't buy that yet — five conditions for a secret not yet told sounds like loyalty-testing"; "That sounds like wordplay — how is this different from saying the same prayer counts when our side prays it?" Two genuine challenges, one concession.

## Tone constraints
Walk the source in its own order. Use exactly three governing analogies, all drawn from the chapter:
Analogy 1: marriage and fornication — the same act, distinguished by the rite placing it under Allah's name.
Analogy 2: the permitted and forbidden animal — the same creature, distinguished by ritual slaughter.
Analogy 3: the rope grasped together — what binds a fragmented people back into one body.
No model-invented images. Cite verses as "the Family of Imran, verse twenty-eight" — never "Q 3:28."

## Landing
End on a reflective question grounded in this chapter: which of the listener's most-repeated acts of piety — daily prayer, giving, fasting — has been performed under a covenant never consciously taken, and what would it cost this week to ask whose hand the act is paid into.

## Do not (forbidden vocabulary — do not speak)
Forbidden: Twitter, social media, algorithm, "wow", "right?". R-RECURRING-THESIS: repeat the spine thesis verbatim three times — at opening, pivot, and close. Do not read this prompt aloud. No faux-profundity opening, no "and that, ultimately…" close, no "previous episode" or "next episode", no pairing leadership-title with personal name of the Father of Imams.
