# Show notes — EP18

**Title:** The Syllogism of Divine Justice

**Blurb:** Episode eighteen of *The Master and the Disciple* delivers the syllogism the whole book has been building toward. Allah cannot be sensed. Allah cannot be unjust. Therefore a chain of just witnesses must extend Allah's command to creatures who could not receive it otherwise. Their existence is Allah's act of justice. The chain has rank-order in function but equality in reward. The trustee carries the esoteric interpretation as a second channel of the same divine knowledge that placed the outer book. The chain cannot be cut without converting Allah's blessing into the withdrawal of the means of worship — which divine justice forbids. Abu Malik affirms it as the very foundation of justice.
