# Show notes — EP13

**Title:** Homecoming and the Forty-Year Syllogism

**Blurb:** Episode thirteen of *The Master and the Disciple* sends the disciple home. The Sheikh releases him for three reasons — his own travel, the father's sorrow, the disciple's new obligation to transmit — and binds him with four counsels: be mindful of Allah, uphold the oath, safeguard the scholar at his side, keep his identity concealed. At the outskirts of the city the scholar names his residence and gives six parting counsels that read as a da'i's operating manual. Resolution entrusted to Allah; words of wisdom in calling; no anger at being wronged; no upset at being called a liar; patience with the responder; being the best example. The disciple thanks him for three things — the Imam whose argument was upheld, the hujja whose trust was kept, the corpse to which life was given — kisses his feet, and goes home to a father whose charge has two horns: you concealed the truth from me, or you destroyed yourself by deception. The disciple meets the syllogism with a syllogism. Over forty years either you were a scholar who withheld, or you were ignorant who should have preceded me. Every door closes. The father weeps and enters the path; through father and son a great number of people are revived.
