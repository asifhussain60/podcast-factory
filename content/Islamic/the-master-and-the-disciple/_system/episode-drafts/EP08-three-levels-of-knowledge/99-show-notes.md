# Show notes — EP08

**Title:** Three Levels of Knowledge — Angels, Humans, and Beasts

**Blurb:** Episode eight of *The Master and the Disciple* opens Part Three by turning the architecture inside out. The disciple asks whether the inner has an inner, and the scholar answers yes — there is an outer, an inner, and an innermost of that inner aspect, and the triple structure is everywhere in Allah's book and in the very grammar of how anything is understood. The verse of the garment carries it. The grammar of name, attribute, and meaning carries it. The shell, white, and yolk of an egg carries it. And then the scholar maps the three layers of knowledge onto three levels of creatures: the beasts, who know only the outer; the humans, who know the inner; and the angels, who know the inner of the inner — at whose rung sit the prophets, Allah's vicegerents on earth. The disciple asks the question that follows: can the highest rung be climbed from below? The scholar answers with the farmer who cannot make the plant grow and with the rope of Allah held tightly through the Imam — reached through his proofs and his gateway. The disciple completes the move himself: there is no power and no strength except through Allah. The next episode will open that formula and show what has been waiting inside it.
