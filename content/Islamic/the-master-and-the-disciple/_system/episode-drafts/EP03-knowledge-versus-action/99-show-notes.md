# Show notes — EP03

**Title:** Knowledge versus Action

**Blurb:** Episode three of *The Master and the Disciple* turns from cosmology to the spiritual economy of the seeker. The Master lays down the three-level doctrine of *ẓāhir / bāṭin / bāṭin al-bāṭin* — the outer aspect, the inner, and the innermost — and maps the three onto the three orders of creature: whoever knows only the outer operates at the level of beasts; whoever knows the inner is one of the true descendants of Adam; and whoever knows the innermost resembles the angels. From there he refuses the literal glosses of *lā ḥawla wa-lā quwwata illā billāh* and unfolds the formula as a naming of the chain: *ḥawl* is the *nāṭiq* or the Imam, who *transforms* the Creator's speech into the body and form of the words of wisdom; *quwwa* is the *bāb* or the *ḥujja*, to whom strength has been given to bear and disseminate those words. *There is no Imam and no ḥujja except through Allah.* Yūsuf's dream is read as the prophetic cabinet of *Isḥāq*; the king's dream as the seven heavenly intermediaries and the seven *nuṭaqāʾ*. The chapter's strongest practical verdict follows: the one who keeps the inner without the outer is *fāsiq*; the one who keeps the outer without the inner is *kāfir*; only the *muʾmin* who joins both walks the path. Inner faculties are equal by divine justice — *no scholar has three eyes* — and the differences among people come not from instrument but from the corruption of the root. Necessity is met with mercy in the case of the thirsting woman whom Maulā Ali (peace be upon him) declared free of sin. The disciple presents his own five-part purification of possessions — *zakāt*, *kaffāra*, *shukr*, the brothers' portion given by his own hand, and one offered to the Master — and the Master, moved to tears, refuses his own portion and reassigns the others as trusts pointing forward to the Imam. He then departs to his own *wālid al-akbar*, the Sheikh, and when he returns finds the disciple's body weak and his complexion pale — the marks of *ʿishq*, the lover's grief at the beloved's absence. The Master tells him: *start preparing for your journey, for I am taking you to the doorstep of your ultimate goal* — *and there is no power but through Allah*.
