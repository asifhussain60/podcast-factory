# Knowledge versus Action

## Opening directive

Host A (male, scholar) opens with a brief warm welcome (1–2 sentences), names *The Master and the Disciple* (an early Ismaili dialogue), and previews ONE teaching: three levels of existence mapped onto three creatures and the hidden meaning of the formula about power and strength. Land spine VERBATIM (1/3): **There is no Imam and no proof except through Allah.** Forbidden openers: "today's episode", "let's dive in", "Have you ever wondered…".

## Name discipline

- Disciple → **the young man** → the aspirant / the seeker / the young disciple (rotate after oath).
- Author → **the author of this dialogue**. Book → **this dialogue**. Never speak Arabic titles.
- Commander of the Faithful → **the Commander of the Faithful, peace be upon him** (first only). Never pair title with personal name.
- The Prophet → **"peace and blessings of Allah be upon him and his family"** at first mention.
- Both unnamed prophets (thrown-into-well; prophet of patience) → *(upon him peace)* at first mention.
- Qarun → **the rebel against Moses**. Sheikh → **the elder teacher**. Imam of the time → **the Imam of the time**.

## Host dynamic

Host A (scholar) carries the three-level doctrine; Host B (seeker) presses back at least three times; Host A concedes once (the outer-only = beast verdict sounds impossibly harsh) before answering from necessity-mercy. Forbidden first words for Host B: "Exactly", "Yeah", "Right", "Of course". Sample friction: "That sounds like wordplay — beast or angel doesn't change what a person is doing"; "How is this different from a simple gradient of more or less devotion?" At least two genuine challenges before the resolution.

## Tone constraints

Analogy 1: three creatures (beast / human / angel) as a vertical ladder of integration.
Analogy 2: the formula as a name-pair — transformer of divine speech + bearer to the community.
Analogy 3: fruit peeled before ripening (outer fails); seed buried unplanted (inner fails).

No invented analogies. Quranic citations in English chapter-name only.

## Three-part focus

**Beat 1 — The three-level verdict.** Stakes: without knowing one's level, a person cannot understand how their actions are judged. Three-creature verdict: outer only = beasts; inner = Adam's descendants; innermost = resembling angels. Quote the "indeed, mankind is but" verse verbatim, English after. Angelic level requires sincere certainty and pure attentive heart — not a ladder.

**Beat 2 — The hidden formula.** Young man offers two literal glosses (suckling verse 2:233; "those of strength" 28:76); failed answer: author refuses both. Pivot: the formula names two office-holders — the transformer of divine speech, the bearer to the community. Land spine VERBATIM (2/3): **There is no Imam and no proof except through Allah.** Dream of the prophet-in-the-well: sun = prophet of patience, moon = his gateway, eleven stars = eleven chieftains. King's dream (12:43): seven intermediaries; seven speaker-prophets.

**Beat 3 — Practical verdict.** Crisis: Host B — how can a sincere follower of the outer law be condemned as an inner-denier? Host A: inner faculties are equal; differences come from corruption of the root. Outer-only = the beast (fruit peeled before ripening). Inner-only = the denier (farmer buries the seed). Only the union = the full believer. Mercy: the thirsting woman freed by the Commander. Five-portion division briefly named. Land spine VERBATIM (3/3): **There is no Imam and no proof except through Allah.**
