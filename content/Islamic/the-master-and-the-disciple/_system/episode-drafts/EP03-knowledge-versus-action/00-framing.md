# Knowledge versus Action

Host A (male) = scholar/teacher. Host B (female) = seeker/questioner. Roles do not rotate.

## Opening directive

Host A opens with a brief warm welcome (1–2 sentences), names the book — *The Master and the Disciple*, an early Ismaili dialogue — and previews ONE teaching: three levels of knowledge mapped onto three creatures, and the hidden meaning of the formula every Muslim recites about power and strength. Land the spine VERBATIM (1/3): **There is no Imam and no proof except through Allah.**

Forbidden openers: "deep dive", "today's episode", "let's dive in", "journey into", "In a world where…", "Have you ever wondered…".

## Three-part focus

**Beat 1 — The three-level verdict.** From the chapter on the heights (Quran 7:26 — clothing, modesty, garment of piety) and the moon-rising syllogism (name, attribute, meaning): outer-only = beasts; inner = Adam's true descendants; innermost = resembling angels. Quote *innama al-nas rajulan* verbatim. The angelic level cannot be climbed by ladder — only sincere certainty and pure attentive heart.

**Beat 2 — The hidden formula.** Young man offers two literal glosses (suckling-year 2:233; "those of strength" 28:76); the author refuses both. *Hawl* names the speaker-prophet or Imam who transforms the Creator's pure speech into wisdom; *quwwa* names the gateway and proof who bears those words. Land spine VERBATIM (2/3): **There is no Imam and no proof except through Allah.** Yusuf's dream (12:4): sun = Ishaq, moon = Yusuf's gateway, eleven stars = eleven chieftains. King's dream (12:43): seven heavenly intermediaries; seven speaker-prophets.

**Beat 3 — Practical verdict.** Inner-without-outer = *fasiq* (fruit peeled before ripening). Outer-without-inner = *kafir* (the farmer who buries the seed). Only the union = *mu'min*. Mercy under necessity: the thirsting woman freed by the Commander of the Faithful. Five-portion division — *zakat*, *kaffara*, *shukr*, the brothers' portion, and one offered to the author (refused, reassigned to the Imam of the time). The young man's pale complexion — the marks of *ishq*. Land spine VERBATIM (3/3): **There is no Imam and no proof except through Allah.**

Host B pushes back genuinely at least three times. Host A concedes once that the *fasiq*/*kafir* verdicts sound nearly impossible, then answers from necessity-mercy. Quranic citations: English chapter-name only. Verbatim Arabic followed by plain English.
