# Show notes — EP04

**Title:** Originator and the Three Primordial Words

**Blurb:** Episode four of *The Master and the Disciple* begins the doctrinal core of the book with the architecture of creation laid down with mathematical precision. The scholar starts with what kind of First Allah is — Originator without template, first of every first, inheritor of all existence. He names the three primordial words that fall from the first Light: will, command, speech — densifying as they descend. From those three words come seven principles: air the unpaired first and king, then water, darkness, and physical light, then their denser pairs of smoke, mud, and fire. From smoke come the seven heavens; from mud the seven earths. And the letters give the numerical signature: seven in the command kun fa-yakun, twelve in the three primordial words — stamped into the cosmos as seven heavens, seven seas, seven days; twelve zodiacs, twelve regions, twelve hours of day and twelve of night. The signature of cosmology turns out to be the letter-count of its own genesis.
