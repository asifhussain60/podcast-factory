# Show notes — EP11

**Title:** Purifying Possessions and Parting from the Master

**Blurb:** Episode eleven of *The Master and the Disciple* closes the dialogue between scholar and disciple. The disciple's question is the most concrete he has asked: what do I do with what I own? The scholar gives a rule — lawful acquisition; zakat from the whole; khums from the finest portion you love most; the rest for family with the surplus to the needy. The disciple surprises him by having already divided his possessions into five portions with pedagogical weights — zakat; atonement kept as remembrance of sins; gratitude held as a trust until maturity; brothers' portion given by his own hand; and a portion to support the scholar. The scholar weeps and refuses his own portion, redirecting each of the others and turning the purification portion into a trust for the Imam — named here as the rightful judge whose hands hold the keys of the gardens and whose palms reveal the light of Sinai. Then the scholar travels to consult the Sheikh, his own spiritual father, who instructs him to hurry back because the young man is to become a gateway of loving kindness in his own time. And when the scholar returns he finds the disciple pale and weak with longing — ishq, the source names it, extreme love at separation from the beloved. The scholar announces the journey, and the disciple sighs so deeply with joy and awe that his spirit almost leaves his body.
