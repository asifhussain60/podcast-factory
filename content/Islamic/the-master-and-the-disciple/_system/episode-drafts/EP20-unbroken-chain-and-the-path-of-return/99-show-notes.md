# Show notes — EP20

**Title:** Unbroken Chain and the Path of Return

**Blurb:** The final episode of *The Master and the Disciple*. Salih refutes the very notion of an interval between prophets by naming the chain link by link, and rereads the Quranic word fatra as a period of fear in which the call is made in secret rather than as an absence of the witness from the earth. The concealment of the friends of Allah in the listener's own time is explained by the way of Moses fleeing into Midian. The three categories of people — relentless murderer, rageful enemy, sympathetic abandoner — locate the moral weight not on the enemies but on those who feel for the witnesses but abandon them. The scholars who legitimate the oppressors are named as worse than the murderers themselves. The path of return is brief and exact: help the friends of Allah and they will reveal themselves; abandon their enemies and they will not be victorious. Abu Malik repents. The source seals the book by declaring its arc a replicable model from individual conversion to community-building. The closing praise-and-blessings seal closes the work.
