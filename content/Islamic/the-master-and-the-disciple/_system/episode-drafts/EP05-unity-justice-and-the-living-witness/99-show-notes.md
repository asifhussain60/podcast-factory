# Show notes — EP05

**Title:** Unity, Justice, and the Living Witness

**Blurb:** The climax of *The Master and the Disciple*. The cup has been emptied. The description of religion — *the rope extending from the heavens to the earth* — has been given. And the question is on the table: *what about the reality of what you have described?* Episode five is the answer. The Master distinguishes true unity from the names by which it is spoken — *it is like saying fire is hot; the words are true, but you cannot cook food with the words* — and then delivers the syllogism of divine justice in four steps: Allah cannot be perceived directly; Allah is not unjust and would not punish for what He gave no means to receive; therefore a just intermediary must carry the command; therefore a living proof must exist in every age. *This is the very foundation of justice.* The hierarchy of witnesses — the speaking prophet who carries the unseen in the revealed book, the trustee who carries the unseen in its esoteric interpretation — is then shown to be continuous by its own structure: Allah's justice is unified, and He would not send a witness to one age and deny one to another. The conspiracy formula follows: a rebellious devil, a willing oppressor, and a hypocritical religious scholar — the triad that has appeared in every community, converting the love of the prophet into the very instrument of cutting off his chain. The formula is traced through Zoroastrian, Torah, Gospel, and present community. The consensus argument is turned back and then dissolved. The *fatra* is refuted by the simple recitation of the figures who fill it. The concealment of the witnesses is located in the conditions the community has created, not in their absence. The scholars who serve oppressors are named as bearing greater weight than the killers they enable. The path of return is offered in two exact clauses. The opposing scholar repents. And the work closes by naming itself a model — *not a fictitious story, but the confirmation of what Allah has commanded* — a replicable *daʿwa* arc from one seeker to a great many of Allah's servants.
