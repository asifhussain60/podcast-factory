# Show notes — EP06

**Title:** Zahir, Batin, and the Seven Natiqs

**Blurb:** Episode six of *The Master and the Disciple* names the seven speaking prophets — Adam, Noah, Abraham, Moses, Jesus, Muhammad, Mahdi — and shows how the calendar of cosmology (sevens, twelves, thirties) is the calendar of spiritual transmission. Then it gives the deepest framing of the whole architecture: zahir and batin are not two textual layers but the body and the spirit of religion, each subsisting only through the other. Religion is the soul of the revealed paths; the ordinances of shari'a are the body. Each lives through the other; neither lives without the other. Finally the scholar answers the disciple's question about the world by walking the visible cosmos as a catalogue of symbols: the heaven points to the Natiq; the zodiac to the Nuqaba; the earth to the Bab and Wasi; the islands to the Hujaj; the rivers and springs to the Du'at. And the salty sea encodes outward knowledge while the sweet spring encodes inner knowledge — both are water, both are life, but only one feeds the field.
