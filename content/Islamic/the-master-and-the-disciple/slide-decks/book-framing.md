# Slide Deck Framing, The Master and the Disciple

## Audience
Asif's children and thoughtful young adults who heard the audio and want the book's architecture in diagrams.

## Core Principle
The audio carries the argument. The deck renders the book's STRUCTURES visible: hierarchies, contrast pairs, genealogies, process flows, signature-counts. A slide that could be a single audio sentence is not a slide.

## Visual Priorities
- Densification ladder of the Originator: will, command, speech, then the seven principles with Air unpaired at the top.
- Numerical signature: seven letters of the command, twelve letters of the three primordial words, mapped onto heavens, zodiac, regions, hours.
- Chosen ranks mirrored in the sky: Natiq, Imam, Bab, Du'at against sun, moon, stars.
- Forty-year syllogism as a two-horn dilemma closing every escape route.
- Conspiracy formula as a triangle of three actors plus a roll-call timeline.

## Prohibited Patterns
- No slides that bullet-point the audio.
- No literal-text slides whose only content is a chapter title.
- No stock-photo imagery.
- No bullet lists masquerading as diagrams.
- No restatement of verbatim Quran quotes as slide bodies.

## Steering Phrases
- Each slide must add visual information not present in audio.
- For the central tension, use a contrast pair with both columns named.
- For the lineage of witnesses, use a directed genealogy chain. Show edges, not just nodes.
- Show relationships between entities, not lists of them.
- Do not produce a slide titled "Overview" or "Key Takeaways."
