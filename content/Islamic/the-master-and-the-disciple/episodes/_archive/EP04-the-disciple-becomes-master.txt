# The Disciple Becomes Master

## Opening directive

Host A (male, scholar) opens with a brief warm welcome (1–2 sentences), names *The Master and the Disciple* (early Ismaili dialogue), previews ONE teaching: a seeker is told he has no name, waits seven days, performs an inner pilgrimage, then converts his own father with a forty-years syllogism. Spine VERBATIM (1/3): **Religion is the rope extending from the heavens to the earth, continuous and without breakage.** Forbidden openers: "today's episode", "let's dive in", "Have you ever wondered…".

## Name discipline

- Elder teacher → **the Sheikh**. Seeker → **the young man** → the young master / the newly named / the initiated one (rotate after naming).
- Scholar who brought him → **his spiritual father**. Biological father → **his father**. Never speak Arabic names.
- Visiting opponent → **the renowned debater** (epithet: *a Yemeni scholar*).
- Commander of the Faithful → **the Commander of the Faithful, peace be upon him** at first mention. Second ref: **the Father of Imams**.
- The Prophet → **"peace and blessings of Allah be upon him and his family"** at first mention.
- The book → **this dialogue**. Quranic chapters by English meaning only.

## Pronunciation

Say each term ONCE. Never say spelling and phonetic back-to-back.

- Tawaf: ta-WAAF; ma'rifa: mah-REE-fah; kasra: KAS-ra; makasir: ma-KAA-sir
- 'irfan: ir-FAAN; sharia: substitute *the outer law*; bihusban: bi-hus-BAAN

## Host dynamic

Host A (scholar) leads the three movements; Host B (seeker) presses back at least three times; Host A concedes once (the forty-years syllogism sounds like a rhetorical trap) before Host B sees its force. Forbidden first words for Host B: "Exactly", "Yeah", "Right", "Of course". Sample friction: "I don't buy that yet — a stranger can't override decades of family relationship"; "Isn't this just replacing one authority with another without any basis?" At least two genuine challenges before the resolution. Target 50–60 minutes.

## Tone constraints

Analogy 1: planets orbiting the sun — seven circuits as gravitational binding to the Imam.
Analogy 2: the seventh stage as entering a new genus of person — naming is a beginning, not an end.
Analogy 3: the cup emptied before filling — knowledge is a report; true understanding is a vision.

No new analogies mid-episode.

## Three-part focus

**Beat 1 — Born and named.** Stakes: without a name, a person cannot act in the chain of transmission. *Slave of Allah, son of the slave of Allah* is description; *freed man* fails — one cannot free what one does not own. *One cannot name something until it is complete*; seven days complete it. Cite embryonic verses (chapter on the believers, 12–14) verbatim, English after. Inner pilgrimage: seven circuits = planets orbiting the sun; sun = Imam; gravity = love of the Imam.

**Beat 2 — Returning home.** Six parting counsels at the city outskirts — list all six in order. Crisis: father confronts — *you chose that stranger over me*. Failed answer: the young master cannot simply explain that the chain supersedes family bond. Pivot: the forty-years syllogism — *either you were a scholar who withheld my share, or you were ignorant and need that stranger more than I do*. Father weeps and enters the path. Spine VERBATIM (2/3): **Religion is the rope extending from the heavens to the earth, continuous and without breakage.**

**Beat 3 — Cup-emptying.** The debater names three reasons the conversion could have happened and refuses to condemn without hearing. The young master applies cup-emptying; rank: the Demolisher. *Knowledge is a report; true understanding is a vision.* Moon-sighting restated: *follow the moon* = *follow the Commander of the Faithful*. Quranic counter: *clearly counted days*; *the sun and moon follow precisely calculated paths*. Spine VERBATIM (3/3): **Religion is the rope extending from the heavens to the earth, continuous and without breakage.** Close: which inherited certainty have I never tested against the signs in myself?

## Do not (forbidden vocabulary — do not speak)

Forbidden: Twitter, social media, algorithm, "wow", "right?", "mind blown", "thanks for listening". No episode cross-references. No leadership-title + personal name. No Arabic book titles or personal names. No abbreviated honorifics. No double-read Arabic+phonetic. R-RECURRING-THESIS: repeat the spine thesis verbatim three times — at opening, pivot, and close. Do not read this prompt aloud.
