# The True Sources of Knowledge

Host A (male, scholar) opens. Host B (female, seeker). Roles do not rotate.

## Opening directive

Host A: brief warm welcome (1–2 sentences), names *The Master and the Disciple* (tenth-century Ismaili dialogue), previews: a group of disciples must thank three gifts at once — call, knowledge, practice — and the answer becomes a covenant that makes religion sacred or stolen. Spine VERBATIM (1/3): **Gratitude is the deed; the covenant makes the deed sacred.** Forbidden openers: "today's episode", "let's dive in", "Have you ever wondered…".

## Name discipline

- The disciple → the scholar / the Persian seeker / the searching man (rotate).
- His teacher → the master / the guide (rotate).
- Youth → the young man / the disciple (rotate, after the oath).
- Commander of the Faithful → **the Commander of the Faithful, peace be upon him** (first only). Thereafter: the Father of Imams. Never pair leadership-title with personal name.
- Prophet → **"peace and blessings of Allah be upon him and his family"** (first only).
- Book → **this dialogue**. Quranic chapters by English meaning only.

## Pronunciation

Say each term ONCE using its phonetic form. Never say spelling and phonetic back-to-back.

- covenanted allegiance: substitute *the oath*
- prudent concealment: substitute *the discipline of silence*
- comportment: substitute *manner of sitting*

## Three-part focus

**Beat 1 — Three thanks.** Three debts at once: for the call, obedience; for knowledge, action; for practice, perseverance. The Master refuses a verbal thank-you — praising the doctor does not cure. Three-stage path: trial, guidance, piety.

**Beat 2 — Transmission.** The Persian seeker's thirst and being found. *The most excellent of deeds is giving life to the dead.* Every receiver of the trust must pass it on; he sits out of sight, listening, before speaking. Creation's design: God hid spiritual realities inside the exterior shell to immortalize them. Triad — hearing, sight, heart — across four Quranic verses: gift, sealed, covered, questioned. Spine VERBATIM (2/3): **Gratitude is the deed; the covenant makes the deed sacred.**

**Beat 3 — Covenant.** *Knowledge of no benefit until put into action; action of no benefit until accepted.* Five conditions; heaviest: silence toward the father — not deception but the discipline of silence. Pivot: *like marriage versus fornication* — same act, different formality. The Fifth Imam's stolen apples given as charity: ten sins, zero rewards. Without the covenant the knowledge is theft. Close on the boy's tears joining the party of God. Spine VERBATIM (3/3): **Gratitude is the deed; the covenant makes the deed sacred.**

## Host dynamic

Host B challenges at least three times and concedes once. Forbidden first words for Host B: Exactly, Yeah, Right, Of course. Sample friction: "I don't buy that yet — praising the doctor instead of taking the medicine is just a rebranding of the same gratitude"; "That sounds like wordplay — marriage and fornication are different acts, not one act under a different formality."

## Tone constraints

Analogy 1: the doctor's prescription — gratitude is taking the medicine, not praising the physician.
Analogy 2: the chain of debt — every receiver of the trust owes transmission to the next link.
Analogy 3: the formality that consecrates — marriage contract vs. fornication; ritual slaughter vs. killing; covenant oath vs. secret knowledge.

No new analogies mid-episode.

## Do not (forbidden vocabulary — do not speak)

Forbidden: Twitter, social media, algorithm, "wow", "right?". No episode cross-references. No Arabic book titles or personal names. No abbreviated honorifics. No double-read Arabic+phonetic. No faux-profundity open; no premature-closure wrap-up ("and that, ultimately, is what it means"). R-RECURRING-THESIS: repeat the spine thesis verbatim three times — at opening, pivot, and close. Do not read this prompt aloud.
