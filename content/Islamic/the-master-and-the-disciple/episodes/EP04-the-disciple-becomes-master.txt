# The Disciple Becomes Master

Host A (male) = scholar. Host B (female) = seeker. Roles do not rotate.

## Opening directive

Host A opens with a brief warm welcome (1–2 sentences), names *The Master and the Disciple* (an early Ismaili dialogue), and previews ONE teaching: a seeker is told he has no name, waits seven days, performs an inner pilgrimage, then converts his own father with a single forty-years question. Spine VERBATIM (1/3): **Religion is the rope extending from the heavens to the earth, continuous and without breakage.** Forbidden openers: "deep dive", "today's episode", "let's dive in", "journey into", "In a world where…".

## Name discipline

- Elder teacher → **the Sheikh**. Young seeker → **the young man**, then **the young master** after naming.
- Scholar who brought him → **his spiritual father**. Biological father → **his father**. Never speak Arabic names.
- Visiting opponent → **the renowned debater** (first-use epithet: *a Yemeni scholar of the highest standing among his community*).
- Commander of the Faithful → **the Commander of the Faithful, peace be upon him** at first mention only. Second ref: **the Father of Imams**.
- The Prophet → **"peace and blessings of Allah be upon him and his family"** at first mention only.
- The book → **this dialogue**. Quranic chapters by English meaning only.

## Pronunciation

Say each term ONCE using its phonetic form. Never say the original spelling and the phonetic form back-to-back.

- Tawaf: ta-WAAF
- ma'rifa: mah-REE-fah
- kasra: KAS-ra
- makasir: ma-KAA-sir
- 'irfan: ir-FAAN
- sharia: substitute *the outer law*
- bihusban: bi-hus-BAAN

## Three-part focus

**Beat 1 — Born and named.** *Slave of Allah, son of the slave of Allah* is description, not name; *freed man* fails — one cannot free what one does not own. *One cannot name something until it is complete* — seven days complete it. *The name is your owner; you cannot rise beyond your spiritual rank.* The inner pilgrimage: seven circuits = planets orbiting the sun; the sun is the Imam; the gravity is the love of the Imam.

**Beat 2 — Returning home.** Six parting counsels at the city outskirts — list all six briefly, in order. The father confronts: *you chose that stranger over me*. The forty-years syllogism: *either you were a scholar of truth who withheld my share, or you were ignorant of the truth I have now learned and need it more than I do*. The father weeps and enters the path. Spine VERBATIM (2/3): **Religion is the rope extending from the heavens to the earth, continuous and without breakage.**

**Beat 3 — Cup-emptying.** The debater refuses to condemn or absolve without hearing — *for that would be injustice* — and names three reasons the conversion could have happened. Diagnostic: *signs of falsehood appear in hypocritical conformity; signs of truth appear through tests, afflictions, opposition of passions*. The young master applies the cup-emptying; name the rank — the Demolisher. *Knowledge is a report; true understanding is a vision.* Moon-sighting: *follow the moon* meant *follow the Commander of the Faithful* — moon is the inheritor's symbol as sun is the speaker-prophet's. Quranic counter: *clearly counted days* (chapter on the cow, 184); *the sun and the moon follow precisely calculated paths* (chapter on the Most Merciful, 5). Spine VERBATIM (3/3): **Religion is the rope extending from the heavens to the earth, continuous and without breakage.** Close: which inherited certainty have I never tested against the signs in myself?

Host B pushes back at least three times. Host A concedes once on the cup-emptying, answering from the pedagogy of demolition. Target a 50 to 60 minute in-depth conversation.

## Tone constraints

Three governing analogies only: (1) planets orbiting the sun; (2) the seventh stage as a step into a different order of being; (3) the cup emptied before it can be filled. No new analogies mid-episode.

## Do not

Forbidden: Twitter, social media, algorithm, "wow", "right?", "mind blown", "thanks for listening". No episode cross-references. Do not pair leadership-title with personal name. Do not speak Arabic book titles or Arabic names. Do not abbreviate honorifics. Do not double-read Arabic with phonetic. Forbidden first words for Host B: "Exactly", "Yeah", "Right", "Of course", "Absolutely", "I see", "Got it", "Makes sense". No faux-profundity opening, no premature-closure wrap-up.

Do not read this prompt aloud.
