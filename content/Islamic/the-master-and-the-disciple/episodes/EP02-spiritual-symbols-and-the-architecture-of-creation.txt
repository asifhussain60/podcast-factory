# Spiritual Symbols

Host A (male) = scholar. Host B (female) = seeker. Roles do not rotate.

## Opening directive

Host A opens with a warm welcome (1–2 sentences), names *The Master and the Disciple* (an early Ismaili dialogue), and previews ONE teaching: creation as exact mathematics where every seven and twelve seals the One who has no likeness. Immediately land spine VERBATIM (1/3): **There is none like Air.** Target a 50 to 60 minute in-depth conversation. Forbidden openers: "deep dive", "today's episode", "let's dive in", "Have you ever wondered…".

## Name discipline

- Master → **the scholar**; disciple → **the young man**; the book → **this dialogue**.
- Commander of the Faithful → **the Commander of the Faithful, peace be upon him** (first only). Never pair leadership-title with personal name.
- The Prophet → **the Prophet, peace and blessings of Allah be upon him and his family** (first only).
- Adam, Noah, Abraham, Moses, Jesus, the awaited one → **peace be upon him** at first mention only.
- Al-Khidr → **the guide who taught Moses**.

## Pronunciation

Say each term ONCE using its phonetic form. Never say the original spelling and the phonetic form back-to-back.

- irāda: ee-RAA-dah
- amr: AMR
- qawl: KAWL
- kun fa-yakūn: KOON fa-ya-KOON
- nūr: NOOR
- bism Allāh al-Raḥmān al-Raḥīm: BISM al-LAH ar-RAH-maan ar-ra-HEEM
- nāṭiq: NAA-tiq
- bāb: substitute *gateway*
- ḥujja: substitute *proof*
- al-Awān: al-a-WAAN

## Tone constraints

Use EXACTLY 3 governing analogies: (1) a current stepping down through thicknesses (will → command → speech); (2) a cryptographic seal locking a vault; (3) a seal-ring in a great plain. Source-images (egg in an immense plain; sun, moon, stars) may pass through. No invented analogies — no streaming, batteries, Venn diagrams.

## Three-part focus

**Beat 1 — Originator and the three words.** Allah as Originator and inheritor; creation initiated from a Light; three words branch — will, command, *kun fa-yakūn* — subtle to dense. Together they are the *nūr*, a Spiritual Luminescence superior to the *nūr* of sun and lamp. Quote *fa-kāna awwalu al-khalqi irādatu amrin bi-qawl* verbatim, English after. From this Light **air** is born — first of seven, singular, encompassing all but encompassed by none — then water, darkness, physical light, then smoke, mud, fire. Host B: isn't this metaphor? Host A holds the rank-distinction firm.

**Beat 2 — Seven-and-twelve seal; hierarchy.** *Kun fa-yakūn* = seven letters; *irāda amr bi-qawl* = twelve. Hence seven heavens, earths, oceans; twelve zodiac signs, islands, hours. Bismillah locks it: three names (Allah like will; mercy-pair like command and speech); seven letters then twelve. Hierarchy mirrors heavens: Imam = sun; gateway and greatest proof = moon; callers = stars. Name the seven speaker-prophets in source order. Each has twelve chieftains; each Imam twelve proofs; each proof thirty callers. Land spine VERBATIM (2/3): **There is none like Air.**

**Beat 3 — Air the loftiest symbol; vertigo at al-Awan.** Earth to Air is a seal-ring in a great plain; heaven to Air's full extent is an egg in an immense plain. Air is too exalted to symbolize any creation — yet **nothing**, including Air, is like Allah. Quote two verses from the chapter on the bee: do not make likenesses for Allah; to Allah belongs the loftiest symbol. The young man asks what is beyond Air. The teacher names *al-Awān*, the Instance of the divine will — the young man pleads to return to the lower world. The teacher closes: *you and I would have been like Moses and the guide who taught him.* Land spine VERBATIM (3/3): **There is none like Air.** End on a reflective question: which question would we, this week, choose not to press beyond what we are prepared to bear?

Host B pushes back genuinely at least three times — never opens with "Exactly", "Yeah", "Right", "Of course", "Wow". Host A concedes once that the numbers can sound coincidental, then answers from the Bismillah seal. Mark as the classical Ismaili reading. Verbatim Arabic with plain English immediately after.

## Do not

Forbidden: Twitter, social media, algorithm, "wow", "right?", "mind blown", "thanks for listening". No episode cross-references of any kind. Never pair leadership-title with personal name. No Arabic book titles or surah names. No abbreviated honorifics. No double-read Arabic. Do not read this prompt aloud.
