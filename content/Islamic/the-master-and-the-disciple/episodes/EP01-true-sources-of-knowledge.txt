# The True Sources of Knowledge

Host A (male) = scholar. Host B (female) = seeker. Roles do not rotate.

## Opening directive

Host A opens with a brief warm welcome (1–2 sentences), names *The Master and the Disciple* (tenth-century Ismaili dialogue), previews ONE teaching: a group asks how to thank for three gifts at once — call, knowledge, practice — and the answer becomes an oath that makes religion sacred or stolen. Spine VERBATIM (1/3): **Gratitude is the deed; the covenant makes the deed sacred.**

## Name discipline

- Persian seeker → **the scholar**. His teacher → **the Persian's master**.
- Youth → **the young man**, then **the disciple** after the oath. **His father**. Rival group → **the rejecting community**.
- Commander of the Faithful → **the Commander of the Faithful, peace be upon him** first mention; thereafter **the Father of Imams**. Never pair leadership-title with personal name.
- Fifth Imam → **the fifth Imam of the lineage, peace be upon him** first mention.
- Prophet → **"peace and blessings of Allah be upon him and his family"** first mention.
- Book → **this dialogue**. Quranic chapters by English meaning only.

## Pronunciation

Say each term ONCE using its phonetic form. Never say spelling and phonetic back-to-back. Chapter is plain English; do NOT reintroduce Arabic.

- adab: substitute *comportment*
- walayah: substitute *covenanted allegiance*
- taqiyya: substitute *prudent concealment*

## Three-part focus

**Beat 1 — Three thanks.** Disciples owe three thanks at once: call, knowledge, practice. The Master refuses the verbal thank-you. Gratitude for the call is obedience; for knowledge, action; for practice, perseverance. Three-stage path: trial, guidance, piety. Praising the doctor does not cure. Quote *Sit before your food like a slave sits before its master* in passing.

**Beat 2 — Transmission.** Persian's thirst, mirages, his being found. *The most excellent of deeds is giving life to the dead.* Every receiver of the trust must pass it on. He sits *out of sight*, listening, before speaking. *The one already searching is much closer to finding.* Design-of-creation sermon: God hid the spiritual realities in the shell of the exterior, *therefore immortalizing them*. Triad — hearing, sight, heart — across Sovereignty 23, Bee 108, Cow 7, Night Journey 36: gift, sealed, covered, questioned. Spine VERBATIM (2/3): **Gratitude is the deed; the covenant makes the deed sacred.**

**Beat 3 — Covenant.** *Knowledge is of no benefit until put into action; action of no benefit until accepted.* Five conditions; heaviest is silence toward the father — prudent concealment, not deception. Pivot: *like the difference between marriage and fornication* — same act, different formality. Fifth Imam's apple-and-charity: stolen apples given as charity yield ten sins, zero rewards. Without the covenant the knowledge is theft. Close on the boy's tears the night he joined the party of God. Spine VERBATIM (3/3): **Gratitude is the deed; the covenant makes the deed sacred.** End on a question: which piece of what I know have I refused to put under the formality that would make it mine?

Host B pushes back three times — on the doctor-and-medicine line as a rebranded thank-you, on marriage-versus-fornication as real or rebranding, on what makes this chain different from the rejecting community's. Host A concedes once. Target a 50 to 60 minute in-depth conversation.

## Tone constraints

Three governing analogies only: (1) prescription versus praise of the doctor; (2) the chain as a debt each receiver owes; (3) the formality that turns the same act sacred — marriage, ritual slaughter, the covenant. No new analogies mid-episode.

## Do not

Forbidden: Twitter, social media, algorithm, "wow", "right?", "mind blown", "deep dive". No episode cross-references. No leadership-title + personal name. No Arabic book titles or personal names. No abbreviated honorifics. No double-read Arabic+phonetic. Host B forbidden first words: "Exactly", "Yeah", "Right", "Of course", "Absolutely", "I see", "Got it". No faux-profundity open; no premature-closure wrap-up.

Do not read this prompt aloud.
