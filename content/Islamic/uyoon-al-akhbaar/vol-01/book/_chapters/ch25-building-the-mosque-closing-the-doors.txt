# Building the Mosque, Closing the Doors

**Building the Mosque, Closing the Doors**

The Messenger of Allah then migrated to Medina. He entered it on a Monday, twelve nights having passed of the month of Rabi' al-Awwal. On the day he entered Medina he was fifty-three years of age, complete, having resided in Mecca for thirteen years after Allah, mighty and majestic, sent him with prophethood on a Monday, when forty years had passed from his birth.

'Ali ibn Abi Talib (ع) remained in Mecca three days after him, until he had discharged what he had been commanded, and then followed him to Medina, where he lodged with him at the house of Kulthum ibn Hidm in Quba'. After the arrival of 'Ali (ع), the Messenger of Allah stayed in Quba' no more than a night or two. His stay there fell on Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday and Thursday. Then he prayed the Friday prayer among Banu Salim ibn 'Awf, and that was the first Friday prayer he prayed in Medina.

**The tribes intercepted him, each desiring that he alight among them**

Men gathered about him and said, "O Messenger of Allah, stay with us, among numbers and equipment." They meant to hold his she-camel back from travelling, and he said:

> «دَعُوها فَإِنَّها مَأْمُورة»
>
> "Leave her, for she is under command."

So they released her and she went on. Whenever he passed through the quarters of the Ansar, they asked him to stay among them and sought to halt his camel, and he would say, "Leave her, for she is under command."

She continued until she came to the dwellings of Banu Malik ibn al-Najjar, where she knelt at the door of what is now his mosque. In those days the place was a drying floor belonging to two orphan boys of Banu al-Najjar, who were under the guardianship of Mu'adh ibn 'Afra'. When she knelt, the Prophet of Allah did not dismount from her back. She sprang up and went a short distance, the Messenger of Allah leaving her rein slack and not turning her with it. Then she looked behind her, returned to the place where she had first knelt, knelt there, and settled her breast upon the ground. At that he dismounted from her and gave orders concerning his saddle, and it was carried into the house of Khalid ibn Zayd, that is, Abu Ayyub. There the Messenger of Allah lodged until the building of the mosque was completed. He asked to whom the drying floor belonged, then bought it from the two orphans and satisfied them for it.

**The building of the mosque of Medina**

The Messenger of Allah ordered that the mosque be built in that place, and he worked in it with his own hand. He urged the people to the work, and the Muhajirun and the Ansar laboured in it. One of the Muslims said:

> لَئِنْ قَعَدْنا وَالنَّبِيُّ يَعْمَلُ
> لَذاكَ مِنَّا العَمَلُ الْمُضَلَّلُ
>
> If we sit while the Prophet works,
> that on our part is work gone astray.

And the Muslims chanted as they built it, saying:

> لا عَيْشَ إلاَّ عَيْشُ الآخِرَةْ
> اللَّهُمَّ ارْحَمِ الأَنْصَارَ والْمُهَاجِرَةْ
>
> There is no life but the life of the Hereafter.
> O Allah, have mercy on the Ansar and the Muhajirun.

These words are not rajaz verse; they are speech. And the Messenger of Allah would say:

> «لاَ عَيْشَ إِلاَّ عَيْشُ الْآخِرَةِ، اللَّهُمَّ ارْحَمْ الْمُهَاجِرِيْنَ وَالأَنْصَارِ»
>
> "There is no life but the life of the Hereafter. O Allah, have mercy on the Muhajirun and the Ansar."

**The chant of 'Ali (ع) at the building of the mosque**

'Ali ibn Abi Talib (ع) and his companions were the hardest working of the people, and he began to chant:

> لا يَسْتَوِي مَنْ يَعْمُرُ الْمَسَاجِدا
> يَدْأَبُ فِيْها قَائِماً وَقَاعِدًا
> كَمَنْ يَمُرُّ عانِدا مُعانِدا
> عَنِ الغُبارِ لا يَزَالُ حَائِداً
>
> He who builds up the mosques is not equal,
> toiling in them standing and sitting,
> to one who passes by turning aside, obstinate,
> ever swerving away from the dust.

'Ammar ibn Yasir (رض) took up the lines and chanted them, working with great exertion. 'Uthman ibn 'Affan was standing to one side, and he understood that the verses alluded to him, so he said to 'Ammar, with a stick in his hand, "يا ابن سُمَيَّة؛ إِنِّي سأعرض هذه العصا لأنفك" ("O son of Sumayyah, I shall lay this stick across your nose!").

The Messenger of Allah heard 'Uthman's words and became greatly angry, and said:

> «مالهم ولعَمَّار، يَدْعوهم إلى الجنَّة، ويدعونه إلى النَّار»
>
> "What is the matter with them and with 'Ammar? He calls them to the Garden, and they call him to the Fire."

And he said:

> «إِنَّ عمَّاراً جِلْدَةُ ما بين عيني وأنفي، فمتى تُنْكَأُ الْجِلْدَةُ يدْمَى الوجه. وهذه مطاياك فارحل إذا شئت»
>
> "'Ammar is the skin between my eyes and my nose; whenever that skin is wounded, the face bleeds. And these are your mounts, so depart if you wish."

The work in the mosque of the Messenger of Allah continued until it was finished. He then ordered that his own dwellings be built beside it, and his companions built their houses around his mosque, each one of them making a door opening into the mosque. They remained so for years, there being in the house of every man among the companions of the Prophet a door into the mosque. Then Allah revealed to His Prophet that he should command them to close their doors and to leave the door of 'Ali (ع).

**The most excellent of the virtues of the Commander of the Faithful (ع)**

It has been narrated that a group of people asked the Commander of the Faithful (ع), saying, "O Commander of the Faithful, tell us of the most excellent of your virtues." He (ع) said: "The most excellent of my virtues is that in which I had no part of my own making." It was said, "And what is that, O Commander of the Faithful?"

He said: "When the Messenger of Allah came to Medina, he ordered the mosque to be built, and there was not a man among his companions who did not pierce a door into the mosque. Then Jibra'il (ع) came to him and commanded him to order them to close their doors and to leave my door. So the Messenger of Allah sent Mu'adh ibn Jabal to them. He came to Abu Bakr and ordered him to close his door, and he said, 'Hearing and obedience,' and closed his door. Then he sent to 'Umar and ordered him to close his door, and 'Umar came to the Messenger of Allah and said, 'O Messenger of Allah, leave me the measure of an opening by which I may look upon you with my eye.' The Messenger of Allah refused him, and he closed his door. Then he sent him to Talhah, al-Zubayr, 'Uthman, 'Abd al-Rahman, Sa'd, Hamzah and al-'Abbas, ordering them to close their doors, and they heard and obeyed. Hamzah and al-'Abbas said, 'He commands us to close our doors and leaves the door of 'Ali.'

"That reached the Messenger of Allah, and he said:

> «قَدْ بلغني ما قُلْتُم في سدّ الأَبْواب، والله ما أنا فعلت ذلك ولكن الله فعله، وإِنَّ الله أَوْحَى إلى مُوسى أنْ يَتَّخِذَ بَيْتاً طهراً لا يُجْنِبُ فيه إلاَّ هو وهارون وابناه»
>
> 'What you have said about the closing of the doors has reached me. By Allah, I did not do this; rather Allah did it. Allah revealed to Musa that he should take a house of purity in which none should become junub save he, and Harun, and Harun's two sons.'"

Abu Ja'far said: he meant that none other than they should have marital relations in it.

> «وإِنَّ الله أَوْحَى إليّ أن أَتَّخذَ هذا البيت طهراً، لا ينكح فيه إلاّ أنا وعليّ والحسن والحسين. والله ما أنا أمرت بسدّ أبوابكم، ولا فتحت باب عليّ، بل الله أمرني به»
>
> "And Allah has revealed to me that I should take this house as one of purity, in which none may take a wife save I, and 'Ali, and al-Hasan, and al-Husayn. By Allah, I did not command the closing of your doors, nor did I open the door of 'Ali; rather Allah commanded me with it."

The narration is longer than this, and it has been abridged here. It has come with its chains by various paths.
