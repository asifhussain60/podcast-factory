Abu Sufyan and those with him turned back toward Mecca. When word reached the Prophet, peace and blessings of Allah be upon him, he said: "By the One in whose hand my breath rests, I had intended to pursue them; had they pressed forward, they would have been like yesterday."

The Messenger of Allah, peace and blessings of Allah be upon him, encountered Mu'awiya ibn al-Mughirah ibn Abi al-'As ibn Umayya ibn 'Abd Shams — who was the grandfather of 'Abd al-Malik ibn Marwan through his father, and 'A'isha bint Mu'awiya — and said: "O Zubayr, strike his neck." So al-Zubayr struck his neck. It was said that 'Uthman had wanted to grant him protection, whereupon the Messenger of Allah, peace and blessings of Allah be upon him, ordered two of his companions — Zayd ibn Haritha and 'Ammar ibn Yasir — to strike his neck. And Allah knows best.

The Messenger of Allah, peace and blessings of Allah be upon him, returned to Medina. Seventy men were martyred on that Sunday, four of them from the Muhajireen, among them Hamza ibn 'Abd al-Muttalib, peace be upon him — "the Master of the Martyrs."

Twenty-two men were killed among the polytheists:

The Messenger of Allah, peace and blessings of Allah be upon him, slew 'Amr ibn 'Abdullah ibn Umayra ibn Wahb ibn Hudhaqah ibn Jumah Sabra.

He also killed — may Allah bless him and his family — Ubayy ibn Khalaf from Banu Jumah.

'Ali ibn Abi Talib, peace be upon him, killed: Talha ibn Abi Talha and Abu Sa'id ibn Abi Talha — though it was also said that Sa'd ibn Abi Waqqas killed the latter — both of whom were from Banu 'Abd al-Dar. As for the right account: an Abyssinian slave was killed by 'Ali ibn Abi Talib, peace be upon him.

Abu Ummah ibn Abi Hudhayfah ibn al-Mughirah, from Banu Makhzum, was killed by 'Ali ibn Abi Talib, peace be upon him.

'Abdullah ibn Humayd ibn Zuhayr, from Banu Asad ibn 'Abd al-'Uzza ibn Qusay, was killed by 'Ali ibn Abi Talib, peace be upon him.

Abu al-Hakam ibn al-Akhnas, from Banu Zuhra ibn Kilab, was killed by 'Ali ibn Abi Talib, peace be upon him.

'Uthman ibn Abi Talha, from Banu 'Abd al-Dar, was killed by Hamza, peace be upon him. Artah ibn 'Abd Sharhabil ibn Hashim ibn 'Abd Manaf ibn 'Abd al-Dar was likewise killed by Hamza ibn 'Abd al-Muttalib, peace be upon him.

Saba' ibn 'Abd al-'Uzza, an ally of theirs from Khuza'a, was killed by Hamza, peace be upon him.

Musafi' and al-Jallas, the sons of Talha ibn 'Abd al-Dar, were killed by 'Asim ibn Thabit ibn Abi al-Aqlah.

Among the rest of the slain polytheists:

Al-Harith ibn Talha was killed by a man from the Ansar.

Kilab ibn Talha, from Banu 'Abd al-Dar, was killed by 'Abd al-Rahman ibn 'Awf.

Abu Zayd ibn 'Umayr ibn Hashim was killed by Qarman — and Qarman was an ally of the Banu al-Najjar among the Ansar.

Al-Qasit ibn Shurayh, from Banu 'Abd al-Dar, was killed by Abu Dajanah al-Ansari.

Hisham ibn Abi Umayya ibn al-Mughirah was killed by Qarman.

Khalid ibn al-A'lam was killed by Abu Dajanah al-Ansari.

'Ubaydah ibn Jabir was killed by 'Abdullah ibn Mas'ud.

Shayba ibn Malik ibn al-Mu'dabh was killed by Qarman.

It is related in the biography of Ibn Hisham: There was a man among us whose identity was uncertain; it was said his name was Qarman. The Messenger of Allah, peace and blessings of Allah be upon him, said: "Qarman is one of the people of the Fire." On that Sunday, Qarman fought the polytheists with extraordinary ferocity, killing eight or seven of them single-handedly. He was struck by many wounds, and was carried to the house of Banu Zafar. Some of the Muslims came to him and said: "By Allah, you have worn yourself out today, O Qarman — rejoice!" He said: "What should I rejoice about? I fought only for the sake of my people, and for no other reason." When his wounds grew severe, he drew an arrow from his quiver and killed himself with it.
