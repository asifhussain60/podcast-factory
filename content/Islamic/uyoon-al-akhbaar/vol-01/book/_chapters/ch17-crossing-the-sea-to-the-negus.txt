# Crossing the Sea to the Negus

The Quraysh saw that the harder they pressed those who had followed him, the firmer those people grew. So every clan among them turned upon those of its own who had accepted Islam, harming them, torturing them, and beating them. The Muslims complained of this to the Messenger of Allah, and he gave them permission to migrate to the land of Abyssinia.

A company of them set out for that migration, and among them was Jaʿfar ibn Abi Talib (ع). Those who emigrated from among the Muslims to the land of Abyssinia numbered eighty-three men, apart from the women and children who went with them.

Abu Talib sent a letter with his son Jaʿfar al-Tayyar (ع) to al-Najashi, the king of Abyssinia, informing him of the affair of the Prophet, of the rising of the Banu ʿAbd Shams and those of Quraysh who allied themselves with them against him, and of what had befallen his followers. He wrote that they had sought sanctuary with him and taken refuge in his kingdom, and he asked the king to guard them and to keep them safe. Among what he wrote to him were these verses:

> تَعْلَمْ أَبَيْتَ اللَّعْنَ أَنَّ مُحَمَّداً
> وَزِيْرٌ لِمُوسَى وَالْمَسِيْحِ بْنِ مَرْيَمِ
> أَتَى بِالْهُدَى مِثْلَ الَّذِي أَتَيَا بِهِ
> فَكَانَ بِأَمْرِ اللهِ يَهْدِي وَيَعْصِمُ
> وَإِنَّكُمُ تَتْلُونَهُ فِي كِتَابِكُمْ
> بِصِدْقِ حَدِيْثٍ لا حَدِيْثِ التَّرَجُّمِ
> وَإِنَّكَ مَا تَأْتِيكَ مِنَّا عِصَابَةٌ
> لِفَضْلِكَ إِلاَّ أُرْجِعُوا بِالتَّكَرُّمِ
> فَلا تَجْعَلُوا لِلَّهِ نِدّاً وَسَلِّمُوا
> فَإِنَّ طَرِيْقَ الْحَقِّ لَيْسَ بِمُظْلِمِ

"Know, and may you be spared all cursing, that Muhammad is the counterpart of Moses and of the Messiah, son of Mary. He came with guidance like that which those two brought, and by the command of Allah he guides and preserves. You recite this in your own Book, in truthful report, not in the report of mere hearsay. No company of ours comes to you seeking your bounty but is sent back honored. So set up no equal to Allah, and submit, for the road of truth is not dark."

Jaʿfar (ع) delivered his father's letter to the Negus, and it was among the causes of the kindness and generosity the Muslims received from him.

When the Quraysh saw the honor the companions of the Messenger of Allah had received among them, that they had grown settled and secure in the land of Abyssinia, and that the Negus had lodged them in the finest lodging under his protection, this grieved them. They agreed to choose two men of cunning and eloquent speech and to send them to the Negus. They chose ʿAmr ibn al-ʿAs and ʿUmara ibn al-Walid, gathered a great sum of wealth, bought many gifts from among the choice things of the Hijaz, and sent them with the two men to the king of Abyssinia and to all his men and the people of his court.

When the two arrived with the gifts, they came to the patriarchs and gave them the gifts appointed for them, and delivered to the Negus what had been sent to him. The patriarchs mentioned the two men to the Negus, and he admitted them to his presence. They said:

"O king, there have come into your land foolish young men of ours who have abandoned the religion of their people and have not entered into your religion, and have brought an innovation that neither you nor we know. The nobles of our people, their fathers, their uncles, and their clans, have sent us to you so that you may return them to them."

The patriarchs, on account of the gifts that had reached them, said:

"They speak the truth, O king. Their own people know them best, so return them to them."

The king grew angry and said:

"I will not surrender to them a people who have come to dwell beside me and have chosen me above all others, until I summon them and question them about what they say."

Al-Najashi sent for the companions of the Messenger of Allah. When they had come before him, he had already summoned the bishops, and they spread their scriptures around him. Then he questioned the companions of the Prophet, saying:

"What is this religion for which you have parted from your people, while you have not entered into our religion, nor into any of the known creeds?"

The one who spoke to him was Jaʿfar ibn Abi Talib. He said:

"O king, we were a people of ignorance. We worshipped idols, ate carrion, committed immoralities, cut the bonds of kinship, and treated our neighbors badly, and the strong among us devoured the weak. We remained in that state until Allah sent to us a messenger from among ourselves, whose lineage, truthfulness, trustworthiness, and chastity we knew. He called us to the oneness of Allah, that we should declare Him one and worship Him and abandon what we and our fathers had worshipped besides Him of stones and idols. He commanded us to speak truthfully, to render back trusts, to keep the ties of kinship, to be good neighbors, and to hold back from what is forbidden and from bloodshed. He forbade us immoralities, false speech, devouring the wealth of orphans, and slandering chaste women. He commanded us to worship Allah alone and to associate nothing with Him, and he commanded us to pray, to give zakat, and to fast. He then set out for the king the obligations of Islam. So we believed him and had faith in him and followed him in what he brought us from Allah. We worshipped Allah alone and associated nothing with Him. We forbade what He forbade us and made lawful what He made lawful for us. Then our people fell upon us, tortured us, and tried to turn us from our religion, to bring us back from the worship of Allah to the worship of idols, and to make us hold lawful again the foul things we once held lawful. When they overpowered us, wronged us, straitened our lives, and came between us and our religion, we departed to your land and chose you above all others. We desired your protection, and we hoped that we would not be wronged in your presence, O king."

Al-Najashi said to him:

"Have you with you anything of what he brought from Allah, exalted is He?"

Jaʿfar ibn Abi Talib said:

"Yes."

Al-Najashi said:

"Then recite it to me."

So he recited to him the opening of:

> ﴿كَهَيَعَصَ﴾ [مريم: ١]

"Kaf Ha Ya ʿAyn Sad."

The Negus wept, and with him the bishops who were present,

> حَتَّى أَخْضَلَتْ لِحَاهُمْ

"until their beards were wet."

He said:

> إِنَّ هَذَا وَالَّذِي جَاءَ بِهِ عِيْسَى لَيَخْرُجُ مِنْ مِشْكَاةٍ وَاحِدَةٍ

"Truly this and what Jesus brought come forth from a single lamp."

He said to ʿAmr and ʿUmara:

"Depart. By Allah, I will never surrender them to you."

When the two had gone out from his presence, ʿAmr ibn al-ʿAs said:

"Tomorrow I will bring him something concerning them that will tear them up by the root."

He then said to the Negus:

"They say a grave thing concerning Jesus son of Mary."

So the Negus sent for them and questioned them, saying:

"What do you say concerning Jesus son of Mary?"

They gathered together and said:

"What shall we say?"

Jaʿfar ibn Abi Talib answered them and said:

> نَقُولُ الَّذِي جَاءَنَا بِهِ نَبِيُّنَا: هُوَ عَبْدُ اللهِ وَرَسُولُهُ وَرُوحُهُ وَكَلِمَتُهُ أَلْقَاهَا إِلَى مَرْيَمَ الْعَذْرَاءِ الْبَتُولِ

"We say what our Prophet brought us: he is the servant of Allah, and His messenger, and His spirit, and His word which He cast to Mary, the chaste virgin."

At this the Negus struck his hand to the ground, took up a stick from it, and said:

> مَا عَدَا عِيْسَى بْنُ مَرْيَمَ مِمَّا قُلْتَ هَذَا الْعُوْدَ

"Jesus son of Mary did not exceed what you have said by so much as this stick."

His patriarchs snorted in displeasure around him, and he said:

> وَإِنْ نَخَرْتُمْ فَوَاللهِ ذَلِكَ الْحَقُّ، اذْهَبُوا فَأَنْتُمْ آمِنُونَ، مَنْ سَبَّكُمْ غَرِمَ

"Even if you snort, by Allah that is the truth. Go, for you are safe; whoever reviles you shall pay for it."

Then he said:

> رُدُّوا عَلَيْهِمَا هَدَايَاهُمَا فَمَا لَنَا إِلَيْهِمَا مِنْ حَاجَةٍ؛ فَوَاللهِ مَا أَخَذَ اللهُ مِنِّي رِشْوَةً حِيْنَ رَدَّ إِلَيَّ مُلْكِي، فَآخُذَ الرِّشْوَةَ فِيهِ

"Return to these two their gifts, for we have no need of them. By Allah, Allah took no bribe from me when He restored my kingdom to me, that I should take a bribe in it."

The kingship had passed away from the Negus and then returned to him.

When news of those who had emigrated to Abyssinia was slow in reaching the people of Mecca, Abu Talib said concerning that:

> أَلاَ لَيْتَ شِعْرِي كَيْفَ فِي النَّأْيِ جَعْفَرٌ
> وَعَمْرٌو وَأَعْدَى الْعَدُوِّ الأَقَارِبُ
> وَهَلْ نَالَ إِكْرَامُ النَّجَاشِيِّ جَعْفَراً
> وَأَصْحَابَهُ أَمْ عَاقَ عَنْ ذَاكَ شَاغِبُ
> تَعَلَّمْ أَبَيْتَ اللَّعْنَ أَنَّكَ مَاجِدٌ
> كَرِيْمٌ فَلاَ يَشْقَى لَدَيْكَ الْمُجَانِبُ
> تَعَلَّمْ بِأَنَّ اللهَ زَادَكَ بَسْطَةً
> وَأَسْبَابَ خَيْرٍ كُلُّهَا بِكَ لاَزِبُ
> وَأَنَّكَ فَيْضٌ ذُو سِجَالٍ غَزِيْرَةٍ
> يَنَالُ الأَعَادِي نَفْعَهَا وَالأَقَارِبُ

"Would that I knew how Jaʿfar fares in his distance, and ʿAmr, for the bitterest of enemies are one's own kin. Has the honoring of the Negus reached Jaʿfar and his companions, or has some troublemaker barred the way to it? Know, and may you be spared all cursing, that you are noble and generous, so that no stranger comes to misery at your side. Know that Allah has increased you in amplitude, and that every means of good clings fast to you, and that you are an overflowing spring of abundant streams, whose benefit reaches enemies and kinsmen alike."

A man of the Abyssinians then disputed the kingship with the Negus while the Muslims were with him. The Negus went out to meet him, but before that he sent to Jaʿfar and his companions and made ships ready for them. He said:

"Embark in them and remain as you are. If I am defeated, go wherever you wish; if I am victorious, stand firm."

Then he took a document and wrote in it:

> هُوَ يَشْهَدُ أَنْ لاَ إِلَهَ إِلاَّ اللهُ، وَأَنَّ مُحَمَّداً عَبْدُهُ وَرَسُولُهُ، وَيَشْهَدُ أَنَّ عِيْسَى بْنَ مَرْيَمَ عَبْدُهُ وَرَسُولُهُ وَرُوحُهُ وَكَلِمَتُهُ أَلْقَاهَا إِلَى مَرْيَمَ

"He bears witness that there is no god but Allah, and that Muhammad is His servant and His messenger; and he bears witness that Jesus son of Mary is His servant and His messenger and His spirit and His word which He cast to Mary."

He placed it in his tunic at the right shoulder and went out to the Abyssinians.

They drew up their ranks before him, and he said:

"O company of Abyssinians, am I not the most rightful of people over you?"

They said:

"Indeed."

He said:

"Then how have you found my conduct among you?"

They said:

"The best of conduct."

He said:

"Then what ails you?"

They said:

"You have departed from our religion, and you claim that Jesus is the servant of Allah."

He said:

"And what do you yourselves say concerning Jesus?"

They said:

"We say that he is the son of Allah."

Then the Negus, placing his hand upon his breast, said:

"And he bears witness that Jesus is the son of Mary."

He added nothing beyond this, pointing all the while to the document he had placed in his tunic.

When the Negus died, the Prophet prayed over him in Medina, and sought forgiveness for him, and asked mercy upon him.
