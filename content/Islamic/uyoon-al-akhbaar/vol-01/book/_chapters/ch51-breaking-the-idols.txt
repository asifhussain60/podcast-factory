# Breaking the Idols

Ali (ع) came in still carrying his sword, and with her he found two men of Banu Makhzum whose blood the Messenger of Allah had declared forfeit on that day. They had sought her protection because of a marriage tie between them. When Ali (ع) saw them he took up the sword and rose to kill them, but Umm Hani stood between him and them and said: "يا أخي؛ إِنِّي قد أجرتهما," "My brother, I have granted them my protection." He (ع) said: "إنَّ رسول الله قد أمر بقتلهما، ولو كانا تحت أَسْتَارِ الكَعْبَةِ," "The Messenger of Allah has ordered them killed, even were they beneath the coverings of the Kaaba." She seized his hand, and her grip was strong, and twisted it until she drew the sword from his hand and held it fast. She then had the two men taken into a house and locked the door upon them.

She went to the Messenger of Allah, and when he saw her he welcomed her and asked after her state and what had brought her. She told him what had happened, and he laughed and said:

> "قَدْ أَجَرْنَا مَنْ أَجَرْتِ يَا أُمّ هانىء"
>
> "We have granted protection to whomever you have granted protection, O Umm Hani."

He then sent for Ali (ع), and when he came he laughed to him and said: "غَلَبَتْكَ أُمّ هانىء," "Umm Hani has overcome you." Ali (ع) said: "يا رسول الله؛ والّذي بعثكَ بالحقِّ نبياً لا قدرتُ على أن أمسكَ السَّيف حتَّى خَلَّصَتْهُ مِنْ يَدي," "O Messenger of Allah, by Him who sent you with the truth as a prophet, I was not able to keep hold of the sword until she had freed it from my hand." The Messenger of Allah laughed and said:

> "لَوْ أنَّ أبا طالِب ولد النَّاس كلّهم لكانوا أَقْوياءِ أَشِدَّاءٍ"
>
> "Had Abu Talib fathered all mankind, they would all have been strong and mighty."

**The Messenger of Allah confirms Uthman ibn Talha in the office of al-sidana**

On that day Ali (ع) took the keys of the Kaaba and brought them to the Messenger of Allah, saying: "يا رسول الله؛ هذا مفتاح الكَعْبَةِ، فإِنْ رأيتَ أنْ تعطينا مفاتيح الكَعْبَة، لتجمع لنا السِّقاية والحِجابة، فافعل," "O Messenger of Allah, this is the key of the Kaaba. If you see fit to give us the keys of the Kaaba, so that the watering of the pilgrims (al-siqaya) and the custodianship (al-hijaba) may be joined for us, then do so." He said:

> "يَا عَلِيّ؛ أُعْطِيْكُمْ مَا هُوَ أفضل مِنْ ذَلِكَ؛ مَا أَعْطانا الله مِنْ فَضْلِهِ، وَهَذا يَوْمُ بِرٍّ وَوَفَاءٍ، وَإِنَّمَا أُعْطِيْكُمْ مَا تُرْزَءُونَ لاَ مَا تَرْزَءُونَ. فادْفَعِ المفاتيح إلى عُثْمَان بن طَلْحَة أخي بني شَيْبَة بن عبد الدَّار بن عبد مَنَاف"
>
> "O Ali, I give you what is better than that: what Allah has given us of His bounty. This is a day of righteousness and fidelity. I give you what is borne for you, not what is borne by you. Hand the keys to Uthman ibn Talha, the brother of Banu Shayba ibn Abd al-Dar ibn Abd Manaf."

He handed them to him and said: "رضينا يا رسول الله ما رضيته لنفسك، وإنّا معك يا رسول الله," "We are content, O Messenger of Allah, with what you are content with for yourself, and we are with you, O Messenger of Allah."

**His pride (ع) in his precedence in faith and in striving in the way of Allah**

It is related from the Commander of the Faithful, Ali ibn Abi Talib (ع), that he said:

"I was with al-Abbas ibn Abd al-Muttalib and Uthman ibn Talha, the brother of Banu Shayba, in the Sacred Mosque, and the two of them boasted over me. Uthman ibn Talha said: 'أعطاني رسول الله السِّدانة,' 'The Messenger of Allah gave me al-sidana,' meaning the keys of the Kaaba. And al-Abbas said: 'أعطاني رسول الله السِّقاية,' 'The Messenger of Allah gave me al-siqaya,' which is the keys of Zamzam. They said: 'وَلَمْ يعطكَ شيئاً يا عليّ,' 'And he gave you nothing, O Ali.' So Allah, Mighty and Majestic, sent down concerning that:

> أَجَعَلْتُمْ سِقَايَةَ ٱلْحَآجِّ وَعِمَارَةَ ٱلْمَسْجِدِ ٱلْحَرَامِ كَمَنْ آمَنَ بِٱللَّهِ وَٱلْيَوْمِ ٱلْآخِرِ وَجَهَدَ فِى سَبِيلِ ٱللَّهِ ۚ لَا يَسْتَوُۥنَ عِندَ ٱللَّهِ ۗ وَٱللَّهُ لَا يَهْدِى ٱلْقَوْمَ ٱلظَّلِمِينَ
>
> "Do you consider the giving of drink to the pilgrims and the maintenance of the Sacred Mosque equal to one who believes in Allah and the Last Day and strives in the way of Allah? They are not equal in the sight of Allah, and Allah does not guide the wrongdoing people."
>
> الَّذِينَ آمَنُوا وَهَاجَرُوا وَجَاهَدُوا فِي سَبِيلِ اللهِ بِأَمْوَالِهِمْ وَأَنْفُسِهِمْ أَعْظَمُ دَرَجَةً عِندَ اللهِ وَأُولَئِكَ هُمُ الْفَائِزُونَ يُبَشِّرُهُمْ رَبُّهُم بِرَحْمَةٍ مِّنْهُ وَرِضْوَانٍ وَجَنَّاتٍ لَّهُمْ فِيهَا نَعِيمٌ مُّقِيمٌ خَالِدِينَ فِيهَا أَبَداً إِنَّ اللهَ عِندَهُ أَجْرٌ عَظِيمٌ
>
> "Those who believed and emigrated and strove in the way of Allah with their wealth and their lives are greater in rank with Allah, and it is they who are the triumphant. Their Lord gives them good tidings of mercy from Him and good pleasure, and gardens wherein there is lasting bliss, abiding therein forever. Truly with Allah is a great reward." (Quran 9:19-22)

**The sermon of the Messenger of Allah at the door of the Kaaba**

The Messenger of Allah rose and delivered a sermon, saying:

> "لاَ إِلَهَ إِلاَّ اللهُ وَحْدَهُ لاَ شَرِيكَ لَهُ، صَدَقَ وَعْدَهُ، وَنَصَرَ عَبْدَهُ، وَهَزَمَ الْأَحْزَابَ وَحْدَهُ، أَلاَ كُلُّ مَأْثَرَةٍ، أَوْ دَمٍ، أَوْ مَالٍ يُدَّعَى فَهْوَ تَحْتَ قَدَمَيَّ هَاتَيْنِ إِلاَّ سِدَانَةَ الْبَيْتِ وَسِقَايَةَ الْحَاجِّ. أَلاَ وَقَتْلُ الْخَطَأِ شِبْهِ الْعَمْدِ بِالسَّوْطِ وَالْعَصَا، فَفِيهِ الدِّيَةُ مُغَلَّظَةً، مِائَةٌ مِنَ الإِبِلِ، أَرْبَعُونَ مِنْهَا فِي بُطُونِهَا أَوْلاَدُهَا. يا مَعْشَرَ قُرَيْشٍ؛ إِنَّ اللهَ قَدْ أَذْهَبَ عَنْكُمْ نَخْوَةَ الْجَاهِلِيَّةِ وَتَعَظُّمَهَا بِالْآبَاءِ، النَّاسُ مِنْ آدَمَ وَآدَمُ مِنْ تُرَابٍ."
>
> "There is no god but Allah alone, no partner has He. He fulfilled His promise, aided His servant, and defeated the confederates alone. Every claim to ancestral glory, or blood, or wealth is beneath these two feet of mine, except the custodianship of the House and the watering of the pilgrims. And killing by error that resembles the deliberate, by whip and by staff, in it is the heavy blood-money: a hundred camels, forty of them pregnant with their young. O assembly of Quraysh, Allah has removed from you the arrogance of the Jahiliyya and its boasting of forefathers. Mankind is from Adam, and Adam is from dust."

He then recited this verse:

> يَٓأَيُّهَا ٱلنَّاسُ إِنَّا خَلَقْنَكُم مِّن ذَكَرٍۢ وَأُنثَىٰ وَجَعَلْنَكُمْ شُعُوبًۭا وَقَبَآئِلَ لِتَعَارَفُوٓا۟ ۚ إِنَّ أَكْرَمَكُمْ عِندَ ٱللَّهِ أَتْقَىٰكُمْ ۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ عَلِيمٌ خَبِيرٌۭ
>
> "O mankind, We created you from a male and a female and made you peoples and tribes that you may know one another. Truly the most honoured of you with Allah is the most God-fearing of you. Truly Allah is Knowing, Aware." (Quran 49:13)

Then he said: "يَا مَعْشَرَ قُرَيْشٍ؛ ما تَرَوْنَ أَنِّي فَاعِلٌ فِيكُمْ؟" "O assembly of Quraysh, what do you think I shall do with you?" They said: "خَيْراً، أَخٌ كَرِيمٌ، وَابْنُ أَخٍ كَرِيمٍ," "Good. A noble brother and the son of a noble brother." He said: "اذْهَبُوا فَأَنْتُمُ الطُّلَقَاءُ," "Go, for you are the freed ones."

**From another sermon of the Messenger of Allah**

Then he said:

> "يَا أَيُّهَا النَّاسُ، إِنَّ اللهَ قَدْ حَرَّمَ مَكَّةَ يَوْمَ خَلَقَ السَّمَوَاتِ وَالأَرْضَ، فَهْيَ حَرَامٌ مِنْ حَرَامٍ إِلَى يَوْمِ الْقِيَامَةِ، فَلاَ يَحِلُّ لِامْرِئٍ يُؤْمِنُ بِاللهِ وَالْيَوْمِ الآخِرِ، أَنْ يَسْفِكَ فِيهَا دَماً، وَلاَ يَعْضِدَ فِيهَا شَجَراً، لَمْ تَحْلِلْ لأَحَدٍ كَانَ قَبْلِي، وَلاَ تَحِلُّ لأَحَدٍ يَكُونُ بَعْدِي، وَلَمْ تَحْلِلْ لِي إِلاَّ هَذِهِ السَّاعَةَ غَضَباً عَلَى أَهْلِهَا. أَلاَ، ثُمَّ قَدْ رَجَعَتْ كَحُرْمَتِهَا بِالأَمْسِ، فَلْيُبَلِّغِ الشَّاهِدُ مِنْكُمُ الْغَائِبَ، فَمَنْ قَالَ لَكُمْ إِنَّ رَسُولَ اللهِ صَلَّى اللهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ قَدْ قَاتَلَ فِيهَا، فَقُولُوا: إِنَّ اللهَ قَدْ أَحَلَّهَا لِرَسُولِهِ وَلَمْ يُحْلِلْهَا لَكُمْ."
>
> "O people, Allah made Mecca sacred on the day He created the heavens and the earth, so it is sacred by the sanctity of Allah until the Day of Resurrection. It is not lawful for a man who believes in Allah and the Last Day to shed blood in it, nor to cut down a tree in it. It was not made lawful for anyone before me, nor shall it be made lawful for anyone after me, and it was made lawful for me only for this hour, in anger against its people. Then it has returned to its sanctity as it was yesterday. Let the one present among you convey it to the one absent. And whoever says to you that the Messenger of Allah fought in it, say: Allah made it lawful for His Messenger and did not make it lawful for you."

**The march of Khalid ibn al-Walid against Banu Judhima, and the march of Ali (ع) to repair Khalid's error**

When the Messenger of Allah had conquered Mecca and the affairs of its people had settled, he sent out men to summon the Arabs to Allah, Exalted be He, and to His Messenger, that they might enter into what the people of Mecca had entered into. Among those he sent was Khalid ibn al-Walid, and he commanded none of them to fight anyone.

Khalid came to Banu Judhima ibn Amir ibn Abd Manat with a squadron. When they saw him they took up their weapons, and he said to them: "Lay down your weapons, for the people have accepted Islam and the war has laid down its burdens. The Messenger of Allah has sent us only to call people to Islam, and he did not command us to fight anyone." So they laid down their weapons, except one man among them called Jahdam, who said to them: "ويحكم! إنَّه خالِد بن الوَلِيد! والله ما بعد وضع السِّلاح إلا الأَسْر، وما بعد الأَسْر إلا ضرب الأعناق," "Woe to you! This is Khalid ibn al-Walid! By Allah, after the laying down of weapons there is nothing but captivity, and after captivity nothing but the striking of necks." They all rose against him and said: "O Jahdam, do you wish to spill our blood? The people have accepted Islam and laid down their weapons." He said: "By Allah, I shall not lay down my weapon." They overpowered him and wrested his weapon from his hand. When they had laid down their weapons, Khalid ordered them bound, and then put them to the sword and killed a number of them.

The news reached the Messenger of Allah. He rose to his feet, lifted his hands to the sky, and said: "اللَّهُمَّ إِنِّي أَبْرَأُ إليكَ مِمَّا صَنَعَ خَالِدٌ," "O Allah, I declare myself innocent before You of what Khalid has done."

Then he called Ali (ع) and said:

> "يَا عَلِيّ؛ اخْرُجْ إِلَى هَؤُلاَءِ القَوْمِ فَانْظُرْ فِي أَمْرِهِمْ، وَاجْعَلْ أَمْرَ الْجَاهِلِيَّةِ تَحْتَ قَدَمَيْكَ"
>
> "O Ali, go out to these people and look into their affair, and place the ways of the Jahiliyya beneath your feet."

He handed him wealth and said to him: "أَعْقِلْ لَهُمْ مَنْ قُتِلَ مِنْهُمْ، وَادْفَعْ إِلَيْهِمْ ثَمَنَ مَا أُخِذَ مِنْهُمْ وَأَنْصِفْهُمْ," "Pay them the blood-money for those of them who were killed, hand them the price of what was taken from them, and deal justly with them."

So Ali (ع) went out and paid them the blood-money for the slain and the price of the property that had been taken from them, until he was giving them even the price of a dog's drinking bowl, and until nothing remained of blood or property that he had not paid to them. Then he said to them: "Does any remainder of blood or property remain to you that has not been paid?" They said: "No." He said: "There remains with me a remainder of the wealth that the Messenger of Allah sent with me, so take it as a precaution on behalf of the Messenger of Allah." He handed to them what was left with him after what he had already paid, and they took it, thanked him, and prayed good for him.

Then he came to the Messenger of Allah and told him what had happened, and he said: "أَحْسَنْتَ يَا عَلِيّ وَأَصَبْتَ؛ أَصَابَكَ اللهُ الْمَرَاشِدَ," "You have done well, O Ali, and you have hit the mark. May Allah guide you to right courses."

Then he turned toward the qibla, standing, raising his hands to the sky until what was beneath his shoulders could be seen, saying three times: "اللَّهُمَّ إِنِّي أبرأُ إليك مِمَّا صَنَعَ خالِد بن الوَلِيد," "O Allah, I declare myself innocent before You of what Khalid ibn al-Walid has done."

Khalid had done that to them only because they had killed his uncle al-Fakih ibn al-Mughira in the Jahiliyya.

**The poetry of Hassan ibn Thabit on the Conquest**

Hassan ibn Thabit said on the day of the Conquest, though it is said that he had composed it before that:

> عَدِمْنَا خَيْلَنَا إِنْ لَمْ تَرَوْهَا ... تُثِيرُ النَّقْعَ مَوْعِدُهَا كَدَاءُ
> يُنَازِعْنَ الأَعِنَّةَ مُصْغِيَاتٍ ... عَلَى أَكْتَافِهَا الأَسَلُ الظِّمَاءُ
> تَظَلُّ جِيَادُنَا مُتَمَطِّرَاتٍ ... يُلَطِّمُهُنَّ بِالْخُمُرِ النِّسَاءُ
> فَإِمَّا تُعْرِضُوا عَنَّا اعْتَمَرْنَا ... وَكَانَ الْفَتْحُ وَانْكَشَفَ الْغِطَاءُ
> وَإِلاَّ فَاصْبِرُوا لِجِلاَدِ يَوْمٍ ... يُعِينُ اللهُ فِيهِ مَنْ يَشَاءُ
> وَجِبْرِيلٌ رَسُولُ اللهِ فِينَا ... وَرُوحُ الْقُدْسِ لَيْسَ لَهُ كِفَاءُ
> أَلاَ أَبْلِغْ أَبَا سُفْيَانَ عَنِّي ... مُغَلْغَلَةً فَقَدْ بَرِحَ الْخَفَاءُ
> بِأَنَّ سُيُوفَنَا تَرَكَتْكَ عَبْداً ... وَعَبْدُ الدَّارِ سَادَتُهَا الإِمَاءُ
> هَجَوْتَ مُبَارَكاً بَرّاً حَنِيفاً ... أَمِينَ اللهِ شِيمَتُهُ الْوَفَاءُ
> فَإِنَّ أَبِي وَوَالِدَهُ وَعِرْضِي ... لِعِرْضِ مُحَمَّدٍ مِنْكُمْ وِقَاءُ
> لِسَانِي صَارِمٌ لاَ عَيْبَ فِيهِ ... وَبَحْرِي لاَ تُكَدِّرُهُ الدِّلاَءُ
>
> "We have lost our horses if you do not see them raising the dust, their appointed place being Kada. They contend with the reins, ears bent forward, thirsty spears upon their shoulders. Our steeds keep galloping while the women strike them aside with their veils. Either you turn aside from us and we perform the pilgrimage, and the Conquest comes and the veil is lifted, or else endure the hard combat of a day in which Allah aids whom He wills. Jibril, the messenger of Allah, is among us, and the Holy Spirit has no equal. Carry to Abu Sufyan from me a message plainly sent, for concealment has departed: that our swords have left you a slave, while the masters of Abd al-Dar are slave women. You have satirized a blessed one, righteous and upright, the trusted one of Allah whose nature is fidelity. My father, and his father, and my honour are a shield for the honour of Muhammad against you. My tongue is a sharp blade without flaw, and my sea is not muddied by the buckets."

**The poetry of Bujayr on the day of the Conquest**

Bujayr ibn Zuhayr ibn Abi Sulma said on the day of the Conquest:

> صَبَحْنَاكُمْ بِسَبْعٍ مِنْ سُلَيْمٍ ... مُزَيْنَةُ غُدْوَةً وَبَنُو خِفَافِ
> ضَرَبْنَاهُمْ بِمَكَّةَ يَوْمَ فَتْحِ النَّـ ... ـبِيِّ الْخَيْرِ بِالْبِيضِ الْخِفَافِ
> وَأَعْطَيْنَا رَسُولَ اللهِ مِنَّا ... مَوَاثِقَنَا عَلَى حُسْنِ التَّصَافِي
>
> "We came upon you at morning with seven troops of Sulaym, and Muzayna at daybreak, and Banu Khifaf. We struck them at Mecca on the day of the Conquest of the Prophet, the good one, with light white blades. And we gave the Messenger of Allah from ourselves our covenants upon a fair and sincere accord."

**The poetry of al-Abbas ibn Mirdas on the Conquest of Mecca**

Al-Abbas ibn Mirdas said concerning the Conquest of Mecca:

> مِنَّا بِمَكَّةَ يَوْمَ فَتْحِ مُحَمَّدٍ ... أَلْفٌ تَسِيلُ بِهِ الْبِطَاحُ مُسَوَّمُ
> نَصَرُوا الرَّسُولَ وَشَاهَدُوا أَيَّامَهُ ... وَشِعَارُهُمْ يَوْمَ اللِّقَاءِ مُقَدَّمُ
> اللهُ مَكَّنَهُ لَهُ وَأَذَلَّهُ ... حُكْمُ السُّيُوفِ لَنَا وَجَدٌّ مِزْحَمُ
>
> "Of us at Mecca, on the day of Muhammad's Conquest, a thousand marked warriors with whom the valleys flowed. They aided the Messenger and witnessed his days, and their watchword on the day of encounter was foremost. Allah gave him power and abased his enemy: the rule of the swords is ours, and a crowding fortune."

**The Islam of Ibn al-Zibara and his poetry concerning it**

Ibn al-Zibara had joined Najran, and Hassan ibn Thabit shot at him with a verse he composed:

> لاَ تَعْدَمَنَّ رَجُلاً أَحَلَّكَ بُغْضُهُ ... نَجْرَانَ فِي عَيْشٍ أَحَذَّ لَئِيمِ
>
> "May you never lack a man whose hatred has settled you in Najran, in a life cut short and mean."

So Ibn al-Zibara went out to the Messenger of Allah and accepted Islam, and said:

> مَنَعَ الرُّقَادَ بَلاَبِلٌ وَهُمُومُ ... وَاللَّيْلُ مُعْتَلِجُ الرِّوَاقِ بَهِيمُ
> مِمَّا أَتَانِي أَنَّ أَحْمَدَ لاَمَنِي ... فِيهِ فَبِتُّ كَأَنَّنِي مَحْمُومُ
> فَالْيَوْمَ آمَنَ بِالنَّبِيِّ مُحَمَّدٍ ... قَلْبِي وَمُخْطِئُ هَذِهِ مَحْرُومُ
> مَضَتِ الْعَدَاوَةُ وَانْقَضَتْ أَسْبَابُهَا ... وَدَعَتْ أَوَاصِرُ بَيْنَنَا وَحُلُومُ
> فَاغْفِرْ فِدًى لَكَ وَالِدَايَ كِلاَهُمَا ... زَلَلِي فَإِنَّكَ رَاحِمٌ مَرْحُومُ
> وَعَلَيْكَ مِنْ عِلْمِ الْمَلِيكِ عَلاَمَةٌ ... نُورٌ أَغَرُّ وَخَاتَمٌ مَحْتُومُ
> وَلَقَدْ شَهِدْتُ بِأَنَّ دِينَكَ صَادِقٌ ... حَقٌّ وَأَنَّكَ فِي الْعِبَادِ جَسِيمُ
>
> "Anxieties and cares have barred sleep, and the night is dark, its canopy heaving. It comes from what reached me, that Ahmad has blamed me, so that I passed the night as though fevered. O best of those whom a swift, strong-handed she-camel has borne upon her limbs, I offer you my apology for what I did when I wandered in error, in the days when the worst of courses commanded me and the arrow of ruin directed me. I stretched out the ropes of destruction, and the affair of the misguided led me, and their affair is ill-omened. Today my heart believes in the Prophet Muhammad, and whoever misses this is deprived. The enmity has passed and its causes have ended, and the ties between us and forbearance have called out. So forgive my slip, may both my parents be your ransom, for you are compassionate and shown compassion. Upon you is a sign from the knowledge of the Sovereign, a shining light and a sealed seal. I have borne witness that your religion is truthful and true, and that you are mighty among the servants."

**Ali (ع) brings down the idols from the roof of the Kaaba and breaks them**

In the morning the Messenger of Allah went to the idols the polytheists had set in the Kaaba and smashed and broke them.

The Messenger of Allah saw idols above the Kaaba and said to Ali ibn Abi Talib: "يَا عَلِيّ؛ ارْقَ فَوْقَ كَتِفِي، وَكَسِّرْ هَذِهِ الْأَصْنَامَ الّتي على سَقْفِ الكَعْبَةِ," "O Ali, climb upon my shoulder and break these idols on the roof of the Kaaba."

He said: "Rather you, O Messenger of Allah, climb upon my shoulder."

He said: "يا عليّ؛ لَوِ اجْتَمَعَ أَهْلُ الأَرْضِ لِيَحْمِلُونِي مَا أَطَاقُوا ذَلِكَ، فَافْعَلْ مَا أَمَرْتُكَ," "O Ali, were the people of the earth to gather to carry me, they would not be able to do it. So do what I have commanded you."

Ali (ع) said: "I climbed upon the shoulder of the Prophet, and he rose so high with me that had I wished to touch the sky and take hold of its stars I could have done so."

When Ali (ع) rose upon the roof of the Kaaba, he cast the idols to the ground and they were shattered. Then, out of modesty before the Messenger of Allah, he leapt from the roof of the House to the ground, landed upright, and smiled.

The Messenger of Allah said: "ما تبسُّمكَ يا عليّ؟" "What is your smiling, O Ali?"

He said: "O Messenger of Allah, I did not think I could leap such a leap and come safely down from such a height."

The Prophet said: "إِنَّكَ طَلَعْتَ على كتف محمَّد، ونزلتَ على كتف جَبْرَائِيلَ," "You ascended upon the shoulder of Muhammad, and you descended upon the shoulder of Jibril."

**The command of the Messenger of Allah to demolish al-Uzza**

The Messenger of Allah ordered that every idol around Mecca be smashed, and he gave his command concerning al-Uzza. It was at Nakhla, a shrine venerated by this clan of Quraysh, by Banu Kinana, and by all Mudar. Its custodians and keepers were Banu Shayban of Banu Sulaym, allies of Banu Hashim. When the Sulami who tended it heard of this, he hung his sword upon it and climbed the mountain in which it stood, saying:

> أَيَا عُزَّ شُدِّي شِدَّةً لاَ شَوَى لَهَا ... عَلَى خَالِدٍ أَلْقِي الْقِنَاعَ وَشَمِّرِي
> يَا عُزَّ إِنْ لَمْ تَقْتُلِي الْمَرْءَ خَالِداً ... فَبُوئِي بِإِثْمٍ عَاجِلٍ أَوْ تَنَصَّرِي
>
> "O Uzza, strike a blow that leaves nothing surviving, against Khalid. Cast off the veil and gird yourself. O Uzza, if you do not kill the man Khalid, then bear a swift sin, or else become a Christian."

He meant Khalid ibn al-Walid, who was among those the Prophet had sent to demolish it. When they reached it, they tore it down.

The Conquest of Mecca was with ten nights remaining of the month of Ramadan, in the eighth year of the Hijra, and the Messenger of Allah remained in Mecca fifteen nights.
