# Whoever Enters His House Is Safe

The Messenger of Allah said to him:

> "وَيْحَكَ يَا أَبَا سُفْيَانَ؛ أَلَمْ يَأْنِ لَكَ أَنْ تَعْلَمَ أَنِّي رَسُولُ اللهِ؟"
>
> "Woe to you, O Abu Sufyan! Has the time not come for you to know that I am the Messenger of Allah?"

Abu Sufyan answered: "By my father and my mother, how forbearing you are, how noble, how mindful of kinship! But as for this, by Allah, there is until now something of it lingering in my soul."

Al-Abbas said to him:

> "وَيْحَكَ! أَسْلِمْ وَاشْهَدْ أَنْ لاَ إِلَهَ إِلاَّ اللّهُ وَأَنَّ مُحَمَّداً رَسُولُ اللهِ قَبْلَ أَنْ تُضْرَبَ عُنُقُكَ."
>
> "Woe to you! Submit, and bear witness that there is no god but Allah and that Muhammad is the Messenger of Allah, before your neck is struck."

So Abu Sufyan pronounced the testimony, finding before him nothing but this or the sword.

Al-Abbas (رض) said: "I said: O Messenger of Allah, Abu Sufyan is a man who loves distinction, so grant him something." He said:

> "نَعَمْ؛ مَنْ دَخَلَ دَارَ أَبِي سُفْيَانَ فَهْوَ آمِنٌ، وَمَنْ أَغْلَقَ عَلَيْهِ بَابَهُ فَهْوَ آمِنٌ، وَمَنْ دَخَلَ الْمَسْجِدَ فَهْوَ آمِنٌ."
>
> "Yes. Whoever enters the house of Abu Sufyan is safe, whoever shuts his door upon himself is safe, and whoever enters the mosque is safe."

When Abu Sufyan was about to depart, the Messenger of Allah said to al-Abbas:

> "احْبِسْهُ بِمَضِيْقِ الْوَادِي عِنْدَ خَطْمِ الْجَبَلِ حَتَّى تَمُرَّ بِهِ جُنُودُ اللَّهِ فَيَرَاهَا."
>
> "Hold him back at the narrows of the valley, at the shoulder of the mountain, until the armies of Allah pass by him and he sees them."

Al-Abbas (رض) said: "I did what the Messenger of Allah commanded me. The tribes passed by with their banners, and not one tribe passed but that he asked about it. He would say: Who are these? I would say: Sulaym. He would say: What have I to do with Sulaym? Then another tribe would pass by him, and he would say: Who are these? I would say: Muzaynah. He would say: What have I to do with Muzaynah? So it went on, no tribe passing him without his asking me about it, and when I told him who they were he would say: What have I to do with the sons of so-and-so? Until the Messenger of Allah passed by him in his green battalion, in which were the Muhajirun and the Ansar, nothing to be seen of them for the iron but the whites of their eyes. He said:

> "سُبْحَانَ اللهِ يا عَبَّاس؛ مَنْ هؤلاء؟"
>
> "Glory be to Allah, O Abbas! Who are these?"

"I said: This is the Messenger of Allah among the Muhajirun and the Ansar. He said:

> "ما لأَحَدٍ بهؤلاءِ قِبَلٌ ولا طاقةٌ، والله يا أبا الفَضْلِ لقد أصبح مُلْكُ ابن أخيك الغَداةَ عظيماً!"
>
> "No one has any power or strength against these. By Allah, O Abu al-Fadl, the kingship of your nephew has become mighty this morning!"

"I said: إِنَّهَا النُّبُوَّةُ يا أبا سُفْيان, 'It is prophethood, O Abu Sufyan.' He said: فَنَعَمْ إِذَنْ إِنَّهَا النُّبُوَّةُ, 'Then yes, it is prophethood.' Then I said to him: Hasten to your people."

**Abu Sufyan returns to the people of Mecca to warn them**

When Abu Sufyan came to them he cried out at the top of his voice:

> "يا معشر قُرَيْشٍ؛ هذا مُحَمَّدٌ قَدْ جاءَكم فيما لا قِبَلَ لكم به، فمن دخل دار أبي سُفْيان فهو آمِنٌ."
>
> "O assembly of Quraysh! This is Muhammad, who has come upon you with that which you have no power to face. Whoever enters the house of Abu Sufyan is safe."

His wife Hind bint Utbah rose to him, seized him by his moustache, and said:

> "أُقْتُلُوا الْحَمِيْتَ الدَّمِيْمَ الخائِنَ قُبِّحَ مِنْ طَلْعَةِ قَوْمٍ."
>
> "Kill this bloated, ugly traitor! What a foul vanguard of a people he is!"

He said: "Woe to you all! Do not let this woman delude you concerning yourselves, for there has come upon you that which you have no power to face. Whoever enters the house of Abu Sufyan is safe."

They said: "Woe to you! What can your house avail us?"

He said: "And whoever shuts his door upon himself is safe, and whoever enters the mosque is safe."

So the people scattered to their houses and to the mosque.

**The Prophet reaches Dhu Tuwa**

When the Messenger of Allah came to Dhu Tuwa he halted upon his mount, his head wrapped in a length of red Yemeni cloth. He lowered his head in humility before Allah when he saw the honour Allah had bestowed upon him in this conquest, until the tip of his beard almost touched the middle of the saddle.

**The Muslim armies enter Mecca**

When the Messenger of Allah advanced with the gathered Muslims, and Allah had given strength to their victory and turned them toward Mecca, its people looked upon what they had neither power nor strength to meet, so they were humbled and submitted, and asked for pardon and for entry into peace.

The Messenger of Allah came on the day of the entry into Mecca in armies the like of which the Arabs had never seen in number or in equipment. They were sheathed in weapons, so that nothing could be seen of them but the whites of their eyes. He placed the Ansar on the right wing, their banner with Sa'd ibn Ubadah, and the Muhajirun on the left wing, their banner with al-Zubayr ibn al-Awwam, and he said to each of the two: "ادخل من موضع كَدَاء," "Enter from the place of Kada."

**The fear of the Muhajirun for Quraysh on account of Sa'd ibn Ubadah**

The Messenger of Allah was in the main body of the Muhajirun and the Ansar and the rest of the people, and with each people of the Arab tribes was a great number. Umar ibn al-Khattab heard Sa'd ibn Ubadah, the banner in his hand, as he was about to enter Mecca, saying:

> اليَوْمَ يَوْمُ الْمَلْحَمَهْ
> الْيَوْمَ تُسْتَحَلُّ الْحُرْمَةْ
>
> *Today is the day of slaughter,*
> *Today the sanctuary is made lawful.*

Umar came to the Messenger of Allah and reported his words to him, and the Messenger of Allah said to Ali ibn Abi Talib (ع):

> "إِذْهَبْ فَخُذِ الرَّايَةَ مِنْهُ، وَكُنْ أَنْتَ الَّذِي تَدْخُلُ بِهَا."
>
> "Go and take the banner from him, and be the one who enters with it."

So he did. And Ali (ع) was the place of the Messenger's war and the place of his peace.

**The Messenger's command concerning a number of men he named**

The Messenger of Allah commanded the leaders of the battalions not to kill any except those who fought them, save a number of men whom he named for them. He ordered that these be killed even if they were found beneath the coverings of the Kaaba, on account of the enormity of the crimes that were theirs. Yet many of them were spared by those who met them, among men who had prior acquaintance with them and concern for them, and some sought protection for others, and they were bold with the Messenger of Allah in bringing his command concerning them back to him.

**The account of Abdullah ibn Sa'd**

Among them was Abdullah ibn Sa'd, of the brethren of Banu Amir ibn Lu'ayy. His crime was the gravest of theirs, and the Messenger of Allah was more severe in anger against him than any other. His name had been the first among those whose blood was declared forfeit that day, and he said: "Kill him even if you find him beneath the coverings of the Kaaba." That was because he had entered Islam, and the Messenger of Allah had taken him as a scribe, and he used to write the revelation for him. The Messenger of Allah would dictate to him "غَفُورٌ رَّحِيْمٌ," "Forgiving, Merciful," and he would write "عَزِيزٌ حَكِيْمٌ," "Mighty, Wise," and the like of that.

Then he apostatized as a disbeliever and joined the polytheists, and said to them:

> "لقد أنزلتُ قرآناً، وأثبتّه عن نفسي!"
>
> "I have sent down a Quran and set it down out of myself!"

Concerning him Allah, exalted be He, revealed:

> ﴿وَمَنْ قَالَ سَأَنْزِلُ مِثْلَ مَا أَنْزَلَ اللهُ﴾ [الأنعام: ٩٣]
>
> "And whoever says, 'I shall send down the like of what Allah has sent down.'" [al-An'am: 93]

He took refuge with Uthman ibn Affan, who brought him concealed until he entered with him upon the Messenger of Allah and asked him for his life. The Messenger of Allah turned away from him repeatedly and was silent, giving him no answer. Uthman pressed him, and he set him free.

Then he said to the Muslims present with him:

> "لَقَدْ صَمَتُّ طَوِيْلاً لَعَلَّ أَحَدَكُمْ يَقُومُ إِلَيْهِ فَيَضْرِبَ عُنُقَهُ، كَمِثْلِ مَا أَمَرْتُ، فَمَا فَعَلْتُمْ."
>
> "I stayed silent a long while, hoping that one of you would rise to him and strike his neck, according to what I had commanded, yet you did nothing."

They said: "O Messenger of Allah, if only you had signalled to us with something of that."

He said:

> "إِنَّ النَّبِيَّ لاَ يَقْتُلُ بِالإِشَارَةِ."
>
> "A prophet does not kill by a signal."

**The account of al-Huwayrith ibn Nuqaydh**

Ali ibn Abi Talib (ع) met al-Huwayrith ibn Nuqaydh, who was among those whose blood the Messenger of Allah had declared forfeit that day. There was acquaintance between al-Huwayrith and Ali (ع). Ali (ع) said to him: "يا عدوّ الله؛ أنتَ هاهنا!" "O enemy of Allah, are you here!" Al-Huwayrith said to him: "أَبْقِ عَلَيَّ يا ابن أبي طالِب," "Spare me, O son of Abu Talib." Ali (ع) said: "لا أَبْقَى الله عَلَيَّ إِنْ أَبْقَيْتُ عليكَ," "May Allah not spare me if I spare you." And Ali (ع) killed him.

**Umm Hani grants protection to two men**

Then he (ع) entered upon his sister Umm Hani bint Abi Talib.
