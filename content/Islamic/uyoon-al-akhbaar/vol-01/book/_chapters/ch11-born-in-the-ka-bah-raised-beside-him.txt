# Born in the Ka'bah, Raised Beside Him

When Fatima bint Asad conceived the Commander of the Faithful, Ali ibn Abi Talib (ع), she saw in her sleep as though a pillar of iron had been wrenched from the crown of her head, and then blazed as a light in the air until it reached the sky, and then was returned to her and stood a while beside her, and was drawn out from her foot. She said, "What is this?" And it was said to her:

«هذا قاتل أهل الكفر، وصاحب ميثاق أهل النَّصْر، بأسه شديد؛ تجزع مِنْ صولته الجنود، وهو معونة الله لنبيّه، وتأييده على عدوِّه، فاز بحبّه الفائزون، وسعد به السُّعداء المُتّقون، وهو مَثَل في السَّماء المرفوعة، والأرض الموضوعة، والجبال الشَّامخة، والبحار الزَّاخرة، والنُّجوم الزَّاهرة»

"This is the slayer of the people of unbelief, the holder of the covenant of the people of victory. His might is severe; armies quail before his onset. He is God's aid to His Prophet and His support against His enemy. By loving him the successful have succeeded, and through him the godfearing have been made happy. He is a token in the raised heaven, and the spread earth, and the towering mountains, and the swelling seas, and the shining stars."

When the pangs of birth came upon her, her husband Abu Talib bade her circle the Ka'bah, and wipe her hand within it, and pour out such supplications as she was able. She did so, and gave birth to him inside the Ka'bah. She had meant to leave, but the labour came swiftly upon her. Then she carried him home and turned to his rearing.

God cast love for him into the heart of His Prophet Muhammad, who was in those days in the fullness of his youth and the beauty of his splendour, and he said to Fatima bint Asad, addressing her as he was accustomed to address her:

«يا أُمّه، اجعلي مهد عليّ إلى جانبي»

"O my mother, set Ali's cradle beside mine."

And he himself undertook the greater part of his upbringing, until the boy grew and became strong, and his guidance was made plain and he was rightly directed.

The House had fallen into ruin, and the Quraysh set about rebuilding it. The Messenger of Allah was in those days a youth who had grown up upon purity and the character of the prophets, and they called him al-Amin, the Trustworthy. When the builders came to the place of the Black Stone, every clan of the Quraysh desired to be the one to lift it to its place, and over this they differed and quarrelled. Then they agreed among themselves to submit the matter to the judgment of the first man who should come upon them.

It was the Messenger of Allah who came, and they said, «هذا الأمين قد طلع» "The Trustworthy one has appeared." They told him the matter, and he drew off his cloak and laid the stone upon it and said:

«ليأخذ مِنْ كل بطن من قُرَيش رَجُل بحاشِية الثّوبِ، وارفعوه معاً»

"Let one man from every clan of the Quraysh take hold of the edge of the garment, and lift it together."

His ruling pleased them and satisfied them, and they did as he said, until they had brought it to its place, and the Messenger of Allah set it in its position with his own hand. The Quraysh named him al-Amin for what had become manifest to them of his excellence, his trustworthiness, and his continence.

When the Messenger of Allah reached fourteen years and entered his fifteenth, the war of al-Fijar flared up between the Quraysh with those of Kinana who were with them, and Qays ibn Aylan. It was named the war of al-Fijar because these two parties made lawful in it what was sacred between them.

The Messenger of Allah was present at some of those battles when he was twenty years of age, and he said:

«كنتُ يومئذٍ أُنَبِّل على أَعْمامِي»

"On that day I was passing arrows to my uncles," meaning that he returned upon their enemy the arrows they shot at them.

When he was present, the Quraysh and Kinana prevailed, and they came to know this, so they would ask his uncle Abu Talib to bring him out with them. The war of al-Fijar has a long account, here set down in brief.

Khadijah bint Khuwaylid ibn Asad ibn Abd al-Uzza ibn Qusayy ibn Kilab ibn Murrah ibn Ka'b ibn Lu'ayy ibn Ghalib ibn Fihr beheld the excellence of the Messenger of Allah, and what appeared of his proofs and was made plain of his signs, and she rejoiced at that. Maysarah her servant had gone out with him on some of his journeys, and had seen the cloud shading him, and had heard the monks speak of his excellence and tell of the nearness of his mission.

Khadijah was a woman of resolve and understanding, noble among her people. She sent to the Messenger of Allah and said:

«يا ابن عمِّي، إِنِّي قد رغبت فيك لقرابتك، وفضلك في قومك، وأمانتك، وحسن خلقك، وصدق حديثك»

"O son of my uncle, I have desired you for your kinship, and your excellence among your people, and your trustworthiness, and the beauty of your character, and the truthfulness of your speech."

Then she offered herself to him in marriage. She was in that day the noblest of the women of Quraysh in lineage, and the greatest of them in honour and descent. Abu Talib came forward and asked for her hand on behalf of the Messenger of Allah.

The scholars of biography said: Abu Talib was present at the contract, together with the chief men of Banu Hashim, and the notables of the Quraysh, and the uncles of the Messenger of Allah. And Abu Talib rose and delivered the address.
