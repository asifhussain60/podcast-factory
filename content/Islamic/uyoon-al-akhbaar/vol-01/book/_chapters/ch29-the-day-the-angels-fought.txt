# The Day the Angels Fought

The Muslims left the Messenger of Allah, may Allah's peace and blessings be upon him, in the field shelter. They said: "If we are victorious, we will remain by your side." Abu Bakr stayed with him and did not fight.

It is narrated from al-Baqir Muhammad ibn 'Ali, peace be upon him, that he said: The Messenger of Allah, may Allah's peace and blessings be upon him and his family, arranged the ranks of his companions on the day of Badr and ordered them that no one should leave his position. If anyone was pressed by the polytheists, he was to fall back to his place and regroup with his fellows.

The Messenger of Allah, may Allah's peace and blessings be upon him, said to his companions: "Rejoice, for here is Gabriel, leading a horse by the halter, fully armed."

The Messenger of Allah, may Allah's peace and blessings be upon him, went among the people and urged them on. He said: "By Him in Whose hand is Muhammad's soul, no man who fights them this day, killing patiently in God's cause, will fail to enter Paradise."

The Muslims held their positions and stood firm. Then the Messenger of Allah, may Allah's peace and blessings be upon him, took a handful of pebbles, flung them toward the Quraysh, and said: "May those faces be disfigured." Then he gave the order to advance, saying to his companions: "Press forward." And the rout came.

'Ali and Hamza, may Allah be pleased with them, fought magnificently. The people witnessed 'Ali, peace be upon him, enduring hardship and difficulty, holding firm against adversity, in a manner they could scarcely believe came from any human being, given his age.

The polytheists were routed, and Allah granted the Muslims victory through slaying and capture.

'Ali ibn Abi Talib, peace be upon him, slew: al-'Aas ibn Sa'id ibn al-'Aas ibn Umayyah, and 'Uqbah ibn Abi Mu'ayt of Banu Umayyah ibn 'Abd Shams. And 'Amir ibn 'Abd Allah, of Banu Annar ibn Baghidh, an ally of theirs. And Ta'imah ibn 'Adi ibn Nawfal. Nuqal ibn Huwaylid ibn Asad — he is the son of al-'Adawiyyah of 'Adi Khuza'ah — and he was the one who had bound Abu Bakr ibn Abi Quhafa and Talhah ibn 'Ubayd Allah when they became Muslim, binding them together with a rope. He was one of the devils of Quraysh. Al-'Arr ibn al-Harith of Banu 'Abd al-Dar — 'Ali killed him in single combat before the Messenger of Allah, may Allah's peace and blessings be upon him and his family. And 'Umayr ibn 'Uthman ibn Tayyim ibn Murrah.

And Harmalah ibn 'Amr, an ally of Banu Makhzum. And Mas'ud ibn Abi Umayya ibn al-Mughirah al-Makhzumi. And Abu Qays ibn al-Fakah ibn al-Mughirah, of Banu Makhzum. And 'Abd Allah ibn al-Mundhir ibn Abi Rifa'ah. And Hajib ibn al-Sa'ib.

And Abu al-'As ibn Umayya ibn al-Hajjaj al-Sahmi. And Aws ibn Mu'ir ibn Luthan ibn Jumaah. And Mu'awiya ibn 'Amir, of 'Abd al-Qays, an ally of theirs.

We have already mentioned that al-Waleed ibn 'Utbah was slain in single combat — he is counted among those killed specifically by 'Ali, peace be upon him. 'Ali and Hamza ibn 'Abd al-Muttalib, peace be upon them, shared in the killing of 'Utbah ibn Rabi'ah — as has been noted — together with 'Ubaydah ibn al-Harith, may Allah be pleased with him.

Hamza and 'Ali, peace be upon them, and Zayd ibn Haritha shared in the killing of Hanzalah ibn Abi Sufyan. Zama'ah ibn al-Aswad ibn al-Muttalib shared with 'Ali and Hamza, peace be upon them, in killing his man.

'Aqeel ibn al-Aswad ibn al-Muttalib also shared with 'Ali and Hamza, peace be upon them, in a killing.

Shaybah ibn Rabi'ah, as we have mentioned, was killed by Hamza ibn 'Abd al-Muttalib, peace be upon him, in single combat.

Abu Qays ibn al-Waleed ibn al-Mughirah was killed by Hamza, peace be upon him; some say by 'Ali ibn Abi Talib, peace be upon him.

Al-Aswad ibn 'Abd al-Asad al-Makhzumi was killed by Hamza ibn 'Abd al-Muttalib, peace be upon him.

Nabeeh ibn al-Hajjaj ibn 'Amir was killed by Hamza ibn 'Abd al-Muttalib, peace be upon him; some say that Sa'd ibn Abi Waqqas shared in killing him.

Al-Harith ibn al-Hadrami was killed by 'Ammar ibn Yasir, may Allah be pleased with him.

'Umayr ibn Abi 'Umayr and his son — two mawali of Banu 'Abd Shams — were killed by Salim, the mawla of Abu Hudhayfa.

'Ubaydah ibn Sa'id ibn al-'As ibn Umayyah was killed by al-Zubayr ibn al-'Awwam.

Al-Harith ibn 'Amir ibn Nawfal ibn 'Abd Manaf was killed by Khubaib ibn Issaf, brother of Banu al-Harith ibn al-Khazraj.

Al-Harith ibn Zama'ah ibn al-Aswad was killed by 'Ammar ibn Yasir.

Abu al-Bakhtari — who was al-'As ibn Hisham — was killed by al-Mujdhir ibn Dhiyad al-Balawi.

Uzayd ibn Malis was killed by Bilal ibn Rabah, along with Zayd, an ally of Banu 'Abd al-Dar; some say al-Miqdad ibn al-Aswad killed him.

'Uthman ibn Malik ibn Ka'b was killed by Suhayb ibn Sinan.

Abu Jahl ibn Hisham — whose name was 'Amr ibn Hisham ibn al-Mughirah ibn 'Abd Allah ibn 'Amr ibn Makhzum — was killed jointly by Mu'adh ibn 'Amr ibn al-Jamuh, Mu'awwidh ibn 'Afra', and 'Abd Allah ibn Mas'ud.

Al-'As ibn Hisham was killed by 'Umar ibn al-Khattab.

Yazid ibn 'Abd Allah, an ally of Banu Makhzum who was known for his bravery, was killed by 'Ammar ibn Yasir.

Abu Musafi' al-Ash'ari, an ally of theirs, was killed by Abu Dajana al-Sa'idi.

Rifa'ah ibn Abi Rifa'ah al-Makhzumi was killed by Sa'd ibn al-Rabi', brother of Banu al-Harith ibn al-Khazraj.

Al-Mundhir ibn Abi Rifa'ah was killed by Ma'an ibn 'Adi, an ally of Ibn 'Ubayd Allah ibn Zayd ibn Malik ibn 'Amr ibn 'Awf.

Al-Sa'ib ibn Abi al-Sa'ib al-Makhzumi was killed by al-Zubayr ibn al-'Awwam.

And 'Uwaymar ibn al-Sa'ib was killed by al-Nu'man ibn Malik al-Qawqali.

'Amr ibn Sufyan was killed by Yazid ibn Ruqaysh.

Jabir ibn Sufyan was killed by Abu Burdah ibn Nayyar.

Munabbih ibn al-Hajjaj ibn 'Amir was killed by Abu al-Yusr, brother of Banu Salamah.

And his son al-'As ibn Munabbih ibn al-Hajjaj was killed by Hamza ibn 'Abd al-Muttalib, with Sa'd ibn Abi Waqqas sharing in the killing.

Abu al-'As ibn Qays ibn 'Adi ibn Sa'd ibn Sahm was killed by 'Ali ibn Abi Talib; some say by al-Nu'man ibn Malik al-Qawqali, and some say by Abu Dajana.

'Asim ibn Abi 'Awf ibn Dhubayrah, of Banu Sahm, was also killed by Abu al-Yusr.

Umayya ibn Khalaf ibn Wahb, of Banu Jumaah, was killed by Mu'adh ibn 'Afra', with Kharijah ibn Zayd and Khubaib ibn Issaf sharing in the killing.

His son 'Ali ibn Umayya was killed by 'Ammar ibn Yasir.

Abu Ma'bad ibn Wahb, an ally of theirs from Banu Kalb, was killed by Khalid and Iyas ibn al-Bukayr; some say by Abu Dajana.

The total count of those killed at Badr from among the polytheists was fifty men. Some say the dead of Badr were seventy men, and likewise the captives were seventy — this is the opinion of Ibn 'Abbas and Sa'id ibn al-Musayyib.

Allah the Exalted says in His Book:

> ﴿ أَوَلَمَّا أَصَابَتْكُم مُّصِيبَةٌ قَدْ أَصَبْتُم مِّثْلَيْهَا ﴾
> "Even when a calamity struck you, though you had struck them with one twice as great." (Al 'Imran: 165)

And that was on a Sunday — he was martyred on a Sunday.

The Muslims numbered seventy men, and Allah says: "You have struck the like of them on the day of Badr." On Sunday, seventy were killed and seventy taken prisoner.

Ka'b ibn Malik sang: Seventy of them were struck down by the stinging snake, and the lions.

On the day of Badr, fourteen Muslims were martyred, six of whom were Muhajireen. They are:

'Ubaydah ibn al-Harith, whose leg was severed and who died of the wound. Amir ibn Abi Waqqas, brother of Sa'd ibn Abi Waqqas. Dhu al-Shamalayn — Umayr — ibn 'Abd 'Amr ibn Nadlah, an ally of Banu Zahra from Khara'ah, then from Bani Ghabshan.

And from Banu 'Adi ibn Ka'b ibn Lu'ay: 'Aqil ibn Abi al-Bakir, who was an ally of theirs, and the freedman of 'Umar ibn al-Khattab. And Safwan ibn Bayda from Bani Fahr.

Eight of the Ansar: From Banu Amr ibn 'Awf: Sa'd ibn Khaythama, and Mubashir ibn 'Abd al-Mundhir ibn Zanbar.

From Banu al-Harith ibn al-Khazraj: Yazid ibn al-Harith and 'Umayr ibn al-Hammam. Rafi' ibn al-Mu'alla, Haritha ibn Saraqa, 'Awf and Mu'awdh ibn al-Harith ibn Rifa'ah — these last two being the sons of Afra' — may Allah have mercy on them all.
