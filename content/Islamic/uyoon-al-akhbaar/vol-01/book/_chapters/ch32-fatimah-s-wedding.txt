# Fatimah's Wedding

After the affair of Badr came the marriage of the Commander of the Faithful, Ali ibn Abi Talib (ع), to Fatimah, the daughter of the Messenger of Allah (صلى الله عليه وعلى آله). Abu Bakr and Umar had each sought her hand, and the Messenger of Allah declined them both. They had heard him mention her with praise and say:

> «فاطِمَة بَضْعَة مِنِّي»
>
> "Fatimah is a part of me."

A number of the companions of the Messenger of Allah spoke of this among themselves and said, "Have you not seen that the Messenger of Allah holds back from giving his daughter Fatimah in marriage?"

Ammar ibn Yasir said, "We do not think he has kept her back for anyone but his cousin, Ali ibn Abi Talib (ع)."

Ali (ع) had not yet married. So Ammar, al-Zubayr ibn al-Awwam, and Sa'd ibn Ubadah said, "Let us speak to Ali about this."

They came to him, and Ammar said to him, "We see no one among those who emigrated to Medina but that a son has been born to him after his emigration, except you."

Ammar said further, "If the only thing that has kept you from this is the little wealth in your hand, then here is my wealth; take from it whatever you wish."

Ali (ع) said to him, "O Abu al-Yaqzan, what Allah granted me as spoil at Badr I have set aside for this very matter."

They said, "If only you would mention his daughter Fatimah (عليهم السلام) to the Messenger of Allah. We see that he has refused to marry her to any of his companions, and we do not think he has kept her back for anyone but you."

Ali (ع) said, "Fatimah is a handmaid among the handmaids of Allah, and her affair is in the hand of Allah. I have no tongue that turns in my mouth to mention her to the Messenger of Allah, for I am shy before him."

They said to him, "And who has a better right to her than you? We ourselves will mention her to the Messenger of Allah."

Then they returned to the Messenger of Allah and spoke with him. Gabriel (ع) descended upon the Prophet (صلى الله عليه وعلى آله) and said:

> «إنَّ الله سبحانه يأمرك أن تُجيبهم إلى ما سألوك، فإِنِّي قد زوَّجت فاطِمَة من عليّ في وقت ما زُوِّجَتْ حَوَّاءُ من آدم، وكان ذلك في قَضائي، وقد سَبَقَ به عِلْمي في خِيرَتي مِنْ خَلْقِي»
>
> "Allah, glory be to Him, commands you to answer them in what they have asked of you, for I have wedded Fatimah to Ali at the very hour that Eve was wedded to Adam. That was in My decree, and My knowledge of it went before, concerning the chosen of My creation."

The Messenger of Allah smiled at this and said, "Allah, exalted is He, has answered you in what you asked. Go, O Ammar, and bring me Ali."

Ammar said: "I came to Ali (ع), and found him bowing and prostrating, entreating Allah, mighty and majestic, in the matter of Fatimah (عليهم السلام). I said, 'O my master, receive glad tidings, be of good heart, let your eye be cooled, and answer the Messenger of Allah.'"

So Ali (ع) came to the Messenger of Allah, and when he entered upon him, the Prophet said to him, "O Ali, was it from your own mind that al-Zubayr spoke, and Sa'd and Ammar?"

He said, "Yes, O Messenger of Allah."

The Prophet (صلى الله عليه وعلى آله) said, "O Ali, Gabriel has told me that Allah has bestowed Fatimah upon you and bestowed you upon her, and has joined her to you and joined you to her. What have you by way of dowry?"

He said, "Whatever my hand possesses."

The Messenger of Allah said, "And what is that?"

Ali (ع) said, "What Allah granted me as spoil at Badr, which I set aside for this: twenty ounces of gold and silver, two fine watering camels, two black slaves, my horse for single combat, and my coat of mail for meeting my enemy."

The Messenger of Allah (صلى الله عليه وعلى آله) said, "O Ali, let not the two fine camels go out of your hand, nor the two black slaves; do not come down from your horse, and do not part with anything of your weapons. Bring me instead what you have of gold and silver."

Then the Messenger of Allah contracted for him the marriage of Fatimah (عليهم السلام). The Prophet (صلى الله عليه وعلى آله) gave orders, and a ram was bought for him, with clarified fat, flour, and Ajwa dates, and food was prepared. Then he ordered a crier, who called out through the quarters of Medina that the people should answer the Messenger of Allah, and the people gathered to him.

Those who ate were counted, and they reached one thousand six hundred, yet the food remained as it was, nothing of it diminished, through the blessing of the Messenger of Allah (صلى الله عليه وعلى آله) and his excellence.

The Messenger of Allah heard the beating of a tambourine and said, "What is this?"

Asma' bint Umays said, "O Messenger of Allah, I wished to gladden Fatimah, so that she would not say within herself: my mother has died, and I find no one to bring me joy."

He said, "O Asma', what is it that you are saying?"

She said, "I do not know what to say, O Messenger of Allah."

He said, "Say:

> «أَتَيْنَاكُمْ أَتَيْنَاكُمْ فَحَيُّونَا تُحَيِّيَكُمْ»
>
> 'We have come to you, we have come to you, so greet us and we shall greet you,'

and the like of that, and do not say anything unseemly."

Then he (صلى الله عليه وعلى آله) said:

> «أَفْرَحَكِ اللهُ يَا أَسْماءُ كَمَا أَفْرَحْتِ ابْنِي»
>
> "May Allah gladden you, O Asma', as you have gladdened my child."

The Messenger of Allah (صلى الله عليه وعلى آله) then gave orders that Fatimah be led in procession to Ali (ع).

By the chain of al-Mufaddal ibn Muhammad, from Ibn Abbas, who said: "When Fatimah (عليهم السلام) was led in procession to Ali (ع), the Messenger of Allah (صلى الله عليه وعلى آله) was before her, Gabriel on her right and Michael on her left, and seventy thousand angels escorted her from behind, glorifying Allah, mighty and majestic, and declaring His holiness until the dawn broke."

From Asma' bint Umays, who said: "I was among those who led Fatimah (عليهم السلام) in procession to Ali (ع). When she entered her house, the Messenger of Allah (صلى الله عليه وعلى آله) came forward until he entered upon her. He called for water and mentioned the name of Allah over it, then drank from it, and sprinkled some of the water from his mouth between Fatimah's shift and her body, and sprinkled some of it likewise between Ali's garment and his body.

Then he said:

> «اللَّهُمَّ احْفَظ أهل هذا البيت، وبارِك فيهم، وبارِك عليهم، واجْعَلْهِم مُبارَكِيْنَ أَيْنَ كانوا»
>
> 'O Allah, guard the people of this house, bless them and bless upon them, and make them blessed wherever they may be.'

Then he said:

> «جَزَى اللهُ أَسْمَاءَ وَصُوَيْحِبَاتَهَا خَيْراً»
>
> 'May Allah reward Asma' and her companions with good.'"

From Ahmad ibn Muhammad al-Tabari, by his chain, from Anas ibn Malik, who said: "I asked my mother about the appearance of Fatimah (عليهم السلام), and she said: White, radiantly white, as though she were the moon on the night of its fullness, or the sun when it comes out from behind the clouds."

The virtues of Ali and Fatimah (عليهم السلام) are many and well known. It is narrated of the Prophet (صلى الله عليه وعلى آله) that when he married Fatimah to Ali (عليهم السلام), he said:

> «اليوم ازدوج الإِيمان والإسلام»
>
> "Today faith and Islam have been wedded together."

The marriage of Ali took place after the affair of Badr, though it has also been said that it was after the affair of Uhud.
