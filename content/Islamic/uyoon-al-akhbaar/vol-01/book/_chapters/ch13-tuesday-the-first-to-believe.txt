# Tuesday: The First to Believe

Ali (ع) was reared in the house of the Messenger of Allah, who kept him and brought him up, forming him in purity and piety. It was the custom among the nobles of the Arabs, the men of standing among them, that when a son grew toward manhood and his father wished him disciplined and rightly formed, the father would hand him over to one of the honored men of his people, who would take charge of him and employ him in what would straighten him, so that no indulgence might spoil him.

Abu Talib had among his children Talib, Aqeel, Ja'far, and Ali (ع), and between each of them and the one who followed him there were ten years. He gave Aqeel to al-Abbas, and Ja'far to Hamza (رض), and he gave Ali to the Messenger of Allah. It is also said that he kept Aqeel with himself and said: leave Aqeel to me, and take whom you wish.

So Ali (ع) remained with the Messenger of Allah and grew up beside him in purity. He worshipped no idol and bore no burden of sin; rather he came of age in purity and piety, keeping far from what is forbidden and shameful. His character was the character of the Messenger of Allah, and his conduct was the Prophet's conduct. Khadija knew the love the Messenger of Allah bore for Ali (ع), and so she honored him and held him in high regard.

Then Allah, Exalted be He, chose His Prophet Muhammad, master of mankind, seal of the warners, and best of the children of Adam (ع). He raised him as a Prophet and sent him as a guide rightly guided. When the revelation came to him and Gabriel (ع) descended upon him with the message, he called Khadija to Islam on the day he was sent, and that was on the twenty-seventh of the month of Rajab. She accepted Islam at once, bearing witness to Allah, Exalted be He, in His oneness, and to Muhammad in his messengership. That was a Monday.

The Messenger of Allah then called Ali ibn Abi Talib (ع) on Tuesday, the day after, and informed him of what Allah had singled him out for in calling to Him, and that He had sent him to all people. Ali said: "By my father and my mother, grant me respite for an hour of my day." The Messenger of Allah said:

> «أَنَا أُنْظِرْكَ مَا شِئْتَ؛ وَلَكِنْ مَا قُلْتُ لَكَ بِأَمَانَةٍ عِنْدَكَ لا يَطَّلِعُ عَلَيْهِ غَيْرُكَ»

"I grant you respite as long as you wish; but what I have said to you is a trust with you, of which none but you shall learn."

Then Ali (ع) said: "I only wished to put the matter first before my father. But since you have said what you said, then

> «أَشْهَدُ أَنْ لاَ إِلهَ إِلَّا اللهُ وَأَنَّكَ رَسُولُ اللهِ»

I bear witness that there is no god but Allah and that you are the Messenger of Allah."

So the prophethood of Muhammad was on a Monday, and the Islam of Ali (ع) was on Tuesday, the second day. He is, among the men, the foremost of the foremost, and the first of the believers who affirmed the truth. This is a matter of consensus among the whole community, which no denier denies and no opponent contests.

The most that anyone says who wishes to push aside the merit of Ali (ع) and to hide what Allah made plain in him is to say: "His Islam came before he reached the age of discretion, and such a thing is not counted as Islam." Yet such people forget the word of Allah, Exalted be He, concerning Yahya ibn Zakariyya (ع):

> ﴿يَا يَحْيَى خُذِ الْكِتَابَ بِقُوَّةٍ وَآتَيْنَاهُ الْحُكْمَ صَبِيًّا﴾ [مريم: ١٢]

"O Yahya, take the Book with strength. And We gave him wisdom while yet a child."

And His word concerning the Messiah (ع):

> ﴿وَيُكَلِّمُ النَّاسَ فِي الْمَهْدِ﴾ [آل عمران: ٤٦]

"And he shall speak to the people in the cradle."

That is said of prophethood itself. How much more, then, of Islam and faith, which stand below that degree and are lesser than that station? Their objection is nothing but a denial of the merit of Allah's friends and an ingratitude for His favor, since they have rejected what is plain to the eye and pushed away the proof.

The argument here runs long, and this is not the place to set it out; whoever wishes to search into it will find it in many composed books and compiled disputations. It has been related from Abdullah ibn Umar ibn al-Khattab, who is among those whose word they themselves accept, that he said:

> «إذا بلغ الصَّبِيُّ سبع سنين كُتِبَ لإيمانِهِ وكُفْرِهِ»

"When a boy reaches seven years, his faith and his disbelief are written down for him."

The proofs on this matter run long and wide and pass beyond the limit of this book. A little suffices for one who lets his reason judge, deals fairly with himself, follows the word of Allah his Lord, does not aim at his own desire, and does not let hatred make him partisan against one whom Allah chose and approved.

Ali (ع) remained for a number of years praying with the Messenger of Allah, the two of them alone. They would pray in the ravines of Mecca with no one else beside them, and they continued so for eight years, while there was not upon the face of the earth anyone who held to the religion of Islam except the two of them and Khadija bint Khuwaylid (رض).

Abu Talib came upon them and said to the Messenger of Allah: "What is this that you are doing, son of my brother?" He said:

> «يَا عَمِّ؛ هَذَا دِيْنُ اللهِ أَرْسَلَنِي بِهِ، وَهَذِهِ مِلَّةُ أَبِيْنَا إِبْرَاهِيْمَ، وَأَنْتَ يَا عَمِّ أَحَقُّ مَنْ بَدَأْتُ لَهُ بِالنَّصِيْحَةِ وَدَعَوْتُهُ إِلَى دِيْنِ اللهِ، وَأَنْتَ أَحَقُّ مَنْ أَجَابَ إِلَيْهِ وَأَعَانَ عَلَيْهِ»

"O uncle, this is the religion of Allah with which He has sent me, and this is the creed of our father Ibrahim. You, uncle, are the most deserving of those with whom I should begin in counsel and whom I should call to the religion of Allah, and you are the most deserving of those who should answer it and lend it aid."

The narrators hold that he said to him:

> «أَمَّا مُفَارَقَةُ مَا أَنَا عَلَيْهِ فَمَا لِي إِلَى ذَلِكَ مِنْ سَبِيْلٍ، وَأَمَّا عَوْنُكَ فَوَاللهِ لاَ يَخْلُصُ إِلَيْكَ أَحَدٌ بِشَيْءٍ تَكْرَهُهُ مَا بَقِيْتُ»

"As for parting from what I am upon, I have no way to that. But as for helping you, by Allah, no one shall reach you with anything you dislike so long as I remain."

And he said to Ali (ع):

> «اتَّبِعْ مَا يَقُولُ لَكَ مُحَمَّدٌ، وَلاَ تُفَارِقْهُ، وَالْزَمْهُ فَإِنَّهُ لَمْ يَدْعُكَ إِلاَّ إِلَى دِيْنِ اللهِ»

"Follow what Muhammad says to you, and do not part from him, and keep close to him, for he has called you to nothing but the religion of Allah."

It has been related from Abu Uthman, the judge of Mosul, by a chain going back to Abu Ayyub al-Ansari, that he said:

"I heard the Messenger of Allah say: I prayed, I and Ali ibn Abi Talib, for seven years, and that was because no male believed before him. That is the word of Allah, Mighty and Majestic:

> ﴿الَّذِينَ يَحْمِلُونَ الْعَرْشَ وَمَنْ حَوْلَهُ يُسَبِّحُونَ بِحَمْدِ رَبِّهِمْ وَيُؤْمِنُونَ بِهِ وَيَسْتَغْفِرُونَ﴾ [غافر: ٧]

'Those who bear the Throne and those around it glorify their Lord with praise, and believe in Him, and seek forgiveness' — for those on the earth. And that was for me and for Ali and for Khadija bint Khuwaylid, and then for whoever believed afterward."

It is also related that Abu Talib passed by the Messenger of Allah while Ali (ع) was with him and the two of them were praying, and Ja'far ibn Abi Talib was with his father. Abu Talib turned to his son Ja'far al-Tayyar (ع) and said: "Go and complete the wing of your cousin." So Ja'far (رض) came to the Messenger of Allah and accepted Islam and prayed with the two of them (عليهم السلام), and that was the first prayer the Messenger of Allah prayed in congregation.

It has been narrated from Ali ibn Abi Talib (ع) that he said:

"When Allah, Mighty and Majestic, sent down upon His Messenger:

> ﴿وَأَنْذِرْ عَشِيْرَتَكَ الأَقْرَبِيْنَ﴾ [الشعراء: ٢١٤]

'And warn your nearest kinsfolk,' the Messenger of Allah gathered the Banu Abd al-Muttalib over the haunch of a sheep and a cup of milk. Among them that day were ten, not one of whom but would eat a whole young sheep and drink a farq of milk by himself, and they were some forty-odd men. Yet they ate until they were full and drank until they were satisfied. Among them that day was Abu Lahab. Then the Messenger of Allah said to them:

> «يَا بَنِي عَبْدِ الْمُطَّلِبِ؛ أَطِيْعُونِي تَكُونُوا مُلُوكَ الأَرْضِ وَحُكَّامَهَا، إِنَّ اللهَ لَمْ يَبْعَثْ نَبِيًّا إِلاَّ جَعَلَ لَهُ وَصِيًّا وَوَزِيْرًا وَوَلِيًّا وَوَارِثًا وَأَخًا، فَأَيُّكُمْ يَكُونُ أَخِي وَوَصِيِّي وَوَزِيْرِي وَوَارِثِي؟»

'O sons of Abd al-Muttalib, obey me and you shall be the kings of the earth and its rulers. Allah has sent no prophet without appointing for him a legatee, a minister, a guardian, an heir, and a brother. So which of you shall be my brother, my legatee, my minister, and my heir?'

They were silent. He offered it to them man by man, and there was not one among them who would say it or accept it."

Ali (ع) said: "Until there remained none of them but me, and I that day was the youngest of them in age. He offered it to me, and I said: Yes, I, O Messenger of Allah. He said:

> «نَعَمْ؛ أَنْتَ يَا عَلِيُّ أَخِي وَوَصِيِّي وَوَزِيْرِي وَوَارِثِي»

'Yes, you, Ali, are my brother, my legatee, my minister, and my heir.' And he said to them:

> «اسْمَعُوا لَهُ وَأَطِيْعُوهُ»

'Listen to him and obey him.'

Then, when they had left, Abu Lahab said to them:

> «لَوْ لَمْ تَسْتَدِلُّوا عَلَى سِحْرِ صَاحِبِكُمْ إِلاَّ بِمَا رَأَيْتُمْ: أَتَاكُمْ بِفَخْذِ شَاةٍ وَقَدَحٍ مِنْ لَبَنٍ فَأَشْبَعَكُمْ وَرَوِيْتُمْ»

'If you had no evidence of your companion's sorcery but what you have seen, it would be enough: he brought you the haunch of a sheep and a cup of milk, and he filled you and quenched your thirst.'

And they began to mock, saying to Abu Talib: 'Your son has been set above you today.'"
