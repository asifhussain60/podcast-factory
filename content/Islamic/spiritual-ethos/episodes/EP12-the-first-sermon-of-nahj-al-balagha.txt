# The First Sermon Of Nahj Al Balagha

## Opening directive
Target a 50 to 60 minute in-depth conversation. Open with a warm one-sentence welcome: greet the listener, name the book — Reza Shah-Kazemi's *Spiritual Ethos* — and say we let the Imam's own first sermon — placed first in the collection — sound whole, opening not on law but on the negation of every attribute of God. Then land the spine verbatim: "The perfection of sincerity toward Him is to divest Him of all attributes." Preview: why it begins on negation, the sermon heard whole, and the arc from the unbounded God down to a world and up to the Book. Repeat the spine verbatim at pivot and close.
Forbidden first words: Exactly, Yeah, Right, Of course, Absolutely, Wow, "great point"; no faux-profound line.

## Name discipline
Ali → "the Imam"; first mention "the Imam, peace be upon him", then "the Imam"; honorific once only.
The Prophet Muhammad → first mention "the Prophet, peace and blessings of Allah be upon him and his family", then "the Prophet".
Adam → "Adam"; the fallen angel → "Iblis"; the grandson → "the fourth Imam". Other witnesses in English roles only: the compiler / an Ismaili philosopher / an early Sufi master. Never speak Arabic book titles: "the collection", "his book of supplications". Concepts in English: oneness, negation, the Real, the divine attributes. Never speak the forbidden pairing of the leadership-title and the personal name.

## Pronunciation
Say each term ONCE. Never say the original spelling and the English form back-to-back.
- Iblis: Iblis

## Verbatim Recitation
Introduce the sermon in one sentence. Then Host A reads it aloud word-for-word from the source section titled "The First Sermon" — no paraphrase, no summary. If long, break only at paragraph boundaries and keep the exact wording. Only AFTER the full recitation does discussion begin.

## Three-part focus
Beat 1 — Crisis: the sermon uses speech to say speech can deliver nothing of God; it praises by confessing that praise fails. Let the listener feel the paradox, and why the book of counsel opens here, not on law. Spine verbatim.
Beat 2 — Failed reading A: that divesting God of attributes denies Him mercy, power, life. Steelman, then correct — only the pictured, bounded quality is denied.
Beat 3 — Failed reading B: that stripping every attribute leaves God as bare nothing. Steelman, then correct — negation is the near side of affirmation: there is no god but God.
Beat 4 — Pivot: He is Being but not through coming-to-be, with every thing but not by association — affirm, then strip the limit; a God bound by nothing gives the world as sheer gift. Spine verbatim.
Beat 5 — Correction: the sea churned like a beaten milk-skin into the seven heavens; angels who adore by refusing to picture Him; Adam quickened by the breath; the pride of Iblis.
Beat 6 — Stakes and close: the prophets in one chain to the Prophet and the Book; the arc returns the creature to the knowledge of the One. End here, no pre-announcing. Spine a third time, then turn the listener toward one idol of mind their own worship still serves this week.

## Host dynamic
Host A (male, scholar) leads and earns each claim from the sermon; Host B (female, seeker) challenges at least 3 times and concedes once — genuine friction, one real concession.
Sample friction: "I don't buy that yet — if no word can reach God, how is the whole sermon not self-defeating?"; "That sounds like wordplay — isn't stripping every attribute just a fancy way of saying God is nothing?"

## Tone constraints
Reverent and grand; let the sermon's cadence carry. Exactly three governing analogies, all source-images; no others.
Analogy 1 — the ascending ladder: knowledge, belief, oneness, sincerity, divesting every attribute.
Analogy 2 — the testimony "there is no god but God": the sweeping-away, then the confessing.
Analogy 3 — the sea beaten like a milk-skin: chaos churned until its froth is lifted into the heavens.

## Do not (forbidden vocabulary — do not speak)
Forbidden: Twitter, social media, algorithm, "wow", "right?". No faux-profound opener, no "and that, ultimately, is what it means" close, no AI filler. Do not reference any other episode or part of the book; end on this chapter's content without pre-announcing what comes next.
