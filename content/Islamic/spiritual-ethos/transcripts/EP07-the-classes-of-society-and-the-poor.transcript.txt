All the governor's justice, the fierce gentleness of the army, the incorruptibility of the judges, the self-forgetting care for the poor who can give absolutely nothing in return, it all has to be drawn from somewhere. It cannot be manufactured by sheer human effort alone. It drains away under the immense crushing strain of governance unless it is continually daily replenished. The ruler's justice flows from their prayer. from the quiet unseen remembrance of God that keeps the vertical axis of their soul open and alive. That is the still point where the ruler meets their Lord. This inner sanctum, not the bustling administration of policy, is where the whole of their justice is actually born and sustained. This is onerous for the governors, but God makes it light for those who aspire to the hereafter. We've looked at the wheel and its invisible absol, the vital necessity of giving each diverse class its due, and the absolute unyielding claim of the poor at the very bottom of the spokes. But the true weight of this ancient text does not rest safely in the past. It demands to be carried forward into your life today. When you strive for fairness in your own sphere of influence, whether managing a team, raising a family, or navigating your community, Are you acting as the merchant seeking a return, the slave avoiding A consequence, or the free person acting out of sheer radical gratitude for what you've already been given?
