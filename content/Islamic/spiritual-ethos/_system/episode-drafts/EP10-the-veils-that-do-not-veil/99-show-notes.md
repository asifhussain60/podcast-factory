# Show notes — EP10

**Title:** The Veils That Do Not Veil

**Blurb:** If God is closer than the vein in your own neck, what is left for the world to be but a veil? We follow the Imam's four startling words — the veils do not veil Him — into the logic by which creation both hides and discloses God, the balance of nearness and incomparability that guards the vision from idolatry, and the abundant invocation of the Name that grounds the whole discipline.
