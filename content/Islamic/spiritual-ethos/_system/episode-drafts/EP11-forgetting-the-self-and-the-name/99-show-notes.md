# Show notes — EP11

**Title:** Forgetting the Self, Becoming the Name

**Blurb:** Why would the highest form of remembering God turn out to be a kind of forgetting — and what exactly is the Name a person invokes? This closing episode of the remembrance arc follows the Imam from perfect remembrance to the reality of remembrance, through the extinction the tradition calls fana and the Imam's own fainting in the orchard, to the reversal by which God, not man, is the true invoker. From there it takes up the boldest of the Sufi maxims — the Name is the Named — alongside the Imams' apparent denial of it, and follows the resolution to its end, where the friends of God, effaced in Him, become themselves the most beautiful Names, sealed at the last in the death of Ali (ع), with the invocation still moving on his tongue.
