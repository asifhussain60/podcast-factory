# Show notes — EP12

**Title:** The First Sermon of Nahj al-Balagha

**Blurb:** Why does the greatest collection of Ali (ع)'s words open not on law but on the negation of every attribute of God? We hear the first sermon of Nahj al-Balagha whole — the apophatic ladder from the knowledge of God to the divesting of all attributes, the churning of the primordial sea into the seven heavens, the ranks of tireless angels, the fashioning of Adam and the breath of the spirit, the pride of Iblis, and the succession of prophets that ends in Muhammad and the Book — and follow the single arc by which a doctrine of oneness becomes a cosmos and a history.
