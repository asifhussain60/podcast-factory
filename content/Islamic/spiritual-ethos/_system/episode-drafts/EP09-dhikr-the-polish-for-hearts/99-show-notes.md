# Show notes — EP09

**Title:** Dhikr, the Polish for Hearts

**Blurb:** Why is the simplest act of worship — the bare turning of the heart toward God — called the greatest? We follow Ali (ع) at the head of the whole mystical tradition of Islam, the meaning of dhikr as rendering the divine present, the Imam's image of remembrance as a polish for the clouded heart, and the strange vision by which the polished heart sees God with a sight that is finally God's own.
