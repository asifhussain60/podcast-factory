# Show notes — EP13

**Title:** The Letter of Ali (ع) to Malik al-Ashtar

**Blurb:** What is the first thing you would hand a man to whom you were about to give a province? Ali (ع) hands Malik al-Ashtar neither an army nor a treasury but a warning about his own soul. We hear his longest letter whole, from first line to last — the ladder of authority that ends above every appointment, the common folk chosen over the flattering elite, the gentle commander and the incorruptible judge, the reversal that puts the cultivation of the land above the tax drawn from it, the cry — "O God, O God" — over the destitute, the unguarded assembly stripped of guards, and the pledges and the blood on which a ruler''s whole account with God is finally settled.
