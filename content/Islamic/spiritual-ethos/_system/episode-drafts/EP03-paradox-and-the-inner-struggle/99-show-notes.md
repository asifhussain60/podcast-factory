# Show notes — EP03

**Title:** Paradox and the Inner Struggle

**Blurb:** The Imam's most exalted speech about God is built almost entirely out of negations, and the wisest man of his age waited until a graveyard lay before him to give his most important counsel. We follow why, in this ethos, the higher the truth the deeper the paradox, how the spirit breathed into clay turns the intellect into a living faculty, and how that faculty is only realized through the greatest of all struggles — the war to win the whole soul to the side of the intellect.
