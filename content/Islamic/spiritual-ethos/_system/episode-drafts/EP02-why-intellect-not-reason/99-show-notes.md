# Show notes — EP02

**Title:** Why Intellect, Not Reason

**Blurb:** The word we reach for, reason, may be exactly the wrong word for how Ali (as) knew what he knew. We recover the older meaning of intellect as contemplative vision, hear the Imam refuse a merely numerical oneness on the edge of a battlefield, and learn why revelation and the intellect are not rivals but partners.
