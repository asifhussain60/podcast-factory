# Show notes — EP06

**Title:** Pride and Conscience

**Blurb:** How can a feeling of superiority be called war with God, and where is that war actually fought? We trace pride as the theft of an attribute belonging to God alone, meet Iblis as the archetype of arrogance and the hidden idolatry lurking in vanity, then follow the same war inward to the conscience every tyrant must silence, the self-reform and self-lowering that alone reverse it, and a compassion that turns out to be not softness but justice being faithful to the merciful nature of the Real.
