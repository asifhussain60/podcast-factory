# Show notes — EP05

**Title:** The Sacred Conception of Justice

**Blurb:** We reduce justice to fairness and fair play — but in Ali (ع)'s letter to Malik al-Ashtar it is nothing less than the nature of God Himself. We hear MacIntyre's charge that ethics has lost its anchor in the Good, meet the hadith of Gabriel and virtue as worshipping God as if you could see Him, and learn why to be just is to become like God while remaining His slave.
