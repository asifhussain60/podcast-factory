# Slide Deck Framing, Spiritual Ethos

## Audience
Thoughtful adults who have listened to the audio episodes of this book and know its argument, but are not scholars of Islamic philosophy or Sufism. Asif's own circle of educated, non-specialist readers who want the structures made visible.

## Core Principle
The audio episodes carry the argument and the reasoning. This deck exists only to make the book's STRUCTURES visible: the contrasts, hierarchies, genealogies, and processes prose can only describe in sequence. If a slide could be a single spoken sentence, it does not belong here.

## Visual Priorities
- Intellect against reason, contemplative vision versus discursive comparison, as two columns attribute by attribute.
- The war for the soul: the intellect leading the forces of the Compassionate and caprice the forces of the devil, the soul between them as the prize.
- The wheel of society, the governor as hub and the classes as spokes, turning only on the invisible axle joining it to the Real.
- The genealogy placing Ali at the head of the Sufi chains, Prophet to Ali to the orders.
- The great descent of the First Sermon, from the Essence no word can reach, through the churning sea, the angels, and Adam's clay, to the Book.

## Prohibited Patterns
- No slides that bullet-point or restate the audio.
- No literal illustrations of quoted verses or sayings.
- No stock-photo imagery or decorative scenery.
- No bullet-list slides masquerading as diagrams.
- No title, "thank you," or "key takeaways" slides.

## Steering Phrases
- "Each slide must add visual information not present in the audio."
- "Show the relationships between entities, not a list of them."
- "When two positions are contrasted, show them side by side as columns, attribute by attribute."
- "For the lineage of ideas, use a directed genealogy tree. Show edges, not just nodes."
- "Every slide must have a named diagram type, or it does not exist."

## Visual Style
Use a clear scholarly visual style: conceptual diagrams over decorative slides. Every slide must be a DRAWN diagram, a contrast, hierarchy, genealogy, process, or annotated structure, not a text-heavy bullet list. Let the structure of each slide do the teaching.
