They affirm the *hikmat* and the fact that its source is the *Imam-e-mustaqarr*. Every word of *hikmat* comes from there. Only the people of intellect know it. People of intellect, meaning those who are rightly guided and who are firm on the *din-e-hanif* (the true religion), they know. Read one more *ayat*.

*Rabbana la tuzigh qulubana ba'da idh hadaytana wa hab lana min ladunka rahmatan innaka anta al-wahhab. Rabbana innaka jami'u al-nasi li-yawmin la rayba fihi inna Allaha la yukhlifu al-mi'ad* (Our Lord, let not our hearts deviate after You have guided us and grant us from Yourself mercy. Indeed, You are the Bestower. Our Lord, surely You will gather the people for a Day about which there is no doubt. Indeed, Allah does not fail in His promise).

That is, the *rasikhun fi al-'ilm*... The translation is not given. The translation... The translation is... the translation of the Quran, you can look it up, it's easy. It's understandable. "That is..." you read from there. "That is..." he has written. Yes, starting from "that is." That is, the *rasikhun fi al-'ilm* (those firm in knowledge), being aware of the *ma'rifat* of the Quran, make this *du'a* (supplication): Ya Allah... *Rabbana*... *Rabbana*, "O our Lord." But who is the Lord? Here it is the Imam of the time. What do they say? They make this *du'a*: Ya Mawlana Imam al-Zaman, after we have received guidance, do not let us deviate from your *da'wat*. And grant us your *ta'yid* (divine support) and mercy. You are the Forgiver. Ya Mawlana Imam, you are the one who gathers these *suwarat* (spiritual forms), which are prepared by your *da'wat*, for the *haykal* (spiritual form/body) of the Imam coming after you. There is no doubt in this. And you have promised that every person will be given a place in the *haykal-e-imami* according to their worthiness. Certainly, you do not go against it.

Look, the whole *ma'rifat* is laid bare. The Imam of the time, the things that are gathered with you, for whom are they? For your son, for the coming Imam. So in a way, "*ihdina al-sirat al-mustaqim*" (Guide us to the straight path) which we read, the *sirat* is the coming *hujjat*, who is your son. We believe in you, of course. We also believe in your son, because it is *wahidan ba'da wahidin*, "*qullun 'an ba'd*" (each from the other). This is its confirmation. Your successor is this. That's it. And in the *haykal-e-imami*, what place will one get? That is his paradise (*jannat*). According to their worthiness, they will be given a place in the *haykal-e-imami*. That is, there will be no violation of it. Meaning, Allah, whatever the thing is, is just. He is 'Adil (Just), not Zalim (Unjust). Allah does not possess injustice (*zulm*). Someone was asked, "Allah has power over everything. Is there something Allah doesn't have? A thing you cannot answer?" The first person couldn't answer, the second couldn't. They came to Ali. "What is it with Allah that He doesn't have?" "Injustice (*zulm*)." That is the one thing Allah doesn't have. What is the wisdom in this? He is just. Allah is 'Adil.
