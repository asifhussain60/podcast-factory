`Alladhina yunfiquna amwalahum bil-layli wa-n-nahar, sirran wa-`alaniyatan, fa-lahum ajruhum `inda rabbihim, wa-la khawfun 'alayhim wa-la hum yahzanun` (Those who spend their wealth by night and day, secretly and publicly, they will have their reward with their Lord. And no fear will there be concerning them, nor will they grieve).

Those people who spend their wealth, night and day, secretly and openly, for them their reward is with their Lord. They will have no fear, and they will have no grief. Meaning, those who in the eras of concealment and manifestation, openly and secretly, spend their wealth of knowledge and material wealth, their reward is with their Cherisher. They will have no fear of going astray, and they will not grieve for having left the people of error. Yes.
