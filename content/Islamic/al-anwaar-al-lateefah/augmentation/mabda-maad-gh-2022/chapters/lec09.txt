Just as among the followers of the `Ashir`, there were the remorseful ones, and among the remorseful ones there were Imams, `Quwwam`, and their followers, in the same way, of that Shaytan, that Shaytan, his... his agents were formed... his followers, in them, that quality was created. O Allah, make this reading of the *Fatiha* reach the soul of our master and our Mawla, `al-Mu'ayyad Fi-d-dinillah` Ibn Musa al-Shirazi, may Allah sanctify his noble and illumined soul. And make its blessings, ranks, and confirmations apparent to us. And accept from us this reading and this benefiting in the best way that can be. By the right of the master of the messengers and the successor of our master, Amir al-Mu'minin, and the Great Quran and the pure and purified Ahl al-Bayt, may the blessings and peace of Allah be upon them all.
