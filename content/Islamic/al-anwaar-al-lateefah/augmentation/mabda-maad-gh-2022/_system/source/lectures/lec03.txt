And in this way, that... *riyaah*, in their nourishment, they are continuously... received. *wa ummahat al-a'immah salawat Allahi 'alayhim mutahharatun... min dam al-tamt* (and the mothers of the Imams, may Allah's blessings be upon them, are purified... from the blood of menstruation). And the mothers of the *Imam*, meaning, the mothers of the Imams, are pure. From the blood of menstruation, they are pure and clean. They... that which generally happens to women, the menses, they are pure and clean from it. Allah has kept them pure and clean. They are *batul*. They are called *batul*. Yes. That they are pure and clean. And all... the Imams have several wives. So not in every wife, but the one from whom the son of the *Imam* is born, she is the *batul*. Yes. And the one who becomes the *Ummahat al-A'immah* (Mothers of the Imams), for her, a story happened, that during Hajj, Hazrat Shah Sahib Muqaddas, Maulana Ghulam Husayn Sahib Muqaddas, had gone. And at that time, a large delegation had gone. It was the time of Sayyidna 'Ali bin Husayn al-Mukarrami. So you... there was a gathering, so you asked various questions. Among them, one question was that, what is the reason that... for being the Mothers of the Imams? What is the reason for becoming *Ummahat al-A'immah*? How does one become *Ummahat al-A'immah*? It's a matter of great honor, no? To become the mother of an *Imam*. So, what is the honor of being *Ummahat al-A'immah*, and what is its reason? So everyone remained silent, but Mawla Shaykh Husayn Sahib gave the answer. And Sayyidna said, "Correct, it's correct." In the presence of Sayyidna 'Ali bin Husayn. In Makkah al-Mukarramah. Yes. And he said that the reason for this is that from them, great services have been rendered in a previous existence. The great services they have rendered as a *mansub* (appointee), as a *da'i* (missionary)... so they are great services. For which they are given the reward that they become *Ummahat al-A'immah*. Even though they come in a female form. But their honor is great, that they are becoming the mothers of the Imams. *qala ta'ala...* (The Exalted said...).
