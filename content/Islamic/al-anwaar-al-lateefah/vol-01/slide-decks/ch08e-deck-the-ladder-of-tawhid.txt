# The Ladder of Tawhid

## Where this episode picks up

Contrast pair, two responses to the seeker's bewilderment.

Column A: Stop at the teacher (the danger)
- Reading: the teacher is the seeker's god in the absolute sense
- Result: a created rank mistaken for the Creator
- Motion: the orbit halts at the center it first found
- Verdict: a created rank wrongly worshipped

Column B: Look up past the teacher (the opportunity)
- Reading: the teacher's own bewilderment points beyond him
- Result: the created rank becomes the first rung of an ascent
- Motion: the orbit discovers it turns within a larger orbit
- Verdict: the beginning of declaring the Oneness

Shared row: the same bewilderment that fixed the seeker upon his teacher contains the means of its own correction, for the seeker can look and see that his teacher is bewildered too.

Annotated structure, the seeker's starting position.

Whole: the seeker circling his teacher as a circle turns about its center.
- Center: the teacher, called "god" in a carefully limited sense
- Orbiting body: the dazzled seeker, fixed upon that one center
- Hidden fact not yet seen: the center is itself in orbit around a higher center
Callout: to see that hidden fact is to convert vertigo into ascent. To miss it is to freeze at the first circle and call a creature the Creator.

## The chain of bewilderment

Genealogy chain, the ascent of bewilderment, each rank bewildered before the next, arrows pointing upward.

Seeker → bewildered before the Mukathir
Mukathir → poor, needy, bewildered before the one who bestows upon him
Each higher rank → bewildered before the rank above it, in the same manner through the physical ranks
Physical ranks → the Imam, upon him be peace
Imam → ascends toward the abstract intellects, those not in the physical world at all, the angelic realities of the higher realms
Abstract intellects → the First Intellect at the summit
First Intellect → bewildered before all the originated and created beings, perplexed at how it attained its precedence, though all were brought into existence together at one time
First Intellect → bewildered before its Originator, the Originator of itself and of all its kinds

Process flow, why bewilderment is the engine and not the obstacle.

Start: the confession that one does not have knowledge, the first step in acquiring it
  to
One becomes, as it were, ignorant, owning one's not-knowing before what stands above
  to
One is made ready to receive, the very posture the angels assumed
  to
End: the revolving in bewilderment is itself the beginning of gnosis, as the circle revolves around its center

Verbatim, the angels confess the limit of their knowing and hand all knowledge back to its source.

> They said, "Glory be to You! We have no knowledge save what You have taught us. Truly You are the Knower, the Wise." (chapter 2, verse 32), trans. Seyyed Hossein Nasr et al., *The Study Quran*.

Contrast pair, the first appearance versus the true nature of bewilderment.

Column A: First appearance
- A vertigo that might trap the seeker
- The seeker's private weakness
- A reason to stop

Column B: True nature
- The universal condition of every created thing before what stands above it
- Shared by every rank, all the way to the First Intellect before the Originator
- The engine of the ascent

## Negating divinity, affirming Allah

Process flow, the act of Tawhid performed rung by rung.

Start: confess that the teacher has become a god, in the sense of the center of the seeker's bewildered orbit
  to
Verify that the teacher, the Mukathir, is himself bewildered before another above him
  to
Negate divinity from the teacher in the absolute sense
  to
Negate divinity from every rank above him, physical and spiritual, to the end of the chain
  to
Recognize that the First Intellect itself is bewildered before the Originator
  to
End: affirm divinity for Allah alone and proclaim His greatness, He besides whom there is no god

Visual metaphor, linked circles ascending.

Metaphor: nested orbits, each linked to the one above it.
- Lowest circle: the teacher's circle, around whom the seeker turns
- Next circle: the one who bestows upon the teacher
- Next circle: another above that one
- Final circle: the First Intellect
Reading: each center is itself in orbit around the center above it, so the structure of bewildered revolution repeats at every level. The bewilderment is not denied, it is followed home to the true God beyond them all.

Annotated structure, the believer's path of Tawhid stated as one motion.

Whole: confession, then verification, then ascent.
- First: he confesses that his teacher has become a god for him, the center of his bewildered orbit
- Then: he verifies that the teacher is himself bewildered before another, and denies that the teacher is his god in the absolute sense
- Then: he follows the linked circles up until he reaches the true God beyond them all
Callout: the recognition that the First Intellect held toward the Originator is reproduced at every level, including the seeker's own relation to his teacher.

## The circles of the da'wah

Process flow with a loop, how circle was generated from circle in the spiritual world.

Start: the summons is given there; a forerunner stands at the center of a circle
  to
He calls; those who answer and repent with his repentance ascend with him, going up with him
  to
The final individual left behind, who has not ascended, is given the command: "Our work is finished; now you do the work"
  to
He becomes the center of a new circle; those who repent with his repentance gather around him
  to
Loop back: again the last individual left is given the command in his turn, and a further circle forms
End pattern: circle generated from circle, each formed upon the pattern of the first, the first pattern established there in the spiritual world

Rule governing the structure: the center of a given circle, around whom others revolve, must himself revolve around the center of the circle above his own; he too is in orbit, so the bewildered revolution is reproduced at every level.

Annotated structure, the word ilah dissected into three letters.

Whole: the word for "god," ilah, composed of three letters: alif, lam, ha.
- alif: precedence without an inspirer, that is, the First Intellect, who preceded all the rest by his own act with none above him to prompt him
- lam: advancing not by independent precedence but by seeing another and repenting with his repentance, the station of the one emanated from the First, who attained his rank by following
- ha: the giving of the summons, performed by the seven intellects, the tenth not giving it
Callout: these three conditions are present in every circle, for in every circle there is everything; the whole story of the life of the universe is contained in this composition.

Comparison matrix, the three letters and the three conditions.

Rows: alif, lam, ha
Columns: condition shown | who embodies it | how the rank is attained

- alif: precedence without an inspirer | the First Intellect | by its own act, with none above to prompt it
- lam: following another, repenting with his repentance | the one emanated from the First | by following the one before
- ha: the giving of the summons | the seven intellects, the tenth not giving it | by calling others upward

## Created gods and the true God

Contrast pair, the created gods set against the one true God.

Column A: A created god
- Origin: created, invented, and originated
- Distinguishing mark: carries within himself the name of the nourisher who stands above him
- Inner state: impelled by a need that calls him upward toward that nourisher
- Sufficiency: never self-sufficient, sustained by one above him

Column B: The one true God, the Originator
- Origin: none; He is the Originator of all
- Distinguishing mark: no nourisher above Him and no upward-calling need
- Inner state: bewildered before none, while every subordinate is bewildered before Him
- Sufficiency: the loftiness of His state occasions the bewilderment of high and low alike

Shared row: there is no god besides Him.

Process flow, the threefold movement completed at the highest level.

Step 1, tanzih: declare the one God transcendent above the physical ranks, which are limited, and above the spiritual ranks, which are unlimited, for the nourisher of all is beyond both hierarchies alike
  to
Step 2, tajrid: strip from the Originator all the names, attributes, descriptions, and relations affirmed for the ranks, for He is God in the absolute sense, divested of all that can be predicated
  to
Step 3, return: come back to one's own rank, to one's bewilderment within it, and to one's obedience and commitment to the teacher
End: at that moment the seeker performs true Tawhid, true tanzih, and true tajrid, with gnosis, with religion, with knowledge, and with certainty

Verbatim, the Father of Imams placed the stripping of attributes at the summit of declaring the Oneness.

> The foremost in religion is the knowledge of Him, and the perfection of professing His Oneness is the affirmation of His purity, and the perfection of His purity is to deny Him attributes, because every attribute is a proof that it is other than that to which it is attributed, and everything to which something is attributed is other than the attribute.
> Ali ibn Abi Talib, the Father of Imams, *Nahj al-Balagha*, Sermon 1, trans. Sayed Ali Reza.

Reading: to fasten any attribute upon the Originator is already to set a second thing beside Him, the attribute that is other than Him, and so to compromise the very Oneness one means to affirm. The stripping is not an impoverishment of God but the only honest way to leave Him truly one.

## The tranquil soul

Annotated structure, the verse spoken to the tranquil soul.

Whole: O tranquil soul, return to your Lord, well-pleased and well-pleasing (chapter 89, verses 27 and 28).
- "return": the crucial word; the soul ascends to the very top and then comes back down
- "your Lord": the one who bestowed upon you
- "well-pleased": you yourself are well-pleased
- "well-pleasing": the one who bestowed upon you is well-pleased with you
Callout: both are pleased together; the soul's tranquility lies in what it has come to know, and its pleasure lies in the command of its Lord. It is well-pleasing in His sight and in the sight of all the ranks to whose gnosis the soul has reached.

Visual metaphor, the journey as a circle and not a one-way road.

Metaphor: an ascent and a return along the same chain of ranks.
- Upward arc: up the chain of bewilderment, negating and affirming, to the summit
- Turning point: "return to your Lord"
- Downward arc: back to one's own station, one's obedience, and one's place among the ranks
Reading: the climb was not a flight that abandons the world below. The soul that has seen the truth of the whole hierarchy comes home to its own place, and it is exactly there, content in its station and accepted by those above it, that its tranquility is found.

Verbatim, the Sufi tradition heard this homeward longing in the cry of the reed flute.

> Listen to the reed, how it tells a tale, complaining of separations, saying, "Ever since I was parted from the reed-bed, my lament has caused man and woman to moan. Every one who is left far from his source wishes back the time when he was united with it."
> Rumi, *Mathnawi*, Book I (the Song of the Reed), trans. Reynold A. Nicholson.

Verbatim, the movement closes in grateful praise.

> praise be to Allah who guided us to this; we would not have been guided had Allah not guided us; indeed the messengers of our Lord came with the truth (chapter 7, verse 43).

## The Originator in the Qur'an

Process flow, locating the Originator in scripture.

Question: is there mention of this Originator, and of the world of souls, in the Holy Qur'an and in our supplications?
  to
Answer: yes; different phrases at different places convey the matter, as in the verse that we were created from a single soul
  to
Summary: the opening chapter, the chapter of praise, whose details are founded upon its first verse

Verbatim, the founding verse from which the qualities of Allah are unfolded.

> Praise be to Allah, Lord of the worlds (chapter 1, verse 2).

Reading: He is the Lord of all the worlds, and the matter is mentioned both in supplication and in the Qur'an, its summary being the opening chapter, within which the whole teaching has been explained.

Comparison matrix, the three classes of people encoded in the opening chapter.

Rows: the repentant, those who incur wrath, the astray
Columns: Quranic phrase | inner condition | standing on the path

- the repentant: those upon whom favor was bestowed | the favor given them was that they should return | favored to return
- those who incur wrath: those upon whom is wrath | the insistent ones, who persist in their error | wrath has descended upon them
- the astray: the astray | the bewildered, lost in perplexity, who have not found their way through | they go astray

Verbatim, the verse that carries the three classes.

> Guide us upon the straight path, the path of those upon whom You have bestowed favor, not of those upon whom is wrath, nor of the astray (chapter 1, verses 6 and 7).

Annotated structure, why the Originator Himself cannot be grasped.

Whole: He is glorified above whatever they describe, pure of every attribute ascribed to Him, beyond them all; this is the very meaning of tanzih.
- Even the First Intellect, their own bestower, was incapable of the gnosis of the Originator; it did not possess that gnosis but was bewildered before Him, and on account of this bewilderment it became a god for those below
- Even the Prophet, at the summit of created beings, handed the praise of God back to God, unable to compass it himself

Verbatim, the Prophet's confession that praise outruns every tongue.

> I cannot enumerate Your praise. You are as You have praised Yourself.
> Prophet Muhammad, narrated by Aisha, *Sahih Muslim*, Kitab al-Salat, no. 486.

Process flow, why the only word finally ventured is "He."

To affirm our own existence we must affirm the Existent
  to
To affirm Him we must address Him by some word
  to
The only word that does not falsely circumscribe Him is "He"
End: even "He" is used under compulsion, for they had no other word; the naming is a fault coupled with a plea for forgiveness, uttered out of necessity

## What this teaching lands

Process flow, the complete act of Tawhid from start to close.

Start: the seeker left circling his teacher in bewilderment
  to
Looks upward: the teacher is himself bewildered before the rank above him, rank upon rank through the physical and spiritual hierarchy
  to
Reaches the First Intellect, bewildered before the Originator
  to
Recognizes bewilderment as the universal condition and as the first step of all knowledge, the confession that one does not know
  to
Negates divinity from every rank and affirms it for Allah alone
  to
Completes tanzih above both the limited and the unlimited ranks, and tajrid of every name and attribute
  to
Returns to his own station and his obedience
End: arrival, by negation and affirmation, at the One before whom all are bewildered and who is bewildered before none

Annotated structure, one structure carried in three places.

Whole: a single pattern of forerunner, follower, and summons appearing throughout the teaching.
- The linked circles of the divine summons: circle generated from circle upon the first pattern
- The letters of the word for "god": alif the forerunner, lam the follower, ha the summons
- The act of Tawhid itself: negation up the ranks, affirmation for Allah alone
Callout: the created gods are marked by their need of a nourisher above them; the true God has none. With this the first great movement of the volume comes to its close.
