# Slide Deck Framing for EP07-ascent-decline-and-the-birth-of-a-god

## Audience
Asif's children and thoughtful listeners who have heard the audio but never read Al-Anwaar al-Lateefah.

## Core Principle
The audio carries the argument as a linear story; the slides exist only to hold what the line cannot, the structures two men, two beasts, and two meanings of one word form against each other. If a slide could be a single audio sentence, omit it.

## Visual Priorities
- The Mukathir against the man who is not a Mukathir, two columns from one platform ending in opposite directions.
- The single hinge of prayer as a process flow branching up to ascent and down past cattle.
- The two meanings of "god," origination denied against center-of-the-circle affirmed, beside an annotated circle and orbit.
- The ladder from bewildered disciple up to the First Intellect before its Originator, a directed genealogy chain.

## Prohibited Patterns
- No slides that bullet-point or restate the audio narration.
- No literal illustrations of source quotes, prayer, cattle, or teachers.
- No stock-photo-style imagery.
- No bullet-list slides masquerading as diagrams, no opening, agenda, or takeaways slides.

## Steering Phrases
- "Each slide must add visual information not present in the audio."
- "When two positions are contrasted, show them side by side as columns, attribute by attribute."
- "For the ladder of ranks, use a directed genealogy tree. Show edges, not nodes."
- "Do not produce 'general illustration' slides. Every slide must have a named diagram type."
