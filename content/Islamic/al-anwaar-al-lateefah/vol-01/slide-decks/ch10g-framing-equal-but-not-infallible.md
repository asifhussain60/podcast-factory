# Slide Deck Framing - EP10-equal-but-not-infallible

## Audience
Asif's children, thoughtful young adults who heard the audio but never read the Arabic source and do not yet know terms like tawhid, the first and second perfection, or seeking the means.

## Core Principle
The audio carries the argument and the drama of how equality became hierarchy. Each slide must add spatial structure the spoken word cannot hold. If a slide could be one narrated sentence, drop it.

## Visual Priorities
- Perfect equality at the beginning reconciled with the ordered hierarchy at the end, as two columns joined by the freedom between them.
- The two faces of "error", slipping versus making others slip, as a small matrix and a contrast pair.
- The first form's single act enacting all three branches of Tawhid at once, as an annotated structure.
- The inversion of seeking the means, where the Originator's greatness is both barrier and ladder, as a visual metaphor.

## Prohibited Patterns
- No slides that bullet-point or restate the audio.
- No literal illustrations of the Quranic quotes, no stock-photo imagery.
- No "Overview", "Agenda", or "Key Takeaways" slides.
- No bullet list dressed up as a diagram.

## Steering Phrases
- "Each slide must add visual information not present in the audio."
- "When two positions are contrasted, show them side by side as columns, attribute by attribute."
- "Do not produce 'general illustration' slides. Every slide must have a named diagram type."
