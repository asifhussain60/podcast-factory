# Slide Deck Framing for EP06-the-refined-mukathir-house-of-allah

## Audience
Asif's children, thoughtful young adults following the audio series of Al-Anwaar al-Lateefah, comfortable with the argument but new to the vocabulary of the ranks (Mukathir, hudud, malakut, da'wah).

## Core Principle
The audio carries the argument; each slide must hold still a relationship the spoken line cannot. If a slide could be one sentence of narration, omit it.

## Visual Priorities
- Same flesh, different reality, as two columns identical in composition yet belonging to wholly different orders of being.
- The pillars matrix, each outward pillar (allegiance, purity, prayer, alms, fasting, the fifth, pilgrimage, struggle) set beside its inner meaning.
- The alms of wealth versus the alms of knowledge: wealth diminishes by spending, knowledge multiplies by it.
- The hinge where the summit of the human is the threshold of the spiritual world.

## Prohibited Patterns
- No slides that bullet-point or restate the audio.
- No literal illustrations of the Quran or hadith quotations.
- No stock-photo imagery or scenic backgrounds.
- No bullet list dressed up as a diagram, and no "Key Takeaways" slide.

## Steering Phrases
- "Each slide must add visual information not present in the audio."
- "When three or more positions are compared, use a matrix with rows for entities and columns for attributes."
- "When two positions are contrasted, show them side by side as columns, attribute by attribute."
- "Avoid stock-photo-style imagery. Every slide is a diagram or it does not exist."
