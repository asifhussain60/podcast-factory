# The Word and the Greatest Name

## Where this episode picks up

Annotated structure, the question this episode opens with.

Whole: a single sentence said to contain everything that has happened and everything that ever will, and to end, by design, at the number seven.
- The sentence: the testimony there is no god but Allah
- Its scope: all that was, all that will be
- Its strange terminus: it halts at seven
Callout: the volume has brought the first luminous form to the point of reaching toward the Originator through the means of His own greatness, having become the first to declare the divine Oneness. That reaching did not go unanswered.

Process flow, the arc of the whole episode.

Start: the first form's seeking of means, answered by a great descent
  to
A flowing knowledge and a spreading light descend, and that light has a name and a structure, the Word of Allah
  to
The teaching reads the inner composition of that Word as the skeleton showing through scripture and creation
  to
It follows the Word into the First Intellect, who is made more humble, not more proud
  to
It raises the First Intellect to his supreme station
  to
End: the volume closes on the one circle no created being can ever enter

## The Word of Allah

Process flow, how the Word reached the first form.

Start: the seeking of means brings a great descent of confirmation upon the first form
  to
There reach him the divine supports and the confirmations of oneness
  to
There connects with him the flowing knowledge and the spreading light
  to
End: this flowing knowledge and spreading light is the Word of Allah, which is the testimony there is no god but Allah

Annotated structure, the intermediate station of the Word.

Whole: the testimony there is no god but Allah, standing between the Originator and the originated.
- Above it: the Originator
- The Word itself: above the intellect and below the Originator, the continuous link
- Below it: the intellect, to which the light flows down through the Word
Callout: the flowing light came from the Originator down to the Word, and through it to the intellect. In the Word the Originator's will and the testimony of Oneness are manifested together.

Comparison matrix, the three names of one reality.

Rows: the flowing light, the Word, the command
Columns: what it names | its function | its duration

- the flowing light: the spreading light that connects with the first form | carries knowledge from the Originator to the intellect | remains as long as creation endures
- the Word: the testimony there is no god but Allah | the link between Originator and originated | a link established for as long as creation endures
- the command: the same Word renamed | all the commands are placed within it | terminates the world of origination at seven

Annotated structure, why origination ended at seven.

Whole: the will of the Originator that the world of origination should end at seven.
- He placed everything within the Word
- He willed that it terminate there
- So the luminous world ended at seven forms
Callout: the Word is no momentary thing. As long as the creation remains, the Word remains, and the flowing light remains.

Verbatim, the classical Ismaili reading of origination through the Command.

> His Command when He desires a thing is only to say to it, "Be!" and it is. (Quran 36:82, Surat Ya Sin), trans. Seyyed Hossein Nasr et al., *The Study Quran*.

Reading: for Abu Ya'qub al-Sijistani in his *Kitab al-Yanabi'* (trans. Paul E. Walker, *The Wellsprings of Wisdom*), the first originated being, the Intellect, is brought into existence through the Command, the divine "Be," which is itself the Word. The Word is the very means of origination, the link by which the Originator brings the first thing into being.

## Encompassing all knowledge

Contrast pair, what the First Intellect had versus what the Word supplied.

Column A: What he already possessed
- The complete knowledge, given with the first perfection
- All powers, held but unrecognized
- A gift he did not yet know he held
- Result without the Word: he did not recognize it, did not know how to use it

Column B: What the light of the Word added
- The understanding of what he had been given
- The way to use the powers he possessed
- Knowledge that encompassed every thing, what had been and what would be
- Result with the Word: he encompassed all things, for the testimony contains everything that has happened and all that will happen

Shared row: the knowledge was already there with the first perfection; the Word did not add new facts but supplied the understanding and the use.

Process flow, why self-negation precedes the gift.

Start: before he could obtain the Word and its light, the First Intellect had first to negate himself
  to
He confesses, as the first form did, "I am not my creator; our creator is someone else"
  to
He affirms that he and all his kind were not their own creators, but that their Creator was another
  to
The moment of self-negation: he obtains the Word
  to
End: in the light of the Word all things are obtained by him, and he finds within his essence such joy, delight, protection, praise, greatness, and pride that no pen could write its description and no tongue could translate it

Reading: this is the same law the seeker met on the ladder of bewilderment, where the first step of knowledge was the confession that one does not know. Here it appears at the very summit of creation, in the First Intellect himself. From the light of the Word he knew that there would be salvation.

## The numbered composition

Hierarchy, the layered count within the testimony there is no god but Allah.

- Layer 1: negation and affirmation, two, the denial and the assertion
- Layer 2: the word for "god" within it, three letters
- Layer 3: the jewels of the letters, five
- Layer 4: the words themselves, four
- Layer 5: the count comes to its end at seven, the seven disjoined letters at which the pen, or the tongue, halts
- Layer 6: after the seven come twelve, the ascending spirits
- Sum: seven plus twelve make nineteen

Annotated structure, why the world ended at seven.

Whole: the seven points where the pen stops.
- In the luminous world the matter ended at seven forms
- It ended there because the Word, by the will of the Originator, was to end at seven
- This composition of two, three, five, four, seven, and twelve works everywhere
Callout: it is found in every Imam, in every isthmus, in every believer, among all existents.

Verbatim, the Qur'anic signature of nineteen.

> Over it are nineteen. (Quran 74:30, Surat al-Muddaththir), trans. Seyyed Hossein Nasr et al., *The Study Quran*.

Reading: scripture stations nineteen as the guard over the fire, and the teaching finds that same nineteen inscribed in the opening formula of the Qur'an, in the name of Allah, the Most Gracious, the Most Merciful, as seven and twelve.

Comparison matrix, the numbers and where they reappear in the opening formula.

Rows: the two, the seven, the twelve
Columns: what it counts | where it appears in the formula | its meaning

- the two: the two kinds of grammatical construction | the additive construction and the descriptive construction | the additive is the more excellent, the descriptive the lesser
- the seven: the seven letters of the formula's first word | the week of the seven Imams | each Imam stationed in his place in the week
- the twelve: the deputies with each Imam | the Most Gracious, the Most Merciful | twelve deputies attend every Imam

Reading: the teaching cites the explanation of Sayyidna Muhammad Taher, may Allah sanctify his soul, that the seven letters are the week of the seven Imams, and that with each Imam are twelve of his deputies, the Most Gracious, the Most Merciful.

Annotated structure, the inverted order within the testimony of Oneness.

Whole: the deeper reading in which affirmation precedes negation.
- The surface order: negation, then affirmation
- The inverted order: affirmation precedes negation
- The saying of it: "Let my affirmation be established by your negation; let me be negated such that only You remain"
Callout: those who appear with the seventh Imam are of a very high rank, higher than the twelve before them, for he is a messenger. The additive and descriptive constructions encode the seven Imams and their twelve deputies, and the additive construction is the more excellent.

## The fear of the learned

Process flow, knowledge deepening into reverence.

Start: the First Intellect obtains the Word
  to
He does not grow proud
  to
He increases in humility and submission toward his Originator
  to
End: the more he came to know, the more humble and reverent he became, in bewilderment before His greatness

Verbatim, the Qur'anic ground of the learned one's fear.

> Only those of His servants who have knowledge fear God. Truly God is Mighty, Forgiving. (Quran 35:28, Surat Fatir), trans. Seyyed Hossein Nasr et al., *The Study Quran*.

Reading: the teaching notes that the particle in the verse lends the force of "indeed," carrying the sense of "except," so that it reads "indeed it is only the learned among His servants who fear Allah." Every knower of the secret of creation is, among all people, the one who fears Allah most.

Contrast pair, the two meanings of fear.

Column A: Fear as dread (the common assumption)
- What it is: trembling before a threat
- Who feels it: assumed to be the ignorant
- What it implies: that knowledge makes a person bolder before God
- Verdict: overturned by the teaching

Column B: Fear as acknowledgment of greatness (the teaching's reverse)
- What it is: the recognition of His majesty
- Who feels it: the one who knows most, who has seen enough of the divine greatness
- What it implies: the more knowledge increases, the more the awe in the divine presence increases
- Verdict: not the dread of punishment, but the clarity of standing knowingly in the presence of majesty

Shared row: Amir al-Din al-Sadiq, upon him be peace, when the color of someone's face would change in his presence, would say, "Do you not know in whose court you are standing?"

Verbatim, the Father of Imams on the register of this fear.

> The Creator is great in their minds, and so everything else is small in their eyes. (Ali ibn Abi Talib, *Nahj al-Balagha*, Sermon 193, the Sermon of the Pious), trans. Sayed Ali Reza, *Peak of Eloquence*.

Reading: their fear is not dread but the effect of a greatness that has so filled the heart that nothing else looms large beside it, the awe of one who has seen enough of the divine greatness that all else has shrunk to its true size.

## Veil, gate, and Greatest Name

Annotated structure, the supreme station of the First Intellect.

Whole: Allah took him as a veil for Himself and made him a gate for Himself.
- The veil: Allah took the First Intellect as a veil for Himself
- The gate: an access gate to the divine, through whom alone one may approach
- The title earned: His Greatest Name and His most noble, most honorable station
Callout: three exclusive functions were vested in him, and by this exclusive mediation he became the Greatest Name of Allah.

Comparison matrix, the three exclusive functions vested in the First Intellect.

Rows: seeking the means, intimate discourse, declaring the Oneness
Columns: the act | the rule of exclusivity | what depends on it

- seeking the means: reaching toward the Originator | the means can be sought only through the First Intellect | no approach to Him otherwise
- intimate discourse: holding converse with Allah | can be held only from his presence | no discourse otherwise
- declaring the Oneness: performing true Tawhid | can be performed only through his means | until one acknowledges the wali, one cannot attain the second perfection

Reading: this is the cosmological root of everything the volume opened with. The volume opened by teaching that the soul reaches the Oneness of Allah only through loving allegiance to the Friends of Allah and the ranks they appoint. Allegiance to the wali is not an extra devotion added onto Tawhid; it is the only door through which Tawhid can be performed at all.

## The delusion of equality

Contrast pair, the first emanation against the second.

Column A: The First Intellect (the first emanation)
- Standing: taken as veil and gate, raised to the Greatest Name
- Self-negation: confessed he was not his own creator
- Vision: the vision through which all means are sought
- Outcome: attained the supreme station

Column B: The second emanation
- Standing: deceived by a corrupt notion
- Acknowledgment: conceded the First Intellect's superiority, recognized that both their existences were real
- Vision: imagined his own vision was equal to the vision of the First Intellect
- Outcome: could not attain the second perfection

Shared row: both acknowledged the First Intellect's reality and superiority; the ruin lay in a single further thought, that one's own seeing was as good as the seeing of the one just called greater.

Comparison matrix, equality in the two perfections.

Rows: first perfection, second perfection
Columns: status of equality | rank order | the second emanation's error

- first perfection: all are indeed equal | no fixed before-and-after | equality is proper here
- second perfection: no one can be equal | ranks fixed in their before-and-after order, each unique | he carried the equality of the first perfection into the second

Reading: this is the subtlest failure in the whole volume, and the most cautionary, because it looks so much like faith. The second emanation did not deny the First Intellect; he confessed his superiority outright. To acknowledge a rank is necessary; to imagine oneself its equal is the very error that bars the way upward, a quiet self-flattery that undoes the soul precisely because it has otherwise believed rightly.

Visual metaphor, the circle of oneness.

Metaphor: a circle that belongs to the Originator alone, standing above and apart.
- The outermost circle: the circle of the first Originator, which no one has reached and no one can reach
- At its threshold: the First Intellect, the first originated being, the possessor of the veil
- Within his own measure: each being is its own circle, for each must be within its own measure
Reading: when it was asked of Sayyidna Sadiq how oneness can be spoken of as a circle, he replied that oneness here is a term of special honor, and that sanctity likewise is called a circle, a circle that the First Intellect has encompassed.

Verbatim, the Sufi definition of the same boundary.

> The affirmation of God's oneness is the separation of the Eternal from that which was originated in time. (Junayd al-Baghdadi, as recorded in al-Qushayri, *al-Risala al-Qushayriyya*), trans. Alexander Knysh, *Al-Qushayri's Epistle on Sufism*.

Reading: to declare the Originator One is to draw the line no originated being may cross, to hold the Eternal apart from everything ever brought into being, the First Intellect included. The First Intellect encompasses this separation and stands at its threshold as veil and gate, but the circle itself belongs to the Eternal alone.

Process flow, the sequence by which the First Intellect rose, outside time.

Start: his state was that of the first perfection
  to
He looked with his essence at his essence
  to
He took the means of the greatness of Allah
  to
All these things happen one after another, with their before and after, yet within the circle of oneness into which no other being entered
  to
End: this is not a circle of time, for time itself comes only from the sun; in the abode of origination there is only the priority and posteriority that precede time, and even a single point is its own circle

Reading: the testimony there is no god but Allah is the essence of it all, appointed such that after seven it is broken, the pen halting at the seventh. With this the second gate of the first canopy is brought to its close, in the name of Allah, the Most Gracious, the Most Merciful.

## What this teaching lands

Process flow, the volume coming full circle.

Start: in answer to the first form's seeking of means, the divine supports descended
  to
There connected with him the Word of Allah, the testimony of Oneness, above the intellect and below the Originator, the link through which all knowledge and light flow for as long as creation endures
  to
Through that Word the First Intellect came to encompass all that was and will be, but only after first negating himself, for self-negation always precedes the gift
  to
The Word's numbered composition, two, three, five, four, seven, twelve, nineteen, was shown to be the skeleton showing through scripture, the opening formula, the seven Imams and their twelve deputies, and the originated world itself
  to
Knowledge was seen to deepen reverence, so that it is the learned who most fear Allah, with a fear that is the clear acknowledgment of His greatness
  to
The First Intellect was raised to be Allah's veil, gate, and Greatest Name, the sole means to the seeking, the discourse, and the declaring of Oneness
  to
Against his success stands the second emanation, undone not by denial but by the delusion that his vision equaled the First Intellect's
  to
End: above them all stands the circle of oneness that belongs to the Originator alone and that no created being can ever enter

Annotated structure, what closes the first canopy.

Whole: the first canopy of this book of lights brought to its close.
- From: what Tawhid truly is, the gnosis of the ranks
- Up: the ladder of bewilderment to the one true God
- Back down: through the act of origination to the first Word by which a creature ever declared Him One
Callout: allegiance to the wali is the only door to Tawhid, because the Originator placed the whole approach to Himself behind a single veil and through a single gate.
