# Slide Deck Framing: EP11-the-word-of-allah-and-the-greatest-name

## Audience
Asif's children, thoughtful young adults who heard the audio but never read the Arabic source and do not know terms like tawhid or the Greatest Name.

## Core Principle
The audio carries the argument and the reverence of the volume's close. Each slide adds spatial structure the spoken word cannot hold. If a slide could be one narrated sentence, drop it.

## Visual Priorities
- The Word's intermediate station, above the intellect and below the Originator with light flowing down through it, as a three-tier structure.
- The numbered composition (two, three, five, four, seven, twelve, nineteen) as a layered hierarchy, then a matrix mapping seven and twelve onto the Imams and deputies.
- The First Intellect against the second emanation, success versus the delusion of equality, as columns.
- The circle of oneness, an outermost circle no created being can enter.

## Prohibited Patterns
- No slides that bullet-point or restate the audio.
- No literal illustrations of the quotes and no stock-photo imagery.
- No "Overview", "Agenda", or "Key Takeaways" slides.
- No bullet list dressed up as a diagram.

## Steering Phrases
- "Each slide must add visual information not present in the audio."
- "When two positions are contrasted, show them side by side as columns, attribute by attribute."
- "Do not produce 'general illustration' slides. Every slide must have a named diagram type."
- "Avoid stock-photo-style imagery. Every slide is a diagram or it does not exist."
