# Slide Deck Framing — EP04-what-tawhid-really-is

## Audience
Asif's children and thoughtful adult listeners who have heard the audio episode but have never read Al-Anwaar al-Lateefah. They follow ideas, not jargon.

## Core Principle
The audio carries the argument linearly. Each slide exists only to show a relationship the spoken word cannot hold still: a contrast, an axis, a chain, a layered map. If a slide could be one sentence of audio, omit it.

## Visual Priorities
- The relocation of Tawhid: "Allah is one" on the essence versus the gnosis of the ranks on the hierarchy. Render as side-by-side columns.
- The 2x2 of effort versus grace, all four quadrants populated (true ascent, falls short, passive waiting, acquires nothing).
- The limits matrix: Prophet to veil, Fatimah to Hujjah, Abd al-Muttalib to the Jadd's ten degrees.
- The closing metaphor: tanzih and tajrid as two cleared margins flanking Tawhid as the center.

## Prohibited Patterns
- No slides that bullet-point or restate the audio.
- No literal-text slides reproducing the quotations as wall text.
- No stock-photo-style imagery.
- No bullet list dressed up as a diagram.

## Steering Phrases
- "Each slide must add visual information not present in the audio."
- "Show the relationships between entities, not a list of them."
- "For the central tension, use a 2x2 with effort vs grace. Specify both axes explicitly."
- "Do not produce 'general illustration' slides. Every slide must have a named diagram type."
