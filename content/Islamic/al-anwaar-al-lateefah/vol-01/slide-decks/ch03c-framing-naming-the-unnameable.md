# Slide Deck Framing, EP03-naming-the-unnameable

## Audience
Asif's children and thoughtful adult listeners who have heard the audio but never read the source. They follow the argument but need the relationships drawn, not re-narrated.

## Core Principle
The audio carries the linear argument and its weight. Each slide must add visual information the audio cannot hold: a structure, a contrast, a lineage, a ladder. If a slide could be a single audio sentence, omit it.

## Visual Priorities
- The First Intellect's founding act as a two-step flow, negation then affirmation, ending in the testimony of oneness.
- The contrast between knowledge granted (life, knowledge, power, choice) and knowledge withheld (the divine essence), as two columns.
- The omnipotence debate as capacity versus its exercise: a matrix where having the power and refusing to misuse it sit together.
- The ten-rank ladder as one ascending chain ending at the bare "He," Huwiyyah, the threshold of the unknowable.

## Prohibited Patterns
- No slides that bullet-point or restate the audio.
- No literal illustrations of the Qur'anic verses or the desert anecdote.
- No stock-photo-style imagery.
- No bullet-list slides masquerading as diagrams.

## Steering Phrases
- "Each slide must add visual information not present in the audio."
- "Show the relationships between entities, not a list of them."
- "When two positions are contrasted, show them side by side as columns, attribute by attribute."
- "Avoid stock-photo-style imagery. Every slide is a diagram or it does not exist."
