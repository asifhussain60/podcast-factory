# Slide Deck Framing for EP05-outer-and-inner-gnosis-and-the-mukathir

## Audience
Asif's children, thoughtful young adults following the audio series of Al-Anwaar al-Lateefah, comfortable with the ideas but new to the vocabulary of the ranks (Mukathir, Mustajib, Mu'min, hudud).

## Core Principle
The audio carries the argument; each slide must show a relationship the spoken line cannot hold still. If a slide could be one sentence of narration, omit it.

## Visual Priorities
- Outer versus inner gnosis as two columns: friend and enemy equal on the left, the enemy shut out on the right.
- The two difficulties as a contrast pair: outer gnosis a wall any climber may scale, inner gnosis a door that opens only from within.
- The seeker's ascent as a ladder: Mustajib, then Mu'min, then the door of the hudud, then Mukathir, the first rank within.
- Breaking the self as a branching flow: break the self first, then gain standing to break others, or by indulgence lose it.

## Prohibited Patterns
- No slides that bullet-point or restate the audio.
- No literal illustrations of the Quran or hadith quotations.
- No stock-photo imagery or scenic backgrounds.
- No bullet list dressed up as a diagram, and no "Key Takeaways" slide.

## Steering Phrases
- "Each slide must add visual information not present in the audio."
- "When two positions are contrasted, show them side by side as columns, attribute by attribute."
- "Avoid stock-photo-style imagery. Every slide is a diagram or it does not exist."
