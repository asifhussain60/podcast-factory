# Slide Deck Framing — EP08-the-ladder-of-tawhid

## Audience
Asif's children, thoughtful young adults who heard the audio but never read the Arabic source and do not know terms like tawhid, ilah, tanzih, or tajrid.

## Core Principle
The audio carries the argument and the feeling of the ascent. Each slide must add spatial structure the spoken word cannot hold. If a slide could be one narrated sentence, drop it.

## Visual Priorities
- The chain of bewilderment from seeker to the First Intellect bewildered before the Originator, as a directed genealogy of ranks with upward arrows.
- The three letters of "god" (alif, lam, ha) mapped to forerunner, follower, summons, as an annotated structure beside a three-row matrix.
- The created god (carries a nourisher's name, driven by upward need) against the true God (no nourisher, bewildered before none), as side-by-side columns.
- The journey as a circle, not a one-way road: ascent, turning at "return", descent to one's station.

## Prohibited Patterns
- No slides that bullet-point or restate the audio.
- No literal illustrations of the quotes and no stock-photo imagery.
- No "Overview", "Agenda", or "Key Takeaways" slides.
- No bullet list dressed up as a diagram.

## Steering Phrases
- "Each slide must add visual information not present in the audio."
- "For the lineage of ranks, use a directed genealogy tree. Show edges, not just nodes."
- "When two positions are contrasted, show them side by side as columns, attribute by attribute."
