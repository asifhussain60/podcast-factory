# Slide Deck Framing — EP02-the-unknowable-originator-and-the-first-intellect

## Audience
Asif's children and thoughtful newcomers who heard the audio but never read Al-Anwaar al-Lateefah.

## Core Principle
The audio carries the argument; slides render only what audio cannot hold spatially: the faculties that fail at the threshold, names relocating off God onto the First Intellect, and a creation circling a center it cannot reach. If a slide could be a single spoken sentence, omit it.

## Visual Priorities
- The physical eye that fails set against the basirah that affirms, two columns anchored by Quran 6:103.
- Names relocating off Allah onto the First Intellect, a process flow plus a contrast pair for the two unequal distances.
- The universal incapacity as a hierarchy leveling the First Intellect with the lowest body.
- The created order as circles turning about a center, where the turning IS the bewilderment.

## Prohibited Patterns
- No slides that bullet-point or restate the audio narration.
- No literal-text slides reproducing the Quran, Nahj al-Balagha, or al-Sijistani as wall-text.
- No stock-photo imagery of eyes, light, or orbits.
- No bullet lists masquerading as diagrams.

## Steering Phrases
- "Each slide must add visual information not present in the audio."
- "When two positions are contrasted, show them side by side as columns, attribute by attribute."
- "Avoid stock-photo-style imagery. Every slide is a diagram or it does not exist."
- "Do not produce a slide titled 'Key Takeaways' — takeaways belong to the audio."
