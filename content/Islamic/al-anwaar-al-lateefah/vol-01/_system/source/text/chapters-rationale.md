## Source chapter 1 — The Opening Covenant and the Science of Realities

`ch01a-the-trust-and-the-path-to-tawhid.txt` — This episode gathers the opening movement of the source chapter: the provenance of the lessons (the ijazat of Hadrat 'Abdullah Sahib and their deposit in the Da'wat Sharief), the covenant of safekeeping with its sanction of cold iron, the fivefold architecture of the book (Suradiq, bab, fasl), the declared method of pure reality with no ta'weel and no symbol, and the doctrinal core — the two pillars of wilayah and 'amal by which the soul reaches the Oneness of Allah, ending on the return to the original homeland and the circling creatures who cannot reach the knowledge of their origin except through the Imams. These units cohere as a single orientation arc: they tell the listener what this knowledge is, how it is guarded, and where the journey leads, before any cosmology of the Originator begins. Six concept sections were used because the chapter carries six genuinely distinct teachings here (trust, covenant, architecture, method, the two pillars, the return) and none could be folded into another without losing a teachable unit; the episode lands at ~3,020 words, mid-band for the longer tier. It is `deep_dive` because the master is transmitting one inherited doctrine layer by layer with no named opposing voice, and `core` because it states the book's governing thesis that the whole volume presupposes.

`ch02b-the-unknowable-originator.txt` — This episode takes the second section of the source chapter, the turn from the soul's journey to the nature of the One toward whom it travels. It develops the doctrine of transcendence in four concept sections: the Originator exalted beyond all indication and thought (with Imam Ali's answer to Ibn al-Kawwa' that he saw God with insight, not the eye); the negation of names and attributes from Allah on the principle that a name falls only upon its like and our names are created things; the relocation of all names and attributes onto the First Intellect, His origination, with the comprehensive 'ijz that levels every creature; and the name "Allah" read as the cry of bewilderment, imaged in the circle whose turning is its perplexity about its center. Four concept sections (rather than six) match the source's own shape here — this section advances one tightly-argued metaphysical claim with fewer discrete teachings, each given fuller development (~700 words) to sustain the longer tier; the episode lands at ~2,870 words. It is `deep_dive` because the Ibn al-Kawwa' exchange is a brief illustrative anecdote, not a sustained named opposition, and `core` because the unknowability doctrine is the metaphysical spine on which the naming, tawhid, and ranks teachings all rest.

`ch03c-naming-the-unnameable.txt` — This episode covers the close of the first section of the source chapter, the resolution of the paradox the previous episode opened. It uses six concept sections: the First Intellect's primal negation-and-affirmation (with the witness verse "Allah testifies that there is no god but He," chapter 3, verse 18, read as the First Intellect's own confession); why we name the Unnameable, given total knowledge of life but no knowledge of the essence; the gift of existence that Allah does not withdraw, with the Arya Samaji exchange on power and justice; naming as a sin coupled with istighfar under the rule of compulsion, illustrated by Imam Ali freeing the woman driven by thirst; the ten ascending ranks culminating in the bare "He"; and the esoteric referents of shafa', witr, and julus as the Prophet with Lady Fatima, Imam Ali, and Hasan with Husayn. Six concept sections were needed because the section packs six distinct teachings into its close, including two short codas that are self-contained teachable units; the episode lands at ~3,020 words. ## Source chapter 2 — Tawhid, Tanzih, and the Knowledge of the Hudud

`ch04a-tawhid-is-the-gnosis-of-the-ranks.txt` — This episode opens the second source chapter with its boldest doctrinal move: the correction of the most common definition of Tawhid. It uses six concept sections — Tawhid as the gnosis of the ranks (not the counting of Allah as one, which is shown to be shirk); the boundlessness of its object and how a soul's rank is established through effort meeting grace; the three branches of Tawhid, tanzih, and tajrid; the division of the gnosis of the ranks into outer (where friend and enemy are equal) and inner (the beginning, level, and return of a rank's essence); the two thresholds of the Mustajib and Mu'min below the ranks and the Mukathir as the closest rank; and the breaking of the self that admits a man to that first rank. Six sections were used because the source packs six genuinely distinct teachings into this stretch and none could be folded into another without losing a teachable unit; the book's fivefold architecture, already taught in the opening chapter, is given only a brief callback rather than re-taught. The episode lands at ~3,410 words, mid-band for the longer tier. It is `deep_dive` because the doctrine unfolds layer by layer with no named opposing voice — the ordinary believer's mistaken definition is corrected by the teaching, not debated by a foil — and `core` because the redefinition of Tawhid as the gnosis of the ranks is the governing claim the rest of the volume presupposes.

`ch05b-the-house-of-allah-and-two-destinies.txt` — This episode covers the close of the first gate and the opening of the second, developing the moral and metaphysical stakes of the ranks in six concept sections: the refinement of the Mukathir's very composition though it looks unchanged; his becoming a house of Allah and a place where revelation alights, anchored in the buyut verses of Surah al-Nur and Surah al-Jumu'ah; the esoteric inner meanings of the pillars of the religion (wilayat, purity, prayer, alms, fasting, the fifth, pilgrimage, and struggle) taken as one teachable unit on the ta'weel of the law; the two destinies of two men on one platform, parted by the establishing of prayer; the lesson of the lawful and forbidden animals proving the same logic out of one created stuff; and the transmutation of the Mukathir's sensory soul, with the discernment that where the purity is real the rank is real though hidden. Six sections match the six distinct teachings of this stretch; the inner meanings of the pillars are kept whole as a single concept rather than fragmented into eight. The episode lands at ~2,840 words, in the longer band. It is `deep_dive` because the man-who-fails-to-pray is a foil for contrast, not a named voice in dialogue, and `core` because the House-of-Allah transformation and the two destinies establish what is at stake in the whole doctrine of the ranks.

`ch06c-the-ladder-of-bewilderment.txt` — This episode delivers the volume's central act of Tawhid, converting the doctrine from a formula into a climb, in six concept sections: the birth of a "god" in the disciple's bewilderment (the deferred second sense of ilah, the teacher as center of a turning circle and not a creator); the ladder of bewilderment verified rank by rank up to the First Intellect bewildered before the Originator; the formation of the linked circles of the da'wah and the three letters of the word for "god"; the created gods versus the one true God, with tanzih and tajrid completed at the summit; the tranquil soul's return to its Lord (Surah al-Fajr); and the Originator in the opening chapter of the Qur'an with its three classes of men. The circle-and-center image of bewilderment, established earlier in the teaching on naming, is given only a brief callback and the words are spent on the new ladder, the circles, and the created-god test. The episode lands at ~2,860 words. The major thematic seam falls at the end of this episode, where the fifth lecture turns from the gnosis of the ranks to the act of origination, making this the natural close of the first half. It is `deep_dive` because the teacher-disciple scene illustrates the doctrine rather than contesting it, and `core` because the climb of negation-and-affirmation is the practical heart of the book.
