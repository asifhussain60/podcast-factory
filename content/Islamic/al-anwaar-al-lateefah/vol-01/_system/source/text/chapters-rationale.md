## Source chapter 1 — The Opening Covenant and the Science of Realities

`ch01a-the-trust-and-the-science-of-realities.txt` — This episode opens the volume with everything that must be settled before a single doctrine of the Oneness is taught (the source chapter's opening sections on provenance, custody, architecture, method, and the path), in six concept sections: a trust bequeathed (the lessons as inherited wisdom from the sciences of the prophets and saints, recorded by permission and entrusted to the sacred mission); the covenant of safekeeping and its sanction of cold iron; the fivefold architecture of five Suradiqs, five chapters, five sections; the method of pure reality stated before any doctrine, with the grammar of categorical negation; the twin path to Tawhid through allegiance and action alone; and the return to the origin, with the image of all creatures circling a center only the few who hold fast to the Imams can break. Six concept sections were used because the opening packs six genuinely distinct foundational teachings and none could be folded into another without losing a teachable unit. The episode lands at ~2,830 words, at the floor of the longer tier. It is `deep_dive` because the recorder unfolds a single position with no named opposing voice, and `core` because it sets every premise — authority, method, and the allegiance-and-action path — that the rest of the volume presupposes.

`ch02b-the-unknowable-originator-and-the-first-intellect.txt` — This episode turns from the path to the One the path leads toward (the source chapter's second-section material on the transcendence of the Originator and the First Intellect), in six concept sections: the Originator beyond reach (no indication, no thought can bridge to Him; He is glorified, not glorifier); seen only by insight (the Ibn al-Kawwa exchange and the Commander of the Faithful's vision by basirah, not the eye); names fall only on their like (names and attributes are created things, made by us, and cannot characterize their maker, with Sayyidna Mu'ayyad al-Din on the originated letters of the alphabet); where the names land (negated from Allah, they fall on the First Intellect — the first existent, the first emanation); an incapacity that spares none (the inability to know the Originator is comprehensive over the worlds of intellect and sense, leveling the highest creature with the lowest); and the cry of bewilderment (our saying "Allah" arises from the First Intellect's bewilderment, imaged as the circle whose very motion is its perplexity before its center). Six sections match the six distinct teachings of this stretch; the bewilderment here is the foundational first treatment of the theme — the question of why we say "Allah" — kept distinct from the later rank-by-rank chain of bewilderment in the second source chapter. The episode lands at ~3,000 words, mid-band. It is `deep_dive` because the Ibn al-Kawwa exchange is an illustration the teaching adjudicates rather than a sustained opposing voice, and `core` because the unknowability of the Originator and the relocation of all naming onto the First Intellect are the cosmological premises the whole volume rests on.

`ch03c-naming-the-unnameable.txt` — This episode resolves the paradox the previous one left open and closes the volume's opening movement (the source chapter's closing sections on the First Intellect's act, the reason for naming, the gift of existence, the forgiven sin, and the ranks), in six concept sections: the First Intellect's first act (it negated divinity from itself and its genus and affirmed it for Allah alone, so the "Allah" who testifies in the witness verse is the First Intellect); naming from inadequacy (the intellects were given total knowledge of life — life, knowledge, power, choice — but never knowledge of the essence, so we name Him with the only vocabulary we were given); the gift not withdrawn (Allah never reclaims existence, and the Arya Samaji injustice challenge is met with the eat-the-filth counter — a capacity is not a compulsion, and not doing wrong is the perfection of power); a sin coupled with forgiveness (Sayyidna Mu'ayyad's prayer, the sin of inadequate naming paired with istighfar under compulsion, extending even to the First Intellect, sealed by the case of the compelled woman judged before Umar and released by the Father of Imams); the ten ascending ranks (the ladder from the Imamate of spirituality to Huwiyyah, the bare "He" in which all naming gives out); and the hidden pairs (the esoteric referents of the paired, odd, and sitting prayers — the Prophet with Lady Fatima, the Father of Imams, and Imam Hasan with Imam Husayn). The two closing source sections on the ranks and the prayer-referents were kept as two distinct concepts rather than merged, since each is a self-contained teaching. The episode lands at ~2,881 words. It is `deep_dive` because the injustice challenge and the case before Umar are illustrations the teaching adjudicates rather than voices in dialogue, and `core` because the negation-and-affirmation and the doctrine of compelled, forgiven naming complete the cosmological foundation on which the second source chapter's account of the ranks and origination is built.

## Source chapter 2 — Tawhid, Tanzih, and the Knowledge of the Hudud

`ch04a-what-tawhid-really-is.txt` — This episode opens the second source chapter with its boldest doctrinal move: the correction of the most common definition of Tawhid. It uses six concept sections — what Tawhid is NOT (the reflexive "Allah is one," shown to be shirk because it subjects the One to number); Tawhid as the gnosis of the ranks, with the levels of Tawhid and the highest knowledge; the boundlessness of its object via the body-of-three-hundred-and-sixty-parts analogy; salvation by rank, with the station established through effort meeting grace; the limits and their stations (the Prophet's veiling, the Hujjah, the ten degrees of the Jadd); and the second and third branches, tanzih and tajrid. Six sections were used because the source packs six genuinely distinct teachings into this stretch (sections one through three of the source chapter) and none could be folded into another without losing a teachable unit; the volume's fivefold architecture, already taught in the opening chapter, is given only the briefest callback rather than re-taught. The episode lands at ~2,980 words, mid-band for the longer tier. It is `deep_dive` because the doctrine unfolds layer by layer with no named opposing voice — the believer's mistaken definition is corrected by the teaching, not debated by a foil — and `core` because the redefinition of Tawhid as the gnosis of the ranks is the governing claim the rest of the volume presupposes.

`ch05b-outer-and-inner-gnosis-and-the-mukathir.txt` — This episode takes the source's division of the gnosis of the ranks and the approach to the hierarchy (sections four through six of the source chapter), in six concept sections: the outer gnosis in which friend and foe are equal; the inner gnosis reserved for the people of truth; the stakes of the inner gnosis (the beginning, level, and return of a rank's essence, and the severe verdict that without it there is no Tawhid at all); the closest rank, the Mukathir, with the rule of proportion that the lower establishes the higher; the two thresholds below the hudud, the Mustajib and the Mu'min with their ten levels each; and the breaking of the self by which one enters the first rank. Six sections match the six distinct teachings; the outer/inner split and the threshold/rank distinction are each kept whole. The episode lands at ~2,890 words. It is `deep_dive` because the friend and enemy appear only as illustrations of the outer gnosis, never as voices in dispute, and `core` because it establishes the two registers of the gnosis of the ranks and names the entry point on which the later ladder of Tawhid is built.

`ch06c-the-refined-mukathir-house-of-allah.txt` — This episode covers the close of the first gate and the opening of the second (sections seven through nine of the source chapter), in five concept sections: the same-composition paradox (the Mukathir and the ordinary man identical in flesh yet not the same); the refinement of his essence through ascent, so the summit of the human is the threshold of the spiritual world; his becoming a house in which revelation alights, anchored in the buyut verses of Surah al-Nur; the establishing of the da'wah, with the prayer-and-summons bound together and the Surah al-Jumu'ah verse; and the inner meanings of the pillars of the religion taken as one teachable unit on the ta'weel of the law. Five concept sections (rather than six) suit the source's shape here — this stretch carries fewer discrete teachings, each given fuller development to sustain the longer tier, and the eight inner meanings of the pillars are deliberately kept whole as a single concept rather than fragmented. The episode lands at ~2,890 words. It is `deep_dive` because the man who fails to pray is a foil for contrast, not a named voice in dialogue, and `core` because the House-of-Allah transformation and the inner law establish what is at stake in the whole doctrine of the ranks.

`ch07d-ascent-decline-and-the-birth-of-a-god.txt` — This episode gives the doctrine its moral urgency (sections ten through thirteen of the source chapter), in six concept sections: two men of identical flesh on one platform; the parting of prayer and the descent below the level of cattle to the isthmus of fear; the lesson of the lawful and forbidden animals proving the same logic out of one created stuff; the transmutation of the Mukathir's sensory soul by the divine sciences; the discernment that where the soul's purity is real the rank is real though hidden; and the birth of a "god" in the disciple's bewilderment (the deferred second sense of ilah, the teacher as the center of a turning circle and not a creator). Six sections match the six distinct teachings of this stretch. The episode lands at ~2,920 words. It is `deep_dive` because the man who fails and the predatory animal are foils for contrast and the teacher-disciple scene illustrates the doctrine rather than contesting it, and `core` because the two destinies and the birth of a "god" set up the bewilderment that the next episode converts into the climb of true Tawhid.

`ch08e-the-ladder-of-tawhid.txt` — This episode delivers the volume's central act, converting the doctrine from a formula into a climb (sections fourteen through eighteen of the source chapter), in six concept sections: the chain of bewilderment verified rank by rank up to the First Intellect bewildered before the Originator, with humility as the first step of knowledge; the negation of divinity from every rank and its affirmation for Allah alone; the formation of the linked circles of the da'wah and the three letters of the word for "god"; the created gods versus the one true God, with tanzih and tajrid completed at the summit and the return to one's own rank; the tranquil soul's return to its Lord (Surah al-Fajr); and the Originator in the opening chapter of the Qur'an with its three classes of men. The short al-Fatiha/three-classes passage is merged here rather than left as a sub-band standalone, as the source-toc directs. The episode lands at ~2,875 words. The major thematic seam falls at the end of this episode, where the source turns from the gnosis of the ranks to the act of origination, making this the natural close of the first half. It is `deep_dive` because the teacher-disciple relation illustrates the doctrine rather than contesting it, and `core` because the climb of negation-and-affirmation is the practical heart of the book.

`ch09f-origination-from-nothing.txt` — This episode turns from the gnosis of the ranks to the act that produced them (sections nineteen through twenty-two of the source chapter), drawing on Sayyidna Mohyuddin and citing Sayyidna Hamiduddin, in five concept sections: the world of origination and its three negations (no prior matter, no concurrent matter, no underlying place); the distinction of origination from ordinary making; origination as pure favor and not from need or sport, reached by eliminating every motive that would imply a lack; the luminous imaginal forms on which the human body is patterned, so the body is the fallen copy; and the first perfection of perfect equality as an act of justice, attested by Surah al-Nisa. Five concept sections (rather than six) suit the denser, more compressed source material here, each given fuller development to reach the longer-tier floor; the episode lands at ~2,820 words. It is `deep_dive` because the cited masters are authorities the teaching summarizes, not opposing voices, and there is no contested proposition, and `core` because origination from nothing and the first perfection are the cosmological foundation on which the account of the First Intellect rests.

`ch10g-equal-but-not-infallible.txt` — This episode resolves how rank and error arise from an equal beginning and narrates the first act of Tawhid in creation (sections twenty-three through twenty-seven of the source chapter), in six concept sections: the absence of any obstacle and existence in a single instant, imaged as sunlight flooding the air and fire filling a dark house; the simultaneity of potential and actual intellects with no interval between the two perfections; equality without infallibility, error defined as the failure to attain the second perfection; the two faces of error (slipping and being made to slip) through the grammar of Mohyuddin's prayer; the first form to declare Oneness (Sahib al-Tawhid), who looked at his own essence and performed all three branches at once; and the seeking of means (tawassul) through the Originator's own greatness, the obstacle becoming the road. Six sections match the six distinct teachings. The episode lands at ~2,860 words. It is `deep_dive` because the cited masters are authorities and the first form's act is narrated rather than contested, and `core` because the emergence of rank and error and the first act of Tawhid are the hinge of the cosmology and the model of the believer's own approach.

`ch11h-the-word-of-allah-and-the-greatest-name.txt` — This episode closes the second gate of the first canopy, and with it the volume (sections twenty-eight through thirty-four of the source chapter), in six concept sections: the descent of the divine supports and the Word of Allah — the testimony of Oneness, above the intellect and below the Originator, also named the command; the First Intellect's all-encompassing knowledge attained only after self-negation, with the joy beyond all writing folded in; the numbered composition of the Word and of the opening formula of the Qur'an (two, three, five, four, seven, twelve, nineteen), with Muhammad Taher's reading of the seven Imams and their twelve deputies; the increase in reverence and the fear of the learned (Surah Fatir), with the Amir al-Din al-Sadiq anecdote; the First Intellect raised to veil, gate, and Greatest Name, with its three exclusive functions; and the second emanation undone by the delusion of equality, merged with the circle of oneness that belongs to the Originator alone and closes the canopy. The second emanation's failure and the circle of oneness were merged into a single closing concept to keep the section at six concepts and to let the volume's final doctrine land as one movement. The episode lands at ~3,035 words. As the final episode of the volume, its closing frame lands the whole arc rather than handing off to a next episode. It is `deep_dive` because the second emanation is a contrasting case set against the First Intellect, not a named voice in dialogue, and `core` because the veil-and-gate station is the cosmological root of wilayah and the structural keystone of the volume.
