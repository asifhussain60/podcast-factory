# Show notes — EP04

**Title:** What Tawhid Really Is

**Blurb:** 'The volume''s boldest move: the teaching overturns the most common answer to the most basic question. Asked what it means to affirm the Oneness of Allah, almost everyone says it means to consider Him one — and the hudud reply that this, taken as a definition, is shirk, because it subjects the One to number. Tawhid is something else entirely: the gnosis of the ranks, the recognition that each rank of the spiritual hierarchy is unique in its place, and the knowledge of where each stands and how high it rises. Because the beings are innumerable, this is the highest and most inexhaustible of all knowledge, and a soul''s own station is set by how much of it he attains — through his effort meeting the grace from above. The episode closes on the three branches of the full saying: Tawhid, the gnosis of the ranks; tanzih, the denial that any rank can originate; and tajrid, the stripping of every name and attribute from Allah.
