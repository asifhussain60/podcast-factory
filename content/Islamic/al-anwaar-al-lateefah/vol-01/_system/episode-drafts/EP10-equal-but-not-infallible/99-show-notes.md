# Show notes — EP10

**Title:** Equal but Not Infallible

**Blurb:** 'If every being was created perfectly equal, with nothing to separate the first from the last, how did anything ever go wrong? The teaching answers by drawing out what follows from the absence of any obstacle in the abode of origination: the whole order came into being not in time but in a single instant — sunlight flooding the air, fire filling a dark room — with the potential and the actual intellects existing together and no interval between the first perfection and the second. The equal forms, though created without the slightest disparity, were not yet infallible; and error, the teaching makes precise, is exactly the failure to attain the second perfection — whether by one''s own slipping or by making others slip. From that trial-state of free equality, one form looked at its own essence, recognized an Originator unlike it and its kind, and negated, affirmed, and bore witness in a single act — becoming the first Possessor of Tawhid, and reaching toward the Originator through the means of His own unreachable greatness.
