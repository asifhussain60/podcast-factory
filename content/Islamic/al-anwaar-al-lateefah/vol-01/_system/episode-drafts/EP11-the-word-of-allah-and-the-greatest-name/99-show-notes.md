# Show notes — EP11

**Title:** The Word and the Greatest Name

**Blurb:** 'What single sentence could contain everything that has ever happened and everything that ever will — and end, by design, at the number seven? In answer to the first form''s seeking of means, the divine supports descended, and there connected with him the Word of Allah — the testimony of Oneness, above the intellect and below the Originator, the link through which all knowledge and light flow for as long as creation endures. Through that Word the First Intellect came to encompass all that was and will be, but only after first negating himself, for self-negation always precedes the gift. The Word''s numbered composition — two, three, five, four, seven, twelve, nineteen — is the skeleton showing through scripture, the opening formula of the Qur''an, the seven Imams and their twelve deputies, and the originated world itself; and knowledge was seen to deepen reverence, so that it is the learned who most fear Allah. The First Intellect was raised to be Allah''s veil, gate, and Greatest Name — the sole means to the seeking, the discourse, and the declaring of Oneness, which is why allegiance to the wali is the only door to Tawhid. Against his success stands the second emanation, undone not by denial but by the delusion that his vision equaled the First Intellect''s, while above them all stands the circle of oneness that belongs to the Originator alone. So the first canopy of this book of lights is brought to its close.
