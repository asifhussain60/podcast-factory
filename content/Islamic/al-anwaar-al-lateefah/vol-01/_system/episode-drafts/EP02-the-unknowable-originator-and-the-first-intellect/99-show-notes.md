# Show notes — EP02

**Title:** The Unknowable Originator

**Blurb:** 'The opening lesson laid the road home; this one turns to face what waits at its end — and finds the One toward whom the whole return is directed beyond every reach we have. No indication falls upon the Originator, no thought can bridge the gap to Him, and the names and attributes we use slide off Him entirely, since a created name can only characterize its like and never its own maker. Those names land instead upon the First Intellect, the first thing He brought forth; and the incapacity to know Him is shown to be universal, shared by the highest intelligence and the lowest body alike. The episode closes on the strangest teaching of all — that our very word "Allah" is not a description but the cry of a bewildered creation circling a center it cannot comprehend, lit up through the famous exchange in which the Commander of the Faithful says he saw God not with the eye but with insight.
