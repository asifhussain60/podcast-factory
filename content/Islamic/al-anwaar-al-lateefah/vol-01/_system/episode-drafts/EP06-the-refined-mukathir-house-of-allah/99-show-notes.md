# Show notes — EP06

**Title:** The Mukathir, House of Allah

**Blurb:** 'What if the holiest man you ever met looked, to the eye, exactly like everyone else — and the whole difference lay in something no inspection could detect? The Mukathir and the ordinary man are made of the same flesh and blood, gathered within the same bodily bounds, and yet they are not the same: the Mukathir''s temperament has been refined and his essence purified through an ascent that reaches even into his outward composition, though no eye can see it. Standing at the summit of the human — which is the threshold of the spiritual world — and holding the command of Allah and the Prophet before him in every thought, word, and deed, he becomes a house from the houses of Allah, a place on which revelation descends; the Qur''an''s houses, and the men whom no commerce diverts, are these very ranks. Their "establishing of prayer" is, in its depth, the establishing of the divine summons — and beneath every pillar of the religion there lies just such an inner meaning, the whole law one continuous training of the soul in knowledge and allegiance.
