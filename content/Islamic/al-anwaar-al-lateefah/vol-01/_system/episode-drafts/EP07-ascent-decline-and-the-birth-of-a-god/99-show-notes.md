# Show notes — EP07

**Title:** Ascent, Decline, and a God

**Blurb:** 'Two men stand on the same platform, made of the same flesh, born into the same world — and one is rising toward Allah while the other is sinking below the level of cattle. What separates them is nothing in their composition but only the establishing of prayer in its inner sense. The same logic is written into the animal kingdom, where lawful and forbidden creatures of one created stuff part ways by their nearness or aversion to man, and the obedient and disobedient among men are separated by a distance greater still. The Mukathir''s sensory soul, meanwhile, has been dyed luminous by the divine sciences flowing into it; and where such purity is genuinely present, a true rank is genuinely present, though it rests hidden with Allah. At the heart of the episode stands the bewildered disciple, for whom his overwhelming teacher has become a "god" — not a creator, but the center of the circle around which his astonishment turns.
