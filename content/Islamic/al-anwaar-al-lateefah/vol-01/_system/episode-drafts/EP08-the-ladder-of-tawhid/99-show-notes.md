# Show notes — EP08

**Title:** The Ladder of Tawhid

**Blurb:** 'If the guide leading you to Allah is himself bewildered before someone greater, is he still your god — or is that bewilderment the thing that points past him? The disciple who was left circling his teacher looks upward and sees that the teacher is himself bewildered before the rank above him, and that one before the next, rank upon rank to the First Intellect bewildered before the Originator. Recognizing that bewilderment as the universal condition — and as the first step of all knowledge, which is the confession that one does not know — he negates divinity from every rank and affirms it for Allah alone, completing tanzih above both the limited and the unlimited ranks and tajrid of every name, before returning to his own station and obedience. The linked circles of the divine summons, and the very letters of the word for "god," carry this same structure; the created gods are told apart from the one true God by their need of a nourisher above them; and the tranquil soul is sent home to its Lord, its serenity the fruit of its gnosis — the whole matter summarized, the teaching says, in the Qur''an''s opening chapter and its three classes of men.
