# Show notes — EP01

**Title:** Trust and the Science of Realities

**Blurb:** 'Before a single doctrine of the Oneness is taught, the recorder of these lessons stops to say what they are and how they reached us: an inherited wisdom drawn not from speculation but from the sciences of the prophets and the Friends of Allah, recorded by explicit permission and deposited in the treasury of the sacred mission. He binds the listener with a covenant of safekeeping — and names a punishment of cold iron for those who break it — then lays out the book''s symmetrical fivefold architecture and its method: to state pure reality directly, with no interpretation and no symbol. From there the doctrine opens, declaring that the Oneness of Allah is reached by two things only, loving allegiance and lawful action, and that this is the road by which the exiled soul returns to the homeland of spirits from which it first came.
