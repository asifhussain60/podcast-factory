# Show notes — EP03

**Title:** Naming the Unnameable

**Blurb:** 'The previous lesson closed on a paradox: the First Intellect cannot know the Originator, yet it is the First Intellect that first speaks of Him. This episode follows that speaking to its strange conclusion. It opens with the primal act in which the First Intellect negated divinity from itself and affirmed it for Allah alone — the act the Qur''an records as "Allah testifies that there is no god but He." It shows why we name Him only out of compelled inadequacy, having been given knowledge of life but never knowledge of His essence; why the existence He grants is never reclaimed, so that His refusal to do injustice is the perfection of His power; and why naming the Unnameable is a sin that arrives already joined to the asking of forgiveness — excused, like the woman judged before Umar, because it is compelled. It closes with the ten ascending ranks of the spiritual ladder, whose summit is the bare "He," and the hidden meanings folded into the pairings of prayer.
