# Show notes — EP09

**Title:** Origination From Nothing

**Blurb:** 'Before anything existed, where was the world that was about to be made? The answer is stranger than "nowhere": there was no "where" at all. The teaching turns from the gnosis of the ranks to the act that produced them, summarizing a long exposition of Sayyidna Mohyuddin. The Originator brought the world of origination into being in a single instant, from no prior matter, no concurrent matter, and no underlying place — an act utterly unlike the making we know, which only reshapes what already exists. He created not from need, for a god who needed his creation would be a god in lack, but from pure favor, drawing the existents out of a nothingness that was no place at all. What He brought forth were luminous imaginal forms on which the human body was later patterned — the soul a fallen copy of the form, the body a fallen copy of the soul, so that salvation is a return from dark density toward luminous subtlety. And He created all the forms perfectly equal in their first perfection, as an act of the justice that gives to none more and to none less.
