# Show notes — EP05

**Title:** The Two Gnoses and the Mukathir

**Blurb:** 'How can a saint''s most devoted disciple and his sworn enemy know him equally well? Because the gnosis of the ranks comes in two kinds. The outer gnosis knows a rank''s person and station — and in it the friend and the foe stand on exactly the same footing, for both see the same outward man. The inner gnosis reaches the beginning, the level, and the return of a rank''s essence, and is reserved for the disciplined and the verifying among the people of truth; without it, the teaching warns, a man may have no Tawhid at all. The episode then maps the approach to the hierarchy: two thresholds that are not yet ranks — the Mustajib who binds himself by the tongue and the Mu''min who joins tongue, heart, and limb, each with ten levels — and the first true rank, the Mukathir, entered by breaking one''s own self against the command of Allah and the Prophet, which is why knowledge is indispensable to him.
