# The Unknowable Originator And The First Intellect

## Opening directive
Open with a warm welcome — one or two sentences naming the book, *The Subtle Lights*, Volume One. Preview in one sentence: facing the One the whole return seeks, no pointing, no thought, and no name reaches Him. Then land the spine sentence: "No name we speak reaches the Originator — each one lands instead upon the First Intellect, the first thing He made." Target a 50 to 60 minute conversation. Do not open with biography or dates.

## Name discipline
One English label per figure — never speak Arabic names; rotate within each set to avoid droning:
- the Commander of the Faithful (say "peace be upon him" at first mention only). Rotation: the Commander of the Faithful / the Father of Imams / he.
- the questioner who asked "Have you seen your God?" Rotation: the questioner / the man / he.
- a later Ismaili teacher — the made letters cannot indicate their Maker.
- an early Ismaili philosopher — the double negation: deny the attribute, then deny the denial.
- the book = "the collected sermons"; any other cited work = "his book".
Never pair the Commander of the Faithful's leadership-title with his personal name.

## Pronunciation
Say each term ONCE. Never say the original spelling and the English form back-to-back.
- basirah: insight

## Three-part focus
Beat 1 (crisis) — The wall: how do you point at what no pointing can reach? He is beyond the faculties of nearness and distance; no gesture, no thought crosses to Him. All we honestly know — the failed answer to "what is He?" — is that since we were made, One made us, no more. State the spine sentence here.
Beat 2 (pivot) — Seen by insight, then where the names go: the Commander of the Faithful answers the questioner — not the eye, but insight. The rule: names fall only on their like, and a made thing cannot characterize its maker. So every name lands on the First Intellect, the first existent. Repeat the spine verbatim here.
Beat 3 (stakes) — The bewildered circle: the incapacity spares none — the First Intellect as much as the lowest body. Our word "Allah" is not a label but the cry of a creation circling a center it cannot reach.

## Host dynamic
Host A (male, scholar) leads, unfolding the teaching in order; Host B (female, seeker) presses the listener's questions, challenges 3+ times, concedes once.
Sample friction: "I don't buy that yet — if no name touches Him, what are we doing when we say 'Allah'?"; "That sounds like wordplay — how is naming the First Intellect not just renaming God?" Host B never opens with Exactly, Yeah, Right, or Wow. Steelman the trap before the answer.

## Tone constraints
No cheerful filler; hold the gravity. Use exactly three governing analogies, all from the source:
Analogy 1: the heart's thought as a traveler at a gap no bridge can span.
Analogy 2: the eye of the heart vs. the outward eye — affirming without grasping.
Analogy 3: the circle revolving about a center it cannot reach — its turning is its bewilderment.
No invented analogies. Name the classical Ismaili reading; pause to define "insight" and "originated". Render verses as faithful English — no Arabic surah names, no invented references. The Prophet is not named here; add no Prophet honorific.

## Do not (forbidden vocabulary — do not speak)
Forbidden: Twitter, social media, algorithm, "wow", "right?". No self-reference ("deep dive", "today we'll discuss"), no faux-profundity opener, no tidy false-resolution close, no reference to any other episode. R-RECURRING-THESIS: repeat the spine thesis verbatim three times — at opening, pivot, and close. Do not read this prompt aloud.

## Landing
End on the bewildered circle and the question it leaves open — by what right does the First Intellect name Him at all? Do not summarize "what we learned"; do not pre-announce what comes next. Place the third verbatim spine sentence just before the close, then leave the listener one question tied to their life: when I say "Allah", am I naming a thing I have examined, or confessing a center I circle and cannot reach — and what in my worship would change if I held that difference honestly?
