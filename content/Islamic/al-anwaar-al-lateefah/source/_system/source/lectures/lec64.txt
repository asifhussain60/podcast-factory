Bismillah al-Rahman al-Rahim (In the name of Allah, the Most Gracious, the Most Merciful). Wal-fajr (By the dawn). Wa layalin 'ashr (And [by] ten nights). Wash-shaf'i wal-watr (And [by] the even and the odd). Wal-layli idha yasr (And [by] the night when it passes). Hal fi dhalika qasamul li dhi hijr (Is there [not] in that an oath for one of understanding?). Alam tara kayfa fa'ala rabbuka bi 'aad (Have you not considered how your Lord dealt with 'Aad). Irama dhatil 'imaad (With Iram of the lofty pillars). Allati lam yukhlaq mithluha fil-bilad (The like of which were not created in the land). Wa thamoodalladhina jab-us-sakhra bil-wad (And [with] Thamud, who carved out the rocks in the valley). Wa fir'awna dhil-awtad (And [with] Pharaoh, owner of the stakes). Alladhina taghaw fil-bilad ([All of] whom transgressed within the lands). Fa aktharu fihal-fasad (And increased therein corruption). Fa sabba 'alayhim rabbuka sawta 'adhab (So your Lord poured upon them a scourge of punishment). Inna rabbaka la bil-mirsad (Indeed, your Lord is in observation). Fa ammal-insanu idha mab-talahu rabbuhu fa akramahu wa na''amahu fa yaqoolu rabbi akraman (And as for man, when his Lord tries him and [is] generous to him and favors him, he says, "My Lord has honored me."). Wa amma idha mab-talahu fa qadara 'alayhi rizqahu fa yaqoolu rabbi ahanan (But when He tries him and restricts his provision, he says, "My Lord has humiliated me."). Kalla bal la tukrimoonal-yateem (No! But you do not honor the orphan). Wa la tahaaddoona 'ala ta'amil-miskeen (And you do not encourage one another to feed the poor). Wa ta'kuloonat-turatha aklal-lamma (And you consume inheritance, devouring [it] altogether). Wa tuhibboonal-mala hubban jamma (And you love wealth with immense love). Kalla idha dukkatil-ardu dakkan dakka (No! When the earth has been leveled - pounded and crushed). Wa ja'a rabbuka wal-malaku saffan saffa (And your Lord has come and the angels, rank upon rank). Wa jee'a yawma'idhim bi jahannam (And brought [within view], that Day, is Hell). Yawma'idhiy-yatadhakkarul-insanu wa anna la hudh-dhikra (That Day, man will remember, but how will the remembrance avail him?). Yaqoolu ya laytani qaddamtu li hayati (He will say, "Oh, I wish I had sent forth [good] for my life."). Fa yawma'idhil-la yu'adhdhibu 'adhabahu ahad (So on that Day, none will punish [as severely] as His punishment). Wa la yoothiqu wathaqahu ahad (And none will bind [as severely] as His binding). Ya ayyatuhan-nafsul-mutma'innah (O reassured soul). Irji'i ila rabbiki radiyatam-mardiyyah (Return to your Lord, well-pleased and pleasing [to Him]). Fadhkuli fi 'ibadi (And enter among My [righteous] servants). Wadhkuli jannati (And enter My Paradise).
