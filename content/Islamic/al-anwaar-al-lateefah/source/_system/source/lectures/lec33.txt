"*Wa 'ala hadha al-manha...*" (And according to this method...). And according to this method... "*...fa-inna kulla muqimin li-maqamin min al-maqamat ba'da nuqlatihi fa-huwa al-khayal lahu. Wa alladhi qablahu huwa al-fath. Wa al-sabiqu 'alayhima huwa al-jadd.*" (...every establisher of a station from the stations, after his transfer, is the *khayal* for it. And the one before him is the *fath*. And the one preceding them both is the *jadd*). So every establisher... for a station... So every establisher... who establishes, for a station from the stations... whichever Imam is established, his *muqim* (predecessor) is in which station? In the station of *khayal*. After his transfer, after his passing. "*Fa-huwa al-khayal lahu.*" (So he is the *khayal* for him). And the one before him is the *fath*. Before him, who is there? He is in the station of *fath*. And the one preceding them both... from those two, the preceding one is the *jadd*. He is in the station of *jadd*.

"*Wa kullu wahidin 'amilun fi mandudihi.*" (And each one is an agent within his extension/mandud). And every one is an agent in his *mandud*. In his *mandud*. First is *khayal*, after that is *fath*, after that is *jadd*. These three are... and who is *khayal*? The *muqim*. The *muqim*. He helps him in all his tasks. Then his *muqim*, then his *muqim*.

So Sir 'Abdullah is... and Abu Talib are... The Prophet, peace and blessings be upon him, passed away, didn't he? Before. That's why you are... established... This is why... Abdullah is *fath*. And *khayal* is Abu Talib, who is alive. So he is the *khayal*. He passed away later, according to that. But as it is said, "*'Abdullah 'ind Allah.*" (Abdullah is with Allah). When it was asked, what did he say? "*'Abdullah 'ind Allah.*" He didn't put a dot. Abdullah, 'Abdullah can be read. He didn't put dots. No dot here, no dot there.

"*Kama qala salawatullahi 'alayh: 'Takhaffafu talhaqu.'*" (As he, peace and blessings be upon him, said: "Lighten yourselves and you will catch up."). As he, peace be upon him, said, "O people... lighten yourselves, so that you may join." Meaning, reduce yourselves of sins so that you may go and join the angels. "*Fa-innama yantaziru bi-awwalikum quduma aakhirikum.*" (For the first of you only await the arrival of the last of you). For the first of you are waiting for the steps of your last ones. The departed are waiting for the ones who will come later to come and join them.
