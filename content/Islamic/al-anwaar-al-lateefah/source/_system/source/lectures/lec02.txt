Alhamdulillah rabb al-'alamin (All praise is for Allah, the Lord of the worlds). Al-Rahman al-Rahim (The Most Gracious, the Most Merciful). Malik yawm al-din (Master of the Day of Judgment). Sirat alladhina an'amta 'alayhim (The path of those upon whom You have bestowed favor), ghayr al-maghdubi 'alayhim wa la al-dallin (not of those who have earned [Your] anger, nor of those who are astray).
