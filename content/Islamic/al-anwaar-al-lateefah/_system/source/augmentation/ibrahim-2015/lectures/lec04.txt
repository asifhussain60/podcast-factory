Qabil... that in the time of Adam, Harith bin Murrah, who... and Allah said to him, "Go, I give respite to your foot soldiers and cavalry, that you do whatever you want to torment my believers." But those who are the followers of truth, who are steadfast on the true religion, they will never be turned back. It will never happen. And in this way, the world has been repeating. Always, before the truth, an opposition has come.

*Yaa ayyuhal ladheena aamanoo anfiqoo mimma razaqnaakum min qabli any ya'tiya yawmul laa bay'un feehi wa laa khullatun wa laa shafaa'ah* (O you who have believed, spend from that which We have provided for you before there comes a Day in which there is no exchange and no friendship and no intercession). O believers, spend from the provision We have given you, before that day comes in which there is neither trade, nor friendship, nor intercession of any kind will be of use. See, this is the variety (*tanawwu'*) of the Holy Quran, that one discussion has finished, just now the story was of Da'ud and his... of Musa, many things happened, and now a new topic has been brought. This variety of the Quran is what creates a special quality in it. Otherwise, if it were the same boring thing, it wouldn't be good. But we always get new topics. Every verse has its own separate place. O believers, the provision We have given you... this is its translation. The provision that has been given, spend from it. And before that day comes, in which there is neither trade, nor friendship, nor intercession of any kind will be of use. This is regarding *zakat*. That *zakat*, which is obligatory on us, whatever our earning is, the fortieth part of it... one rupee in forty, must be given. This is the purification of our wealth.

Meaning, O you who obey the *wasi* and the *imam*! See, this is the translation of the verse, and its *ta'wil* is this. Meaning, O you who obey the *wasi* and the *imam*, benefit others with the esoteric sciences and true gnosis with which We have endowed you. Before that day of resurrection arrives when the *Qa'im* (peace be upon him) appears and brings the *qiyamah* (resurrection), and the transmission of beneficial knowledge from the *a'immah* and *du'at* to the people also ceases and is stopped. Because that is a day of examination and reckoning, not a day of give-and-take and teaching.

*Wal kaafiroona humuz zaalimoon* (And the disbelievers, they are the wrongdoers). The disbelievers are the very wrongdoers. Meaning, those who conceal the stations of the *wasi* and *imam* and do wrong are the wrongdoers. That's it.

Al-Fatiha.
