A'udhu Billahi min al-Shaytan al-Rajim. Bismillah al-Rahman al-Rahim. Wa sallallahu 'ala rasulihi wa alihi al-tayyibin al-tahirin wa sallama 'alayhim ajma'in. (I seek refuge in Allah from the accursed Satan. In the name of Allah, the Most Gracious, the Most Merciful. And may Allah's blessings be upon His Messenger and his pure and purified family, and may peace be upon them all.) Believing brothers and sisters, the lessons of the book *Anwaar al-Latifah* are comprised of the lofty contents of the sciences of realities (*'uloom-e-haqa'iq*), which have been interpreted based on the sciences of the *anbiya'* and *awliya'*. I had recorded these lessons with the permission (*ijazat*) of Maulaya 'awliy al-ne'mati al-mansub al-karim wal-faqeem, Hadrat 'Abdullah Sahib, 'atala Allahu baqa'ahu wa ja'alna fida', for the benefit of my father and other members of my family. By the command of my Aaqa and Maula, this recording has now been deposited in the treasury of the *Da'wat Sharief*. I most humbly request (*istida'*) that you please do not email these lessons, nor attempt to exchange them in any way on the internet, nor store them on your computer. Because despite all secure exchanges, there is a possibility that once something is on the web, then after that, all these things become accessible to everyone. And these lessons can fall into the hands of the undeserving (*ghair-mustahiqqeen*). Now, if anyone, despite this request, commits this act, then on the Day of Judgment (*baroz-e-qiyamat*), before Allah and His Messenger, the one who does this will be solely responsible, and I will be free of responsibility for it. If you are copying, listening to, transmitting, giving to someone else, modifying, or committing any such act (*fe'l*) with these lessons without permission which is a sin (*gunah*) in the sight of Allah and His Messenger, then you and only you are responsible for it, and I will be free of responsibility for this act on the Day of Judgment. The *Awliya'* of Allah have warned of a most severe punishment (*'azab*) for those who do not safeguard these lofty sciences (*'uloom-e-'aliya*), specifically giving news of the punishment of cold iron, which is the most severe in its terribleness. A'adhana Allah (May Allah protect us). May Allah prolong the shadow of Sayyidna al-Da'i and the Manasib-e-Kiram over us and make their lights (*anwaar*) flow upon us. Ameen. Bi-haqqi Sayyidina Maulana Muhammadin wa 'ala aali Sayyidina Maulana Muhammadin wa barik wa sallim. (By the right of our master, Maulana Muhammad, and upon the family of our master, Maulana Muhammad, and grant blessings and peace.)
