So it presents itself to everyone who hears it and sees it in the state of its departure from its body and its soul.) `Wa-qawniha mun'ifan wa-ṭaratan yamza'u man yara-ha bi-hay'ati insanin wa-ṭaratan saniyyatan fi hay'at shayṭanin yuzayyin qabiḥa af'alihi wa-aqbahihi wa-qawlihi...` (And its being `mun'ifan`, and at times startling whoever sees it in the form of a human, and at other times in the form of a shaitan, beautifying the ugliness of its deeds and its most ugly [deeds] and its speech...) And the statement of the speaker... and he falls to the `asfal al-safilin`. He whispers temptations with misguidance into the hearts of people. By this, the speaker intends that the departure of this evil `surah` from its body and `nafs`, and its journey, it roams between the sphere of the moon and the earth. And it presents itself to everyone who hears and sees it in a state of separation from its body and `nafs`. And its being a `mun'ifan`. And at times it startles whoever sees it in the form of a human, and at other times in a satanic form, making its evil and most evil deeds seem beautiful. `Wa-qawlan liman yara-ha bi-hay'ati jinniyyatin aw jinniyyin, wa-ṭaratan fi tara'iha fil-nawm li-amthaliha min ahl al-nufus al-khabithah, fa-yuhi ilayhi ma waswasat-ha lahu fima yujrihi min al-ḥaqqi wa-ahlihi, wa-ṭaratan min ittihadiha bi-aḥadi al-ajsadi al-khabithah al-muzlimah, wa-qawniha yantiqa 'ala lisanihi bil-kufriyyat wal-fawahishi wal-ḥawadithi al-ka'inat.` (And as a statement to whoever sees it in the form of a female jinn or a male jinn, and at times in its appearance in sleep to its likes from among the people of evil souls, so it inspires it with what it whispered to it concerning what it carries of [enmity towards] the truth and its people, and at times from its union with one of the evil, dark bodies, and its being such that it speaks on its tongue with blasphemies, abominations, and future events.) And the statement that... and as a statement to whoever sees it, that he saw a female jinn or a male jinn. And at times, seeing it in a dream... its likes, from among the people of evil souls, so it gives them revelation about the whispers it casts into them, about what distances them from the truth and its people, and from its connection with one of the evil, dark bodies, and its being such that it speaks through his tongue with blasphemies, abominations, and future events (`hawadith al-ka'inat`). `Al-Fasl al-thalith min al-bab al-thalith min sawabit al-'adad.` (The third section of the third chapter from *Sawabit al-'adad*.) `Wa-yaqulu man yasma'u nutqahu fil-umuri al-ka'inati wal-fawahishi wal-kufriyyat: huwa ṣadiqun fi jami'i ma yantiqa bihi wa-yukhbiru 'anhu.` (And he who hears its speech concerning future events, abominations, and blasphemies says: "He is truthful in all that he speaks and informs of.") So, it is said... he says... so he says, the one who hears... its speech (`nutq`). Its speech about matters (`umuri al-ka'inah`). That he is... `suwarat al-khabithah` (evil forms)...
