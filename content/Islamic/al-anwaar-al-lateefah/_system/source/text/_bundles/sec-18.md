# SECTION 18: Muhammad ibn Isma'il and the Cycle of Concealment

Lecture range: [35, 36, 37]


## LEDGER SPINE TEACHINGS YOU MUST PRESERVE (each at full depth)

- [S595] (doctrine) Maulana Husayn departed and the rest of the A'immah mutimmin (completers) continued until reaching Maulana Muhammad ibn Isma'il, the seventh, to whom the ultimate goal of the religious laws culminates and who is the Qa'im bil-quwwah (potential Qa'im), master of the first unveiling (sahib al-kashf al-ula).  ::terms: husayn, mutimmin, muhammad ibn isma'il, qa'im bil-quwwah, kashf al-ula
- [S596] (doctrine) The Qa'im bil-fi'l (kinetic Qa'im) who appears at the end of the dawr is the master of the later unveiling (kashf al-ukhra) and the great strike (batshat al-kubra), while Muhammad ibn Isma'il is master of the first unveiling.  ::terms: qa'im bil-fi'l, kashf al-ukhra, batshat al-kubra, muhammad ibn isma'il
- [S597] (doctrine) The qiyamat are many: a qiyamah is a gathering (ijtima') of forms with hashr and nashr, and the ranks (ma'dhun makfuf, ma'dhun mutlaq, da'i muharram, da'i mutlaq, da'i al-balagh, hujjat, abwab) each represent a qiyamah for those connected to them.  ::terms: qiyamat, ijtima', hashr, nashr, ma'dhun, da'i, hujjat
- [S598] (doctrine) These minor qiyamat are followed by the Qa'im of the great resurrection (qiyamat al-kubra), the master of the batshat al-kubra (great strike), with whom gather all the noble stations and all the Imams from the first 3,000 years (dawr al-fitrah) to the end of 7,000 years of the dawr al-satr.  ::terms: qiyamat al-kubra, batshat al-kubra, dawr al-fitrah, dawr al-satr
- [S599] (scripture) Amir al-Mu'minin alluded with 'ala ra's kulli asharat alaf sanah yaqumu min al-suwarat' — at the head of every 10,000 years a form stands up — pointing to the Qa'im who gathers the dawr al-fitrah (3,000) and dawr al-satr (7,000), totaling 10,000 years.  ::terms: amir al-mu'minin, asharat alaf sanah, qa'im, dawr al-fitrah, dawr al-satr
- [S600] (doctrine) Muhammad ibn Isma'il retains the name 'seventh Natiq' for his subtlety in the affair of God, yet he is unique in the rank of divine oneness (rutbat al-wahdat al-ilahiyyah), not arranged in the chain (silk) of the dawr al-satr but its terminator, having surpassed the seventh-Natiq rank to a higher one.  ::terms: muhammad ibn isma'il, natiq al-sabi', rutbat al-wahdat al-ilahiyyah, silk, dawr al-satr
- [S601] (doctrine) Counting any Natiq with his wasi and six mutimmin yields a seventh Natiq: Adam yields Nuh, Nuh yields Ibrahim, Ibrahim yields Musa, Musa yields 'Isa, 'Isa yields Muhammad, and Muhammad yields Muhammad ibn Isma'il.  ::terms: natiq, wasi, mutimmin, adam, nuh, ibrahim, musa, isa, muhammad
- [S602] (doctrine) The three concealed completers (mutimmu dawr al-satr) of Muhammad ibn Isma'il's dawr are the concealed Imams Maulana 'Abdullah, Maulana Ahmad, and Maulana Husayn, with their three abwab commanded to keep the Imam in taqiyyah and present themselves as Imam, the fourth being Imam 'Ali the Natiq to complete the count of seven.  ::terms: mutimmu dawr al-satr, a'immah mastUrin, abdullah, ahmad, husayn, abwab, taqiyyah
- [S603] (definition) The Ahl al-Haqq (People of Truth) are of two kinds: those of true gnosis and divine sciences, and those of pure devotion and righteous deeds; both perform righteous deeds and have loyalty to all the Imams.  ::terms: ahl al-haqq, ma'rifat al-haqiqah, ulum al-ilahiyyah, a'mal saliha, wilayat
- [S604] (doctrine) Upon separating from their dense bodies (kasa'if), the Imams who possess gnosis become the chief members (a'za' ra'isah) of the luminous temple (haykal nurani) of the Imam, with the form (surat) pulling the sensory soul (nafs hissiyah) to reach the haykal.  ::terms: kasa'if, a'za' ra'isah, haykal nurani, surat, nafs hissiyah
- [S605] (doctrine) The level of luminosity in the haykal is according to each one's measure in knowledge (ilm), gnosis (ma'rifat), and devotion (wilayat); those with pure devotion and righteous deeds but without true knowledge follow them at lesser ranks (lata'if below kasa'if).  ::terms: ilm, ma'rifat, wilayat, haykal nurani, lata'if
- [S606] (doctrine) The dense bodies (kasa'if) are preserved in the mightiest protection: returning to earth, then the tempered mixture, becoming rain, dissolving to vapor, ascending through the rays of the seven planets to the falak al-buruj (Saturn, Jupiter, Mars, Sun, Venus, Mercury, Moon below it).  ::terms: kasa'if, mizaj muntazid, falak al-buruj, kawakib, aflak
- [S607] (doctrine) The body parts ascend and take a place in the buruj as a successor (khalifa); from the buruj the prior occupant's portion (flesh, skin, marrow, nerves, veins) descends as a fine dust to earth and eventually becomes a human in the alam al-alfiyyah (thousand-year realm).  ::terms: buruj, khalifa, alam al-alfiyyah, kasa'if
- [S608] (doctrine) Allah created only essences (zawat/uqul); soul (nafs), body (jism), animals, plants, and minerals are progressively fallen forms of the intellect that delayed (ta'khir) in using its God-given powers, with the hardest fallen state being rock (sakhrah) — and we, not Allah, caused this fall.  ::terms: zawat, uqul, nafs, jism, ta'khir, sakhrah
- [S609] (doctrine) When these descended human forms reach maturity (bulugh al-hulm) they are called, respond, and the noble covenant (al-'ahd al-karim) is taken from them, ascending in religious ranks and true knowledge without doubt because their first karra was on certainty.  ::terms: bulugh al-hulm, al-'ahd al-karim, karra, rutab al-diniyya
- [S610] (scripture) A good deed once established (a created point) cannot be destroyed and its reward remains forever — its reward is tenfold while evil is onefold and can become non-existent — anchored in 'qad aflaha al-mu'minun' and the reward principle.  ::terms: hasanat, sayyi'at, karra, nuqta
- [S611] (authority) Sayyidna 'Ali ibn Husayn taught that all the points (nuqta) of those who will become believers in a given kawr are present with the Sayyid al-Ida'iyya, manifesting in the era appointed by his governance; likewise the mudabbir of the mawr holds the points of his whole mawr, and Adam al-Ibda'i holds the points of all who will progress.  ::terms: ali ibn husayn, nuqta, kawr, sayyid al-ida'iyya, adam al-ibda'i, mudabbir
- [S612] (ruling) Each rank returns to its same rank — bab as bab, hujja as hujja, da'i as da'i, mu'min as mu'min, mustajib as mustajib — and no one transgresses his first state even if he reaches higher knowledge; breaching ranks (kharq-e-maratib) like the hour-to-hour transition requires traversing all sixty minutes (degrees).  ::terms: bab, hujja, da'i, mustajib, kharq-e-maratib, rutbah
- [S613] (doctrine) When a soul is purified and ascends to the Chosen One, another soul (a member of the body that became worthy of ascent) is sent forth in the remaining body by the governance of the governors, eventually becoming a single essence; for martyrs (shuhada') both heart and liver become soul, granting a degree or two of advance.  ::terms: nafs, mustafa, munba'itha, shuhada', mudabbirat
- [S614] (scripture) These successful believers are those alluded to in 'qad aflaha al-mu'minun' — humble in prayer, turning from vain speech, active in zakat, guarding chastity except with wives or right-hand possessions — these qualities being their distinguishing mark (sima) of mustaqim al-sayr in outward and inward.  ::terms: qad aflaha al-mu'minun, salah, zakat, furuj, mustaqim al-sayr
- [S615] (ruling) Prayer is founded upon four pillars/shares (as-hum): wudu' (ablution), ruku' (bowing), sujud (prostration), and khushu' (humility), the first degree of khushu' being awareness of the meaning of what is recited.  ::terms: salah, as-hum, wudu', ruku', sujud, khushu'
- [S616] (doctrine) The purification of a mustaqim al-sayr (one of upright conduct who committed no gravely forbidden sin) is completed over 360 karrat, in which his whole body becomes pure — the sole of the foot reaching the station of the heart.  ::terms: mustaqim al-sayr, karrat, tasfiya
