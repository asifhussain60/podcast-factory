# SECTION 22: The Creative Sphere and the Resurrection Cycle

Lecture range: [47, 48]


## LEDGER SPINE TEACHINGS YOU MUST PRESERVE (each at full depth)

- [S780] (doctrine) Returning to the discourse of the Qaim's state: when he appears, the reckoning (hisab) occurs, the people of punishment meet their torment, and the reward of the people of reward is multiplied.  ::terms: qa'im, hisab, ahl al-'iqab, thawab
- [S781] (doctrine) The Qaim establishes the da'wah to extract a successor whose station is in establishing the da'wah, and he transfers with those in his fold to the rank of the Tenth Intellect (al-'aql al-'ashir), a perfect luminous intellect, one of the Primordial Intellects.  ::terms: da'wah, al-'aql al-'ashir, al-'uqul al-ibda'iyyah
- [S782] (doctrine) The principle 'la yukhlifu ahadun illa mithluhu' (no one succeeds except one like him): no one can be promoted until a universal (kulli) successor like himself is born; a partial (juzwi) successor does not enable promotion.  ::terms: khalifah, kulli, juzwi, jussa-i ibda'iyyah
- [S783] (authority) Sayyidna Sadiq told Sayyidna Jumali that where 'juzwi' is said the meaning is 'kulli' and vice versa — each term implies the other.  ::terms: Sadiq, Jumali, juzwi, kulli
- [S784] (doctrine) The Tenth Intellect ascends to the rank of the Ninth, and this Qaim, in the rank of the Tenth Intellect, becomes administrator (mudabbir) of the entire world of nature, celestial and terrestrial, perpetually transferring upward as each successor is appointed.  ::terms: al-'aql al-'ashir, al-'aql al-tasi', mudabbir, 'alam al-tabi'ah
- [S785] (doctrine) The chain of intellects ascends: ninth takes eighth's place, and so on, but the second (Munba'ith al-awwal, the First Emanation) remains in its sphere and is exalted in its second perfection (kamal-e-thani); the sphere of the First Intellect (the circle of unity, wahdat) can be reached by no one.  ::terms: Munba'ith al-awwal, 'aql awwal, wahdat, kamal-e-thani
- [S786] (doctrine) Every station transfers to the horizon (ufuq) of the Tenth by the customary habit until the completion of the term, which is 7,000 years (in the period of concealment, satr).  ::terms: ufuq, al-'ashir, satr
- [S787] (doctrine) In the dawr-e kashf the period is 10,000 years; after each 10,000 years a Qaim manifests, reaches the horizon of the primordial jussa, and the jussa places the Imams' forms under his authority; the Qaim manifesting after 50,000 years gathers all five qawa'im and becomes a universal Qaim.  ::terms: dawr-e kashf, qa'im, jussa-i ibda'iyyah, qawa'im
- [S788] (doctrine) After seven dawr-e kashf and seven dawr-e satr, a universal-of-universal Qaim gathers the qawa'im of the seven satr cycles (under which sit the kashf-cycle Qaims); when a qawr ends a new primordial jussa comes, is promoted, and the prior one is exalted, with each succession resting under the jussa-i ibda'iyyah's horizon (ufuq).  ::terms: dawr-e satr, qawa'im, jussa-i ibda'iyyah, ufuq, qawr
- [S789] (doctrine) At the start of the dawr-e kashf the resurrection (ba'th) of the bodies of the people of the dawr-e satr occurs; 'innal-ba'tha haqq' (indeed resurrection is true).  ::terms: ba'th, dawr-e kashf, dawr-e satr
- [S790] (doctrine) The first bodies taken/resurrected are those of the people of the Muhammadan dawr, due to their nearness to the Qaim's dawr, their nobility, and their being last in the dawr-e satr; then the bodies of the awliya' whose souls ascended to the world of purity ('alam al-safa') and whose noble bodies fermented bit by bit.  ::terms: dawr al-muhammadi, ba'th, awliya', 'alam al-safa'
- [S791] (doctrine) Then the bodies of all the sects (firaq) and the opposites whose souls transformed in their bodies are resurrected and made to taste the lesser punishment (al-'azab al-adna), not the greater.  ::terms: firaq, azdad, al-'azab al-adna
- [S792] (doctrine) Each dawr is established for 7,000 years, after which its Qaim rises and faces the resurrection (ba'th) of the people of the preceding nabi's dawr, in sequence: 'Isa, then Musa, then Ibrahim, then Nuh, then Adam — completing the dawr-e kashf.  ::terms: dawr, ba'th, Isa, Musa, Ibrahim, Nuh, Adam
- [S793] (doctrine) After the dawr-e kashf comes the dawr-e satr of 10,000 years, of which 3,000 years is the dawr of the people of astronomy, philosophy, and mathematics (nujum, falsafah, riyadiyyah) and 7,000 years is of Shari'ah.  ::terms: dawr-e satr, nujum, falsafah, riyadiyyah, shari'ah
- [S794] (doctrine) The dawr of kashf and satr, the Imam and Imamate, continue in unbroken chain (one to another, child after parent, no interruption or deviation from lineage) until the passing of the qawr of Adam, which is 360,000 years multiplied by its like.  ::terms: dawr al-kashf, dawr al-satr, imamah, qawr Adam
- [S795] (definition) A small cycle (qawr asghar / dawr) is 400,000 years; the great cycle (qawr a'zam) is 360,000 years multiplied by itself.  ::terms: qawr asghar, qawr a'zam, dawr
- [S796] (doctrine) At the universal resurrection, the tethers of the celestial spheres (rubatat al-aflak / pegs/gravity) loosen, everything on earth perishes (plants, animals, humans), the affair becomes as at first, and administration (tadbir) passes to Zuhal (Saturn).  ::terms: rubatat al-aflak, tadbir, zuhal
- [S797] (doctrine) Mountains and stones are pulverized, extreme cold and piled snow, clouds, fog, atmospheric darkness arise, vapor rises, rains and rivers burst until the entire earth becomes like a turbulent sea covered in darkness, the sun's light unable to penetrate the dense clouds due to Saturn's influence.  ::terms: zuhal, ghuyum, bukhar, shams
- [S798] (doctrine) When the time comes for the Qaim of the millennium to appear from the caves (mugharat) as at first, a pure light/substance ascends from the subtle parts (lata'if) and purest of the earth to become a successor (khalifah) to the sun and the rest of the stars and constellations.  ::terms: qa'im al-alfiyyah, mugharat, lata'if al-ard, khalifah, shams
- [S799] (doctrine) Things from the Ka'bah ascend to take the place of the sun; the sun, the six (other) stars, and the twelve constellations descend to earth and become fine dust, from which the new primordial jussa is nurtured after the earth settles.  ::terms: Ka'bah, shams, kawakib, buruj, jussa-i ibda'iyyah
- [S800] (doctrine) From the earth arise twenty-eight noble, excellent persons (fudala al-kurama) of the primordial corporeality (jussa al-ibda'iyyah); the sun becomes their Imam and chief, and the remaining stars and constellations are the twenty-seven who appear with his appearance.  ::terms: fudala al-kurama, jussa al-ibda'iyyah, shams, imam
- [S801] (doctrine) The Hudud, peace be upon them, are servants (khuddam) in the world of the body ('alam al-jirm) and the world of religion ('alam al-din).  ::terms: hudud, khuddam, 'alam al-jirm, 'alam al-din
- [S802] (doctrine) When a Hudud's subtle essences transfer to the spiritual world at death, his body remains in the earth; for three days whatever remained of the sensory soul (nafs al-hissiyyah), also called the airy soul (nafs al-rihiyyah), ascends to the sphere of the sun.  ::terms: hudud, nafs al-hissiyyah, nafs al-rihiyyah, falak al-shams
- [S803] (doctrine) How much of the vegetative soul (nafs al-namiyyah) ascends or remains in the grave for punishment depends on each Hudud's rank and degree of respect for the shari'ah during life.  ::terms: nafs al-namiyyah, nafs al-hissiyyah, shari'ah
- [S804] (doctrine) Upon the body's dissolution it becomes pulverized dust; the flesh, blood, nerves, veins, and bodily excesses (except the bones) become subtle vapor, ascend to the sphere of the constellations (falak al-buruj), take the place of a constellation, then descend to earth as dust, then to temperament and the blended, returning to edibles that do not enter fire.  ::terms: turab sahiq, bukhar, falak al-buruj, mizaj, ma'kulat
- [S805] (doctrine) The nourishment becomes a nutfah, then a person who is invited and answers, joining the awliya', ascending in the gnostic sciences (ma'arif) to become one of the serving Hudud in the Da'wat of Truth, with his final rank matching his initial rank.  ::terms: nutfah, awliya', ma'arif, hudud, da'wat al-haqq
- [S806] (ruling) A believer crosses one rank per cycle (there are ten ranks for a believer); only a martyr (shahid) crosses one or two ranks at once ('darajatan aw darajatayn').  ::terms: rutbah, shahid, darajah
- [S807] (scripture) The seventeen-fold du'a of light (Nuran fi...) names ten internal locations (heart, hearing, sight, tongue, hair, skin, blood, flesh, bones, nerves) and seven directions (front, behind, right, left, above, below, in the grave).  ::terms: Nuran, du'a, qabr
- [S808] (doctrine) The bones, due to their hardness (salabah), remain in the earth a long period as the Disposer (mudabbir) wills; they are then ground to dust, ascend as vapor to the temperament and blended, return to earth, and solidify in mines as rubies, pearls, and precious stored jewels.  ::terms: 'izam, salabah, mudabbir, ma'adin, yaqut, durr
- [S809] (doctrine) A human comprises an essence/soul (the divine creation), a sensory soul, and a vegetative soul; the luminous form (rational soul), developed through knowledge and action and free will granted by God, becomes greater than the soul itself.  ::terms: nafs, nafs al-hissiyyah, nafs al-namiyyah, surat al-nuraniyyah
- [S810] (doctrine) The vegetative soul is purified by ascending to the moon via the sun's rays, refined with the sun, passing through Mercury and Venus, then descending until the physical form (nasut) of the Imam's progeny is formed, after which the connection of Lahut (divinity) is established — this nasut-Lahut union is the meaning of 'La ilaha illallah'.  ::terms: nafs al-namiyyah, nasut, Lahut, La ilaha illallah
- [S811] (doctrine) The full return cycle is 1,000 years: 360 years residing in twelve constellations (30 years each) ascending and 360 returning, plus 280 years for ascending/descending to manifest as a human, so that a believer manifests again as a believer with consolidated point.  ::terms: buruj, istighath, nuqtah
- [S812] (doctrine) In each cycle one soul is consumed/transformed (the crimson heart-blood taking the form of soul); over 360 cycles (360,000 years) a person of straight path who avoided major sins reaches the station of the heart, and a respondent attains the rank of a natiq.  ::terms: dawr, natiq
- [S813] (doctrine) At the time of return, all the dispersed elements (vegetative soul, sensory soul, form, body, points) are assembled at the Qaim (the universal-intellect court), where individuality both merges and remains distinct, ultimately forming a single whole that seeks to become the Universal Intellect.  ::terms: qa'im, 'aql al-kulli, nuqtah
- [S814] (doctrine) The primordial jussa (jussa-i ibda'iyyah) that gathers the great cycle holds all the points (nuqat) from a single respondent up to a Qaim, each point's intensity (hiddat) established according to the intensity of faith in the prior cycle.  ::terms: jussa-i ibda'iyyah, qawr, nuqtah, hiddah
- [S815] (doctrine) The bones progress and solidify in mines as jewels, preserved long because they must manifest with the primordial jussa; old bones preserved from past cycles come to take the place of the body that has gone.  ::terms: 'izam, ma'adin, jussa-i ibda'iyyah
- [S816] (doctrine) When the Great Cycle's fulfillment arrives, the jewels (transformed bones) are ground, ascend, and become planets succeeding the seven planets and twelve constellations, preserving their places arranged by rank until the supports of the heavens loosen at the next cycle.  ::terms: kawr a'zam, jawahir, kawakib, buruj
- [S817] (doctrine) From the descending planets/constellations becoming dust, the physical form (nasut) of the primordial jussa is formed; the sun becomes the unique individual of the rank of oneness (the Qaim in the station of the First Originator), and the moon becomes its gate (bab) receiving the ascending forms.  ::terms: jussa al-ibda'iyyah, nasut, shams, bab, al-Mubdi'
- [S818] (doctrine) All the Hudud follow in service upon this pattern until all their bodies are purified, and nothing of them remains in the world of generation and corruption ('alam al-kawn wal-fasad) except returning to the abode of purity and eternity — honored, gifted, rich, forgiven, living, eternal, happy.  ::terms: hudud, 'alam al-kawn wal-fasad, dar al-baqa, mahall al-safa
- [S819] (scripture) Quran 37:61 ('and for the like of this let the workers work') is cited as an exhortation to strive for this eternal return.  ::terms: al-'amilun
- [S820] (doctrine) The paradise for the soul is Jannat al-Ma'wa (Sidrat al-Muntaha); the paradise for the body is the paradise of the presences (jannat al-hadarat) — worldly peace and comfort free of social strife — distinct from the realm of kashf.  ::terms: Jannat al-Ma'wa, Sidrat al-Muntaha, jannat al-hadarat, kashf
- [S821] (ruling) In the lofty paradises (jannat al-'aliyat) fasting is not obligatory and there is no hunger or hardship, since obligations of hardship are removed in the state of free choice.  ::terms: jannat al-'aliyat
- [S822] (claim) The seven planets and twelve constellations total nineteen, equal to the numerical value of Bismillah al-Rahman al-Rahim.  ::terms: kawakib, buruj, Bismillah
- [S823] (ruling) Of the 354-day (lunar) year, 100 days are the highest, marked by a tasbih; within these, 18 special days include 1st and 10th Muharram, 1st/15th/27th Rajab, 15th Sha'ban, the odd nights of Ramadan, Eid al-Fitr, Yawm Nuzulika, 'Arafah, Eid al-Adha, the three Ayyam al-Tashriq, and 18th Dhu'l-Hijjah.  ::terms: tasbih, Muharram, Rajab, Ayyam al-Tashriq
- [S824] (authority) The 18th of Dhu'l-Hijjah, Yawm al-Ghadir, is the most excellent day of the year; Imam Ja'far al-Sadiq affirmed three times by oath ('No, by God') that no day is better than it.  ::terms: Yawm al-Ghadir, Ja'far al-Sadiq
