# SECTION 12: The Souls and the Planetary Spirits

Lecture range: [24, 25]


## LEDGER SPINE TEACHINGS YOU MUST PRESERVE (each at full depth)

- [S345] (doctrine) Each of the seven planets governs (tadbir) the fetus for one month in sequence (Zuhal, Mushtari, Mirrikh, Shams, Zuhra, Utarid, Qamar), so it is born complete in the seventh month under the moon's governance.  ::terms: tadbir, kawakib al-sab'a, Qamar, shahr al-sabi'
- [S346] (doctrine) The heart's formation and movement occur in the fourth month under the sun's (Shams) governance, which is why the fetal heartbeat is felt then.  ::terms: Shams, qalb, harakah
- [S347] (doctrine) If the child is not born in the seventh month it remains until the eighth, when governance returns to Zuhal (the inauspicious star, nahs al-falak) whose action is coldness and dryness (the nature of death), so a child born in the eighth month appears dead.  ::terms: Zuhal, nahs al-falak, burudah, yubs, tab' al-mawt
- [S348] (doctrine) If the child remains until the ninth month it comes under Mushtari (the greater good fortune, sa'd al-akbar) whose action is heat and moisture (the nature of life), so it is born alive; the nayyirayn are Shams and Qamar, the sa'dayn are Mushtari and Zuhra, and Utarid is the scribe of the sphere (katib al-falak).  ::terms: Mushtari, sa'd al-akbar, nayyirayn, sa'dayn, katib al-falak
- [S349] (authority) Amir al-Mu'minin Ali defined the nafs al-namiyya (vegetative soul) as a natural power (quwwah tabi'iyyah) whose origin is the four natures, whose union is at the deposit of the drop, whose substance is from refined foods, whose action is growth and increase, and whose separation is caused by disharmony of materials, returning to the moon whence it began.  ::terms: nafs al-namiyya, quwwah tabi'iyyah, Ali ibn Abi Talib, numuww
- [S350] (definition) Everything beneath the sphere of the moon down to the point of the earth (the rock, sakhrah) is the Greater Vegetative Soul (namiyyat al-kubra), from which every lesser vegetative soul branches; the lesser vegetative soul is in reality the animal (hayawan).  ::terms: namiyyat al-kubra, sakhrah, namiyyat al-sughra, hayawan
- [S351] (definition) The Greater Sensory Soul (hissiyyat al-kubra) extends from the encompassing sphere (muhit) to the sphere of the moon; the lesser sensory soul (hissiyyat al-sughra) is the rational obligated human soul; the greater rational souls are the Hudud, and the lesser rational soul is the respondent (mustajib).  ::terms: hissiyyat al-kubra, muhit, hissiyyat al-sughra, natiqat al-kubra, mustajib
- [S352] (claim) These realities were never disclosed except by allusion (ramz) and from a tongue to an ear, but the author wrote them plainly without symbol or ta'wil at the questioner's request, in the language of haqa'iq.  ::terms: ramz, ta'wil, haqa'iq, daqa'iq
- [S353] (doctrine) When the newborn appears into the expanse of the earth a sensory power connects with it from the rays of the sun (heart of the sphere, center of the sensory), spreading through the body, sending movement, and uniting with the vegetative soul to become one thing nourished by the temperate breeze (nasim).  ::terms: mawlud, quwwah hissiyyah, shams, nafs al-namiyya, nasim
- [S354] (authority) Ali defined the nafs al-hissiyya as a celestial power (quwwah falakiyyah) whose origin is the spheres, union at physical birth, seat the heart, action sensation/movement/assault/victory, separation by disharmony of materials, returning to the sun whence it began.  ::terms: nafs al-hissiyya, quwwah falakiyyah, maqarruha al-qalb, Ali ibn Abi Talib
- [S355] (authority) Sayyidna al-Mu'ayyad (Hibatullah ibn Musa al-Shirazi, Bab al-Abwab) in his Majalis admonished: 'I wish I knew why you were drawn out from the spheres and bodies and made to descend stage by stage into the narrow places of the loins and wombs and brought out into the expanse of this abode.'  ::terms: al-Mu'ayyad, Bab al-Abwab, Majalis, aslab, arham
- [S356] (doctrine) The nutfah enfolds the vegetative soul drawn from terrestrial nourishment of the three kingdoms (mineral, plant, animal), which is drawn from the four mothers (fire, air, water, earth), and these four mothers are the powers of the celestial spheres.  ::terms: nutfah, mawalid al-thalathah, ummahat al-arba'ah, quwa al-aflak
- [S357] (doctrine) Allah created only spiritual essences (zawat ruhaniyya / ashbah nuraniyya), granting them existence, knowledge, life, and choice (ikhtiyar); those who misused their choice fell stage by stage into nufus, then sensory and vegetative souls, then bodies, animals, plants, and minerals, all being fallen spiritual essences.  ::terms: zawat ruhaniyya, ashbah nuraniyya, ikhtiyar, ilm, hayat
- [S358] (doctrine) Salvation (najat) is the return of all things to being luminous specters (ashbah nuraniyya); the units of a person's various fallen parts are held by the Governor (Mudabbir) and gathered at the Qa'im, then at greater Qa'ims, ascending ultimately toward the Ashir and the Jannat al-Ma'wa, none reaching Sidrat al-Muntaha.  ::terms: najat, ashbah nuraniyya, Mudabbir, Qa'im, Jannat al-Ma'wa, ashir
- [S359] (doctrine) God created the essences in the human form (shukhusan ala shakli al-insan) before man existed; the one who arrived first occupies the station of the heart/head and the one who came last the station of the feet, and they ascend in that same human-form arrangement.  ::terms: shakl al-insan, qamat alifiyya, sabiq
- [S360] (doctrine) The human form has 28 parts corresponding to the 28 letters of 'La ilaha illa Allah': the head is 'illa Allah,' the body is 'La ilaha,' with three parts, four parts, seven parts, and twelve apertures (eyes, ears, nostrils, mouth, nipples, navel, chest openings, lower orifices).  ::terms: shakl insani, la ilaha illa Allah, ajza', jharoka
- [S361] (authority) Sayyidna al-Mu'ayyad in Taj al-Aqa'id refutes that God created out of need, to take work, or to be worshipped, concluding that the cause of creation is unknowable except that God by His grace (fadl) and kindness brought existence from non-existence.  ::terms: al-Mu'ayyad, Taj al-Aqa'id, sabab al-khalq, fadl
- [S362] (doctrine) The newborn learns speech from its parents through babbling (munaghat) until its speech matures, and learning the Book of Allah increases its speech and perfection.  ::terms: mawlud, munaghat, nutq, kitabullah
- [S363] (doctrine) When the person connects with the true teacher (mu'allim al-sadiq), is given longing to enter the circle of the people of truth, takes the noble covenant (ahd al-karim) through the Imam of the time, and enters the secure sanctuary of Allah, a point of light neighbors his soul and connects with the Pillar of Light flowing from the Imam of the time.  ::terms: mu'allim al-sadiq, ahd al-karim, nuqtah min al-nur, amud al-nur, Imam al-zaman
- [S364] (doctrine) The more one benefits from the knowledge, gnosis, and love of the Imam and his hudud and walks in chastity, purity, and obedience to the Wali al-Amr, the more the point of light increases in vastness and light and the soul's illumination and substance increase.  ::terms: ma'rifat, mahabbat, wali al-amr, ishraq, nuqtah nuraniyyah
- [S365] (authority) The luminous point is a form (surah) which is the rational soul (nafs natiqah) imaged by the beneficiary from his benefactor; Ali defined it as an intellectual power (quwwah ilmiyyah), a transparent substance knowing in potentiality and actuality, living by nature.  ::terms: surah, nafs natiqah, quwwah ilmiyyah, jawhar shaffaf, Ali ibn Abi Talib
- [S366] (doctrine) Once the nafs natiqah is formed it cannot be destroyed, per the principle 'evil has no origin in the primary creation' (al-sharr la asla lahu fi'l-ibda'): evil, lying, darkness, and coarseness are not things but the absence/distance from good, truth, light, and subtlety.  ::terms: al-sharr la asla lahu fi'l-ibda', nafs natiqah, kathafah
- [S367] (doctrine) Allah created only truth and sustenance; man himself created the body and turned sustenance into excretions and falsehood, so man is the cause of his own coarseness, which is removed by progressing on the path.  ::terms: haqq, rizq, kathafah, jism
- [S368] (scripture) The spirit (ruh) is 'from the command of my Lord' (qul al-ruhu min amri rabbi); ruh is also used for the nafs natiqah, and when the nafs natiqah ascends high the command of the Lord (amr-i rabb) is at work.  ::terms: qul al-ruhu min amri rabbi, ruh, nafs natiqah
- [S369] (argument) The subtle nafs natiqah, by abiding with the coarse nafs hissiyya, gradually transforms it into itself, which is salvation, as a stone takes on fragrance from prolonged company with perfume.  ::terms: nafs natiqah, nafs hissiyya, najat
- [S370] (authority) Ali defined the nafs al-kulliyyah (Universal Soul) as a divine power (quwwah ilahiyyah), a widespread substance knowing in potentiality and actuality, whose origin is the (First) Intellect, from which it takes support and toward which it calls, its union being at the religious birth (ahd al-sharif) and its seat being knowledge and reality.  ::terms: nafs al-kulliyyah, quwwah ilahiyyah, al-'aql, Ali ibn Abi Talib, wiladat al-diniyyah
- [S371] (doctrine) Each soul returns to its origin: the nafs namiyya to the moon, the nafs hissiyya to the sun, the nafs natiqah to the Pillar of Light / world of life, and the nafs al-kulliyyah to the Intellect / world of lights; the lower's origin is always the rank above it.  ::terms: ila ma minhu bada'at, nafs namiyya, nafs hissiyya, nafs natiqah, nafs al-kulliyyah
- [S372] (doctrine) The nafs al-kulliyyah is the supreme Word of Allah (kalimatullah al-ulya), the tree of Tuba (shajarat al-tuba), and the Garden of Abode (Jannat al-Ma'wa) near the Sidrat al-Muntaha (the First Intellect); whoever knows it precedes and is saved, whoever is ignorant of it is misguided.  ::terms: kalimatullah al-ulya, shajarat al-tuba, Jannat al-Ma'wa, Sidrat al-Muntaha
- [S373] (doctrine) Every station in the world of religion corresponds to the First Intellect (named Sidrat al-Muntaha) and every Imam to it; the Bab corresponds to the First Emanated (muba'ath al-awwal, named Jannat al-Ma'wa), so Ali alluded to the Bab by the nafs al-kulliyyah and to Jannat al-Ma'wa by the muba'ath al-awwal.  ::terms: al-'aql al-awwal, Sidrat al-Muntaha, muba'ath al-awwal, Bab, nafs al-kulliyyah
- [S374] (doctrine) The Pillar of Light flows from the Bab to the Hujjah, to the Da'i, to the Ma'dhun, to the believer (mu'min), and to the respondent (mustajib) who takes the noble covenant.  ::terms: amud al-nur, Bab, Hujjah, Da'i, Ma'dhun, mustajib
- [S375] (authority) Ali ibn Abi Talib founded Arabic grammar by explaining to Abu'l-Aswad al-Du'ali that the 28 letters form meaningful words (kalimah) of three kinds, ism (noun), fi'l (verb), and harf (particle), corresponding to luminous essences, their intentions, and the relation between them.  ::terms: Ali ibn Abi Talib, Abu'l-Aswad al-Du'ali, ism, fi'l, harf
- [S376] (doctrine) The grammatical rules mirror the luminous world: every subject (fa'il) is raised (marfu') because it acts and advances foremost, every object (maf'ul) is established (mansub), and every preposition-governed noun (majrur) is broken (maksur).  ::terms: fa'il marfu', maf'ul mansub, majrur maksur
- [S377] (argument) Arabic is the original, still-existing language because the foundation of 'La ilaha illa Allah' rests on its 28 letters, which encode all the laws, and Allah revealed the Quran in 'a clear Arabic tongue.'  ::terms: la ilaha illa Allah, bi lisanin 'arabiyyin mubin
- [S378] (doctrine) At the time of a respondent's departure, the Tenth Intellect (receiving the command, linked to the Imam) moves the Pillar of Light named 'the flowing' (al-jari), and the movement connects to the Imam of the time, who knows it signals the soul's purification and time of transfer.  ::terms: al-'aql al-'ashir, amud al-nur, al-jari, Imam al-zaman, naqlah
- [S379] (doctrine) The Imam moves the Pillar of Light and attracts the departing one toward himself; the luminous form (nafs natiqah) is drawn by the pillar's attraction, and the neighboring sensory soul (nafs hissiyya) is drawn along with it to the extent it obeyed the rational soul in the world, in the form of the universal body, leaving nothing small or great.  ::terms: amud al-nur, nafs natiqah, nafs hissiyya, mujawir, haykal al-jism al-kulli
- [S380] (scripture) Souls cannot pass through the regions of heaven and earth except by an authority (sultan), which is the noble form (haykal) of the Imam where all forms gather, per 'you will not pass through except by an authority.'  ::terms: la tanfudhuna illa bi sultan, haykal, Imam
