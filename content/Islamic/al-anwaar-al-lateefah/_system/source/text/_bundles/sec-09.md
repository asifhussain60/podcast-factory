# SECTION 09: Man, the Quintessence of the Two Worlds

Lecture range: [17, 18]


## LEDGER SPINE TEACHINGS YOU MUST PRESERVE (each at full depth)

- [S251] (argument) The most virtuous single person among the twenty-two/twenty-eight individuals is, among the kingdoms, like the red ruby among mineral gems, pure gold among malleable bodies, the date palm among plants, and man among animals, yet more noble, subtle, and exalted than all.  ::terms: yaqut al-ahmar, dhahab al-ibriz, nakhlah, insan
- [S252] (doctrine) This virtuous person is the quintessence (zubdah) and choicest of the two worlds, the celestial (jirmani) and the physical (jismani).  ::terms: zubdat al-'alamayn, jirmani, jismani, safwah
- [S253] (argument) The first man, looking at himself, his kind, and the heavens, earth, stars, rivers, spheres, night and day, came to know by his own self that there is a Creator unlike them, negated divinity from all created existents, and affirmed it for their Originator alone, bearing witness to His oneness (wahdaniyyah).  ::terms: wahdaniyyah, mubdi', tawhid, bi-dhatihi
- [S254] (doctrine) Through this witnessing the man connected with the divine sources, the radiant lights, and the angelic supports, reached the second perfection (al-kamal al-thani), was supported, illumined, and made to know all the arts and what was and what will be (ma kana wa ma sayakun).  ::terms: al-kamal al-thani, ma kana wa ma sayakun, anwar al-sha'sha'aniyyah
- [S255] (doctrine) He stood in the world of bodies in the station of the First Intellect / Tenth Intellect, unique in the rank of oneness (wahdah), marked with origination (ibda'), and called the twenty-seven persons to the oneness of Allah (tawhid), purification (tanzih), and abstraction (tajrid).  ::terms: tawhid, tanzih, tajrid, rutbat al-wahdah, ibda'
- [S256] (ruling) He took confirmed covenants from them, taught them the lawful and forbidden, the rulings and laws, legislated the laws of religion (qawanin al-diyanah), and commanded them to marry forbidden women whom the sunnah made permissible so as to populate the world.  ::terms: al-uhud al-mu'akkadah, halal, haram, qawanin al-diyanah, nikah
- [S257] (doctrine) He chose sixteen persons: a Bab, a Hujjah, two da'is (da'i balagh and da'i mutlaq), and twelve made hujaj al-layl kept in his presence; the remaining eleven (and a twelfth in his own island) were stationed in the islands as preserved ranks until the end of time.  ::terms: Bab, hujjah, da'i balagh, da'i mutlaq, hujaj al-layl
- [S258] (doctrine) Each of the chosen ones was commanded to conduct the mission (da'wah) in his assigned island and given twelve hujaj al-nahar; these hujaj are free of the afflictions of falsehood and of veiling/deception owing to the loftiness of their ranks.  ::terms: da'wah, hujaj al-nahar, sitr, takayid
- [S259] (scripture) The twenty-eight individuals are the 'possessors of knowledge' (ulu al-'ilm) and people of witnessing whom Allah mentioned in 'Allah bears witness that there is no god but Him, as do the angels and the possessors of knowledge,' where Allah=First Intellect, angels=other intellects, and ulu al-'ilm=the twenty-eight.  ::terms: ulu al-'ilm, shahida Allahu annahu la ilaha illa huwa, al-mala'ikah
- [S260] (doctrine) The twenty-eight exist upon originating bodies (al-jasad al-ibda'iyyah) without procreation (bi-ghayri tanasul); their head, the negator-affirmer-unifier who established the shari'ah, is opposite the First Originator and learned tawhid without any teacher preceding him, by his own self.  ::terms: al-jasad al-ibda'iyyah, bi-ghayri tanasul, al-nafi al-muthbit al-muwahhid, min dhatihi bi-dhatihi
- [S261] (doctrine) With every Imam the twenty-eight individuals manifest: the Imam (Dhu'l-Sayf Da'iyah) with twenty-seven, and the Qa'im with twenty-eight; Allah established this pattern (naqsh) to recur perpetually.  ::terms: Dhu'l-Sayf Da'iyah, Qa'im, naqsh
- [S262] (scripture) Allah created the world in six days, and one day with Allah equals one thousand years, so creation occurred over six thousand years.  ::terms: sittat ayyam, yawm, alf sanah
- [S263] (doctrine) The kalimah 'La ilaha illa Allah' has twenty-eight letters/qualities and structures the ranks: nafi=Bab, ithbat=Dhu'l-Sayf Da'iyah, with the common letters and the four words mapping to the Imam's hierarchy.  ::terms: la ilaha illa Allah, nafi, ithbat, huruf
- [S264] (doctrine) Between the Originator and the originated is a Word (bayna al-mubdi' wa al-mubda' kalimah), the light of the kalimah 'La ilaha illa Allah,' which is higher than the First Originated.  ::terms: bayna al-mubdi' wa al-mubda' kalimah, nur, la ilaha illa Allah
- [S265] (definition) The Pillar of Light (amud al-nur) is the knowledge flowing from Allah to the First Intellect, then through each successive intellect down to the Tenth Intellect, the governor of the world of nature.  ::terms: amud al-nur, al-aql al-awwal, al-aql al-'ashir
- [S266] (doctrine) Whoever connects with the Pillar of Light through the noble covenant (ahd al-sharif) attains success (fawz) and grace and becomes among the people of mercy; it is the strong rope of Allah and the firmest handhold (al-urwat al-wuthqa) that never breaks.  ::terms: ahd al-sharif, fawz, al-urwat al-wuthqa, amud al-nur
- [S267] (doctrine) The Pillar of Light is spiritual, not physical light; it descends not upon the body but upon the rational soul (nafs natiqah), which is present at the time of the noble covenant.  ::terms: amud al-nur, nafs natiqah, ahd al-sharif
- [S268] (doctrine) The Pillar of Light is a magnet (maghnatis) that attracts the forms of the beneficiaries to the forms of the benefactors, holding and preserving their forms and souls after death.  ::terms: maghnatis, suwar al-mustafidin, suwar al-mufidin
- [S269] (doctrine) From the hypocrite (munafiq), apostate (murtadd), and the one who incurs wrath (maghdub alayhi), the divine form connected to them is snatched away by the appropriate pillar, and their soul returns deficient and base into the body.  ::terms: munafiq, murtadd, maghdub alayhi, tuslabu suratuhu
- [S270] (doctrine) The Pillar of Light preserves and conveys to each person only the portion of food/sustenance (his ma'bar) destined for him, distinguishing it from others and extracting it from wherever it may be; this is divine providence and divine decree.  ::terms: ma'bar, fuzalat al-awliya', inayat ilahiyyah, muqaddarat ilahiyyah
- [S271] (ruling) The Pillar of Light is reserved for those who take the noble covenant and possess wilayah (devotion), and the wilayah of Mawla Ali is necessary for it.  ::terms: wilayah, ahd al-sharif, wali
- [S272] (doctrine) It is called the flowing knowledge, the pervading light, the spirit, and the Word; all forms of spiritual and physical existence from the First Intellect to the respondent are connected and joined to it, as in 'a person will be resurrected with whom he loves' (al-mar'u yuhsharu ma'a man ahabba).  ::terms: al-ilm al-jari, al-nur al-sari, al-mar'u yuhsharu ma'a man ahabba
- [S273] (argument) The forms cling to the Pillar of Light like flies stuck to an extended stick smeared with honey, since it carries, attracts, and elevates them at the time of their ascent.  ::terms: amud al-nur, su'ud, haml
- [S274] (ruling) When one of the ranks or respondents has intimacy with his spouse, the divine forms turn away and deviate from him, returning only after completion (state of purity is restored by ghusl), as a kindness and mercy from Allah.  ::terms: janabah, ghusl, amud al-nur, mubasharah
- [S275] (ruling) The Prophet said 'in every hair there is janabah' (fi kulli sha'ratin janabah), so one must wash every hair thoroughly in ghusl, and clothes worn during intimacy should not be worn for prayer.  ::terms: fi kulli sha'ratin janabah, ghusl
