# SECTION 16: Husayn, the Imamate, and the Line of Wilayah

## LEDGER SPINE TEACHINGS YOU MUST PRESERVE (each at full depth)

- [S533] (doctrine) When Amir al-Mu'minin was in the unmatched state no one else reached, the Tenth Intellect connected with him totally (ittisalan kulliyan), the creative intellects glanced at him eternally and cast their rays, and the divine substances connected with him above every other station except the Qa'im.  ::terms: amir al-mu'minin, aql al-ashir, ittisal kulliyan, uqul ibda'iyah, mawad ilahiyah
- [S534] (doctrine) Amir al-Mu'minin's nuqta of the muhtajib has the highest station after Dhu al-Sa'y al-Ibda'iyah, manifested from Sarandip at the start of the Daur al-Kashf, until the inbi'ath of the Qa'im's nuqta which will be greater still.  ::terms: nuqta, muhtajib, dhu al-sa'y al-ibda'iyah, sarandip, inbi'ath
- [S535] (doctrine) Amir al-Mu'minin is a part (juz') in relation to the Qa'im who is whole (kull), and likewise the stations of the Imams are partial relative to 'Ayn al-'Azimah the Imam of Imams, who is in turn partial relative to the Qa'im, who is partial relative to Dhu al-Sa'y al-Ibda'iyah.  ::terms: juz', kull, ayn al-azimah, qa'im, imam al-a'immah
- [S536] (scripture) The Prophet alluded to this with 'al-Hasan wa al-Husayn imamani bi-haqqin qama aw qa'ada wa abuhuma khayrun minhuma' (Hasan and Husayn are two rightful Imams whether they rise or sit, and their father is better than them).  ::terms: hasan, husayn, imamani, qiyam, qu'ud
- [S537] (argument) The Prophet placed qiyam (rising, Husayn) before qu'ud (sitting, Hasan) though Hasan is the earlier Imam, because to enact the imamate openly among the people is qiyam (as Husayn did) while Hasan remained silent.  ::terms: qiyam, qu'ud, hasan, husayn, imamate
- [S538] (doctrine) From his state divine miracles and great signs appeared in Amir al-Mu'minin, all of nature became obedient to him, and some Shi'a out of deification (ilahiyat) called him God.  ::terms: mu'jizat ilahiyah, amir al-mu'minin, ilahiyat
- [S539] (scripture) 'Ali's saying 'ana al-awwal wa al-akhir' (I am the First and the Last) means he is the first of the forms of the preceding cycles and the last of them (the last being the Qa'im), being first and last of the adwar.  ::terms: ana al-awwal wa al-akhir, adwar, suwar
- [S540] (scripture) Surat al-Shams takes 14 oaths and demonstrates Arabic grammatical gender (shams feminine, qamar masculine, sama' and ard feminine by convention), with all pronouns 'ha' referring to the sun.  ::terms: wa al-shams, mu'annath sama'i, qiyasi
- [S541] (scripture) 'Ali's saying 'ana al-batin' refers to the hidden knowledge from which the subtle hidden divine essences and substantial luminous souls (forming the haykal of 'Ayn al-'Azimah) came; 'ana al-zahir' is his appearance to sight through divine miracles.  ::terms: al-batin, al-zahir, ayn al-azimah, haykal
- [S542] (doctrine) The righteous manifest deeds are the first forms established for the awliya' from interpretive hearts and divine wisdoms in all cycles, all interpretive/real/divine wisdoms ultimately connecting through their Imams to the Imam of Imams Abu Talib.  ::terms: a'mal zahirah, awliya', qulub ta'wiliyah, hikam ilahiyah, abu talib
- [S543] (doctrine) All Imams gather through the bab/bab al-abwab process (da'i, then hujjat, then bab) and connect with 'Ayn al-'Azimah through the Prophet as the bab; capable forms join that haykal while the rest are given to the coming ones.  ::terms: bab al-abwab, ayn al-azimah, hujjat, da'i, haykal
- [S544] (scripture) 'Ali's saying 'ana nuqiltu Adam wa Nuh wa Ibrahim wa Musa wa Isa, wa ana naba'tu al-nabiyyin wa ana arsaltu al-mursalin' (I transported the prophets, I made the prophets prophets, I sent the messengers), the speaker of these forms from the end of the dawr al-kashf until his rising.  ::terms: nuqiltu, naba'tu al-nabiyyin, arsaltu al-mursalin, dawr al-kashf
- [S545] (scripture) When the flowing light connected with 'Ali totally he knew what was and what will be, and all the world of nature became before him like a thrown ball with nothing hidden, anchored in 'inna li manzilah lam takhtur ala qalb bashar'.  ::terms: nur jari, ittisal kulliyan, manzilah
- [S546] (scripture) Lordship (rububiyah) and Divinity (ilahiyah) do not occur to human hearts except for the Qa'im among them — interpreted not as a divinity claim but as the relation of the lord above and the one below.  ::terms: rububiyah, ilahiyah, qa'im
- [S547] (doctrine) All creation knows Allah with outward gnosis (zahir al-ma'rifah), the people of realities know Him with the reality of His gnosis, and no one knew 'Ali with the reality of his gnosis except the Messenger and the Imams of his progeny.  ::terms: ma'rifah zahirah, haqiqat ma'rifah, ahl al-haqa'iq, ali
- [S548] (doctrine) When people heard 'Ali's claims, groups variously said he is al-ghayb (the Unseen), the First Originated (al-mubda' al-awwal), or the First Emanation (al-inbi'ath al-awwal), and voices diverged; the subtle secret was hidden from all but the fewest of the awliya'.  ::terms: al-ghayb, al-mubda' al-awwal, al-inbi'ath al-awwal, awliya'
- [S549] (doctrine) 'Ali's hijab was revealed (after Dhu al-Sa'y al-Ibda'iyah) so that he as the mustaqarr could settle the accounts owed to the mustaqarrin by the prophets who served them in earlier eras, while the Prophet, the qaim over prophets and messengers, gathers all the prophets.  ::terms: hijab, mustaqarr, mustaqarrin, qisasat
- [S550] (doctrine) Imam Husayn performed qiyam to settle the accounts of the transgressions in the wars of the Prophet's and 'Ali's times; without it the haykal of Imam Husayn (with the Master of the Universe) could not have developed.  ::terms: husayn, qiyam, haykal, qisasat
- [S551] (doctrine) The reward in the durud (salutation) upon the Prophet and his family exists to establish one's spiritual connection with them; it is a prayer whose acceptance is the reward, drawn from their vast unseen treasuries containing gathered forms of crores of years.  ::terms: durud, salli ala, suwar
- [S552] (claim) A qawr (also dawr) equals 400,000 years; 399 qawrs have passed since Dhu al-Sa'y al-Ibda'iyah manifested, and the world is in the last part of the first 400,000-year dawr whose forms are with 'Ayn al-'Azimah.  ::terms: qawr, dawr, dhu al-sa'y al-ibda'iyah, ayn al-azimah
- [S553] (authority) Ja'far ibn Mansur al-Yaman stated that Allah does not accept the prophethood of any prophet, the selection of any wasi, the imamate of any wali, nor any act of obedience — however exhaustive the worship — except through the wilayah of 'Ali ibn Abi Talib, and whoever comes without it has his prophethood, successorship, and deeds nullified.  ::terms: ja'far ibn mansur al-yaman, wilayah, ali ibn abi talib, nubuwwah, wasayah, imamah
- [S554] (doctrine) 'Ali is the confluence (majma') of all the Imams and successors (awsiya') from the beginning of the cycles until his advent, which is why nothing is accepted without his wilayah.  ::terms: majma', a'immah, awsiya', wilayah, adwar
- [S555] (authority) Maulana Abdan's saying (in Majmu' al-Tarbiyah) that 'the jum'ah is upon the service of the master for his slave' is proven, because the children of Ishaq (manifest da'wat) served the children of Isma'il (the lofty divine stations) throughout the past cycles.  ::terms: abdan, majmu' al-tarbiyah, jum'ah, ishaq, isma'il
- [S556] (doctrine) When the forms gathered with 'Ali (Qa'im of Isma'il's progeny) and the children of Ishaq gathered with the Natiq (the Prophet, Qa'im of Ishaq's progeny), justice made it obligatory for 'Ali to serve the Prophet as requital (qadha') for the prior service of Ishaq's children to Isma'il's children.  ::terms: amir al-mu'minin, natiq, qadha', mizan al-adl, ishaq, isma'il
- [S557] (definition) The Arabic qadha' means to complete/fulfill (qadha'an ma fata) what was missed, not merely a missed-then-made-up prayer as commonly understood in Urdu.  ::terms: qadha', qadha'an ma fata
- [S558] (doctrine) This service of the master for his slave — 'Ali standing before, striving with, fighting the enemies of, and defending the Prophet — was requital for the service rendered by the prophets in the eras of Ibrahim, Musa, and 'Isa, measure for measure (hazwan bi-hazwin), with nothing small or great left unaccounted because the scale of justice is established.  ::terms: khidmat al-mawla, ibrahim, musa, isa, mizan al-adl
- [S559] (doctrine) The Tenth Intellect ('Aql al-'Ashir), the musta'id da'iya in the realm of life, is veiled by Muhammad, supports and looks upon him, and aids him through the mediation of jadd, fath, and khayal upon his reaching the rank of hijabiyyah.  ::terms: aql al-ashir, musta'id da'iya, muhtajib, jadd, fath, khayal, hijabiyyah
- [S560] (scripture) Every natiq, wasi, and imam must receive instruction and progress rank by rank, anchored in 'wa Allahu akhrajakum min butuni ummahatikum la ta'lamuna shay'a' (Allah brought you from your mothers' wombs knowing nothing).  ::terms: natiq, wasi, imam, ta'lim
- [S561] (doctrine) The Prophet acquired knowledge from Ubayy ibn Ka'b (alluded to as Jibra'il) at his beginning, then was elevated to Zayd ibn 'Amr the da'i of his island (alluded to as Mika'il), then to the one above (Israfil).  ::terms: ubayy ibn ka'b, jibra'il, zayd ibn amr, mika'il, israfil
- [S562] (scripture) Ibrahim's progression through the ranks is shown by the Quranic account of seeing the star, moon, and sun and rejecting each that sets, anchored in 'fa-lamma janna alayhi al-laylu ra'a kawkaban'.  ::terms: ibrahim, kawkab, qamar, shams, maratib
- [S563] (scripture) 'Ali progressed similarly, learning at the side of the Prophet and of Khadijah bint Khuwaylid (the hujjah of Imam Abu Talib), and was alluded to as Ibn Maryam (analogized to 'Isa, Khadijah to Maryam), anchored in 'wa lamma dhuriba ibnu Maryama mathalan'.  ::terms: ali, khadijah, hujjah, abu talib, ibn maryam
- [S564] (authority) 'Ali said the Messenger taught him a thousand doors (chapters) of knowledge, and from every door a thousand more opened (one lakh / a thousand thousand).  ::terms: ali, alfa bab, ilm
