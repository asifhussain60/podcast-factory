# SECTION 14: The Cycle of Unveiling and the House of Ibrahim

Lecture range: [28, 29]


## LEDGER SPINE TEACHINGS YOU MUST PRESERVE (each at full depth)

- [S450] (doctrine) At death the Nafse Namiyah of believers travels with the senses in the form of rihyat from the moon (qamar) toward the sun (shams), then through the kawakib and aflak, descending onto things fire does not touch.  ::terms: nafse namiyah, rihyat, qamar, shams, aflak
- [S451] (doctrine) The Imam and his household consume the descended rihyat, and from it the Imam's offspring are formed, their bodies taking shape from rihyat and their Nafse Namiyah from the sun's subtleties.  ::terms: rihyat, nafse namiyah, shams, takwin
- [S452] (doctrine) When the Imam's child reaches four years, the gathered forms and souls of the believers reach the Babul Abwab in order and connect with the nafs of the Imam's child, while the Imam's form becomes a ruh.  ::terms: babul abwab, nafs, ruh, tarbiyat
- [S453] (definition) The human form at the Babul Abwab becomes the haykal, and the Babul Abwab becomes the qalbe haykal, with each rank retained at its proper station.  ::terms: haykal, babul abwab, qalbe haykal
- [S454] (doctrine) When the Qaimul Qiyam (Imam of the Imams) comes, a similar grand gathering occurs forming qawaim of qawaim and oceans of oceans, including all departed believers.  ::terms: qaimul qiyam, qawaim
- [S455] (doctrine) Every Imam is brought out (istikhraj) from the da'wat of his father, each establishing his da'wat until he extracts a successor, continuing forever in the Daur al-Kashf and Daur as-Satr.  ::terms: istikhraj, da'wat, daur al-kashf, daur as-satr
- [S456] (doctrine) In the Daur al-Kashf the esoteric/real sciences appear and are read in gatherings and from pulpits, with no enforcement of the apparent (zahir) ordinances.  ::terms: daur al-kashf, ulum al-batina, haqiqa
- [S457] (doctrine) Lahut cannot manifest without Nasut, and the Nasut-e-tabi'i (in which relationships exist but the five senses do not) is necessary for the manifestation of the relationships of the khuddam and the Imam.  ::terms: lahut, nasut, nasut-e-tabi'i, khuddam
- [S458] (claim) Although Shari'ah injunctions are not enforced in the Daur al-Kashf, its people are so pious that their voluntary acts (tatawwu'at) of prayer, fasting, and charity exceed our obligatory duties (fara'id).  ::terms: daur al-kashf, tatawwu'at, fara'id, sadaqat
- [S459] (definition) In the Daur as-Satr the da'wat is of two kinds: an esoteric hidden da'wat (batinah khafiyyah) and a manifest clear da'wat (zahirah jaliyyah).  ::terms: daur as-satr, da'wat batinah, da'wat zahirah
- [S460] (definition) The esoteric hidden da'wat is the division of the divine stations (maqamat ilahiyyah), luminous structures (hayakil nuraniyyah), and their abwab, hujaj, du'at, and ma'dhun.  ::terms: maqamat ilahiyyah, hayakil nuraniyyah, abwab, hujaj, du'at
- [S461] (doctrine) In the era of Adam the people of the esoteric da'wat were in extreme concealment (istitar) out of fear of opponents, pharaohs, and adversaries due to scarcity of affinity, as the progeny of Qabil gained dominance and Habil's progeny went into concealment.  ::terms: istitar, taghatti, qabil, habil, imam hunaid
- [S462] (doctrine) The manifest du'at openly call toward the Imam of the time because of the proximity of affinity between them and the rest of the world, and they extract those with sound guidance toward the esoteric da'wat of their masters.  ::terms: du'at zahirah, munasabah, mawali
- [S463] (doctrine) The du'at act under the command of the Imams, take responsibility from them, speak by their word, recognize the loftiness of their stations, and continue thus through all eras.  ::terms: du'at, imam, adwar
- [S464] (authority) Sayyidna Ali (cited as Ali bin al-Husayn al-Jamali) said in a khutbah, 'I am nothing but like a pen in the hand of the Imam,' as a pen in the hand of a scribe.  ::terms: sayyidna ali, khutbah, qalam
- [S465] (doctrine) When Adam's era completed, Nuh rose as Natiq with his wasi Sam, then the Imams of his era and their du'at, until Ibrahim rose as Natiq.  ::terms: natiq, nuh, sam, ibrahim, wasi
- [S466] (doctrine) Ibrahim was a confluence (majma') of the forms of the people of the two preceding eras (Adam and Nuh), unlike Adam and Nuh who were themselves da'i for that confluence.  ::terms: majma', ibrahim, adam, nuh
- [S467] (argument) When a Natiq abrogates his Shari'ah, the emergence of fudala' necessitates changing some Shari'ah laws (concerning halal/haram), while the worship and commands of Allah remain unchangeable, anchored in 'Innad-dina indallahil-Islam'.  ::terms: natiq, shari'ah, fudala', halal, haram
- [S468] (scripture) Ibrahim is a luminous structure (haykal nurani) who attained all three ranks, with Adam standing in the station of tanzil, Nuh opposite ta'wil, and Ibrahim opposite the haqa'iq, anchored in the Quranic 'wa Ibrahimalladhi waffa'.  ::terms: haykal nurani, tanzil, ta'wil, haqa'iq, ibrahim
- [S469] (doctrine) Ibrahim is the son of the preceding Imam Salih, the one who brought him into being and pointed toward him, and in him were gathered prophethood, messengership, successorship, and imamate.  ::terms: salih, nubuwwah, risalah, wasayah, imamah
- [S470] (doctrine) The Daur al-Fitrah is the last 3,000 years of the 50,000-year Daur al-Kashf, in which two kinds of people emerged: bearers of the sciences of realities and bearers of the apparent sciences.  ::terms: daur al-fitrah, daur al-kashf, haqa'iq, zahir
- [S471] (claim) The total count is 57,000 years (50,000 Daur al-Kashf plus 7,000 Daur as-Satr) multiplied to reach 400,000 years, and the present is the last part of this first 400,000-year dawr.  ::terms: daur al-kashf, daur as-satr, dawr, qawr
- [S472] (doctrine) Adam began the Daur as-Satr after the Daur al-Fitrah; in the Daur al-Fitrah there were prophets, and the era of priesthood, astrology, and sorcery was at its peak.  ::terms: adam, daur as-satr, daur al-fitrah
- [S473] (authority) The Qaim of the Daur al-Kashf is Imam Hunaid, who became the Hijab-e-A'la (Highest Veil).  ::terms: qaim, daur al-kashf, imam hunaid, hijab-e-a'la
- [S474] (definition) With the nutaqa' the people of their eras gather as a gathering of forms, but no haykal is formed; the term haykal applies only when all forms gather at the Babul Abwab to form the complete human form (form of alif).  ::terms: nutaqa', haykal, babul abwab, alif
- [S475] (doctrine) The ten ranks ascend in order: Mustajib, Momin, Mukasir, Ma'dhun, Da'i, Hujjat, Bab, then Imam at the eighth rank, with Wasayat and divine speech (nutq-e-ilahi) above imamate.  ::terms: mustajib, momin, mukasir, ma'dhun, da'i, hujjat, bab, imam, nutq-e-ilahi
- [S476] (doctrine) The bearers of divine speech (nutq-e-ilahi) are the three Ha's: Hunaid, Ibrahim, and Hashim, with the Prophet in the second rank, distinguishing a Natiq from a divine Natiq.  ::terms: nutq-e-ilahi, hunaid, ibrahim, hashim, natiq
- [S477] (doctrine) Allah created from non-existence the intellects (uqul), luminous specters (ashbah nuraniyah), and essences (zawat), not forms, and the word nafs was not yet used for them.  ::terms: uqul, ashbah nuraniyah, zawat, ibda'
- [S478] (doctrine) Allah bestowed three things upon the created intellects: life/existence (hayat), knowledge (ilm), and free will (ikhtiyar) — gifts that no one can take away and present in every human.  ::terms: hayat, ilm, ikhtiyar
- [S479] (scripture) The tenth intellect (ashir) delayed in using its free will due to corrupt imagination (wahm-e-fasid), descending from intellect into the form of a nafs, anchored in 'Ya ayyatuhan-nafsul-mutma'innah... irji'i'.  ::terms: ashir, wahm-e-fasid, nafs, nafsul-mutma'innah
- [S480] (doctrine) The word nafs (and nufus) originated only from the tenth intellect (ashir); when he later returned to the form of intellect he became the 'Aql-e-Kulli (Universal Intellect) and attained flawless perfection.  ::terms: nafs, ashir, aql-e-kulli
- [S481] (doctrine) The Nafse Kulli is the ashir (also called Nafse Mutma'innah); the souls of the Imams are also called Nufuse Kulli because they immediately accepted the call of the ashir and gained a station in the realm of life.  ::terms: nafse kulli, ashir, nafse mutma'innah, alam-e-hayat
- [S482] (doctrine) The center of the Nafse Hissiyah is the sun, the center of the Nafse Namiyah is the moon, and the Nafse Kulli is in the dimensionless realm of life encompassing all, with effects flowing into the dimensional world.  ::terms: nafse hissiyah, nafse namiyah, nafse kulli, shams, qamar
- [S483] (doctrine) Through further descent the nafs becomes a body (jism); the body was a nafs and the nafs was an intellect, showing the chain of descent.  ::terms: jism, nafs, aql
- [S484] (definition) The Nafse Natiqa is the spiritual form of knowledge and action created by one's own essence (which Allah created), making it the believer's real acquisition through free will.  ::terms: nafse natiqa, zat, ilm, ikhtiyar
- [S485] (doctrine) A non-believer (ghair-e-momin) is a fallen physical form of a believer who has a soul but not a Nafse Natiqa, lacking the form of knowledge and action established through the 'Ahd-e-Sharif.  ::terms: ghair-e-momin, nafse natiqa, ahd-e-sharif
- [S486] (doctrine) The essence is all intellect; soul and body are merely changed forms of intellect, and the return journey ascends in the reverse order of descent (body first, then soul).  ::terms: zat, aql, nafs, jism
- [S487] (doctrine) At the 'Ahd-e-Sharif one receives the Nafse Natiqa as the khameer-e-nizamat performed by one's predecessor a thousand years earlier, who planted a seed now obtained by the successor.  ::terms: ahd-e-sharif, nafse natiqa, khameer-e-nizamat
- [S488] (doctrine) When the four ranks gathered in Ibrahim, he established his da'wat until he brought forth his son Isma'il, though his son Ishaq had been born before Isma'il.  ::terms: arba' al-rutab, ibrahim, isma'il, ishaq
