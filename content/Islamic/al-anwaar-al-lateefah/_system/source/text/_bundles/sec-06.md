# SECTION 06: The Elements and the Frame of the Cosmos

## LEDGER SPINE TEACHINGS YOU MUST PRESERVE (each at full depth)

- [S145] (doctrine) In a second, weaker motion the stars cast rays toward the rock, found no passage, and (being weaker than the first motion) were cut off before returning to their origins, stopping mid-way and mixing with the body of air; from this mixing the sphere of the breeze (kura-e-nasim, the breathable atmosphere, ~100 miles) formed — cool, moist, moderate — by which plants and animals have life.  ::terms: kura-e-nasim, atmosphere, hararah, ummahat
- [S146] (doctrine) The four mothers/elements (ummahat, called the pillars/arkan) are the second group: fire, air, water, earth; the breeze connecting to the earth's caves congealed into water, producing perpetual springs aided by spring-water and rain.  ::terms: ummahat, arkan, uyun, springs
- [S147] (definition) The world of substance ('alam al-jirm) and world of body ('alam al-jism) together are called the Absolute Body (al-jism al-mutlaq), where corporeality (jismaniyyat) began; collectively they are prime matter (hayula) and form (surah) — hayula being in reality the world of mothers (fire, air, water, earth) and surah being the world of spheres from the muhit to the moon.  ::terms: alam al-jirm, al-jism al-mutlaq, hayula, surah
- [S148] (doctrine) The aflak are in the station of fathers (aba') and the ummahat in the station of wives/spouses (azwaj — four, as four wives are permitted in Shari'ah), and the offspring (awlad) born in the world are minerals (ma'dan), plants (nabat), and animals (hayawan).  ::terms: aba, azwaj, awlad, ma'dan, nabat, hayawan
- [S149] (definition) The world of mothers is in reality the Greater Vegetative Soul (al-namiyat al-kubra), its limit being from the sphere of the moon to the center (of the rock); the world of spheres is the form (surah), called the fathers (aba') and in reality the Greater Sensitive Soul (al-hissiyat al-kubra), from the muhit to the sphere of the moon.  ::terms: namiyat al-kubra, hissiyat al-kubra, nafs
- [S150] (doctrine) The sphere's first universal motion imparted intense heat and dryness near it, forming the sphere of fire (kurat al-nar / kurat al-athir, ether); a more distant, moderate heat with moisture formed the sphere of air (kurat al-hawa); when motion became distant and parts static, coldness and dryness formed the sphere of the frozen (kurat al-zamharir) — like waves around a swimmer, strongest near, motionless far.  ::terms: kurat al-nar, kurat al-athir, kurat al-hawa, kurat al-zamharir
- [S151] (doctrine) When the mothers were arranged (called the pillars/arkan) and their smoke rose and mixed, every abstract intellect looked toward one sphere among the nine spheres (falak al-muhit, falak al-buruj, and seven planets); the First Intellect's attention was on the falak al-muhit, the second on the falak al-buruj, and so on — the tenth's attention on the earth, which is separate from the nine planets.  ::terms: arkan, uqul mujarrada, nine spheres, earth
- [S152] (doctrine) Divine providence made the seven planets the managers/arrangers (mudabbir) of the world of mothers and the generations (mawalid); the world of mothers is called the world of generation and corruption ('alam al-kawn wa al-fasad).  ::terms: mudabbir, mawalid, alam al-kawn wa al-fasad
- [S153] (argument) The whole arrangement of spheres, mothers, and rock exists for the rock (the earth) because man's descent is upon it; things descend easily from the spheres but cannot climb up from the rock (which accepts nothing) — man is in the world of mud, and the entire process is to enable his ascent.  ::terms: sakhrah, earth, descent, ascent
- [S154] (doctrine) The whole cosmic process unfolds in three divisions: first division = length, breadth, depth; second division = the shell (sadaf) was created; third division = the trenches/valleys (khudd) were created — all to enable the final creation (man) to be purified gradually through trials, which the original luminous world (without time-constraint) could not accomplish.  ::terms: sadaf, khudd, three divisions
- [S155] (doctrine) The seven planets were appointed managers (tadbir) of the world of generation and corruption, each for a duration of fifty-one thousand years and a little more (nif); their effects elevate the corrupt parts of the mothers so things ascend from falak to falak al-muhit, to the world of life, to the world of light — beginning purification.  ::terms: tadbir, fifty-one thousand years, kawn wa fasad, alam al-nur
- [S156] (doctrine) Ascent to perfection proceeds through nested temples (haykal): preparation for the Millennial Resurrection (qiyamat al-alfiyyah) raises beings into the Sensible Divinity (lahut al-mahsus), then from haykal to haykal — Imam, Qa'im, Hujjat, Mawla — and finally from the world of life to the world of light ('alam al-nur).  ::terms: haykal, qiyamat al-alfiyyah, lahut al-mahsus, alam al-nur
- [S157] (doctrine) The seven planets are: Zuhal (Saturn), Mushtari (Jupiter), Mirrikh (Mars), Shams (Sun), Zuhrah (Venus), 'Utarid (Mercury), Qamar (Moon); all are made from the penitent/repentant ones (nadimin/ta'ibin), but Zuhal and Mirrikh retained the quality of inauspiciousness (nuhusat) because their companions had listened to Iblis before repenting.  ::terms: Zuhal, Mirrikh, nadimin, nuhusat
- [S158] (doctrine) Governance began with Zuhal (Saturn) because it is the highest planet; its place was assigned for its coldness, dryness, inauspiciousness, and power of annihilation — its function is to demolish and level everything first (a demolition squad), which is its specific action, not its essence (it is itself a penitent/tawwab).  ::terms: Zuhal, nuhusat, tawwab
- [S159] (doctrine) The entire world of spheres ('alam al-aflak) is of a fifth nature (tabi'atun khamisah) — a special combining nature from which the four natures (heat, dryness, moisture, coldness) emerged; each planet is associated by approximation with the action that issues from it.  ::terms: tabi'atun khamisah, four natures, aflak
- [S160] (doctrine) Each planet's 51,000-year reign begins with 1,000 years of solitary governance (mufarrad), after which each remaining planet serves as its minister (wazir) for 1,000 years successively; Zuhal's first minister was Mushtari (saf'd al-akbar, softening dryness, sowing every praiseworthy thing via takhmir), then Mirrikh (generating coldness/dryness and the evil/vile things via takhannuth), ending with Qamar.  ::terms: wazir, mufarrad, takhmir, takhannuth, sa'd al-akbar
- [S161] (definition) Takhmir is sowing/fermenting good things (placing seed-parts in the earth) and takhannuth is sowing corrupt/vile things; the cycle (dawr) of each planet completes 357,000 years plus 3,000 nif = 360,000 solar years (400,000 lunar), called the lesser cycle (kawr al-asghar).  ::terms: takhmir, takhannuth, dawr, kawr al-asghar
- [S162] (scripture) A thousand years equals one day with Allah (per the Qur'anic measure); a person's spiritual manifestation in the luminous world ('alam nurani), where his pledge (bay'at) was taken, corresponds in time to his worldly manifestation — if not fully manifested in a thousand years, part of it definitely manifests.  ::terms: thousand years, bay'at, alam nurani
- [S163] (doctrine) The greatest cycle (kawr al-a'zam) is 360,000 (or 400,000 lunar) years multiplied by itself; after one kawr al-a'zam the bonds (magnetic pulls) of the spheres loosen — this is the total resurrection (qiyamat al-kulliyya), in which the entire world of things is annihilated while the falak al-muhit remains safe and continues governing.  ::terms: kawr al-a'zam, qiyamat al-kulliyya, falak al-muhit
- [S164] (doctrine) At death, only vapors leave the body, but the subtle nafs and sensory soul (nafs hissi) ascend; the major dense part of a being remains and is gathered later through successive returns (karrat) — gathered with the Imam, then the Qa'im, then a greater Qa'im — the spirit (ruh) acting as a magnet drawing one's scattered parts together into a single unit.  ::terms: nafs hissi, karrat, ruh, Qa'im
- [S165] (scripture) At the end of a kawr, governance returns to the first sphere as the first form, after the devastation of all inhabitants of the earth's surface — fine and heavy clouds, snow, sleet, and severe cold spread (Zuhal's function) — fulfilling the Qur'an 'As We began the first creation, We shall bring it back; a promise upon Us.'  ::terms: kawr, first creation, Zuhal, resurrection
- [S166] (doctrine) Resurrection (husayb) is initiated at the beginning of every kawr (not in the very first kawr, which was only preparation); 400,000 kawr al-a'zam make one death (mawt), and after each death a total governance (tadbir kulli) occurs in which the spheres' shells and constellations are transformed.  ::terms: husayb, kawr, mawt, tadbir kulli
- [S167] (doctrine) In Zuhal's solitary first millennium the world had severe cold, accumulated snow, light and heavy clouds, thick fog, abundant rains, the occurrence of mountains and stones, and atmospheric darkness; in the second millennium (Mushtari as minister) rains dried, stones and mountains broke, the atmosphere warmed, and the earth became a wet plain across the twelve islands.  ::terms: Zuhal, Mushtari, twelve islands
- [S168] (doctrine) In the third millennium (Mirrikh as Zuhal's minister) coldness and dryness generated whole crawling creatures of the earth (harashat), scorpions, snakes, geckos (wazagh), lizards (zab), Banat Wardan, locusts (jarad), and water creatures — produced as complete organisms, unlike the first-cycle fermentation (khamair).  ::terms: harashat, Mirrikh, jarad, khamair
- [S169] (ruling) Next came predatory animals (siba'), wolves (zi'ab), clawed and tusked beasts (the disobedient/arrogant, who tear apart), and every hard mineral originating from iron and copper — generated by the conjunction of the two inauspicious ones (Zuhal and Mirrikh), which are the two magnets most specific for the vile and base; iron and copper are why wearing them in prayer is forbidden.  ::terms: siba, Zuhal, Mirrikh, iron, copper
- [S170] (doctrine) Ra's and Dhanab (the two 'slow ones,' the jawzahar / 'uqdatayn, head and tail of the dragon) are imaginary calculations with no ascended physical body; they are parts of those who hastened in the 'Ashir's repentance then became most disobedient, so they were kept high (beneath the moon's sphere, near the two hujjats) rather than among the base, by special divine intent to preserve Allah's justice.  ::terms: Ra's, Dhanab, jawzahar, uqdatayn
- [S171] (doctrine) Ra's and Dhanab are the two opposites for the two worlds (earthly nature and the world of light), especially the enemies of the two luminaries (Shams and Qamar), causing the killings and vile things spread in the world; their effect on the sun and moon is the eclipses (kusuf and khusuf), and the dragon/tail (Dhanab) is more severe than Ra's.  ::terms: Ra's, Dhanab, Shams, Qamar, kusuf, khusuf
