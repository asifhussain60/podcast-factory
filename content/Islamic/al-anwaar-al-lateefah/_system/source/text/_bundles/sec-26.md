# SECTION 26: Retribution, Inheritance, and the Word of Unity

Lecture range: [57, 58, 59]


## LEDGER SPINE TEACHINGS YOU MUST PRESERVE (each at full depth)

- [S1008] (doctrine) The scale of justice (mizan al-'adl) is established by Allah upon all His servants — wali, exoteric person, or opponent (didd) — leaving out neither small nor large deed but enumerating it, and recompensing good with good and evil with evil.  ::terms: mizan al-'adl, wali, zahiri, didd, la yughadiru saghiratan wa-la kabiratan illa ahsaha
- [S1009] (authority) The natiq passed away poisoned and his wasi (Amir al-Mu'minin) killed; likewise Hasan, Husayn, al-Mustansir and al-'Amir passed by poison or being killed — and this befell them by the requirement of wisdom and the demand of justice as retribution (qisas).  ::terms: natiq, wasi, qisas, al-Hasan, al-Husayn, al-Mustansir, al-'Amir
- [S1010] (doctrine) Even the Friends of Allah, the chosen and trustees over His revelation, are subject to qisas with no escape; had pardon (musamaha) been permissible for anyone they would be most deserving of it.  ::terms: Awliya' Allah, qisas, musamaha, safwat Allah, umana'
- [S1011] (doctrine) A person's deeds extend to and are accounted against the people of his era, so retribution due from one era can be exacted in another — as Imam Husayn bore recompense for battles of Mawla Ali.  ::terms: qisas, ahl al-dawr, Imam Husayn, Mawla Ali
- [S1012] (scripture) There is no escape from the scale of justice for any creature — noble or base, mighty or humble — and Allah shows no favoritism (muhabat) in any act of good or evil, as in 'whoever does an atom's weight of good/evil will see it'.  ::terms: mizan al-'adl, muhabat, mithqala dharratin
- [S1013] (ruling) Everything a person does of good or evil returns to him as recompense except what the shari'a makes lawful and is delivered by mutual consent and submission — like marriage on Allah's Book and the Prophet's sunna, in which there is no qisas.  ::terms: mukafat, ihlal al-shari'a, tarad, inqiyad, nikah, qisas
- [S1014] (ruling) Good deeds earn a tenfold reward and evil deeds a single recompense; evil (sharr) and sin (gunah) differ, with sin born from evil.  ::terms: sharr, gunah, thawab
- [S1015] (ruling) Sins against Allah are forgiven by Allah through repentance, but rights/retributions owed to His servants are not forgiven until the servant forgives or Allah arranges means for the dues to reach the wronged party, else qisas occurs.  ::terms: istighfar, qisas, huquq al-'ibad
- [S1016] (ruling) Acts done lawfully by the shari'a's command — such as killing on a valid order — do not obligate retribution.  ::terms: ahkam al-shari'a, qisas
- [S1017] (ruling) Stoning for adultery (zina) is commanded collectively by Allah's order to remove the collective harms that such acts breed in the community, and it was the command in earlier religions too.  ::terms: zina, rajm, amr Allah
- [S1018] (authority) The Prophet said no scratch of a twig, throbbing of a vein, or stumbling of a foot afflicts anyone except by a sin (dhanb), and what Allah forgives is much more — meaning such afflictions are pending retributions.  ::terms: khadshat 'udin, ikhtilaj 'irqin, nakbat qadamin, dhanb, qisas
- [S1019] (authority) Unaccepted supplications in the world are stored and rewarded on the Day of Judgment, and a sacrifice (intended for a sick person who still dies) yields its benefit, multiplied in the next cycle if not reached in this one.  ::terms: du'a, qurbani, karrat, dawr
- [S1020] (ruling) A man's delivering the present gift, dower, possession and mutual consent with shari'a's permission nullifies any right of qisas; but intercourse without shari'a's permission or without delivering dower obligates qisas.  ::terms: hadir, mahr, qabd, tarad, ihlal al-shari'a, qisas
- [S1021] (doctrine) Qisas became obligatory on the Imams (maqamat) for their lawful actions because there was no mutual consent from the killed party, and it may be exacted in the same cycle or a previous one — illustrated by 'the master served his slave' (khadam al-mawla 'abdahu): prophets served the hidden Imams, repaid by the Prophet through Mawla Ali.  ::terms: qisas, maqamat, khadam al-mawla 'abdahu, Mawla Ali, anbiya'
- [S1022] (doctrine) The kalima La ilaha illallah relates to numerical structures (negative/positive, dotted/undotted letters) such as 'ayn-lam-ya being three letters and the letters yielding totals of 40, 70, and 12 through their compositions.  ::terms: la ilaha illallah, nuqta, 'ayn, lam, ya
- [S1023] (authority) In Ali were combined seventy special characteristics (khususiyat), each testified to by Allah and His Messenger, that could not be gathered in any other person (in whom more than ten could not combine).  ::terms: khususiyat, Ali
- [S1024] (authority) Ali delivered extempore (murtajjal/fil-badih) sermons demonstrating his mastery — a roughly three-page khutba containing no alif, and a thirteen-page khutba containing no dotted letters.  ::terms: khutba, alif, murtajjal, Nahj al-Balagha
- [S1025] (authority) Umar said 'Lawla Ali lahalaka Umar' (Were it not for Ali, Umar would have perished) — recorded as said seventy times.  ::terms: Lawla Ali lahalaka Umar
- [S1026] (doctrine) The deeds of the Imams and their friends — jihad against opposites, killing, applying hadd, stoning by command of Allah and the Imam — earn a reward that returns to their luminous structures (hayakil nuraniyya) and souls even while the qisas remains in place.  ::terms: af'al al-maqamat, mujahadat al-azdad, hadd, hayakil nuraniyya, qisas
- [S1027] (ruling) Killing in self-defense (dafa'-i hifazat), by the command of Allah, the Messenger, or those in authority, is rewarded though its qisas also remains; such deeds seeking Allah's reward are not lost (their reward not wasted) but the fullest requital still applies.  ::terms: dafa'-i hifazat, thawab Allah, qisas al-awfa, Uli'l-amr
- [S1028] (doctrine) The qisas of the opposites falls upon their dense bodies (katha'if) by Allah's command, due to the aversion (karahiya) of the opposites' souls to what was done to them and their lack of pleasure in it — distinguishing defense from those who initiate bloodshed.  ::terms: qisas, katha'if, nufus al-azdad, karahiya
- [S1029] (ruling) Inheritance law arises at death (not lifetime gifts/hiba), and a gift in terminal illness (marad al-mawt) has no consideration; valid hiba requires giving possession.  ::terms: wirathat, hiba, marad al-mawt, qabd
- [S1030] (ruling) In inheritance the parents each receive one-sixth, the husband a fourth (or eighth with children), the wife an eighth (or fourth without children); the order of death between mother and child decides who inherits.  ::terms: wirathat, thumun, rub', sudus
- [S1031] (scripture) The scale of justice is established by Allah even in the date-stone's membrane (qitmir) and groove (naqir) — i.e., in the most minute matters.  ::terms: mizan al-'adl, qitmir, naqir
- [S1032] (doctrine) The Imams' actions against their opposites in their cycles are taking qisas from them for deeds committed in previous cycles and ages, which divine providence ('inaya ilahiyya) necessitated by gathering them in the Imams' time as justice and requital for their sins.  ::terms: qisas, adwar salafat, 'inaya ilahiyya, ijtima'
- [S1033] (authority) Mawla Ali composed the grammatical maxim that every doer is nominative (kullu fa'ilin marfu'un), every object accusative (kullu maf'ulin mansubun), every genitive broken (kullu majrurin maksurun) — esoterically: the one who acts is elevated, the imitator supported, the one needing to be drawn is broken.  ::terms: fa'il, maf'ul, majrur, marfu', mansub, maksur
- [S1034] (doctrine) Passing the hand over the face at Bismillah symbolizes the connection (ittisal) between the five upper and five lower limbs of the body, mirroring the connection of the five luminous essences ('aql, nafs, jadd, fatah, khayal) with the five worldly essences (Imam, Bab, Hujjat, Da'i, Ma'dhun).  ::terms: Bismillah, ittisal, 'aql, nafs, jadd, fatah, khayal, Imam, Bab, Hujjat, Da'i, Ma'dhun
- [S1035] (doctrine) Bismillah al-Rahman al-Rahim is the highest summary, but La ilaha illallah is higher; its 28 letters split into 15 dotted and 13 undotted, while Bismillah shows five repeated letters facing five non-repeated (khamsatun bi-iza'i khamsah).  ::terms: la ilaha illallah, Bismillah, khamsatun bi-iza'i khamsah
- [S1036] (doctrine) The five non-repeated letters of Bismillah correspond to the five Luminous-Realm individuals who need not manifest, the five repeated to those who manifest in the world repeatedly, their connection forming one kalima — paralleling the body and the spiritual realm.  ::terms: 'alam nurani, 'alam jismani, 'alam ruhani, kalima
- [S1037] (doctrine) La ilaha illallah is the light of the word (nur-e kalima) from God; Bismillah and all Quranic verses descended from the Circle of the First Intellect / Circle of Origination, but their entire source is La ilaha illallah.  ::terms: nur-e kalima, da'irat 'aql al-awwal, da'irat ibda', la ilaha illallah
- [S1038] (doctrine) Acts a person does that the shari'a has not made lawful and the Imam has not commanded — killing one's own kind, looting, usurping, taking by oppression or treachery — quickly darken the soul's essence and become a barrier between it and the path of guidance.  ::terms: qatl, nahb, ghadb, zulm, ghashm, jawhar al-nafs, sabil al-rushd
- [S1039] (doctrine) Whether a sin is erased after the Imam applies a hadd depends on the qisas of the Mudabbir across the cycles and returns (karrat), of which each Imam has 360; the Prophet said one who seeks forgiveness (istighfar), even seventy times, is not among the persistent sinners.  ::terms: hadd, qisas, Mudabbir, karrat, istighfar, Ma asarra man istaghfara
- [S1040] (ruling) Sins against Allah are forgiven through seeking forgiveness, but rights of His servants are not forgiven until that servant forgives or Allah arranges means (like the Bitaq method) to bring the recompense to the wronged.  ::terms: istighfar, huquq al-'ibad, Bitaq
- [S1041] (scripture) The darkened soul is driven into the paths of error, ensnared in sins, made to hate the hudud and the people of truth, and cast into the fire of Hell — but the Quran says the disbelievers abide (khalidina) without 'forever' (abada), unlike the believers' eternal gardens, a mercy from Allah.  ::terms: nar jahannam, khalidina fiha, abada, ula'ika hum sharr al-bariyya, khayr al-bariyya
- [S1042] (ruling) Vile acts like adultery (zina) inescapably require qisas (la budda min al-qisas), with the soul's essence darkening, eclipsing and descending.  ::terms: zina, la budda min al-qisas, jawhar al-nafs, takassuf, inhidar
- [S1043] (doctrine) The qisas for human major sins is exacted quickly in the descending garments (qumus): human form (Riss), then monkey-like (Waqs), 'Aks, Khars, then mineral, passing through the seven stages.  ::terms: qamis al-riss, qamis al-waqs, qamis al-'aks, khars, kabair
- [S1044] (doctrine) Remaining qisas for adultery passes into the Qamis al-Waqs, the human-like composition (monkey, nasnas, etc.), because copulation among monkeys exceeds that of other animals; the nasnas is a northern species said to have faces on their chests and to stand on one leg.  ::terms: qamis al-waqs, qird, nasnas, munakaha
- [S1045] (doctrine) When many men sin with one woman, the qisas is distributed across the descending garments — some in Riss, most in Waqs and 'Aks and Khars and other descended animals — proportional to the abundance or scarcity of the act.  ::terms: qisas, qumus al-inhidar, qamis al-riss, qamis al-waqs
- [S1046] (doctrine) The descending forms (qumus) are temporary; only the thousand-year stature (Qamat-e Alfiyyah) has a (contingent) permanence, being the form on which the luminous essences and the world were created, with density and atoms stripped away stage by stage.  ::terms: qumus, Qamat-e Alfiyyah, zawat nuraniyyah
- [S1047] (ruling) Qisas and punishment apply in the descending animal stages despite the acts there occurring with consent and payment, because the original human act was done without the shari'a's permission, though the form of punishment differs.  ::terms: qisas, marada, taslim qima, tahlil al-shari'a
- [S1048] (doctrine) The descending garments (darakat) apply to adultery and its likes (theft, robbery, usurpation), whereas the opponents of the Imam descend into the lower levels (adrak) rather than the darakat.  ::terms: darakat, adrak, zina wa ma shakalahu, azdad
