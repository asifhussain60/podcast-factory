# SECTION 11: The Cycles of Concealment and Unveiling

## LEDGER SPINE TEACHINGS YOU MUST PRESERVE (each at full depth)

- [S305] (doctrine) Upon completion of every 7,000 years a Qa'im arises like the previous one, continuing until the completion of 50,000 years (the seven Qa'ims of the dawr al-kashf).  ::terms: sab'at alaf sanah, Qa'im, khamsun alf sanah
- [S306] (doctrine) The governance (tadbir) of the primordial body (jussa ibda'iyyah) of the first mawr extends over the entire kawr, including all natural laws and even partial phenomena like viruses and plagues, which are corrupted particles drawn to one by magnetic affinity from a prior connection.  ::terms: jussa ibda'iyyah, tadbir, mawr, kawr
- [S307] (ruling) Allah forgives His own due (sins against Him), but debts and wrongs between servants must be settled by the servants themselves or compensated through other means.  ::terms: qisas, qarz
- [S308] (doctrine) When the seventh Qa'im of the dawr al-kashf rises he has power, honor, and grace not possessed by the preceding six, gathering all six Qa'ims with him, since he stands for them in the station of the 'other creation' (khalq al-akhar) which is the spirit to the body and the culmination of the dawr al-kashf.  ::terms: al-Qa'im al-sabi', khalq al-akhar, ruh, muntaha dawr al-kashf
- [S309] (doctrine) Upon the seventh Qa'im's passing the dawr al-sitr begins; his son rises after him, weakness (du'f) enters the mission, the dawr al-kashf reaches its term, and the mission goes into concealment (tasattur), known only secretly among its people through symbols and indications.  ::terms: dawr al-sitr, du'f, tasattur, ramz
- [S310] (doctrine) In the dawr al-sitr no apparent knowledge exists except mathematical sciences (engineering, astronomy, and the like), the Imams are in extreme taqiyyah and concealment, and the hands of opponents are dominant for the first 3,000 years.  ::terms: dawr al-sitr, ulum al-riyadiyyah, taqiyyah, sitr
- [S311] (doctrine) The first of the concealment-cycle (mutaqa'idi dawr al-sitr) was Adam (the mustawda' Adam, Abdullah bin Tayjun), established as Natiq by the command of his Imam (Hunayd), each Natiq governing by the command of the Imam of his time.  ::terms: Adam, mustawda', Natiq, Hunayd
- [S312] (doctrine) Each Natiq establishes laws and a shari'ah whose outward rites are symbols pointing to knowledge of God's spiritual and physical limits (hudud) and His oneness, and appoints a wasi/asas to interpret the inner meaning, the Imamate continuing in succession.  ::terms: shari'ah, hudud, tawhid, wasi, asas
- [S313] (doctrine) The succession of Natiqs in the dawr al-sitr: Adam (opponent unnamed), Nuh (Imam Hud, opponent Awj bin Unaq), Ibrahim (Imam Salih, opponent Namrud bin Kan'an), Musa (Imam Ud bin Udad, opponent Fir'awn), Isa (Imam Khuzayma), and Muhammad (Imam Abu Talib, opponents Abu Lahab and Abu Jahl).  ::terms: Nuh, Ibrahim, Musa, Isa, Muhammad, zidd
- [S314] (doctrine) Every prophet has an opponent (zidd/enemy), as the Prophet said, since every light has a shadow.  ::terms: zidd, li-kulli nabiyyin 'aduwwun
- [S315] (definition) Tawhid, tanzih, and tajrid are distinct: tawhid is recognizing the createdness of all hudud, tanzih is purifying Allah from all attributes and names, and tajrid is abstracting the state of ibda' from all creatures (a state none can perform).  ::terms: tawhid, tanzih, tajrid, ibda'
- [S316] (doctrine) The wasis of the Natiqs: Adam's was Shith (after Habil), Nuh's was Sam, Ibrahim's was Isma'il, Musa's was Harun, Isa's was Sham'un al-Safa, and Muhammad's was Ali ibn Abi Talib, who designated Hasan as mustawda' and Husayn as mustaqarr.  ::terms: wasi, Shith, Sam, Isma'il, Sham'un al-Safa, mustawda', mustaqarr
- [S317] (doctrine) Basis of precedence (awwaliyyat) is not the light but who reaches the homeland (watan) first; despite Natiqs being established first but appearing later, the first remains first.  ::terms: awwaliyyat, sabq, watan
- [S318] (doctrine) The line of Imams ran to Muhammad ibn Isma'il (the seventh, Natiq al-Sadis), then the a'immat al-khulafa' beginning with Abdullah ibn Muhammad ibn Isma'il, completing with al-Mu'izz li-Din-illah; then the dawr al-ashhad beginning with al-Aziz billah, completing with al-Imam al-Tayyib Abi'l-Qasim; then the a'immat al-abdal beginning with the son of al-Tayyib.  ::terms: Muhammad ibn Isma'il, a'immat al-khulafa', al-Mu'izz, al-Tayyib, a'immat al-abdal
- [S319] (doctrine) Muhammad ibn Isma'il is the Natiq al-Sadis and gatherer of the six nutaqa'; his rank is very high and his lights are acquired from the Prophet's horizon, from Imam Hasan and Imam Husayn.  ::terms: Muhammad ibn Isma'il, Natiq al-Sadis
- [S320] (doctrine) In the Tayyibi era 'Bismillah al-Rahman al-Rahim' is interpreted of the Imams: Allah=Muhammad ibn Isma'il, the ism=Imam al-Tayyib (21st Imam), and al-Rahman al-Rahim=Ma'add (14th Imam al-Mu'izz) and Ma'add (28th Imam Ahmad).  ::terms: Bismillah al-Rahman al-Rahim, al-Tayyib, Ma'add, al-Mu'izz
- [S321] (doctrine) After al-Tayyib's line, the Hujjah of the Qa'im al-Qiyamah rises and the 7,000 years of the dawr al-sitr (the dunya) complete, as the Natiq said 'the age of the dunya is 7,000 years.'  ::terms: Qa'im al-Qiyamah, dawr al-sitr, umr al-dunya saba'at alaf sanah
- [S322] (authority) The book Anwaar al-Latifah was copied by Sayyidna Da'ud bin Ahmad, the first mansub (buried in Ahmedabad), who marked two places where Sayyidna Ja'far derived proofs (hujjat) by istimbat from the original text.  ::terms: Da'ud bin Ahmad, mansub, Ja'far, istimbat
- [S323] (doctrine) The original Natiq al-Sabi' is the Qa'im al-Qiyamah who gathers all six nutaqa'; but because Muhammad ibn Isma'il's rank rose to hijabiyyah (the veil of Ayn Azimah), higher than Natiq al-Sabi', the point of that rank was conferred upon him.  ::terms: al-Natiq al-sabi', Qa'im al-Qiyamah, hijabiyyah, Ayn Azimah
- [S324] (doctrine) The Prophet's final station is hijabiyyah (the maqam mahmud), the veil of Ayn Azimah, giving him superiority over all mustawda' and mustaqarr Imams up to the Qa'im.  ::terms: hijabiyyah, maqam mahmud, Ayn Azimah
- [S325] (doctrine) The noble stations connect with the Qa'im as the gathered forms near the Bab (bab al-abwab) connect with the soul of the born Imam, his soul becoming a spirit (ruh) for them and forming one luminous structure and holy intellect.  ::terms: bab al-abwab, nafs al-imam al-mawlud, haykal nurani, aql qudsani
- [S326] (scripture) All people of the dawr al-sitr, first and last, are gathered to the Qa'im, who takes account of each, makes them stand upon their deeds, and personifies every Imam with the people of his cycle, per 'the prophets and witnesses will be brought forward and judged with justice.'  ::terms: qul inna al-awwalina wa al-akhirina la-majmu'un, wa ji'a bi'l-nabiyyina wa al-shuhada', hisab
- [S327] (scripture) The believer will be shown a record (kitab) accounting every small and great deed ('what is this book that leaves neither small nor great but has accounted for it'), and the Qa'im is the Lord who rewards and punishes, the total reward and punishment being established before him.  ::terms: kitab, ma li hadha al-kitab la yughadiru saghiratan, thawab kulli, iqab kulli
- [S328] (doctrine) Accepted supplications (du'a) in this world are recompensed in the world; supplications not accepted are stored and rewarded at the reckoning, leading the believer to wish that none of his prayers had been hastened ('layta innaka lam tu'ajjil li shay'an').  ::terms: du'a, thawab, layta innaka lam tu'ajjil li shay'an
- [S329] (doctrine) Every rank (Imam, Bab, Hujjah, Da'i, Ma'dhun) is a partial (juzwi) Qa'im al-Qiyamah for those who return to it, while the rank above is its universal (kulli) Qa'im, ascending until all reach the Qa'im of the entire resurrection, master of the greatest resurrection (qiyamat al-uzma) and the greatest seizure (batshat al-kubra).  ::terms: qa'im al-qiyamah juzwi, kulli, qiyamat al-uzma, yawma nabtishu al-batshat al-kubra
- [S330] (doctrine) The perfected form (the luminous intellect / haykal nafsani) is prepared in 7,000 years in the dawr al-kashf (where there is purity and openly manifested wisdom-sciences), whereas the Qa'im al-Qiyamah's form takes 10,000 years in the dawr al-sitr due to density, doubts, turbidity, and abundance of trials.  ::terms: dawr al-kashf, dawr al-sitr, aql nurani, haykal nafsani, kathafah
- [S331] (doctrine) In the dawr al-kashf a Qa'im (qiyamah) occurs every 7,000 years (seven Qa'ims), but in the dawr al-sitr one Qa'im comes after 10,000 years (the 7,000 of sitr plus the 3,000 of fatrat), because the 3,000 fatrat years belong to the domain of dawr al-sitr thought.  ::terms: dawr al-kashf, dawr al-sitr, dawr al-fatrat, qiyamah
- [S332] (doctrine) Wiping the hand over the face after Bismillah signifies connecting the five lower (adwan) ranks with the five higher (ashraf) ranks; the face's five (intellect, soul, jadd, fath, khayal) are non-repeated (achieved second perfection), while the hands' fives are repeated (manifested in the world: Imam, Bab, Hujjah, Da'i, Ma'dhun).  ::terms: Bismillah, adwan, ashraf, aql, nafs, jadd, fath, khayal
- [S333] (doctrine) All points appearing until the universal resurrection are present in the primordial initial body (jussa ibda'iyyah) of the first mawr; their manifestation occurs in appointed time, and the one holding the mawr governs its qawr until he prepares a successor like himself, whereupon he is elevated.  ::terms: jussa ibda'iyyah, mawr, qawr, janashin
- [S334] (doctrine) When the Imam manifests in the world of intention his Bab manifests with him in a dual capacity (qiran), and ranks subordinate to the Qa'im (jadd, fath, khayal, and his physical kin such as Abd al-Muttalib, Abu Talib, mothers) correspond as a reflection of the original assigned points.  ::terms: qiran, Bab, Abd al-Muttalib, Abu Talib, alam al-niyyat
- [S335] (scripture) Man's existence is from two ways: as a plant grown from the earth and through inter-marriage/lineage (munakahat), per 'Allah grew you from the earth as a plant' and 'We created man from an extract of clay, then made him a drop in a firm resting place.'  ::terms: wallahu anbatakum min al-ard nabatan, min sulalatin min tin, nutfatan fi qararin makin, munakahat
- [S336] (doctrine) The formal apparitions (ashbah suwariyyah), having fallen into the three dimensions through their slip and error, became distant, dense, and turbid; divine providence dispersed (tabdid) them into the spheres and elements to purify them through trials, as gold is purified by fire.  ::terms: ashbah suwariyyah, zallah, tabdid, dhahab al-ibriz, imtihan
- [S337] (doctrine) After punishment and trial, divine providence gathers what was prepared and manifests it as the gnostic human form (qamat al-arafiyyah) from the earth as a plant, then by a second, faster, closer way encompasses the three kingdoms (mineral, plant, animal) as food.  ::terms: qamat al-arafiyyah, mawalid al-thalathah, ma'dan, nabat, hayawan
- [S338] (doctrine) The primordial body eats these foods, taking the subtle and discarding the dense, making them flesh and blood combining the four natures (the four humors); the body so formed is a physical veil over the primordial body.  ::terms: jussa ibda'iyyah, taba'i al-arba', lahm, dam
- [S339] (doctrine) At intimacy between man and woman the subtlest extract descends from both bodies, ascends to the brain to be refined and whitened (from dense blood drawn from every organ), then descends from both into the womb to become a single entity gathering the four natures.  ::terms: mulamasah, dimagh, rahm al-imra'ah, taba'i
- [S340] (definition) Within that single entity is a complete vegetative life hidden like fire in flint-wood, named tabi'ah, the primordial leaven (khamirah ibda'iyyah), the vegetative soul (nafs namiyyah), and the (growing) form (surah).  ::terms: tabi'ah, khamirah ibda'iyyah, nafs namiyyah, surah
- [S341] (doctrine) When the entity is balanced in temperament and given permission to manifest, it settles in the pure balanced womb, faced by the ascendant, descendant, tenth, and fourth astrological houses by the decree of the cosmos, fermenting like rennet and milk becoming cheese.  ::terms: rahm, tali', gharib, ashir, rabi', takhmir
- [S342] (doctrine) The first organ formed from the drop (nutfah) is the heart (the point of the compass, nuqtat al-birkar), whose nature is the sun's action (heat and dryness); it attracts the subtle entity and becomes its house and center, after which the remaining organs form.  ::terms: nutfah, nuqtat al-birkar, qalb, shams, hararah, yubus
- [S343] (doctrine) The fathers (planets/kawakib) act upon the forming organs and the mothers (wombs/spheres) nurse them; each organ receives its share of nourishment through its mother's corresponding organ, missing nothing.  ::terms: aba', kawakib, ummahat, rahm, azw
- [S344] (argument) The sun, heart of the celestial sphere, provides aid to what is above and below it step by step; likewise the sun of the world of religion, the Natiq, gives the divine sciences and angelic confirmations to the hudud above and below him.  ::terms: shams, qalb al-falak, shams alam al-din, Natiq, ta'yidat malakutiyyah
