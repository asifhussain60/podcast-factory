# SECTION 04: The Spiritual Hierarchy: the Mumaththal, the Asas, and the Cherubim

Lecture range: [8, 9]


## LEDGER SPINE TEACHINGS YOU MUST PRESERVE (each at full depth)

- [S099] (doctrine) The First Intellect (al-Natiq), the Asas (foundation), and the seven uqul transmit the kingly luminous light (nur-e shahshani / nur-e shashani ilahi) downward; whatever happened, happened, and the hudud are made just ('adilan) between them and the other intellects.  ::terms: nur-e shahshani, Natiq, Asas, hudud
- [S100] (doctrine) The believer (mu'min) in the period of occultation (ghaybah), upon attaining the state of nur-e shashani, becomes Ahl al-Bayt; the ghaybah is itself a great blessing (ni'mah), and the knowledge of the Awwal and Thani is obtained through intellect while knowledge of the Imam comes through the knowledge of his occultation, call, and summons.  ::terms: ghaybah, nur-e shashani, Ahl al-Bayt, ni'mah
- [S101] (scripture) The Qur'anic 'Allah witnesses that there is no deity except Him, and the angels and those of knowledge' (shahida Allahu... wal-mala'ikatu wa ulu al-'ilm) means three give witness: Allah, the angels (al-mala'ikah = al-Sabiq, the first-sent precedent), and the people of knowledge (ulu al-'ilm = al-Mumaththal al-Awwal / ahl al-da'wat al-sahihah).  ::terms: shahida Allah, mala'ikah, ulu al-ilm, Mumaththal al-Awwal, Sabiq
- [S102] (doctrine) The Mumaththal al-Awwal (First Representative) establishes the call and is above the Sabiq; the Sabiq (precedent) is the first to respond to the call (mujib li-da'watihi); no indication toward the Originator is possible except through the Nabi (Prophet) and Wasi (Successor).  ::terms: Mumaththal al-Awwal, Sabiq, Nabi, Wasi
- [S103] (definition) True existence (wujud haqiqi) is the second perfection (kamal al-thani); the first existence is only life (zindagi). This world is called 'alam al-amr, 'alam al-ruh, 'alam al-arwah, 'alam al-ibda', 'alam al-'aql al-kull, and 'alam al-quds — all due to its relation to the command of God (amrillah).  ::terms: wujud haqiqi, kamal al-thani, alam al-amr, alam al-arwah
- [S104] (definition) The world of subtlety ('alam-e latafat) and world of attributes ('alam-e sifat) have no physical density; the eight worlds are 'alam-e ruhani, 'alam-e abdan, 'alam-e 'aql, 'alam-e nafs, 'alam-e latafat, 'alam-e sifat (and the world of bodies and existence) — the souls being a later, fallen degradation of the luminous forms and the bodies an even more fallen form.  ::terms: alam-e latafat, alam-e sifat, eight worlds
- [S105] (doctrine) From one divine command (hukm) seven forms emerged, and from those seven came innumerable forms (suwaran kathiran); after the first seven, the gate of permission closed, and within each of the seven many forms appeared through response (ijabat) — the last form of the first circle being superior to the second circle.  ::terms: hukm, suwar kathira, ijabat, da'ira
- [S106] (doctrine) In every da'wah cycle the chief is like an Imam (he is to them as imam, they to him as ma'mum), like a boundary (had) to which they are the bounded (mahdudin), and like a chief (ra'is) to whom they are subjects (mar'usin); each chief is one form, and his circle, once drawn (beginning meeting end), is connected to him as center.  ::terms: Imam, had, ra'is, da'ira, markaz
- [S107] (definition) The seven intellects, by the sharpness (hiddah) and speed of their vision, linked heaven with the seven 'qurubiyyah' (the Kurubiyyin / Angels Brought Near, called the chiefs/ru'asa'); those under their charge are named the spiritual angels (al-mala'ikah al-ruhaniyyin).  ::terms: hiddah, Kurubiyyin, mala'ikah ruhaniyyin, qurubiyyah
- [S108] (definition) The hierarchy of angels: highest are the Mala'ikah Kurubiyyin (seated from the beginning), then the Mala'ikah Ruhaniyyin in their circle, then the Mala'ikat al-Muqarrabun (the Angels Brought Near) — those ascending from below the tenth had toward them, called Muqarrab because they are 'coming near'; the tenth had ('ashir) are the people of the barzakh (ahl al-barzakh).  ::terms: Kurubiyyin, Muqarrabun, ahl al-barzakh, ashir
- [S109] (definition) Above the barzakh is the Jadd, which remains above every Imam as his hujjat (above all 24 Imams); the Jadd is the Lisan al-Hikmah (Tongue of Wisdom) and the Lisan al-Imamah, while the Lisan al-Shari'ah is the tongue of the law by which the soul is reformed.  ::terms: Jadd, Lisan al-Hikmah, Lisan al-Shari'ah, hujjat
- [S110] (doctrine) The Third Intellect (third shibh / Second Emanation / al-tali), when he erred concerning the First Emanation and thought himself equal (masawat), had his supply (madda) cut off, his essence became dark, and he was distanced and became the Tenth ('ashir) in the horizon of the Ninth Intellect, after having been the Third.  ::terms: shibh al-thalith, ashir, masawat, madda
- [S111] (doctrine) The Tenth ('Ashir), seeing the decline of his rank while the nine spheres attained the second perfection, awoke from heedlessness (ghaflah), knew he had fallen into error, contemplated the cause, turned to the nearest (Ninth) Intellect supplicating, confessed his error, repented (taba), and humbled himself (anaba).  ::terms: Ashir, ghaflah, tawba, inaba
- [S112] (doctrine) The 'Ashir's denial of his preceding limit (had al-sabiq) came from arrogance (istikbar) and ignorance; he sought intercession (tawassul) through all those preceding him to his Lord (the First Intellect) and sought forgiveness, whereupon all his sins were forgiven.  ::terms: istikbar, tawassul, istighfar, aql al-awwal
- [S113] (doctrine) The abstract intellects showed kindness (hannat) to the 'Ashir and cast their rays upon him in mercy; the distant promises (mawa'id qusiyya) and higher supports (ta'yidat 'ulwiyya) connected with him, his essence shone totally, he was stripped of darkness into a luminous intellect, and he reached the second perfection (kamal al-thani) — attaining infallibility ('ismah) and stations of mercy.  ::terms: ta'yidat ulwiyya, kamal al-thani, isma, Ashir
- [S114] (scripture) The Qur'an 'O reassured soul, return to your Lord, well-pleased and well-pleasing; so enter among My servants and enter My Paradise' (ya ayyatuha al-nafs al-mutma'inna...) is spoken by the First Intellect (from the circle of oneness) and refers to the 'Ashir; the 'Paradise' (jannah) is the First Emanation's circle.  ::terms: nafs al-mutma'inna, irji'i, jannah, Ashir
- [S115] (scripture) The 'Ashir is the spiritual Adam (Adam al-ruhani); the Qur'anic story of Adam refers to him, and 'Then Adam received words (kalimat) from his Lord and He relented toward him' means the kalimat are the seven abstract intellects whose intercession Adam received.  ::terms: Adam al-ruhani, kalimat, Ashir, tawba
- [S116] (doctrine) The First Intellect alone is the true Kalimah (Kalimatullah), who established the world by uttering the kalimat al-ikhlas before every speaker; the seven intellects are called 'kalimat' only metaphorically by attribution to him, just as the worlds of intellect, origination, and holiness are named by attribution to him.  ::terms: Kalimatullah, kalimat al-ikhlas, kalimat
- [S117] (definition) The First Intellect has two worships: practical worship ('ibadah 'amaliyya) = uttering the kalimah/testimony (his testimony for his Originator in His origination), and gnostic worship ('ibadah 'ilmiyya) = what came into existence for him from his Originator's divinity and oneness (his thought); gnostic worship is more noble (ashraf), practical worship is lower (adwan).  ::terms: ibadah amaliyya, ibadah ilmiyya, ashraf, adwan
- [S118] (doctrine) The First Intellect surpasses the abstract intellects in virtue, nobility, splendor, and perfection by his precedence, action, worship, knowledge, and deed; however much they advance they cannot surpass the one above them, and Allah's treasures have no deficiency, so his joy and bounty keep increasing forever.  ::terms: aql al-awwal, sabaqiyat, ibadah, ilm
- [S119] (doctrine) Those distanced by the 'Ashir's distance (a large group in his category) divided into three sects (firaq): the first negated divinity from itself and its kind and affirmed it for the Originator but erred regarding the First Emanation (like 'Ashir) — this is the spiritual Adam's spouse (zawj, Hawwa', his receptive faculty), per the Qur'an 'O Adam, dwell, you and your wife, in Paradise.'  ::terms: firaq, zawj, Hawwa, quwwah munfa'ila
- [S120] (doctrine) The second sect affirmed the Originator but 'left' (denied the precedence of) the First Intellect and First Emanation, thinking themselves equal to them (masawat/mumathalah) — they remain in the first perfection and never enter the second.  ::terms: firqat al-thaniyah, masawat, kamal al-awwal
- [S121] (doctrine) The third sect established a da'wah in the abode of origination, denied, were arrogant, did not affirm the oneness of their Originator, and adhered to none of the intellects — claiming they created themselves from nothing; they are the most severe of the sects in disbelief (kufr) and the greatest in opposition and denial, and from this sect Iblis was born.  ::terms: firqat al-thalithah, kufr, Iblis, da'wah
