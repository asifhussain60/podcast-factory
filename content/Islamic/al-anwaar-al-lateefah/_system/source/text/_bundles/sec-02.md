# SECTION 02: Tawhid, Tanzih, and the Knowledge of the Hudud

Lecture range: [4, 5]


## LEDGER SPINE TEACHINGS YOU MUST PRESERVE (each at full depth)

- [S023] (doctrine) True Tawhid is the gnosis of the hudud (ma'rifat al-hudud) — knowing that every had (rank) is unique in its own place and recognizing its station — NOT considering Allah as one, which would be shirk since Allah admits no number or definition.  ::terms: tawhid, ma'rifat al-hudud, had, shirk
- [S024] (definition) Tawhid has three branches: Tawhid (gnosis of the hudud), tanzih (negation of divinity / ilahiyyat meaning the power of originating beings, ibda' al-zawat, from the hudud), and tajrid (divestment of all names and attributes from Allah).  ::terms: tawhid, tanzih, tajrid, ibda' al-zawat
- [S025] (doctrine) The knowledge of Tawhid is the highest of all knowledge (ajall al-'ilm); since Allah created innumerable beings (zawat), there is innumerable gnosis, and a person's station (martaba) and haykal-rank are established according to how much gnosis they attain in life through 'ilm and 'amal.  ::terms: ajall al-ilm, zawat, martaba, haykal
- [S026] (doctrine) Salvation (najat) is a good end (khatima bil-khair); gnosis is attained both by one's own effort (juhd) and by the grace (fazl) of those above.  ::terms: najat, khatima bil-khair, juhd, fazl
- [S027] (doctrine) The limit (nuqta-e irtifa') of the Prophet is Hijabiyyat, of Janab-e Fatimah is Hujjiyyat, and of Abd al-Muttalib is Jaddiyyat (which itself has ten stations).  ::terms: Hijabiyyat, Hujjiyyat, Jaddiyyat
- [S028] (definition) Gnosis of the hudud is of two kinds: outer gnosis (ma'rifah zahirah) by which the person and station of a had are known and in which friend and enemy are equal; and inner gnosis (ma'rifah batinah) of the beginning, end, and return of a had's existence, specific to the people of truth (ahl al-haqq).  ::terms: ma'rifah zahirah, ma'rifah batinah, ahl al-haqq
- [S029] (claim) Inner gnosis — knowing the beginning, level, and end (ma'ad) of a had's essence (khamira) — can be attained only by the Imam al-Zaman and by the disciplined (murtazin) and verifiers (muhiqqin); one whose gnosis is deficient has no Tawhid at all.  ::terms: ma'ad, khamira, Imam al-Zaman, murtazin, muhiqqin
- [S030] (doctrine) The closest had to us is the Mukathir; what is established for the lower of the hudud is established for the higher (ma thabata fi al-dani thabata fil-'ali).  ::terms: Mukathir, hudud, dani, ali
- [S031] (definition) The ranks below the hudud are Mustajib (one who takes the noble pledge with his tongue, with ten levels graded by the intensity/hiddat of the pledge) and Mu'min (defined as profession with the tongue, affirmation with the heart, and action with the limbs — al-qawl bil-lisan wa al-tasdiq bil-jinan wa al-'amal bil-arkan, with ten levels).  ::terms: Mustajib, Mu'min, hiddat, arkan
- [S032] (definition) The Mukathir is the first rank within the hudud; 'mukathir' derives from kasr (breaking with proofs/hujjat), and he must first break his own self (kasr-e nafs) by binding his every word, action, and intention to the command of Allah and the Prophet — requiring acquisition of knowledge ('ulum).  ::terms: Mukathir, kasr-e nafs, hujjat, ulum
- [S033] (doctrine) Although the Mukathir and ordinary humans share the same outer composition (flesh, blood, bones, nerves, veins), they are not the same: the Mukathir's temperament has become refined (talattafa mizajuhu) and his essence pure (safa jawharuhu), ascending through many stations to the highest ranks of humanity, which are the first ranks of the spiritual world (malakutiyya).  ::terms: Mukathir, talattafa mizajuhu, safa jawharuhu, malakutiyya
- [S034] (doctrine) The Mukathir is the place of descent for Allah's revelation (mahbat li-wahyi Allah) and a house from His houses (baytun min buyutihi), because he keeps the command of Allah and the Prophet before him in thought, word, and deed.  ::terms: Mukathir, mahbat al-wahy, buyut Allah
- [S035] (scripture) The Qur'anic 'houses' in which Allah permitted His name to be exalted (fi buyutin adhin Allahu an turfa') are the hudud, and the 'men whom commerce does not divert from the remembrance of Allah, prayer, and zakat' refers to establishing the da'wah (iqamat al-da'wah).  ::terms: buyut, hudud, iqamat al-da'wah, Surah al-Jumu'ah
- [S036] (definition) The pillars of religion have esoteric (ta'weel) meanings: wilayat = repenting in the world of motes; taharat = purifying from the filth of Iblis through the knowledge of the Tenth; salat = acting on the commands of the Tenth/Imam in whose sphere one is; zakat = giving a portion of the knowledge given to you.  ::terms: wilayat, taharat, salat, zakat, ta'weel
- [S037] (definition) Further esoteric pillars: sawm (fasting) = concealing knowledge from the undeserving; khums = giving a fifth of knowledge received unexpectedly (as Imam Husayn gave the khums of his salary); hajj = preparing oneself to go toward the bab al-batin (Imam al-Zaman is the House of Allah); jihad = sacrificing or slaying the commanding self (nafs).  ::terms: sawm, khums, hajj, bab al-batin, jihad, nafs
- [S038] (doctrine) One who does not establish prayer (iqamat al-salah) declines from the first principle, is pushed back below the thousand-fold human form (surat al-alfiyya), becomes more astray than cattle, and ends in the barzakh al-khawf — while the Mukathir who establishes inward prayer ascends.  ::terms: iqamat al-salah, surat al-alfiyya, barzakh al-khawf
- [S039] (argument) Lawful (halal) animals are like the ascending pure human/Mukathir — they become food, take intimacy with man (the caliph of Allah), and ascend on the straight path (sirat al-sawiyy, the human person) toward the first principle; predatory (forbidden) animals are averse and proceed in decline away from the first principle.  ::terms: baha'im, siba, sirat al-mustaqim, mabda al-awwal
- [S040] (doctrine) When a seeker (mustafid) receives the spiritual speech (al-kalam al-malakuti) and divine knowledge from the Mukathir, his intellect is bewildered and the Mukathir becomes for him a 'god' (ilah) and master of his neck — 'god' meaning the center of the circle around which he revolves in bewilderment, NOT the creator of beings.  ::terms: mustafid, kalam al-malakuti, ilah, markaz
- [S041] (argument) The mu'min verifies that the Mukathir himself needs and is bewildered before the had above him, ascending through the hudud to the Imam, then to the abstract intellects (al-'uqul al-mujarradat), until reaching the First Intellect, which is bewildered before its Originator — then he negates divinity from all and affirms it for Allah alone (la ilaha illa Hu).  ::terms: mu'min, uqul mujarradat, aql al-awwal, la ilaha illa Hu
- [S042] (doctrine) In the spiritual world the da'wah formed nested circles: each circle has a center (the sabiq/forerunner); those who joined repented with him (tawabu bi-tawbatihi) and ascended; the final individual left was commanded to give the da'wah, becoming the center of a new circle — establishing the circles of Imam, Bab, Hujjat, etc., all on the first pattern.  ::terms: da'wah, sabiq, tawabu bi-tawbatihi, da'ira
- [S043] (argument) The word 'ilah' is composed of alif-lam-ha encoding three conditions present in every circle: alif = precedence without an inspirer (aql al-awwal); lam = advancing by seeing/repenting with another's repentance (munba'ith min al-awwal); ha = the da'wah given by the seven uqul (the 'ashir did not).  ::terms: ilah, alif lam ha, munba'ith, ashir
- [S044] (doctrine) The created 'gods' (aliha) — created, invented, originated — each carry the name of a nourisher (murabbi) above them and a need calling them upward; the only true God (la ilaha siwahu) is the one beyond the limited physical hudud and the unlimited spiritual hudud, from whom even names and attributes are stripped.  ::terms: aliha, murabbi, hudud al-jismaniyya, hudud al-ruhaniyya
- [S045] (scripture) The Qur'anic verse 'O tranquil soul, return to your Lord, well-pleased and well-pleasing' (irji'i ila rabbiki radiyatan mardiyya) means the soul ascends to the top then returns to its mufid (Lord), its tranquility (itmi'nan) lying in what it has known and its pleasure in its Lord's command.  ::terms: irji'i, nafs mutma'inna, mufid, radiya mardiyya
- [S046] (scripture) The summary of the Qur'an's teaching about the Originator is Surah al-Fatiha, in which the three classes of people are mentioned: the favored/repentant (an'amta 'alayhim = nadimin), those who incurred wrath (maghdubi = the insistent/musirrin), and the astray (dallin = the bewildered/mutahayyirin).  ::terms: Surah al-Fatiha, nadimin, musirrin, mutahayyirin
- [S047] (scripture) The reality of Allah cannot be understood, as proven by the Qur'an 'Glory be to Him above what they describe' (Subhan Allah 'amma yasifun) — whatever attributes are ascribed to Him, He is pure (tanzih) from them.  ::terms: subhan Allah, amma yasifun, tanzih
- [S048] (doctrine) Allah Ta'ala created the worlds of origination — alluded to by seven names: 'alam al-amr (command), 'alam al-'aql (intellect), 'alam al-quds (sanctity), 'alam al-safa (purity), 'alam al-latafah (subtlety), 'alam al-ruhani (spiritual) — in a single instant (daf'atan wahidatan), not in time, with no prior matter (hayula), no accompanying thing, and no place.  ::terms: alam al-ibda, daf'atan wahidatan, hayula, alam al-amr
- [S049] (definition) Ibda' (origination) is creating from nothing; khalq (creation) is forming a new composite from pre-existing things by changing their arrangement.  ::terms: ibda, khalq
- [S050] (authority) Allah originated the existents from non-existence ('adam) purely as a favor (tafaddul), generosity (jud), and bestowal (tatawwul) — not from need nor for sport — since it is the nature (shimah/sajiyyah) of the Generous and Mighty to bestow; Sayyidna Mohyuddin establishes this by negating all other purposes.  ::terms: adam, tafaddul, jud, tatawwul, Mohyuddin
- [S051] (doctrine) The originated beings were brought into existence as luminous imaginal forms (ashbah suriyyah nuraniyya) possessing thousand-fold statures (qamatin alfiyya) like the human corporeal stature — in reality the human stature is patterned on them — distinct from their later material (hayulaniyya) souls and dark bodies.  ::terms: ashbah suriyya, qamat alfiyya, nuraniyya, hayulaniyya
- [S052] (doctrine) The luminous forms were created equal in the first perfection (kamal al-awwal) — which is existence (wujud), life (hayat), strength (quwwah), and ability/knowledge (qudrah/'ilm) — with no disagreement, preference, or distinction among them, as an act of justice (adl) from Allah.  ::terms: kamal al-awwal, wujud, hayat, quwwah, adl
- [S053] (scripture) The equal creation of all beings is anchored on the Qur'an 'And if it had been from other than Allah, they would have found therein much contradiction' (wa law kana min 'indi ghayrillahi la-wajadu fihi ikhtilafan kathira), and matches Sayyidna Hamiduddin's statement in Rahat al-'Aql.  ::terms: ikhtilafan kathira, Hamiduddin, Rahat al-Aql
- [S054] (argument) In the abode of origination and emanation (dar al-ibda' wa al-inbi'ath) there is no obstacle, because the forms are pure (mahdah) and free of obstructing matter (maddah); thus the existence of all existents was in a single instant, like light filling air from the sun or a fire illuminating a dark house.  ::terms: dar al-ibda, dar al-inbi'ath, maddah, daf'atan wahidatan
- [S055] (doctrine) The intellects existing in actuality (bi-l-fi'l) and in potentiality (bi-l-quwwah) existed together at one time, so no period of time separated the first perfection (kamal al-awwal) from the second perfection (kamal al-thani).  ::terms: bi-l-fi'l, bi-l-quwwah, kamal al-awwal, kamal al-thani
- [S056] (doctrine) In the state of first perfection all beings are equal but NOT infallible (ghayr ma'sum) from error; the 'error' (khata) is failing to attain the second perfection — delay in invitation, refusing the call, misleading others; the perfect/infallible state is only the second perfection.  ::terms: ghayr ma'sum, khata, kamal al-thani
- [S057] (doctrine) One of the luminous forms looked with his essence at his essence and at his kind (nazara bi-dhatihi ila dhatihi), knew there was an Originator unlike them, negated divinity from himself and his kind, and affirmed it for the Originator — thus performing tawhid, tajrid, and tanzih all together as Sahib al-Tawhid.  ::terms: nazara bi-dhatihi, tawhid, tajrid, tanzih, Sahib al-Tawhid
- [S058] (doctrine) He acknowledged the Originator's Lordship, glorified Him, humbled himself, and crucially sought the means (tawassul) of the Originator's own greatness toward Him — taking His greatness as the means to reach Him, since His greatness cannot be reached directly.  ::terms: tawassul, azmah, rububiyah
- [S059] (doctrine) As a result, divine supports (mawad ilahiyya) and confirmations of oneness (ta'yidat wahdaniyya) reached him, and the flowing knowledge ('ilm al-jari) and spreading light (nur al-sari) connected with him — which is the Word of Allah (kalimatullah), namely 'La ilaha illa Allah.'  ::terms: mawad ilahiyya, ilm al-jari, nur al-sari, kalimatullah
- [S060] (doctrine) The kalimah 'La ilaha illa Allah' (also named al-amr, the command) is above the intellect and below the Originator (fawqa al-'aql wa dun al-mubdi'); it is the continuous link between Originator (mubdi') and originated (mubda'), and through it flow knowledge and light, lasting as long as creation remains.  ::terms: kalimah, al-amr, mubdi, mubda
- [S061] (doctrine) The first perfection's gifts (existence, knowledge, power, freedom) cannot be taken away by Allah once given; the being is ghayr ma'sum in that state but retains those powers, and must use them correctly to journey toward and activate the second perfection — and humanity is currently in a fallen state of the first perfection, journeying toward the second.  ::terms: kamal al-awwal, kamal al-thani, ghayr ma'sum, ikhtiyar
- [S062] (doctrine) Through the kalimah the First Intellect encompassed all things in knowledge ('ilm) and knew what was and what will be (ma kana wa ma sayakun); it had to negate itself (affirm it was not its own creator) before obtaining the kalimah and its light.  ::terms: kalimah, ilm, aql al-awwal
- [S063] (argument) The kalimah 'La ilaha illa Allah' is composed in numbered layers: negation+affirmation (2); three letters alif-lam-ha (3); four words la/ilaha/illa/Allah (4); the world ended at seven luminous forms because of its seven muqatta'at (where pen/tongue stops); twelve are the ascending spirits — and seven plus twelve equal nineteen, as in Bismillah al-Rahman al-Rahim.  ::terms: kalimah, muqatta'at, nineteen, Bismillah
- [S064] (authority) Bismillah al-Rahman al-Rahim encodes the week of the seven Imams (Bismillah, seven letters) each with twelve naqibs (al-Rahman al-Rahim), per Sayyidna Muhammad Taher; the additive construction (tarkib-i-idafi) is more excellent than the descriptive (tarkib-i-tawsifi).  ::terms: Bismillah, tarkib-i-idafi, tarkib-i-tawsifi, Muhammad Taher, naqib
- [S065] (scripture) The First Intellect increased in humility, submission, bewilderment, and reverence (khushu') after obtaining the kalimah; this is why the Qur'an says 'Indeed, only those of knowledge among His servants fear Allah' (innama yakhsha Allaha min 'ibadihi al-'ulama'), fear being acknowledgment of His greatness.  ::terms: khushu, yakhsha Allah, ulama
- [S066] (doctrine) Allah took the First Intellect as a veil (hijab) and gate (bab) for Himself, through which alone one may seek means (tawassul), have intimate discourse (munajah), or declare Him One (yuwahhad); thus the First Intellect became His Greatest Name (ism al-a'zam) and most noble station.  ::terms: hijab, bab, ism al-a'zam, munajah, tawassul
- [S067] (doctrine) The Second Emanation (munba'ith/mumbai al-thani) failed to attain the second perfection because, while acknowledging the First Intellect's superiority, he was deceived by the notion of equality (wahm-e masawat) — claiming his vision equaled the First Intellect's; in the first perfection all are equal, but in the second perfection none can be equal.  ::terms: munba'ith, wahm masawat, kamal al-thani
- [S068] (authority) The circle of the First Originator (mubdi') is the circle of oneness (da'irat al-wahdat) which no other being can enter; the First Intellect possesses the veil (hijab), and Sayyidna Sadiq explained that 'oneness' here is a special honorific and that quds is also called a circle encompassed by the First Intellect.  ::terms: da'irat al-wahdat, hijab, Sadiq, quds
