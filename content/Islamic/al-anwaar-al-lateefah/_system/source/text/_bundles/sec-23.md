# SECTION 23: The Knots, the Barzakh, and the Great Kawr

Lecture range: [49, 50]


## LEDGER SPINE TEACHINGS YOU MUST PRESERVE (each at full depth)

- [S825] (doctrine) In the common speech of the exoteric people, at the time of death there must come a mubashshir and bashir (bearers of glad tidings) for the people of good, and Munkar and Nakir for the people of evil, and these are two angels present with the dying person.  ::terms: mubashshir, bashir, Munkar, Nakir, ahl al-zahir, malak
- [S826] (claim) This saying about the two angels at death is a true saying heard from the people of truth (ahl al-haqq), though its inner reality became hidden from the world.  ::terms: ahl al-haqq
- [S827] (doctrine) Every Natiq of a cycle (dawr) and his Wasi are themselves the two angels referred to in their era: mubashshir and bashir for their friends (awliya') and Munkar and Nakir for their opponents (addad).  ::terms: Natiq, dawr, Wasi, awliya, addad
- [S828] (doctrine) The two angels are a single personality that appears as Munkar and Nakir to opponents and as mubashshir and bashir to the friends, depending on whether the person's end is with good and love or not.  ::terms: mubashshir, bashir, Munkar, Nakir, addad, awliya
- [S829] (doctrine) Likewise every Imam of the Time and his Hujjat is mubashshir and bashir for his friends and Munkar and Nakir for their opponents, so this reality persists in every age.  ::terms: imam al-zaman, hujjat, awliya, addad
- [S830] (scripture) Amir al-Mu'minin Ali said in verse to Harith Hamdan: whoever dies, believer or hypocrite, will see me soon; he recognizes me with his eye and I recognize him by his name and kunya and what he did.  ::terms: Amir al-Muminin, Harith Hamdan, mumin, munafiq, kunya
- [S831] (doctrine) The reality is that the form/imagination (khayal) of every station (Natiq, Wasi, Imam) at the time of each one's death moves the Pillar of Light ('umud al-nur), which flows from the world of holiness ('alam al-quds) and is connected to the form of every wali, the Master of the Time.  ::terms: khayal, umud al-nur, alam al-quds, wali, sahib al-zaman
- [S832] (doctrine) Between the Originator (mubdi') and the originated (mubda') is a Word (kalimah), which is the kalimah 'La ilaha illa Allah,' and from it the Pillar of Light is connected.  ::terms: mubdi, mubda, kalimah, La ilaha illa Allah, umud al-nur
- [S833] (doctrine) All luminous forms up to the First Intellect ('aql al-awwal) are connected to the kalimah 'La ilaha'; above the First Intellect is the kalimah, which connects to the Originator, and no one can pass beyond the Originator.  ::terms: aql al-awwal, kalimah, mubdi
- [S834] (authority) The detailed explanation of the Pillar of Light ('umud al-nur) has come in Akhbar al-Latifa.  ::terms: umud al-nur, Akhbar al-Latifa
- [S835] (doctrine) The Pillar of Light has different centers, the largest being the First Intellect, then the 'ashir, then the Imam of the time, like graded centers of electricity, and all good forms are connected to it like a beehive.  ::terms: umud al-nur, aql al-awwal, ashir, imam al-zaman
- [S836] (doctrine) At the last minute of his divine life and righteous deed, the one transitioning imagines a luminous person, rejoices and is delighted at seeing it, and knows by its pleasure that he is headed toward good as he departs the moment.  ::terms: nurani, amal salih
- [S837] (doctrine) That luminous form is the form the believer acquired from the walayah (friendship/allegiance) of the people of truth, their divine knowledges, and their righteous deeds with the friends of God.  ::terms: walayah, ahl al-haqq, ulum ilahiyyah, amal salih, awliya Allah
- [S838] (scripture) Mawlana al-Sadiq alluded to this, transmitted by Sayyidna al-Nu'man in Da'a'im al-Islam: when God raises the believer from his grave, a beautiful image (mithal hasan) emerges with him and says through the hardships 'Do not fear, there is no harm upon you.'  ::terms: Mawlana al-Sadiq, al-Numan, Daaim al-Islam, mithal hasan
- [S839] (doctrine) That luminous image is the believer's real master/guardian, acting as a supervisory angel over his body, keeping watch over every part of it.  ::terms: mithal, malak
- [S840] (doctrine) The image continually reassures and gives glad tidings to the believer until it brings him to God, who calls him to an easy reckoning (hisab yasir).  ::terms: mithal, hisab yasir
- [S841] (scripture) When the believer asks the image who it is, it answers: 'I am a creation from the creation of my Lord, created from the joy (surur) you used to bring upon the believers, so today I make you happy.'  ::terms: mithal, surur, muminin
- [S842] (doctrine) By God's decree, no particle can go astray; like a magnet each particle draws its connected particles, so the believer's parts cannot be scattered and progress to their station.  ::terms: maghnatis
- [S843] (definition) This is the reality of mubashshir and bashir for the friend (wali).  ::terms: mubashshir, bashir, wali
- [S844] (doctrine) For the opponent, the evil dark magnet — the two knots (al-'uqdatan) — by the movement of the station moves the satanic dissolver (al-muhill al-iblisi) and the evil deeds, knowledge, enmity, and hatred he harbored against the people of truth.  ::terms: uqdatan, muhill al-iblisi, ahl al-haqq
- [S845] (doctrine) One center (the Pillar of Light) was made for the good; two (the two knots) were made for the evil — only one is mentioned for the Pillar of Light but the knots are two.  ::terms: umud al-nur, uqdatan
- [S846] (doctrine) The two knots were formed at the head of the snake when God made the celestial spheres (aflak); high beings of the world of light who took precedence into satanic nature (iblisiyyat) yet repented were placed there, kept high beneath the sphere of the moon rather than with the hellfire (saqar), by a specific intention of the 'ashir within divine providence.  ::terms: uqdatan, aflak, iblisiyyat, saqar, ashir, inayat ilahiyyah
- [S847] (doctrine) Through the movement of the Imam's station, the decree (tadbir) of the Imam is in effect on the two knots, so the Imam also grants the requital of his opponents' evil deeds.  ::terms: uqdatan, tadbir, addad
- [S848] (doctrine) The opponent's dark disfigured form is what he acquired bit by bit from his evil; at his last minute he imagines a dark, disfigured, most-ugly person which he rejects, fears, and is horrified by, then it is cut off and he knows he is of the people of evil — this is the reality of Munkar and Nakir.  ::terms: Munkar, Nakir, muzlim, ahl al-sharr
- [S849] (scripture) The Messenger of God alluded to this in his sermon to Qays ibn 'Asim: you have a companion who is buried with you alive while you are buried dead, and he will not be resurrected except with you.  ::terms: rasul Allah, Qays ibn Asim, qarin
- [S850] (scripture) If the companion is noble he honors you, and if base he abandons you; you are raised and questioned only about him, so make him only righteous — for he is your deed ('amal): your luminous form built by knowledge and deed is your provision for the hereafter, higher than your self (zat).  ::terms: qarin, amal, ilm, zat, zad-e-akhirat
- [S851] (scripture) If the companion is righteous you find comfort only in him, and if obscene you feel terror only from him, for he is your deed.  ::terms: amal
- [S852] (authority) Sayyidna Hamiduddin alluded to this in the third mashrab of the fourth tasawwur of Rahat al-'Aql where the opposite is mentioned: the interpretation of one's evil deeds is a harmful animal that harms and terrifies him.  ::terms: Hamiduddin, Rahat al-Aql, mashrab, tasawwur
- [S853] (authority) Rahat al-'Aql by Sayyidna Hamiduddin is profoundly difficult (mughlaq); a learned Arabic scholar (the Nawab of Rampur) could not understand it, and Mawlana called it 'Kulfat al-'Aql' (agony of the intellect) for the uninitiated.  ::terms: Rahat al-Aql, Hamiduddin, Ghulam Husain, mughlaq
- [S854] (doctrine) The dark form (surat zulmaniyyah) is formed from knowledge and deeds done contrary to God's commands, knowledge acquired from people from whom it should not be taken, and enmity held against the people of truth.  ::terms: surat zulmaniyyah, ahl al-haqq
- [S855] (doctrine) The dark form separates from the souls of bodies; the form's station is the two knots (uqdatayn), while the soul, instead of progressing with the form, expands within the body and acquires materiality — the opposite of believers' souls which progress with the form.  ::terms: uqdatayn, nafs, surat, muminin
- [S856] (doctrine) The dark form roams (ja'ilah) in the air and in vile places — latrines, places where blood flows, where wine and filthy forbidden things are made — because it is inclined toward what is forbidden.  ::terms: jailah, hawa
- [S857] (definition) The dark forms reach toward the barzakh which is dark and vile, namely the two knots (uqdatan); barzakh is the path between paradise and hell, and the uqdatayn is the quarantine/short-stay station before reaching their final hell.  ::terms: barzakh, uqdatan, jannat, jahannum
- [S858] (doctrine) For the punished (mu'adhdhabin) the uqdatayn (beneath the sphere of the moon) is the barzakh; their final destination is the sakhrah, where they remain until the Great Cycle (kawr-e-a'zam) of four hundred thousand cycles, each cycle being four hundred thousand years.  ::terms: muadhdhabin, uqdatayn, sakhrah, kawr-e-azam, dawr
- [S859] (doctrine) The dark forms pass in turn through the air, vile places, empty habitations, and terrifying wells (bir muhawwishah) before reaching the uqdatayn, and they are the blameworthy jinn given the subtle lower punishment each according to his measure.  ::terms: jinn, bir muhawwishah, uqdatayn
- [S860] (doctrine) It is necessary that every Imam in his time calls the punished jinn to Islam, for the Imam knows them by their names and places — as Sayyidna Ali did, calling each by name with nothing of their affair hidden from him.  ::terms: imam, jinn, islam, Ali
- [S861] (doctrine) Whoever among the punished completes his lower punishment ('adhab al-adna) and is permitted deliverance submits to the one who calls him, repents, and becomes one of the praiseworthy jinn (jinn al-mahmudin).  ::terms: adhab al-adna, jinn al-mahmudin, jinn al-madhmumin
- [S862] (doctrine) Upon becoming a praiseworthy jinn, he ascends from the side of the moon to the sphere of the sun, serving there as a servant (khadim) among the mustaqdimin in a glorious station.  ::terms: jinn al-mahmudin, khadim, mustaqdimin
- [S863] (authority) The jinn-e-mustaqdimin respond to the call to prayer (adhan); Sayyidna Ali bin Sulayman recounts a crow that came at the adhan guiding lost travelers to a village, an instance illustrating the mustaqdimin.  ::terms: jinn-e-mustaqdimin, adhan, Ali bin Sulayman
- [S864] (authority) The adhan recited by the saints (e.g. Hazrat Fathullah, Hazrat Ishaq, Hazrat Nasrullah, and the narrator at Arafat during Hajj) brought about miraculous reversals — receding floodwaters, redirected fires and winds.  ::terms: adhan, Fathullah, Ishaq, Nasrullah, Arafat
- [S865] (doctrine) There are one hundred and forty thousand mustaqdimin (suwarat-e-mustaqdimin) centered in the sun, who are entrusted ministering angels (mala'ikah).  ::terms: mustaqdimin, malaikah
- [S866] (doctrine) Among the mustaqdimin are attractors (jadhibah) who draw up the praiseworthy forms, repellers (dafi'ah) who send forms down to the world, and restrainers (masikah) for what travels within; when one ascends another descends in his place to be born a believer, perpetually.  ::terms: mustaqdimin, jadhibah, dafiah, masikah
- [S867] (claim) The one hundred and forty thousand mustaqdimin and the one hundred and twenty-four thousand prophets (anbiya') are separate counts and must not be confused.  ::terms: mustaqdimin, anbiya
- [S868] (definition) The second chapter (al-bab al-thani) of the fourth path contains five sections (fusul).  ::terms: al-bab al-thani, fusul
- [S869] (doctrine) Whoever among the mustakhdamin (those made to serve) completes his service is brought down to earth and travels into a thing from the edibles (ma'kulat) and drinkables (mashrubat), taking sustenance suited to him.  ::terms: mustakhdamin, makulat, mashrubat
- [S870] (doctrine) He then appears at the union (mulaqah) between the seed (nutfah) of the da'i and his wife as a trustee, progresses through the stages of the fetus (janin) until born a complete person, and is raised until puberty (bulugh).  ::terms: dai, nutfah, janin, bulugh
- [S871] (doctrine) At puberty he is called and responds, entering the circle of the people of truth (ahl al-haqq); he can be born to anyone but will come to the people of truth.  ::terms: ahl al-haqq, bulugh
- [S872] (doctrine) Because his purification is done and his service accepted, he progresses through the religious ranks (maratib al-diniyyah) in the swiftest and nearest manner, reaching the high ranks and lofty stations with no need ever to return.  ::terms: maratib al-diniyyah, manazil al-samiyah
- [S873] (doctrine) He never returns because he fulfilled what was upon him of sins, repented, and accepted the caller of God (da'i Allah) in the beginning and in the end — in the beginning he accepted the covenant (qawl) in the world of light, and again here.  ::terms: dai Allah, qawl, alam-e-nurani
- [S874] (doctrine) His swift progress is because he rendered his service, stood by his obligation, entered the sacred sanctuary (haram-e-amin), and obeyed the Lord of the worlds.  ::terms: haram-e-amin, Rabb al-alamin
- [S875] (doctrine) On the platform of this world gather various kinds of people — those destined for punishment by their prior states, and those who arrive with a clean slate now in the realm of free choice to accept or not.  ::terms: mustakhdamin
- [S876] (doctrine) As animals are halal or haram, so among arrivals some will progress (halal) and some descend (haram), and within halal some become food for a believer and some food for a disbeliever (kafir).  ::terms: halal, haram, kafir
- [S877] (doctrine) In the realm of free choice no one can pull a person from his choice between the straight and crooked path; the call is presented and he chooses.  ::terms: mustakhdamin
- [S878] (doctrine) Dark forms (suwarat-e-zulmah) can only progress by becoming mustakhdam: held in the uqdatayn, they complete the lower punishment, serve, are presented with the call, and if accepting become mustakhdam.  ::terms: suwarat-e-zulmah, mustakhdam, uqdatayn
- [S879] (doctrine) The station of the cursed forms (surat-e-la'inah) whose end was not good is the sakhrah; their barzakh is the uqdatayn, their bodies undergo the lower punishment in the world, and bodily purification occurs through halal animals.  ::terms: surat-e-lainah, sakhrah, uqdatayn, barzakh, halal
- [S880] (doctrine) The mustakhdamin remain in service until the rising of the Qa'im (qiyam-e-qa'im), after which accounting occurs, fire descends from the sky to burn the enemies' bodies, and they enter the sakhrah through its mouth, remaining there for one kawr — the greater punishment.  ::terms: mustakhdamin, qiyam-e-qaim, sakhrah, kawr, adhab-e-akbar
- [S881] (definition) The lower punishment ('adhab-e-adna) has two forms: the punishment of the seven levels of hell (adrak) and the punishment of the peripheries (atraf); the great punishment ('adhab-e-akbar) is the punishment of the sakhrah.  ::terms: adhab-e-adna, adrak, atraf, adhab-e-akbar, sakhrah
- [S882] (doctrine) Whoever refuses, is arrogant, and persists in disobedience and disbelief is made to tread the bridges (qanatir) of the lower bodily punishment until the Qa'im stands, then is made to tread the greater punishment.  ::terms: qanatir, adhab al-adna al-jismani, qaim, adhab al-akbar
- [S883] (authority) Sayyidna Zu'ayb alluded in his Risalat al-Nafs: at every transition there is a halt (tawqif); if they repent they return to a higher rank, and if they insist they descend to the lowest rank, to the Great Cycle, the product of three hundred and sixty thousand multiplied by its like.  ::terms: Zuayb, Risalat al-Nafs, tawqif, kawr-e-azam
- [S884] (scripture) God said He will make them taste the lower punishment short of the greater punishment so that perhaps they may return — evidence that the lower punishment offers repeated chances to return, a mercy of God.  ::terms: adhab al-adna, adhab al-akbar, lallahum yarjiun
- [S885] (scripture) Amir al-Mu'minin indicated this in his saying 'wa yathanuhum al-dawru fi madarij al-kawr' — the cycle grinds them in the stages of the cycle; in every cycle they are presented the call, and if they reach the sakhrah, lava comes out of it.  ::terms: Amir al-Muminin, dawr, madarij al-kawr, sakhrah
- [S886] (claim) The white days (ayyam-e-bid) are the thirteenth, fourteenth, and fifteenth, the best of days; of the 354 days in a year about one hundred fifty are distinguished days.  ::terms: ayyam-e-bid
