# SECTION 05: The Descent of the Tenth Intellect and the First Cohort

Lecture range: [10, 11]


## LEDGER SPINE TEACHINGS YOU MUST PRESERVE (each at full depth)

- [S122] (ruling) After the Tenth Intellect's repentance, the Originator obligated him to deliver (khalas) the three groups and tasked him, because he was a chief for them and a cause of some of their error — per the principle 'he who breaks a bone, upon him is its mending' (man kasara 'azman fa-'alayhi jabruhu).  ::terms: khalas, jabr, Ashir, man kasara azman
- [S123] (doctrine) The Tenth Intellect was established among the three groups in the station the First Intellect held in the world of origination, and was commanded to deliver them; he addressed them, but they refused, were rebuked and admonished, turned away in arrogance, increased in darkness, and so deserved descent (hubut), degradation (tasafful), and the fall (suqut).  ::terms: hubut, tasafful, suqut, Ashir
- [S124] (doctrine) The groups mixed together like accumulating clouds (sahab) and moved from their own selves with a first motion producing length (tul), beginning with heat/Aries (hamal) and ending in coldness/Libra (mizan) — creating four constellations: tali', hamal, gharib, mizan.  ::terms: tul, harakah, hamal, mizan, buruj
- [S125] (doctrine) A second motion produced breadth ('ard), beginning with moisture/Capricorn (jady) and ending with Cancer (sartan)/dryness; a third motion sought to remedy themselves and produced depth ('umq) — all three motions by intentional divine grace ('inayat ilahiyya) directed by the Tenth Intellect.  ::terms: ard, umq, jady, sartan, inayat ilahiyya
- [S126] (doctrine) The Tenth Intellect determined there was no deliverance for the fallen except through coming into Hanafiyyah human forms similar to the imaginal forms (since the human form is the best and noblest of forms), and that this requires the establishment of a da'wah similar to the original da'wah of origination — but, unlike the original, with place, time, great trial, and test (imtihan).  ::terms: Hanafiyyah, da'wah, imtihan, ashbah suwariyya
- [S127] (argument) The wise say 'man is the first of thought and the last of action' (al-insan awwal al-fikr wa akhir al-'amal): through the test, that which has risen and is pure (taraffa'a wa safa) is separated and ascends, while that which remained base and harsh falls back (yartakisu).  ::terms: awwal al-fikr, akhir al-amal, hukama
- [S128] (doctrine) When the accumulated/mixed matter formed, the more transparent and subtle part became the form (surah) and the more obscure and dense part became prime matter (hayula); divine grace made some an active instrument like the male (al-dhakar) — the world of spheres ('alam al-aflak) — and some passive like the female (al-untha) — the world of mothers ('alam al-ummahat).  ::terms: surah, hayula, alam al-aflak, alam al-ummahat, dhakar, untha
- [S129] (doctrine) When the three dimensions (length, breadth, depth) gathered in the form of the cross (salib), the spheres (aflak) were made from the purest of the mingled matter — and these spheres are the first group, which affirmed the Originator's oneness, glorified the First Intellect, sanctified it, and acknowledged its right.  ::terms: salib, aflak, firqat al-ula
- [S130] (doctrine) The first group, when the Tenth Intellect addressed it along with the other two groups, insisted with their insistence (the equality view), was arrogant and descended with them, becoming dense and ignorant of subtleties — but at the moment of falling it became aware, regretted, and sought repentance; since full descent was no longer possible, it was made into spheres, stars (kawakib), and constellations (buruj).  ::terms: firqat al-ula, aflak, kawakib, buruj
- [S131] (doctrine) From the two remaining groups (the polytheists and atheists, mushrikin wa mulhidin) the rest was extracted; the order of densities formed step by step (shay'an ba'da shay'in): first the sphere of the encompassing celestial sphere (falak al-aflak/muhit) of imaginary density (kasafat wahmiyya), then the sphere of constellations (denser), then the seven planetary spheres, then the four mothers (fire, air, water, earth).  ::terms: mushrikin, mulhidin, falak al-muhit, kasafat wahmiyya, ummahat
- [S132] (doctrine) The ordering of the world of spheres ('alam al-aflak) is like the gradual creation of man (thing after thing); the first thing acted upon (munfa'il) and formed from the spheres was the Sun (Shams), because it is connected to the heart of man (qalb al-insan) and is the center of innate heat (al-hararat al-ghariziyya).  ::terms: alam al-aflak, Shams, qalb al-insan, hararat ghariziyya
- [S133] (doctrine) The Sun is the center connected to the point of the compass (nuqtat al-barkar); innate heat is emitted from it to the three planets above it (Saturn/Zuhal, Jupiter/Mushtari, Mars/Mirrikh) and the three below it (Mercury/'Utarid, Venus/Zuhrah, Moon/Qamar) and the four mothers (ummahat); it is the origin of the first motion.  ::terms: Shams, Zuhal, Qamar, ummahat
- [S134] (definition) The encompassing sphere (falak al-muhit, the Atlas) is the most subtle, of conceptual/imaginary density, containing nothing; the sphere of constellations (falak al-buruj) holds all the fixed stars (which revolve in their own orbits), while the seven planets (sayyarah) revolve around the Sun as center.  ::terms: falak al-muhit, falak al-buruj, sayyarah, Shams
- [S135] (doctrine) The Sun is connected to the heart, the Moon (Qamar, center of moisture and coldness) to the head, and Saturn (Zuhal, center of coldness and dryness) to the two feet; the whole world of spheres became like a single body called the Absolute Body (al-jism al-mutlaq), the counterpart to the human body.  ::terms: Shams, Qamar, Zuhal, al-jism al-mutlaq
- [S136] (doctrine) The encompassing sphere (falak al-muhit) was made a spirit (ruh) for the Absolute Body because it is the most subtle and noble; it is the mover (muharrik) of the Absolute Body and the very essence of space and foundation (al-makan wa al-usquss), turning the body every day and night with a single divine turn (qalabah ilahiyya).  ::terms: falak al-muhit, ruh, muharrik, makan
- [S137] (doctrine) The Sun follows the encompassing sphere in nobility and virtue because it was the first to repent and seek forgiveness (after the falak al-muhit) while in descent, and the first to glorify the First Intellect and declare Allah's oneness after the falak al-muhit; its radiant light filling heavens and earth was made as a reward for what it did.  ::terms: Shams, nadima, taba, falak al-muhit
- [S138] (doctrine) The lights of the stars and zodiacal signs are each given according to what preceded from each (the measure of its repentance); the scale of justice (mizan al-'adl) is established, leaving neither small nor large thing without reward or punishment — what we see with the apparent eye is only the shell (sadaf).  ::terms: anwar, mizan al-adl, sadaf
- [S139] (doctrine) After the lines of the cross formed, a center arose from accumulated matter at the meeting of length and width — the first depth ('umq al-awwal); there a splitting space (al-jaww al-munfaqih) formed in which temperament (mizaj) and the tempered (muntazij) became balanced, yielding eight temperaments (combinations of heat/cold with dryness/moisture).  ::terms: umq al-awwal, jaww, mizaj, eight temperaments
- [S140] (doctrine) The earth is the rock (sakhrah), the densest, darkest, and murkiest of all, formed from the third group (firqa-e-thalithah) which denied the creator, did not affirm the Originator's oneness, and adhered to no intellect; the four mothers (fire, air, water — the second group) and soil revolve around and above it.  ::terms: sakhrah, firqat al-thalithah, ummahat
- [S141] (doctrine) The aflak groups map to the three sects: the spheres (falak al-muhit etc.) are the first group; the mothers — fire, air, water, earth — are the second group (believed in Allah but not the First Intellect/Emanation); the rock (sakhrah) is the third group (denied the creator).  ::terms: aflak, ummahat, sakhrah, firaq
- [S142] (doctrine) When the sphere turned a conceptual revolution (dawra wahmiyya), all planets cast their rays on the rock but found no passage (the rock was stubborn and accepted nothing); a subtle smoke (dukhan latif) remained around the rock from the first group, kept distinct by intentional divine grace (the second intention from the 'Ashir).  ::terms: dawra wahmiyya, dukhan latif, sakhrah, inayat
- [S143] (doctrine) When the planets' rays could not penetrate the rock they returned to their origins, and a portion of the subtle smoke (its own affinity) united with each ray, forming a shell (sadaf) for each planet; the rays-and-smoke together became the bodies (ajram) of those conceptual (wahmi) planets, which clothed them.  ::terms: sadaf, ajram, kawakib wahmiyya, dukhan
- [S144] (argument) The 'alam al-aflak (world of light/nadimin/penitents) is composed of those who repented in the luminous world and became dense; the elements descend easily but rise with difficulty because the rock accepts nothing — the human's descent is upon the rock, from which he must make progress.  ::terms: alam al-aflak, nadimin, sakhrah
