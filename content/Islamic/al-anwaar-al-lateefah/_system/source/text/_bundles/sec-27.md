# SECTION 27: The Farthest Mosque and the Ascent of the Faithful

Lecture range: [60, 61, 62, 63]


## LEDGER SPINE TEACHINGS YOU MUST PRESERVE (each at full depth)

- [S1049] (claim) The 27th of Rajab unites three events: Ali's birth (30 years before), the Prophet's mission (Mab'ath, when Ali was 10 and the Prophet 40), and Laylat al-Isra'.  ::terms: Laylat al-Isra', Mab'ath al-Nabi, wiladat 'Ali, 27 Rajab
- [S1050] (scripture) On the Night of Ascension Allah raised the Prophet to the knowledge of His Kingdom (malakut), informed him of the limits of the heavens, taught him the unseen, favored him over all prophets and brought him near the celestial limits.  ::terms: malakut, hudud al-samawat, 'ilm al-ghayb, al-hudud al-'ulwiyyah
- [S1051] (authority) Fifty prayers were made obligatory and reduced to five at Moses' counsel during the Ascension; the lecturer holds the Prophet was more correct to accept five, given prayer's great blessings.  ::terms: Isra', salat, Musa, istahya rasul Allah
- [S1052] (scripture) The verse 'Glory to Him who took His servant by night from al-Masjid al-Haram to al-Masjid al-Aqsa' declares the Creator's transcendence; God showed His signs (ayat), not Himself, and the inability to grasp comprehension is itself comprehension (al-'ajzu 'an dark al-idrak idrakun) — a saying of Ali.  ::terms: asra bi-'abdihi, al-masjid al-haram, al-masjid al-aqsa, ayat, al-'ajzu 'an dark al-idrak idrakun
- [S1053] (doctrine) God took 'His servant' ('abd), not 'the prophet' or 'the messenger', because servitude precedes messengership, as in the testimony 'Muhammad is His servant and His messenger' ('abduhu wa rasuluhu).  ::terms: 'abd, 'abduhu wa rasuluhu, ashhadu anna Muhammadan
- [S1054] (doctrine) In ta'wil 'night' (layl) is an example of the veil and concealment (sitr/kitman) of the inner dimension; God concealed the Prophet and made him travel in the inner world between himself and the Completer (mutimm) of his era, who is the sixth Imam (Abu Talib).  ::terms: layl, sitr, kitman al-batin, mutimm, al-masjid al-aqsa, Abu Talib
- [S1055] (doctrine) The Prophet's elevation by 40 years signifies completing 40 stages of prophethood then becoming messenger, surpassing all prophets; reaching qaba qawsayni aw adna (Abd al-Muttalib and Abu Talib as the two bows, Abdullah as adna), attaining the rank of the veil (hijabiyya) below 'Ayn Hazira (the Originator).  ::terms: qaba qawsayni aw adna, 'Abd al-Muttalib, Abu Talib, 'Abdullah, hijabiyya, 'Ayn Hazira
- [S1056] (doctrine) The verse 'they feed the indigent, orphan, and captive' is interpreted: miskin = the Da'i from whom hearts find peace, yatim = a high-ranking one whose supporters were finished, asir = one imprisoned where evil scholars controlled — each freed by the call of the five companions (Ali, Fatima, Hasan, Husayn, Fidda).  ::terms: miskin, yatim, asir, da'i, khamsat al-ashab, yut'imuna al-ta'am
- [S1057] (scripture) On the Ascension the Prophet saw the greatest signs of his Lord with the sight of the pure soul that did not swerve (ma zagha al-basar), narrated by A'isha that he never left her, indicating the vision was of the soul, not bodily absence.  ::terms: ma kadhaba al-fu'ad, ma zagha al-basar, ayat rabbihi al-kubra, basar al-nafs al-safiya
- [S1058] (doctrine) The Buraq in ta'wil is the flashing, illuminating knowledge (al-'ilm al-mubriq) — the bayan, above the knowledge of the successor (lahiq) and below the Imam's knowledge; riding it is mastery over it, and ascension to heaven is being informed of its kingdom.  ::terms: Buraq, al-'ilm al-mubriq, bayan, lahiq, malakut
- [S1059] (scripture) God showed Abraham the kingdom of the heavens and earth (malakut), meaning the spiritual world after the loftiness of his rank, and the earthly kingdom means the physical hudud (star, moon, sun) from whom he learned in the beginning.  ::terms: malakut al-samawat wa al-ard, Ibrahim, kawkab, qamar, shams, hudud jismaniyyun
- [S1060] (authority) The Prophet led all the prophets in prayer (esoterically his call to all nations), with Abraham as the muezzin at Safa and Marwah; he was sent to all nations whereas other prophets were sent only to their specific peoples.  ::terms: salla bi-jami' al-anbiya', da'wa, mu'adhdhin, Ibrahim, Safa, Marwa
- [S1061] (doctrine) The Prophet conveyed the knowledge he received to his foundation (asas) and instructed transmission one to one to each successor; al-Masjid al-Aqsa is the mutimm upon whom initiation is forbidden, and the blessing (baraka) is the support and initiation.  ::terms: asas, mutimm, al-masjid al-aqsa, baraka, ta'yid, mufataha
- [S1062] (ruling) The 27th Rajab prayer of 22 rak'at (with 22 surahs, then Hamd, Inna Anzalna, Ayat al-Kursi each seven times) and its supplication commemorate the Prophet's mission; Rajab is the month of the Imams from the Ahl al-Bayt, Sha'ban the Prophet's month, Ramadan Allah's month.  ::terms: 22 rak'at, Rajab, Sha'ban, Ramadan, Ahl al-Bayt
- [S1063] (doctrine) Rajab's 30 days correspond to 30 Imams of the progeny of Isma'il; its nights are for the progeny of Isma'il and its days for the progeny of Ishaq; the 27th Mab'ath signifies 27 hidden Imams before the Prophet, with three days remaining for Abd al-Muttalib, Abdullah, and Abu Talib.  ::terms: Rajab, Isma'il, Ishaq, 'Abd al-Muttalib, 'Abdullah, Abu Talib
- [S1064] (doctrine) The three Imams (Abd al-Muttalib, Abdullah, Abu Talib) of the 27th-Rajab indication have a connection to both the Prophet and Mawla Ali, present at the Prophet's birth (and prophethood, 'Kuntu nabiyyan wa Adamu bayna al-ma'i wa al-tin').  ::terms: 'Abd al-Muttalib, 'Abdullah, Abu Talib, Kuntu nabiyyan, Hijab, Muhtajib
- [S1065] (doctrine) The 'Ayn 'Azimah is the station of the Muhtajib in the realm of life, of which is said 'I am the First, the Last, the Manifest, the Hidden' (ana al-awwal, al-akhir, al-zahir, al-batin), with worldly figures made its servants.  ::terms: 'Ayn 'Azimah, Muhtajib, 'alam al-hayat, ana al-awwal
- [S1066] (definition) The ranks ascend: Mustajib, Mu'min, Mukasir, Ma'sum, Da'i (mahsur/balagh), Hujjah, Bab, Imam (ten ranks), then Nubuwwa, Risala, Imama, Wasiyya (four ranks), above which is the divine utterance (nutq ilahi) counted among the ranks of Qa'imiyya.  ::terms: mustajib, mu'min, mukasir, ma'sum, da'i, hujjah, bab, imam, nutq ilahi, Qa'imiyya
- [S1067] (doctrine) The bearers of divine utterance (nutq ilahi) in this era are Hunayd, Ibrahim, and Hashim, plus the seventh of each era (the Prophet); the 124,000 prophets occupy lower levels below the rank of nutq ilahi.  ::terms: nutq ilahi, Hunayd, Ibrahim, Hashim, 124000 anbiya'
- [S1068] (scripture) All human beings — natiq, wasi, Imam, follower or others — are in need of instruction (ta'lim) at the start of existence, per 'And Allah brought you out of your mothers' wombs knowing nothing', which is general for noble and ignoble alike.  ::terms: ta'lim, akhrajakum min butuni ummahatikum la ta'lamuna shay'an
- [S1069] (doctrine) The difference among people is only in intelligence, sagacity, thought and acceptance (dhaka, fitna, fikr, qubul); the noblest — the natiq Muhammad and his father Ibrahim, and Amir al-Mu'minin Ali — learned and progressed bit by bit until reaching the pinnacle of utterance, with far greater preparedness ('iddat).  ::terms: dhaka, fitna, fikr, qubul, 'iddat, natiq, Ibrahim, Amir al-Mu'minin
- [S1070] (scripture) Abraham's 'seeing the star, moon and sun set' (la uhibbu al-afilin) refers to the physical hudud who undertook his upbringing in the beginning of his affair and from whom he learned.  ::terms: kawkab, qamar, shams, la uhibbu al-afilin, hudud jismaniyyun
- [S1071] (doctrine) Amir al-Mu'minin learned in the beginning from the Prophet, from Khadijah bint Khuwaylid (his teacher), and other hudud; he was attributed to Khadijah and likened to 'Isa ibn Maryam (Maryam = Khadijah, 'Isa = Ali).  ::terms: Amir al-Mu'minin, Khadijah bint Khuwaylid, 'Isa ibn Maryam, duriba ibn maryam mathalan
- [S1072] (authority) Ali said 'Rasulullah taught me a thousand chapters of knowledge, from each of which a thousand chapters opened up for me' (allamani rasulullah alfa babin... infataha li min kulli babin alfa bab).  ::terms: allamani rasulullah alfa babin, infataha li alfa bab
- [S1073] (authority) The natiq's saying 'I took from five, delivered to five, and between me and my Lord are five' means: the five taken from are the five preceding speaking prophets (Adam, Nuh, Ibrahim, Musa, 'Isa); the five between him and his Lord are the religious hudud who taught him (Ubayy ibn Ka'b, Zayd ibn 'Amr, 'Amr ibn Nufayl, Maysarah, Khadijah).  ::terms: akhadhtu min khamsah, khamsa nutuqa', al-hudud al-diniyyah, Ubayy ibn Ka'b, Zayd ibn 'Amr, 'Amr ibn Nufayl, Maysarah, Khadijah
- [S1074] (doctrine) The Master of the Time who established the Prophet, elevated and entrusted him is his uncle Abu Talib; the five hudud are Abu Talib's hudud.  ::terms: sahib al-waqt, Abu Talib, hudud
- [S1075] (authority) The five to whom the Prophet delivered are Amir al-Mu'minin, Hasan, Husayn, al-Baqir (Muhammad ibn Ali), and Muhammad ibn Isma'il; entrustment to Hasan and Husayn via 'al-Hasan wa al-Husayn imaman qama aw qa'ada wa abuhuma khayrun minhuma'.  ::terms: sallamtu ila khamsah, Amir al-Mu'minin, al-Hasan, al-Husayn, al-Baqir, Muhammad ibn Isma'il
- [S1076] (authority) Entrustment to al-Baqir came via the Prophet's message to Jabir ibn Abdillah ('O Baqir, cleave knowledge open'); entrustment to Muhammad ibn Isma'il via the second testimony of messengership in the adhan, since the Prophet's testimony for himself is impermissible.  ::terms: al-Baqir, Jabir ibn Abdillah, abqirhu baqran, adhan, Muhammad ibn Isma'il, ashhadu anna Muhammadan rasulullah
- [S1077] (authority) Imam Muhammad al-Baqir, not Zayn al-'Abidin, spread the Prophet's knowledge in an era of dissemination; Ja'far is called al-Sadiq because he narrated his father's teachings with truth (sidq).  ::terms: Muhammad al-Baqir, Zayn al-'Abidin, Ja'far al-Sadiq, sidq, nashr
- [S1078] (doctrine) There are 49 Imams (seven weeks) after the Prophet; the seventh of each week are superior, with Muhammad ibn Isma'il, al-Mu'izz, Imam al-Tayyib, al-Mansur etc. as week-heads; among them four are highest (la ilaha kalima's four words), and Muhammad ibn Isma'il is unique (sahib kasbat al-ilahiyya).  ::terms: 49 a'imma, hafti, Muhammad ibn Isma'il, al-Mu'izz, al-Tayyib, sahib kasbat al-ilahiyya
- [S1079] (doctrine) The double testimony in the adhan: the first points to the Prophet, the second to Muhammad ibn Isma'il, completer of the era (mutimm al-dawr) and master of the apostles, the seventh natiq, indicated by al-Mu'izz in the Saturday prayer.  ::terms: adhan, Muhammad ibn Isma'il, mutimm al-dawr, sahib al-rusul, natiq sabi', al-Mu'izz
- [S1080] (doctrine) The Qa'im al-Qiyamah's affair is loftier than the apostles because each apostle is a limb of his limbs; he has become a Universal Intellect ('aql kulli) like the primordial abstract intellects and maintains the administration of the entire world, like the Tenth's administrators of nature.  ::terms: Qa'im al-Qiyamah, 'aql kulli, al-'uqul al-ibda'iyyah, tadbir al-'alam, rutbat al-'ashir
- [S1081] (doctrine) Muhammad ibn Isma'il is from the divine speakers (nutuqa' ilahiyyin) along with Muhammad and Ibrahim, being the third to them; each natiq is a summary of the stations preceding him and a summoner (da'i) of the Imams of his time, a limb of the following Imam.  ::terms: Muhammad ibn Isma'il, nutuqa' ilahiyyin, Muhammad, Ibrahim, da'i, majmal al-maqamat
- [S1082] (doctrine) When an Imam (e.g., al-Tayyib) is in infancy after his father's death, his teachers are the Bab, Hujjah and Da'i al-balagh who never leave him from the time of his father's designation (nass) and concealment (satr); they instruct and elevate him, then he returns as their benefactor (mufid) bestowing blessings and support.  ::terms: Imam al-Tayyib, Bab, Hujjah, da'i al-balagh, nass, satr, mufid, muraqqi
- [S1083] (doctrine) When one of the three (Bab/Hujjah/Da'i) is to pass, he does not depart until he appoints a successor in his place; their time of death is by Allah's decree, not their own power — as Ja'far became mufid at twenty when Muhammad ibn al-Fayd entrusted his affairs ('Now the support has become Ja'fari').  ::terms: nuqlat, khalifa, mufid, Sayyidna Ja'far, Muhammad ibn al-Fayd
- [S1084] (doctrine) The Imam is connected to the sun, providing support to the three preceding ranks connected to Saturn, Jupiter and Mars; just as the heart is connected in the body, supporting the senses above and below it.  ::terms: Imam, shams, Zuhal, Mushtari, Mirrikh, qalb, mumidd
- [S1085] (doctrine) The Prophet distributed the spiritual forms (suwar) of the five prophets: Adam to Ubayy ibn Ka'b, Nuh to Fatima (hence her lamentation/nawha), Ibrahim to Ali, Musa to Hasan, 'Isa to Husayn — explaining Husayn's likenesses to 'Isa (six months in the womb, persecution, a batul mother, post-martyrdom apparition).  ::terms: suwar, Adam, Nuh, Ibrahim, Musa, 'Isa, Fatima, al-Hasan, al-Husayn, batul
- [S1086] (authority) The first arrivers at the great gathering wait, by means of the first, for the arrival of the last (yantazir bi-awwalikum quduma akhirikum), since their elevation completes when all gather in the station of the Tenth.  ::terms: yantazir bi-awwalikum quduma akhirikum, 'ashir, taraqqi
- [S1087] (doctrine) In the concealment of martyrdom (hijabat), there is no qisas on the pure noble form (nasut/surat) or pure soul; qisas is only on the earthly part, so the Imam (the mudabbir) brings the form whose dues are pending to unite with the one being killed, in any human shape — e.g., the slayer thinks he kills Imam Husayn but it is the pending form taking its due.  ::terms: hijabat, nasut sharif, surat, qisas, mudabbir, Imam Husayn
- [S1088] (doctrine) The fida'i of Imam Husayn is Hazrat Abbas, whose fida'i is Muslim ibn Aqil; the Imam connects pending-qisas forms to such events, the slayer believing he kills Muslim ibn Aqil while really the form with pending dues is present.  ::terms: fida'i, Hazrat Abbas, Muslim ibn Aqil, qisas, nasut
- [S1089] (authority) The phenomenon of one figure appearing as another (shubbiha lahum) is illustrated by Mawlana Aminuddin appearing simultaneously at many hosts' meals, Ali leading Salman al-Farsi's funeral in distant Mada'in, and Judas being changed to resemble 'Isa.  ::terms: shubbiha lahum, Aminuddin, Salman al-Farsi, Mada'in, 'Isa, Judas
- [S1090] (authority) When a woman challenged Ali's claim 'ask me before you lose me' (saluni qabla an tafqiduni) about leading Salman's prayer in Mada'in yet not Uthman's in Madina, the reason was that the Muslims did not permit Uthman's burial in al-Baqi' (he was buried in the adjacent Jewish graveyard, later incorporated).  ::terms: saluni qabla an tafqiduni, Salman al-Farsi, Uthman, Jannat al-Baqi'
- [S1091] (ruling) Names from companions like Uthman ibn Maz'un may be kept; Ali named a son Uthman after the pious companion Uthman ibn Maz'un, and names like Abu Bakr and Abd al-Rahman are permissible.  ::terms: Uthman ibn Maz'un, Abu Bakr, Abd al-Rahman
