## LEDGER SPINE TEACHINGS YOU MUST PRESERVE (each at full depth)

- [S233] (doctrine) When the primordial water reached the state previously described and the sun entered the zodiac sign of Jauza, the air grew hot, the bawarih (hot summer winds) blew, and the earth's surface heated, causing the oil-like substance to dry and congeal.  ::terms: burj al-jauza, bawarih, duhn, in'iqad
- [S234] (doctrine) Divine providence (al-inayat al-ilahiyyah) outlines and forms the human shape out of heat and cold as God wills.  ::terms: al-inayat al-ilahiyyah, taswir, harr, bard
- [S235] (doctrine) Every star (kawkab) was placed in charge of forming a specific member of those primordial bodies.  ::terms: kawkab, mutawalli, a'da'
- [S236] (doctrine) The human form was engraved (naqasha surat al-insaniyyah) when the governance reached Utarid (Mercury) with the assistance of Zuhal (Saturn) and Qamar (the Moon).  ::terms: naqsh surat al-insaniyyah, Utarid, Zuhal, Qamar
- [S237] (scripture) The first man grew from the earth, rising from its place little by little like a fetus, seated with chin on knees and arms wrapped, as the Quran states 'Allah grew you from the earth as a [progressive] growth.'  ::terms: wallahu anbatakum min al-ard nabatan, nabat
- [S238] (definition) The nafs al-namiya (vegetative soul) is what governs the being in the womb, and its center/origin is the moon (Qamar).  ::terms: nafs al-namiya, Qamar
- [S239] (doctrine) At birth into the world God blew into man a life-giving divine spirit (al-ruh al-muhyiyah) connected to the rays of the sun and moon, which is the nafs al-hissiyyah (sensory soul).  ::terms: al-ruh al-muhyiyah, nafs al-hissiyyah, shams, qamar
- [S240] (authority) Amir al-Mu'minin Ali ibn Abi Talib, asked about the nafs al-hissiyyah, defined it as a celestial power (quwwah falakiyyah) whose origin is the celestial spheres, whose union with the body is at physical birth, whose seat is the heart, and whose action is sensation, movement, assault, and dominance.  ::terms: nafs al-hissiyyah, quwwah falakiyyah, maqarruha al-qalb, Ali ibn Abi Talib
- [S241] (doctrine) The cause of the nafs al-hissiyyah's separation from the body is the disharmony of the four humoral materials (safra, sawda, balgham, damm), and on death it returns to the sun whence it began.  ::terms: ikhtilaf al-mawadd, safra, sawda, balgham, damm
- [S242] (doctrine) Each of the seven planets governs (tadbir) for 51,000 years in sequence beginning with Zuhal (Saturn), then Mushtari, Mirrikh, Shams, Zuhra, Utarid, and Qamar.  ::terms: tadbir, ahad wa khamsun alf sanah, Zuhal, Utarid
- [S243] (doctrine) The creation/impression of the human form (naqsh surat al-insaniyyah) occurred in the governing cycle of Utarid with the companionship (murafaqat) of Zuhal and Qamar, after roughly 250,000-300,000 years of celestial revolutions.  ::terms: murafaqat, Utarid, Zuhal, Qamar
- [S244] (doctrine) The celestial revolutions before human creation are an uncounted period (fatrat) of preparatory arrangements for the Juz' al-Ibda'i; the dawr al-kashf is counted only from the beginning of human creation.  ::terms: fatrat, Juz' al-Ibda'i, dawr al-kashf
- [S245] (doctrine) When man appeared, Zuhal began the governance of the seventh millennium (tadbir alf al-sabi') with the participation of Qamar.  ::terms: tadbir alf al-sabi', Zuhal, Qamar
- [S246] (claim) Human forms appeared with the millennial stature in each of twelve islands (jaza'ir) according to the variety of their races: Arabs, Persians, Indians, Romans, Sindhis, Ethiopians, Berbers, Khazars, Zayalis, Slavs, Turks, Zanj, and others.  ::terms: jaza'ir, qamah alfiyyah, ajnas
- [S247] (claim) On the island of Sarandib (on the equator) many humans appeared, among whom twenty-eight individuals were the purest, most refined, most noble, and most perfect of all creation, the pinnacle of the human species.  ::terms: Sarandib, khatt al-istiwa', thamaniyah wa 'ishrun, nihayat al-jins al-bashari
- [S248] (doctrine) The twenty-eight individuals correspond to the twenty-eight abjad letters (huruf al-mu'jam) and to the twenty-eight lunar mansions (manazil) of the moon's monthly cycle, to which they are attributed.  ::terms: huruf al-mu'jam, manazil, thamaniyah wa 'ishrun
- [S249] (doctrine) These first human individuals reached perfection of body, speech, and action without passing through ordinary maturation (ihtilam), turning their vision toward the Tenth (Ashir) with divine assistance.  ::terms: rutbat al-ihtilam, kamal, ashir
- [S250] (doctrine) The Ashir (Tenth) is the ancestor of the final ancestor, Adam al-Hayat, who carries out the governance, occupies the station opposite the First Intellect in the world of origination (alam al-ibda'), and is the place where the rays of the First Intellect fall.  ::terms: ashir, Adam al-Hayat, al-aql al-awwal, alam al-ibda', matrah shu'a'atihi
