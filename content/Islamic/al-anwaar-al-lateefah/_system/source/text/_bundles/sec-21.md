## LEDGER SPINE TEACHINGS YOU MUST PRESERVE (each at full depth)

- [S723] (doctrine) As for the followers (atba') from the pillars ('umud) and subjects (ra'aya), some return after the perception-levels and some complete them; their attainment of punishment is according to their earnestness for the people of truth and their actions.  ::terms: atba', 'umud, ra'aya, adrak
- [S724] (doctrine) On the ascending return, the soul begins in minerals beneficial to mankind, then non-forbidden plants, then lawful animals — the good path, as opposed to the bad bodies entered while descending.  ::terms: ma'adin al-nafi'ah, nabat, hayawan al-muhallal
- [S725] (doctrine) The lawful animal is used by whoever must use it (a wali or a zidd) and becomes a passage (ma'bar) for the person to emerge in the fifth millennium, where tribulations (balaya) are presented to him.  ::terms: ma'bar, wali, zidd, khams al-alfiyyah, balaya
- [S726] (ruling) Those undergoing punishment are not subject to shari'ah rulings, since shari'ah obligations apply to persons of intellect, not to animals or those in the qisas period.  ::terms: shari'ah, qisas
- [S727] (doctrine) The returning soul is presented with the wilayah of the hadd it had denied; if it accepts, it is saved (naja) and enters the company of the people of truth — a clean-slate state like the first perfection (kamal-e-awwal).  ::terms: wilayah, hudud, ahl al-haqq, kamal-e-awwal
- [S728] (doctrine) If it refuses and is arrogant, it returns falling (saqitan) into the classes of punishment; this continues until the rising of the Qaim.  ::terms: saqit, asnaf al-'adhab, qiyam al-qa'im
- [S729] (doctrine) When the Qaim's appearance draws near, all of creation from the time of Adam to the era of his cycle manifests with his appearance; no one remains, obedient or sinner, except he appears, journeying to earth then to temperament and compounding, then into foods and seminal drops, ascending in ranks to appear as a person upon his first invited state.  ::terms: qa'im, Adam, mizaj, nutfah, al-mad'uwwah ila-l-haqq
- [S730] (doctrine) Each soul is restored upon the very state (old age, youth, smallness, bigness, acceptance, denial, even skin color) in which it was first invited to the truth.  ::terms: halatihim al-ula, alwan
- [S731] (doctrine) Every prophet, wasi, imam, and hadd is personified (yashkhasu) for the people of his era as a witness over them.  ::terms: nabiyy, wasiyy, imam, hadd
- [S732] (scripture) Quran 39:69 ('the prophets and the witnesses will be brought, and it will be judged between them with truth') means each maqam is appointed a witness (shahid) over the deeds of the people of its time; shahid here means a saint of higher rank, not a literal witness.  ::terms: nabiyyin, shuhada', shahid, maqam
- [S733] (scripture) Quran 56:49-50 ('Say, indeed the former and the latter will surely be gathered for the appointed time of a known day') narrates the gathering of all creation at the appointed time.  ::terms: awwalin, akhirin, miqat
- [S734] (doctrine) Each maqam/hadd disputes (yunafishu) with the people of his era — the Qaim among them and the one sent to them — confronting them about their belying, stubbornness, hostility, and all their actions and sayings, small and great.  ::terms: munafashah, maqamat, hudud
- [S735] (doctrine) The gathering of resurrection occurs in the plains of qiyamah, identified as the western line of the equator (khatt al-istiwa al-gharbi), the land of Ma'rib, because the rising of the Qaim will be there.  ::terms: 'arasat al-qiyamah, khatt al-istiwa, ard al-ma'arib, qa'im
- [S736] (doctrine) Only those creatures (khala'iq, including animals) whose time for qiyamah has come will gather at Ma'rib, rushing from all islands; not the entire world, since the place would become constricted.  ::terms: khala'iq, jaza'ir, qiyamah
- [S737] (scripture) Quran 21:96-97 ('Until, when Ya'juj and Ma'juj are released, and they from every elevation descend... and the true promise has drawn near, the eyes of those who disbelieved will stare') is interpreted: Ya'juj and Ma'juj are the opposites (azdad), their followers are the disbelievers, and their objects of worship are their chiefs who stood in the station of the true Imams.  ::terms: Ya'juj, Ma'juj, hadab, azdad, a'immat al-haqq
- [S738] (doctrine) When the creatures gather before the Qaim and every hadd, imam, wasi, and natiq is individualized for the people of his era and made to face all their deeds, the necks of the opposites (azdad) are struck and a fire is sent that burns them all, transforming them into vapor at that moment.  ::terms: qa'im, hudud, a'immah, awsiya', nutaqa', azdad
- [S739] (doctrine) The vapor of the destroyed opposites ascends to the two knots because the two knots attract it through the foul affinity (al-munasabah al-khabithah) between them.  ::terms: bukhar, uqdatain, al-munasabah al-khabithah
- [S740] (doctrine) From the two knots beneath the earth, the Sakhrah (the rock in the lowest part of the earth) absorbs that vapor through affinity, drawing it into its parts and pores until it reaches its center.  ::terms: sakhrah, uqdatain, munasabah, bukhar
- [S741] (doctrine) By the will of the Mudabbir, a reaction occurs in the Sakhrah's center that re-forms each individual upon the state (the millennial stature, qamat al-alfiyyah) in which he opposed the Master of the Age (Sahib al-Zaman).  ::terms: mashi'at al-mudabbir, sakhrah, qamat al-alfiyyah, Sahib al-Zaman
- [S742] (definition) The Sakhrah is shaped like a brass pot (qidr al-sufr): hard smooth bottom, hollow middle of sulfuric (kibritiyyah) stones, a hard rock roof — closed (mulamlamah) on all sides with pores only in its lower part to vent the vapor.  ::terms: sakhrah, qidr al-sufr, kibritiyyah, mulamlamah, masam
- [S743] (definition) The Sakhrah is what the Noble Book names Sijjin, and the wise ones (hukama') modeled the bathhouse (bayt al-hammam) upon its pattern.  ::terms: sijjin, bayt al-hammam, hukama'
- [S744] (doctrine) The fuel (waqid) beneath the Sakhrah is the heat of the sun, which heats it from below as the sun passes beneath the earth.  ::terms: waqid, shams, sakhrah
- [S745] (doctrine) Upon reaching the center, the individuals observe (mu'ayanah) those who preceded them, recognize they deserve the punishment they are in, and confess their negligence and wrongdoing.  ::terms: mu'ayanah, 'iqab, butah
- [S746] (scripture) Quran 21:97-98 ('woe to us, we were in negligence... you and what you worship besides Allah are the fuel of Hell') is applied to the punished opposites and their false objects of worship.  ::terms: hasab jahannam, waridun
- [S747] (scripture) Quran 21:99 ('Had these been gods they would not have entered it') means: had the chiefs they followed been true Imams of truth, they would not have entered the Sakhrah.  ::terms: alihah, a'immat haqq
- [S748] (argument) The Quran's 'khalidina fiha' without 'abadan' for the disbelievers/People of the Book indicates the abiding in Hell is temporary (khulud 'aridi) for purification, whereas 'khalidina fiha abadan' for the righteous indicates eternal abiding (khulud abad) in Paradise.  ::terms: khalidin, abadan, khulud abad, khulud 'aridi
- [S749] (doctrine) When the sun's path goes beneath the earth each night, it heats the Sakhrah like the bottom of a hot pot, igniting the sulfuric, calcareous, and glassy stones so the accursed ones tumble in the boiling middle, crying for help unanswered.  ::terms: sakhrah, kibritiyyah, kalsiyyah, zujajiyyah
- [S750] (doctrine) This Sakhrah/Sijjin punishment is specific to the opposites (azdad) of the nutaqa', awsiya', a'immah and all their hudud — and their hostile claimants, fanatical helpers, doubt-whisperers, and mockers of the people of truth.  ::terms: azdad, nutaqa', awsiya', a'immah, hudud
- [S751] (doctrine) The foolish rabble (huhud and ru'a') who merely followed are turned back from the last perception-level and are not put into the periphery or Sakhrah punishment, because they acted without knowing intent.  ::terms: huhud, ru'a', idrak
- [S752] (doctrine) The pores of the Sakhrah are located beneath the Ka'bah, since by 'ard' (earth) the Ka'bah is always meant; entry into the Sakhrah occurs from beneath the Ka'bah.  ::terms: sakhrah, Ka'bah, masam
- [S753] (doctrine) The earth is both elliptical (baydwi) and flat (musattah): a flat platform for humans (the land of da'wat) whose center on one surface is the Ka'bah and on whose opposite surface is the Sakhrah/Sijjin (the Great Hell), the abode of the Ahl al-Tabut.  ::terms: baydwi, musattah, Ka'bah, sakhrah, sijjin, Ahl al-Tabut
- [S754] (doctrine) The earth has four corners (atraf al-zamin) adjacent to the Sakhrah where the periphery-punishment befalls the opponents, and on which the Sakhrah's vents (manafiz) are located, with cold so severe no bird can fly past.  ::terms: atraf al-zamin, sakhrah, manafiz
- [S755] (claim) Besides the periphery vents, there are many vents in the inhabited quarter, the largest being Durdur, located in the sea between China and India, toward which all rivers flow and from which various sounds emanate.  ::terms: manafiz, Durdur, rub' al-maskun
- [S756] (doctrine) The Ahl al-Sakhrah experience reversed day/night relative to earth's people, with intense heat in their day and intense cold in their night, the punishment increasing a hundredfold during the sun's revolution.  ::terms: Ahl al-Sakhrah, 'adhab
- [S757] (doctrine) God created the Sakhrah before the earth, and in the Quran earth, Sakhrah, and sky are mentioned as three separate individualities in a single ayah.  ::terms: sakhrah, khilqat
- [S758] (doctrine) Around the earth and Sakhrah revolve nine celestial spheres (aflak) with imaginary lines revolving on a plane, and seven planets (kawakib) that are circular and revolve both around and on their axes.  ::terms: aflak, kawakib
- [S759] (doctrine) Beneath the Ka'bah, Maqam Ibrahim, Ghadir, and the Rawda places there is no Sakhrah but a void containing the luminous and good souls (arwah nuraniyyah, arwah hasanah), while at amyal, Wadi al-Shaytan, and the Jamarat al-Thalathah there is a great multitude of filthy souls (arwah khabithah).  ::terms: Ka'bah, Ghadir, arwah nuraniyyah, arwah khabithah, Jamarat
- [S760] (authority) Sayyidna al-Malik states the pores' locations are not in the aflak but are free of physical limits — all spiritual and imaginary, not physical.  ::terms: al-Malik, manafiz, hudud
- [S761] (scripture) Quran 19:71 ('And there is none of you except he will come to it; this is upon your Lord an inevitability decreed') means everyone must arrive in human form on the earth (Sakhrah included), after which progress occurs.  ::terms: wariduha, hatman maqdiyya
- [S762] (doctrine) The order of creation: from the remorseful (nadimin) the aflak were made; the ummahat (fire, air, water, earth-ball) were made; from the insistent (musirrin) the Sakhrah was made; and after 51,000 years of revolution the earth on which we pray was created.  ::terms: nadimin, musirrin, aflak, ummahat, sakhrah
- [S763] (doctrine) The 'alam al-'uqul (World of Intellects) was made in the luminous world; the delayed tenth became the contented soul (nafs al-mutma'innah, per Quran 89:27) which ascends to take the place of the final intellect.  ::terms: 'alam al-'uqul, nafs al-mutma'innah, 'aql
- [S764] (doctrine) Upon the command 'ihbitu' (descend), some immediately repented (forming the World of Life), and the increasingly delayed ones formed the falak al-muhit (imaginary, no materiality, state of nafs), then the falak al-buruj where materiality and near-material light first appear with the fixed stars (thawabit).  ::terms: ihbitu, falak al-muhit, falak al-buruj, thawabit
- [S765] (definition) Thawabit (fixed stars) revolve on their own fixed center, while planets (sayyare/kawakib) revolve around a larger star.  ::terms: thawabit, sayyare, kawakib
- [S766] (doctrine) The spheres of Saturn, Jupiter, Mars, and Sun derive from the nadimin and ta'ibin (repentant), while Venus, Mercury, and Moon are all from the nadimin, the more delayed but repentant elements whose effects can carry residual badness.  ::terms: zuhal, mushtari, marrikh, nadimin, ta'ibin
- [S767] (doctrine) The Sakhrah was made from the most insistent and arrogant (musirrin, mustakbirin), and the most arrogant of all became the station of Sijjin, which contains the Tabut.  ::terms: musirrin, mustakbirin, sijjin, Tabut
- [S768] (scripture) The Prophet said 'Mu'awiyah min al-Tabut' (Mu'awiyah is from the Tabut), placing him in the lowest station (asfal).  ::terms: Mu'awiyah, Tabut, asfal
- [S769] (doctrine) The opposites remain in the belly of the Sakhrah until the end of the Great Cycle (al-kawr al-a'zam), which is 360,000 years multiplied by its like, equaling 129,600 million years.  ::terms: azdad, sakhrah, al-kawr al-a'zam
- [S770] (doctrine) Within that cycle the opposites perpetually curse and kill one another in eternal enmity and warfare, and whoever is killed is transformed into an uglier deformed person (two/three heads, faces turned backward or sideways) burning in the fires.  ::terms: azdad, mushawwah, niran
- [S771] (doctrine) Upon completion of the Great Cycle, the opposites kill one another, are transformed into vapor, ascend, and exit from four vents (manafiz) at the top of the Sakhrah to the earth's surface.  ::terms: sakhrah, bukhar, manafiz
- [S772] (doctrine) The four vents are: a well called Barhut (and Shafahu) in a valley of Hadramawt (east); a well in Khurasan; a well in Arabia; and a fourth in an unnamed land — named in the four directions as Barhut/Shafahu (east), Buqa/Han/Har (west), Saydum/Jub/Kharqa (north), Sabr/Sahiq (south).  ::terms: Barhut, Hadramawt, Khurasan, Buqa, Saydum, Sabr
- [S773] (doctrine) After exiting as vapor, the souls re-enter the world through temperament and compounding (mizaj wal-muntazij), then praiseworthy minerals, plants, and lawful animals, until appearing in the millennial form (qamat al-alifiyyah) as individuals invited again to the truth — a clean-slate choice like the first perfection.  ::terms: mizaj, muntazij, ma'adin al-mahmudah, qamat al-alifiyyah, walayah
- [S774] (doctrine) Three things created by God can never be destroyed but only temporarily hidden: existence (wujud), knowledge/intellect, and free choice (ikhtiyar/freedom); intentions made in the eternal pre-existence ('alam al-azal) are matters of this choice.  ::terms: wujud, ikhtiyar, 'alam al-azal
- [S775] (doctrine) The current Qaim (qaim al-muntadhar) is cyclical and is the first cycle of the 400th cycle; 399 cyclical resurrections (qiyamahs) have already passed and we are at the start of the 400th, in its seventh satr cycle.  ::terms: qaim al-muntadhar, qiyamah, satr
- [S776] (doctrine) The true universal resurrection (qiyamat-e-kubra) is the resurrection of the cycle (qiyamah of the dawr), when 'the earth will be changed to another earth' (Quran 14:48), distinct from the partial cyclical resurrection.  ::terms: qiyamat-e-kubra, qiyamah, dawr
- [S777] (claim) Earthly life cannot be older than four lakh (400,000) years because at each cycle's end everything is destroyed and the earth begins anew; the first jussa of the current cycle manifested at Sarandip.  ::terms: dawr, jussa, Sarandip
- [S778] (doctrine) The 400th jussa will manifest in the Ka'bah (Khana-e Ka'bah) and will gather the 399 prior cycles (jami'-e-arba'); cyclical jussas of the past manifested at holy places like Ghadir Khumm, Rawda, Bayt al-Muqaddas, Najaf, Karbala, Maqam Ibrahim, and Hijr Aswad, which are visited, whereas Sarandip is not counted among the holy places.  ::terms: jussa, Ka'bah, Ghadir Khumm, Bayt al-Muqaddas
- [S779] (doctrine) The greatest jussa is the initial jussa (jussa-e-ibtida'iyyah) at the beginning of a mawr; no other jussa in the whole mawr can reach it, and the final jussa at the world's end may equal it; the true successor of a mawri jussa is the next mawr's jussa (kulli successor), not a cyclical jussa.  ::terms: jussa-e-ibtida'iyyah, mawr, khalifah
