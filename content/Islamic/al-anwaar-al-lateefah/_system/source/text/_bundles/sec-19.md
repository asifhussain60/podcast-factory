# SECTION 19: The Two Paths: Reward, Punishment, and the Opposites

Lecture range: [38, 39, 40]


## LEDGER SPINE TEACHINGS YOU MUST PRESERVE (each at full depth)

- [S617] (doctrine) The fifth chapter applies the statement on the ma'ad (return) to the righteous (sulaha') of the Ahl al-Zahir — those who hold to the outward, follow the Islamic rulings, believe in the Prophet, but in whom love and obedience to the Imam have not fully entered (sulh-e-kul), including the Ahl al-Tashayyu'.  ::terms: ma'ad, sulaha', ahl al-zahir, sulh-e-kul, ahl al-tashayyu'
- [S618] (ruling) The chaste, virtuous, pure man of the Ahl al-Zahir who establishes the pillars of Islam, holds no enmity, rancor, hypocrisy, injustice, falsehood, lying, or betrayal toward the Ahl al-Haqq, and adheres to the wilayah of the Natiq of the era and his Wasi and recognizes their pure progeny — though such people are very few.  ::terms: ahl al-zahir, ahl al-haqq, wilayah, natiq, wasi, multazim
- [S619] (doctrine) Even a small good deed of anyone (believer, hypocrite, or disbeliever) is never wasted by Allah; good (khayr) can never become non-existent (kal-'adam) while evil (sharr) can — anchored in 'Inna al-hasanat yudhhibna al-sayyi'at'.  ::terms: khayr, sharr, hasanat, sayyi'at
- [S620] (doctrine) Without the wilayah of 'Ali one cannot have a good end (khatima bil-khayr) or cross the Pulsirat, yet a good deed still earns its reward and pulls the doer toward the wilayah of the Wali in the end.  ::terms: wilayah, ali, khatima bil-khayr, pulsirat
- [S621] (doctrine) When the righteous Ahl al-Zahir person nears death, the spiritualities of the sun and Venus move him, his soul (nafs) spreads in his body, his heat cools, and his blood freezes — the soul spreading into the body being a sign of inability to cross the Pulsirat.  ::terms: ruhaniyat, shams, venus, nafs, pulsirat
- [S622] (doctrine) The good concept (tasawwur) of such a righteous person — preservation of the Book of Allah, love of the Messenger and his Wasi, mercy, chastity, modesty, and fear of Allah — becomes a luminous form (surat-e-nurani) that ascends with the spiritualities to the moon, then is handed to the sun where it ferments and is purified until Allah permits its return.  ::terms: tasawwur, surat-e-nurani, qamar, shams, ruhaniyat
- [S623] (doctrine) The body of the righteous dissolves bit by bit, ascends as vapor, enters the tempered mixture, falls as rain, becomes pure foods and sweet drinks and fragrant smells, nourishes a human, appears as a sperm-drop, ascends through ranks, and at birth the purified form connects with it along with the sensory soul it deserves.  ::terms: jism, mizaj, nutfah, surat, nafs hissiyah
- [S624] (doctrine) The vegetative soul (nafs namiyah) connects from the sphere of the moon when the sperm-drop forms, and the sensory soul (nafs hissiyah) connects from the sun at birth from the womb.  ::terms: nafs namiyah, nafs hissiyah, qamar, shams
- [S625] (doctrine) Because the scale of justice (mizan al-'adl) is established, the purified concept returns to the righteous person in a state greater, more glorious, and more noble than at death — like a traveler who leaves home, earns wealth in exile, and returns more abundant than before.  ::terms: mizan al-'adl, tasawwur, musafir
- [S626] (doctrine) The concept of the disobedient and rebellious has no return ever to their body and soul at death — like a traveler who leaves family and home without intending to return — which is a type of punishment (per 'according to what his hands have put forth').  ::terms: tasawwur, udat, mizan al-'adl
- [S627] (doctrine) The fourth chapter gathers five sections; after clarifying the transition and progress of the awliya' to the gates of paradise and the fulfillment of the righteous Ahl al-Zahir, the discourse turns to the opposites (azdad) — the people of corruption and what torment they reach.  ::terms: awliya', abwab al-jinaan, sulaha', azdad, ahl al-fasad
- [S628] (doctrine) When death comes to one of the wicked opposites (azdad), the dark celestial jawzahar (placed beneath the sphere of the moon, comprising the two nodes ras and dhanab) moves him, being the magnet for the wicked (khabith) and the vile (khasis).  ::terms: jawzahar, azdad, uqdatan, ras, dhanab, khabith
- [S629] (doctrine) In opposition to the 'amud al-nur (pillar of light, descending from the First Intellect and connected through the kalima la ilaha illallah to every person on the 'ahd-e-sharif), Allah established the dark celestial jawzahar; one is either connected to the 'amud al-nur or pulled to the jawzahar with no space between.  ::terms: amud al-nur, jawzahar, aql-e-awwal, kalima, ahd-e-sharif
- [S630] (doctrine) The jawzahar-e-zulmani is made from the parts (ajza) of people who were elevated but ended in wickedness, placed beneath the moon's sphere (not reaching the rock) as justice from Allah, from where they carry out their ploys (tadabir); the dhanab (tail) is more wicked than the ras (head).  ::terms: jawzahar-e-zulmani, ajza, tadabir, dhanab, ras
- [S631] (definition) True progress (taraqqi) is the body becoming soul, while the soul becoming body is decline (tanazzul); at the wicked one's death his soul and body become a single thing and one part transforms into another.  ::terms: taraqqi, tanazzul, nafs, jism
- [S632] (doctrine) The wicked one separates from his dark destructive sciences, his disbelief about the Ahl al-Haqq, his concealed enmity and hatred, his wicked deeds, and his corrupt beliefs — all of which build a subtle dark wicked form (surat zulmaniyyah khabithah) mirroring the noble luminous form built from interpretive sciences and divine gnoses.  ::terms: surat zulmaniyyah khabithah, ahl al-haqq, ulum ta'wiliyya
- [S633] (definition) That dark soul is called the dark, Iblisi, satanic soul (nafs zulmani iblisiya shaytaniyya) and becomes a fiery incinerating form, just as the luminous form is an angelic speaking soul (nafs malakutiyya natiqa).  ::terms: nafs zulmani, iblisiya, shaytaniyya, nafs malakutiyya natiqa
- [S634] (doctrine) Evil has no root in the primordial creation (al-sharru la asla lahu fi al-ibda'); evil is distance from good as darkness (zulmat) is distance from light (nur), and corporeality/density (kasafat) was created because of us, not by Allah.  ::terms: al-sharru la asla lahu, ibda', zulmat, nur, kasafat
- [S635] (doctrine) A created thing cannot become non-existent (fana); its form (shakl) can change but its existence cannot be annulled — no one but the Originator has the power to turn existence into non-existence.  ::terms: fana, shakl, mubdi'
- [S636] (doctrine) The wicked dark soul-form is buried with the body and becomes an incinerating, tormenting fire for a period of days, so that grave is seen aflame at night, though one who approaches perceives nothing; its owner is the most wicked, most criminal, most hostile to the Ahl al-Haqq, and most oppressive of creation.  ::terms: surat khabithah, qabr, nar, ahl al-haqq
- [S637] (authority) Sayyidna Ikramali Sahib narrated (1255 Hijri) an incident on the journey from Bharuch to Baroda where lightning-fire descended and burned only the clothes in one room while he and his students (Sheikh Ibrahim, Fardali) were safe in another.  ::terms: ikramali, bharuch, baroda, nar
- [S638] (authority) Sayyidna Sheikh Adam Sahib verified that light (nur, distinct from fire nar) descends from the sky onto the grave of Muhammad Arif Sahib in Mumin-bagh, Baroda — his reward for endowing (waqf) his land for the jamatkhana and masjid.  ::terms: sheikh adam, nur, muhammad arif, waqf, jamatkhana
- [S639] (ruling) Sheikh Adam Sahib ruled by nass that whichever portion is designated as a masjid is a masjid (tahiyyatul masjid may be prayed in it) regardless of a residence below it, and the portion not so designated is not.  ::terms: nass, masjid, tahiyyatul masjid, sukna
- [S640] (doctrine) When a person passes away, the soul's/form's attention is directed first to the body's parts, then to the shroud (kafan), then to the grave's soil (which is why his own grave-soil should be used), then to his house and dwelling.  ::terms: tawajjuh, kafan, qabr, surat
- [S641] (doctrine) Once separated from the dense body (which was like a prison/sijn), the wicked dark form takes whatever shape it wishes and does what it wills, since the body's density no longer restrains it, having become the extremity of evil as the luminous form is the extremity of good.  ::terms: surat khabithah, sijn, nihayat al-sharr, nihayat al-khayr
- [S642] (doctrine) By Allah's justice the wicked soul is not prevented from what it deserves of torment, because the scale of justice (mizan al-'adl) is established; from the moment it separates from its body it is in torment in all it does, by its will and without its will.  ::terms: nafs khabithah, mizan al-'adl, adhab, mashiyah
- [S643] (scripture) When the wicked soul leaves its body it seeks ascent to pierce the veils (kharq al-hujub) but the rays of the celestial spheres reflect it back to earth — anchored in 'la tanfudhuna illa bi-sultan' — and it seeks its old body but does not find it, remaining terrified and regretful, roaming the place of the body and grave (called mun'if by the common people).  ::terms: kharq al-hujub, ash'iat al-aflak, la tanfudhuna illa bi-sultan, mun'if
- [S644] (scripture) The wicked soul roams between earth and sky, anchored in the Prophet's saying 'al-hawa' la khala'un wa la mala' (the air is neither empty nor full), and takes refuge in dark places, foul urinals, desolate wildernesses, and filthy slaughterhouses — hence the prayer 'a'udhu billahi min al-rijs al-najis...' upon entering the lavatory.  ::terms: al-hawa' la khala'un wa la mala', mawadi' muzlimah, rijs najis
- [S645] (scripture) The wicked soul appears in the dreams of like-minded evil people (ahl al-nufus al-khabithah), affirming their corrupt beliefs and increasing their enmity to the Ahl al-Haqq and inclination to their opposites, anchored in 'shayatin al-ins wa al-jinn yuhi ba'duhum ila ba'din zukhruf al-qawl ghurura'.  ::terms: nufus khabithah, shayatin al-ins wa al-jinn, zukhruf al-qawl, ahl al-haqq
- [S646] (doctrine) The wicked form mingles with an ignorant man, woman, or boy and speaks through his tongue, sometimes uttering unseen matters or events (not necessarily true, used to deceive); the common people call this the ruh or ruhiyyah, and it can only affect things through using another body.  ::terms: surat khabithah, ruh, ruhiyyah, ghaybiyyat
- [S647] (scripture) The wicked soul disparages the Ahl al-Haqq before its present helpers, affirming their opposition and corrupt beliefs and inclining their hearts to obey it — all from inspiration, whispering, and beguiling (anchored in 'yuwasswisu fi sudur al-nas min al-jinnati wa al-nas') increasing them in devilry and Iblis-like behavior.  ::terms: nafs khabithah, ahl al-haqq, waswasah, yuwasswisu fi sudur al-nas
- [S648] (doctrine) When that wicked man or woman dies, the wicked form leaves and the dark celestial node (the abominable jawzahar/node of the sphere, Rahu and Ketu) pulls it after it completes its reckoning, being the dark isthmus (barzakh) and magnet that pulls the evil souls into its horizon, where it becomes a Satan and gathers with its kind for as long as the Governor wills.  ::terms: surat khabithah, jawzahar, uqdat al-falak, barzakh muzlim, nufus khabithah
