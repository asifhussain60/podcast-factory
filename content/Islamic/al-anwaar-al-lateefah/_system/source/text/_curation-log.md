## Augmentation disposition

Per the strict curated-merge rule, the default is NOT to merge. An augmentation passage
is merged inline only where it directly extends a *ledgered spine teaching* that the spine
itself leaves open. On review, every augmentation span is a parallel exposition of the same
subject by another teacher rather than a gap-filling extension of a specific spine teaching;
merging any of them inline would let augmentation reshape the backbone or restate doctrine
the spine already carries at full depth. All augmentation spans are therefore classified
`atom` — reserved for the later enrichment-atom step, where they are surfaced as supporting
material keyed to the relevant teaching without altering the spine's flow. None were merged
inline; none were dropped.
