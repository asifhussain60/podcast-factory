# A Shield Against the Fire

I turn now to the Book of Fasting, and to the mention of the obligation of fasting the month of Ramadan.

He stated in the book Mukhtasar al-Musannaf: fasting is a shield from the Fire, a shield upon the tongues, upon the hearing, upon the eyes, and upon all the other limbs. It is not a matter of food and drink alone; rather, every limb has its portion in it, and whoever renders that portion is truly fasting. Whoever abandons anything of it diminishes the merit of his fast in proportion to what he has left. Fasting the month of Ramadan is an obligation upon every Muslim who is of age, sound of mind, healthy and resident, and upon every woman likewise. He also stated therein: there is no fasting for one who is a hypocrite, disobedient to God.

He stated in the book al-Idah: what has come concerning fasting upon the sighting of the crescent, and what is mentioned in this chapter and in the two chapters before it, pertains only to one who is absent from the presence of the Imam and does not know the measures of the crescents.

Our master and Mawlana Ja'far ibn Sayyidina Sulayman, may God exalt his sanctity, stated in his noble epistle entitled The Sharpest Sword in Nullifying Innovations, with which he counselled his people and roused those among them who had grown heedless and whose sleep had lengthened, from the people of the island of Yemen, while he dwelt there and settled among them:

"Chapter: Among these matters also is what has been heard and known from most of you, that the honoured month of Ramadan is not honoured with the honour that God, glorified be He, and His Messenger (ع) have prescribed for it, for it is the month of God, the Great, the Generous. That honour lies in placing the word 'month' before it, as God, glorified be He, said concerning it:

> شهر رمضان

The month of Ramadan.

Yet you, when you name it, call it 'Ramadan' alone, and that is never seemly in the judgement of the intellect, nor does it guide in this affair toward right guidance, because a person does not address one who is dear to him among people except with a word he places before his name, according to what is known among you and whose glory and form are recognized, together with what is established by the aforementioned word of God, glorified be He:

> وإلى الله ترجع الأمور

And to God all matters return.

Beyond this, it has come concerning it from our master al-Qadi al-Nu'man ibn Muhammad, may God exalt his sanctity, in the book Mukhtasar al-Athar al-Sharif, that your Mawla the Imam al-Baqir Abu Ja'far Muhammad ibn Ali (ع) said:

> لا تقولون رمضان ولكن قولوا كما قال الله تعالى شهر رمضان

Do not say Ramadan, but say as God Almighty said, the month of Ramadan.

So whoever has had God, glorified be He, cause his ear to hear this saying, how can it be permissible for him to oppose it and turn away from it, being of the people of piety and faith? The heedless one who turns away from it after having come to stand upon it makes light of God, glorified be He, and of His noble month mentioned above, and of the weighty saying of the Imam al-Baqir (ع) mentioned above. And the one who speaks by this noble book while turning away from acting upon what is in it enters under the censure contained in the words of God, glorified be He, in the verses of His noble and guarded Book, where He, glorified be He, says:

> ⟪ar:يَا أَيُّهَا الَّذِينَ آمَنُوا لِمَ تَقُولُونَ مَا لَا تَفْعَلُونَ⟫
>
> ⟪ar:كَبُرَ مَقْتًا عِندَ اللَّهِ أَن تَقُولُوا مَا لَا تَفْعَلُونَ⟫

O you who believe, why do you say what you do not do? It is greatly hateful in the sight of God that you say what you do not do."

He stated in the book Ta'wil al-Shari'ah: fasting is made obligatory upon the boy once he knows his right hand from his left.

He stated in the fourteenth majlis of the Mustansiriyya councils, God's peace be upon its author: what follows is what we recite to you concerning the meaning of fasting, and that it is divided into categories, of which seven are obligatory and twelve are of the practice and commended. The obligatory ones are:

1. Fasting the month of Ramadan every year.
2. Fasting two consecutive months for one who deliberately breaks his fast on a day of it and cannot find a slave to free.
3. Fasting two consecutive months for one who kills a believer by mistake, if he likewise cannot find a slave to free, together with paying the blood money to the family.
4. In the expiation of zihar, for one who cannot find a slave to free, fasting two consecutive months.
5. In the expiation for breaking an oath, for one who can find neither the feeding nor the clothing nor the freeing of a slave, fasting three days.
6. The fast of one who performs tamattu' with the lesser pilgrimage up to the pilgrimage, when he cannot find an offering, and it is ten days.
7. The fast of a vow.

As for the fasts of the practice and the commended:

1. The fast of i'tikaf.
2. The fast of Sha'ban.
3. The fast of the first Thursday of every month.
4. The fast of the middle Wednesday of it.
5. The fast of the last Thursday of it.
6. The fast of the three white days.
7. The fast of Dawud (ع), which is fasting one day and breaking the fast the next, and it is the most rigorous of fasts.
8. The fast of Friday joined to Thursday or to Saturday, without singling out Friday alone for fasting.
9. The fast of the day of Arafah for one who is not on pilgrimage.
10. The fast of the boy when he reaches seven years.
11. The fast of recompense for game taken in the sacred state, which varies according to what is ruled by the one to whom God has entrusted the ruling in it.
12. The fast for shaving the head on account of harm.

Our master Zaid ibn Muhammad, may God sanctify his soul, stated in the Concealed Epistle: God Almighty imposed upon His servants, after the performance of prayer and the giving of zakat, the fasting of the month of Ramadan, and He commanded that one month out of twelve be fasted. He stated therein: and just as the fasting of the month of Ramadan is obligatory, it is thirty days.

Mawlana Amir al-Mu'minin Ali ibn Abi Talib (ع) stated in his Four Hundred Manners:

> صوم ثلاثة أيام من كل شهر وصوم شعبان يذهب بوسواس الصدر وبلابل القلب

Fasting three days of every month and fasting Sha'ban drive away the whisperings of the breast and the disquiet of the heart.
