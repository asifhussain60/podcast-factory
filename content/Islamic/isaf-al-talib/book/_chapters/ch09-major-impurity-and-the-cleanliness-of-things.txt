# Major Impurity and the Cleanliness of Things

### Mentioning the Ghusl from Janabah

I take the questions and answers that follow from the book of question and answer mentioned earlier, which one of its compilers arranged according to the chapters of the book *Da'a'im al-Islam*.

Question: What do you say, O best of those whose praise and thanks our tongues fall short of, concerning what has come in certain books of the Shari'ah: that when a man fears the cold and a ghusl is due upon him, he performs tayammum and prays, and then, when he is able to use water, he washes and repeats the prayer? Is this ruling the same for the resident and the traveler, or not?

Answer: If he fears that the water will harm him, whether he is a resident or a traveler, he performs tayammum and prays, and there is nothing further upon him.

Question: What do you say, O one to whose excellence the far and the near bear witness, concerning the ghusl from janabah (major ritual impurity), since marital relations are a likeness of imparting and receiving? Explain, then, the likeness of the washing, according to what the one who washes is obliged to call to mind while he washes, for the exposition of this in the books of the guiding Da'wah is very long.

Answer: The washing is a likeness of the removal of doubt, and doubt arises only at the time of imparting and receiving.

Question: What do you say, O one whom God Most High has made a rope extended from heaven to this world for the people of the earth who remember God while sitting and while standing, concerning what has come in the Shari'ah: that one who has fallen unconscious, when he revives after some days and does not know whether he may have become in a state of janabah, must perform the ghusl? O our master, what is the measure of this if he sees no trace of semen?

Answer: If he does not know his own condition, the ghusl becomes obligatory upon him once doubt has entered him concerning it.

Question: What does our master say, may God inspire him with the welfare of the Da'wah, concerning a weak man who has been overtaken by janabah and who fears the cold if he washes, when the time of prayer has come? May he perform tayammum as the sick man does, or not?

Answer: If he fears the water, he performs tayammum and prays, and he does not repeat the prayer.

Question: What does our master say, may God perpetuate his support, concerning a man who wakes from sleep and sees on his garment the trace of semen, wet or dry, though he did not see in his sleep that he had emitted? Is the ghusl obligatory upon him or not?

Answer: The ghusl is obligatory upon him.

Question: What do you say, O fulfiller of the needs of those who ask, concerning what is mentioned in the Shari'ah, that it is disliked for the one in janabah and for the menstruating woman to remove from themselves any hair or nail, and that the woman in post-natal bleeding is in the state of the menstruating woman? O our master, is this permitted to them along with the dislike, or is it not permitted at all?

Answer: To leave it is better, until such a one is purified.

Question: What do you say, O one whom I approached from where I came out of nothing but pure love, concerning the one in janabah and the menstruating woman who read supplications or other books in which there is something of the Quran, whether the reader recognizes it or does not recognize it? How is such a one to act, reading it or leaving it, when it has come in the radiant Shari'ah that the two do not recite Quran and do not touch it? And may the one in janabah and the menstruating woman recite the declaration of God's oneness, the magnification, the praise, and the glorification, all of which are in the Quran? And may such a one recite the *basmalah*, when it has been said that it is a verse of the Quran?

Answer: Neither the menstruating woman nor the one in janabah may recite the Quran, nor any part of it in a supplication or in books, deliberately; and neither may recite the *basmalah* with the intention of reciting the Quran.

In the book *Mukhtasar al-Musannaf* it is said that whoever forgets the ablution until he has performed the ghusl performs the ablution, and there is nothing against him. It is also said there: when a man has performed the ablution and then begins the ghusl, and a minor impurity occurs before he has completed his washing, he continues on with the ghusl and performs the ablution after it for the prayer.

Among the answers of certain summoners of Yemen to certain dignitaries of the Indian lands (رض): if one in janabah and a deceased person are brought together and there is not found of water except what suffices for one of them, the one in janabah performs the ghusl and tayammum is performed for the deceased.

The author of *al-Risala al-Maknuna* said: God has imposed the ghusl from janabah, for He said, exalted be He who speaks:

> وان كنتم جنباً فاطهروا

"And if you are in a state of major impurity, then purify yourselves."

And the Prophet said:

> تحت كل شعر جنابة فبلوا الشعر وانقوا البشر

"Beneath every hair there is major impurity, so wet the hair and cleanse the skin."

Then he said: whoever does not do that is *junub*, and *al-junub* is the one far from a thing. It is said, "he is a stranger to me," that is, far from my lineage. And obedience to the one in janabah is void and false.

The Commander of the Faithful, our master Ali ibn Abi Talib (ع), said in his Four Hundred Manners:

> لا ينام المسلم وهو جنب ولا ينام الا وهو على طهور فان لم يجد الماء فليتيمم بالصعيد فان روح المؤمن ترتفع إلى الله عزّ وجل فيقبلها ويبارك عليها فان كان قد حضر أجلها جعلها في أحسن صورة وان لم يحضر أجلها بعث بها مع أمناءه من الملائكة فردّوها إلى جسده

"Let not the Muslim sleep while he is in major impurity, and let him not sleep except in a state of purity. If he finds no water, let him perform tayammum with clean earth, for the spirit of the believer rises up to God, mighty and majestic, and He receives it and blesses it. If its appointed term has come, He sets it in the fairest form; and if its term has not come, He sends it back with His trusted ones among the angels, and they return it to its body."

### Mentioning the Purity of Bodies, Garments, Ground, Carpets, and What Belongs to That

I take these also from the book of question and answer mentioned earlier, arranged according to the chapters of *Da'a'im al-Islam*.

Question: What do you say, O best of those who have commanded us to endure with patience the decree of God and His judgment, concerning vomit? Does it render impure what it falls upon, as blood and pus and the other impurities do, or not?

Answer: Vomit does not render impure what it falls upon, though it is better that the place be washed.

Question: What do you say, O one whom God Most High has made a provision for the traveler who wishes to arrive at God Most High, concerning ground on which children urinate and relieve themselves, which then dries so that the smell of the impurity and its substance are gone from it? Is prayer upon it permissible without a mat, and is tayammum from it permissible, or not?

Answer: If the substance of the impurity has gone, its ruling is the ruling of pure ground, provided it is not ground set aside as a privy for need.

Question: What do you say, O one whom God Most High has made a measure by which the corrupt is tested against the pure in the religion of God, concerning what has come in the Shari'ah: that when impurity strikes the ground and water then runs over it and it dries, and the smell of the impurity and its substance are gone from it, prayer upon it and tayammum from it are permitted? And it has also come that there is no harm in praying in the dry places among the resting grounds of camels and the folds of sheep. O our master, what is the difference between this and that?

Answer: The sun and the light purify it. So when the substance of the impurity and its smell have gone from it, prayer upon it and tayammum from it are permitted.

Question: What does our master say, may God make lasting the throne of his governance, concerning the meaning of the words of the Commander of the Faithful:

> من مسّ جسد ميّت بعد أن يبرد لزمه الغسل

"Whoever touches the body of a dead man after it has grown cold must perform the ghusl."

What is the meaning of his words, "after it has grown cold"?

Answer: Its meaning is: after it has become impure. So when it touches the living with its moisture, he becomes impure, and this is the meaning of the ghusl becoming binding, that it be decreed upon him. And the washing performed by the one who washes the corpse is from the Sunnah.

Question: Concerning a thing that a man wishes to purify but is not able to rub or to wring out three times, such as a fur, a mat, a rug, the quilts used in the mosque, and bedding: what is the manner of purifying it?

Answer: A thing that a man wishes to purify but is not able to rub or to wring out three times, he passes his hand over it until the impurity and its smell are gone, then lifts it from the water and leaves it until what is in it has dripped out, then repeats this three times. As for what is other than this, he treads it with his feet in the manner described, then takes it out of the water and presses upon it until the water has come out of it, then repeats this three times.

Question: Concerning a woman's milk that falls upon the garment of another, is it pure or not?

Answer: From the Walid Izz al-Din Ma'ad ibn Abdullah ibn Ali: the milk of a nursing woman, if she is a Muslim, is pure, both for her and for others. And I say that this is an established position, since her milk is like her saliva, and the saliva of every Muslim is pure and does not render impure what it touches.

Question: Concerning a man who shaves his head, and something of his hair falls while it is wet upon his garment or upon his body: does that hair render impure what it touches or not?

Answer: If that is a small amount, then a small amount of impurity has been excused.

Question: Concerning the animals that feed upon filth, of cattle and camels and sheep, and the milk and clarified butter of these and the white of the eggs of hens, when they fall upon the garments of those praying or upon their bodies: does this render them impure or not?

Answer: That follows what was mentioned in the first case: a small amount of it is excused.

Question: Concerning the traveler and the sick man, whose garments are touched by impurity and who are not able to cleanse them, is the prayer complete for them with tayammum, or must it be made up afterward?

Answer: The prayer is not complete except with a pure garment. Tayammum has been mentioned for ritual impurity, and it has not been mentioned for the impurity of garments. As for the sick man, he instructs someone to wash his garments for him.

Question: The polytheist worships idols and knows neither the lawful nor the forbidden, and he weaves the cloth, and after that he bleaches it, and after that he sews it. Is his work pure, and is prayer in it permitted or not? For God Most High has said:

> إنما المشركون نجس

"The polytheists are nothing but impurity."

Answer: All such garments are pure until he wears them; and if he has worn them, then they are impure, or if impurity has appeared in them. As he said in the book *al-Da'a'im*:

> ورخصوا الأئمة صلوات الله عليهم في الصلاة في الثياب التي يعملها المشركين ما لم يلبسوها أو تظهر فيها النجاسة

"The Imams gave dispensation for praying in the garments that the polytheists make, so long as they have not worn them or impurity has not appeared in them."

And he said also: likewise the dyed garment, when the dyer is a polytheist, is pure until he wears it; and if he has worn it, it is impure.

Question: What does our master say, may God perpetuate his support, concerning the bleaching done by polytheists to what they bleach: does it carry purity or not? Be so good as to make this clear.

Answer: I say, and upon the granting of success by God I rely, and from the blessings of His friends I draw support, that there is no way to the purity of the garments of polytheists except those they have made before wearing them. As for what they have bleached and washed with soap, its purity is not allowed except by washing. Perhaps those who heard the saying about this did not grasp the root of the question in it.

Question: What does our master say, may God perpetuate his support, concerning the garments of polytheists that they have made and have not worn, which they sprinkle with impure water at the time of the weaving and the spinning, after which the water dries and the garments are sold, and they have neither worn them nor washed them with soap? Is prayer in them permitted or not?

Answer: There is no harm in praying in them, and the radiant Shari'ah has permitted them.

Question: What do you say, O one whose people of response have come to take pride in the praiseworthy account of his conduct, concerning the wide, long garment and the bedding of which one part is pure and another part impure? Is prayer performed upon the part of it that is pure? For the common people say that if the garment is of such a size that when one end of it is moved the other end does not move, prayer is permitted upon the part of it that is pure.

Answer: One does not pray upon it, and prayer upon it is not permitted.

In the book *Mukhtasar al-Musannaf* it is said that prayer in the garments of children who guard themselves against impurity is permitted so long as no impurity is known in them, and dispensation has been given for a slight sprinkling. It is said there also: as for the houses out in the open country, praying in them has not been forbidden, though it is not recommended.

I turn now to the book of question and answer whose questions are from the most excellent Sheikh Yusuf ibn Ismail ibn Ibrahim al-Samti al-Hindi and whose answers are from our master Idris ibn al-Hasan.

Question: Is prayer permitted upon stone and upon plaster? Favor us with a sufficient and clear answer.

Answer: The Prophet said:

> جُعلت لي الأرض مسجدا وطهورا

"The earth has been made for me a place of prostration and a means of purification."

And everything of the ground from which tayammum is not permitted is not permitted to be prayed upon.

Question: What do you say concerning the sweeping of houses? For most of the people of India hold that houses are not to be swept at night at all, and they make a scruple of it, and likewise they hold that shops and stalls are not to be swept at night. How is this, may God make us your ransom?

Answer: This is not to be relied upon, and there is no blame upon one who does it.

In the book *Ta'wil al-Shari'ah* it is said that the Prophet said:

> إذا ولغ الكلب في إناء أحدكم فليغسله سبع مرات إحداهن بالتراب

"When a dog laps in the vessel of one of you, let him wash it seven times, one of them with earth."

And in *Mukhtasar al-Musannaf* it is said: when a vessel becomes impure from the lapping of a dog, it is washed seven times, the first of them with earth, and the pig is like it. It is said there also: when impurity falls into one of two vessels and he does not know which of them it is, he does not approach them; he performs tayammum and prays.

The Commander of the Faithful Ali ibn Abi Talib (ع) said in his Four Hundred Manners:

> أخذ الشارب من النظافة وهو من السنّة

"Trimming the mustache is part of cleanliness, and it is of the Sunnah."

> غسل الرأس بالحطمي يذهب بالدرن وهو من السنّة وينفي الأقذار

"Washing the head with mallow takes away grime, and it is of the Sunnah, and it drives off filth."

> النورة مشدّة للبدن وطهور للجسد

"The depilatory paste strengthens the body and is a purification for the flesh."

> تقليم الأظافر يمنع الداء الأعظم ويجلب الرزق ويدرّه

"Trimming the nails wards off the greatest disease, and draws sustenance and makes it flow."

> نتف الآباط ينفي الرائحة المنكرة وهو طهور وهو من السنّة

"Plucking the armpits drives off the offensive odor; it is a purification, and it is of the Sunnah."

> غسل الأعياد طهور لمن طلب الحوائج بين يدي الله عز وجل وأتباع السنّة

"The washing on the feast days is a purification for whoever seeks his needs before God, mighty and majestic, and a following of the Sunnah."

> غسل الثياب يذهب بالهم ويكبت العدو وهو طهور للصلاة

"Washing the garments takes away anxiety and subdues the enemy, and it is a purification for the prayer."

> لا تنتفوا الشنب فانه نورٍ للمسلم

"Do not pluck out the mustache, for it is a light for the Muslim."

> من شاب في رأسه شيبة في الإسلام كانت له نورا يوم القيامة

"Whoever grows a white hair upon his head while in Islam, it will be a light for him on the Day of Resurrection."

> ولا يتغوّطنّ أحدكم على الحجة ولا يبل في سطح الهواء ولا في ماء راكد فمن فعل فأصابه شيء فلا يلومنّ الا نفسه فان للماء أهلاً وللهواء أهلاً فإذا بال أحدكم فلا يطمحنّ ببوله ولا يستقبل به الربح

"Let none of you relieve himself upon the road, and let him not urinate into the open air nor into standing water. Whoever does so and something befalls him, let him blame none but himself, for the water has its folk and the air has its folk. So when one of you urinates, let him not make his urine splash, and let him not face the wind with it."

> ولا ينا منّ أحدكم على الحجة ولا على وجهه فمن رأيتموه نام على وجهه فانبهوه

"And let none of you sleep upon the road, nor upon his face. Whomever you see sleeping upon his face, wake him."

> تنظفوا بالماء من الريح المنتنة وتعاهدوا أنفسكم فان الله تعالى يبغض العبد القاذورة الذي یتأذى به من جلس إليه

"Cleanse yourselves with water from foul odor, and attend regularly to yourselves, for God Most High hates the filthy servant, by whom whoever sits with him is offended."

> تنزهوا عن قرب الكلاب فمن أصابه كلب جاف فلينضح ثوبه بالماء وان كان الكلب رطباً فليغسله

"Keep yourselves far from the nearness of dogs. Whomever a dry dog touches, let him sprinkle his garment with water; and if the dog was wet, let him wash it."

> احبّ للمؤمن أن يطلي في كل خمسة عشر يوما مرة بنورة

"I love for the believer that he anoint himself once every fifteen days with the depilatory paste."

I take what follows from the book of question and answer whose questions are from the most excellent Sheikh Shimon ibn Muhammad Ghori al-Hindi (رض) and whose answers are from our master Badr al-Din, our master Hasan ibn Idris.

Question: What do you say, O one by whom our souls have life, concerning a man who lets the hair of his head grow and does not comb it or part it each day? Does a defect enter upon him in his prayer, or not? And how often ought he to comb it and part it?

Answer: If it is in two halves, there is no defect upon him; and it is not fitting for him to leave it without a parting. If he does that deliberately against the ruling, his prayer is void.

Question: What do you say concerning the meaning of the words of Ali (ع):

> من لا يطهره البحر فلا طهّره الله

"Whomever the sea does not purify, may God not purify him."

Answer: Water purifies and is not itself purified. So likewise is the knowledge of the Imam: whomever it does not purify, God does not purify him.
