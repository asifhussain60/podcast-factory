# The Disputed Words of the Call

I turn now to the mention of the adhan and the iqama.

In the book of question and answer named at the outset, which one of its compilers arranged according to the order of the chapters of the book Da'a'im al-Islam, this is recorded.

The question: What do you say concerning a man of the people of the call whom a sultan of tyranny, or his agents, appoints to a post under the name of leading the prayer in the mosque, or serving as its muezzin, or teaching, or some other post, paid out of the proceeds of the port levy or of the jizya, while the people know that the holder of the port levy takes from the wealth of the people what he sees fit and not what is due? Is he permitted to take the post or not?

The answer: This is from the public treasury (bayt al-mal). If it is according to the view of one appointed by the friends of God, it is lawful for him to consume it; and if not, it is not lawful for him to consume it.

From Mukhtasar al-Idah, on the authority of Ja'far ibn Muhammad ibn Ali (ع), concerning the man who gives the call to prayer while he is walking, or upon the back of his mount, he said:

> نعم إذا كان وقت التشهد مستقبل القبلة فلا بأس

Yes; if at the time of the testimony he is facing the direction of prayer, there is no harm in it.

And they are agreed, so far as I have learned and so far as I have seen in what I have gathered from the books attributed to the Ahl al-Bayt (عليهم السلام), that the iqama is not valid except standing upon the ground.

From the book Mukhtasar al-Musannaf: it behoves the muezzin, when he says twice

> اشهد أن محمداً رسول الله

I bear witness that Muhammad is the Messenger of God,

that he say within himself

> اشهد أن علياً ولي الله

I bear witness that Ali is the friend of God.

And when he says twice

> حي على خير العمل

Come to the best of deeds,

that he say within himself

> آل محمد خير البشر

The family of Muhammad are the best of humankind.

It is said in that same book that it does not behove one who is in the mosque, when the muezzin gives the call, to go out of it until he has prayed, unless he goes out for a need and intends to return, knowing that he will still reach the prayer; or unless he is without ablution, so he goes out to perform the ablution and strives to return in time to reach the prayer. If he does not reach it, he prays alone, or with another congregation in another mosque, and there is nothing upon him.

It is said in the book Ta'wil al-Shari'a that it is permissible for the muezzin to give the call while he is not in a state of purity, but the iqama is not valid except from one who is pure. It is said in it: he faces the direction of prayer and puts the intention first. It is said in it: the obligatory prayer is not prayed when the call has been given, and the obligatory prayer is prayed when the iqama has been given. It is said in it: the supererogatory prayer is joined to the adhan and the obligatory prayer to the iqama, and the supererogatory comes first. And it is said in it that the Prophet (ع) said:

> من أحرم فقد دخل جنّة أهل الحق

Whoever enters the sacred state of the prayer has entered the garden of the people of truth.

Our sayyid and mawla Idris ibn Sayyidina Hasan said in his book known as Uyun al-Akhbar, in the sixth part of it, in the matter of the call to prayer with the testimony that our mawla Ali is the friend of God, and that Muhammad and Ali are the best of humankind and their kindred the best of kindred, and this in his account of our mawla the Commander of the Faithful al-Hakim bi-Amr Allah (ع) and his pure forefathers, these being his words:

The muezzins gave the call in the time of the caliphate of the Commander of the Faithful al-Hakim bi-Amr Allah (ع), and they proclaimed in their call, after the testimony that Muhammad is the Messenger of God,

> أشهد أن مولانا عليا ولي الله

I bear witness that our mawla Ali is the friend of God,

in confirmation of the proclamation of the Prophet (ع) on the Day of Ghadir:

> من كنت مولاه فعلي مولاه

Whoever's master I am, Ali is his master.

That was a declaration of loyalty to the Messenger and of the guardianship of his legatee, upon whom he had expressly conferred it and to whom he had pointed. Then they proclaimed in the call:

> محمد وعلي خير البشر وعترتهما خير العتر

Muhammad and Ali are the best of humankind, and their kindred are the best of kindred.

This was a making plain of the excellence of the Messenger of God, in whose glory they took pride, and who was raised from majesty to the highest of his rank; and there was none nearer to him than his brother and legatee Ali ibn Abi Talib, and his daughter Fatima al-Zahra, and his two grandsons al-Hasan and al-Husayn, and the pure Imams whom He chose from the progeny of His Messenger and made guides for His creation to the open road of His path. Abdullah ibn Abbas was asked who the near kindred are, and he said: Ali, and Fatima, and al-Hasan, and al-Husayn. Abdullah ibn Abbas, for all his excellence and his learning and his kinship, made no claim of it for any other. The kindred of a man are his nearest folk; yet they relate from Abu Bakr ibn Abi Quhafa that he said:

> نحن عترة رسولِ الله

We are the kindred of the Messenger of God.

By my life, Abu Bakr had no kinship with the Messenger (ع) except that he was of Quraysh, and that is remote when the lineage is traced. Ali and Fatima and al-Hasan and al-Husayn (عليهم السلام) are worthier and nearer than every one who kept his company and drew close. Al-Kumayt said:

> بني هاشم رهط النبي محمد
> ولهم وبهم أرضى مراراً وأغضب

The sons of Hashim are the clan of the Prophet Muhammad; for them and through them I am pleased time and again, and for them I am angered.

This shows that they are his kin. We have already mentioned the near kindred and what has come from Ibn Abbas (رض) in that exposition.

Many of the common people have denied that Ali is the best of humankind. They have said: we grant that Muhammad is the best of humankind, but we do not grant that of Ali, because among humankind there are prophets. This is a saying whose right sense they have not known and whose grounds they have not understood; a saying of obstinacy, an argument feeble, refuted, and turning back upon itself. All the Muslims are agreed that the community of Muhammad is the best of communities, for the word of God Most High:

> كنتم خير أمة أخرجت للناس

You were the best community brought forth for the people.

And among the communities before them were prophets sent as messengers and friends of God given precedence. If they say that this is by way of figurative speech and magnification, we say as they say, and every transgressing sinner is brought to loss. The Prophet (ع) said, in the sound narration from him on the authority of Hudhayfa ibn al-Yaman and others:

> أني مخلف فيكم ما أن تمسكتم به لن تضلوا كتاب الله وعترتي أهل بيتي كسفينة نوح من ركبها نجى ومن تخلف عنها غرق

I leave behind among you that which, if you hold fast to it, you will never go astray: the Book of God and my kindred, the people of my house. They are like the ark of Noah: whoever boards it is saved, and whoever holds back from it is drowned.

And he (ع) said:

> علي خير البشر من أبى ذلك فقد كفر

Ali is the best of humankind; whoever refuses this has disbelieved.

This is guide enough for one for whom the way of truth has been made a way. As for the community which is the best community brought forth for the people, its explanation has come from the Imams (عليهم السلام), and we have mentioned that in this book.

In the narration with its chain from Ali ibn al-Husayn al-Qari at the inhabited palace in Cairo, he said: I was with the Imam al-Hakim bi-Amr Allah, the Commander of the Faithful (ع), at al-Gharafa, by a mosque known as that of al-Quda'i, and he was standing, when he heard a great outcry and tumult. He said to one of his attendants: Go and see what this shouting and tumult is, and tell me of it. The young man went and came back and said: O our mawla, they are the people of Atfih, and they are split into two parties. He (ع) said: Bring them to me. The young man went and brought them, and among them was a black man clinging to a man and pressing him hard. He (ع) said to him: Release the man. So he released him. The Commander of the Faithful said to the black man: Who are you? He said: I am the preacher at Atfih. He said to him: And who is the other man? He said: He is the da'i. He said: What has passed between you? He said: O our mawla, there has appeared in our town what we never heard at all. He said: And what is it? He said: He gave the call, and he said in his call that Muhammad and Ali are the best of humankind and their kindred the best of kindred. We never heard this call before this. It may be allowed that Muhammad (ع) is the best of humankind, but it is not allowed that Ali be the best of humankind, because among humankind are Adam and Noah and Abraham and Moses and Jesus, and it is not allowed that he be better than these prophets, the possessors of the revealed laws.

Our mawla (ع) said: I shall make plain to you, O shaykh, that Ali (ع) is the best of humankind together with the Prophet (ع). Have you read anything of the hadith, O shaykh? He said: Yes, O Commander of the Faithful, a great deal. He said: This is a report I bring to you; if it is sound, then confirm it, and if it is false, then refute it, so that I may bring you another. He said: Yes, O our mawla (ع).

He said: Ibn Khallad related to us from Sajja ibn Suwayd, relating it from al-Ma'mun al-Abbasi, from his father al-Rashid, from his father al-Mahdi, from his father al-Mansur, from Abdullah ibn Abbas, who said: We were gathered one day with the Messenger of God (ع), and he was speaking to us and teaching us the understanding of our religion, when his daughter Fatima al-Zahra (عليهم السلام) came to him with her eyes pouring out tears. The Prophet sprang up and said: O my daughter, what makes you weep? She said: O my father, I have lost my two sons al-Hasan and al-Husayn, and I feared that my heart would split for the loss of them, O my father. He said: The Prophet raised his gaze to the heavens and grew tender and prayed, and then Gabriel the Trustworthy descended upon him and said: O Muhammad, peace be upon you, do not be alarmed. The Peace sends you peace, and He says to you: your two sons al-Hasan and al-Husayn went off to the enclosure of the Banu al-Najjar and played there, and sleep overcame them, so He sent to them an angel of the angels, kindly and gentle, who spread his right wing beneath them and covered them with his left. So he found them and took charge of them.

The Messenger of God (ع) set out, and his companions with him, to the enclosure of the Banu al-Najjar, and he saw al-Hasan and al-Husayn (عليهم السلام) sleeping, with the angel seated, guarding them by the command of God, mighty and majestic. The Prophet lifted them onto his shoulder, and Abu Bakr said: Let me carry them for you, O Messenger of God. He said:

> ذرهما فنعم المطي مطيهما ونعم الراكبان هما وأبوهما خير منهما

Leave them be, for excellent is their mount, and excellent riders are the two of them, and their father is better than the two of them.

So the Prophet carried them upon his shoulders and began to hasten in his walking, that he might bring the good news to Fatima (عليهم السلام), until he reached the mosque and came upon a company of people who had been alarmed at that. He said:

> أيها الناس أني أخبركم بخير الناس أباً وأماً هما هذان الحسن والحسين أبوهما عليّ وصيي أفضل الوصيين وأمهما فاطمة ابنتي أفضل نساء العالمين ألا أخبركم بخير الناس جداً وجدة هذان الحسن والحسين جدهما رسول الله وجدتهما خديجة أول من آمن بالله ورسوله ألا أخبركم بخير الناس عماً وعمة

O people, I tell you of the best of people in father and mother: they are these two, al-Hasan and al-Husayn. Their father is Ali, my legatee, the most excellent of legatees, and their mother is Fatima, my daughter, the most excellent of the women of the worlds. Shall I tell you of the best of people in grandfather and grandmother? These two, al-Hasan and al-Husayn. Their grandfather is the Messenger of God, and their grandmother is Khadija, the first to believe in God and His Messenger. Shall I tell you of the best of people in paternal uncle and paternal aunt?

They said: Yes, O Messenger of God. He said:

> هذان الحسن والحسين عمّهما جعفر بن أبي طالب وعمّتهما أم هاني بنت أبي طالب ما أشركت بالله طرفة عين ألا أخبركم بخير الناس خالاً وخالة هذان الحسن والحسين خالهما قاسم بن رسول الله وخالتهما زينب بنت رسول الله اللهم أنك تعلم انهما وأباهما وأمهما وجدهما وجدتهما وخالهما وخالتهما وعمهما وعمتهما وشيعتهما ومحبيهما في الجنة

These two, al-Hasan and al-Husayn: their paternal uncle is Ja'far ibn Abi Talib, and their paternal aunt is Umm Hani bint Abi Talib, who never associated anything with God for the blink of an eye. Shall I tell you of the best of people in maternal uncle and maternal aunt? These two, al-Hasan and al-Husayn. Their maternal uncle is Qasim the son of the Messenger of God, and their maternal aunt is Zaynab the daughter of the Messenger of God. O God, You know that these two, and their father, and their mother, and their grandfather, and their grandmother, and their maternal uncle, and their maternal aunt, and their paternal uncle, and their paternal aunt, and their Shi'a, and those who love them, are in the Garden.

Is this hadith sound? He said: Yes, by God, O Commander of the Faithful, and it is indeed among the choicest of reports. The Commander of the Faithful said: And in the hadith the Messenger of God (ع) said, before a company of his companions:

> ولداي هذان إماما حق قاما أو قعدا وأبوهما خير منهما

These two sons of mine are two rightful Imams, whether they rise or sit, and their father is better than the two of them.

He said: Yes. And the Prophet (ع) said:

> ولداي هذان سيدا شباب أهل الجنة

These two sons of mine are the two masters of the youth of the people of the Garden.

Is the report sound, O shaykh, or not? He said: It is sound, O Commander of the Faithful. He said: Is it not in the report that the Messenger of God (ع) said:

> إن أهل الجنة شباب لا يهرمون

The people of the Garden are youths who do not grow old.

He said: Yes. He said: Is that sound? He said: Yes, O Commander of the Faithful. He said to him: O shaykh, are Adam and Noah and Abraham and Moses and Jesus in the Garden, or not? He said: Yes indeed, O Commander of the Faithful. He said: O shaykh, then if al-Hasan and al-Husayn are the two masters of the youth of the people of the Garden, and their father is better than the two of them, it is established that Ali is the best of humankind together with the Prophet (ع). At that the shaykh said: I have believed and I have affirmed it as true. Then our mawla al-Hakim bi-Amr Allah, the Commander of the Faithful (ع), turned to the da'i and said: O shaykh, give the call as you wish, for no one will oppose you.

Now I come to the matter of the Commander of the Faithful Ali (ع) and the matter of the call to prayer. I set down in it a complete epistle of our sayyid and mawla and father Muhammad ibn Fahd, may God exalt his sanctity: the epistle entitled al-Mu'riba 'amma jarat bihi al-mubahatha, in which there is a lesson for whoever takes a lesson from it, on the question of the words of the one who says in the call to prayer that Muhammad and Ali are the best of humankind and their kindred the best of kindred; between the one who holds that this saying is not permitted in the canons of the upright religion and is not among what has been narrated in the transmitted reports, and the one who holds that it is among what the Noble Book has commanded, and that the keeping of it belongs to whoever obeys the command of those whose command is to be obeyed.
