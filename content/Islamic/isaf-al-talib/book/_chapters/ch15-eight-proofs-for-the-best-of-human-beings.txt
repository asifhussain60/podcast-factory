# Eight Proofs for the Best of Human Beings

In the name of God, the Most Gracious, the Most Merciful, and in Him I seek help.

> بسم الله الرحمن الرحيم وبه استعين

Praise belongs to God, Lord of the worlds, and may God bless His messenger, our master Muhammad, and his family, and grant them abundant peace. After praising God, Lord of the worlds, and invoking blessings upon the best of His messengers, Muhammad al-Amin, and upon his guardian, the most honorable of guardians, and upon all who are of the household of the two of them, the good and the pure, and (عليهم السلام):

A discussion took place between two jurists among the people of knowledge, and it obliges me to set down from it both the words of the questioner and the words of the one questioned, so that whoever comes upon this account may know which of the two spoke the truth and which of the two spoke what lies outside truth, guidance, and reasonable speech.

One of the two jurists said to the other: "How is it that the people of your school say in the call to prayer, 'Muhammad and Ali are the best of human beings, and their kindred are the best of kindred'? No other group of Islam says this."

The other jurist, the one questioned about it, answered him with these words, drawn from the saying of God, blessed and exalted:

> مَنْ عَمِلَ صَالِحاً فَلِنَفْسِهِ وَمَنْ أَسَاءَ فَعَلَيْهَا

Whoever does righteous work does so for his own soul, and whoever does evil does so against it.

"If our saying in the call to prayer, 'Muhammad and Ali are the best of human beings, and their kindred are the best of kindred,' is of righteous work, then we have done it only for ourselves; and if it is of work that is not righteous, but rather of wrongdoing, then it returns upon us alone, especially after His saying, exalted is He:

> وَلَا تَزِرُ وَازِرَةٌ وِزْرَ أُخْرَى

No bearer of burdens shall bear the burden of another."

At that the questioner said to him: "You have spoken truly in what you have brought forward from these two verses of the most truthful of speakers. But I ask you for the clear argument and the plain proof you possess for the soundness of what you have mentioned, namely that Muhammad, may God bless him and his family, and Ali are the best of human beings, and that you set Ali as the like of Muhammad, blessings be upon them both, according to what you employ in the call to prayer. For it is a known matter that Muhammad, may God bless him and his family, is the best of human beings, since it is established that he is the master of the messengers. But as for Ali ibn Abi Talib (ع), it is not established that he is the like of the Messenger in this excellence until you prove that to me with a plain proof."

The respondent answered him: You have asked me a question that must be answered, and I shall make this clear with illuminating proofs according to the measure of my capacity and my strength, and above every possessor of knowledge there is One who knows.

**1. The first proof.** We say, with the help and enabling grace of God, exalted is He, that since the word of God is:

> إِنَّمَا يُرِيدُ اللهُ لِيُذْهِبَ عَنكُمُ الرِّجْسَ أَهْلَ الْبَيْتِ وَيُطَهِّرَكُمْ تَطْهِيرًا

God only wishes to remove all impurity from you, people of the household, and to purify you completely.

there is established for the people of the household an equality with the Prophet in purity. And since the most excellent of the people of the household after him is Ali ibn Abi Talib, the Commander of the Believers, the blessings of God be upon him, who is his guardian and his friend, may God bless him and his family, and his successor in his community, likeness to him is thereby established for him. So he is the best of human beings together with him, and their kindred are the best of kindred, because of their likeness to the two of them in purity. This is among the clear matters in which there is no ambiguity, since it comes from the saying of the glorious Quran:

> لَا يَأْتِيهِ الْبَاطِلُ مِنْ بَيْنِ يَدَيْهِ وَلَا مِنْ خَلْفِهِ تَنزِيلٌ مِنْ حَكِيمٍ حَمِيدٍ

Falsehood does not come to it from before it nor from behind it; it is a revelation from One who is Wise, Praiseworthy.

**2. The second proof.** We say, with the help and enabling grace of God, that since the word of God, exalted is He, in His clear Book is:

> سَلَامٌ عَلَى آلِ يَاسِينَ إِنَّا كَذَلِكَ نَجْزِي الْمُحْسِنِينَ

Peace be upon the family of Yasin. Thus do We reward those who do good.

this is a declaration that the family of Yasin, upon whom God, glorified is He, has sent peace, are the family of the Messenger of God, may God bless him and his family, the excellent ones who are the people of his household. And the first and most excellent of them was the one whom the Messenger of God, may God bless him and his family, chose as his guardian in his community, and he is Ali ibn Abi Talib. It is therefore established that none can be the most excellent of the family of Yasin mentioned, upon whom God sent peace and whom He described as those who do good, except one who is the best of human beings, since it is established that those of the people of the household who come after him, who are the family of Yasin, are among human beings and of them, so he is the best of them. This is among the clear matters in which there is no ambiguity, since it is from the saying of the glorious Quran, to which falsehood does not come from before it nor from behind it, a revelation from One who is Wise, Praiseworthy.

**3. The third proof.** We say, with the help and enabling grace of God, exalted is He, that since the word of God, exalted is He, in this noble verse:

> كُنتُمْ خَيْرَ أُمَّةٍ أُخْرِجَتْ لِلنَّاسِ تَأْمُرُونَ بِالْمَعْرُوفِ وَتَنْهَوْنَ عَنِ الْمُنكَرِ وَتُؤْمِنُونَ بِاللهِ

You are the best community brought forth for humankind: you enjoin what is right, forbid what is wrong, and believe in God.

is an address from Him to the people of the household of the Messenger of God, and since His saying, exalted is He, that they are the best community brought forth for humankind, requires that there be no alteration of what He has made them to be, namely the best community brought forth for humankind; and since the most excellent and the first of them is the Commander of the Believers, Ali ibn Abi Talib, according to what has already been explained in the two preceding proofs, it is therefore sound and established, and again sound and established, that he is the best of human beings after the Prophet and together with him, blessings be upon them both. This is among the clear matters in which there is no ambiguity, since it is from the saying of the glorious Quran, to which falsehood does not come from before it nor from behind it, a revelation from One who is Wise, Praiseworthy.

**4. The fourth proof.** We say, with the help and enabling grace of God, exalted is He, that God has recounted in His noble Book the request of His prophet Moses (ع), which is his saying:

> وَاجْعَل لِّي وَزِيرًا مِّنْ أَهْلِي هَارُونَ أَخِي اشْدُدْ بِهِ أَزْرِي وَأَشْرِكْهُ فِي أَمْرِي كَيْ نُسَبِّحَكَ كَثِيرًا وَنَذْكُرَكَ كَثِيرًا إِنَّكَ كُنتَ بِنَا بَصِيرًا

And appoint for me a minister from my family, Aaron my brother. Strengthen my back through him and let him share in my task, so that we may glorify You much and remember You much. Indeed, You are ever watching over us.

And the answer to him from the Creator, exalted is He, was His saying:

> قَدْ أُوتِيتَ سُؤْلَكَ يَا مُوسَى

You have been granted your request, Moses.

This makes plain that Moses (ع) asked God, glorified is He, to make his brother Aaron a minister for him, through whom his back would be strengthened and who would share in his task, for the purpose he himself stated, that they might glorify Him much and remember Him much, since He is ever watching over them. Then came the word of God, exalted is He, in another place of His noble Book, relating the saying of the one He addressed, Moses (ع):

> وَأَخِي هَارُونُ هُوَ أَفْصَحُ مِنِّي لِسَانًا فَأَرْسِلْهُ مَعِيَ رِدْءًا يُصَدِّقُنِي إِنِّي أَخَافُ أَن يُكَذِّبُونِ

And my brother Aaron is more eloquent than I in speech, so send him with me as a helper to confirm me. I fear that they will call me a liar.

And His saying, exalted is He, in answer to that:

> سَنَشُدُّ عَضُدَكَ بِأَخِيكَ وَنَجْعَلُ لَكُمَا سُلْطَانًا فَلَا يَصِلُونَ إِلَيْكُمَا بِآيَاتِنَا أَنتُمَا وَمَنِ اتَّبَعَكُمَا الْغَالِبُونَ

We shall strengthen your arm through your brother and grant you both authority, so that they will not reach you. By Our signs, you two and those who follow you will be the victors.

And His saying, exalted is He, to His prophet Muhammad (ع) in another verse of His noble Book:

> أُولَٰئِكَ الَّذِينَ هَدَى اللهُ فَبِهُدَاهُمُ اقْتَدِهْ

Those are the ones whom God has guided, so follow their guidance.

And in another verse of it:

> مَا يُقَالُ لَكَ إِلَّا مَا قَدْ قِيلَ لِلرُّسُلِ مِن قَبْلِكَ

Nothing is said to you except what was said to the messengers before you.

And in another verse of it:

> شَرَعَ لَكُم مِّنَ الدِّينِ مَا وَصَّى بِهِ نُوحًا وَالَّذِي أَوْحَيْنَا إِلَيْكَ وَمَا وَصَّيْنَا بِهِ إِبْرَاهِيمَ وَمُوسَى وَعِيسَى أَنْ أَقِيمُوا الدِّينَ وَلَا تَتَفَرَّقُوا فِيهِ

He has ordained for you of religion what He enjoined upon Noah, and what We have revealed to you, and what We enjoined upon Abraham and Moses and Jesus: that you establish the religion and not be divided in it.

All of this makes it binding upon the Messenger that he make the Commander of the Believers, Ali ibn Abi Talib, his guardian, his minister, and his brother, that he strengthen his back through him and let him share in his task, and it establishes that God, glorified is He, has strengthened his arm through him and granted to him and to the other the authority mentioned, especially after His saying to him, exalted is He, in one place of His noble Book:

> وَلَن تَجِدَ لِسُنَّةِ اللهِ تَبْدِيلًا

You will never find any change in the way of God.

and His saying in another place:

> وَلَن تَجِدَ لِسُنَّةِ اللهِ تَحْوِيلًا

You will never find any alteration in the way of God.

Since the matter is so, it is sound and established that Ali (ع) is the minister of the Messenger of God, a brother to him, and a partner with him in his task and in the authority that God appointed for the two of them. This is confirmed by what has been narrated from him, of his saying concerning him:

> عَلِيٌّ مِنِّي بِمَنْزِلَةِ هَارُونَ مِنْ مُوسَى إِلَّا أَنَّهُ لَا نَبِيَّ بَعْدِي

Ali is to me in the station of Aaron to Moses, except that there is no prophet after me.

And when the matter is so, it is sound and established, and again sound and established, by the requirement of these noble verses of the glorious Quran, to which falsehood does not come from before it nor from behind it, a revelation from One who is Wise, Praiseworthy, of which He said:

> إِنَّ هَٰذَا الْقُرْآنَ يَهْدِي لِلَّتِي هِيَ أَقْوَمُ

Indeed, this Quran guides to that which is most upright.

and by the requirement of the saying of the Prophet mentioned, that Ali, the Commander of the Believers, is among those who are the best of human beings after the Messenger, since God associated him with him in his task and his authority. This is among the plain and clear matters in which there is no ambiguity, since it is from the saying of the glorious Quran, to which falsehood does not come from before it nor from behind it, a revelation from One who is Wise, Praiseworthy.

**5. The fifth proof.** We say, with the help and enabling grace of God, exalted is He, that since the word of God, mighty and majestic, in His noble Book is:

> إِنَّمَا وَلِيُّكُمُ اللهُ وَرَسُولُهُ وَالَّذِينَ آمَنُوا الَّذِينَ يُقِيمُونَ الصَّلَاةَ وَيُؤْتُونَ الزَّكَاةَ وَهُمْ رَاكِعُونَ

Your guardian is only God, and His Messenger, and those who believe: those who establish the prayer and give the alms while bowing.

> وَمَن يَتَوَلَّ اللهَ وَرَسُولَهُ وَالَّذِينَ آمَنُوا فَإِنَّ حِزْبَ اللهِ هُمُ الْغَالِبُونَ

And whoever takes God and His Messenger and those who believe as guardians, then the party of God are the victors.

this requires that those whom God, exalted is He, named in it as those who believe, and whom He described as establishing the prayer and giving the alms, and as the victorious party of God, are among the people of bounty, of whom God said:

> وَيُؤْتِ كُلَّ ذِي فَضْلٍ فَضْلَهُ

And He gives every possessor of bounty his bounty.

and of whom He said:

> إِنَّ الْفَضْلَ بِيَدِ اللهِ يُؤْتِيهِ مَن يَشَاءُ وَاللهُ ذُو الْفَضْلِ الْعَظِيمِ

Indeed, bounty is in the hand of God; He gives it to whomever He wills, and God is the possessor of great bounty.

And no one among human beings had a better right or a stronger claim to this bounty than the people of the household of the Messenger of God, of whom God said:

> إِنَّمَا يُرِيدُ اللهُ لِيُذْهِبَ عَنكُمُ الرِّجْسَ أَهْلَ الْبَيْتِ وَيُطَهِّرَكُمْ تَطْهِيرًا

God only wishes to remove all impurity from you, people of the household, and to purify you completely.

for His will is turned back by none of the created. And God also said of them:

> كُنتُمْ خَيْرَ أُمَّةٍ أُخْرِجَتْ لِلنَّاسِ تَأْمُرُونَ بِالْمَعْرُوفِ وَتَنْهَوْنَ عَنِ الْمُنكَرِ وَتُؤْمِنُونَ بِاللهِ

You are the best community brought forth for humankind: you enjoin what is right, forbid what is wrong, and believe in God.

And God also said of them:

> سَلَامٌ عَلَى آلِ يَاسِينَ إِنَّا كَذَلِكَ نَجْزِي الْمُحْسِنِينَ

Peace be upon the family of Yasin. Thus do We reward those who do good.

And the first and most excellent of them was the one whom the Messenger made his brother, his guardian, his minister, and his successor after him in his community. It is therefore sound and established, and again sound and established, that Ali ibn Abi Talib is the holder of the command after the Messenger, the one intended in the verse mentioned, and his partner in guardianship over the believers. And none is such except one who is the best of human beings after him and together with him, because of his partnership with him in the guardianship mentioned. This is among the clear matters in which there is no ambiguity, since it is from the saying of the glorious Quran, to which falsehood does not come from before it nor from behind it, a revelation from One who is Wise, Praiseworthy, and especially after the clarification given by the Messenger of God on the day of the festival of Ghadir Khumm, which is the day of the explicit designation of him, that he had made him, like himself, a master to the believers in his lifetime, by his saying:

> مَنْ كُنْتُ مَوْلَاهُ فَهَذَا عَلِيٌّ مَوْلَاهُ اللَّهُمَّ وَالِ مَنْ وَالَاهُ وَعَادِ مَنْ عَادَاهُ وَانْصُرْ مَنْ نَصَرَهُ وَاخْذُلْ مَنْ خَذَلَهُ وَأَدِرِ الْحَقَّ مَعَهُ حَيْثُ دَارَ

Whoever I am his master, this Ali is his master. O God, befriend whoever befriends him, oppose whoever opposes him, help whoever helps him, forsake whoever forsakes him, and turn the truth with him wherever he turns.

And truly, some part of what we have brought forward of these verses and arguments is sufficient, fully sufficient, for one who has a heart or who gives ear while he is a witness.

**6. The sixth proof.** We say, with the help and enabling grace of God, exalted is He, that since the word of God, exalted is He, in this noble verse of His noble Book is:

> إِنَّ الَّذِينَ آمَنُوا وَعَمِلُوا الصَّالِحَاتِ أُولَٰئِكَ هُمْ خَيْرُ الْبَرِيَّةِ

Indeed, those who believe and do righteous deeds are the best of creation.

and since it is a known matter that the people of the household of the Messenger are the best of creation, because they are the best of those who believed in God and did righteous deeds, and because of the saying of God concerning them that they are the best community brought forth for humankind; and since it is also a known matter that Ali, the Commander of the Believers, is the most excellent of the people of the household, it is therefore sound and established that our saying in the call to prayer, that Muhammad and Ali are the best of human beings and their kindred are the best of kindred, is the truth in which there is no ambiguity, since it is from the saying of the glorious Quran, to which falsehood does not come from before it nor from behind it, a revelation from One who is Wise, Praiseworthy.

**7. The seventh proof.** We say, with the help and enabling grace of God, exalted is He, that since the word of God in His noble Book is:

> إِنَّ اللهَ اصْطَفَى آدَمَ وَنُوحًا وَآلَ إِبْرَاهِيمَ وَآلَ عِمْرَانَ عَلَى الْعَالَمِينَ ذُرِّيَّةً بَعْضُهَا مِنْ بَعْضٍ وَاللهُ سَمِيعٌ عَلِيمٌ

Indeed, God chose Adam and Noah and the family of Abraham and the family of Imran above all the worlds, descendants one from another, and God is Hearing, Knowing.

this is a declaration making plain that God chose the family of Abraham and the family of Imran just as He chose Adam and Noah, whom He singled out with the message and prophethood. And since it is a known matter that the Messenger of God is more excellent than Adam and Noah, it is therefore sound that his family is more excellent than the family of Abraham and the family of Imran (عليهم السلام) altogether. And when the matter is so, it is sound that our master the Commander of the Believers, Ali ibn Abi Talib, is the most excellent of the family of the Messenger. It is therefore sound and established, and again sound and established, that our saying in the call to prayer, that Muhammad and Ali are the best of human beings and their kindred are the best of kindred, is among that whose soundness and firmness are witnessed by the verses of the glorious Quran, to which falsehood does not come from before it nor from behind it, a revelation from One who is Wise, Praiseworthy.

**8. The eighth proof.** We say, with the help and enabling grace of God, exalted is He, that since the word of God, exalted is He, in this noble verse is:

> وَلَقَدْ نَجَّيْنَا بَنِي إِسْرَائِيلَ مِنَ الْعَذَابِ الْمُهِينِ مِن فِرْعَوْنَ إِنَّهُ كَانَ عَالِيًا مِّنَ الْمُسْرِفِينَ وَلَقَدِ اخْتَرْنَاهُمْ عَلَى عِلْمٍ عَلَى الْعَالَمِينَ

And We saved the Children of Israel from the humiliating torment, from Pharaoh. He was high-handed, one of the transgressors. And We chose them knowingly above all the worlds.

it makes plain that God chose the Children of Israel in their era above the worlds and preferred them over them, and that the worlds are human beings and are the creation. Then the family of the Messenger are the best of human beings in their own era, and indeed in the eras of those who went before them, since it is a known matter that the Messenger is the master of all the messengers and of all the prophets, the most honorable and the most excellent of them. And when the matter is so, it is sound and established, and again sound and established, that his family, who are his kindred, are more excellent than the family of every prophet and more honorable than the kindred of every prophet, and that they are chosen above all the people of his era; and that our saying in the call to prayer, that Muhammad and Ali are the best of human beings and their kindred are the best of kindred, is among true and correct speech, because Ali is the most excellent of the family of the Messenger of God, the most honorable of them and the best of them; and that the judgment to this effect is a true judgment, according to what has been witnessed by these verses of the noble Book which we have brought forward here, apart from the like of them which we have not brought forward, and according to what has been witnessed by the noble verse we have mentioned, from the verses of the glorious Quran, to which falsehood does not come from before it nor from behind it, a revelation from One who is Wise, Praiseworthy.

Having finished the mention of these eight answers concerning the matter spoken of, and the proofs, which are from the saying of the most truthful of speakers, which expand the breast and remove doubt from the hearts of the believers, let us seal this treatise of ours, which we have devoted to that subject, with praise to God, Lord of the worlds, and with blessings upon our master Muhammad, master of the messengers, and upon his pure family until the Day of Judgment. (عليهم السلام). God is sufficient for us, and how excellent a trustee.

In the name of God, the Most Gracious, the Most Merciful.

There is a confusion put forward in this place by some objectors who oppose the noble school, namely that what Umar ibn al-Khattab removed from the call to prayer, the phrase

> حَيَّ عَلَى خَيْرِ الْعَمَلِ

Hasten to the best of deeds.

is not to be accepted as a charge against him, because no such narration exists in our books; and that even granting it, it is neither ugly nor an error, since it was a good exercise of judgment; and that if it were permissible to call it an error on his part, then what some of your imams added, the saying

> أَشْهَدُ أَنَّ مَوْلَانَا عَلِيًّا وَلِيُّ اللهِ

I testify that our master Ali is the friend of God.

would likewise be an error, since one who adds to the call to prayer is like one who subtracts from it.

I say: this is a confusion on his part and a sophistry played upon fancies, as we shall set out and clarify in the explanation that follows, with the help and enabling grace of God. Even if we were to concede, for the sake of argument, that error lies in both addition and subtraction in the call to prayer, we do not concede that the one who adds and the one who subtracts stand equal in the degree of error. To make them equal is gross error and plain misguidance, for several reasons:

**The first:** let it first be considered how many are those who follow the subtraction from the call to prayer in relation to those who follow the addition in it. They are as one to a hundred thousand or fewer. The one who subtracted therefore led great numbers of people astray and cast them into error, so his offense is greater than that of the one who added.

**The second:** he ascribes this addition to our master the Imam al-Hakim bi-Amr Allah, the blessings of God be upon him. If that be granted, then the one who subtracted must have preceded him in error and in leading the community astray by more than four hundred years, since al-Hakim was not born until after the fourth century. The one who goes before in error holds a greater degree of offense, and the two are not equal.

**The third:** had the one who subtracted confined himself to subtraction, he and the one who added would stand in a single degree. But along with his subtraction he added, by his own opinion, a sentence of the ugliest and feeblest sort, namely his saying:

> الصَّلَاةُ خَيْرٌ مِنَ النَّوْمِ

Prayer is better than sleep.

Glory be to God! Where is prayer and where is sleep, that the two should be set against each other in terms of better and lesser, like one who says, "This sword is better than wood," not knowing whether he is praising or mocking? So he holds two errors, while the one who adds to the call to prayer holds one error, and the two are not equal.

**The fourth:** grant that it was his exercise of judgment. Why then did he command the dropping of the Prophet's phrase from the five prayer times, and attach his own sentence once only, in the dawn prayer? And how many hideous and feeble things are contained in this sentence, which we have left aside for fear of length.

As for the ascription of this addition to our master al-Hakim bi-Amr Allah, the blessings of God be upon him, while the Twelver Shia, despite their disagreement with him in school, employ this sentence in the call to prayer, it is a plainly void ascription, and the claim of the one who says it is raving and slander.

As for his saying that ascribing this subtraction to Umar ibn al-Khattab is unacceptable because it does not exist in the books of that people, his saying is of no weight, for as to the books relied upon for soundness, such as Sahih al-Bukhari and the rest of the five and al-Mishkat, the disagreement of the people of their own persuasion over their soundness, and the fabricated reports brought out within them, relieve the people of truth of the need to refute them and to set up proofs against them. Moreover, the narration transmitted by our master al-Qadi al-Nu'man from our master Ja'far al-Sadiq precedes the compilation of the books of that people, and it may well be that when they saw in it an opening for censure against them they dropped it and erased it, for fear of giving room to the censure of the people of truth; and such has been their custom. This is the refutation of the saying that the one who adds to the call to prayer is like the one who subtracts from it.

As for the origin of the ascription of this addition, the answer concerning it is that this phrase and the testimony to guardianship issued from the Commander of the Believers, Ali ibn Abi Talib, concerning himself, when the office of guardianship passed to him after the Prophet, just as the Prophet testified for himself to prophethood and messengership, and as God, glorified is He, testified concerning His own essence, saying:

> شَهِدَ اللهُ أَنَّهُ لَا إِلَهَ إِلَّا هُوَ

God bears witness that there is no god but He.

and that was after the verse of guardianship was revealed concerning him, His saying, "Your guardian is only God and His Messenger," to the end of it. Though they have disagreed about the occasion of its revelation, out of a desire on their part to deny his guardianship, their books are nonetheless filled with testimony that it was revealed concerning the Commander of the Believers, the blessings of God be upon him. In this there is sufficiency for those who seek right direction and guidance; and as for the stubborn disputer, he has nothing but going astray.

> وَكُلُّ إِنَاءٍ يَتَرَشَّحُ بِمَا فِيهِ وَاللهُ يَهْدِي مَنْ يَشَاءُ إِلَى صِرَاطٍ مُسْتَقِيمٍ

Every vessel seeps with what is in it, and God guides whom He wills to a straight path.
