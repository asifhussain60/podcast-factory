# Friday and the Two Festivals

### On the Friday Prayer

I turn now to the Friday prayer, and I set down first what I find in the book of questions and answers mentioned at the outset, which one of its arrangers ordered according to the order of the chapters of the book *Da'a'im al-Islam*.

The question: What do you say concerning what has come in the Book of Purity, that if there are three miles between two congregational mosques, the Friday prayer is established in each of them, whether they are in one city or in two cities; but if there is less than that between them in a single city, the Friday prayer is established in only one of them? Our master, if there is less than three miles between them and they belong to two cities, is the Friday prayer established in both of them or not?

The answer: The Friday prayer is not established in both of them when there is less than three miles between them, whether they are in one city or in two.

The question concerns the words of our master al-Mu'ayyad in the Friday sermon:

> فمن تركها متعمداً أو مستخفاً بحقها من غير عذر له مع إمام عدل أو جائر فلا تجمع الله شمله

Whoever abandons it deliberately, or making light of its due, without an excuse, whether under a just imam or an unjust one, may God not gather his affairs together, to the end of the passage.

The answer: It is not permissible to pray behind the people of injustice except for the sake of dissimulation, as has come in the Shari'ah from Mawlana al-Baqir Muhammad ibn Ali (ع), who said:

> لا تعتدّ بالصلاة خلف الناصب ولا الحروري واجعله سارية من سواري المسجد واقرأ لنفسك كأنك وحدك

Do not count as valid the prayer behind a hostile opponent or a Haruri. Treat him as one of the pillars of the mosque, and recite for yourself as though you were alone.

It has come in the commentary on the reports that al-Husayn ibn Ali (ع) put the governor of Mu'awiya forward to lead the prayer over his brother al-Hasan ibn Ali (عليهم السلام), and said:

> لولا السنّة لما قدّمتك

Were it not for the practice, I would not have put you forward.

By that he intended the practice of the People of the House (عليهم السلام) in dissimulation, as one of the Imams (عليهم السلام) said:

> التقية ديني ودين آبائي

Dissimulation is my religion and the religion of my fathers.

As for what is intended by "the unjust" in his saying, it is whoever has been set up among the summoners and from whom injustice and transgression have appeared: it is obligatory to pray behind him, and it is upon the people, when they observe that from him, to bring it up to the holder of authority and to await his answer. This is the meaning of his saying:

> أيها الصالحون فتحققوا ذلك ولا ترتابوا بعد اليقين فقد اعتصمتم بجبل الله المتين

O righteous ones, verify this and do not fall into doubt after certainty, for you have taken firm hold of the strong mountain of God.

He says in the book *Mukhtasar al-Musannaf*: The Friday prayer behind the Imam of the Muslims is more excellent than the prayer behind his deputies. If the Imam is delayed because he is occupied with some of the interests of the Muslims, and his deputies pray in his stead in mosques that are near one another, then the prayer in his own mosque, the one in which it has been his custom to pray, is more fitting than in any other.

He says in it: The call to prayer which obliges the one upon whom the Friday duty rests to leave off buying and selling is the call that is given in the presence of the preacher when he sits in the place from which he delivers the sermon. If two of those obliged to Friday transact a sale at the time in which it is forbidden, their sale is not annulled. Whoever grows thirsty while the Imam is on the pulpit may drink.

He says in it: If the Imam delivers the sermon on Friday and then his state of purity is broken, he descends and performs the ablution; then if he wishes he repeats the sermon, and if he wishes he prays, and all of that suffices him. If he delivers the sermon seated because of an ailment, that suffices him. It is disliked for him to omit the sitting between the two sermons, but if he has done so, repetition is not obligatory upon him.

If the Imam delivers the sermon on Friday and then some matter overtakes the people and they go off to it, and one man remains with him, then he prays four cycles. If he has delivered the sermon to the people on Friday and prayed one cycle with them and then they all departed and he remained alone, he prays the Friday prayer as two cycles. If he has opened the prayer and they departed before he bowed, he begins afresh the noon prayer of four cycles. If the Imam prays the Friday prayer with the people, and when he has finished the second cycle he rises to stand and then remembers, it is upon him to sit, to make the testimony, to give the greeting of peace, and to perform the two prostrations of forgetfulness.

And from it: Whoever among the people of Friday sleeps while waiting for the Friday prayer, and the prayer is established and purification is not possible for him, performs the dry ablution and prays with the Imam, and then repeats it as four.

He says in the book of reports: And he leans upon his sword; if he does not find a sword, then upon a staff.

He says in the book *Ta'wil al-Shari'ah*: The sermon is before the prayer, and the prayer after the sermon is two cycles. He divides the sermon into two portions with a sitting that separates them. The raising of the voice in its recitation distinguishes it from the noon prayer on the remaining days, and the noon prayer is at seven hours. He said: The call to stand for prayer comes after the sermon.

He says in it: The practice of the respondents in the assembly of the summoner is like the practice of the congregation on Friday, or the congregation behind the Imam. When the preacher on Friday ascends the pulpit and faces the people, silence becomes obligatory, so that it is not permissible for anyone to speak, nor to occupy himself with anything other than listening to him and giving ear to him.

He says in it: It is not permissible for him to give suck unless his summoner has appointed him to the suckling and chosen him for it. When the Imam recites, listening becomes obligatory and nothing else, and it is not permissible to turn away from him. Likewise it is not permissible for the respondent to press proposals upon the summoner and to demand from him the various branches of knowledge, for he knows better how to raise his children.

Our master Zayd ibn Muhammad ibn al-Fadl said in his *Hidden Epistle*: The Friday prayer points to Muhammad (ع), who is the gate of the Garden, and he has two summonses, an outward summons to the outward face of his Shari'ah and an inward summons to the interpretation of his law. Then he said: Because the prayer on the remaining days was four cycles, on Friday it became two, and the sermon was made a substitute for the two cycles. It is praise, admonition, supplication, and exposition from the Lord of exposition, and it is never without warning. The sitting during the sermon is the separation between the upper light and the lower light; the Qa'im (ع) rises with the two lights. Then he said: The preacher is not to deliver the sermon upon the call to prayer that has already been given, until the muezzin who stands before the seat gives the call after the Imam has ascended the pulpit. Then he said: The call is given only in the congregational mosque, as an announcement. Then he said: Whoever gives the call on that day in one of the mosques other than the congregational mosque has erred, and if he did it deliberately he is punished and commanded to perform the washing of Friday. Then he said:

> فاعرف فضل يوم الجمعة حتى يجمع الله شملك بيوم السبت فتستريح وتسبت ولا تشقى إن شاء الله تعالى

Know the excellence of the day of Friday, so that God may gather your affairs together on the day of the Sabbath, and you may rest and repose and not be wretched, if God the Exalted wills.

Mawlana Amir al-Mu'minin Ali ibn Abi Talib (ع) said in his Four Hundred Points of Conduct:

> من كانت له إلى الله حاجة فليطلبها في ثلاث ساعات: ١- في يوم الجمعة ساعة زوال الشمس ٢- وحين تهب الرياح وتفتح أبواب السماء وتنزل الرحمة ٣- وساعة في آخر الليل عند طلوع الفجر. وإن ملكين يناديان هل من تائب فيتاب عليه هل من سائل فيعطى هل من مستغفر فيغفر له هل من طالب حاجة فأجيبوا داعي الله

Whoever has a need before God, let him seek it in three hours:

1. On the day of Friday, at the hour when the sun passes its zenith.
2. When the winds blow, and the gates of heaven are opened, and mercy descends.
3. An hour at the end of the night, at the breaking of dawn.

And two angels call out: Is there one who repents, that his repentance may be accepted? Is there one who asks, that he may be given? Is there one who seeks forgiveness, that he may be forgiven? Is there one who seeks a need? So answer the caller of God.

He says in it: The supplication of devotion in the Friday prayer is before the bowing in the second cycle. The recitation on Friday is, in the first cycle, the chapter of the Congregation after the Opening of the Book, and in the second the Opening of the Book and the chapter of the Hypocrites.

He says in it, among legal questions that are all marvels, another question: What do you say of a preacher who was delivering the sermon on Friday until a man entered by the door of the congregational mosque, whereupon the sermon of that preacher was spoiled, and so was the prayer of those who prayed with him? The answer: That preacher is the property of the man who entered by the door of the congregational mosque, and the man had appointed him as his deputy; so when he came in upon him, it was no longer permissible for him to go before his master, nor to lead him in prayer.

### On the Prayer of the Two Festivals

I set down likewise what is in the book of questions and answers mentioned at the outset, arranged according to the order of the chapters of the book *Da'a'im al-Islam*.

The question: What do you say concerning what has come in the Shari'ah, that there is no tashriq incumbent upon the traveler except in a congregational city? Our master, is what is intended by tashriq here the festival prayer or not? And whoever forgets one of the magnifications in the festival prayer, or more than one, how is he to act?

The answer: It has come in the book *al-Mujzi*, in the mention of the ages of camels and cattle and the like, that there is no tashriq except in a congregational city, that is, no prayer of the two festivals, because the two are prayed at the rising of the sun, and the one who prays them is called المشرّق (al-mushriq). And it has come in *Mukhtasar al-Musannaf*: Whoever forgets part of the magnification completes it before he bows; and if he has passed beyond the bowing and is certain of the omission, he performs the two prostrations of forgetfulness.

The question: What do you say concerning the magnification from the day of Arafah to the last of the days of tashriq being twenty three magnifications, while in our lands they add one magnification after the festival prayer so that it becomes twenty four? And what is the meaning of it?

The answer: The magnification in those days is twenty three, and it is a likeness of what obtains in the summons of the five speakers, the possessors of firm resolve, who correspond to the five prayers, drawn from the mention of the ten spiritual ranks and the ten bodily ranks, and the mention of the speaker of the cycle, his executor, and the Imam of the age.

> فخذ ما أوتيت وكن من الشاكرين

So take what you have been given, and be among the thankful.

From another source: Whoever prays the festival prayer while owing a makeup of the dawn prayer, his festival prayer is valid and he does not repeat it; and likewise the funeral prayer.

From our master al-Hasan ibn Idris: Whoever prays the festival prayer before bringing out the alms of the breaking of the fast does not repeat it, and he hands over the alms afterward. The washing for the two festivals is a practice of the Prophet. The first of its time is the breaking of dawn, and the last of it is the time of the sun's passing the zenith. Whoever prays the festival prayer before the sun's passing the zenith, for an excuse, it is valid for him.

He says in the book *Mukhtasar al-Musannaf*: The washing for the two festivals is a practice of the Prophet; the first of its time is the breaking of dawn and the last of it is the time of the sun's passing the zenith.

He says in the book of reports: And the supplication of devotion is:

> اللهم اغفر لي وارحمني وعافني واعف عني في الدين والدنيا إنك على كل شيء قدير

O God, forgive me, have mercy on me, grant me well being, and pardon me in religion and in the world. You are powerful over all things.

He says in it: If the people of the desert attend, and those travelers who choose to attend the festival, that is good, and there is merit in it for them. And from it: It is fitting for whoever is of the people of the desert, at two leagues from the city or less, that he witness the festival.

He says in it: Its congregation is five, one of them the Imam, as with the congregation of the Friday prayer; and where there is one who establishes it, it is an obligation like the obligation of Friday. Whoever misses it with the one who establishes it but catches the two sermons prays it as four as well. Whoever misses one cycle of the festival prayer and catches the second with the Imam adds to it another cycle with its magnifications. The practice regarding the magnification is that it begins after the completion of the fast, on the night of the breaking of the fast and on the day of the breaking of the fast, until he prays and finishes the prayer. Whoever prays the festival prayer raises his hands with every magnification. Whoever forgets part of the magnification completes it before he bows; and if he has passed beyond the bowing and is certain of the omission, he performs the two prostrations of forgetfulness.

He says in the book *al-Musannaf*: The festival prayer is not prayed at Mina. Likewise our master al-Qadi al-Nu'man ibn Muhammad said in *Ta'wil al-Da'a'im*; and it has been verified from our master Idris ibn Hasan that the festival prayer is not prayed there in congregation, but each one prays it by himself, one by one.

He says in the book of questions and answers mentioned at the outset: Intercourse with one's wife is permitted on the night of the festival of the breaking of the fast and before the festival prayer.

Our master Ja'far ibn Mansur al-Yaman said in the book *al-Rida' al-Batin*: It is permitted after the festival of the breaking of the fast to fast on the following day and thereafter. And he says in it: It is not permitted after this festival, meaning the festival of the sacrifice, as it is permitted after the festival of the breaking of the fast.

Our master Zayd ibn Muhammad ibn al-Fadl said in the *Hidden Epistle*, concerning the prayer of the festival of the breaking of the fast: الفطر (al-fitr) in the language is appearance and beginning. It is said, تفطرت الأرض وانفطرت (tafattarat al-ard wa-infatarat), that is, it split open and what was within it appeared and came forth. Likewise تفطّرت الشجرة (tafattarat al-shajarah), that is, its hidden fruit appeared. And فطر ناب البعير (fatara nab al-ba'ir), that is, it came up and appeared. In all these witnesses there is this, that it is the day of the appearance of the command and of the things that were hidden. From this the leaving of the fast was named fitr, because the fast is silence and concealment. God the Exalted said in the story of Maryam:

> إني نذرت للرحمن صوماً فلن أكلم اليوم إنسياً

I have vowed a fast to the Most Merciful, so I shall not speak to any human being today.

That is, I have vowed silence and stillness, that I should not show anything of what is with me. So fitr is the appearance of the secrets and of the knowledge of hidden things. And His saying,

> وإذا السماء انفطرت

And when the sky is split open,

means that it was cleft and what was in it came forth. In another witness, al-fitr is the new; and فطر الله الخلق (fatara Allah al-khalq) means the beginning of His fashioning of them. So the day of the breaking of the fast is the day of the manifestation of the command that had been veiled. Then he said: Whoever has fasted the days of the month of Ramadan, for him is the breaking of the fast, the festival, and joy; and whoever does not know the meaning of the reality of fasting and its limits is in nothing of God, and he is as God, mighty and majestic, said:

> عاملة ناصبة تصلى ناراً حامية

Toiling, weary, burning in a scorching fire.

And the festival prayer has no call to prayer and no call to stand. Then he said: The sermon at the breaking of the fast is after the prayer. Then he said: The recitation in it is aloud. Then he said: The prayer of the sacrifice is on the day of the sacrifice, the day of the pilgrimage. Then he said: The beginning of that sermon, being contrary to the beginning of the other sermons, is an indication that the command appears otherwise than it was in the cycles that have passed. Every sermon begins with praise except on the day of the sacrifice, for that one begins with the four magnifications.
