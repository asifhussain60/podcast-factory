# What Must Be Learned, What Must Be Concealed

I turn now to what is sought after in the pursuit of knowledge, in the Qur'an, and in what belongs with them.

Mawlana Amir al-Mu'minin Ali ibn Abi Talib (ع) said in his four hundred maxims of conduct:

> لا صمت إلى الليل الا في ذكر الله ، لا تعرّب بعد الهجرة

There is no silence until nightfall except in the remembrance of God; and do not turn back to the desert after the migration.

And he said in it:

> لا يقرأ العاقل القرآن إذا كان على غير وضوء حتى يتطهر له

The man of sound mind does not recite the Qur'an while he is without ablution, until he has purified himself for it.

I mention now the one from whom knowledge must be taken, and the one who is to be shunned and whose word is refused, and what belongs with that.

Mawlana Amir al-Mu'minin Ali ibn Abi Talib (ع) said in his four hundred maxims of conduct:

> لا يغلب عليكم المرجئة برأيها

Let not the Murji'ah overcome you with their opinion.

And it is said in the book of questions, which comes from the most exalted Sheikh Shamoun ibn Muhammad Ghori al-Hindi (رض), together with the answers, which come from our master Badr al-Din, Sayyidina Hasan ibn Sayyidina Idris ibn Sayyidina al-Hasan ibn Sayyidina Abdullah al-Anf.

The question: What do you say, O illuminator of the truths for those who deserve them, concerning this? Our master Hamid al-Din mentioned in the book al-Hadi wa'l-Mustahdi, with conclusive proofs, and likewise our master al-Nu'man mentioned in the book al-Da'a'im, and the most exalted and most excellent Da'i, the most perfect in composition, Sayyidina Idris ibn al-Hasan, said in the book Zahr al-Ma'ani, that al-Sadiq (ع) had granted al-Shafi'i license while he kept it concealed. So, our master, did his condition change as the conditions of al-Mughirah and Abu al-Khattab and their like changed? Or did the ignorant invent a falsehood in claiming his school, as one faction claimed the Imamate of Muhammad ibn al-Hanafiyya and Abdullah ibn Muawiyah, when neither of those two ever claimed it for himself? Be gracious with an answer to this, brief and sufficient for those who possess insight in religion.

The answer:

> نعم قد كان أطلق له لاقراره فضل أهل البيت عليهم السلام لا غير

Yes, he had granted him license, only because of his acknowledgment of the excellence of the Ahl al-Bayt, and for nothing else.

And it is said in the same book of questions.

The question: What do you say, O school of insights and of the inmost thoughts, concerning the saying of the Prophet:

> من نظر في كتاب أخيه بغير أذنه فكأنما ينظر في النار

Whoever looks into the book of his brother without his permission is as one who looks into the Fire.

Our master, when the people of a school gather together, this one looks into the book of that one, and that one into the book of this one, and they do not ask permission. What then is intended by this saying?

The answer:

> هذا في كتاب الحقيقة لا ينظر الإنسان الا بأذن من أطلق له ذلك وأما في غيرها فأدب وليس بلازم

This concerns the book of the inner truth, into which a man may not look except by the permission of the one who has granted him that. As for other books, it is a matter of good manners and is not binding.

And it is said in it.

The question: What do you say, O comfort of our eyes, concerning a man who asks another man for the Qur'an or for other books as a loan, and he sees in it damage and disorder? Is it permitted to him, and to whoever sees this in it, to cut away the disorder and to set it right without the permission of its owner, or not?

The answer:

> لا بأس بأن يصلحه وليس له لازم

There is no harm in his setting it right, and it is not binding upon him.

And it is said in it.

The question: What do you say, O gate of our sought goal, which none reaches except through it, concerning a man who has died leaving behind the books of the da'wah and other goods, and who bears a debt owed to people which the price of the goods will not discharge? Are the books to be placed in their treasury, or is his debt to be discharged by them?

The answer:

> إن كانت الكتب من كتب الدعوة فليس له ذلك لأنها للدعوة لا تورث

If the books are among the books of the da'wah, that is not permitted to him, because they belong to the da'wah and are not inherited.

And it is said in the questions that come from certain of the sheikhs of India, with the answers from certain of our masters, the callers of Yemen.

The question: Is it permitted for those who have been granted license (المفسوحين) to read the benefits of the religion to women who are close kin and to others in a single gathering?

The answer:

> إذا كان بين الرجل والمرأة سترة قراء عليها بحيث تسمع قوله ولا يراها ولا يخلو رجل بامرأة الاّ أن يكون معها غيرها أو معه غيره

If there is a screen between the man and the woman, he may read to her in such a way that she hears his speech and he does not see her. And let no man be alone with a woman unless there is another woman with her or another man with him.

And it is said in the questions already mentioned:

> وأمّا ما سألت من العلم الواجب الذي لا يسع جهله فهو ما تعهّد الله عزّ وجل العباد به من معرفته وتوحيده وتنزيهه عن التشبيه والتعطيل والعدم ثم الإقرار برسله أوّلهم وآخرهم والشهادة بالبلاغ لهم والإقرار بمحمد خاتم النبيين وسيد المرسلين والتصديق بما جاء به والإقرار بالأئمة من ذريته الذين أمر الله عزّ وجل بتوليهم وردّ المسألة إليهم ووصل في كتابه طاعته وطاعة رسوله بطاعتهم فقال عزّ وجل أطيعوا الله وأطيعوا الرسول وأولي الأمر منكم ومعرفة إمام الزمان واعتقاد إمامته وولايته والتسليم لأمره والأخذ عنه وأن الله عزّ وجل لم يخل الأرض من حجة له على عباده وإمام يدعوهم إلى طاعته لقوله لا شريك له يوم ندعوا كلّ أناس بإمامهم وقال لمحمد صلى الله عليه وآله إنما أنت منذر ولكل قوم هاد فمن علم هذا وأعتقده وأقرّ به وصدّقه وجب علينا اتباع الدليل واقتفاء اثار الرسول وسؤالهم والأخذ عنهم قال الله أصدق القائلين فاسألوا أهل الذكر إن كنتم لا تعلمون فبقدر ما يعلم المؤمن بعد الإقرار والتصديق ترفع درجّه وتعلو منزلّه قال الله تعالى يرفع الله الذين آمنوا منكم والذين أتوا العلم درجات

As for what you asked concerning the obligatory knowledge whose ignorance is not permitted, it is that with which God, mighty and exalted, has charged His servants: the knowledge of Him, the declaration of His oneness, and the holding of Him far above all likeness, above the stripping away of His attributes, and above nonexistence; then the acknowledgment of His messengers, the first of them and the last of them, and the testimony that they delivered their message; and the acknowledgment of Muhammad, the Seal of the Prophets and the Master of the Messengers, and the affirmation of what he brought; and the acknowledgment of the Imams from his progeny, whom God, mighty and exalted, commanded us to take as our guardians and to whom He commanded that every question be referred, and in His Book He joined obedience to Himself and obedience to His Messenger with obedience to them, for He said, mighty and exalted: Obey God, and obey the Messenger, and those vested with authority among you. And it is the recognition of the Imam of the age, and belief in his Imamate and his guardianship, and submission to his command, and the taking of knowledge from him; and that God, mighty and exalted, has not left the earth without a proof of His against His servants and without an Imam calling them to His obedience, for He who has no partner said: On the day We shall call every people by their Imam. And He said to Muhammad: You are only a warner, and for every people there is a guide. So whoever knows this, and believes it, and acknowledges it, and affirms it as true, it is incumbent upon us to follow the guide, to trace the footsteps of the Messenger, to ask them, and to take from them. God, the most truthful of those who speak, said: Ask the people of remembrance, if you do not know. And in the measure of what the believer knows, after acknowledgment and affirmation, his degree is raised and his station is made high. God, exalted, said: God raises those of you who believe, and those who have been given knowledge, by degrees.

And Ali, Amir al-Mu'minin (ع), said:

> قيمة كل امرئ ما كان يحسن من العلم

The worth of every man is what he masters of knowledge.

And the Messenger of God said:

> طلب العلم فريضة على كل مسلم ومسلمة

The seeking of knowledge is an obligation upon every Muslim man and every Muslim woman.

I mention now the prohibition of analogy in religion.

Mawlana Amir al-Mu'minin Ali ibn Abi Talib (ع) said in his four hundred maxims of conduct:

> لا تقيسوا الدين فأنه لا يقاس وسيأتي قوم يقيسون الدين وهم أعداءه وأول من قاس إبليس

Do not measure the religion by analogy, for it is not to be measured. A people will come who measure the religion by analogy, and they are its enemies. The first to reason by analogy was Iblis.

And he said in it:

> إذا سمعتم من حديثنا ما لا تعرفونه فردّوه إلينا وقفوا عنده تسلموا

When you hear from our discourse what you do not recognize, refer it back to us and halt before it, and you will be safe.

I mention now the referring back, in all that is obscure, to God, to His Messenger, to his legatee, to the Imams from his progeny, and to their callers.

Our master al-Qadi al-Nu'man ibn Muhammad said in his book known as Mafatih al-Ni'mah, and al-Sadiq (ع) has said:

> ما أشتكل عليكم شيء من علومنا فردّوه إلينا ولا تردّوه علينا يعني ردّوا إلينا لنبرهن لكم برهانه ونعرفكم تأويله ولا تردّوه علينا بأنّ تكذّبوا به وترجعوا عنه

Whenever anything of our sciences is obscure to you, refer it back to us and do not throw it back at us. That is: refer it to us, so that we may establish its proof for you and make its interpretation known to you; and do not throw it back at us by calling it false and turning away from it.

I mention now the command to conceal knowledge, the secrets, the religion, and the practice of precaution.

Amir al-Mu'minin Ali ibn Abi Talib (ع) said in his four hundred maxims of conduct:

> من أذاع سرنا أذاقه الله برد الحديد

Whoever divulges our secret, God will give him the cold of iron to taste.
