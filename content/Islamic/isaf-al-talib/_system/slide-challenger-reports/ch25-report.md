#### SL-P3, six annotated structures name parts and place none

The taxonomy entry for Annotated Structure requires "Parts (named, **with positions**)" and names
its anti-pattern as "Callouts without spatial relation. Annotated structures need the spatial
layout to do work." Four of the ten annotated structures in this deck do supply position (M03 uses
left/centre/right, M17 places its parts as ordinals around a 360-degree circuit, M21 places the
planets overhead at noon, M37 lays its labels out as horizon circle, meridian and crossing path).
The other six supply none, and NotebookLM has nothing to draw from but a bulleted list.

**SL01. M06 (:64), "The condition of the ending"**
- File: `slide-decks/ch25-deck-astronomy-calculation-and-religious-obligation.txt:64`
- Content: `Whole: A final argument that names a diagram it cannot show. / - Part: the instruction in the text, as shown in this picture. / - Part: the picture itself, absent, and what survives, a scatter of badly transmitted labels.`
- What is missing: positions for both parts, and one annotation covers both rather than one per part.
- Suggested re-authoring: this moment duplicates M37 and M43. The cleanest fix is to cut it from the opening movement entirely and let M37 carry the lost figure once.
- VERIFIED

**SL02. M10 (:124), "The proverb that calls a man blind"**
- File: `...ch25-deck...txt:124`
- Content: `Whole: the proverb that calls a man blind. / - Part: the word used, blind. / - Part: what is actually meant, that he is slow and late in knowing things... / - Part: what is not meant, anything at all about his eyes.`
- What is missing: positions. The three parts are a word, its meaning, and its non-meaning, which is a contrast, not a dissection.
- Suggested re-authoring: retype as a contrast pair. Column A "What the word says" (organ named: the eye; defect named: blindness), Column B "What the speaker means" (organ actually named: the understanding; defect actually named: slow and late in knowing things in their absence), shared row: the man's eyesight is perfect and everyone using the insult knows it.
- VERIFIED

**SL03. M16 (:191), "The scriptural bridge into the technical section"**

Retype M16 the same way: rows = Qur'an 10:5, 2:189, 9:36; columns = what it fixes | what it makes knowable in advance | what it removes from the eye's jurisdiction.

**SL04. M24 (:294), "The opponent's doctrine"**

- What is missing: positions, and the "parts" are two sentences rather than components of a laid-out whole.
- Suggested re-authoring: the doctrine has a before and an after, which is spatial. Render as a two-state annotated structure with positions: left state "before sight arrives" (knowledge holds the place, standing in), right state "once sight arrives" (the stand-in retires, sight rules), with the arrow between them labelled "the moment sight becomes available". That is a picture; the current block is a quotation.
