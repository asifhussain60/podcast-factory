#### SL-P3, three annotated structures name parts and place none

The taxonomy entry for Annotated Structure requires "Parts (named, **with positions**)" and names
its anti-pattern as "Callouts without spatial relation. Annotated structures need the spatial layout
to do work." Six of the nine annotated structures here do supply position and supply it well (M03
centre/facing/between/margins, M08 halfway mark/past the mark/the band between, M25 front/inside/end
of a sentence, M29 centre/beside/mirrored opposite/in the interval, M30 surface/beside/centre, and

**SL1. M04 (:37), "The lost figure, reconstructed only as far as its surviving labels allow"**
- File: `slide-decks/ch26-deck-two-sightings-and-higher-meaning.txt:37`
- Content: `Whole: a drawing that did not come down to us. / Parts, in the order the labels reach us: / - A phrase about something being on this line / - A single bare letter / - A fragment about something coming out ...`
- What is missing: positions. Ten parts, zero placements. "In the order the labels reach us" is a reading order, not a spatial layout, and the three annotations attach to the whole rather than to any part, which is the taxonomy's named anti-pattern exactly.
- Suggested re-authoring: the labels themselves name a geometry, and ch25's M37 already demonstrates the fix on this same lost figure. Place the six placeable labels on a drawn frame, east at the left edge, the westward label (arrived as a country name) at the right edge, "the centre" at the circle's centre, "altitude and arc" on the curve, "on this line" on the tangent, and set the four unplaceable fragments outside the frame in a bin labelled as unplaceable. The honesty point the block wants to make becomes visible rather than asserted: the frame is mostly empty, and the empty parts are the argument.
