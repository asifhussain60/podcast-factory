### P0 (blocks ship)

#### SL-P3: Structure-vs-Description — 6 moments name a type and supply none of its commitments

`slide-deck-patterns.md` states, per type, what the source MUST specify. **Annotated Structure** requires the whole, parts named **with positions**, and one annotation per part — its named anti-pattern is "callouts without spatial relation." **Hierarchy** requires named levels and parent-child relations — its anti-pattern is a flat list. **Process Flow** requires nodes, transitions (with conditions if branching), and **start AND end states**. Each moment below declares a type and supplies none of it, so NotebookLM has nothing to draw and will render a bullet list.

**M24 — "Claims on the estate"** (the clearest failure in the deck)
- **File:** `slide-decks/ch19-deck-condolence-mourning-and-burial.txt:314`
- **Content (≤300 chars):** `Hierarchy. Claims on the estate, from the Book of Reports. / - Level 1: first claim / - Level 2: the shroud and its preparation / - Level 1: second claim / - Level 2: the debt / - Level 1: third claim / - Level 2: the bequest`
- **What's missing:** parent-child relations. "First claim / second claim / third claim" are ordinals, not levels, and each has exactly one child which is the item itself. Three Level-1 siblings under no parent is a ranked list wearing a hierarchy label — the "bullet-list-as-diagram" anti-pattern, and the same defect this challenger cited at ch17 M16.
- **Suggested re-authoring:** this is a precedence contest with a real loser, and the deck's own note already knows it ("a creditor with a valid claim waits behind a length of cloth, for money he will never see"). Render the contest, not the ranking: a process flow over a finite estate (estate → shroud taken out first, regardless of amount → remainder → debt → bequest) with the insolvency test case as the branch that makes the ordering bite, or a contrast pair of "what the shroud is owed" against "what the creditor is owed."
- **VERIFIED**

**M34 — "Who may be prayed over"**
