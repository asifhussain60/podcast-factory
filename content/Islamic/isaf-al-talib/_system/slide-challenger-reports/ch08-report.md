  3. **The `Reading:` line is an interpretive claim, not a structural one**, and it is about the Imam's method rather than about the chain — it would sit more naturally on M21, where the granted-premises argument actually runs.
- **Suggested Worker re-authoring (pick one):**
  1. **Deliver the promised generations.** Group the seven names into the transmission generations the header claims and state the edge semantics explicitly (see P2-3 — the arrow currently runs backward in time relative to every other chain in this deck). An isnad rendered as dated generations, with the Companion at the root and the reciting Imam at the leaf, is a real diagram and does work the audio cannot: it shows at a glance that the chain the Imam chose runs through Abbasid caliphs, which is the whole reason the preacher could not refuse it.
