#### SL-P3: Structure-vs-Description — 10 moments name a type and supply none of its commitments

The taxonomy in `slide-deck-patterns.md` states, per type, what the source MUST specify. **Annotated Structure** requires the whole, parts named **with positions**, and one annotation per part; its named anti-pattern is "callouts without spatial relation." **Process Flow** requires nodes, transitions, and start **and** end states; its anti-pattern is "numbered list pretending to be a flow." Nine of the ten moments below are typed Annotated Structure and supply no positions at all; the tenth is a Process Flow with no transitions. NotebookLM has nothing to draw from any of them and will render each as a text slide.

M22 reads the same four protections twice, once against the collector and once against the payer, with aligned rows and a shared row that names the asymmetry. M31 opposes the questioner's reading of the covenant to the answer's reading and lands on an exemption where an enforcement was sought. And M32 renders misdirected zakat as a loop that opens when the money leaves and closes only at the guardian, which is the chapter's governing conviction made into a shape.

The framing is the strongest part of the bundle. All five required H2s are present, the audience is named concretely, the body runs 247 words inside the 150–250 band, and four steering phrases sit inside the 3–5 band. Critically, **all four Visual Priorities map to structures that actually exist in the deck** — the concentric circles to M08, ten-against-seven to M18, the eight names in seven categories to M19, the misdirected-zakat loop to M32 — and the Core Principle's four named artefacts ("a containment, a ledger of names, a threshold table, an unclosed loop") map to M08, M19, M21 and M32 respectively. The prohibition list is specific to this chapter's risks (no stock photography of coins, herds or harvests) and the black-line-art constraint is a real steering commitment rather than a slogan. This is why SL-P2 passes without qualification.

Mechanically the bundle is clean: no em dashes in either file (verified by full-file scan), no inline phonetic parens, one H1, zero prose paragraphs over 100 words (the over-length blocks are all lists, tables or permitted verbatim blockquotes), Arabic retained in `⟪ar:…⟫` blocks with translation and citation beside them at L303 and L437, and the undated verse at L437 carries an explicit note that the source names no chapter or verse.

What blocks the bundle is the floor, not the ceiling, and it is one narrow habit repeated ten times: reaching for "Annotated structure" as a default label for a paragraph that has no spatial structure, then filling it with that paragraph's own clauses.
