#### SL-P3-a: M24's 2x2 quadrant labels contradict its own vertical axis — the deck's only internal contradiction

This is the single most mechanical defect in the report and the cheapest to fix, and it is new this
iteration (the prior deck had no 2x2 at all).

- **File:** `slide-decks/ch20-deck-zakat-obligation-and-intention.txt:324-334`
- **Content (≤300 chars):** `Axes: - Horizontal: what the thing does with value (holds it → produces it) / - Vertical: how the law treats it (exempt → assessed) // Quadrants: - Top-left, holds value and exempt: houses, jewelry... - Bottom-right, produces value and assessed: livestock that breed...`
- **What's wrong:** the vertical axis is written `(exempt → assessed)`. The deck's own convention,
  set by its other 2x2 at M33 (`spending ... (absent → present)` with `Top-right ... spending
  present`), puts the **second** term of the arrow at the **top**. Under that convention `exempt →
  assessed` places *assessed* at the top and *exempt* at the bottom. All four of M24's quadrant
  labels do the opposite: exempt items are labelled Top-left, assessed items Bottom-right. The
  horizontal axis is consistent (left = holds, right = produces); only the vertical is inverted.
  NotebookLM given this draws an axis label pointing one way and quadrant contents sitting the other,
  producing a figure whose axis contradicts its own contents. The taxonomy's 2x2 contract requires
  both axis ends AND quadrant assignment; supplying two that disagree is worse than omitting one.
- **Suggested Worker re-authoring:** one-line fix, but it must be a decision, not a coin flip.
  Either rewrite the vertical as `(assessed → exempt)`, or swap top/bottom in all four quadrant
  labels. **Pick whichever agrees with M33**, i.e. swap the quadrant labels so that assessed sits at
  the top — then both 2x2s in the deck read second-term-at-top, and the figure's populated diagonal
  runs bottom-left (holds + exempt: houses, jewelry) to top-right (produces + assessed: herds,
  crops, mines), with the two empty quadrants on the other diagonal. The moment's argument is
  untouched and it becomes one of the deck's strongest.
- **VERIFIED**

`slide-deck-patterns.md` states the Annotated Structure contract: **the whole, parts named _with
positions_, and one annotation per part**; its named anti-pattern is "Callouts without spatial
relation. Annotated structures need the spatial layout to do work." Eleven of the deck's twelve
annotated structures supply a whole and named parts and **no position for any part**. NotebookLM has
nothing to lay out and will render each as a text slide.

The deck proves this is a habit rather than a capability gap: **M01** (L5) is the same type and
supplies real positions ("Part 1, the first placement: near the opening"; "Part 2, the body
between"; "Part 3, the second placement: the final word of the chapter"), which is why M01 is one of
the deck's most memorable moments.
