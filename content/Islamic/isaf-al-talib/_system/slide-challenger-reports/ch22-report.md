Iteration 1 graded SL-P1 primarily on content-word overlap between a deck moment and its matching
audio paragraph, reporting 70.0% to 87.5% as evidence of restatement. That metric does not
discriminate. Measured on this deck against the same audio:

|---|---|---|---|

| **M26 the seven obligatory fasts (control)** | 101 | **76.2%** | **not restatement** |
| **M27 the seven sorted by imposer (control)** | 76 | **72.4%** | **not restatement** |
| **M14 the visibility matrix (control)** | 70 | **65.7%** | **not restatement** |

Three moments that unambiguously do visual work the prose cannot (a 7x4 grid, a 1/5/1 tree, a 4x4
grid with a visible none/nobody/identical stripe) score at or above the moments that fail. High
overlap is expected of every moment in this deliverable, because `slide-deck-format.md` defines the
deck source as a re-presentation of the same content at the same depth, not a summary. The
discriminator used here is the spec's own test: does the moment commit to an axis, a position, a
column, a level, a node or an edge that a spoken sentence cannot carry? The Worker should not chase
the overlap percentage down; doing so would strip attributions and depth the format spec requires.
