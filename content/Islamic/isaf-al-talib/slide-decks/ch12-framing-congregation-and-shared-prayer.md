# Slide Deck Framing: EP12 Congregation and Shared Prayer

## Audience
Asif's children and adult students of Ismaili fiqh who have heard the episode but never read Isaf al-Talib.

## Core Principle
The audio carries the reasoning and the admonition. Slides hold only what a spoken line cannot: placement, sequence, nesting. A slide that could be one narrated sentence is omitted.

## Visual Priorities
- The 2x2 of bodily nearness against lawful order. Name both axes, place the complete row top-right, the non-prostrating claimant bottom-left.
- The screened congregation as a branching flow, connected place against unconnected, ihraam the threshold that closes looking.
- The row as an annotated structure: completeness, continuity, a gap measured by one prostrating body.
- Concentric rings from intention outward through the five limits, seven duties, twelve sunnahs, to adhan and iqama.

## Prohibited Patterns
- No literal-text slides reprinting quotations or verses.
- No slides restating the narration or a section heading.
- No stock photographs, mosque imagery, or depictions of people.
- No bullet list dressed as a diagram.
- No colour fills, colour-coded categories, gradients, or dark backgrounds.

## Steering Phrases
- "Each slide must add visual information not present in the audio."
- "Render every slide as black line art on white, gray shading only where unavoidable."
- "Show the relationships between entities, not a list of them."
- "For the central tension, use a 2x2 and specify both axes."
- "Do not produce a slide titled 'Key Takeaways' or 'Overview'."
