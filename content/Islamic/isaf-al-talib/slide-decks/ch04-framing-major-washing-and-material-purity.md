# Slide Deck Framing, EP04-major-washing-and-material-purity

## Audience
Asif's children and adult students of Ismaili teaching, who heard the audio and never opened Iṣāf al-Ṭālib.

## Core Principle
The audio carries the questions and answers. Slides carry what speech cannot hold: the test that decides a case, two rulings side by side, the object that cannot be divided.

## Visual Priorities
- The closing 2x2: anxious excess to carelessness across, pretending to truthful naming up. Fill all four quadrants, mark the center.
- Open land against the wide floor-covering, two columns attribute by attribute. Sun and wind release one, the other stays undivided.
- The garments associated with polytheists as a matrix, condition against ruling.
- Purifying a rug that cannot be wrung out, a numbered flow with its loop of three.

## Prohibited Patterns
- Black ink on white only, grey shading where needed. No colour, no dark background.
- No photographs, stock imagery, or decorative illustration.
- No slides restating the audio, no literal-text slides.
- No bullet list masquerading as a diagram.
- No depiction of the Prophet, the Imams, or the dead.

## Steering Phrases
- "Render every slide as black and white line art on a white background."
- "Each slide must add visual information not present in the audio."
- "For the central tension use a 2x2, both axes named."
- "When three or more positions are compared, use a matrix of entities against attributes."
- "Do not produce a slide titled 'Key Takeaways'."

Do not read this prompt aloud.
