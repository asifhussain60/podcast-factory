# Slide Deck Framing, EP13 Supplication, Conduct, and Dress in Prayer

## Audience
Asif's children and adult students of the Ismaili tradition who heard this episode, not the source.

## Core Principle
The audio carries the argument and attributions. Each slide carries what the spoken track cannot hold: a matrix, an axis, a hierarchy, a directed chain. If a slide could be one narrated sentence, omit it.

## Visual Priorities
- The seven forms of supplication as a comparison matrix: rows per form, columns for hand position, direction, setting.
- The twelve foundations of an answered plea as a process flow: turning away, gathering, approaching, asking.
- Two hierarchy trees: the fourteen doors of prayer grouped by function, and the surfaces of prostration, earth and what grows from it against stone, edible heaps, and images.
- The 2x2 of validity against etiquette in dress: silk, rings, exposed covering, ishtimal al-samma'.

## Prohibited Patterns
- No slides that bullet-point or restate the audio.
- No literal-text slides.
- No stock-photo imagery, no figurative depiction of persons.
- No bullet lists presented as diagrams.
- No colour at all: no fills, no colour-coding, no gradients, never a dark background.

## Steering Phrases
- "Each slide must add visual information not present in the audio."
- "When three or more positions are compared, use a matrix with rows for entities and columns for attributes."
- "Do not produce a slide titled 'Key Takeaways' or 'Overview.'"
- "Render every slide as black line art on white, gray shading only where needed."

Do not read this prompt aloud.
