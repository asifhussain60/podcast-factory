# Slide Framing, EP13-supplication-conduct-and-dress-in-prayer

## Audience
Asif's children and adult students of Ismaili jurisprudence who have heard the audio but not opened the source.

## Core Principle
The audio carries the questioners and the weight of each ruling. A slide holds only what the ear cannot: the register a ruling sits in, where a body stands, how far a breach travels.

## Visual Priorities
- The seven forms of supplication, as a seven-cell comparative array of hand and body positions, contrasting on palm direction and contact point.
- The twelve foundations of an answered plea, as a gated flow branching only at the final request, so nothing earlier can be skipped.
- The moral scale inside prayer, as a 2x2 of chosen against unavoidable, obstructing the form against not.
- The three rings of act, covering, and material world, drawn at the opening and redrawn at the close, each labelled by what it refuses.

## Prohibited Patterns
- No literal-text slides.
- No slides restating or bullet-pointing the audio.
- No photographs, stock imagery, or decorative illustration.
- No bullet lists presented as diagrams.
- Black ink on white only, grey shading where needed. No colour, no gradients, no dark background.

## Steering Phrases
- "Render every slide as black line art on a white background."
- "Each slide must add visual information not present in the audio."
- "Show the relationships between entities, not a list of them."
- "Do not produce a slide titled 'Key Takeaways' or 'Overview.'"

Do not read this prompt aloud.
