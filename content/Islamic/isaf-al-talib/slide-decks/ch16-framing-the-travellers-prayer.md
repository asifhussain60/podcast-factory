# Slide Deck Framing, EP16 The Traveller's Prayer

## Audience
Asif's children, and adult students of the Ismaili tradition who know the episode but never opened a Tayyibi question-and-answer book.

## Core Principle
The audio carries the reasoning. The slides carry only what reasoning cannot hold in the head: circles of kinship, thresholds, and points where a ruling splits in two.

## Visual Priorities
- The 2x2 of Miles against Displacement, versus Outside the man against Inside him. Name both axes, place the cases, leave the Miles plus Inside quadrant empty.
- The circle of womb relatives, labelled twice: people a man may not marry, and places where he is never a stranger. Put the brother-in-law, wife's mother, and milk relation outside the line.
- The outbound gate against the inbound door, one threshold diagram, road running gate to doorstep.

## Prohibited Patterns
- No slides that bullet-point or restate the audio.
- No literal-text slides quoting the source as the whole slide.
- No stock-photo imagery: caravans, deserts, ships.
- No bullet list posing as a diagram.
- No colour: black ink on white, grey shading only where a fill is needed.

## Steering Phrases
- "Each slide must add visual information not present in the audio."
- "Show the relationships between entities, not a list of them."
- "Every slide is a diagram or it does not exist."
- "Render every slide as black and white line art on a white background. Never a dark or coloured background."
