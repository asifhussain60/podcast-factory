# Slide Framing EP21, Zakat on Wealth, Livestock, Crops, Fitr

## Audience
Asif's children and adult students of Ismaili fiqh who know the audio but not the source.

## Core Principle
The audio carries the reasoning and the caveats. Slides add only what speech cannot hold: an order of claims, a rising scale, a split rate, a boundary in time.

## Visual Priorities
- The order claims leave an estate after death: zakat, Hajj, khums, expiation, al-Sharwa, heirs last and possibly empty handed.
- Increase that arrived on its own against increase the owner paid for: grazing animal against fed animal, river-watered against bucket-watered field.
- The morning of al-Fitr as one time axis, dawn, sunrise and the Fitr prayer marked, the payment changing category at each.

## Prohibited Patterns
- No slides that bullet point or restate the audio.
- No literal text slides reprinting quotations.
- No stock photography of coins, camels or fields.
- No bullet list dressed up as a diagram.
- No colour: black line art on white, grey shading only in tables, never a dark background.

## Steering Phrases
- "Each slide must add visual information not present in the audio."
- "Do not produce 'general illustration' slides. Every slide must have a named diagram type."
- "When two positions are contrasted, show them side by side as columns, attribute by attribute."
- "Render everything as black line art on a white background. No colour fills, no gradients, no photographs."
