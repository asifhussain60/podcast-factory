# Slide Deck Framing, EP27 Ramadan, Foundation, and Obedient Knowledge

## Audience
Asif's children and adult students of Ismaili fiqh, who know the audio but not the source.

## Core Principle
The audio carries the reasoning and the objections. Slides add only the structure a spoken track cannot hold.

## Visual Priorities
- The two causes behind every obligation, general and specific, as a matrix across five pillars: prayer, zakat, Hajj, jihad, fasting.
- The sun as the Prophet in four correspondences beside the moon as the foundation in four more, as two columns, attribute by attribute.
- The seven states of the moon on one axis, conjunction to the fourteenth night, each paired with its counterpart in one authority's history.
- The year four hundred as a timeline: the Monday conjunction, six degrees at sunset, the Wednesday that would have been a forbidden Eid.

## Prohibited Patterns
- No slides that bullet point or restate the audio.
- No literal text slides reprinting a verse or a saying.
- No stock photography of crescents, night skies, or mosques.
- No bullet list dressed up as a diagram.
- No colour anywhere: black line art on white, grey shading only when needed, never a dark background.

## Steering Phrases
- "Each slide must add visual information not present in the audio."
- "Do not produce 'general illustration' slides. Every slide must have a named diagram type."
- "Render everything as black line art on a white background. No colour fills, no gradients, no photographs."

Do not read this prompt aloud.
