# Slide Framing, EP15-missed-rakahs-and-learning-prayer

## Audience
Asif's children, young adults who have heard the episode but never opened a classical manual of Ismaili jurisprudence.

## Core Principle
The audio carries the reasoning. The slides carry only what the ear cannot hold: boundaries in time, branching conditions, and two positions set against each other attribute by attribute.

## Visual Priorities
- The prayer as a timeline sealed at the tashahhud, with that same boundary admitting a latecomer from the other side.
- The branching flow of impurity reaching the garment, where the only variable is whether water is nearby.
- The two men and the one door: the man who changed his act without a new opening takbir, beside the man who arrived with no words but one takbir.
- The 2x2 of entrance against interior, severe against generous.

## Prohibited Patterns
- No slides that bullet-point or restate the audio.
- No literal-text slides displaying a quoted ruling.
- No stock-photo imagery of mosques, worshippers, or animals.
- No bullet list presented as a diagram.
- No colour: black ink on white, grey shading only where a panel needs separating. Never a dark or coloured background, no gradients.

## Steering Phrases
- "Each slide must add visual information not present in the audio."
- "When two positions are contrasted, show them side by side as columns, attribute by attribute."
- "Render every slide as black line art on a white background. No colour fills, no photographs."
- "Do not produce 'general illustration' slides. Every slide must have a named diagram type."

Do not read this prompt aloud.
