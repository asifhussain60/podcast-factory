# Friday, Eid, and Prayer Corrections

## Where this episode opens

Diagram type: Comparison matrix. The three subjects and their tests.

Rows: Friday prayer, the two Eid prayers, the prostration of omission.
Columns: what it tests | what it refuses | its governing question.
- Friday prayer: public authority and lawful attendance | that Friday is a private noon prayer with a larger audience | is the community gathered around the right public center.
- The two Eid prayers: outward festivity against inward disclosure | that Eid is a decorative festival after fasting or after sacrifice | does celebration reveal what was hidden.
- The prostration of omission: the border between a real error and an unsettled mind | that the prostration patches every anxious doubt | is this an established defect or a passing uncertainty.

Cell commitment: all three ask one question. Under pressure, how does the community know where the valid act is.

Reading: each threshold puts one three-way verdict to the act. A defect that breaks it, a defect that is repaired, or not a defect at all.

## Friday's Gathered Order

Diagram type: Annotated structure. The three-mile measure as one line.

Whole: the distance between two congregations.
The measuring line: three miles.
Parts, with position and ruling:
- Three miles or more, one city or two. Position: beyond the line. Ruling: Friday may be held in each.
- Less than three miles, a single city. Position: inside the line. Ruling: only one holds the Friday prayer.
- Less than three miles, two cities, the case the questioner raises. Position: inside the line. Ruling: not in both, whether in one city or two.

Annotation: the measure overrides the city boundary, so Friday is not multiplied because people can gather in more than one place.

Diagram type: Contrast pair. Rightful following, against protective accommodation.

Column B: Following under taqiyya
- Who stands in front: a figure of injustice whose presence is endured
- What the follower gives: presence, not recognition
- What the standing establishes: nothing about the figure's right to lead

Excluded from both: making a Nasibi or a Haruri one's imam.

Shared row: contempt for the sermon does not become casual withdrawal, and attendance does not become surrender to every leader.

## Friday's Gathered Order, Continued: Pulpit, Silence, and the Two Lights

Diagram type: Annotated structure. The Friday act in its parts.

Whole: the Friday prayer as a public form.
Parts, in performed order, with annotation:
- The Imam ascends the pulpit. Annotation: the muezzin before the pulpit gives the adhan after the ascent.
- The preacher sits in the place from which he delivers the sermon. Annotation: the binding adhan for the one bound to Friday is the adhan given at that sitting.
- The preacher greets the people. Annotation: silence becomes obligatory from this moment.
- The first sermon. Annotation: praise, exhortation, supplication, and a statement from the Lord of Manifestation, which is warning.
- The sitting between the two sermons. Annotation: the separation between the upper light and the lower light.
- The second sermon. Annotation: the sermon comes before the prayer, never after it.
- The prayer, two rak'ahs. Annotation: recitation aloud distinguishes Friday from the noon prayer of other days.
- The preacher's support throughout. Annotation: he leans on his sword in the book of narrations, and on a staff if he does not draw it.

Annotation on the whole: the sound is not floating in the city. It is attached to pulpit and sermon.

Diagram type: Contrast pair. Friday prayer against the noon prayer of other days.

- First rak'ah: Surah al-Jumu'ah after the Opening

Shared row: the sermon is not a preliminary speech before the real prayer. The public word does what bodily prayer ordinarily does.

Diagram type: Comparison matrix. The ceremony's contingencies.

Rows: the cases the source puts.
Columns: the ruling | the deciding fact.
- The two officers accept the pledge of allegiance at the appointed time: the transaction is not cancelled | it does not compete with the prayer's time.
- The imam is thirsty on the pulpit: he may drink | bodily need does not break the ceremony.
- He delivers the khutbah, descends, performs ablution: he may repeat the sermon or proceed to pray, both rewarded | the sermon delivered is not voided.
- Distress interrupts the sitting between the two sermons: the ruling turns on whether that sitting was completed | completion, not the distress.
- A man enters the mosque door, disrupting sermon and prayer: the preacher owns him, he has become his follower, and may not come before his master or pray without him | entry into the Friday space during the public act.
- Whoever remains in another mosque that day: he has erred, and if deliberate he is penalized and commanded to perform the Friday ghusl | the call is given only in the mosque.

Cell commitment: the ceremony is kept from becoming brittle, but not shapeless. Ownership is not softened into informality.

Diagram type: Process flow. The collapse of the congregation.

Start: the imam delivers the Friday sermon.
  Branch 1, the people leave and one man remains: that man prays four rak'ahs.
  Branch 2, he prays two rak'ahs with them, then all leave and he remains alone:
    Condition, he opened the prayer and they departed before he bowed: he prays four rak'ahs of noon prayer.
  Branch 3, after finishing the second rak'ah he rises and then sits back down: he says the testimony and performs the two prostrations of forgetfulness.
  Branch 4, a man sleeps while waiting and his purity does not allow him to join the imam in time: he must repeat it four times.
End states, two only: the act is still Friday and an internal error is repaired, or the public condition has failed and the act has become Dhuhr.

Reading: either the imam erred within the act, or the act lost the condition that made it Friday. Sleep gives no second Friday.

> Is there anyone who repents? Is there anyone who seeks forgiveness and is forgiven? Is there anyone who asks for something? So answer the call of God.
Attribution: the call of the two angels at the final hour of night.

## Eid's Revealed Interior

Diagram type: Comparison matrix. The opening Eid questions.

Rows: the questions put to the source.
Columns: the ruling | the reason supplied.
- Does Tashriq refer to the Eid prayer, given that the traveler is not obligated to attend the mosque except in Egypt: Tashriq takes place only in Egypt, meaning the mosque of a city | the two Eid prayers are performed there at sunrise, and such a worshipper is called radiant.
- Someone forgets one or more of the Eid takbirs: he completes them before bowing | the repair is fixed to the stage of prayer, not to unease.
- He has passed bowing and is certain he omitted them: he performs the two prostrations of omission | certainty, not suspicion, triggers the repair.
- How many takbirs run from 'Arafah to the last day of Tashriq, when the questioner's country adds one to make twenty-four: twenty-three | take what you have received and be grateful.

Cell commitment: a forgotten takbir is repaired by place in the prayer, never by anxiety.

Diagram type: Annotated structure. The twenty-three takbirs.

Whole: the twenty-three takbirs of the days from 'Arafah to the last day of Tashriq.
Parts, with annotation:
- The five pronouncements of the first intentions. Annotation: aligned with the five prayers.
- The spiritual ten hudud. Annotation: one of the counted sets.
- The ten corporeal hudud. Annotation: its counterpart.
- The male of the presiding speaker and his guardian. Annotation: ordered authority entering the count.
Annotation on the whole: even a festival takbir count carries worship's inner grammar, and is not extended by local practice to twenty-four.

Diagram type: Comparison matrix. Eid's boundaries, and the conditions the source declines to create.

Rows: the cases.
Columns: the ruling | the condition refused.
- Eid is prayed while a makeup of Fajr is still owed: valid, not repeated | the makeup does not invalidate Eid.
- The same with the funeral prayer: the same ruling | a neighbouring duty is not a precondition.
- Eid is prayed before Zakat al-Fitr is distributed: no repetition, and it may be given afterward | the order of the two duties is not a validity test.
- The ghusl for the two Eids: a Sunnah from daybreak until the Eid prayer ends | a valid excuse permits praying before the time ends.
- The abridgment of the Musannaf on the same ghusl: an annual Sunnah, from dawn until the prayer lapses | the same window, second authority.

Cell commitment: the source is not careless about order, and refuses to make every neighbouring duty an invalidating condition.

Attribution: the qunut preserved in the transmitted reports.

Shared row: the law differentiates the feasts rather than making them one holiday category. The opening of sermons is proof, not ornament.

Column B: The one who gains nothing

Reading: the takbirs, the ghusl, the sunrise prayer, and the city congregation all point toward emergence. The feast is worship at that point, not release from worship into festivity.

## Certainty And Omission

Diagram type: Named-axis 2x2. The governing test for whether the prostration of omission applies.

Axes:
- Horizontal: the worshipper's state regarding the lapse (Uncertain → Established)
- Vertical: where the prayer stands when the lapse is recognised (Structure intact → Structure failed)

Quadrants:
- Top-right, Established and structure intact: the prostration of omission applies, because the one who omits is certain of what he omitted and the act can still carry the repair.
- Top-left, Uncertain and structure intact: nothing is required, or he acts on certainty and continues, because the doubter is not certain of what he doubts.
- Bottom-right, Established and structure failed: the prayer is repeated, because a missing opening takbir is not a repairable slip.
- Bottom-left, Uncertain and structure failed: he restarts, and so does the imam, because nothing is secured.

Reasoning for the axes: feeling decides neither. The horizontal is decided by establishment, the vertical by which pillars were secured.

> If it is established that he has indeed committed an omission, he prostrates for it; otherwise he does not.
Attribution: the answer given to the one who stands in for another and does not know all the places requiring prostration of omission.

Diagram type: Comparison matrix. Invalidating, repairable, and unrequiring lapses.

Rows: the cases the source puts.
Columns: the verdict | the deciding fact.
- The opening takbir is not pronounced, or is left deliberately or ignorantly: repeat the prayer | it is a pillar, and its absence is not repairable.
- He forgets to open the prayer after every two rak'ahs in four and six rak'ah prayers, without sitting after each pair: invalidated | the structure itself has failed.
- He adds an extra bowing without having sat for the tashahhud: repeat the prayer | the addition falls before the order is secured.
- He adds a bowing or prostration after sitting for the tashahhud and completing two rak'ahs: he has done so in order | the completed pair secures the sequence.
- He adds an extra prostration: complete the prayer and perform the two prostrations | the addition is repairable.
- He forgets a prostration and remembers before the salaam: he prostrates | the act has not closed.
- He forgets a prostration and has already left the prayer: no recourse | the act has closed.
- He recites the obligatory prayer in reverse order: no prostrations of omission | not a stipulated repair.
- He realizes after the salaam that he prayed only three rak'ahs of Dhuhr: he prays the missed rak'ah, recites tashahhud, gives salaam, and performs the two prostrations | a missing unit is completed before the repair.

Cell commitment: prayer is not repaired because the worshipper feels sorry, and not invalidated because an extra movement occurred.

Diagram type: Hierarchy. The lapses the source declines to legislate.

- Level 1: No explicit text requires a prostration
  - Level 2: The first tashahhud is neglected
    - Level 3: The final tashahhud is recited and he remembers before the salaam
  - Level 2: A single surah is repeated after the Opening
    - Level 3: No stipulation covers it
  - Level 2: Recitation is neglected after the first rak'ah and a surah recited from the beginning in the second
    - Level 3: Neither stipulated nor deliberate, answered to the scholars of al-Hind and the preachers of Yemen
- Level 1: The repair is not multiplied
  - Level 2: One obligation is omitted
    - Level 3: Two prostrations cover it
  - Level 2: Three obligations are omitted
    - Level 3: Two prostrations still cover them
- Level 1: The principle beneath both
  - Level 2: Lack of stipulation is not filled by devotional nervousness
    - Level 3: More omissions do not mean repeated repair rites

Diagram type: Contrast pair. What the Imam carries and what he does not.

Column A: Carried by the Imam
- Pillars neglected while following: the Imam bears responsibility
- The follower's position: he is carried through the lapse
- The council's seventh place: whoever follows an imam bears no responsibility for omission

Column B: Not carried by the Imam
- The bowing: excepted, and the follower must not neglect it
- The opening takbir: excepted, and the follower must not neglect it
- The follower's position: these are the acts that make his participation real

Shared row: the earlier teaching on following without fracture is applied, not reopened.

Diagram type: Contrast pair. Where the intention lives.

Column A: The heart
- What it does: contracts the intention
- Status: the place where intention resides
- If the tongue is silent while the heart holds it: the action is valid
- Authority: repeated in the questions to the scholars of India and the letters to the preachers of Yemen

Column B: The tongue
- What it does: expresses what the heart contracted
- Status: an expression, not the place where intention lives
- If the imam deliberately expresses his intention verbally: his prayer is void
- If he does not say it deliberately: it stands

Shared row: deeds are accepted only with intentions, and the source still refuses to make verbal declaration the essence of intention.

Diagram type: Process flow. The follower's intention against the Imam's.

Start: a man praying behind an Imam makes it clear he intends to be a follower.
  Down to: the Imam gives the salaam of the dhikr while not intending the prayer.
  Branch: if the Imam intends the prayer on his behalf, it is so and the prayer stands; if not, the prayer lapses.
End state: the follower's declared intention does not by itself constitute the act.

## Certainty And Omission, Continued: The Seven Places and the Twelve Doubts

Diagram type: Contrast pair. Omission against doubt.

Column A: Omission
- The worshipper's state: certain of what he omitted
- What occurred: a known lack
- The repair: the prostration of omission, after the salaam
- Why the two prostrations are so named: they force the devil's nose down when he causes forgetfulness

Column B: Doubt
- The worshipper's state: not certain of what he doubts
- What occurred: bewilderment, which resembles omission without being it
- The repair: sometimes a prostration, often nothing at all
- What it may never do: displace a certainty already held

Shared row: doubt is said to be rooted in the prostration of omission because it causes bewilderment, and many cases resemble omission without being it.

> In the name of Allah, and Allah is great, and I bear witness that there is no god but Allah, and may Allah bless Muhammad and his family.
Attribution: the brief testimony recited after the prostration of omission.

> Whoever is upon certainty and is then visited by doubt should proceed upon his certainty, for doubt does not displace certainty or diminish it.
Attribution: the line attributed to Amir al-Mu'minin.

Diagram type: Hierarchy. The seven places of omission named by the Mustansiriyya council.

- Level 1: Places where the two prostrations are performed
  - Level 2: The prostration itself is neglected
    - Level 3: He remembers it and performs the two prostrations
  - Level 2: The first tashahhud is forgotten and he stands for the third rak'ah
    - Level 3: If he has not knelt, he sits, testifies, and prostrates at the salaam
  - Level 2: The omission exceeds the first tashahhud and accumulates
    - Level 3: He prostrates, completes the prayer most completely, and the two prostrations are upon him
  - Level 2: The second tashahhud is neglected before the salutation
    - Level 3: He must perform the two prostrations
  - Level 2: He inattentively prays only two rak'ahs
    - Level 3: He prays two more after them and performs the two prostrations
  - Level 2: He inattentively rises from the final tashahhud to add a fifth rak'ah
    - Level 3: He must perform the two prostrations
- Level 1: The seventh place, where no prostration falls on him
  - Level 2: He follows an imam
    - Level 3: He bears no responsibility; the Imam bears that

Annotation: the repair is small, embodied, decisive, and does not reopen the prayer unless the structure has failed.

Diagram type: Comparison matrix. The twelve situations of doubt.

Rows: the doubts named by the council.
Columns: the verdict | the reason.
- Doubts whether he has bowed, while still in prayer: he bows, and the prostration is upon him | the pillar is still reachable.
- Doubts one rak'ah or two: he acts on the lesser certainty | the lower count is held.
- Doubts two or three: he acts on certainty | the same rule again.
- Doubts three or four: he sits and performs the two prostrations | secured count, then repair.
- Doubts two or four: he gives salaam, prays two rak'ahs standing, and performs the two prostrations | the gap is filled, then repaired.
- Doubts the opening takbir, after bowing: he continues, nothing required | the stage has passed.
- Doubts whether he recited, after bowing: nothing required | the stage has passed.
- Doubts whether he bowed, after prostrating: nothing required | the stage has passed.
- Doubts the first prostration, after the second: nothing required | the stage has passed.
- Doubts prostrating, after the tashahhud: nothing required | the stage has passed.
- Doubts something after the salutation: nothing required | the act has closed.
- Doubts and does not know what he has prayed: he acts on certainty and performs the two prostrations after the salutation | certainty remains the footing.

Cell commitment: not how troubling the doubt feels, but whether the doubted act is still reachable at the prayer's current stage.

Diagram type: Process flow. The fifth rak'ah, branching on two facts.

Start: a man rises at the fifth position.

    Path B, he did not: he sits, gives the testimony, and performs the two prostrations of omission.
  Branch on the second fact, when did memory return:
    Path A, he remembered after bowing, having witnessed the fourth: his prayer is sound and the additional rak'ah becomes voluntary.
    Path B, he did not witness the fourth and did not sit the length of the testimony: he repeats the prayer.
End state: the same outward fact carries four rulings.
Attribution: the Mukhtasar al-Musannaf.

Reading: whether the fourth was secured, whether the testimony-length sitting occurred, and when memory returned are what decide.

## What this episode lands

Diagram type: Comparison matrix. The three movements and their answers.

Rows: Friday, Eid, omission.
Columns: what it asked | what it answered | the edge it tested.
- Friday: whether the community is gathered around the right public center | the center is fixed by measure, by authority, and by visible order | the public sermon, the second congregation, the unjust preacher.
- Eid: whether celebration reveals the hidden meaning of fasting and sacred emergence | the feast is worship at the point of emergence | the Eid takbir, the city obligation, the setting that refuses congregation.
- Omission: whether an established defect can be told from a passing uncertainty | the defect is repaired, the failed structure repeated, the uncertainty answered by certainty | the missed tashahhud, the doubtful rak'ah, the unspoken intention.

Cell commitment: all three are disciplined worship under pressure, and all three are answered by measure.

Diagram type: Visual metaphor. A balance beam.

Abstract relation: how worship is preserved under pressure.
Metaphor: a balance beam with two loaded pans.
Element assignments:
- The left pan: private improvisation, where the worshipper arranges the act to suit himself
- The right pan: frightened repetition, where every unease reopens the act
- The centre, held: ordered action, carried by authority, by meaning, and by certainty
- The fulcrum: measure
Reading: prayer's summons, shape, leadership, time, attention, and material boundaries are tested at their hardest edges, and the answer is the same at each.
