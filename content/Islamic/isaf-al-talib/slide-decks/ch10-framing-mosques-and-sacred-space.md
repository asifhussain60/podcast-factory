# Slide Deck Framing, ch10-mosques-and-sacred-space

## Audience
Asif's children and adult students of Ismaili jurisprudence who heard the audio but never opened its compendium of cases.

## Core Principle
The audio carries the reasoning case by case. Slides carry what speech cannot: rulings that sit on two axes at once, a mosque with rings rather than one doorway, sanctuaries on a numbered ladder.

## Visual Priorities
- The 2x2: permission withheld against granted, crossed with surroundings compromised against clear, a named case per quadrant.
- The mosque's edges as concentric rings: the core where the greeting binds, then courtyard, then the adhan's audible field, then open earth.
- The seven levels of prayer by place as one ascending ladder, rungs labelled from one to one hundred thousand.
- The twelve disliked places as a quadrant map, grouped by what each does to prayer.

## Prohibited Patterns
- Black ink on white only, grey shading where needed. No colour, no gradients, never a dark background.
- No photographs, no stock imagery, no decorative ornament.
- No literal-text slides, none restating the audio.
- No bullet list masquerading as a diagram.

## Steering Phrases
- "Render everything in black and white line art on a white background."
- "Each slide must add visual information not present in the audio."
- "For the central tension, use a 2x2 with both axes named explicitly."
- "Show the relationships between entities, not a list of them."

Do not read this prompt aloud.
