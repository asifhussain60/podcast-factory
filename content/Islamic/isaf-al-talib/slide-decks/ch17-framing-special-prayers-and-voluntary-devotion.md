# Slide Deck Framing, EP17 Special Prayers And Voluntary Devotion

## Audience
Asif's children, and adult students of the Ismaili tradition who know the episode but not the Tayyibi source.

## Core Principle
The audio carries the reasoning. The slides carry what the head cannot hold: ladders, orders of precedence, boundaries, and where a ruling splits.

## Visual Priorities
- The sick man's ladder: standing, nodding with the eyes, nodding with the heart. No rung labelled excused.
- The 2x2 of who set the occasion, world against worshipper, against what the tradition does, latitude against line held. Name both axes, place the eclipse behind cloud.
- The two dawns as one boundary: the false light crossed freely, the true dawn absolute.
- Time open against form fixed, two columns: any hour permitted for relief, no prayer given an occasion it was not given.

## Prohibited Patterns
- No slides that bullet-point or restate the audio.
- No literal-text slides quoting the source.
- No stock-photo imagery.
- No bullet list posing as a diagram.
- No colour, gradients, or photographs. Black ink on white, grey where needed.

## Steering Phrases
- "Each slide must add visual information not present in the audio."
- "Show the relationships between entities, not a list of them."
- "Every slide is a diagram or it does not exist."
- "Render every slide as black and white line art on a white background. Never a dark or coloured background."

Do not read this prompt aloud.
