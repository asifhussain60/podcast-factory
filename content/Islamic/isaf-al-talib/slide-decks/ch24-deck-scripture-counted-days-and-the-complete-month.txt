# A Fast Begun Two Days Early

## Where this episode picks up

Annotated structure. Whole: one chapter laid along a single horizontal length, carrying three documents.

- Far left, the closing edge: the tail of the previous reply. A verse of poetry, a demand put back to the other side, a request for a courteous answer, a blessing.
- Centre, the joint: a short attached message on the inner meaning of the eclipse prayer, which looks like a different subject.
- Far right, the opening edge: a new epistle, addressed to a named man about a named year and a named embarrassment, running several chapters.
- Annotation, centre: the eclipse material sits beside the fast because the fast is where the moon's timetable becomes a legal question.
- Cross axis beneath all three: the subject never changes. It is always when the month starts.

Comparison matrix. Three voices carry the chapter.

Columns: voice | what it supplies | condition of its text | share of the chapter

## The Moon's Own Timetable, the eclipse that keeps an appointment

Blockquote group. The eclipse message, with its damage named.

> Allah, Blessed and Exalted, when He created creation, decreed among them wonders.
> Attribution: our master Zayd ibn Muhammad, in the message attached to the hidden interpretation of the eclipse prayer.

> the first to believe ended it, and made all that was created in the first age a proof for the people of the last age.
> Attribution: the same message. The first half of the clause is broken beyond recovery. The second is clear and load bearing, and is reproduced rather than reconstructed.

Annotated structure. Whole: one lunar month, drawn as a ring of thirty numbered nights.

- Position 14, marked heavily: the decreed position of the covering. He made the eclipse of the moon a covering for it, and decreed that its covering falls on the fourteenth night.
- Position 13, marked lightly, adjacent: the occasional alternate.
- Positions 1 to 12 and 15 to 30, unmarked: nights on which the covering is not appointed.
- Weighting: the majority of eclipses occur on the fourteenth, so the heavy mark carries the rule and the light mark the exception.
- Annotation at the centre: a decreed date, not one noted after the fact.
- Annotation outside the ring, left: the eclipse as dread. The sky breaking its own rules, the one occasion the law answers with a prayer, as the earlier teaching on the register of dread established. An accident argues for no one.
- Annotation outside the ring, right: the eclipse as appointment. A covering, decreed, falling on a fixed night. All of this contains signs and arguments for Allah, and the first age was made a proof for the people of the last age.

Comparison matrix. The two figures given for the moon's absence.

Columns: what it states | reused by its own sentence | matches the observed moon | how this chapter handles it

- Twenty-eight nights: the moon is absent and hidden from the world for twenty-eight nights of every month. Not reused. No. Left standing and named as damaged, because a repaired number would be this chapter's invention rather than its source's claim.
- Two days and two nights: absent and concealed for forty-eight hours, then reappearing after them. Used and repeated. Yes. Carried forward as given.

Shared row: the two figures do not agree, the contradiction sits inside one sentence, and nothing says what the twenty-eight belonged to.

Visual metaphor. What survives the damage.

- Abstract relation: a body whose positions are fixed in advance, and a faculty whose only office is to find where on that order it now is.
- Metaphor: a printed timetable on a wall, and a reader standing before it.
- Element assignments:
  - The timetable: the covering on the fourteenth night, the absence running a fixed span, the reappearance after it.
  - The printer: Allah, Blessed and Exalted, who decreed among His creation wonders.
  - The reader's eye: how you find out where the moon is on it, never the hand that wrote a line of it.
- Reading: the moon has a timetable of its own, and a decree can be known in advance, which is what the first half of this section said with a preposition.

## The Necessary Message on Fasting, the diagnosis before the proof

Named-axis 2x2. The dispute, organised on the two axes that carve it.

Axes:
- Horizontal: what the month's start is timed by. Left end, a human sighting. Right end, the moon's emergence.
- Vertical: where the answer is sought. Bottom end, individual observation. Top end, referral to the one whom Allah has made obligatory to obey.

Quadrants:
- Top right, emergence and referral: the community of the year four hundred. The event is the moon's coming out, and the way to learn when it came out is to refer the question to the address it has.
- Bottom left, sighting and individual observation: the people of the outward. The month begins when somebody catches it, each town answering for itself. The position the epistle answers.
- Top left, sighting and referral: authority consulted only to ratify an eye. The horizon still decides and the authority signs.
- Bottom right, emergence and individual observation: empty, on the letter's account. Granting the emergence as the event and then refusing the one who can say when it occurred leaves a person holding a fact he cannot reach.

Process flow. The fall, with its branch drawn.

- Start: an authority exists and is available, and the equipment of discernment and insight, reason and understanding, is in hand.
- Step: the steed of passion is mounted. A chosen mount. Someone climbs onto it.
- Step: the causes of personal opinion overwhelm them. Personal opinion is a cause of the overwhelming, not a harmless alternative.
- Step: the devil gains hold and they follow him. Had the equipment been in use, his machinations would have been repelled.
- Branch:
  - Path A, the many: they followed him. End state, disagreement as a permanent condition.
  - Path B, the exception: all except a company of true believers. End state, those whom Allah excepts in His Book.
- Annotation on the two ends: agreement is an exemption, and the exemption is granted rather than achieved.

- Inlet: arrogance toward the people of truth, desire and contention.
- Inlet: they became like beasts in their disputes, hesitating in their blindness, running without discernment or insight.
