# Slide Deck Framing, EP05-foods-cleanliness-and-everyday-purity

## Audience
Asif's children and adult students of the Ismaili tradition who have heard the audio but never read Isaf al-Talib.

## Core Principle
The audio carries the reasoning and the historical caution. The slides carry only what speech cannot hold in a straight line: thresholds, contact conditions, chains of authority, and the reversal Ja'far ibn Sulayman names.

## Visual Priorities
- The wet-versus-dry contact rule as a named 2x2, contact state against purity of the thing touched, only the wet-and-impure quadrant transmitting.
- The droppings threshold as a graduated scale, one grain and two removable, three a hard stop.
- The circumcision reversal as a 2x2, girls against boys, hastened against delayed, the commanded diagonal opposite the practiced one.
- Circumcision privacy as concentric circles, child, practitioner, household women, sealed perimeter.

## Prohibited Patterns
- No slides that bullet-point or restate the audio.
- No literal-text slides printing a quoted ruling.
- No stock-photo imagery of food, hair, markets, or people.
- No bullet list presented as a diagram.
- No colour, and never a dark background.

## Steering Phrases
- "Each slide must add visual information not present in the audio."
- "Do not produce 'general illustration' slides. Every slide must have a named diagram type."
- "Show the relationships between entities, not a list of them."
- "Render everything as black line art on white, gray fills only, no colour."
- "Do not produce a slide titled 'Key Takeaways'."
