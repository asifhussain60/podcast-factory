# Slide Deck Framing, EP02-ranks-loyalty-and-communal-affection

## Audience
Asif's children and adult students of Ismaili teaching, who heard the audio, not the book.

## Core Principle
The audio carries the sayings. Slides carry what speech cannot hold at once: where a rank sits between its edges, which right survives when faith is absent, what bounds praise.

## Visual Priorities
- The 2x2 that makes praise lawful: speech about the Ahl al-Bayt, restrained to expansive, crossed with confession of their servanthood. Reason every quadrant.
- The 2x2 on concealment: visibility of loyalty crossed with inward firmness, permitted and forbidden alike from outside.
- The seven kinds of neighbor as a filled matrix, neighborliness against kinship against faith.
- The hudud as one descending tree, Speaker to Respondent, twelve proofs beneath.

## Prohibited Patterns
- Black ink on white only, grey where needed. No colour, gradient, or dark background.
- No photographs, stock imagery, decoration.
- No slides restating the audio, no literal-text slides.
- No bullet list posing as a diagram.
- No depiction of the Imams or the Prophet. Diagram the order.

## Steering Phrases
- "Render every slide as black and white line art on a white background."
- "Each slide must add visual information not present in the audio."
- "For the central tension, use a 2x2 with both axes specified."
- "When three or more positions are compared, use a matrix."
- "Do not produce an 'Overview' or 'Key Takeaways' slide."

Do not read this prompt aloud.
