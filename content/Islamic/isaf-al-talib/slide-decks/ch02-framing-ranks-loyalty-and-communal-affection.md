# Slide Deck Framing, EP02-ranks-loyalty-and-communal-affection

## Audience
Asif's children and adult students of Ismaili teaching, who heard the audio and have never opened Iṣāf al-Ṭālib.

## Core Principle
The audio carries the sayings. Slides carry what speech cannot hold: where a rank sits, which right survives when faith is absent, and the boundary that makes praise lawful.

## Visual Priorities
- The 2x2 that makes praise lawful: speech about the Ahl al-Bayt, restrained to expansive, crossed with confession of their servanthood, absent to present. Place all four quadrants.
- The hudud as one descending tree, Speaker down to Respondent, the twelve proofs beneath.
- The seven kinds of neighbor as a matrix, neighborliness against kinship against faith, the first column never empty.
- Physical parents beside religious parents, column by column, layered not ranked.

## Prohibited Patterns
- Black ink on white only, grey shading where needed. No colour, no gradient, no dark background.
- No photographs, stock imagery, or decorative illustration.
- No slides restating the audio, no literal-text slides.
- No bullet list masquerading as a diagram.
- No depiction of the Imams or the Prophet. Diagram the order, never the persons.

## Steering Phrases
- "Render every slide as black and white line art on a white background."
- "Each slide must add visual information not present in the audio."
- "For the central tension use a 2x2, both axes named."
- "When three or more positions are compared, use a matrix of entities against attributes."
- "Do not produce a slide titled 'Key Takeaways'."

Do not read this prompt aloud.
