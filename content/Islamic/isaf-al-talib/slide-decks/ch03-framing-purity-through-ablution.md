# Slide Deck Framing, EP03-purity-through-ablution

## Audience
Asif's children and adult students of Ismaili teaching, who heard the audio and never opened Iṣāf al-Ṭālib.

## Core Principle
The audio carries the rulings and sayings. Slides carry what speech cannot hold: the order of an act, the threshold that decides a case, the two waters side by side.

## Visual Priorities
- The 2x2 that decides omission: Sunnah to obligation across, forgetful to knowing up. Fill all four quadrants.
- The seven obligations as a numbered flow, pure water and intention before any limb, order as the seventh.
- The judgment of water as a matrix, each case against threshold, characteristic, and trace.
- Outward water beside inner water: visible filth against doubt and ignorance.

## Prohibited Patterns
- Black ink on white only, grey shading where needed. No colour, no dark background.
- No photographs, stock imagery, or decorative illustration.
- No slides restating the audio, no literal-text slides.
- No bullet list masquerading as a diagram.
- No depiction of the Prophet, the Imams, or bodily acts of purification.

## Steering Phrases
- "Render every slide as black and white line art on a white background."
- "Each slide must add visual information not present in the audio."
- "For the central tension use a 2x2, both axes named."
- "When three or more positions are compared, use a matrix of entities against attributes."
- "Do not produce a slide titled 'Key Takeaways'."

Do not read this prompt aloud.
