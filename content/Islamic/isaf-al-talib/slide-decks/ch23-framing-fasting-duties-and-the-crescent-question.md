# Slide Deck Framing, EP23 Fasting Duties and the Crescent Question

## Audience
Asif's children and the adult listeners of the Isaf al-Talib series, who read no Arabic and have no training in law.

## Core Principle
The audio carries the reasoning and the voices. A slide holds only what the ear cannot: a position, an axis, a count.

## Visual Priorities
- The two believers a night's ride apart, as a two column contrast panel: clear sky against cloud, ridge against low ground, Ramadan against Sha'ban on the same day.
- The calendar from Muharram to Ramadan, as one horizontal line, complete and short months in alternating bands.
- The middle night test, as counted night marks: fourteen, one, fourteen for Sha'ban, failing at both candidate nights of a thirty day month.

## Prohibited Patterns
- No slides that bullet point the audio.
- No literal illustration of scripture or of the Prophet.
- No stock photography, crescent or mosque imagery, night skies.
- No bullet list presented as a diagram.
- No colour anywhere, no dark or coloured background, no colour coding, no gradients.

## Steering Phrases
- "Each slide must add visual information not present in the audio."
- "Render every slide as black line art on white, gray shading only where a fill is required."
- "When two positions are contrasted, show them side by side as columns, attribute by attribute."
- "Do not produce 'general illustration' slides. Every slide has a named diagram type."
