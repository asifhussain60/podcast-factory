# Slide Deck Framing, EP22 The Obligation and Meaning of Fasting

## Audience
Asif's children and adult students of Ismaili fiqh who have heard the audio but not the source.

## Core Principle
The audio carries the reasoning and the reported damage. Slides add only what a spoken track cannot hold.

## Visual Priorities
- The fast as a shield laid across the senses, tongue first, then ears, then eyes, then an open clause, food and drink demoted.
- The scale of a fast: fully upheld, diminished in proportion to what was released, then a floor where hypocrisy closes it.
- The seven obligatory fasts in three kinds: one from the calendar, five from inability to pay or manumit, one self bound.
- Ramadan said alone against the month of Ramadan, as two columns.

## Prohibited Patterns
- No slides that bullet point or restate the audio.
- No literal text slides reprinting a quotation or a verse.
- No stock photography of crescents or mosques.
- No bullet list dressed up as a diagram.
- No colour anywhere: black line art on white, grey shading only when needed, never a dark background.

## Steering Phrases
- "Each slide must add visual information not present in the audio."
- "Do not produce 'general illustration' slides. Every slide must have a named diagram type."
- "When two positions are contrasted, show them side by side as columns, attribute by attribute."
- "Render everything as black line art on a white background. No colour fills, no gradients, no photographs."

Do not read this prompt aloud.
