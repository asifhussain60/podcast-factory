# Slide Deck Framing, EP11-imamate-in-prayer

## Audience
Asif's children and adult students of Ismaili jurisprudence who have heard the audio but never opened the source.

## Core Principle
The audio carries the questioners and the reasoning. Slides carry only what a spoken line cannot hold: where a body stands, which claim outranks which, and where a broken prayer is repaired.

## Visual Priorities
- The ordered relations of placement, behind, beside, slightly behind, in line, and the one forbidden position in front of the imam, repeated as rings around the Kaaba.
- The tie break cascade for who leads, emigration, then memorization, then jurisprudence, then age.
- The twelve categories who may not lead, drawn as paired mismatches rather than as defects.
- The narration that the imam is to be followed, clause by clause against what the follower does.

## Prohibited Patterns
- Black ink on white only, grey shading where needed. No colour, no gradients, never a dark background.
- No photographs, no stock imagery, no decorative illustration.
- No slides that restate the audio, and no literal-text slides.
- No bullet list masquerading as a diagram.

## Steering Phrases
- "Render everything in black and white line art on a white background."
- "Each slide must add visual information not present in the audio."
- "Show the relationships between these positions, not a list of them."
- "When two positions are contrasted, show them as columns, attribute by attribute."

Do not read this prompt aloud.
