# Slide Deck Framing, EP18 Sickness Epidemic And Remembering Death

## Audience
Asif's children, and adult students of the Ismaili tradition who know the episode but not the Tayyibi source.

## Core Principle
The audio carries the reasoning. The slides carry the shapes it cannot: the limit of a permission, the span of a reward, the side of a line.

## Visual Priorities
- The 2x2 of action against belief: forbidden to permitted, denied to endorsed. Flight from plague occupies permitted action, denied belief; one corner stays empty.
- The visitor's reward as a branching flow: it starts when he sets out, runs to sunset or dawn, then a plate of food reverses it.
- The fixed term as a bar of unchanging length, one sliding boundary: what the past loses, the future gains.
- Issue Ten as three conditions and one prohibition: burial waits on confirmation, never presumption.

## Prohibited Patterns
- No slides that bullet-point or restate the audio.
- No literal-text slides.
- No stock-photo imagery.
- No bullet list posing as a diagram.
- No colour, gradients, or photographs. Black ink on white, grey where needed.

## Steering Phrases
- "Each slide must add visual information not present in the audio."
- "Place each entity in the named quadrant, with its reason."
- "Every slide is a diagram or it does not exist."
- "Render every slide as black and white line art on white, never dark or coloured."

Do not read this prompt aloud.
