# Slide Deck Framing, EP08-adhan-and-iqama

## Audience
Asif's children and adult students of Ismaili jurisprudence, who heard the audio but never the source.

## Core Principle
The audio carries narration and argument. Slides carry what a spoken line cannot: where a rule sits in a sequence, which words are heard aloud and which within the self, and which premise a conclusion rests on.

## Visual Priorities
- The 2x2 of what governs an utterance, ritual law against doctrinal proclamation, crossed with where it is heard, public against inward. The chapter travels it inward to public.
- The adhan against the iqama, attribute by attribute, purity line between them, then the prayer's interval on one axis: adhan, Sunnah, iqama, obligation.
- The public call and the inward answer as two parallel tracks, meeting twice.
- Atfih: accepted premises, one conclusion.

## Prohibited Patterns
- Black ink on white only, grey shading where needed. No colour, gradients, or dark background.
- No photographs, stock imagery, or decoration.
- No audio-restatement or literal-text slides, no bullet list posing as a diagram.

## Steering Phrases
- "Render everything in black and white line art on a white background."
- "Each slide must add visual information not present in the audio."
- "For the central tension, use a 2x2 with both axes named explicitly."
- "When two positions are contrasted, show them side by side, attribute by attribute."
- "Do not produce a slide titled 'Key Takeaways' or 'Overview.'"

Do not read this prompt aloud.
