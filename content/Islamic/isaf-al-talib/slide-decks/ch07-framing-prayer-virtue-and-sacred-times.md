# Slide Deck Framing, EP07-prayer-virtue-and-sacred-times

## Audience
Asif's children and adult students of Ismaili jurisprudence who heard the audio but never opened the source.

## Core Principle
The audio carries the questioners and the reasoning. Slides carry only what a spoken line cannot hold: which obstacle the servant made himself, which debt comes first, and when a prayer is lawful.

## Visual Priorities
- The 2x2 of self-caused against imposed obstacles, crossed with strict against accommodating rulings: the intoxicated man, the long abandoner, the man floating in water.
- Prayer's four boundaries as the walls of a house, time, qibla, wuduʾ, intention, with a wall missing where the time is wrong.
- The night sequence on one measured axis, through Maghrib, ʿIshāʾ al-Akhirah, Witr, the two seated rakʿahs, and Fajr.
- The debt chains as directed arrows, Maghrib before ʿIshāʾ.

## Prohibited Patterns
- Black ink on white only, grey shading where needed. No colour, no gradients, no dark background.
- No photographs, no stock imagery, no decorative illustration.
- No slides that restate the audio, and no literal-text slides.
- No bullet list masquerading as a diagram.

## Steering Phrases
- "Render everything in black and white line art on a white background."
- "Each slide must add visual information not present in the audio."
- "For the central tension, use a 2x2 with both axes named explicitly."
- "Where priority flows from one item to another, show a directed arrow."

Do not read this prompt aloud.
