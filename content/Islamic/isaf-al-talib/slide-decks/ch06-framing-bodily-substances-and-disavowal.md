# Slide Deck Framing, EP06 Bodily Substances and Disavowal

## Audience
Asif's children and the adult students following this series, familiar with the audio but new to classical purity law.

## Core Principle
The audio carries the reasoning and the moral weight. A slide exists only to hold what speech cannot: a matrix, an axis, a flow, a threshold line.

## Visual Priorities
- Menstruation, nifas, and istihadah as one matrix: rows are the states, columns are prayer, fasting, mosque entry, recitation, approach, make-up owed.
- The day thresholds, three, ten, twenty, forty, forty-five, sixty, two months, nine months, on one axis with period bands.
- Hair cut while the animal lives against hair taken after lawful slaughter, attribute by attribute.
- The 2x2 of substance status against disclosure, all four quadrants populated.

## Prohibited Patterns
- No slides that bullet-point or restate the audio.
- No literal-text slides reprinting a quoted sentence.
- No stock-photo imagery or photographs.
- No bullet list presented as a diagram.
- No colour anywhere. Black ink on white, gray shading only when a cell must be distinguished. Never a dark background, no fills, no gradients.

## Steering Phrases
- "Each slide must add visual information not present in the audio."
- "Render every slide in black and white: black line art on white."
- "When three or more positions are compared, use a matrix: rows entities, columns attributes."
- "Every slide must have a named diagram type."

Do not read this prompt aloud.
