# Slide Deck Framing, EP24 Scripture, Counted Days and the Complete Month

## Audience
Asif's children and adult students of Ismaili fiqh who know the audio, not the letters.

## Core Principle
The audio carries the reasoning and the damaged readings. Slides carry only structure a spoken track cannot hold.

## Visual Priorities
- The one preposition, two columns: the Prophet and his family fasting at the moon's emergence, against a community told to fast so it may be seen.
- The year four hundred as a dated axis: the fast begun, two days with no crescent, then the sighting.
- The eclipse as a kept appointment, a decreed fourteenth night beside the twenty eight nights the source contradicts.
- The seven paired institutions as a tree narrowing inward, prayer with zakat down to the two testimonies.

## Prohibited Patterns
- No slides that bullet point or restate the audio.
- No literal text slides reprinting a verse.
- No stock photography, no illustrated crescents or mosques, no bullet list dressed up as a diagram.
- No colour: black line art on white, grey shading only where needed, never a dark background.

## Steering Phrases
- "Each slide must add visual information not present in the audio."
- "When two positions are contrasted, show them side by side as columns, attribute by attribute."
- "Every slide must have a named diagram type."
- "Render every slide as black line art on white. No colour fills, no gradients, no photographs."

Do not read this prompt aloud.
