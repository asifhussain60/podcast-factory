# Slide Framing EP24, A Fast Begun Two Days Early

## Audience
Asif's children and adult students of Ismaili fiqh who know the audio, not the letters.

## Core Principle
The audio carries the reasoning. Slides add only what speech cannot hold: a marked night on a ring, a dispute on two axes, a bridge left one span short.

## Visual Priorities
- A ring of thirty numbered nights, the fourteenth marked heavily as the decreed night of the moon's covering, the thirteenth lightly as the rarer alternate.
- A two by two: timed by a human sighting against timed by the moon's emergence, crossed with individual observation against referral. Three quadrants filled, the fourth empty.
- A bridge: the granted sighting report as near bank, a universe of pairs and a law of pairs as two spans, the far bank unreached.

## Prohibited Patterns
- No slides that bullet point or restate the audio.
- No literal text slides reprinting verses or poetry.
- No stock photography of moons, skies or manuscripts.
- No bullet list dressed up as a diagram.
- No colour: black line art on white, grey shading only, never a dark background.

## Steering Phrases
- "Each slide must add visual information not present in the audio."
- "Do not produce 'general illustration' slides. Every slide must have a named diagram type."
- "Show the relationships between [entities], not a list of them."
- "Render everything as black line art on white. No colour fills, no photographs."

Do not read this prompt aloud.
