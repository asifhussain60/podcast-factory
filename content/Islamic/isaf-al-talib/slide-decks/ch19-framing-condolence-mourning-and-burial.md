# Slide Framing, EP19-condolence-mourning-and-burial

## Audience
Asif's children and adult students of Ismaili doctrine who have heard the audio but never opened the source.

## Core Principle
The audio carries the sickroom scene and the epistle. A slide holds what the ear cannot: where one rule sits against another, and who outranks whom over a body.

## Visual Priorities
- The washing as a 2x2: water sufficient or scarce against a qualified washer or nobody qualified, a ruling in each cell.
- Wailing and singing on one 2x2: calamity against blessing, voice restrained against raised.
- The right to lead the funeral prayer as a descending tree: public authority, the guardian, the established prayer leader, the family.
- The prepared body as one labelled figure: sites of prostration for camphor, separate sites for cotton.

## Prohibited Patterns
- No literal-text slides.
- No slides restating the audio.
- No photographs, stock imagery, or decorative illustration.
- No bullet lists presented as diagrams.
- No depiction of a corpse, an angel, or a punishment scene.
- Black ink on white only, grey shading where needed. No colour, no gradients, no dark background.

## Steering Phrases
- "Render every slide as black line art on a white background."
- "Each slide must add visual information not present in the audio."
- "For the central tension, use a 2x2 and specify both axes explicitly."
- "Show the relationships between entities, not a list of them."

Do not read this prompt aloud.
