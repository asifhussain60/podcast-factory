# Slide Framing, EP25-astronomy-calculation-and-religious-obligation

## Audience
Asif's children and adult students of Ismaili jurisprudence who have heard the audio but not the letter behind it.

## Core Principle
The audio carries the voice and the force of each reply. A slide holds only what the ear cannot keep: which instrument outranks which, where an obstacle stands, and the geometry of a month.

## Visual Priorities
- Natural vision against intellectual vision as one contrast panel, attribute by attribute, jurisdiction the dividing line.
- The five obstacles as one table, cloud, dust, a wall, a mountain, and the sun. The sun is the row that breaks the pattern.
- The month as a closed circuit from conjunction back to conjunction, the crescent drawn as the lit half swinging into view.
- The thirty-day case as a two-branch flow, both branches ending in the same conclusion.

## Prohibited Patterns
- No literal-text slides.
- No slides restating or bullet-pointing the audio.
- No photographs, stock imagery, or decorative illustration.
- No bullet lists presented as diagrams.
- Black ink on white only, grey shading where needed. No colour, no gradients, no dark background.

## Steering Phrases
- "Render every slide as black line art on a white background."
- "Each slide must add visual information not present in the audio."
- "Compare three or more positions as a matrix of entities against attributes."
- "Do not produce a slide titled 'Key Takeaways' or 'Overview.'"

Do not read this prompt aloud.
