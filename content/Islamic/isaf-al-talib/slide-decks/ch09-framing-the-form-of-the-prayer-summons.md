# Slide Deck Framing, ch09-the-form-of-the-prayer-summons

## Audience
Asif's children and adult students of Ismaili jurisprudence who heard the audio but never opened the proof-letter it argues from.

## Core Principle
The audio carries the argument as it unfolds. Slides carry what speech cannot: which proof closes which escape, where a ritual change sits on two axes at once, and how three testimonies share a form without sharing a rank.

## Visual Priorities
- The ladder of escapes as a downward flow, the proof that closes each rung set beside it.
- The eight proofs as spokes converging on one hub, the disputed phrase.
- The 2x2 of ritual change: removes against adds, crossed with diminishes a truth against proclaims one, the empty quadrant left empty.
- The three self-testimonies as a strict three-level tree, God above the Prophet above Ali.

## Prohibited Patterns
- Black ink on white only, grey shading where needed. No colour, no gradients, never a dark background.
- No photographs, no stock imagery, no decorative or calligraphic ornament.
- No literal-text slides and no slides that restate the audio.
- No bullet list masquerading as a diagram.

## Steering Phrases
- "Render everything in black and white line art on a white background."
- "Each slide must add visual information not present in the audio."
- "For the central tension, use a 2x2 with both axes named explicitly."
- "Do not produce a slide titled 'Key Takeaways' or 'Overview.'"

Do not read this prompt aloud.
