# Slide Deck Framing, EP01 Foundations of Faith and Guardianship

## Audience
Asif's children and other thoughtful young adults who have heard the audio and never read this Ismaili source.

## Core Principle
The audio carries the argument in a line. Slides carry only what a line cannot hold: ordered sets, opposed positions, ranked chains. A slide that could be one spoken sentence should not exist.

## Visual Priorities
- The seven duties of religion against the twelve social obligations, two columns compared attribute by attribute, walaya standing first in the seven.
- The ten things, each harder than the last, from stone up to the command of God, as one ascending ladder.
- The etiquette of who greets first, camel rider through horse rider, donkey rider, walker, and the one standing, as a directed chain.

## Prohibited Patterns
- No slides that bullet-point or restate the audio.
- No literal-text slides displaying a quoted sentence.
- No stock photos, photographs, or decorative scenery.
- No bullet list presented as a diagram.
- Black ink on white only, gray shading where a fill is needed. Never a dark or coloured background, no colour fills, no gradients.

## Steering Phrases
- "Each slide must add visual information not present in the audio."
- "Show the relationships between entities, not a list of them."
- "When two positions are contrasted, show them side by side as columns, attribute by attribute."
- "Render every slide as black line art on a white background."
