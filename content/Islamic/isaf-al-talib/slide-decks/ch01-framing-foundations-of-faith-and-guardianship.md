# Slide Framing, Foundations of Faith and Guardianship

## Audience
Asif's children and adult students of Ismaili doctrine, who know this episode's audio but not the councils, sermons, and reports behind it.

## Core Principle
The audio carries the teaching in a line. Slides hold only what a line cannot: two enumerations that belong to one structure, a chain of transmission, a household answering in turn, an etiquette with a direction.

## Visual Priorities
- The seven duties as a vertical column beside the twelve obligations as a horizontal spread, one structure and not two, with walaya standing first.
- The nine classes of claim in the first level of faith, as a matrix of who is owed against what the claim examines.
- The three interrogations as one matrix, sender against questioner against respondent, and the ten things ascending from stone to the command of God.
- Peace as a descent and a return: seven forms above, twelve below, and the reply sorted as just, complete, or virtuous.

## Prohibited Patterns
- No slides that bullet point or restate the audio narration.
- No literal text slides reprinting a verse, saying, or heading.
- No stock photography, no illustrated deserts, mosques, scrolls, or figures.
- No bullet list posing as a diagram.
- No title, agenda, takeaways, or closing thanks slide.
- No color anywhere: black ink on white, gray shading only. Never a dark background.

## Steering Phrases
- "Each slide must add visual information not present in the audio."
- "When three or more positions are compared, use a matrix with rows for entities and columns for attributes."
- "When influence flows from A to B, show a directed arrow from A to B."
- "Avoid stock-photo-style imagery. Every slide is a diagram or it does not exist."
- "Render every slide as black line art on white, with no color fills."

Do not read this prompt aloud.
