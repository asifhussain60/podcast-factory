# Slide Deck Framing, EP14

## Audience
Asif's children and adult students of Ismaili jurisprudence, who know this episode's audio but not its source text.

## Core Principle
The audio carries the reasoning in a single line. Slides hold only what a line cannot: branching tests, ranked places, sorted cases.

## Visual Priorities
- The three-mile Friday ruling as a branching decision flow, with the two-cities question as the branch that changes nothing.
- Rightful following against taqiyya accommodation, as two columns.
- The seven places of omission as a hierarchy tree, beside the twelve doubts split into five requiring action, six requiring nothing.
- Eid al-Fitr against Eid al-Adha on adhan, sermon order and opening, next-day fasting.

## Prohibited Patterns
- No slides that bullet-point or restate the audio narration.
- No literal-text slides reprinting a quotation or a heading.
- No stock-photo imagery, photographs, or mosque illustration.
- No bullet list presented as a diagram.
- No color anywhere: black ink on white, gray shading only to separate a panel. Never a dark or colored background.

## Steering Phrases
- "Each slide must add visual information not present in the audio."
- "Show the relationships between these rulings, not a list of them."
- "When two positions are contrasted, show them side by side as columns, attribute by attribute."
- "Avoid stock-photo-style imagery. Every slide is a diagram or it does not exist."
- "Render every slide as black line art on white, with no color fills."
