# Slide Deck Framing, EP26 Two Sightings and Higher Meaning

## Audience
Asif's children and adult students of Ismaili doctrine, who have heard the audio but never read the letter.

## Core Principle
The audio carries the argument as a line. Slides carry what a line cannot: the geometry of the sun's arc, the rank between two sightings, the five pillars under one rule. If a slide could be one spoken sentence, omit it.

## Visual Priorities
- Two lines on the sun's daily circle: the soul's line at the arc's midpoint against the eye's line at more than half. Circle, tangent, both crossings, as line art.
- The two sightings as a hierarchy tree, select above, public below, each branch carrying its own duty.
- The five pillars as one comparison matrix: office held, individual's part, failure mode.
- Private instrument against shared trigger: a congregation behind one leader, beside a room of people each keeping his own count.

## Prohibited Patterns
- No slides that bullet-point the audio.
- No literal illustrations of quoted verses or hadith.
- No stock photography, no mosque or crescent-moon images.
- No bullet list posing as a diagram, no title-only slide.
- No colour: black ink on white, gray shading only. Never a dark or coloured background, no gradients.

## Steering Phrases
- "Each slide must add visual information not present in the audio."
- "Show the relationships between entities, not a list of them."
- "Every slide must have a named diagram type."
- "Render every diagram as black line art on white, grayscale only, no photographs."

Do not read this prompt aloud.
