# Slide Framing, EP26-two-sightings-and-higher-meaning

## Audience
Asif's children and adult students of Ismaili doctrine who have heard the audio but never opened the letter.

## Core Principle
The audio carries the opponent's voice and the force of his hadith. A slide holds only what the ear cannot: where a line falls on an arc, and where a person stands relative to a leader.

## Visual Priorities
- The sun's daily arc as one timeline: the soul's line at the midpoint, the eye's line past it, the blind band between them.
- Those two lines as two columns, one a definition varying with nothing, the other varying with the watcher.
- Kirman against Shiraz, one rule of sight yielding two first days of the fast.
- The five pillars as one matrix: rows for prayer, zakat, Hajj, jihad, fasting, columns for the holding office and the verdict on running ahead.

## Prohibited Patterns
- No literal-text slides.
- No slides restating the audio.
- No photographs, stock imagery, or decorative illustration.
- No bullet lists presented as diagrams.
- Black ink on white only, grey shading where needed. No colour, no gradients, no dark background.

## Steering Phrases
- "Render every slide as black line art on a white background."
- "Each slide must add visual information not present in the audio."
- "Compare three or more positions as a matrix of entities against attributes."
- "Do not produce a slide titled 'Key Takeaways' or 'Overview.'"
