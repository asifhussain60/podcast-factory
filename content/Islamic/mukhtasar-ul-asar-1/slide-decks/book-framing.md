# Slide Deck Framing — Mukhtasar ul Asar (book deck)

## Audience
Asif's children and thoughtful young adults who have heard the audio episodes of this jurisprudence compendium but never opened the Arabic source. They know the outline; they need its architecture made visible.

## Core Principle
The audio carries the argument and devotional weight. The deck exists only to render the book's STRUCTURES: the schedules, ladders, decision-rules, and category-lists a linear track cannot hold. If a slide could be a single spoken sentence, omit it.

## Visual Priorities
- The one water decision-rule: does the added thing alter color, taste, or smell? Render as a branching flow.
- The zakat schedules as ascending threshold tables, and the she-camel ages as a maturity ladder.
- The four kinds of fasting as one branching hierarchy tree with its numbered divisions.
- The Hajj rites as one process flow from Tarwiyah through Arafah, Muzdalifah, and Mina, plus the three-stage release from ihram.
- The three enemies Ali named, and the mosque-reward and jizyah tiers, as ranked matrices.

## Prohibited Patterns
- No slides that bullet-point the audio narration.
- No literal-text slides that merely display a verse or hadith.
- No stock-photo-style imagery of mosques, deserts, or worshippers.
- No bullet lists dressed up as diagrams.
- No opening title slide, no "Overview," no closing "Key Takeaways."

## Steering Phrases
- "Each slide must add visual information not present in the audio."
- "Do not produce 'general illustration' slides. Every slide must have a named diagram type."
- "When three or more positions are compared, use a matrix with rows for entities and columns for attributes."
- "Avoid stock-photo-style imagery. Every slide is a diagram or it does not exist."

## Visual Style
Render every slide as black-and-white line-art, like pen-and-ink scholarly diagrams. Hierarchies become tree diagrams; contrast pairs become two-panel layouts; maturity and genealogy chains become arrow diagrams; process flows become numbered-step illustrations; matrices become ruled grids. Clean geometric shapes and lines only. No colour fills, no gradients, no photographs. Each slide must be a drawn diagram, not a bullet list.
