# The First Obligation: Seeking Knowledge

In the name of God, the Most Merciful, the Most Compassionate.

God, mighty and majestic, said: ﴿يَرْفَعِ اللَّهُ الَّذِينَ آمَنُوا مِنكُمْ وَالَّذِينَ أُوتُوا الْعِلْمَ دَرَجَاتٍ﴾, "Allah will raise those of you who believe and those who have been given knowledge by degrees" [Surah al-Mujadalah: 11]. And the Almighty said: ﴿هَلْ يَسْتَوِي الَّذِينَ يَعْلَمُونَ وَالَّذِينَ لَا يَعْلَمُونَ إِنَّمَا يَتَذَكَّرُ أُولُو الْأَلْبَابِ﴾, "Are those who know equal to those who do not know? Only those possessed of understanding take heed" [Surah al-Zumar: 9]. And He said: ﴿بَلْ هُوَ آيَاتٌ بَيِّنَاتٌ فِي صُدُورِ الَّذِينَ أُوتُوا الْعِلْمَ﴾, "Rather, it is clear signs preserved in the breasts of those who have been given knowledge" [Surah al-Ankabut: 49].

The Messenger of God (ص) said: "Whomever God intends good for, He grants understanding of religion." And he said: "Four things are obligatory upon every person of reason and intellect among my community: to listen to knowledge, to preserve it, to act upon it, and to spread it." And he said: "Learn from the scholar of my household, for whoever learns from the scholar of my household will be saved from the Fire." And he said: "There is no rest in life save for one who speaks with knowledge or one who listens with understanding." And he said: "This knowledge is borne in every generation by its upright ones, who repel from it the distortions of the ignorant, the false interpretations of those who go to excess, and the misappropriations of the fabricators." And he said: "When a man goes out in search of knowledge, God records good deeds for his every footstep; and when he meets the scholar and the two recall together something of the command of God, the angels spread their wings over them, and they are called from above: God has forgiven you both." And he said: "The first stage of knowledge is silence; the second is listening; the third is spreading it; and the fourth is acting upon it."

Ali (ع) said: "Seeking knowledge is an obligation upon every Muslim, man and woman." And he said: "Whoever buys and sells without gaining understanding of religion has plunged into usury, then plunged into it again." And he said: "Four things, were riding-beasts to be driven hard toward them until they were worn out, that would be a small price: that the servant place his hope in none but his Lord, that he fear nothing but his own sin, that the ignorant not be ashamed to learn, and that the scholar, when asked about what he does not know, say, 'I do not know.'"

It is reported from Ja'far ibn Muhammad (ع) that he said: "Seek knowledge, and adorn it with forbearance and dignity. Be humble toward those whom you teach, and do not be tyrannical scholars, lest your falsehood undo your truth." And he said: "Were a young man of our Shia brought to me who had not gained understanding in religion, I would discipline him well." And he said: "The believer does not cease to bequeath to his household knowledge and righteous conduct until he brings them all into Paradise, so that not one of them is lost, neither young nor old, neither servant nor neighbor. And the disobedient servant does not cease to bequeath to his household ignorance and evil conduct until he brings them all into the Fire, so that not one of them is lost, neither young nor old, neither servant nor neighbor."
