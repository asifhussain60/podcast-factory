# Slide Deck Framing, Mukhtasar al-Athar Volume 2 (book deck)

## Audience
Thoughtful adult students of Islamic law following the audio episodes of this Ahl al-Bayt legal compendium, who want its rulings held still long enough to see their shape. Not specialists, and not children.

## Core Principle
The audio carries the reasoning, the narrations, and the voices of the Imams. The slides do only what speech cannot: they make the law's hidden architecture visible, its branching tests, graded scales, and matrices of trigger and remedy. If a slide could be a single spoken sentence, it does not belong here.

## Visual Priorities
- The forbidden gharar sales as one hierarchy tree: the unborn, the touched, the thrown, the unreachable.
- The riba rule as a matrix of same kind against different kind, hand to hand against deferred.
- The single drink crossing into wine, as a numbered process flow from fresh juice to fermented froth.
- The escalating hadd of theft and the ascending blood-money scale, each as one rising ladder.

## Prohibited Patterns
- No slides that bullet-point or restate the audio narration.
- No literal text slides that reprint a quotation or a verse.
- No stock-photo-style imagery of marketplaces, scales, or robed figures.
- No bullet list dressed up as a diagram; every slide is a named, populated structure.

## Steering Phrases
- "Each slide must add visual information not present in the audio."
- "When three or more positions are compared, use a matrix with rows for entities and columns for attributes."
- "Do not produce 'general illustration' slides. Every slide must have a named diagram type."

## Visual Style
Render every slide as black-and-white line-art, in the manner of pen-and-ink scholarly diagrams. Hierarchies become tree diagrams; contrast pairs become side-by-side two-panel layouts; ordering chains become flowing arrow diagrams; process flows become numbered-step illustrations with visible branches; graded scales become labeled ascending ladders. Clean geometric shapes and lines only. No color fills, no photographs, no gradients. Each slide is a drawn diagram, never a text-heavy bullet list.
