# Chapters Rationale -- Asas al-Taweel, Volume 1: Adam (pages 1-75)

## Volume scope

This volume covers the Pre-text (editor's intro + author's intro, pages 1-32)
and Chapter 1: Adam -- the first prophetic cycle (pages 33-75).

The Adam chapter is the structural keystone of Asas al-Taweel: it introduces
the cosmological framework (natiq / asas / hujja hierarchy) that all subsequent
chapters presuppose, and it contains al-Numan's core argument about the zahir /
batin relationship through the lens of Adam's covenant with God.

## Figures covered in this volume

| Figure | Role in the cycle |
|---|---|
| Adam | First natiq (Speaker-Prophet); inaugurates the cosmological cycle |
| Iblis | Adversary; refusal to prostrate becomes the type of the zahir-only reader |
| Habil and Qabil | Sons of Adam; type the believing and rejecting heirs of the imam |
| Shith (Seth) | Asas of Adam; carries the inner meaning forward |
| Idris | Bridge hujja; transitions to the Noah cycle |
