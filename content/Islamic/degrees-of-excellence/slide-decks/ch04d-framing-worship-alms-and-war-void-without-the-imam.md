# Slide Deck Framing, EP04-worship-alms-and-war-void-without-the-imam

## Audience
Asif's children and thoughtful adult readers who have heard the audio but not read al-Naysaburi's treatise, following an argument that moves from geometry to the pillars of Islam.

## Core Principle
The audio carries the linear argument and its voice. Each slide must add a spatial or relational structure the spoken track cannot hold. If a slide could be a single spoken sentence, omit it.

## Visual Priorities
- The circle, the number one, and the dot as three symbols of one lesson, an origin that generates all the rest while depending on none.
- The regress of teachers, a directed chain running backward until it must end in a teacher taught only by God.
- The either-true-or-false fork on the later alms-collectors, both branches ending in collapse.
- The partial prayer-leader against the supreme imam, side by side, attribute by attribute.

## Prohibited Patterns
- No slides that bullet-point or restate the audio.
- No literal-text slides printing a Qur'anic verse as decoration.
- No stock-photo imagery of mosques, deserts, or worshippers.
- No bullet list masquerading as a diagram.

## Steering Phrases
- "Each slide must add visual information not present in the audio."
- "When two positions are contrasted, show them side by side as columns, attribute by attribute."
- "When influence flows from A to B, show a directed arrow from A to B."
- "Every slide must have a named diagram type."

Do not read this prompt aloud.
