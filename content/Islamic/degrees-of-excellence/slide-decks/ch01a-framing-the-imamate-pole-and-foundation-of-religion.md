# Slide Deck Framing, EP01-the-imamate-pole-and-foundation-of-religion

## Audience
Asif's children, thoughtful young adults who have heard the audio episode but have never read al-Naysaburi and do not read classical Arabic.

## Core Principle
The audio carries the argument as a linear voice. Slides hold only what a voice cannot: the parallels, hierarchies, and reversals that must be seen side by side to land. If a slide could be one spoken sentence, cut it.

## Visual Priorities
- The three reasons the imam is not outranked by the messenger, as a matrix (containment, audience, permanence), not a list.
- The mirrored failure, Creator and imam both agreed necessary then reduced downward, as two facing columns.
- Abraham's ascent, prophet to friend to messenger to imam, as a directed chain ending at the summit.
- The sky as a diagram of guidance, spheres for prophets and stars for imams, as one annotated structure.

## Prohibited Patterns
- No slides that bullet-point or restate the audio narration.
- No literal-text slides that simply quote the source.
- No stock-photo or scenic imagery.
- No bullet lists dressed up as diagrams.

## Steering Phrases
- "Each slide must add visual information not present in the audio."
- "When three or more positions are compared, use a matrix with rows for entities and columns for attributes."
- "When influence flows from A to B, show a directed arrow from A to B."
- "Avoid stock-photo-style imagery. Every slide is a diagram or it does not exist."
