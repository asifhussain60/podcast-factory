# Slide Deck Framing: ch02b-the-theory-of-degrees-of-excellence-explained

## Audience
Asif's children and thoughtful adults following the audio, who know al-Naysaburi and the Fatimids but never opened his treatise.

## Core Principle
The audio narrates the argument as it unfolds; the slides exist only to make its architecture visible. Any slide that could be a single audio sentence should be dropped.

## Visual Priorities
- The seven cycles, each speaker-prophet paired with his silent legatee, Adam and Seth down to Muhammad and Ali, ending in the qa'im, as a directed genealogy chain.
- The ladder of degrees, one summit per domain, heat, the outermost sphere, the brain, the sun, gold, the unburnable ruby, wheat, the date palm, as a comparison matrix with a parallel column for the imam.
- The speaker-prophet who brings the outer law against the silent companion who keeps the inner meaning, as two columns attribute by attribute.

## Prohibited Patterns
- No slides that bullet-point or restate the audio.
- No literal-text slides that display a verse or quotation.
- No stock-photo imagery of deserts or mosques.
- No bullet lists masquerading as diagrams.

## Steering Phrases
- "Each slide must add visual information not present in the audio."
- "When influence flows from A to B, show a directed arrow from A to B."
- "When three or more positions are compared, use a matrix with rows for entities and columns for attributes."
- "Do not produce 'general illustration' slides. Every slide must have a named diagram type."
