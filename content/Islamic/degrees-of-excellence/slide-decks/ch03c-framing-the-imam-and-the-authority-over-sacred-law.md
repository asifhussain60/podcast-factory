# Slide Deck Framing, EP03-the-imam-and-the-authority-over-sacred-law

## Audience
Asif's children and thoughtful adult listeners who have heard the audio but never read al-Naysaburi, and who do not read Arabic.

## Core Principle
The audio carries the argument, three doctrines that build the imam's authority from ownership, trust, and bare reason. The slides carry only what the ear cannot hold, the interlocking structure of those doctrines and the ladder of spirits beneath them.

## Visual Priorities
- The ladder of nature, one added spirit at each rung as the difference between ruling and being ruled, from earth to plant to animal to human, then vaulting to the imam.
- The three doctrines side by side, dominion, wisdom, necessity, each with its ground and direction.
- The three pictures of necessity, the shepherd, the headless body, the ranks of nature, each answering a different doubter.
- The grammar of the title against its contrary, leader against led, excellent against surpassed.

## Prohibited Patterns
- No slides that bullet-point or restate the audio.
- No literal illustrations of quotes, shepherds, animals, or shadows.
- No stock-photo-style imagery.
- No bullet lists dressed up as diagrams.

## Steering Phrases
- "Each slide must add visual information not present in the audio."
- "When three or more positions are compared, use a matrix with rows for entities and columns for attributes."
- "Show the relationships between entities, not a list of them."
- "Avoid stock-photo-style imagery. Every slide is a diagram or it does not exist."
