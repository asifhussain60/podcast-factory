# Slide Deck Framing, EP02-degrees-of-excellence-the-peak-of-every-kind

## Audience
Asif's children and thoughtful adult listeners who have heard the audio but never read al-Naysaburi, and who do not read Arabic.

## Core Principle
The audio carries the argument's unfolding surprise, a naturalist's catalogue that becomes a proof about authority. The slides carry only what the ear cannot hold: the recurring pattern of domain, summit, and imamate parallel.

## Visual Priorities
- The master matrix: each domain of creation (elements, planets, metals, jewels, grains, trees), its named summit, its crowning trait, and the imamate parallel.
- The red ruby against the stones it defeats (emerald, diamond, turquoise, jade), where only the ruby survives both fire and water.
- Concentric circles of benefit flowing inward: lower creation into the human being, humanity into the imams at the core.
- The single image of medicine branching into two faces, the antidote that saves against the poison that kills.

## Prohibited Patterns
- No slides that bullet-point or restate the audio.
- No literal illustrations of scriptural quotes or of fire, suns, or gems.
- No stock-photo-style imagery.
- No bullet lists dressed up as diagrams.

## Steering Phrases
- "Each slide must add visual information not present in the audio."
- "When three or more positions are compared, use a matrix with rows for entities and columns for attributes."
- "Show the relationships between entities, not a list of them."
- "Avoid stock-photo-style imagery. Every slide is a diagram or it does not exist."
