## Findings

### PR-PC-01 (P0) — "imamate" garbled (book-level)
`imit` x4 plus the book title rendered *"Establishing the Amat."*

### PR-PJ-01a (P0) — The analogy budget is spoken to the listener
*"He gives us his final allowed analogy to answer this. The shepherd and the flock."*
Also *"which brings us to the second of his governing images. The farmer,"* and *"our third major tension."* "Allowed analogy" is not a thing a host says; it is the framing's "Exactly three governing images ... No invented analogies" surfacing as dialogue.

### PR-PH-04 (P1) — A 250-word invented modern analogy sequence
*"Think about a severe medical intervention. Think about chemotherapy ... If you had no overarching knowledge of oncology, you would accuse the doctor of malice ... Or think about physical therapy after a major surgery. The therapist forces you to move a repaired joint ... if you do not push through the pain and break the scar tissue now, you will lose the use of that limb forever."*
The framing lists three governing images and says "No invented analogies." The source's own parallel is deliberately generic — "what life has already taught him to do a hundred times in smaller matters" — precisely so the argument does not depend on a modern illustration. This is the single largest block of non-source material in the series.

### PR-PC-06 (P1) — The technical vocabulary of the excellence argument is mispronounced
*"The word for the one who is surpassed is Mathdul"* (mafdul) and *"the word for the one who is preceded by another is Mazbuck"* (masbuq). The afdal/mafdul pair is the standing technical vocabulary of imamate theory; getting it wrong in the one passage that turns on the literal grammar of the words is self-defeating. Also *"He calls him the steersman. In Arabic, the word is cis"* — sa'is.

### PR-PC-07 (P1) — Book title and author name both wrong, and inconsistent across the series
*"We are opening a book called Establishing the Amat, written by the medieval scholar Al-Nizaburi."* Glossary: `audio_phonetic: an-nay-saa-boo-ree`. Across the six episodes the author's name comes back as **Al-Nah** (EP01), **Al-Naisaburi** (EP02), **Al-Nizaburi** (EP03) — three renderings of the one name the framings license.

### PR-PJ-03 (P1) — The pronunciation block is performed as a vocabulary drill
Seven times: *"So let me give you the Arabic term for this shadow-like return of property. The word is fay," "Let me give you the Arabic for it. is nut Q," "let me name the Arabic term for this concept ... The word is Raya," "Let me define the word he uses for this guide," "Yes, let me introduce those terms now."*
The framing says "Say each term ONCE." The hosts have turned that into a recurring stage direction — announce that a term is coming, then say it — which is audibly mechanical and is also how several of the mispronunciations above get isolated and spotlit.

### PR-PJ-07 (P1) — Forbidden vocabulary and conversation-meta
*"Oh, wow. I mean, that is severe."* and *"Oh, wow. I mean, that is a profound"* — every framing lists "wow" under forbidden vocabulary. Plus two running-time self-references: *"We just spent 20 minutes talking about how rational thought is the spirit that elevates us"* and *"We have spent an hour constructing a towering edifice of abstract theology"* — the second is factually wrong (the episode runs 40.7 minutes total).

### PR-PE-03 (P2) — Spine not verbatim
The spine is *"All was made for the imam; **his spirits** set his reach, and the world's head must be its best"* — plural, because the doctrine is the array of added spirits. Two of the three deliveries say *"his spirit"* singular, which quietly undoes the argument the sentence exists to compress. `R-RECURRING-THESIS` requires verbatim.

### PR-PD-04 (P2) — Two supplied details in the Moses sequence
*"poor fishermen who had kindly taken them across the water"* and *"a tyrant king who was confiscating all sound vessels on the river."* The source has "a sound boat" and "a seizing tyrant"; the hospitality detail comes from the hadith versions of the story and "on the river" from nowhere. Traditional, not fabricated, but not in the uploaded chapter.
