### PR-PC-01 (P0) — "imamate" garbled (book-level)

### PR-PJ-01a (P0) — The name-discipline directive is announced to the listener

That is the framing's "Name discipline" block read out as dialogue. ### PR-PH-01 (P1) — Invented attribution: "the Archon"
*"They are the ultimate building blocks. Some classical traditions refer to them as the Archon. They do."*
The source term is **arkan**, the pillars, and the framing's pronunciation block instructs the English form ("arkan: the pillars"). "Archon" is a different word from a different language. The hosts have both broken the say-the-English rule and attached a fabricated attribution ("some classical traditions refer to them as...") to a mispronunciation.

### PR-PH-02 (P1) — Modern physics imported into the sun argument
*"If the physical mass of the sun swerved from its fixed orbit, or the Earth suddenly halted and reversed its rotation, even for the blink of an eye. The entire physical world would be ripped apart by the gravitational shear."*
The source says only that "the whole world would be destroyed ... which reason and nature alike rule out." Gravitational shear is host-supplied and anachronistic in an argument whose whole force is that it works from **classical** physics. This is the shape `MODERNIZE_DENY` exists to prevent.

### PR-PC-05 (P1) — Three Arabic terms spoken where the framing set English forms, one of them wrong
The framing's pronunciation block maps `tiryaq: the antidote`, `ya'sub: the chief`, `ahl al-zahir: the literalists` — i.e. speak the English. The episode speaks the Arabic instead: *"They are known in the classical tradition as the Tyriac," "This indispensable leader is the Yasub," "They are the Al Zahir the literalists."*
The third is the worst: it drops **ahl** (so "people of the exterior" becomes just "al-Zahir," which is the regnal name of a Fatimid imam-caliph), and it says the Arabic and the English **back-to-back**, which the framing forbids in the same breath ("Never say the original spelling and the English form back-to-back").

### PR-PB-01 (P1) — The ranks of the daawa are dropped from the ruby passage
The source, at the ruby, says the imam is "unique among every rank of the mission — the proofs, the adjuncts, the summoner, the wing, the licensee, the motivator. Above every one of those grades stands the imam." The episode keeps the ruby's incorruptibility and drops the hierarchy entirely. Also dropped: the twelve signs of the zodiac against the twelve proofs of the mission, and the seven jewels read as the seven proclaimers and seven imams. Those three passages are where this chapter carries its distinctively Ismaili structure; none of them is audible.

### PR-PI-02 (P1) — Welcome arrives after the hook

### PR-PH-03 (P2) — "North Africa" supplied
*"It was witnessed as a tangible historical reality when the imit established its sovereignty in North Africa."* The source says only that the reading "has been borne out, witnessed with his own eyes by the grace of God." Historically right, but host-supplied.
