## Findings

### PR-PC-01 (P0) — "imamate" garbled, including into a different office
`imit` x3, `emit` x2, `imana` x1 — and once as **"the Divine Office of the Emirah."** An emirate is not an imamate. This lands in the middle of the Abu Bakr dilemma, the most load-bearing passage in the episode.

### PR-PC-02 (P0) — "vicegerent" never lands, and neither does the designation event
Four garbles in this episode alone: *"a vice adjurant on earth," "the office of the vice turant," "a world of sinless angels was still given a vice to it," "They willingly conceive a designated vice fajan."* The word carries Beat 1 entirely.
And the climax: *"The author recounts the climax of sacred history at **Qadir Kum**."* Ghadir Khumm is the single most consequential proper noun in this chapter, and it is the one the episode gets wrong. Also *"He calls it **Nur Ali Mama**. The light of the Imit"* for nur al-imama (the framing set the English form, "the light of the imamate"), *"He calls it NAS"* for nass, and *"the text calls this unchanging divine pattern the Sana"* for sunna.

### PR-PJ-10 (P1) — A volunteered impartiality statement, and a wellness-app opener
*"And because this text is inherently polemical, I want to state clearly that we are strictly impartial guides in this conversation. We are not taking sides. Not at all. We are not endorsing one historical or sectarian view over another."*
Nothing in the framing asks for this. It is delivered in the first ninety seconds of the episode that weighs the first caliphs, which is precisely where a volunteered disclaimer changes the reading. Preceded by *"I want you to just settle in, clear your mind of whatever you were just doing. Yeah, just take a breath"* — a guided-meditation opener on a scholarly-deep-dive archetype.
