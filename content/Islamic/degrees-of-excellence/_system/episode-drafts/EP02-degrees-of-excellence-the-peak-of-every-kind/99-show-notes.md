# Show notes — EP02

**Title:** The Peak of Every Kind

**Blurb:** 'The introduction sketched the ladder of degrees of excellence as an abstract engine; here al-Naysaburi builds it himself, rung by rung, out of the raw materials of the world. Fire is the summit of the elements, the sun of the planets, gold of the metals, and — finer still — the red ruby that comes through fire and water unchanged is the crown of all jewels; wheat is the peak of the grains and the date palm of the trees, so like a human being that it has male and female and must be fertilized. Each choice quietly names a virtue of the imam: the one who gives light, who cannot be corrupted, who nourishes and heals. And every bright image has its dark twin — the healing antidote against the fatal poison, the sweet tree against the thorned, the fragrant plant against the putrid — by which the usurpers of the imamate are shown for what they are. The climb ends where it always pointed: humankind is the summit of all creation, and the imam is the summit of humankind, without whom, al-Naysaburi warns, a person sinks beneath the beasts.
