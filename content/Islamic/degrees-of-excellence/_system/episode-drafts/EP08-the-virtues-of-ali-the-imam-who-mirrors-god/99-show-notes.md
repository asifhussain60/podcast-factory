# Show notes — EP08

**Title:** The Imam Who Mirrors God

**Blurb:** 'The treatise ends where its ladder always pointed. Every ordered domain has its peak, so the imams too must have one — and that utmost degree, al- Naysaburi says, is the Commander of the Faithful, Ali b. Abi Talib, the summit of all summits, the perfector who gathers into himself the merits of every prophet and imam before him, as the heart gives life to the limbs; and the reigning imam of each age continues and completes that summit for his own time. Then comes the book''s most demanding claim: that the imam''s dealing with people mirrors God''s own. As God gives to the pious and the impious alike, conceals the means of worldly gain so no single path corrupts the world, and moves between nearness and distance to keep His servants between fear and hope, so does the imam — his gifts, unlike any monarch''s, seeking no return at all. Between those who reduce him to an ordinary ruler and those who exaggerate him into a god, al-Naysaburi sets the true measure: the Universal Vicegerent of God, a shadow that imitates in every detail the one who casts it. The series closes on the author''s own prayer of gratitude and striving.
