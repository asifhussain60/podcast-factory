# Show notes — EP01

**Title:** The Fatimid World and al-Naysaburi

**Blurb:** 'The doorway to the series. In 909 a Shi''i caliphate was founded in Ifriqiya — the first major Shi''i rule of an Islamic state since the Father of Imams — and the Fatimids made the imamate the very reason for their existence. Into that world, crowded with Sunni jurists, Mu''tazila, and philosophers all arguing over how reason and revelation fit together, came Ahmad b. Ibrahim al-Naysaburi, a scholar of Nishapur who had drunk deep of both Ismaili theology and Greek philosophy. This episode introduces the man — his formation, his works on the hidden imams and the ideal summoner, his quiet influence on later giants — and the book he wrote: a treatise in seven sections built on a single daring method, to prove the necessity of the imam not by scripture alone but by reason, nature, mathematics, and the shared agreement of humankind. The proof itself waits for the next episode.
