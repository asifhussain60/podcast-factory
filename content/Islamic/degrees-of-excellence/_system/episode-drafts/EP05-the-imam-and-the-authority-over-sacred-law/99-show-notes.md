# Show notes — EP05

**Title:** The Imam's Authority

**Blurb:** 'The ladder has reached its summit; now al-Naysaburi asks what the summit authorizes, and his answers are among the boldest in the book. All the worlds, he argues, were made for humanity and humanity for the imams, so that whatever anyone takes without the imam''s leave is a kind of theft, and all benefit is, in the end, a return to its source. He grounds this in a ladder of spirits: as the plant outranks the earth by the spirit of growth and the animal outranks the plant by sensation and the human outranks the animal by reason, so the imam is given further spirits — the sanctified and the creative — that widen his dominion over all humankind. From there he presses two more arguments: that the imam is absolutely wise, so that to charge him with a foolish act is to fall out of the faith; and that reason alone, without a line of scripture, shows the world must have one supreme leader who is the best, most knowing, and most pure of all — the shepherd without whom the flock is lost, the head without which the body is a corpse.
