# Slide Framing: The Path of Obedience and Struggle

## Purpose
This slide deck must visually articulate the core concepts of obedience, spiritual struggle (mujahadah), and the Prophetic frame as the foundation for the seeker's path. It needs to illustrate the contrast between outward acts and their inner spiritual meaning, and the structured nature of spiritual discipline.

## Steering phrases (apply all of the following)
- Diagram the relationship between outward action and the Prophetic frame.
- Illustrate the "four proclamations of the night" and corresponding human responses.
- Use a "sword" metaphor to depict mujahadah's role in severing lower self desires.
- Map the "four conditions of the sincere seeker" as a structured pathway.
- Show the rooster and dove as symbolic teachers of wakefulness.

## Slide types required
- **Flowchart/Decision Tree:** To show how an act is categorized as "worship" or "sin" based on its alignment with the Prophetic frame (e.g., fasting on Eid, lawful intimacy).
- **Multi-stage Process Diagram:** To illustrate the "four proclamations of the night" and the narrowing circles of responders, from "all worshippers" to "heedless ones."
- **Metaphorical Diagram:** Depicting the "sword of mujahadah" severing the "lower self" or its desires.
- **Symbolic Illustration:** The rooster and dove as teachers of wakefulness, contrasted with a sleeping figure.
- **Numbered List/Checklist Diagram:** Clearly outlining the "four conditions of the sincere seeker" and their implications.
- **Comparative Table/Diagram:** To contrast "knowledge alone" vs. "knowledge + mujahadah" leading to ma'rifah.

## Do not
- Restate the audio discussion in bullet-point slides.
- Produce "title + stock-photo illustration" slides.
- Use generic relationship labels ("influences", "relates to") — name the specific relationship.
