# Slide Framing: The Peril of Unacted Knowledge

## Purpose
This slide deck must visually represent the core argument that knowledge without action is not only insufficient for salvation but can be a cause for severe judgment. It needs to illustrate the interconnectedness of faith and deeds, the consequences of inaction, and the various parables and proofs Ghazali uses to make his case, going beyond a linear summary of the audio.

## Steering phrases (apply all of the following)
- Illustrate the relationship between knowledge and action as a conditional flow.
- Diagram the consequences of inaction using symbolic representations.
- Create visual metaphors for Ghazali's parables and Quranic verses.
- Show the progression of the argument from diagnosis to the call for self-accounting.

## Slide types required
- **Conditional flow diagram:** To show the relationship between knowledge, action, and salvation/judgment (0:00-0:30).
- **Two-part comparison diagram:** To contrast the "learned soul" with the "openly disobedient" (2:00-3:00).
- **Symbolic representation of Prophetic warnings:** Visualizing the "sign of divine withdrawal" and the "forty-year clock" (3:00-5:00).
- **Narrative sequence diagram:** For Shaykh Junaid Baghdadi's dream, showing "discourses/signs" vs. "cycles of prayer" (6:00-7:30).
- **Metaphorical illustration:** For the "lion in the jungle" and "unswallowed medicine" parables (8:00-10:00).
- **Multi-point evidence diagram:** To present the Quranic verses and Hadith on faith and deeds (10:00-14:00).
- **Process flow diagram:** For the story of the angel and the worshipper, highlighting the test and the outcome (15:00-17:30).
- **Conceptual framework diagram:** To summarize the diagnosis and transition to self-accounting (17:30-19:00).

## Do not
- Restate the audio discussion in bullet-point slides.
- Produce "title + stock-photo illustration" slides.
- Use generic relationship labels ("influences", "relates to") — name the specific relationship.
