# Slide Framing: Eight Admonitions and a Closing Prayer

## Purpose
This slide deck must visually represent Imam al-Ghazali's eight admonitions, showing the distinct categories of actions to refuse and actions to take up. It must also illustrate the nuanced distinctions within each admonition, particularly the typology of questioners and the components of a proper sermon, and conclude with the structure and themes of the closing prayer.

## Steering phrases (apply all of the following)
- Create a two-part diagram for the eight admonitions: "Four to Refuse" and "Four to Take Up."
- Use flowcharts or decision trees to illustrate the "refuse religious debate" admonition, especially the "categories of patients" section.
- Design a multi-panel diagram for the "reality of preaching" admonition, detailing "what a sermon must not be" and "what a sermon must not seek."
- Employ a visual metaphor or symbolic representation for the "stay away from the affluent and rulers" and "do not accept gifts from rulers" admonitions, highlighting the corruption mechanism.
- Use a comparative diagram for the "method of developing an intimate relationship with Allah" and "method of relating to the slaves of Allah" admonitions, showing the "auditing oneself" principle.
- Create a timeline or sequence of thought experiments for the "insistent advice to study and learn" admonition.
- Use a spectrum or scale to represent the "not stockpiling food" admonition, showing the varying levels of *Tawakkul*.
- Design a word cloud or thematic cluster for the closing supplication, highlighting the names of Allah and key requests.

## Slide types required
- **Two-part Categorization Diagram:** For the "Eight Admonitions" overview.
- **Decision Tree/Flowchart:** For "First: refuse the habit of religious debate" and "The categories of patients."
- **Multi-panel Diagram with Callouts:** For "Second: the reality of preaching," detailing "Guard one — what a sermon must not be" and "Guard two — what a sermon must not seek."
- **Symbolic Corruption Flow Diagram:** For "Third: stay away from the affluent and rulers" and "Fourth: do not accept gifts from rulers."
- **Comparative Audit Diagram:** For "First — the method of developing an intimate relationship with Allah" and "Second — the method of relating to the slaves of Allah."
- **Thought Experiment Sequence Diagram:** For "Third — an insistent advice to study and learn."
- **Spectrum/Scale Diagram:** For "Fourth — not stockpiling food."
- **Thematic Word Cloud/Cluster:** For "A supplication that may always be recited."

## Do not
- Restate the audio discussion in bullet-point slides.
- Produce "title + stock-photo illustration" slides.
- Use generic relationship labels ("influences", "relates to") — name the specific relationship.
