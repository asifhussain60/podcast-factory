# Eight Admonitions and a Closing Prayer

Knowledge without conduct turns against the scholar on Judgment Day. Imam al-Ghazali outlines eight admonitions: four to refuse, four to adopt. A daily supplication integrates this discipline into prayer.

## The Frame: Four to Leave, Four to Take Up

| Category | Description | Purpose |
| :------- | :---------- | :------ |
| **Refuse** | Four habits to avoid | Prevent knowledge from turning against the scholar |
| **Adopt** | Four disciplines to cultivate | Ensure knowledge is put to auditable conduct |
| **Principle** | Conduct, not knowledge alone, determines outcome | Each admonition addresses a specific trap or forgotten discipline |

## First: Refuse the Habit of Religious Debate

| Aspect | Description |
| :----- | :---------- |
| **Admonition** | Avoid religious debates and arguments (religious or otherwise) |
| **Reason** | Hidden harms outweigh benefits |
| **Harms** | Ostentation (Riya), jealousy, arrogance, resentment, enmity, pride, egotism |
| **Core Issue** | Soul cannot reliably distinguish defending truth from defending self |

### Test for Right Intention in Discussion

| Sign 1: Indifference to Source | Sign 2: Preference for Privacy |
| :----------------------------- | :----------------------------- |
| Truth emerging from self or opponent is equally satisfying | Prefer to discuss in private, away from an audience |
| Focus is solely on truth prevailing | Avoids human applause and show |

### Categories of Patients (Questioners)

Ghazali likens asking questions to describing heart diseases to a physician. Scholars are physicians; perfected scholars (Shaykh al-Kamil) can cure.

#### Patient Type 1: Jealousy and Envy

*   **Motivation:** Jealousy, envy
*   **Outcome:** Regard you as enemy, envy burns fiercely
*   **Ghazali's Instruction:** Turn away (Q2:264), no answer helps.
*   **Poetry:** "Every kind of animosity can be remedied — except the animosity that is born of jealousy."

#### Patient Type 2: Lack of Intelligence and Foolishness

*   **Motivation:** Incomprehension, brief knowledge, yet raises objections
*   **Example:** Prophet 'Isa could not cure the fool
*   **Ghazali's Instruction:** Keep away, offer no response.
*   **Issue:** Cannot grasp depth of scholarly insight.

#### Patient Type 3: Restlessness and Impatience

*   **Motivation:** Relies on deficient understanding, interprets according to personal interest
*   **Issue:** Simple-minded, intellect incapable of grasping truth
*   **Prophetic Saying:** "We, the community of prophets, have been commanded by Allah to speak to people according to the capacity of their minds."
*   **Ghazali's Instruction:** No answer, respect listener's capacity.

#### Patient Type 4: Genuine Seeker of Guidance

*   **Motivation:** Seeking Sirat al-Mustaqeem, obedient, sharp, intelligent, free from base desires
*   **Outcome:** Earnest search for truth
*   **Ghazali's Instruction:** Answering is obligatory (Wajib).

## Second: The Reality of Preaching

| Admonition | Principle |
| :--------- | :-------- |
| Refrain from preaching and making speeches | Preacher must first instruct own soul (Nafs) |
| | "O Son of Mary! First admonish your own soul... otherwise have shame before Me." (Prophet 'Isa) |

### Guard One: What a Sermon Must Not Be

*   **Avoid:**
    *   Colorful narrations, theatrical gestures
    *   Secretive language, flowery speech
    *   Pleasing poetry, embellished prose
    *   Talk transgressing Shari'ah bounds
    *   Fabrications of false Sufi claimants
*   **Reason:** Allah does not love pretentiousness; inner corruption, heedlessness of God.
*   **Purpose of a Sermon:**
    *   Speak of Hereafter's punishment
    *   Describe own shortcomings in servitude
    *   Express regret over wasted time
    *   Detail difficult stages of life to come (Angel of Death, grave questioning, Day of Judgment stations, Mizan, Sirat)
    *   Kindle fear of Hereafter, move to grieve over heedlessness.
*   **Analogy:** Urgent warning of a flood – no ornate language, only gravity and immediate action.

### Guard Two: What a Sermon Must Not Seek

*   **Avoid:**
    *   Desire for praise, ecstasy, clamor, declarations of "wonderful sermon"
*   **Reason:** Such thoughts are Riya (ostentation), proof of lack of wisdom.
*   **Right Intention:**
    *   Summon creation from world to Hereafter
    *   From sin to servitude of God
    *   From greed to detachment and contentment
    *   From miserliness to generosity
    *   From Riya to Ikhlas (sincerity)
    *   From arrogance to humility
    *   From heedlessness to God-consciousness
    *   From pride to piety
*   **Outcome:** Kindle love for Hereafter, loosen worldly attachment, cultivate piety and hope.
*   **Warning:** Sermon failing this becomes ruin for preacher and audience. Such a person is a Shaytan.
*   **Action:** Obligatory to remove such a preacher from the Minbar (pulpit) to prevent distortion of 'Amr bil Ma'roof Wa Nahi 'Anil Munkar.

## Third: Stay Away from the Affluent and Rulers

| Admonition | Reason |
| :--------- | :----- |
| Do not greet kings, wealthy, or rulers | Many hidden calamities |
| Stay away from their company and gatherings | Avoid looking at them |
| Refrain from praising them | Allah is angered when transgressors/oppressors are praised |
| | Loving an oppressor's life = loving perpetuation of sin on earth |

## Fourth: Do Not Accept Gifts from Rulers

| Admonition | Corruption Mechanism |
| :--------- | :------------------- |
| Do not accept gifts from rulers, even if Halal | Desire for possessions causes damage to Deen |
| | Favors lead to oppression, transgression, sin |
| **Minimum Corruption** | Begin to love oppressors |
| | Loving oppressors → praying for their long life |
| | Long life for oppressor → longer oppression, greater turmoil |
| **Shaytan's Lure** | "Take money, distribute to poor" |
| | This pious-sounding intention is a trap |
| | "Satan has shed the blood of many through this very strategy" |
| **Reference** | Ihya' 'Ulum ad-Din for detailed calamities of court-money |

## Four Matters Worthy of Our Actions

### First: Intimate Relationship with Allah

*   **Principle:** Conduct matters with Allah as you would expect a servant to conduct matters with you.
*   **If your servant...**
    *   ...did something you approved of: you would commend it.
    *   ...did something you disliked: you would be displeased.
*   **Asymmetry:**
    *   You merely employed your servant.
    *   You were created, nurtured, and owned by Allah.
*   **Obligation:** Your duty not to displease Allah (thought, word, deed) is immeasurably greater.

### Second: Relating to the Slaves of Allah

*   **Principle:** Conduct matters with others as you would wish them to conduct matters with you.
*   **Prophetic Hadith (Allah speaks):** "My slave's faith (Iman) is never complete until he loves for all human beings what he loves for himself."

### Third: Insistent Advice to Study and Learn

*   **Thought Experiment:** Imagine life ends in one week.
    *   **What you would NOT study:** Religious debate, lexicography, linguistics, grammar, poetry, composition.
    *   **Reason:** Such knowledge offers no benefit in final days.
    *   **What you WOULD do:** Devote to contemplation of heart, recognizing Nafs faults, making amends, purifying heart, worship.
*   **Second Image: King's Visit**
    *   **Scenario:** King visits next week.
    *   **Action:** Clean and adorn everything (body, clothing, house) to avoid anything unseemly.
*   **Application to Allah:**
    *   **Prophetic Hadith:** "Verily, Allah does not look at your faces or your outward actions, but He looks at your hearts and your intentions."
    *   **Question:** If the Supreme Ruler (Ahkamul Hakimeen) gazes on the heart, why is it left unpurified?
*   **Recommended Study:** Ihya al-Uloom ad-Deen and other works by Ghazali.
    *   **Reason:** Knowledge of heart matters is Fard al-'ayn (individual obligation).
    *   **Other Knowledge:** Fard Kifaya (communal obligation), acquired only to extent necessary for obeying Allah.
    *   **Condition:** If Allah grants Tawfeeq (divine enablement).

### Fourth: Not Stockpiling Food

*   **Admonition:** Do not accumulate more than one year's provision for your family.
*   **Prophetic Example:**
    *   Set aside one year's provision for *some* wives.
    *   Supplication: "O my Allah! Make this provision sufficient for the family of Muhammad."
    *   **Note:** Did *not* set aside for all wives.
    *   **Reason:** For wives with weaker Tawakkul (reliance on Allah).
    *   **Example:** 'Aisha Siddiqa (RA) and others with strong Tawakkul had no provisions set aside, not even for a day.
*   **Principle:** One year is a ceiling for those who hoard, not a floor for those with strong reliance.

## A Supplication That May Always Be Recited

Ghazali provides a supplication to be recited after every Salaat (obligatory daily prayer).

### Part 1: Seeking Abundance and Perfection

*   **From Allah:**
    *   Fullness and perfection of blessings
    *   Permanence of inward and outward purity
    *   All-encompassing breadth of mercy
    *   Attainment of health
    *   Increase in sustenance
    *   Happiness in life
    *   True and lasting success in this life
    *   Completion of noble qualities
    *   Most abundant reward
    *   Sweetest plenitude
    *   Most plentiful favor
    *   Best deed
    *   Most beneficial knowledge
    *   Most wholesome sustenance

### Part 2: Seeking Protection and Correction

*   **From Allah:**
    *   Be on our side, grant benefit, do not burden us, protect from harm
    *   Grant salvation at death
    *   Reform our deeds
    *   Grant safety in Your days and nights
    *   Extend mercy in homes and possessions
    *   Cover sins and faults with forgiveness
    *   Correct all shortcomings (inward and outward)
    *   Grant confidence and reliance upon You

### Part 3: Seeking Steadfastness and Deliverance

*   **From Allah:**
    *   Grant steadfastness in Deen
    *   Save from deeds causing shame on Judgment Day
    *   Lighten burden of sins
    *   Grant life of the pious
    *   Be sufficient in all affairs
    *   Protect from evil of evildoers
    *   Liberate us and forebears from Hellfire, debt, oppression

### Part 4: Invocation of Divine Names

*   **Names Invoked:**
    *   Ya Aziz (Victorious One)
    *   Ya Ghaffar (Forgiving One)
    *   Ya Kareem (Generous One)
    *   Ya Sattar (Coverer of Faults)
    *   Ya Haleem (Forbearing One)
    *   Ya Jabbar (Compeller)
    *   Ya Azeem (Magnificent One)
    *   Ya Qahhar (Who Subdues all things)
    *   Allah! Allah! Allah!
    *   Ya Rahman (Compassionate One in this world)
    *   Ya Raheem (Merciful One in the life hereafter)
    *   Ya Arhamu ar-Rahimeen (Greatest of the Merciful)
*   **Closing:** Mercy and blessings upon Muhammad, his descendants, and companions. All praise to Allah, Rabb ul-'Alameen.

The supplication mirrors the letter's structure: begins with abundance, moves to correction, closes with mercy. The disciple is sent forth with this discipline for daily prayer.
