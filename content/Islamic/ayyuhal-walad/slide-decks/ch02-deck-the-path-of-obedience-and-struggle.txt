## The Dawn Watch: Summons and Witnesses

Allah's Pre-Dawn Proclamation:
"Is there anyone who repents from sins?" → "Is there anyone who asks of Me?" → "Is there anyone who seeks My forgiveness?"

Quranic Witnesses:
*   Q51:18: *Kanu Qaleelum Minal Laili Ma Yahja'oon*
    *   "Few servants sleep little in the last part of the night."
*   Q17:79: *Waminal Laili Fatahajjad Bihi Nafilatal Laka*
    *   "Perform Tahajjud prayers for Allah's sake; great benefit."
*   Q3:17: *Wul Mustaghfireena Bil Ashaari*
    *   "True servants seek Allah's forgiveness at dawn."

Prophetic Witnesses:
*   Prophet ﷺ on 'Abdullah ibn 'Umar: "Good man. If only he prayed Tahajjud."
*   Prophet ﷺ to Companion: "Do not sleep overmuch at night; leaves keeper empty-handed on Day of Judgment."

Sounds Pleasing to Allah:
1.  Crowing of the rooster (rouses for Tahajjud)
2.  Recitation of the Holy Quran
3.  Voices pleading for forgiveness at night

## The Four Proclamations of the Night

| Time of Night     | Angel's Proclamation                                  | Responders                                                              |
| :---------------- | :---------------------------------------------------- | :---------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Night begins      | "All worshippers should rise!"                        | Those granted *tawfiq* (divine enablement)                              |
| Middle of night   | "All obedient and devoted servants of Allah should rise!" | Continue in prayer until *sahr* (last portion)                          |
| *Sahr* (pre-dawn) | "All who seek Allah's forgiveness should rise!"       | Rise and seek forgiveness                                               |
| Dawn draws near   | "O heedless ones! Arise — the day has broken!"        | Rise from beds "as the dead will rise from their graves"                |

## Luqman to his Son: The Rooster as Teacher

Luqman's Counsel:
"My son! Do not let the rooster prove more intelligent than you. Let it not be that he rises in the last part of the night to proclaim the hour of remembrance and glorifies his Lord, while you remain sunk in the deep sleep of heedlessness, carelessness, and negligence."

Poetic Indictment:
*   Dove calls to Lord in night; I sleep.
*   Claim love, but eyes dry while animals weep.
*   "By the Lord of the Ka'ba! I am a liar!"

## Worship IS Obedience

Worship Definition:
Following the *Shari'ah* of Prophet Muhammad ﷺ.

| Aspect of Life | Prophetic Frame (Obedience)                                     | Outside Prophetic Frame (Sin)                                           |
| :------------- | :-------------------------------------------------------------- | :---------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Action         | Lawful intimacy (halal)                                         | Fasting on Eid days or Hajj days                                        |
| Prayer         | Performed at permissible times, on lawful ground                | Performed at forbidden times (e.g., after Asr), on unlawfully occupied place |
| Outcome        | Act of obedience, earns reward                                  | Act of transgression (*athim*, *fasiq*)                                 |

Principle:
*   No neutral act for a believer.
*   Every act is either obedience or transgression.
*   Test: Does the act sit *inside* the Prophetic frame?
*   Prophetic frame abrogated all prior dispensations.

## The Sword of Mujahadah

Path of Allah:
Not through accumulated knowledge alone → Requires *mujahadah* (intense inner spiritual struggle).

*Nafs* (Lower Self):
Desires and demands → Must be *severed* with the sword of *mujahadah*.

False Sufis vs. True Sufis:
*   **False Sufis:** Claim station, mimic language, hollow within.
*   **True Sufis:** Attained intimate relationship with God, eradicated evil through genuine *mujahadah*.

Warning:
*   Allah does not delight in philosophy amid sin.
*   Tongue of wisdom + heart enslaved to heedlessness = gravest misfortune.

Outcome of *Mujahadah*:
Unless desires of lower self are severed → Light of *ma'rifah* (intimate knowledge of Allah) will not dawn in the heart.

## The Four Conditions of the Sincere Seeker

1.  **Conviction of Fear of Allah**
    *   Prevents involvement in *bid'a* (innovation contrary to Quran/Sunnah).
    *   Includes Prophet's teachings, actions, conduct, inner states (contentment, patience, gratitude, certitude, *Ma'arifah*, love of God).

2.  **True and Complete Repentance (*Taubatun Nasuh*)**
    *   Never return to the humiliation of sins.

3.  **Right Dealing with Others**
    *   Satisfy even enemies.
    *   No unfulfilled obligations against the seeker.

4.  **Exact Discipline of Study**
    *   Acquire enough *Shari'ah* knowledge for personal obligations.
    *   Other branches of knowledge: only what is obligatory.
    *   Beyond personal obligation: *Fard Kifayah* (communal obligation).

Shibli's Hadith (from 400 masters, 4,000 *ahadith*, acted on one):
"Work for this world as much as you will live in it,
and work for the life hereafter as much as you wish to live there,
and work for Allah as much as you are in need of Him,
and work toward avoiding the Fire as much as you can bear its pain and sufferings."

Lesson:
Not vast quantity of knowledge → Small set of teachings, acted upon, reorders life.
