# Slide Framing: The Disciple's Rule of Life

## Purpose
This slide deck must visually represent the interconnected components of the disciple's rule of life, showing how inward principles, the spiritual guide, and specific inner realities form a cohesive framework for spiritual development. It needs to illustrate the dynamic relationship between observation, Quranic reflection, and action, and clarify the rigorous qualifications and role of a spiritual guide.

## Steering phrases (apply all of the following)
- Diagram the "looking, weighing, and changing" process for each of Hatim's benefits.
- Use a "chain of authority" diagram to show the unbroken lineage of spiritual guidance.
- Create a "qualifications checklist" visual for the Shaykh al-Kamil.
- Employ a "boundary condition" diagram to illustrate the Shari'ah's role in obedience to the guide.
- Use a "three-part definition" diagram for Tasawwuf, servitude, and Tawakkul.

## Slide types required
- **Process Flow Diagram:** For Hatim's eight benefits, showing the sequence of "I looked and saw X," "I contemplated this verse," and "Therefore I did Y."
- **Hierarchical/Chain Diagram:** Illustrating the unbroken chain of spiritual guidance from the Messenger of Allah to the Shaykh al-Kamil.
- **Checklist/Attribute List Diagram:** Detailing the specific qualities of a true Shaykh, distinguishing them from impostors.
- **Boundary/Constraint Diagram:** Visually representing the Shari'ah as the absolute boundary for obedience to the spiritual guide.
- **Multi-part Definition Diagram:** For Tasawwuf, servitude, and Tawakkul, showing their constituent elements.
- **Cause-and-Effect Diagram:** For Ikhlas, illustrating how a metaphysical understanding of Allah's power cures Riya.
- **Journey/Path Diagram:** To represent the idea that answers come through practice, not premature questioning, culminating in "traveling the earth to see."

## Do not
- Restate the audio discussion in bullet-point slides.
- Produce "title + stock-photo illustration" slides.
- Use generic relationship labels ("influences", "relates to") — name the specific relationship.
