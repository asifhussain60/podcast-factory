# Show notes — EP01

**Title:** Knowledge Without Action

**Blurb:** The opening of Imam al-Ghazali's letter *Ayyuha al-Walad* — the diagnosis half of his reply to a long-time disciple who wrote asking which of the many sciences he had been taught would actually save him on the Day of Judgment. Ghazali refuses the assumption that more teaching is the deficit (*if you have not received benefit from the counsels of the Prophet, then tell me — what knowledge have you truly acquired from me?*) and presses a single verdict from seven angles: the Prophet's three warnings on useless knowledge and the forty-year clock and the scholar's severest torment; the dream of Shaykh Junaid Baghdadi whose lifetime of discourse weighed nothing while a small number of voluntary Tahajjud prayers were of benefit; the lion-in-the- jungle parable and the unswallowed medicine; the Quranic verses on the atom's weight and the verses of faith joined to action; the hadith that Islam is *built upon* five pillars and that faith is testimony-tongue-heart-and-action-of-limbs; the careful clarification that this is not works-righteousness but the condition of receiving Mercy (*the Mercy of Allah is close to the doers of good*); and the story of the worshipper among the Children of Israel whose unconditional service drew the forgiveness the angel's test had pretended to withhold. The episode closes on the Prophetic hinge — *take account of yourselves before you are taken to account* — and the three closing witnesses on acting without self-admiration. The lion is coming; the swords are in the hand; whether the arm will swing is the work of the next episode.
