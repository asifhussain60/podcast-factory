# Show notes — EP02

**Title:** The Path of Obedience and Struggle

**Blurb:** The second half of the opening movement of *Ayyuha al-Walad* — the pivot from diagnosis to cure. Ghazali turns the disciple from the verdict that knowledge alone will not save him to the inhabited practice of the dawn watch: Hasan al-Basri's involuntary sigh at the cold cup; the standing pre-dawn proclamation *is there anyone who repents, anyone who asks of Me, anyone who seeks My forgiveness?*; the Quranic command addressed directly to the Prophet ﷺ to perform *Tahajjud*; the four angels of the four stations of the night; the wind Allah has created that carries the voices of His servants at the hour before dawn; and Luqman's testament to his own son not to let the rooster prove more intelligent than he. From the dawn watch Ghazali turns to the principle that worship is another name for obedience to the Prophetic *Shari'ah*, and works it out through four edge-cases — the fast on Eid as sin, the prayer at a forbidden hour as transgression, the prayer on unlawfully occupied ground, and the lawful intimacy between husband and wife that the uninformed would judge as appetite — to establish that the Prophetic frame is the test of whether any act is worship at all. He then turns to the inner condition: the Path cannot be travelled by accumulated knowledge, only by *mujahadah*, and the lower self must be severed with the sword of struggle. The polemic against the simulation of Sufism — and the parenthetical defense of its true practice — closes that movement. The episode ends on Ghazali's first answer to the disciple's second question: the four conditions of the sincere seeker, sealed by Shibli's hadith of the four proportions which contains *the knowledge of all who came first and all who came last*. The disciple now knows what watch to keep, what rule to follow, what struggle to wage, and what four conditions frame the life.
