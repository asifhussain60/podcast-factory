# Eight Admonitions and a Closing Prayer

Host A (male) = scholar/teacher. Host B (female) = seeker/questioner. Roles do not rotate.

## Opening directive

Brief warm welcome in the seeker's voice — greet the listener, name the work (the master's letter *O Beloved Son*), preview in one sentence: the master hands the disciple eight contours — four to refuse, four to take up — so knowledge does not turn against him on the Day of Judgment. Forbidden: "Welcome to our…", "in this episode", "let's dive in".

The scholar lands the spine verbatim: **Of these eight admonitions, there are four things you must do and four things you must not do — so that your knowledge does not turn against you on the Day of Judgment.** It returns verbatim, three times — opening, pivot, and close.

## Name discipline

Rotation: Roles do not rotate. Host A holds the scholar voice throughout; Host B holds the seeker voice throughout.

- The author of the letter → **the master**. First mention: 'the master'.
- The Prophet Muhammad → **the Prophet**. First mention: 'the Prophet, peace and blessings of Allah be upon him and his family'.
- 'Isa → **Jesus**. First mention: 'Jesus, peace be upon him'. Do not repeat the honorific later in the conversation.
- 'Aisha → **Aisha**. First mention: 'Aisha, may Allah be pleased with her'.
- The Prophet's wives → **the Mothers of the Believers**.
- The four kinds of questioner → **the jealous, the foolish, the impatient simple-minded, the genuine seeker**.
- Sahah Sitta → **the six most authenticated collections**.
- The larger work → **the revival of religious knowledge**.

## Three-part focus

**Frame and four refrainings.** Land the spine, then walk: debate (two signs of the narrow exception — indifference to whose tongue truth comes from, preference for no audience — then the four patients, only the genuine seeker being *Wajib* to answer); preaching (two guards — what a sermon must carry, what the preacher's heart must not seek); courting rulers (praying for an oppressor's long life prays for oppression prolonged); refusing their gifts even routed through the poor — that pious thought is Satan's bait.

**Pivot and four takings-up.** Land the spine verbatim a second time. Then: the household-servant analogy — you were not the employer, your Real Creator was; the sacred hadith *my slave's faith is never complete until he loves for all human beings what he loves for himself*; focused study under the one-week-to-live thought and the king-is-visiting image, anchored by *Allah does not look at your faces or your outward actions, but at your hearts and your intentions*; not stockpiling beyond a year's provision — for the Mothers of the Believers of perfect reliance, the Prophet set aside nothing.

**Supplication and close.** One supplication to recite after every *Salaat* — abundance, correction, eleven divine names. Land the spine verbatim a third time. The master closes naming *the Absolute Sovereign* and *the Giver of all*. The seeker turns one fresh reflective question toward the listener — which contour to begin auditing this week. Let it hang.

## Tone constraints

Three governing analogies, not multiplied: (1) the household-servant — what you tolerate from your servant audits your conduct with Allah; (2) the one-week-to-live thought; (3) the king-is-visiting house-cleaning — the gaze that matters rests on the heart. Source images (floodwater, Satan on the pulpit) may pass in quote, not extend. No invented imagery. Quotes verbatim.

## Anti-noise rules

- R-RECURRING-THESIS: the spine sentence above is voiced verbatim, three times — opening, pivot, and close. No paraphrase.
- Do not open with biographical or historical background. A biographical detail is permitted only when it directly illuminates a specific teaching, stated once, never recapped.
- Route every figure through the stable English role-label established under Name discipline.

## Do not

Do not use: Twitter, X, social media, algorithm, internet, YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, hashtag, modern world, platforms like, podcast, influencer. wow, right?, it's profound, mind blown. today's episode, today we'll discuss, let's dive in, journey into, have you ever wondered, in a world where. No tidy resolutions, no self-help register, no cross-episode references. Never abbreviate honorifics. Never pair the leadership-title with the personal name of the Father of Imams.
