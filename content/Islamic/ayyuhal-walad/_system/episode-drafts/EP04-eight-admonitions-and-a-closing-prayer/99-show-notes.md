# Show notes — EP04

**Title:** Eight Admonitions and a Closing Prayer

**Blurb:** The master's closing brief to his beloved disciple. Eight admonitions, structured as four things to refrain from and four things to actively attend to — so that knowledge does not turn against the scholar on the Day of Judgment. The four refrainings: do not engage in religious debate (with the two narrow signs of right intention if the rare licit argument arises, and the long aside on the four categories of patients — only the obedient, sharp, intelligent seeker is worth answering); do not preach (with the *Ya Ibna Maryama* address to Jesus and the *Al-Hazr! Al-Hazr!* flood image governing what a sermon must be — fear, the Hereafter, Munkar and Nakir, the Mizan, the Sirat — and the *summoning of God's creation from this toward that* litany governing what the preacher's heart must seek); do not greet or even look upon kings, the wealthy, or rulers (with the hadith that praying for an oppressor's long life is, in effect, praying for the prolongation of oppression); and do not accept their gifts even from lawful sources (with the *Shaytan*-counsel about routing money through the poor as the lure itself). The four takings-up: an intimate relationship with Allah audited by the household-servant analogy; right relating to the slaves of Allah audited by the sacred hadith *my slave's faith is never complete until he loves for all human beings what he loves for himself*; focused study under the one-week-to-live thought experiment and the king-is-visiting house-cleaning image anchored by *Allah does not look at your faces or your outward actions, but at your hearts and your intentions*; and not accumulating more than one year's provision, with the Prophet ﷺ's calibrated practice for the Azwaj al-Mutahharat and 'Aisha Siddiqa. The chapter closes with the long supplication the disciple is to recite after every Salaat — the abundance block, the correction block, and the eleven divine names sealing the letter — and with the master's final naming of Al-Malik and Al-Wahhab as the help under which the book has been written. The letter ends here.
