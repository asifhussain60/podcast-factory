# The Sphere That Rises From Where It Stands

The second question came from what Imam Ahmad bin Yusuf had taught concerning the *falak al-mustaqeem* (the straight sphere), the *muheet* (the encompassing one), and the saying `wa anna noor al-shamsi min noor al-'azeem` (and that the light of the sun is from the magnificent light). The questioner states that he benefited (*istifadah*) from his *sayyid* (master), from his *madadgaar* (helper), and from his Imam, meaning the one upon whom he relies, Ahmad bin Yusuf. In that era he must have been one of the *hudood* (ranks of the religious hierarchy). From him the questioner learned of the *falak-e-mustaqeem*, that is, the *falak-e-muheet* (the encompassing sphere), which is the highest sphere and is *ghayr-mar'i* (invisible). It is after this *falak-e-mustaqeem* that the *'alam-e-ruhani* (spiritual world) begins. And the light of the sun derives from its light, meaning from the light of the *falak-e-mustaqeem* or *falak-e-muheet*.

And from the Imam came the further inquiry: `wa ma ya'oodu amr al-falak al-mustaqeem` (and what returns of the affair of the straight sphere). What will be the state of the *falak-e-mustaqeem* `'inda irtiqa' al-rubaataat` (at the time of the ascension of the *rubaataat*)? That is, at the end of the *kawr* (cycle), when all things come to their conclusion and the skies fall, what will be the state of the *falak-e-mustaqeem* at that time? `Wa ayna yaseeru` (And where will it go?), and where will it proceed? `Wa ayna su'ooduhu` (And where will its ascent be?), and what will its ascent be like? `Wa ma alladhi yuwasilu ilaihi al-ayimmah minhu` (And what is it that the Imams attain from it?), that is, what will the *ayimmah* (Imams) attain from the *falak-e-mustaqeem*? `Faqad qeela innahu yas'adu` (It has been said that it will ascend), and it is said `innahu yas'adu min mawdi'ihi` (it will ascend from its own place), that the *falak-e-mustaqeem* or *falak-e-muheet* will ascend from right where it is.

The principle in such matters is otherwise. Whoever must ascend, whoever must progress, must first come to the earth and then advance. The state of the *falak-e-mustaqeem* is different in this respect: it will ascend from exactly where it stands. Yet this remains a question, and reliance is not placed on the question itself; reliance will rest upon the answer to be given.

The questioner then closes: `Wa munni 'alayya` (And bestow upon me), that is, do this favor upon me, `wa jud 'ala mithli man yurhamu` (and be generous to one like me who is shown mercy), be generous to one such as me, upon whom mercy is shown. `Warham man laisa lahu rahimun siwak` (And have mercy on one who has no merciful one besides You), have mercy, for there is no one to show him mercy besides You. `Wa la malan illa iyyaka` (And no refuge but You), and none will treasure or benefit him besides You.

`Al-jawab 'ala dhalik al-su'al` (The answer to that question). It is the *da'i* (summoner) Sayyidna Hibatullah who provides the answer.

The question, restated, concerned the *falak-e-mustaqeem*: the concept was that it would depart from its place. And there was also the question of what the *ayimmah* would attain from it, and whether the light of the sun truly derives from the light of the *falak-e-mustaqeem*. The questioner raised all of these points.

The answer begins: `Aqoolu bi 'awnillah al-kabeer al-muta'al` (I say with the help of Allah, the Great, the Most High), `wa minnati wali al-'asr wa al-zaman` (and by the grace of the Master of the Age and Time), that is, by the bestowal of the Master of the Age and Time, `alayhi salawat al-raheem al-rahman` (upon him be the blessings of the Merciful, the Compassionate), `wa ta'eed-e mawlaya al-a'lam` (and by the support of my most learned master), that is, by the support of Mawlaya al-A'lam. By *a'lam* is meant Mawlana Hibatullah; that is his title. `Wa sirrihi al-baahir al-mu'azzam` (and by his manifest and magnificent secret), that is, by his luminous and lofty secret, `anna al-falak al-mustaqeem` (that the straight sphere), `wa anna noor al-shams min noorihi al-'azeem` (and that the light of the sun is from its magnificent light): the light of the sun derives from the magnificent light of the *falak-e-mustaqeem*. `Fa dhalika ka dhalika` (So that is as it is), that is, the matter as conceived is precisely so.

`Wa la budda an yuwasiluha shay'un min anwar al-qalb fi jamee' al-masalik` (And it is necessary that something of the lights of the heart reach it in all the paths). It is necessary that the light of the sun receive lights from the lights of the heart. By "heart" is meant the Baytullah (House of Allah) that stands opposite its heavenly counterpart, the *taamur*; from that Baytullah, lights are received by the sun. This is why the pronoun used here is feminine, *yuwasiluha*, it will receive them. `Fi jamee' al-masalik` (In all the paths), that is, at every moment and in every path.

`Wa qawluka ma yakoonu haal falak al-mustaqeem 'inda irtiqa' al-rubaataat` (And your saying: what will be the state of the straight sphere at the time of the ascension of the *rubaataat*?). At the end of the *kawr*, when the final matter comes to pass, what will be the condition of the *falak-e-mustaqeem*? `Wa yas'adu min mawdi'ihi` (And it will ascend from its place). The understanding is correct: it will ascend from its own place.

It will pass into the tenth *barzakh* (intermediate realm), the tenth *'arsh* (throne) that is made, though this point is not elaborated. And it will ascend from there. `Wa yakoonu 'aqlan qa'iman bil-fi'l` (And it will become an intellect existing in actuality). It will become an intellect that is `qa'iman bil-fi'l` (existing in actuality). Until that point it was `qa'iman bil-quwwah` (existing in potentiality): it was helping others, sustaining the lights of the sun, and the *ayimmah* were receiving assistance from it, and all of this was occurring `bil-quwwah` (in potentiality). Its state `bil-fi'l` (in actuality) will be that it ascends as an intellect.

`Fi jamee' al-haalat wa la budda... wa yakoonu lahu ruhun min noorin... wa maqamuhu ruhun... mashkoorah lahu...` (In all states and it is necessary... and it will have a spirit of light... and its station will be a spirit... acknowledged for it...). Its station will be that of a spirit, for which the full explanation is lengthy. `Ma yuheetu biha illa al-maqaamaat al-nooraniyyah` (None can encompass it except the luminous stations). Its state cannot be encompassed except through the luminous stations, meaning the words of the *ayimmah*. `Aw man kaana min da'ina... al-mutasawwir bi suwar al-imaniyyah... al-muhawwin lil-mushkilat` (Or one who was from our *da'i*s... who are envisioned in the forms of faith... who make the difficult things easy), that is, only our *da'i*, one envisioned in the forms of faith, who renders religious difficulties accessible. `Wal-'ati bil-khawariq al-'adat` (And the one who comes with extraordinary feats), that is, one who possesses great and extraordinary states.

`Wa laqad kashafa lana ma la yakshifuhu` (And indeed he has unveiled for us what he does not unveil), that is, he has unveiled for us what others do not unveil. `Wa tafaddala 'alayna ma la yuhaat` (And he has bestowed upon us what cannot be encompassed), that is, he has bestowed upon us that which cannot be fully encompassed. `Wa la yukayyaf` (and cannot be described), that is, whose nature cannot be described. `Jazahumullahu 'anna ma jaza ahl al-du'at` (May Allah reward them on our behalf as He has rewarded the people of the *du'at*), that is, may he receive the same reward from Allah that the *du'at* (summoners) receive, meaning Sayyidna Hibatullah. `Wa 'ammalana min sirrihi wa barakatihi` (And benefit us from his secret and his blessings), that is, may we receive benefit from his secret and his blessings. `Innahu waliyu dhalika` (Indeed, He is the master of that), that is, He is capable of this matter and is its master. `Ala ma hunalika` (Over what is there).

`Wa amma rooh al-imam al-noorani` (And as for the spirit of the luminous Imam), `wa shabhuhu al-qudsani... fa'lam anna dhalika min asrarillah allati la tusattaru bil-aqlaam` (and his sacred likeness... so know that that is from the secrets of Allah which cannot be written with pens). This matter is among the secrets of the Lord, and these are things that cannot be committed to writing with pens. `Wa dhalika anna al-maqam 'alayhi al-salat wa al-salam mata haanat nuqlatuhu ila dar al-na'eem al-muqeem, tahallala fil-qalb` (And that is because the station, upon him be prayers and peace, whenever the time of its transfer comes to the abode of bliss, the everlasting, it dissolves into the heart). That is, when the time of the Imam's passing (ع) arrives and he departs to the everlasting abode of bliss, his soul dissolves into the heart, meaning into the Baytullah. From there his ascent takes place.

The same holds for the *mu'min* (believer) at burial. The *mu'min*'s ascent also occurs through the Baytullah, and his *fuzala'* (excellent parts) will ascend through it. The *surat* (form) of the *mu'min*, his form, ascends to the *hudood-e-'uliyyah* (higher ranks), while his *riyat* (subtle essences) enter the *aflak* (spheres), passing into the sun and Jupiter and so on. As for his *fuzala'*, they ascend gradually, in a state of prayer, until they reach the *mihrab* (prayer niche) where he used to pray in the mosque, to that very station where he had established his place of worship. They ascend to reach that *mihrab*. From there they proceed to the center, which is the center of his *jazirah* (island). For us, the center of the two *jazirahs* is said to be the tomb of Sayyidna Sulaiman. From there, his ascent continues through twenty-eight stations until he enters the Baytullah. And within the Baytullah there are four pillars; ascending through them, he passes to each pillar in turn and finally arrives at the *rukn-e-aswad* (Black Stone corner), from which his ultimate ascent takes place. These *fuzala'*, in their ascending state, are called `zu'ubat`. Once they have ascended, they become `zakha'ir` (treasures).

The four pillars are the *asatir*, and they are the *atqiyah* (pious ones), present in their physical form.
