# Offerings, Trials, and the Making of a Da'i

Another matter concerns the wife of an Imam whose name was Aziza Bu. Her grave too is present in the mausoleum of Maulana Shahid Saheb Muqaddas, inside the *rawdah* (tomb). She is Aziza Bu bint Qasim Khan, the *zawjah* (wife) of Muhammad Ali bin Swaleh Badri, the first *mansoob* (high-ranking official), who is buried in Ghash. She was so wealthy that she had trade in seven cities. This was from the time of Sayyidna Hibatullah. Every year she used to send her *fitrah* (religious dues), and whatever the *fitrah* was prescribed at that time, whatever the Dai had prescribed, she would send a thousand times that amount. A thousand times more. In this way, for several years, she sent *fitrah* to Sayyidna Saheb. The *mansoob* of that time was instructed to take counsel from her and to keep her with him in consultation. It was said to Shaykh Yasin, who was the *mansoob* in that era, that he should not carry out any affairs of the *da'wat* (mission) without the counsel of Sayyidati Aziza Bu. Because he went astray by not heeding his wife's counsel, he was ultimately removed from the *da'wat*. Removed. Such a great station she had.

Concerning those who go astray, Amir al-Momineen has a saying: do not look at how those who went astray went astray, but see how those who were saved were saved. This is his saying, and in a poem he has said: see how those who were saved were saved, and give attention to that.

There are many such incidents. Dawood bin Fee was a very great scholar. But this was his flaw: he said, "I am greater in knowledge than Sayyidna Hibatullah Saheb." The *nass* (designation) for Sayyidna Hibatullah Saheb was performed by Sayyidna Muhammad bin Isma'il, whose uncle Sayyidna Hibatullah Saheb was. Sayyidna Muhammad bin Isma'il bin Muhammad bin Ibrahim bin Muhammad bin Fahd: Sayyidna Ibrahim's son was Isma'il, and *nass* had been performed upon him, but Sayyidna Isma'il passed away in his lifetime. So *nass* was performed on his grandson, Muhammad bin Isma'il, who holds a very great station. Among the fifty-four or fifty-five *Du'at* (plural of Dai), he is the only Dai who led prayers outside the Arabian Peninsula (*kharij-e-jazirah*), for Sayyidna Muhammad bin Isma'il led prayers in Habasha (Abyssinia) by the Imam's command. He designated his uncle, Sayyidna Hibatullah bin Ibrahim, performing *nass* upon him.

At that time Dawood bin Fee was in Najran, and his claim was that he was greater in knowledge. Sayyidna Hibatullah Saheb had written a letter to be read out to the *ikhwan* (brethren). But Dawood bin Fee did not make that letter public; he did not read it out. Then some *ikhwan* went from there on the side of Sayyidna Saheb, and Arabs came here, for in the time of Sayyidna Hibatullah Saheb there was much coming and going, as there were many migrants. So it was found out that he had not read out the letter at all. *Itab* (censure) was then sent down. Later he repented. Dawood bin Fee repented, and Sayyidna Saheb also forgave him, keeping him in the order of the faithful, among the *momineen*. He was given leave to return, and while going back he passed away at a place called Wasit. He passed away on the way.

Abdul Majid and others went astray in the time of the Imams. Abdul Majid, Dawood Qutub, these were people of great station, and yet they went astray. The one who progressed and was saved was Sayyidna Muhammad bin Fahd. Sayyidna Sulayman Saheb performed *nass-e-sharif* (noble designation) on Sayyidna Ja'far, but he did not write the name of the *mustawda'* (trustee). He left the name blank, saying, "I will clarify it later." And when he performed the second *nass-e-sharif*, he then wrote the name of Sayyidna Muhammad bin Fahd. The hallmark of Sayyidna Muhammad bin Fahd was that he took charge of the *da'wat* and remained steadfast; his feet did not waver at all. For this reason he is called *Ghadab fil Da'wat* (Zeal in the Mission). The *jaza* given to him was this: in recognition of the way he cared for Sayyidna Ja'far and safeguarded the *da'wat*, for Sayyidna Ja'far was only eleven years old at the time of Sayyidna Sulayman Saheb's death, and he taught him, educated him, and made him capable, the *jaza* given was that until *Qiyamah* (the Day of Resurrection) there would be *Du'at* in his progeny. This *jaza* was given.

So too with Sayyidna Ali bin Muhammad bin Walid, who is the fifth Dai. They were very great elders. The *jaza* given to him was this: Sayyidna Ali bin Hatim studied with him. That era had many wars, and with the Zaydis there were many wars. In those wars, Sayyidna Hatim, *qaddasallahu rawahahu*, performed *nass-e-sharif* on Sayyidna Ali bin Muhammad Walid. Sayyidna Ali bin Muhammad Walid said to him, "Maulana, please reconsider. Why not perform *nass* on your own son?" Sayyidna Hatim said, "Brother, you know that we did not get a chance to prepare our son for this position, because all our time was spent in wars and their concerns; in that preparation the time passed." He said, "You should at least test your son." So the Dai administered a very difficult test. Sayyidna Ali bin Hatim was asked very high-level questions, and it was seen that he possessed great substance (*johar*). He gave all the answers. Sayyidna Hatim bin Ibrahim was left speechless. He asked Sayyidna Ali bin Muhammad Walid, "How did he acquire this knowledge?" He said, "Maulana, during the wars that used to happen, we were occupied by day, but there was not a single night, not a single night, that he slept without my teaching him and his studying." A Dai was prepared on the battlefield. Because of this state, Sayyidna Hatim then performed *nass*. And then Sayyidna Ali bin Hatim performed *nass* upon his own teacher. The goodwill shown, with sincerity of intention, had this *jaza*: in his progeny the *Du'at* of Bani Anf continued. Seventeen *Du'at* came, seventeen in all. Today's Dai is approximately the nineteenth or twentieth from the progeny of Sayyidna Muhammad bin Fahd. Sayyidna Ahmed is also from that progeny, from the progeny of Sayyidna Muhammad bin Fahd. Sayyidna Ali bin Hasim is also from his progeny, the twentieth or twenty-first Dai. Sayyidna Taher Saifuddin used to recite this verse often for them:

> *Antum al-anf wa ghayrukum al-dhanab*

You are the nose, and others are the tail. He would often recite it for them.

As for why the name *Anf* (nose) came, *Anf* means nose. In the time of Maulana Ma'add al-Mustansir, the eighteenth Imam, a person used to come from Yemen who had great closeness to the Imam. He would stay with the Imam for a few days and then return to Yemen. The ministers (*wuzara*) there, for they had a kingdom and were ministers, were astonished: "We are so close to the Imam, but who is this person who becomes even closer to the Imam than us? The Imam honors him so much." So one day they asked the Imam, "Who is this person whom you honor so highly and show such regard?" He said:

> *Hadha anf min Yemen*

This is a nose from Yemen. His name was Ali bin Ibrahim, but the name Anf came from the Imam's own words. His progeny were called Bani Anf. That was in the time of the eighteenth Imam, and many centuries later these became Bani Anf.

Their story is also this. The Rasulullah was engaged in a *jihad* (battle) on a high place, on a mountain, with his companions. He saw that in the oasis below, in the valley, the army of the *kuffar* (unbelievers) was gathering slowly. He told his companions, "Look, these *kuffar* are coming to confront us. Among them, the one who is visible on a red camel," and he mentioned his name, perhaps it was Walid, "that person you are seeing is a very base and vile person who has come to confront the Rasul of the time. But in his progeny, very righteous and pious people will be born." Such was the vision of the Rasulullah. This was recorded in history. An investigation then began as to whose progeny this was, and it was found that Ali bin Ibrahim is from his progeny, and then the *Du'at* of Bani Anf are from his progeny. Such is the manner in which God gives *jaza*.

But now no one remains from their progeny. There are two houses in Najran which belong to Bani Anf; the entire Bani Anf became Zaydi. A family from which seventeen *Du'at* came, and the state of their descendants was such that all of them went astray and became Zaydi. So it is: *Allahu yahdi man yasha' wa yudillu*, Allah guides whom He wills and leads astray whom He wills. This is the absolute truth, and it happened so. Two houses in Najran belong to Bani Anf; the rest all became Zaydi.

During the time of Sayyidna Yusuf, when he was appointed as Dai, one from Bani Anf sought to become the Dai, something of that sort happened. In his time there was Sayyidna Muhammad bin Husayn, in Yusuf's time, who is the last Dai of Bani Anf. He is the twenty-fourth Dai, the last Dai of Bani Anf.

There is another matter concerning a Dai whose name is difficult to recall, one also from Yemen, whose family are bankers. The surname appears among the *Du'at*. It is a very fine name, and he is very well known, among the owners of NCB, the National Commercial Bank.

That too is in *Mahfuz*. His name is Ali bin Hanzala al-Mahfuz. Ali bin Hanzala is a very high-ranking *da'i*, and the *risalah* (treatise) that he wrote is named *Simt al-Haqa'iq*. There is another *risalah*, and there is only one such *risalah*, in which the punishments to be visited upon the *kuffar* (unbelievers), the *mushrikin* (polytheists), and the enemies of the *Awliya Allah* (friends of God) are mentioned. Only one such *risalah* exists. As for Sayyidna Husayn bin Hasan, when he would perform the *ratiqah* he would recite the names of all the *du'at*, from Sayyidna Dhu'ayb bin Musa all the way to Sayyidna Jamali Ali bin Husayn al-Makhrami, taking their names with their *alqab* (honorific titles). The *ratiqah*s would therefore be very long, half an hour at a stretch. And when the name of Sayyidna Ali bin Hanzala would come, he would say it thus: Sayyidna Ali bin Hanzala, *Sahib Rutbat al-Fadl* (The Possessor of the Rank of Grace). That was the *laqab* he would use.

In the matter of *fiqah*, there is the case of Sayyidna Ibrahim, *`alayh al-salam*, Muqaddas. When he went there, to Najran, though it was not Najran then but Badr at that time, and after he reached the *da'i*, a few days later the *da'i* said that he had to pay his *fiqah* (religious offering). He was very, very poor. He said, "Mawlana, I have not paid the *fiqah* yet." The *da'i* said, "You have to pay the *fiqah*." He had only two sets of clothes, one kept for ordinary days and the other kept for Jumu'ah (Friday) and special occasions. He presented that good set of clothes as *fiqah* to the *da'i*. This is also a historical matter: fine clothing was presented as *fiqah* to the *da'i*, and only one set of clothes remained, which he would wash and wear. In such hardship he lived and pressed forward. As for the manner of his passing, he fell ill. At that time there were also epidemics and the like.

Similarly, it has been recorded of Sayyidna Ghulam Husayn Muqaddas that when he would be present with Sayyidna Ali bin Muhsin Shawam, his eyes would be watering, tears flowing from them constantly, and they would be red. Sayyidna would ask, "Is your health all right?" He would say, "Yes, it is all right," saying that he had a cold. This went on for several days. Later the *da'i sahib* came to know that where he had his *iqamah* (residence), below it lived those *Udayni* migrants who cooked their food on wood, causing smoke to rise and trouble him. But he had not told Sayyidna sahib that the cooking of the *Udaynis* was burning his eyes. For this reason, in the *Nass al-Sharif* (sacred text of designation), the *laqab* used for him reads: `al-walad al-adib dhi al-khulq`, `al-adab al-allaf al-arib dhi al-khulq al-ajib`, the most well-mannered, intelligent one of wondrous character. A brother of great, great qualities, possessed of most wondrous qualities, the owner of noble morals. He said `dhi al-khulq al-ajib`.

`wa al-fawz wa al-falah fi da'im al-sa'adah. Wa amma al-hajj ila Bayt Allah al-Haram, fa kafa bi qawlihi ta'ala 'alayh`: and triumph and success in everlasting bliss; as for the Hajj to the Sacred House of Allah, the saying of the Exalted suffices concerning it. Regarding the performance of Hajj towards the Sacred House of Allah, the saying of Allah is sufficient, and so too the saying of the Messenger, *`alayh al-salam*: `man arada khayr al-dunya wa al-akhirah`, whoever desires the good of this world and the Hereafter, whoever intends the good and well-being of both worlds; `fa l-ya'umma hadha al-bayt`, let him intend to visit this House, that is, he should intend to visit this House of Allah; `aman ba'da am`, year after year, one year after the other; `wa al-istighfar bi khalis al-niyyat`, and seeking forgiveness with pure intentions, that he should seek forgiveness with pure intentions; `wa yamhu al-dhunub`, and it erases the sins, for that seeking forgiveness with pure intentions erases the sins completely and wipes them out; `wa al-sayyi'at`, and the evil deeds likewise. `Waffaqana Allah wa iyyaka ayyuha al-akh li mardatih`: O brother, may Allah grant us and you success in attaining His pleasure. `wa nazamana fi silk majami' al-anwar`: and may He place us within the assemblies of lights, which are the assemblies of the Imams of the religion, within their illumined gatherings. `wa du'atih`: and of their callers. `wa la yu'akhidhna bi mubighat al-dhunub`: and may Allah the Exalted not hold us accountable for our major sins. `wa satara 'anna jami' al-'uyub`: and may He conceal all our faults. `wa waffaqana wa saddadna fi jami' al-af'al wa al-aqwal`: and may He grant us success and uprightness in all our deeds and words. `bi haqqi Muhammad wa alih khayr al-al`: for the sake of Muhammad and his family, `khayr al-al`, those who are the best of kin.

A question was raised. In the *salawat* that is recited it is said:

> `Allahumma salli 'ala Sayyidina Muhammadin wa 'ala ali Sayyidina Muhammadin kama sallayta wa sallamta wa barakta wa rahimta wa tarahhamta 'ala Ibrahima wa 'ala ali Ibrahim`

O Allah, send prayers upon our master Muhammad and upon the family of our master Muhammad, just as You sent prayers and peace and blessings and mercy and compassion upon Ibrahim and upon the family of Ibrahim. So upon Janab Ibrahim, *`alayh al-salam*, what manner of *salawat* was sent down, the one concerning which it is said `kama sallayta wa sallamta 'ala Ibrahim`? Is that defined anywhere? The answer is that for any Imam a specific formula of *salawat* has not been transmitted. That distinction was not there for any Imam. But it happened for Janab Ibrahim.

There is also this. It is said, `Muhammadun wa 'Aliyun khayr al-bashar`, Muhammad and Ali are the best of creation, and `wa 'itratuhuma khayr al-'itar`, and their progeny are the best of progenies, and it is repeated, said two times. What Sayyidna Ali bin Sulayman says regarding this is that the first utterance refers to the Imams who passed in ancient times up to Sayyidna Hunayd, and declares that the Muhammad and Ali of the present time are the best of creation among all those past Imams up until *kashf* (unveiling), until Janab Hunayd. Then it is said a second time, `Muhammadun wa 'Aliyun khayr al-bashar wa 'itratuhuma khayr al-'itar`, and this second utterance declares that the Muhammad and Ali who have now appeared, and their progeny that has appeared, are better than the Imams from Janab Hunayd up to Janab Hashim. So it refers to the Imam of the time. In this way the phrase is said twice.

Upon the seventh Imam Muhammad this also applies. The seventh Imam, who is Muhammad bin Isma'il, has upon him this same `Muhammadun wa 'Aliyun khayr al-bashar, wa 'itratuhuma khayr al-'itar`. That which is *mawsul* (connected) upon Sayyidna Muhammad bin Isma'il, *salawat Allah 'alayh*, is that when the *shahadah* (testimony of faith) is given, meaning `ashhadu anna Muhammadan rasul Allah`, I bear witness that Muhammad is the Messenger of Allah, that is one testimony. The other testimony given is for Mawlana Muhammad bin Isma'il, who is the *Sahib al-din al-tahir* (Master of the Pure Religion). The one who is the *Sabir*, the seventh, who is the *Sahib al-din al-tahir*, abrogated the *shari'ah* of the Prophet 'Isa. He had the authority to abrogate the *shari'ah*, and in the blink of an eye (*chashm-e-zadan*) he abrogated it and then reinstated that same *shari'ah*. Sayyidna Muhammad bin Isma'il holds a great station, for being the seventh.

In this regard there are four verses in Surah *Qul Huwa Allahu Ahad*. The Surah has four verses, and in one narration five, since `Lam yalid wa lam yulad` is counted as two verses. But there are four verses. In those four verses, corresponding to the *dawr al-nabawi*, the *dawr al-tayyibi*, and the *dawr al-qiyamah*, there are four Imams who are the very essence, and they are attributed to the verses of *Qul Huwa Allahu Ahad*:

1. `Qul huwa Allahu ahad`, Say, He is Allah, the One, indicates Mawlana Muhammad bin Isma'il.
2. `Allahu al-samad`, Allah, the Eternal Refuge, is Sayyidna al-Tayyib, Mawlana 'Ali bin al-Tayyib.
3. `Lam yalid wa lam yulad`, He neither begets nor is born, is the 35th Imam, Sayyidna Ahmad, who is the *waqih* Imam of Sayyidna Sulayman sahib.
4. `wa lam yakun lahu kufuwan ahad`, Nor is there to Him any equivalent, indicates the 49th Imam, the Imam of today, whose name has not been revealed, who is called Abu al-Qa'im, the Qa'im of the Imams.

So it refers to these four Imams, attributed to the four verses of *Qul Huwa Allahu Ahad*.

The *Maghrib ki tatawwa'* also originates from him. For the *Maghrib tatawwa'* there is the saying of Amir al-Mu'minin:

> `man lam yatatawwa' fa laysa minna`

Whoever does not perform the supererogatory prayer is not from us. Whoever leaves the *maghrib tatawwa'* is not from us, which establishes its great importance. And these four *rak'ahs* of the *maghrib tatawwa'* are attributed to Sayyidna Mawlana Muhammad bin Isma'il.

In the progeny of Sayyidna Muhammad bin Isma'il there were three who were *muhtajibin* (veiled) and were also *wasi*s. Such is the station. His son Yad'a became one, and Ali became one. They all became *muhtajibin*. And then their *hujjat*s were appointed: 'Abd Allah, Ahmad, and Husayn.

*Bismillah al-Rahman al-Rahim*. *Sharf al-Fatihah* to the souls: Sayyidna Mawlana 'Alim bin Hibatullah, *qaddasa Allahu ruhahu wa nawwara zarihahu wa ja'alana min barakatihi wa a'azatihi wa ta'idatihi wa taqabbal minna al-ifadah wa al-istifadah bi ahsani ma yakun bi jah al-mursalin wa Sayyid al-awwalin wa al-akhirin wa al-Qur'an al-'Azim wa Ahl al-Bayt al-Tayyibin al-Tahirin salawat Allah 'alayhim ajma'in*. *Al-Fatihah*.

> *Bismillah al-Rahman al-Rahim. Al-hamdu lillahi rabb al-'alamin. Al-rahmanirrahim. Maliki yawmid-din. Iyyaka na'budu wa iyyaka nasta'in. Ihdinas-siratal-mustaqim. Siratal-ladhina an'amta 'alayhim ghayril-maghdubi 'alayhim wa lad-dallin.*

In the name of Allah, the Most Gracious, the Most Merciful. Praise belongs to Allah, Lord of the worlds, the Most Gracious, the Most Merciful, Master of the Day of Judgment. You alone we worship, and from You alone we seek help. Guide us upon the straight path, the path of those upon whom You have bestowed favor, not of those who have earned anger, nor of those who have gone astray.
