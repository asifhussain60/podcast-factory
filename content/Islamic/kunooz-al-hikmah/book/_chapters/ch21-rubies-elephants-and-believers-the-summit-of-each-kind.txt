# Rubies, Elephants, and Believers: The Summit of Each Kind

*A'udhu billahi minash shaitanir rajim. Bi fadli Bismillah al-Rahman al-Rahim. Wa bihi nasta'in wa salla'llahu 'ala rasulihi Sayyidna Muhammadin wa 'ala ali Muhammadin wa barik wa sallim.*

*Al-su'al al-thamin 'an al-jawahir wa'l-yawaqit*, the eighth question, concerning jewels and rubies. The eighth question concerns jewels and rubies, the stone, and what is related to that.

Every thing has a *ghayah*, an ultimate point, meaning its limit and its highest expression. So there is *ghayat al-ahjار*, the ultimate point of stones, and *ghayat al-ma'dan*, the ultimate point of minerals. Among the minerals found inside the earth, and among stones, the question is what their *ghayah* is, the most noble among them. It is the ruby, *yaqut*. Among all jewels, all minerals, and all stones, *ahjar*, their *ghayah* is the ruby.

In the same way there is *ghayat al-haywan*, the ultimate point of animals. Among all animals their *ghayah* is the elephant, *fil*. It has been said that the reason is the elephant's intelligence, for the elephant is the most intelligent among animals, and by virtue of that intelligence it becomes the *ghayah*. Its physical strength is also immense, yet it is by virtue of its length, its breadth, and its shrewdness that it is honored. The horse too holds its own place, but the elephant possesses a greater shrewdness than the horse.

Likewise, *ghayat al-insan*, the ultimate point of man, is the *mu'min*, the believer. The *ghayah* of man is the believer. The station of the *mu'min* is lofty: Allah Ta'ala has used this single word for the believers as well, including them in the rank of those honored with *Ahl al-Jalis*. And wherever in the Glorious Quran the phrase *Ya ayyuha'lladhina amanu*, O you who believe, appears, its primary intention is Amir al-Mu'minin and those who lead the believers, the *amir*.

Allah Ta'ala Himself has called Himself *Mu'min*. Among His names is *al-Mu'min*. So there are ranks, and this is the condition of the matter. There is also *awwalu man amana*, the first who believed. The final two verses of Surat al-Baqarah are extremely noble, and the Rasul has said that one should recite them daily before sleeping. For when the Rasulullah, sallallahu 'alayhi wa alihi wasallam, journeyed on the Mi'raj, Allah bestowed two things upon him, things that, as he himself said, had not been bestowed upon any prophet before him. One was Surat al-Fatiha, which was bestowed upon him, and the second was the last two verses of Surat al-Baqarah. Although the recitation in *sadaqa'llah* takes in the last three verses, the specific mention is of the last two. Within them the phrase *awwalu man amana* has been used, the first who believed. The numerical value derived from *awwalu man amana* equals the numerical value of *'Ali ibn Abi Talib*. Among all the interpretations pointing to Janab 'Ali (ع), this stands out: the numerical value of *awwalu man amana* and the numerical value of *'Ali ibn Abi Talib* are equal.

Concerning the jewels, *jawahir*, and the rubies, *yawaqit*, a question was asked: *hal hiya tashtami'u ila mahallin ma'ruf wa makanin mawsuf*, do they gather in a known place and a described location? That is, does their gathering occur at a special, famous, described place? *Wa tanhallu wa tanqasimu 'ala'l-kawakib*, and do they dissolve and get distributed upon the planets? That is, are they ground up and distributed among the *kawakib*? *Wa idha kana yashtami'u fa-'ala kam muddatuha*, and if it gathers, then for what duration? If their gathering occurs in the *kawakib*, for how long does it persist? *Wa kayfa intiqa'uha*, and how is its selection? How is the selection within the *kawakib* carried out? *Hal hiya ta'udu ila mawdi'iha'l-qadim*, does it return to its ancient place? After being ground and entering the *kawakib*, will these jewels and rubies gather again in their original state? This is what the questioner asked. *Tafaddalu 'alayya*, please favor me, for you are worthy of it. *Fa'l-'abdu yas'alu malikahu min fadlihi*, for the servant asks of his master from his bounty.

*Al-jawab 'ala dhalika's-su'al*, the answer to that question. *Naqulu bi-'awni'llahi'l-Kabiri'l-Muta'al*, we say with the help of Allah, the Great, the Most High. *Wa mannat Waliyyi'l-'Asri wa'z-Zaman 'alayhi salawatu'r-Rahimi'r-Rahman*, and by the grace, meaning the gift, of the one who is the *Wali al-'Asr wa'l-Zaman*, may the blessings of Al-Rahim and Al-Rahman be upon him. And by the *ta'id*, the aid, of *Mawlaya al-'Alam, 'Alam al-Huda*, by the aids of Sayyidna Hibatullah Saheb, which are most luminous. *Wa sirruhu'l-bahir al-sari ila lata'ifi's-suwar*, and his manifest secret, flowing to the subtleties of the forms, streaming toward them. The answer is *anna'l-yawaqit wa'l-jawahir al-hisan tuhfazu fi khaza'ini'l-muluk*, that the fine rubies and jewels are preserved in the treasuries of kings. Fine jewels and rubies have their protection in the treasuries of kings. *Wa tusan*, and they are protected, so that there their complete protection is maintained. *Thumma tansha'u mata sha'a'l-mudabbir*, then it arises whenever the Disposer wills. They remain for a period, set in crowns and placed at positions of honor, and then, when the Disposer wills, they begin to be ground up and enter a state of dissolution. *Wa yasiru fi'l-amakinish-sharifah*, and it travels in the noble places; they then travel to noble and exalted places of distinction. *Wa mata abala'l-aflak wa'l-kawakib sarat hadhihi li-taghliba fi'l-khidmah*, and when the celestial spheres and planets wore out, these traveled to prevail in service. When the *aflak* and *kawakib* fall, each must ascend toward the spiritual realm, and every ascending thing must touch the earth before its ascent, except the *Falak al-Muhit*, the encompassing sphere, which from where it already stands becomes the Universal Intellect, *'Aql al-Kulli*, and enters the spiritual realm directly. All other things, whether the moon, the sun, or the *aflak*, must touch the earth, and only thereafter does their progress occur. Meteorites likewise fall from above to the earth and become extremely valuable, which is consistent with the same principle.

So these *aflak* and *kawakib* ascend, and when they fall, they ascend. What takes their place is the jewels and rubies, which assume their *khilafat*, their vicegerency, in service. *Bi-hikmatin muharriki'l-'awالim*, by the wisdom of the Mover of the worlds, with a wisdom belonging to the Mover of all things. *Wa baqiyat muddata'l-kawni'l-adami*, and it remained for the duration of the Adamite existence; for the duration of a *kawr* of Adam, they remain. Thus *khilafat*s are established: when the *aflak* and *kawakib* progress, the jewels and rubies take their *khilafat*, and for one *kawr* of Adam their duration persists. *Ila an tawaffa ma 'alayha min al-khidam*, until it fulfilled the service that was upon it, until their services are completed. *Wa yajri'l-amru 'ala hadhihi'l-khutiyyah hatta yас'ada'l-'alamu bi'l-kulliyyah*, and the matter proceeds along this path until the world ascends in its entirety, which happens at the end of a *kawr*. *Li-qawli asdaq al-qa'ilin*, as per the saying of the most truthful of speakers, for Allah Ta'ala says: *kama bada'na awwala khalqin nu'iduhu*, as We began the first creation, We will repeat it; *wa'dan 'alayna*, a promise upon Us, this matter is Our promise; *inna kunna fa'ilin*, indeed We are the performers of it, and We will do it again. This coming and going of the *kawr*, all these things will keep unfolding.

The verse from Surah Ya-Sin also speaks to this, that He will create again: *Awa laysa'lladhī khalaqa's-samawati wa'l-arda bi-qadirin 'ala an yakhluqa mithlahum, bala wa huwa'l-khallаqu'l-'alim*, is not He who created the heavens and the earth able to create the like of them? Yes, and He is the knowing Creator. He is the *Khallaq al-'Alim*. Allah created the heavens and the earth, and He is able to create them again. The same system will be recreated. After each *kawr*, a new system comes.
