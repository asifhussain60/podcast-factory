# Noise Audit: Chapters

Status: CLEAN after in-place remediation

Scope: all chapter drafts under the Kunooz al-Hikmah chapter set.

Rule applied: front matter, preface-like setup, descriptions of the book, author posture, reader permission/covenant language, chain-of-transmission framing, and other book-object apparatus are noise unless they directly preserve knowledge or teaching. Real doctrinal instruction, practice guidance, Quranic interpretation, theological structure, and supporting citations are preserved.

## Findings

1. Front-matter and book-object scaffolding had leaked into chapter openings and closings.

   Remediation: removed or rewrote chapter language that described the book, author, reader, permission to read, chapter machinery, and closing apparatus. The affected sections now state the teaching directly.

2. A closing block preserved permission-to-read and author-humility material as if it were doctrine.

   Remediation: condensed the closing to a single doctrinal paragraph about the line, the Dai, the Imam, and the continuity of transmitted wisdom.

3. Some scan hits remain by design.

   Resolution: retained legitimate uses such as Quranic "Book of Allah," book titles inside citations, "inner reading," and doctrinal "stored wisdom." These are not front matter or authorial apparatus.

## Result

The chapter set now preserves the real knowledge and teaching while removing the preface, description, author, reading-permission, and book-wrapper noise requested for removal.
