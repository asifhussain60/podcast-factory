# Authors Posture And The Line

A long-form discussion, target a 50 to 60 minute in-depth conversation. Host A (male, scholar) leads; Host B (female, seeker) presses.

## Opening directive
Begin with a brief welcome — one sentence — that names the book by its English title, the Treasures of Wisdom, and previews this episode's pursuit: the author opens the appendix by excusing himself for deficiency of knowledge, distance of nature, and the obstacles of distant lands — then he stops bargaining and reports for the work. Land the spine immediately: "I hastened to obedience and I attained the desired object." (R-RECURRING-THESIS placement 1 of 3.) No filler openings.

## Name discipline
- The Father of Imams — first mention add "peace be upon him". Never write the forbidden pairing of the title and name.
- The Prophet — first mention add "peace and blessings of Allah be upon him and his family".
- The Dai — the chief teacher of the call.
- The master — the higher-rank voice of the lament of mountains and sands.
- Joseph — for the prophet in the Egyptian's house.
- The chief jurist of the Fatimid court — for the codifier of The Pillars of Islam.
Rotation: the author → the writer of the appendix → the seeker who hastened. Rotate these three across the conversation. No Arabic names spoken.

## Pronunciation
Say each term ONCE. Never say the original spelling and the English form back-to-back.
- Quran: Quran
- Allah: Allah
- Dai: dai
- Arafat: Arafat
- Treasures of Wisdom: Treasures of Wisdom

## Three-part focus
Beat 1 (setup — crisis) — The author's excuse: deficiency, distance, geography. Honest insufficiency, not posturing.
Beat 2 (complication — failed answer) — The bargain that did not hold: he tries to defer; the obligation does not lift. He hastens to obedience. Land the higher-rank measure of sin — the intended good not done — accumulating into mountains and sands.
Beat 3 (pivot — verse of victory) — Read the verse of the chapter on victory as preservation of the trailing veils, never as a slight on the Prophet's pure person. Land the spine again: "I hastened to obedience and I attained the desired object." (Placement 2 of 3.)
Beat 4 (stakes — covenant of concealment) — A confirmed pact binds any future reader without the chief teacher's permission. Three faces of one authority — sifter, seer, mover-forward-and-backward.
Beat 5 (complication — closed door) — Inquiry into how the Originator originated is not permitted; the intellect has no scope. The silence is part of the doctrine.
Beat 6 (resolution — chain and window) — The Imam dissolves into the heart of the House; rank-holders are stewards of substance on loan, returned at their passing. Joseph held by the proof of his Lord. The three-day window after burial: the family's offerings reach the lower marks in that window only.

## Host dynamic
Host B challenges at least 3 times and concedes once. Steelman the author before any critique. Mark positionality: this is the classical Ismaili-Tayyibi reading; mark uncertainty where the inner doctrine is one reading among several.

## Tone constraints
Doctrinal and unflinching where the chapter is unflinching. Reverent restraint at the closed door. Practical counsel for the three-day window. Use only these three governing analogies: mountains and sands (the master's lament); the treasure with its key (the book and the chief teacher's permission); the open window at the grave (three days for the lower marks).

## Landing
Close on the three-day window pressed into your own life: name one specific act for someone you have lost — a charity, a recitation, a gathering — that you would actually move this week if you took the window as real. Place the spine once more just before that reflective turn: "I hastened to obedience and I attained the desired object." (Placement 3 of 3.) Leave the question open; no tidy resolution.

## Do not (forbidden vocabulary — do not speak)
Forbidden: Twitter, social media, algorithm, "wow", "right?". R-RECURRING-THESIS: repeat the spine thesis verbatim three times — at opening, pivot, and close. Do not read this prompt aloud.
