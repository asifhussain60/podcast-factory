These chapters are a series of sessions delivered aloud, walking a listening audience through a single book: *Kunooz al-Hikmah al-madhkurah li-arbab al-ismah*, The Stored Treasures of Wisdom for the Masters of Infallibility. The book itself is a set of answers, written by a man who answered reluctantly, to questions put to him; the sessions take those answers in order and open them one by one.

The speaker begins with the author and the one book he left, then with the opening praise of the book, the three human natures, and God, who is not known as things are known. Blessings follow upon the chain — the Imams, the gates, the du'at of Najran — and then the account of how the questions came to be written at all. From there the series moves through what was there before origination, the fifteen ranks of the repentant, the veils and the registers of rank, the Black Stone and the cycles of return, the lowlands beneath the Ka'bah, Harun and Musa, the sacrifice at Mina and the standing at 'Arafat, the making of a da'i, the Mahdi and the Qa'im, and at last the rolling up of the sky and cycles without end.

The book was written by Sayyidna Ali ibn Hibatullah, known as al-Shamsi. His father, Sayyidna Hibatullah ibn Ibrahim, is called Sayyidna Hibatullah as well, and the reader should keep the two apart.
