No. And Syedna Ja'far did not write a single book on `haqa'iq`. All his books are on `hujjat` (proof) or `ta'wil` (interpretation), although his own station is very lofty.
(Mim and `Ayn` are often mentioned to refer to the `muhtajib`.)
Yes.
(Here Mim and `Ayn` are being used to refer to the `du'at`.)
Yes. Yes, yes.

*Al-'ati bi-ma la yakhtur ‘alal afkar*. (Bringing forth that which had never occurred to the thoughts.) Such things you brought forth that had never crossed the thoughts. They were not manifest in the thoughts. The books you have, *Anwar al-Shashaniyyah* and other books, the lofty realities within them, are very special things that you have presented.
*Wa saribuhu alladhi ghada ghidha' al-nufus bi-asrar al-malakut al-quddus*. (And his drinking companion, who became the nourishment of souls with the secrets of the holy `malakut`.) And your drinking companion (`sarib`). Your `sarib` means your... Sayyidna Ibrahim. He is such that he became the nourishment of souls with the secrets of the holy `malakut`. From the secrets of Allah, the Holy.
*Wa Muhammaduhu al-muhajir min baldatihi li-‘uluwwi shanih wa rutbatihi*. (And his Muhammad, the emigrant from his town for the loftiness of his station and his rank.) And Muhammad, that is, Muhammad bin Isma'il. He is the first `Da'i` who emigrated from his town to... to Najran. Until then, where was the `da'wah`? It was in Yemen. In Sana'a, in Haraz, and in Sa'fan, it was in these places, the entire `da'wah`. But he is the first `Da'i` who left Yemen and came here to Najran. For the loftiness of your station. Your station and rank is lofty. And you are the first `Da'i`, and the only `Da'i`, who was given permission to lead prayers in `ghayr jazirah` (outside the Arabian peninsula). So you led prayers in Habasha (Abyssinia). Yes. Sayyidna Muhammad bin Isma'il.
(Did the `du'at` go back again?)
Yes.
(After him, they went back to Yemen?)
Yes.
(Your passing was in Najran?)
Your passing was in Najran. And then... your... slowly, the entire `da'wah` moved towards Najran.
(No, after him there are in the list...)
A few were there. A few were there, but then they moved.
(It was at the time of Sayyidna Ghulam Hussain Sahib that they all came here to Najran.)
No, before that. Before that... before that... Sayyidna Hasan Shihab... Sayyidna Ali bin 'Ibad... he is from here... they were here.

And what did you do? You emigrated towards Najran. And you also went to Abuna. Sayyidna Muhammad bin Isma'il. So, Sayyidna Sulayman Sahib, then, in his time, he toured Abuna. And he stayed in Abuna for quite some time. Whereas you, when you came from Yemen to India, it was a journey of great hardship. It took you one year. And at that time, you traveled through various cities of Syria, and so on. So you also stayed in Abuna. And you built a mosque there too. Which still exists today. And Sayyidna Hussain bin Hasan enlarged that mosque, and then later in the time of Sayyidna Hussain bin Isma'il, it was further expanded, and rooms were built for those sitting in `i'tikaf` (seclusion). It's a very magnificent mosque.
(Is it near the port?)
No, Abuna is not a port. Abuna is a mountainous area. It's very far. It's a very mountainous area, about 200 kilometers from Najran. And Sayyidna Sahib went there. I also had the chance to go there once. And the grave of Sayyidna Rashid, Rashid bin ‘Isa, is also there. So we went to the grave of Sayyidna Rashid too and performed the *ziyarat*. And Sayyidna Rashid is the one who, after becoming a `Da'i`, for six years stood in his prayer niche and performed his missed prayers. It's possible that in the early period of the `da'wah`, some of your prayers were missed. You made up for six years of prayers standing in the prayer niche. And then, after the passing of Sayyidna Hussain bin Hasan, Sayyidna Hussain bin Isma'il gave one of our companions, Ahmad was his name, a boy from Yemen, he was very intelligent and a very good boy, he gave him the prayers of Sayyidna Hussain bin Hasan, the prayers of four years that were missed, because you... in your last days, you became like an angel, and you couldn't keep track of your prayers. So you made up for four years of prayers. And when Sayyidna Hussain bin Hasan passed away, you came from your house barefoot to his side, and it was a considerable distance. The distance is from here to, let's say, Grand Road station. You came there barefoot.

*Sallam 'alayhim ajma'in abad al-abadin*. (Peace be upon them all, forever and ever.) May peace be upon all of them, for all time.
