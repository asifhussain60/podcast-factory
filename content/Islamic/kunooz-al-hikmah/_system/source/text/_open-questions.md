## Fidelity-gate flags (holistic editorial)

Recommended fix: restore the one-sentence etymology when `jabarut` is first introduced ("Jabarut means the powerful one, from the root `jabr`").
- [P1] T055 (refined-english.md line 129): the verse in Surah al-Hajj that gathers fifteen of the Asma al-Husna in a single sentence is mentioned, but only one name (`Ya Dhal-Jalali wal-Ikram`) is given. Recommended fix: list the fifteen names as the lecture does, or at minimum cite the verse number and say which names are gathered, so the teaching is not a bare summary.
- [P0] T085 (refined-english.md, missing): the Quranic `al-aziz al-alim` reading — that it refers to the Ashir who knows how many mawrs are needed for the purification of each troop — is absent. The Ashir doctrine is otherwise covered, but the specific Surah Yasin verse-attachment that the source lecture builds on is dropped. Recommended fix: insert the verse-reading into the Ashir / nadimin section (around the "fifteen categories" exposition), restoring the link between `al-aziz al-alim` and the Ashir's knowledge of purification.
- [P0] T097 (refined-english.md, missing): the verse `kullu shayin ahsaynahu fi imamim mubeen` (Surah Yasin) and the deliberate contrast between `ahsayna` (enumerated, individuality retained) and `jamana` (gathered/jumbled) is dropped entirely. This is a load-bearing teaching about the Imam-mubeen's preservation of individual identity in the gathering. Recommended fix: restore the verse and the lexical contrast in the Imam-soul / qalb section, where the gathering of believers' forms at the Bab al-Abwab is discussed.

No external doctrine insertions detected.
