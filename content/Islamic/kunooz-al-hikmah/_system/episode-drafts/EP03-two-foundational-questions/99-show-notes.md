# Show notes — EP03

**Title:** The Two Foundational Questions

**Blurb:** 'The first two questions of *Kunooz al-Hikmah* and the doctrines they unfold. A wall before the question of what was before the World of Origination; a map for the question of the encompassing sphere — and in between, the fall of the penitent essences that placed us in the last of the last category, the illusion of equality that was the deepest curse, and the death-bearing veil that absorbs Karbala. The second answer opens into the book''s great teaching: the Imam''s dissolved heart, the substance that flows to the ranks and returns, the *riyat* the believer draws daily at his ablution, his pre-dawn meal, and his breaking of the fast. The number 28 closes the lesson as the eternal order of the higher world.
