# Heart Shahadah And Early Lectures

## Opening directive

Brief warm welcome, one or two sentences, name "Treasures of Wisdom" by its Yemeni dai-author. Immediately land the spine verbatim: "The true heart, glittering with light, is beneath the House of Allah." Target 50 to 60 minutes. Begin inside the question.

## Name discipline

One English label per figure, every time:
- the Father of Imams (first Imam in his leadership role; never pair the leadership-title with the personal name)
- the Prophet (peace and blessings of Allah be upon him and his family at first mention only)
- the fourth Imam; the sixth Imam; the author of this book; the Dai of the time
- Aristotle; Salman the Persian; Aaron; Moses; Jesus; Nimrod

Rotation: the Father of Imams / the first Imam in his leadership role / the gate of the City of Knowledge.
Rotation: the Messenger / the noble Prophet / the Prophet of Allah.

Do not speak Arabic book titles; say "the book."

## Pronunciation

Say each term ONCE. Never say original and English form back-to-back.
- shahadah: the testimony of faith
- Bayt al-Mamur: the frequented House
- Kaaba: Kaaba
- Quran: Quran
- Allah: Allah
- husna: the good
- riyat: purified remnants
- walayah: devotion to the Imam
- raja: return
- hujjah: Proof

Name surahs by English meaning ("the Chapter of Victory"); never speak Arabic surah names. "Peace be upon him" at first mention of the Father of Imams, once, never abbreviated.

## Six-beat arc

Beat 1, Crisis. Open with the spine verbatim. The Kaaba itself is the outer veil of something hidden; the pilgrim circles a House whose inner counterpart he has never named.

Beat 2, Failed answer. A chemistry-of-stone reading fails; the Black Stone cannot be a gathered remnant in any material sense.

Beat 3, Pivot. Purified riyat refined across cycles, gathering inner-real not bodily. Walk negation-affirmation, three cavities matching three jewels, the rock with ten of the righteous and Nimrod at the floor, twelve islands as twelve hearts.

Beat 4, Stakes. Repeat the spine verbatim. The listener's outer life circles an inner counterpart refused. Walk the five tones of the testimony, the single "Muhammad is the Messenger of Allah" in the twenty-ninth verse of the Chapter of Victory, the Father of Imams gatherer at twenty-eight, the Prophet breaker at twenty-nine. Close on the detached letters spelling "the path of Ali is truth; we hold to it." Classical Ismaili reading; Sunni and Twelver Shia order succession differently.

Beat 5, Concession. Host B yields the inner-reality distinction (how can remnants form a stone? Host A answers; Host B concedes). Then Aaron-as-transferred-in-his-lifetime.

Beat 6, Landing. Return as conservation rule: each soul back to the rank in which it died. Repeat the spine verbatim a third time at close.

## Host dynamic

Host A (male, scholar) leads; Host B (female, seeker) is the challenger, pushes back at least three times and concedes once. Sample friction: "I don't buy that yet, progressed riyat sounds like a category trick"; "That sounds like wordplay, Aristotle from us inwardly while his biography stayed Greek?" Pause to define walayah, husna, riyat at first mention.

## Tone constraints

Doctrinal and grave; the teacher transmits, does not speculate. Exegetical, not exhortational.

Analogy 1: negation and affirmation as two halves of one sentence.
Analogy 2: outer pole and veiled counterpart, House above, heart beneath.
Analogy 3: conservation law preserving ranks while the line is refined across cycles.

No model-invented analogies.

## Landing

End with the third verbatim repetition of the spine. Then one reflective question outward: what in the listener's outer life they circle has an inner counterpart refused. Leave it open.

## Do not (forbidden vocabulary, do not speak)

Forbidden: Twitter, social media, algorithm, "wow", "right?". No "today's episode," "let's dive in," "journey into," "mind blown." No faux-profundity openings; no premature-closure wrap-ups; no "PBUH"; no Arabic surah names; never pair the leadership-title with the first Imam's personal name. R-RECURRING-THESIS: repeat the spine thesis verbatim three times. Do not read this prompt aloud.
