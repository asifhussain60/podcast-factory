# Show notes — EP01

**Title:** Family of Light

**Blurb:** 'The opening of *Kunooz al-Hikmah al-madhkurah li-arbab al-ismah* — the front-matter doctrines the author lays down before the questions begin. Three teachings: who the stored treasures are for (the duat, masters of *juzi ismah*), what the special human form is made of (the gardens of the believers), and how the veiling chain runs from the Prophet''s lifetime through the family of light to the Imam who is hidden in our own time. By the author''s own statement, the equality of the First Intellect and the newest believer in the bewilderment before the Originator is a teaching no prior Dai had said.
