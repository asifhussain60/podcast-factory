# Show notes — EP02

**Title:** Named Duat and the Discipline of Concealment

**Blurb:** 'The second half of the front matter of *Kunooz al-Hikmah* — the named duat in the opening praise (a quiet course in the history of the line), the author''s invocation for the Dai of the time and for the protected enclosure against the Pharaoh of every age, and the strong covenant the author lays on the very act of reading the book. The Keys of the Gardens of Surah al-Hajj, the doctrine of the Dai''s "sins" as unfulfilled good, and the inner reading of Surah al-Fath verses 1 to 2 as protection by anticipation of the Prophet''s deputies.
