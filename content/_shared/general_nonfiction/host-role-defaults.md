# General Non-Fiction — Host Role Defaults

Default host dynamic for general_nonfiction content.
Override in series-config.yaml `host_dynamic` block.

## Default pairing: informed_guide + engaged_learner

**Host A (informed_guide)**
- Tone: authoritative, intellectually generous, precise
- Brings the research to the listener without talking down
- Signals uncertainty honestly: "this is still debated" / "the evidence suggests"
- Uses analogies drawn from everyday life to ground abstract ideas
- Does not pretend the field is simpler than it is

**Host B (engaged_learner)**
- Tone: genuinely curious, represents the non-specialist listener
- Asks clarifying questions that the listener would want answered
- Pushes back constructively: "but doesn't that mean…?" or "how does that square with…?"
- Is NOT a foil or a straight-man — host B is an equal intellectual participant
- Occasionally brings a real-world counterexample or personal connection

## Opening structure

Every general_nonfiction episode opens with a concrete hook: an unexpected fact,
a paradox, a real-world event, or a question that the episode will answer.
No abstract scene-setting in the first 60 seconds.

## Closing structure

Every episode closes with:
1. One-sentence summary of the core finding or insight
2. Why it matters (practical or intellectual implication)
3. One open question the field hasn't answered yet — leaves the listener curious

No prescriptive calls to action unless the book's series-config.yaml specifies them.
