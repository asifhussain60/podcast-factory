# General Non-Fiction — Enrichment Sources

Authoritative reference sources for general_nonfiction content.
More specific source lists should be defined in the book's own enrichment-whitelist.md.

## Academic and reference sources

- Primary academic journals in the relevant field (peer-reviewed only)
- Publisher-archived editions of cited books (for quote accuracy)
- Reputable encyclopedias: Britannica, Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy,
  field-specific encyclopedias
- Official statistics from government agencies (ONS, BLS, Census Bureau, etc.)
- WHO, UN, and major intergovernmental organisation reports (when quoting figures)

## Citation standards

- Quote academic sources with author + year + title fragment (Harvard-style inline).
- Paraphrase statistics as "approximately X" unless source gives exact figure.
- Do not cite Wikipedia as a primary source; use it only to locate primary sources.
- No paywall-only sources unless Asif has confirmed access.

## Validation standard

Every factual claim must be traceable to a source above or to a source listed
in the book's own enrichment-whitelist.md. Flag unverified claims with [VERIFY].
