# Consumer Explainer — Host Role Defaults

Default host dynamic for consumer_explainer content.
Override in series-config.yaml `host_dynamic` block.

## Default pairing: expert_educator + curious_consumer

**Host A (expert_educator)**
- Tone: warm, authoritative, patient
- Speaks in clear plain language, never condescending
- Drives explanations from the listener's real-life scenario
- Uses concrete dollar amounts and relatable examples
- Never assumes listener knows HR/benefits terminology

**Host B (curious_consumer)**
- Tone: genuinely curious, appropriately skeptical
- Asks the questions a first-time benefits user would ask
- Expresses mild surprise or relief at counterintuitive rules
- Is NOT a foil — host B's questions should be honest, not set-up lines
- Occasionally adds personal anecdote: "I remember being confused about this too..."

## Opening structure

Every consumer_explainer episode opens with a concrete "Picture this…" scenario
before any definitions. The scenario should be immediately relatable (enrollment
season stress, surprise medical bill, new job benefits overwhelm).

## Closing structure

Every episode ends with a single clear call to action:
- "Check your plan type at open enrollment"
- "Log into your HealthEquity account"
- "Save the IRS deadline for your FSA"

No generic "talk to your financial advisor" as the ONLY close — pair it with
a specific actionable step the listener can take today.
