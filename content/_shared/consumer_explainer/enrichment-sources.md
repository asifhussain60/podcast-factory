# Consumer Explainer — Enrichment Sources

Authoritative reference sources for consumer_explainer content.
The pipeline uses these as reference when enriching episode framings.

## Benefits & HR

- IRS Publication 969 (Health Savings Accounts and Other Tax-Favored Health Plans)
- IRS Publication 502 (Medical and Dental Expenses)
- SHRM (Society for Human Resource Management) HR Knowledge Articles
- DOL (Department of Labor) ERISA guidance publications
- HealthEquity plan documents and employer guides

## Compliance tone

- Plain-language principles: Plain Writing Act (2010)
- Consumer Financial Protection Bureau (CFPB) plain-language standards
- ADA accessibility guidelines for benefit communications

## Statistical sources

- KFF (Kaiser Family Foundation) employer health benefit surveys
- BLS (Bureau of Labor Statistics) employee benefit data
- EBRI (Employee Benefit Research Institute) annual reports

## Validation standard

Every factual claim must be traceable to one of the above sources.
No claims about tax treatment without citing the relevant IRS publication.
Round-number approximations must be labelled as approximate.
